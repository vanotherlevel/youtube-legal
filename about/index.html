About this application

vAnother Level is a small application operated by one developer to produce and publish gaming comparison and ranking videos (including YouTube Shorts). It connects to a YouTube account through Google OAuth so that finished videos can be uploaded to the vAnother Level YouTube channel, with their titles, descriptions and privacy settings.

What it does

  • Uploads finished videos to the connected YouTube channel using the YouTube Data API.
  • Sets video details such as title, description, tags and visibility (private, unlisted or public).
  • Does not collect, sell or share personal information from visitors or other users.

Your data

The application only accesses the Google account that the developer connects. See the Privacy Policy for exactly what is accessed and how it is used, and the Terms of Service for the rules of use.
