Black Myth: Wukong: How to Beat Every Boss, Best Weapons & Enemies

By vAnother Level · Updated 2026-11-12
Home › Guides › Black Myth: Wukong

  1. Every boss (in order)
  2. Best weapons
  3. Normal enemies

This guide covers every boss in Black Myth: Wukong in the order you meet them, across the prologue, all six chapters, the four secret areas and the Mount Mei super boss, with what kills people and how to answer it. Below that you get a staff tier list with how to craft each one, the armor sets, spells, transformations, vessels and spirits worth using, and a chapter-by-chapter rundown of the normal yaoguai.

Five rules that win most fights: (1) Immobilize is your best spell all game. Cast it right as a long combo starts, not after. (2) Learn the perfect dodge timing and put early Sparks into dodge and stamina skills. (3) Charge Focus for a heavy attack after a combo, since a full charge staggers most bosses and breaks Yellowbrow's golden shield. (4) Save your transformation for phase two, because it acts as a second health bar. (5) Match your medicine to the element: Shock-Quelling Powder for thunder, Antimiasma Powder for poison, Body-Cooling Powder for burn. If you catch fire, dodge several times in a row to put it out.

Prologue: Erlang Shen

The opening fight on Mount Huaguo is a scripted tutorial. You play a powered-up Great Sage and cannot really lose, so use it to get a feel for light combos, heavy attacks and dodging. Erlang comes back much later as the hardest optional boss in the game (see the secret ending section).

Chapter 1: Black Wind Mountain

Chapter 1 teaches you the core loop: dodge, punish with light attacks, end with a charged heavy, and Immobilize anything that starts a long combo. Spend your first Sparks on dodge and stamina upgrades.

Guangzhi (Chapter 1, optional)

Where: Forest of Wolves, the left path from the Outside the Forest shrine. He is optional but worth it, because he drops Red Tides, your first transformation (a fire wolf with its own health bar).

  • Fire spear combo: a string of swings that ends in a thrust. Back off and let it play out, then dodge the last thrust and punish.
  • Spinning fire spear: sidestep the moment he starts to spin.
  • Double thrust: a short pause, then two thrusts. Dodge twice.
  • Spear throw: fast, at mid range. Watch the spear and roll sideways.
  • Burn: if he sets you on fire, dodge several times in a row to put it out.
  • Approach: open with Immobilize, land a full light combo, back off. Repeat whenever Immobilize is ready, and never swing into the middle of his combo.

Wandering Wight (Chapter 1, optional)

Where: Forest of Wolves, the right path from Outside the Forest, over the small bridge. This giant-headed ghost hits very hard for so early in the game, but the reward is the Wandering Wight spirit, which guides recommend for almost every hard boss after it, plus the Cat Eye Beads curio (a small crit boost).

  • Ground Pound: three slams, each reaching further out. Back away as soon as the first one starts. A palm thrust often follows.
  • Palm Thrust: a long-range glowing hand that does massive damage. Dodge sideways when the hand lights up.
  • Swipes and stomps: wait for the crouch or the raised leg, then dodge through or to the side.
  • Approach: attack when he turns his back. A light combo into a charged heavy makes him flinch, and a second flinch knocks him down for free hits. Use Red Tides around half health if you have it.

Lingxuzi (Chapter 1)

Where: Guanyin Temple in the Forest of Wolves. The big grey wolf is the first real skill check.

  • Uppercut into ground slam: the move that kills people. He backs up, pauses and turns side-on before it. Back off immediately when you see that pause.
  • Howl: stuns you briefly. He stops and curls up a little first, so move away.
  • Leaping double slash: he taps the ground with his left arm, then leaps. Dodge the first slash, get one or two hits in, then dodge the second.
  • Low bite: a snap at your legs while he stalks you. Jump over it.
  • Approach: Immobilize gives the longest opening. Use it for a charged heavy, then go back to dodging.

Whiteclad Noble (Chapter 1)

Where: Bamboo Grove, past Guangmou, at the Marsh of White Mist shrine. He guards the way into Black Wind Cave and fights in two phases: human with a spear, then a white serpent form.

  • Phase 1: quick spear combos, water slashes (move left, then sidestep the closing thrust) and a spear throw that he recalls, so stay clear of the return path. A heavy attack can knock him out of a combo.
  • Phase 2 (serpent): a wide spin into a long lunge (sidestep as he backs up), double sweeps that leave water rings (back off when he lowers himself) and the Water Crash overhead slam. Dodge Water Crash sideways. Rolling back still gets you hit.
  • Tools: Immobilize, Wandering Wight and Red Tides in phase 2.
  • Reward: Jade Fang, which unlocks the Twin Serpents Staff, and the Serpentscale armor.

Black Wind King (Chapter 1)

Where: Black Wind Cave. You learn Cloud Step when he retreats partway through. Dodge his three-hit spear combos cleanly, run out of the black wind circle when he charges it, and Immobilize him when he goes back to human form. Using your transformation early keeps your real health bar safe while you learn the wind moves.

Black Bear Guai (Chapter 1 final boss)

Where: the top of Bodhi Peak on Black Wind Mountain. Clear the archers on the climb first. Below 50% health the arena fills with fire and his attacks gain burn damage.

  • Charging combo: stay central and dodge into him as each hit lands. Punish after the closing back-slam.
  • Five-hit combo: get distance or Immobilize him before it starts.
  • Fire slam and fire charge (phase 2): roll toward him as the slam lands and stay off the fire vents. The fire charge is five dashes, and the fifth is a melee hit, so stay ready.
  • Invisible leap / jumping stomp: dodge sideways as he lands.
  • Punish windows: after the back-slam, and when he rests at the back of the arena.
  • Prep: the Fireproof Mantle vessel (from the Elder Jinchi secret area) and Body-Cooling Powder make phase 2 much easier. Save Red Tides for phase 2. Beating him unlocks the Wind Bear Staff recipe.

Chapter 2: Yellow Wind Ridge

Chapter 2 is where most players stall, because Tiger Vanguard and the Yellow Wind Sage punish dodge-spamming. You pick up Rock Solid (a parry-style spell) and A Pluck of Many (clones) here. The order depends on which path you take after the rat bosses.

Tiger Vanguard (Chapter 2)

Where: the top of the stairs at Crouching Tiger Temple. He fights bare-handed first, then draws his sword, and that's when the fight turns deadly. Sword combos and his long-range sword wave can take more than half your health.

  • Double punch / double claw: slow and easy to read. Dodge, and be ready for the low spin kick that can follow.
  • Ground pound: he hangs in the air for about a second. Dodge to either side as he drops.
  • Sword phase: stay close and dodge late, just before each swing lands, watching his arms and blade. Rolling early or spamming dodge gets you caught by the later hits. Back off when he winds up the blood tornado or the long-range slash.
  • Approach: open with Immobilize and fast light combos, then pop your transformation and use its heavy hits, then your spirit. Go back to light combos after that, because your own heavies are too slow against him.
  • Reward: the Rock Solid spell.

Stone Vanguard (Chapter 2)

Where: the big open area at Rockrest Flat on Fright Cliff (the Rock Clash Platform). A huge stone creature. Dodge backward from the glowing ground spikes and sideways from the rock volleys, and punish with quick combos after each volley. Beating him unlocks the Galeguard armor (perfect dodges give Focus), which is excellent for the rest of the game.

Fuban (Chapter 2 secret area)

Where: Kingdom of Sahali, opened by finishing the drunk pig's questline and beating the Yellow-Robed Squire. You fight a different Tiger Vanguard there first. Fuban is a giant centipede-like beast. Hit only the front legs, use Immobilize and A Pluck of Many early, dodge sideways from the burrow jumps and backward from the leg swipes. Reward: the Wind Tamer vessel, which makes the Yellow Wind Sage far easier, so do this first.

Yellow Wind Sage (Chapter 2 final boss)

Where: the far end of the Yellow Wind Formation, through the Windseal Gate door. A rat sage with a trident who blows Samadhi Wind and later summons Fuban's head.

  • Vital Stab: a kick that, if it connects, chains into a stab, stomp and kick for huge damage. Dodge the opening kick and the whole thing is cancelled.
  • Trident throw combo: when he jumps to the edge of the arena, move to the opposite side and watch for the final slam.
  • Samadhi Wind: he spins the trident and summons wind that hurts you up close, then chains several wind attacks. Use the Wind Tamer vessel as the wind starts. It stops the phase and stuns him for a big damage window.
  • Summon Fuban: a head rushes at you. Roll through it, and roll late.
  • Punish windows: after his single stomp and single stab. Two charged Smash heavies in a row can stun him.
  • Reward: Samadhi Wind Agate, for the Rat Sage Staff, plus the Ochre armor.

Chapter 3: The New West

Chapter 3 is huge, icy and full of optional bosses. Thunder damage is everywhere, so craft the Loongscale set (from Kang-Jin Star) and carry Shock-Quelling Powder. You get Ring of Fire from the Macaque Chief rematch and Spell Binder from the Treasure Hunter questline.

Kang-Jin Loong (Chapter 3)

Where: Snowhill Path, the temple on the frozen lake at Mirrormere. A flying ice-and-thunder dragon.

  • Double and triple lightning bites: dodge right after each bite, and in the triple, dodge the tail slam from above too.
  • Ice breath: back off as soon as the mouth starts charging.
  • Lightning field (below 50%): keep moving and stay off the marked strike spots.
  • Punish: after the aerial bite thrust it circles low, so Immobilize it and hit the head. After the triple combo, hit the tail while it is on the ground. A Pluck of Many clones add free damage.
  • Reward: Starlit Cloud-Bidden Antler, which unlocks the Kang-Jin Staff.

Captain Wise-Voice (Chapter 3)

Where: the top of the Pagoda Realm prison, at the Mani Wheel shrine. Kill all eight Lantern Wardens in the Pagoda first, or you miss the Auspicious Lantern curio. The room is small, so lock on.

  • Attack the legs until he falls, then put your strongest combo and spells into the head. Cast Immobilize just before he stands up to stretch the window.
  • Electro beam: sidestep the straight line, then rush in.
  • Shockwave stomp: jump just before the ring reaches you.
  • Electrified floor (second half): roll through it and aim for perfect dodges.
  • Aerial dive (~50%): he leaps and lands in a grab. Get distance right away.

Kang-Jin Star (Chapter 3)

Where: Turtle Island on the Bitter Lake. You have to beat her to free Zhu Bajie. In dragon form, focus on dodging. Run in a wide square to avoid her lightning strikes and heal while she changes form. Reward: the Loongscale armor recipe, the best thunder protection for Yellowbrow.

Yin Tiger (Chapter 3, optional)

Where: the back of Zodiac Village, after the Chen Loong and Ruyi Scroll questline. He hits very hard and many players come back later. Use the full opener: A Pluck of Many, Immobilize, Wandering Wight, Cloud Step to reset, then your transformation. Rewards: the Ebon Flow transformation and the Iron-Tough armor (shorter Rock Solid cooldown).

Yellowbrow (Chapter 3 final boss)

Where: Mahavira Hall in New Thunderclap Temple. Three phases. Your gourd and mana refill at the start of each phase, and there is a checkpoint after phase 2. Almost everything is thunder, so wear Loongscale pieces and bring Shock-Quelling Powder.

  • Phase 1: dodge through his mace thrust, then punish from the side (he is stuck in the animation). Jump the floor shockwave. For the long 5-to-8-hit sweeping thunder combo, keep your distance or drop a Cloud Step decoy. When he teleports to the centre for the electric explosion, run to a corner.
  • Phase 2 (empowered Macaque Chief): Zhu Bajie helps and can stun him. Kill the clone that is not busy with Zhu Bajie first. Roll toward the final slash of the spin-slam combo, which is the best punish.
  • Phase 3: his Golden Body makes him immune. Break it with a fully charged heavy or the Wandering Wight spirit, then go all in. Sidestep the falling thunder sword. Below half he vanishes and grabs, so roll backward when he disappears. Wait out his electric shield.
  • Reward: Cosmo Radiance and more, which opens the Spikeshaft Staff recipe, plus the Golden armor set.

Chapter 4: Webbed Hollow and the Yellow Flower

Chapter 4 is poison country: spiders, centipedes and Daoists. Carry Antimiasma Powder at all times. The Chitin Staff line (heals you on charged heavies) is the natural upgrade here.

The Second Sister (Chapter 4)

Where: the courtyard by the Estate of the Zhu shrine in the Village of Lanxi. A fairly easy opener for the chapter. A Pluck of Many, Immobilize, a quick combo, Wandering Wight, then Cloud Step or a transformation when she gets aggressive. Reward: Venomous Hair, which unlocks the Chitin Staff.

Violet Spider (Chapter 4)

Where: next to The Gathering Cave shrine in the Webbed Hollow. Drink a buff, then dodge the opening combo before you commit. Stay out of her webs and keep your health near full, because poison ticks plus a combo can finish you. Spells stagger her well. Reward: Spider Leg, for the Spider Celestial Staff.

The Duskveil (Chapter 4 secret area)

Where: Cloudnest Peak in Purple Cloud Mountain. You reach the area by beating the Venom Daoist twice. Phase 1: quick attacks on the legs, and save your spells. Phase 2: circle the edge of the arena to dodge the knives, avoid the red mist, then spend Immobilize and Wandering Wight. Reward: Weaver's Needle, the vessel that stuns the Hundred-Eyed Daoist Master.

The Scorpionlord (Chapter 4 secret area)

Where: Bounds of Deity's Abode in Purple Cloud Mountain. Break his wine pot to start the fight. This is one of the hardest optional fights in the game: heavy poison and tail swipes with tricky timing. Buff up, carry Antimiasma Powder, and learn the tail timing before you commit. He drops a Sky-Piercing Horn (used for the NG+ Fuban staff) and a piece of the Insect armor.

Hundred-Eyed Daoist Master (Chapter 4 final boss)

Where: the open area past the Court of Illumination shrine, Temple of the Yellow Flower. Fast and hard to pin down. Let him finish his strings, then Immobilize and use heavy attacks.

  • Lightning sword slam: he raises the sword before a lightning area hit. Get distance first.
  • Lightning slices: sweeping lines. Stay far out and move side to side.
  • Poison spray: back away. Use Antimiasma Powder if you're poisoned.
  • Burrow: keep moving and dodge as he comes up under you.
  • Fire hand: sets you alight. Dodge several times to put it out.
  • Golden realm (final quarter): the arena turns gold and he gets much stronger. Weaver's Needle stuns him and stops the switch, so bring it if you did the Duskveil. Wandering Wight into Weaver's Needle into quick attacks is the standard combo.
  • Prep: guides suggest about level 70, the Spider Celestial Staff and upgraded Immobilize and A Pluck of Many.

Chapter 5: Flaming Mountains

Everything burns in Chapter 5. Equip the Fireproof Mantle (it also lets you walk on lava) and carry Body-Cooling Powder. Do the Five Element Carts quest from the Pale-Axe Stalwart: it leads to the Bishui Cave secret area and the Bull King's armor.

Keeper of Flaming Mountains & Yin-Yang Fish (Chapter 5)

Where: The Emerald Hall, Furnace Valley. Phase 1: when the rider lands, clear his summoned adds, then hit him and his black horse. Phase 2 is the Yin-Yang Fish, whose eggs freeze you. Heal to full before you commit, use quick attacks and your spirit, and Weaver's Needle if you have it.

Bishui Golden-Eyed Beast (Chapter 5 secret area)

Where: the end of Bishui Cave, Corridor of Fire and Ice. You open the cave through the Five Element Carts quest. He uses fire and lava, so bring burn resistance (the Fireproof Mantle handles the lava). Spend every spell at the start, then dodge and use quick attacks until Immobilize is back. Guides say it is much easier if you come back during Chapter 6. Rewards: the Bishui Beast Bit (for the Bishui Beast Staff) and the Bull King's armor recipe.

Red Boy & Yaksha King (Chapter 5 final boss)

Where: the Fallen Furnace Crater in the Field of Fire. One boss, two phases. If you die in phase 2, you don't have to beat Red Boy again.

  • Red Boy (phase 1): a fast fire fighter with low health. Spells and charged attacks push him to half fast. At 50% he flies up and throws exploding clones. Tank them with your transformation, or avoid them with the Fireproof Mantle or Cloud Step.
  • Yaksha King (phase 2): long melee combos that end in spike fields. Spikes often explode twice, sometimes with a bigger radius, so dodge, then dodge or back off again.
  • Low sweeps: Pillar Stance lets you hop over his low arm-blade sweeps and counter.
  • Grab at ~40%: heal before it, because it does a lot of damage.
  • Element: the Yaksha King resists fire, so use a non-fire transformation (Umbral Abyss is a common pick).
  • Reward: Kun Steel and Celestial Ribbon, the Yaksha armor, and the Plantain Fan vessel on the chapter results screen.

Chapter 6: Mount Huaguo

Chapter 6 is an open Foothills area you cross on the Somersault Cloud. Beat the four great beasts (Rhino, Deer, Feng-Tail General, Mantis) to collect the Great Sage's armor. Wear all four pieces and visit Water Curtain Cave to get the Jingubang. The Keeper's Shrine before the finale is the point of no return.

Supreme Inspector (Chapter 6)

Where: Verdant Path, the first fight of the chapter. A long fight with constant pressure. Use Immobilize, a strong transformation (Umbral Abyss is popular), Wandering Wight and Weaver's Needle. Skip slow heavies. Dodge the burning wheel and his wing parries. Around 30% he traps you in a ring of fire, so Immobilize him whenever he lands inside it. Reward: Somersault Cloud.

Emerald-Armed Mantis and the four great beasts (Chapter 6)

  • Gold Armored Rhino (Rhino Watch Slope): two phases and fast gap-closing charges. Break its horn with heavy attacks (Umbral Abyss heavies work well), and do it again when the horn regrows. Shock resistance helps. Drops the Gold Suozi Armor.
  • Cloudtreading Deer (Deer Sight Forest): dodge the homing whirlwinds, punish when it lands from jumps, then spend everything in phase 2. Chill resistance helps. Drops the Lotus Silk Cloudtreaders.
  • Feng-Tail General (leaping around the swamp area): a stamina test, not a fight. Fly up, land on it, and hang on through its jumps until you can burn its feelers in one go. You need high burn resistance and health. Drops the Golden Feng-Tail Crown.
  • Emerald-Armed Mantis (near the big tree south of the swamp): appears after the other three. Most of its attacks are short, so dodge backward and land quick hits between combos. Save your spells for its blood form at about 60%. If you die, talk to Zhu Bajie to get back to the arena. Drops the Dian-Cui Loong-Soaring Bracers.

Secret ending: Erlang, the Sacred Divinity

Unlock: beat the four secret-area bosses (Elder Jinchi, Fuban, The Duskveil, Bishui Golden-Eyed Beast) and finish the Chapter 3 Treasure Hunter questline. Then go to the Great Pagoda in the Pagoda Realm (Chapter 3), where the little monk opens Mount Mei. Most guides suggest doing it after you reach Chapter 6, so you have the best gear. It is widely called the hardest fight in the game.

  1. Erlang, the Sacred Divinity: the real test. He blocks and counters, so stop attacking when he guards. Dodge away from his thrust-and-kick to make the kick miss. Sidestep the eye lasers (more of them after 50%). Roll late on his summoned axe. Hold your ground through his summoned swords and dodge each one, especially the last red one. Watch for the spear glow before his grab-stab. Carry thunder resistance, keep your health above about 75%, and use your medicine and mana here, because the later phases lock items.
  2. The Four Heavenly Kings: you become a giant stone ape. Quick attacks build Might, and heavy attacks spend it to heal and deal damage. Turn to stone to block. Mostly scripted.
  3. Erlang Shen: his final giant form. Same ape controls. Dodge his spear beam sideways and keep healing with heavies. A scripted moment at half health powers you up.

Suggested loadout (from guides): Golden Loong Staff (charged Pillar heavies with three or more Focus), Wandering Wight spirit, Immobilize, A Pluck of Many saved for when his guard breaks, Rock Solid if your timing is good, and the Plantain Fan to stun him. Your transformation matters little, because he dispels it. Rewards: the Tri-Point Double-Edged Spear, the Azure Dome transformation and the secret ending.

Frequently asked questions

How many bosses are in Black Myth: Wukong?

Around 90 or more, depending on how you count duos, rematches and multi-phase fights. Game8 says there are over 90 boss battles. This guide's checklist has 97 entries across the prologue, six chapters, four secret areas and Mount Mei.

What is the hardest boss in Black Myth: Wukong?

Erlang, the Sacred Divinity on Mount Mei, the secret ending boss. Among the required bosses, players most often get stuck on Tiger Vanguard, the Yellow Wind Sage, Yellowbrow and the Great Sage's Broken Shell.

What is the best staff in Black Myth: Wukong?

On a first run, the Jingubang (Chapter 6) and the Golden Loong Staff (from the four Loong fights) are the best. In NG+, the Stormflash Loong Staff and the Adept Spine-Shooting Fuban Staff are the top picks.

How do you unlock the secret ending and fight Erlang?

Beat Elder Jinchi (Ch1), Fuban (Ch2), The Duskveil (Ch4) and the Bishui Golden-Eyed Beast (Ch5), and finish the Chapter 3 Treasure Hunter questline. Then visit the Great Pagoda in the Pagoda Realm, where the little monk opens Mount Mei.

Does Black Myth: Wukong have DLC with new bosses?

No. As of November 2026 there is no DLC. The free December 2024 update added Challenge mode boss refights and a boss rush, but no new bosses. Game Science's next project is the separate game Black Myth: Zhong Kui.

How do I beat the Yellow Wind Sage?

Get the Wind Tamer vessel first by beating Fuban in the Kingdom of Sahali secret area, then use it when he starts his Samadhi Wind. Dodge the opening kick of his Vital Stab and punish his single stomps and stabs.

What is the best spirit in Black Myth: Wukong?

The Wandering Wight, from an optional Chapter 1 boss in the Forest of Wolves, is the most recommended. Its headbutt staggers bosses and breaks Yellowbrow's Golden Body. Non-Able is a good fast alternative.

How do you get the Jingubang?

In Chapter 6, beat the Gold Armored Rhino, Cloudtreading Deer, Feng-Tail General and Emerald-Armed Mantis to collect the four Great Sage armor pieces. Wear all four and go to Water Curtain Cave.
