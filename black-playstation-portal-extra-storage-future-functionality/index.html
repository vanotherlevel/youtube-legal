Sony's Black PlayStation Portal Has Extra Storage 'for Future Functionality'

By vAnother Level · Updated 2026-11-12
Home › PlayStation Portal

A streaming handheld with more storage, and Sony won't say why. The PlayStation Portal Midnight Black launches in Japan on December 10 for ¥39,980 (about $250), and Sony says it has extra internal storage "with an eye towards future functionality".

PlayStation Blog Japan, reported by VGC

The Portal only streams games from a PS5, so players never touch its storage. Dataminers estimate the current model has around 6GB used by its Android-based system. Sony says it will share details "as soon as they are finalized".

What could it be?

Our speculation, not confirmed

The obvious guess is something that needs local space, like offline features or cloud-streaming caches. Sony hasn't confirmed anything, so treat any specific feature as a guess until it does.
