Cyberpunk 2 Director Calls GTA 6 the Greatest Artistic Achievement in History

Igor Sarzyński, the creative director of Cyberpunk 2 at CD Projekt Red, called GTA 6 "probably the single greatest artistic achievement in history" in a Bluesky post. He also made clear that Cyberpunk 2 isn't going to try to copy Rockstar's game: the studio is making "something of our own, with a different unique flavor."

The post went viral in GTA 6's first week, partly because of a joke in the middle of it about Cyberpunk 2 being "canceled". It isn't. Here's what he actually said, with the sources.

What Sarzyński said about GTA 6

OFFICIAL: his own Bluesky post, quoted by TheGamer (Nov 22, 2026)
  • On GTA 6's effect on other developers: "GTA 6-induced-open-world-game-dev-depression is real."
  • On the game itself: "Probably the single greatest artistic achievement in history, I mean it."
  • Why: "GTA is impressive in a way all great art is, it expands what is possible, a captivating vision flawlessly executed."

Coming from the person leading the sequel to one of the biggest open-world games of the decade, that's about as strong as praise from a rival studio gets.

Is Cyberpunk 2 canceled? No

In the same post he wrote: "Cyberpunk 2 is canceled, imma go sweep autumn leaves in my backyard now." That line is a joke about how big GTA 6 is, not an announcement. Right after it he added: "Seriously though I think we're doing something of our own, with a different unique flavor."

REPORTED: TheGamer says Sarzyński made clear CD Projekt Red has no intention of rivaling GTA 6 directly.

Neither TheGamer nor OpenCritic gives a new release window or development update for Cyberpunk 2 in this story, so treat any "delay" or "canceled" headlines built on the joke as wrong.

Other developers reacting to GTA 6

REPORTED: TheGamer
  • Michael Douse (Larian Studios' head of publishing) posted that not every game needs to be GTA.
  • Michael Barclay, a Naughty Dog designer, compared GTA 6 to a Bugatti Veyron.
  • Jennifer Kolbe of Rockstar said GTA 6 cost under $1.5 billion, pushing back on the $2 billion rumours. More on the money side on our GTA 6 budget vs sales page.

This follows the launch-day wave of congratulations from other studios, including CD Projekt Red's own Cyberpunk account, which we rounded up on our studios and brands congratulate Rockstar page.

Why it matters

GTA 6 launched on November 19 with a 99 on OpenCritic and Metacritic (see our GTA 6 reviews page). When the director of a rival open-world game says it raised the bar this much, it shows how other studios are looking at it: not as something to copy, but as something to steer away from with their own style.

THEORY (our take): expect more open-world games to lean into what makes them different (first-person, smaller dense cities, RPG systems) rather than chasing GTA 6's scale.
