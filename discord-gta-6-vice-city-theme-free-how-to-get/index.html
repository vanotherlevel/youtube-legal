Discord GTA 6 Vice City Theme Is Free: How to Get It (Until Dec 13)

Yes, the GTA 6 Discord theme is real and it's free right now. Since November 16, 2026, every Discord user can apply a Vice City colour theme, a Vice City app icon and a Vice City Display Name Style, free until December 13 (eligible Nitro members keep it until February 20, 2027).

OFFICIAL: Discord blog, November 16, 2026

What you get

  • Vice City colour theme: recolours the whole Discord app.
  • Vice City app icon: swaps the Discord icon on your desktop or phone.
  • Vice City Display Name Style: changes the font, colour and effects of your display name on your profile.

Discord posted it with "just under 72 hours" to go before GTA 6 launches on November 19. It works on desktop and mobile.

How to apply the GTA 6 theme on Discord

Now until November 22

  1. Open Discord's blog post "Give Discord a Vice City Makeover" (link in sources below).
  2. Tap Apply the Theme. It takes you to Quest Home.
  3. Apply the theme there. It switches on straight away.

After November 22

  • Desktop: User Settings → Appearance for the colour theme and app icon.
  • Mobile: Settings → App Settings for the colour theme and app icon.
  • Display Name Style (both): it shows up the next time you customise your profile.
Quick tip: grab it before November 22 through the Apply the Theme button and you won't have to dig through settings later.

The GTA VI Pre-Load Quest (Orbs)

Discord is also running a Grand Theft Auto VI: Pre-Load Quest, a video quest where eligible users can earn Orbs, Discord's reward currency. It ends November 18, so if you want it, do it today. The same post points fans to the official Rockstar Games Discord server for launch updates.

The leak got most of it right

REPORTED: datamine by @WumpusCentral, covered by GTABoom, Rockstar Intel and Beebom

Back in October, Discord dataminer @WumpusCentral found GTA VI files in a Discord Canary test build under the codename "Hexagon": a Vice City icon, a theme and a custom name font. The items were right. The dates were not: the leak said Nov 17 to Dec 8 (Nitro to Feb 15), but the real campaign started a day earlier and runs longer.

THEORY: the name font

Fans expect the Display Name Style to use Pricedown, the classic GTA logo font. Discord hasn't named the font, so treat that as a guess until you see it on your own profile.

Frequently asked questions

Is the GTA 6 Discord theme free?

Yes. Discord says every user can apply the Vice City theme, app icon and Display Name Style for free until December 13, 2026. Nitro members (excluding Nitro Basic) keep it until February 20, 2027.

How do I get the GTA 6 theme on Discord?

Until November 22, use the Apply the Theme button in Discord's blog post, which opens Quest Home. After November 22, find it in User Settings > Appearance on desktop or Settings > App Settings on mobile.

When does the GTA 6 Discord quest end?

The Grand Theft Auto VI: Pre-Load Quest, which pays Orbs, ends November 18, 2026.

Does the Discord GTA 6 name style use the Pricedown font?

Discord hasn't named the font. Fans expect Pricedown, the classic GTA font, but that is not confirmed.
