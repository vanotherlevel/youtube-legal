Elden Ring: How to Beat Every Boss, Best Weapons & Enemies

This is a complete Elden Ring boss guide for the base game and the Shadow of the Erdtree DLC: every boss with where to find it and how to beat it, the best weapons on the current patch and where to get them, and how to handle the enemies that kill the most players. Story and big optional bosses get full strategy sections; every field, catacomb, cave and evergaol boss is in quick tables by region.

Balance notes assume patch 1.17.2 (October 2026), the newest version as of writing. Version 1.17 (August 2026) was the last balance patch; 1.17.1 and 1.17.2 only fixed bugs.

Five tips that matter in every fight: 1) Explore and come back. If a boss walls you, there is almost always another region open. 2) Upgrade your weapon before your levels: Smithing Stones matter more than Vigor past the early game, and Vigor matters more than anything else (aim for 40 by Altus, 50-60 late). 3) Use Spirit Ashes and NPC summons; they split the boss's attention and are part of the game's design. 4) Elden Ring bosses delay attacks on purpose. Wait for the swing, don't roll on reflex. 5) In the DLC, collect Scadutree Fragments and Revered Spirit Ash; the Scadutree Blessing matters more than your rune level there.

Margit, the Fell Omen

  • Where: the gate in Stormhill before Stormveil Castle, past the Castleward Tunnel grace. Technically skippable through the side path into Stormveil, but almost everyone fights him, and he is the game's first real skill check.
  • Weakness: slash. He strongly resists holy.
  • What kills people: his walking-cane combo has a long pause before the last hit, and his golden daggers come out right after you roll. Count the swings: roll the final hit late. In phase 2 (around half HP) he adds a holy hammer that slams twice and a long sword sweep.
  • Answers: buy Margit's Shackle from Patches (Murkwater Cave) and use it to pin him to the ground for a few seconds of free hits or a safe heal. Spirit Ashes help a lot. Jump attacks with a two-handed weapon stagger him quickly.
  • Punish window: after the hammer's second slam and after the delayed final cane hit. Take two hits, not four.
  • Recommended: reach level 20-25 and a +3 weapon first; Limgrave and the Weeping Peninsula have plenty to do.

Godrick the Grafted

  • Where: the end of Stormveil Castle (Secluded Cell grace). A Shardbearer: you need two Great Runes to enter Leyndell, and Godrick is the usual first.
  • Resistances: he resists magic, fire and lightning, and strongly resists holy. Physical damage is your best bet.
  • Phase 1: axe combos and a wind gust that blows you back. The gust is telegraphed by him stabbing his axe into the floor: roll toward him through the shockwaves, not away.
  • Phase 2 (half HP): he grafts a dragon head onto his arm. It breathes a sweeping fire line. Stay on the side away from the dragon arm and keep hitting his legs.
  • Summon: Nepheli Loux's summon sign is near the boss door if you met her in the castle. Use her; she draws aggro well.
  • Punish: after the double axe slam and after the fire breath ends.

Rennala, Queen of the Full Moon

  • Where: Grand Library at the end of the Academy of Raya Lucaria. You need the Academy Glintstone Key (near Glintstone Dragon Smarag) to enter. Shardbearer.
  • Weakness: physical (standard, slash, pierce). She is nearly immune to magic.
  • Phase 1: she floats in a bubble shield. The students glowing gold are the key: hit those glowing ones and her shield breaks. When she falls, hit her hard. Do this three times.
  • Phase 2: a pure caster. She throws Rennala's Full Moon (a very large, slow orb: sprint sideways), glintstone volleys, and summons spirits (a dragon, a giant troll, soldiers or wolves). Stay close and aggressive: she has little HP and little poise in this phase, so a fast weapon can finish her before the summons matter.
  • Recommended: high physical damage weapon; a fast weapon helps you kill her in short windows.

Starscourge Radahn

  • Where: Wailing Dunes, Caelid. Once the Radahn Festival is running at Redmane Castle (it opens after you have progressed far enough in the story), talk to Jerren and take the waygate behind the plaza.
  • Resistances: resists most damage types and strongly resists holy. Scarlet rot works over his huge HP pool if you can apply it.
  • What kills people: his gravity arrows fired from across the map. Ride Torrent and gallop sideways to dodge them; use the rocks and wreckage for cover.
  • The trick: the arena has many NPC summon signs (Blaidd, Iron Fist Alexander and others). Summon them one after another so Radahn keeps switching targets. Rush in on Torrent while he is busy.
  • Phase 2 (half HP): he flies into the sky and crashes down as a meteor. Ride hard away from where he was the moment the screen goes quiet. He then adds gravity orbs that follow you.
  • Punish: after his big sword slams and his charge.

Rykard, Lord of Blasphemy

  • Where: the end of Volcano Manor, Mt. Gelmir. Shardbearer. One of the two bosses you need to enter the DLC.
  • Key: pick up the Serpent-Hunter spear at the start of the God-Devouring Serpent fight. It deals huge damage to Rykard; its weapon art (Great-Serpent Hunt) fires a long-range lunge. That is the whole fight.
  • Weakness: frost; extremely weak to the Serpent-Hunter. Very strong against fire.
  • Phase 1 (serpent): run forward through the ravine, collecting the spear. Hit the snake's face when it lunges.
  • Phase 2 (Rykard): he summons flaming skulls that chase you (keep moving sideways), a tornado of fire, and sword slams. Lunge with the Serpent-Hunter whenever his head is in range.
  • Stay out of the lava; it burns.

Morgott, the Omen King

  • Where: Leyndell, Royal Capital, at the Elden Throne (Queen's Bedchamber grace). Required Shardbearer.
  • Weakness: slash. Resists holy.
  • Moveset: like Margit, with delays. He throws holy daggers, rains holy spears from the sky (run forward under them), and in phase 2 (half HP) adds a cursed blood sword, a blood pool and a spear thrust.
  • Answers: patience. Roll late. Punish after his combos end, never mid-combo. Margit's Shackle does not work on him.
  • Recommended: Vigor 40+ by here. A spirit ash makes him much more manageable.

Fire Giant

  • Where: Flame Peak, Mountaintops of the Giants. Required.
  • Weakness: slash. Very strong against fire, so drop fire weapons for this one.
  • How to fight: stay on Torrent and attack his legs, then ride out before he stomps or rolls. Fight at his feet; never stand in front where the body slam lands.
  • Phase 2 (half HP): he tears out his eye and adds fire waves, an arm that tracks you and fire breath. Ride away from his big belly flop. He is slow to turn: circle and hit his heels.
  • Punish: when he is down after a slam or roll.

Godskin Duo

  • Where: Dragon Temple in Crumbling Farum Azula. Required.
  • Weakness: slash. They resist fire and holy.
  • Mechanic: the Apostle (thin) and the Noble (fat) share one HP bar. Killing one makes it return after a short time; keep the shared bar falling.
  • Answers: bring a spirit ash or co-op partner to split them. Fight around the pillars. The Noble's ball roll can be outpaced; the Apostle's black flame does damage over time, so keep your distance during the flame tornado.
  • Tip: focus one at a time, but don't let the other get behind you.

Maliketh, the Black Blade

  • Where: Beside the Great Bridge in Crumbling Farum Azula. Required.
  • Phase 1 (Beast Clergyman): slow staff and sword attacks, plus rocks thrown from the air. Hit his legs.
  • Phase 2 (Maliketh): very fast, very aggressive. His black blade attacks inflict Destined Death, which cuts your max HP for a while. Roll toward him through his leaps.
  • Blasphemous Claw: a key item found in Crumbling Farum Azula; it lets you deflect his black blade attacks and stagger him.
  • Answers: heavy, aggressive builds do well, and bleed works. The Bloodhound's Step ash of war is excellent for dodging his combos.

Sir Gideon Ofnir, the All-Knowing

  • Where: Leyndell, Ashen Capital (Erdtree Sanctuary). Required.
  • Moveset: a caster who uses Rock Sling, Black Flame, Ancient Lightning and buffs. He has low poise and HP.
  • Answers: rush him and stay close. Jump attacks stagger him. Roll toward his spells, not away. He is the easiest of the final stretch.

Godfrey, First Elden Lord / Hoarah Loux

  • Where: Elden Throne, Leyndell, Ashen Capital. Required.
  • Resistances: strong against holy, fire and magic. Physical damage is best.
  • Phase 1 (Godfrey): big axe slams and stomps that send shockwaves across the floor. Jump over the shockwaves.
  • Phase 2 (Hoarah Loux, half HP): he throws off his lion and fights like a grappler. His lunging grab is the killer: watch for his arms spreading and roll at the last moment as they close in. His ground stomp needs a jump or a very well-timed roll.
  • Punish: after the earthshaker stomp and after the grab whiffs.

Radagon of the Golden Order / Elden Beast

  • Where: the Elden Throne after Godfrey. Final boss of the base game, two fights back to back.
  • Resistances: both phases extremely resist holy. Leave your holy weapons at home.
  • Radagon: hammer combos, holy shockwaves and teleports. Roll toward him on teleport strikes and punish the recovery.
  • Elden Beast: a huge holy creature in a large arena. It throws Elden Stars (a stream of holy bolts that tracks you), golden rings and a sword sweep. Stay close to its body, keep running toward it and attack its legs and tail; it backs away on purpose.
  • Tips: Vigor 60 is comfortable here. Fighting the Beast with a summon helps because it wanders off the boss.

Malenia, Blade of Miquella

  • Where: Elphael, Brace of the Haligtree (Haligtree Roots). Optional Shardbearer. To reach the Haligtree you need both halves of the Haligtree Secret Medallion (Albus in Village of the Albinaurics, and Commander Niall at Castle Sol), then the Grand Lift of Rold and the Ordina, Liturgical Town puzzle.
  • Status: she is vulnerable to bleed and frost. She resists holy, poison and scarlet rot. Bleed is the most common route because her bleed resistance is relatively low.
  • Lifesteal: she heals when she hits you, even on block. Never block her; roll.
  • Waterfowl Dance: she leaps into the air and pauses. Run away from her while she does the first two slashes, roll through the third, and stay clear of the end. Bloodhound's Step also works.
  • Phase 2 (Goddess of Rot): begins when her first HP bar empties, and she heals to 80%. Scarlet Aeonia (a giant rot flower): roll when her sword lifts, then sprint and roll again. The rot hangs around for a few seconds; when it fades, she is open.
  • Recommended: Vigor 60, the Freezing Pot/bleed weapons, and a spirit ash like Black Knife Tiche or Mimic Tear. Expect many tries.

Mohg, Lord of Blood

  • Where: Mohgwyn Palace. Get there via the Pureblood Knight's Medal (White Mask Varre's quest) or the portal in the Consecrated Snowfield. Optional Shardbearer and one of the two DLC entry bosses.
  • Resistances: extremely resists fire; strongly resists magic, lightning and holy. Physical is best.
  • Nihil (phase 2): at half HP he casts a blood ritual that drains you and heals him. Mix the Purifying Crystal Tear (dropped by Eleonora, Violet Bloody Finger, at the Second Church of Marika in Altus) into your Wondrous Physick and drink it before the ritual. It cancels most of the damage.
  • Moves: trident combos, blood flame pools (don't stand in them), and blood spears that rise from the ground.
  • Punish: after his trident slam; roll toward him through his combos.

Astel, Naturalborn of the Void

Best weapons

Patch 1.17.2

These rankings are for PvE on patch 1.17.2. The famous launch-era monsters (Rivers of Blood, Moonveil, Sword of Night and Flame, Bloodhound's Fang, the Mimic Tear) were all toned down in the 2022 patches, but every one of them is still a strong pick today. The DLC weapons have mostly been buffed since 2024: Rellana's Twin Blades were improved in 1.14 and again in 1.17, Light Greatswords like Milady gained poise damage in 1.13, and 1.14 sped up the first charged heavy attack of most weapon types. The one notable 1.17 nerf was to dual-wielded Rakshasa's Great Katana combos.

The tier list judges a weapon by damage once fully upgraded, how safe its moves are, how good its skill is, and how easy it is to build around. A B-tier weapon you enjoy and have upgraded will always beat an S-tier weapon at +5.

Early-game picks and spirit ashes

  • Melee: Uchigatana (Deathtouched Catacombs), Bloodhound's Fang (Forlorn Hound Evergaol), Reduvia (Nerijus near Murkwater Cave) and Winged Scythe (Tombsward Ruins) are all in Limgrave or the Weeping Peninsula.
  • Early power spike: Moonveil in Gael Tunnel, Caelid, if you are Intelligence. A quick ride into Caelid also gets casters the Meteorite Staff and the Rock Sling sorcery in the Street of Sages Ruins.
  • Spirit ashes: Black Knife Tiche (from Alecto in Ringleader's Evergaol) and the Mimic Tear (Nokron) are the two best for boss fights. Upgrade them with Grave and Ghost Gloveworts from Roderika at Roundtable Hold.
  • DLC: Milady and the Backhand Blade are both near the DLC start; grab them early.

Frequently asked questions

What is the hardest boss in Elden Ring?

Most players name Malenia, Blade of Miquella, in the base game and Promised Consort Radahn in Shadow of the Erdtree. Malenia heals when she hits you and has the Waterfowl Dance; Radahn's second phase is fast and hits hard. Both get much easier with a good spirit ash and high Vigor.

Which bosses are required to finish Elden Ring?

Any two Shardbearers before Leyndell (most players take Godrick and Rennala), then Morgott, the Fire Giant, the Godskin Duo, Maliketh, Sir Gideon Ofnir, Godfrey and Radagon/Elden Beast. Margit can technically be bypassed. Everything else is optional.

How do I start Shadow of the Erdtree?

Beat Mohg, Lord of Blood (Mohgwyn Palace) and Rykard, Lord of Blasphemy (Volcano Manor), then touch the withered arm in Mohgwyn Palace.

What is the best weapon in Elden Ring right now?

On patch 1.17.2, the Blasphemous Blade, Rivers of Blood, Moonveil and Mohgwyn's Sacred Spear are the top base-game picks. In the DLC, Rellana's Twin Blades and Milady are the standouts.

Is Rivers of Blood still good after the nerfs?

Yes. Its Corpse Piler skill was toned down in 2022, but it still builds bleed quickly and remains one of the strongest Dex/Arcane weapons.

How do I beat Malenia's Waterfowl Dance?

Run away from her during the first two slashes, roll through the third, then stay out of range until the combo ends. Bloodhound's Step also works.

What is Scadutree Blessing and how high should it be?

Scadutree Fragments raise your damage and defense in the Realm of Shadow only. Aim for 10+ before mid-game DLC bosses and 18-20 (the max is 20) before Promised Consort Radahn.

How many bosses are in Elden Ring?

The base game has more than 160 boss encounters and Shadow of the Erdtree adds around 40 more, depending on how you count duplicates and duos.
