Fortnite's Free Item Shop Token Has 1 in 137,500 Odds, So We Did the Math

By vAnother Level · Updated 2026-11-12
Home › Fortnite

The token that makes Fortnite's whole Item Shop free is real, and it's rare. Epic confirmed that most of the time, each chest has a 1 in 137,500 chance of giving Pirate Shop Access.

Odds published by Epic Games, reported by VGC
  • Find it and you get two tokens: one to use by December 15, 2026, one for any time in 2027.
  • Activating one makes every V-Bucks item in the shop free for 14 days.
  • Treasure Hours boost the odds 1.5x, and Epic says one recent Treasure Hour hit 2x.
  • Epic says the odds don't change by player: new or returning players get no boost.

Our math

At 1 in 137,500, you'd open about 95,000 chests before your chance of having found one passed 50%. Even during a 2x Treasure Hour, that's still about 47,600 chests. The event, Fortnite Treasure Hunters, runs until December 5.

Odds from Epic Games, reported by VGC; the chest math is ours. Watch more on our YouTube channel: vAnother Level.

Frequently asked questions

What are the odds of getting Pirate Shop Access in Fortnite?

About 1 in 137,500 per chest, boosted 1.5x (sometimes 2x) during Treasure Hours, per Epic.

What does the Pirate Shop Access token do?

It makes every V-Bucks item in the Item Shop free for 14 days.
