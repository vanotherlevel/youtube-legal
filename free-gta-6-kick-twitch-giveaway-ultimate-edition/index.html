Yes, you can win a free copy of GTA 6, but only from a giveaway, and nothing is guaranteed. Kick is giving away 10,000 GTA VI Ultimate Edition codes to people who watch 3 hours of any Kick stream in a day, and Twitch says it will drop Ultimate Edition codes daily from November 19 to the end of November.

OFFICIAL: Kick's own help page; Twitch CEO Dan Clancy on stage at TwitchCon 2026

Neither giveaway is run by Rockstar. Kick says plainly it is "not affiliated with Rockstar Games, Take-Two Interactive or any of their affiliates", and no codes go out before launch day.

How to get a free GTA 6 code on Kick

OFFICIAL: Kick Help Center, "Sunset Reward Case"
  1. Sign in to Kick on the website or app (new users can sign up, finish onboarding and get a free case).
  2. Watch 180 minutes of any live Kick content in one day. It doesn't have to be a GTA stream.
  3. The Sunset Reward Case appears next to your avatar. Open it.
  4. If you win, the code shows up on your Drops and Rewards page once it's issued (not before November 19).
  5. Redeem it on the PlayStation or Xbox account you'll play on.

The watch time resets every day, so you can try again daily. GamingBible reports that the terms set the pool at 10,000 codes, that people who don't win get Kick emotes and badges instead, and that the promotion ends once every code has been found. Kick is also touring a promo truck around Los Angeles and California with a QR code you can scan (Insider Gaming).

PS5 catch: Kick's PS5 codes are tied to the country on your PSN account, and only listed countries qualify (the US, Canada, UK, most of Europe, Australia, Japan, India, Brazil, Mexico and more). Japanese codes expire 170 days after they're issued. Xbox codes are global but work only on Series X|S, not Xbox One.

How Twitch's GTA 6 drops work

OFFICIAL: announced by Twitch CEO Dan Clancy at TwitchCon 2026 (via Shacknews)

Twitch says it is "surprise dropping" GTA 6 Ultimate Edition copies every day from launch (November 19) through November 30. Twitch hasn't said how many codes there are, or the exact rules for who gets one.

REPORTED: details from GamingBible and Notebookcheck, not a Twitch rules page
  • Bling Badge collection: watch 60 minutes of a participating GTA VI stream a day for six days to earn six badges.
  • Heist collection: badges and emotes for buying new or gift subs (three purchases in total).
  • Final Reward: unlocks after you collect all 12 other rewards.
  • Free game codes are reported to drop at random to viewers during the same period. Watching GTA VI category streams with Drops enabled is your best shot.

Link your Twitch account to its Drops page before November 19, so a drop isn't lost because your account wasn't connected.

Is it a scam?

These two are real promotions, run by Kick and Twitch themselves. Copycats will use the hype, though. Rules that keep you safe:

  • A real Kick code only appears on your Drops and Rewards page on kick.com. A real Twitch drop shows up in your Twitch inventory.
  • Nobody legitimate asks for your PSN or Xbox password to "send" a code.
  • "Free GTA 6" links in comments, DMs or on fake 'generator' sites are scams. Ignore them.
  • If you win, redeem it in the PlayStation Store or Microsoft Store yourself.

Should you wait for a free copy?

OUR TAKE

Ten thousand codes against millions of viewers are long odds. If you were going to buy GTA 6 anyway, watching Kick or Twitch while you wait is a free extra chance, not a plan. Frequently asked questions

How do I get GTA 6 for free?

Only through giveaways. Kick gives a daily case to anyone who watches 3 hours of streams (10,000 Ultimate Edition codes in total), and Twitch drops Ultimate Edition codes daily from November 19 to 30. Winning isn't guaranteed.

Is the Kick GTA 6 giveaway real?

Yes. Kick runs it itself and explains it on its official Help Center. It is not affiliated with Rockstar or Take-Two.

When will I get my GTA 6 code from Kick?

Not before November 19, 2026. Codes appear on your Kick Drops and Rewards page after an eligibility check, and can come later than launch day.

Does the free GTA 6 code work on PS5 and Xbox?

Kick offers PS5 codes locked to your PSN country and global Xbox Series X|S codes. Twitch hasn't confirmed platforms yet.
