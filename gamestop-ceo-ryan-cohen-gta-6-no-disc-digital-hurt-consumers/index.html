GameStop CEO: All-Digital Games Like GTA 6 'Hurt Consumers'

GameStop CEO Ryan Cohen says the move to all-digital games has hurt consumers and hurt GameStop. He said it in a Bloomberg interview as GTA 6 launched on November 19, 2026, a game whose physical edition is just a download code in a box, with no disc.

OFFICIAL: Cohen's own words to Bloomberg, relayed by GamesRadar+, VGTimes and Stocktwits

What Ryan Cohen said

Cohen's argument is about ownership. In his words, "When you buy a game, you should be able to sell it, trade it or lend it to a friend." A code you redeem on your own account can't be resold, traded in or lent out once it's used, which is exactly what GameStop's used-games business runs on.

  • On going digital: "Unquestionably, the decision to move to all digital has hurt consumers, and it's also hurt GameStop."
  • On GTA 6 sales at GameStop: asked about presales, he said it's "a code in a box" and added that GameStop is still selling a lot of it.
  • Earlier post (Nov 17): Cohen wrote that players should be able to get GTA on a disc, in a case, on their shelf (VGTimes).
  • Lobbying: he met US Congressman Ritchie Torres, who backs the Power to the Players Act, a proposed bill about keeping access to games you've bought (VGTimes).
  • Publishers: Take-Two declined to comment, per launch-day coverage.
REPORTED: the Torres meeting and the Nov 17 post come from VGTimes' summary; Cohen did not name GTA 6 directly in the Bloomberg quote

Why GTA 6 is the perfect example

Rockstar confirmed that GTA 6's boxed version has no disc. Inside is a printed code that you redeem on PS5 or Xbox Series X|S, and the game downloads like a digital purchase. So every "physical" copy GameStop sells this week still ends up as a digital licence. That's the tension in Cohen's comments: GTA 6 is filling his stores, but no copy can come back as a used trade-in. We covered the code-in-a-box scares, like the viral flashlight trick and the missing codes, in our box code page.

Is a GTA 6 disc coming?

There's no announcement of a disc version. Rockstar and Take-Two haven't said anything about one, and Take-Two declined to comment on Cohen's remarks. If that changes, we'll update this page. For what's in each edition today, see Standard vs Ultimate, and for the money side of launch, our pre-orders page. All launch news is on the GTA 6 news hub.

Our take

Cohen is right that a code can't be resold, and he obviously has a business reason to say it. By his own account, GameStop is still selling plenty of GTA 6 boxes. Either way, if you buy the box, redeem the code the day you open it, because there's no disc to fall back on.

Frequently asked questions

What did GameStop's CEO say about GTA 6?

Ryan Cohen told Bloomberg that going all-digital has hurt consumers and GameStop, because you can't sell, trade or lend a digital game. He also said GameStop is selling a lot of GTA 6's code-in-a-box edition.

Does GTA 6 have a disc?

No. Rockstar confirmed the physical edition is a download code in a box, with no disc.

Can you trade in GTA 6 at GameStop?

A redeemed code is tied to your account, so it can't be traded in like a disc. GameStop hasn't announced any special GTA 6 trade-in scheme.

Will GTA 6 get a disc release later?

Nothing has been announced. Take-Two declined to comment on Cohen's remarks.
