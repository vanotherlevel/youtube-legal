Is Genigods: Nezha the Next Black Myth: Wukong? Everything Confirmed So Far

By vAnother Level · Updated 2026-11-13 · Watch on YouTube
Home › Genigods: Nezha

Two years ago this studio was making phone games. Now Genigods Lab is building Genigods: Nezha, a single-player action game the press is already calling China's God of War, and a real shot at being the next Black Myth: Wukong.

Confirmed by Genigods Lab in interviews and trailers (sources at the bottom)

The numbers

  • About 100 people, a total cost of $20 million, in development since 2023.
  • Unreal Engine 5.7, single player, and the studio says it is not a soulslike.
  • 60+ enemy types, 10 zones, 3 endings, about 25 hours of main story.
  • Target of 60 FPS, on the base PS5 as well.
  • Coming to PS5, Xbox Series and PC (Steam and Epic) in 2028.

The combat

Two stances: on the ground you fight with the fire-tipped spear, in the air you ride the wind fire wheels, and you can switch mid-fight. Collect Nezha's classic artifacts and you unlock an eight-armed battle form. The studio places its difficulty between Black Myth: Wukong and God of War.

Giants you climb

Some bosses are the size of mountains. You climb them and fight on their bodies, and in one chapter you climb the Sun Tree and shoot down nine suns with a bow, straight out of the old Chinese myth.

Our call

Our opinion

The risks are real: 2028 is more than a year away, $20 million is small for mountain-sized bosses, and it's the team's first console game. But since January there's been a playable Gamescom demo, a second trailer and an Xbox version. We think it ships.

Frequently asked questions

When does Genigods: Nezha come out?

The release window is 2028, on PS5, Xbox Series and PC.

Is Genigods: Nezha a soulslike?

No. The creative director places its complexity between Black Myth: Wukong and God of War, with a modern control mode and a harder mode planned.

Who is making Genigods: Nezha?

Genigods Lab, a Chinese studio known for mobile games, with about 100 people and a $20 million budget.
