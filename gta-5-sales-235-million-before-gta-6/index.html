GTA 5 Has Now Sold Nearly 235 Million Copies, 10 Days Before GTA 6

By vAnother Level · Updated 2026-11-09
Home › GTA 6 Guides

Grand Theft Auto 5 has now sold nearly 235 million copies, and its sequel isn't even out yet. Take-Two's latest earnings put GTA 5 at nearly 235 million units sold-in as of September 30, 2026, up from just over 230 million in August. That's roughly 5 million more copies in a single quarter, for a game that first came out in 2013.

Figures from Take-Two Interactive's latest earnings report

'Grand Theft Auto 5' has now sold over 235M copies. 'Grand Theft Auto 6' releases next week

The numbers

  • GTA 5: nearly 235 million units sold-in, the best-selling game Take-Two has ever made.
  • The Grand Theft Auto series: nearly 480 million units.
  • GTA 6 pre-orders: Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick says they are tracking far above the company's initial expectations.
  • GTA 6 launch: November 19, 2026, on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.

Then vs now

In 2013, GTA 5 made headlines for earning $1 billion in three days, which Guinness World Records listed as the fastest any entertainment product had ever done it. It then came out again on PS4 and Xbox One in 2014, again on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S in 2022, and kept selling every year in between. Thirteen years later it is still adding millions of copies a quarter, right up to the week its sequel arrives.
