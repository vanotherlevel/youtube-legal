GTA 6's 100% completion has 35 requirements in three groups: Story & Tasks (12), Exploration & Activities (20) and Discovery (3). You can see your progress under Progress > Stats > 100% Completion, but that tracker only opens after you finish the story. Reaching 100% unlocks the A Long Road trophy/achievement.

The good news: most requirements are "X of Y", not "all of them". You need 100 of 125 Cocaine Flamingos, 300 of 397 stunts and 70 of 80 challenges, for example. PowerPyx estimates the checklist is closer to 60% of everything in the game. The full list below comes from two guide sites that played early copies, and both list the same 35 numbers.

Where to see your 100% progress

REPORTED: PowerPyx 100% Completion guide + GTABase interactive checklist (launch week, early-access copies)
  • Open the pause menu, go to Progress > Stats > 100% Completion.
  • The page only becomes viewable after the main story is done, but everything you do during the story still counts.
  • Most requirements also have their own counters elsewhere in the Progress menu, and challenges have their own page under Progress > Challenges.
  • Reward: the A Long Road trophy/achievement. Neither guide lists a separate in-game item reward.
Missables: PowerPyx says nothing is missable for 100% and everything can be finished in free roam after the story. GTABase's page contradicts itself on this (it also says a handful are missable during the story). Our advice: keep a manual save in each chapter, because GTA 6 has no mission replay. The 80 challenges: what you need

REPORTED: PowerPyx All Challenges guide

There are 80 challenges in 8 categories of 10: Combat, Criminal, Exploration, Fitness, Shopping, Sports, Survival and Vehicle. All are available from the start. Any 50 unlock the One Last Thing trophy; any 70 tick the 100% line, so you can skip the 10 hardest.

CategoryExample (PowerPyx)
CombatSpeed Kills: kill 3 enemies in under 30 seconds
CriminalMugging: rob 5 pedestrians
ExplorationEncyclopedia: discover 100 animals, vehicles and weapons
FitnessPro Swimmer: swim 3 miles in a day
ShoppingNest Egg: bank $1,500,000 in your BuckMe checking account
SportsBasketball Rookie: score at 3 different hoops in a day
SurvivalBig Fish: catch a fish of 20 lb or more
VehicleTop Speed: hit 175 mph in a car, truck or motorcycle

The ones most likely to be skipped are "in a day", "consecutive days" or "without dying" challenges, plus strict timers like Tequesta Swim (under 15 minutes) and Waning Sands Run (under 13 minutes, no damage).

Fastest route to 100%

OUR PLAN (based on the reported list)
  1. Do optional missions as soon as they appear; they unlock most of the robbery lines.
  2. Scan every car, poster and location with WAiNK and study every animal you see from day one, so the Discovery lines fill themselves.
  3. Start Kenton (Chapter 1) and Bret (after "Coffee Break") early, both are long questlines.
  4. After Chapter 4's vault mission, clear the 8 large banks; that also covers most of the marked-bills total.
  5. Finish the story to open the tracker, then sweep the full-count lines (shipwrecks, ranges, drones, gang ops).
  6. Do challenges last and skip the 10 hardest.

Frequently asked questions

Where is the 100% completion tracker in GTA 6?

Progress > Stats > 100% Completion in the pause menu. It only opens after you finish the story, but earlier progress counts.

How many requirements does GTA 6 100% completion have?

35, reported by PowerPyx and GTABase: 12 Story & Tasks, 20 Exploration & Activities and 3 Discovery.

What do you get for 100% in GTA 6?

The A Long Road trophy/achievement. The guides don't list another reward.

Is anything missable for 100% in GTA 6?

PowerPyx says no, everything can be done in free roam after the story. Still keep chapter saves, since there's no mission replay.

How many challenges do you need for 100%?

70 of the 80 challenges. 50 unlock the One Last Thing trophy.
