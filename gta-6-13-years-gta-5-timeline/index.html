4,811 Days: Everything That Happened Between GTA 5 and GTA 6

By vAnother Level · Updated 2026-11-11
Home › GTA 6 Guides

GTA 5 came out on September 17, 2013. GTA 6 comes out on November 19, 2026. That's 4,811 days. Here's what Rockstar did with them, on one timeline.

Release and delay dates announced by Rockstar Games
  • Sep 17, 2013: GTA 5 launches on PS3 and Xbox 360.
  • Nov 18, 2014: GTA 5 again, on PS4 and Xbox One.
  • Apr 14, 2015: GTA 5 again, on PC.
  • Mar 15, 2022: GTA 5 again, on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Three console generations.
  • December 2023: GTA 6 Trailer 1, with a 2025 release window.
  • May 2, 2025: first delay, to May 26, 2026.
  • November 6, 2025: second delay, to November 19, 2026.
  • Nov 19, 2026: GTA 6 launches on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.

Meanwhile GTA 5 just kept selling: Take-Two says it's at nearly 235 million copies (our story). And yes, a kid born on GTA 5's launch day is now 13.

Dates from Rockstar Games and Take-Two Interactive. Timeline by vAnother Level. Watch more on our YouTube channel: vAnother Level.
