GTA 6 at 15 vs 30 vs 60 FPS: See the Difference Side by Side

By vAnother Level · Updated 2026-11-14
Home › GTA 6 Guides

GTA 6 runs at 30 FPS on every console, PS5 Pro included. Rockstar confirmed it on November 10. Since then, clips claiming to show "GTA 6 at 30 FPS" have been everywhere, and some of them are really slowed down to 15 FPS, which looks far worse than the real thing.

So we put the same GTA 6 drive side by side at 15, 30 and 60 FPS. Every panel plays at the same time, so you can see exactly how much smoother each step is.

What you're looking at

15 FPS (left): every other frame is dropped. This is what the fake "30 FPS" clips actually look like. 30 FPS (middle): the real footage, untouched, and the frame rate GTA 6 targets on PS5, PS5 Pro and Xbox Series X|S. 60 FPS (right): GTA 6 isn't confirmed for 60 FPS on console, and Rockstar's footage is 30 FPS, so this panel is simulated with motion smoothing, just to show the difference.

Is 30 FPS bad?

Not really. 30 FPS is clearly smoother than 15, and Rockstar's past games (Red Dead Redemption 2, GTA 5 at launch) played at 30 on consoles. 60 FPS is smoother still, especially when you turn the camera fast. See the full story in GTA 6 is 30 FPS even on PS5 Pro, and more in our GTA 6 hub.
