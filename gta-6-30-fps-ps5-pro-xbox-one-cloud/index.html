GTA 6 Is 30 FPS Even on PS5 Pro, But It's Playable on Xbox One Day One

By vAnother Level · Updated 2026-11-10
Home › GTA 6 Guides
On this page
  1. 30 FPS on every console
  2. GTA 6 on Xbox One (yes, really)
  3. Then vs now

Here's the weirdest spec sheet of the year. Rockstar has confirmed that GTA 6 targets 30 frames per second on every console, including the $700 PS5 Pro. And on the same day, Xbox announced that GTA 6 will be playable on a 2013 Xbox One at launch, streamed through Xbox Cloud Gaming.

Confirmed by Rockstar Games (Newswire) and Xbox Wire, November 10, 2026

30 FPS on every console

  • PS5, PS5 Pro, Xbox Series X and Series S all target 30 FPS, per Rockstar's "Pre-load Grand Theft Auto VI now" Newswire post.
  • No 60 FPS or 40 FPS mode has been announced.
  • PS5 Pro extras: sharper image through PSSR upscaling and ray-traced shadows that only the Pro gets.
  • That means the 120Hz flag dataminers found in the preload (our breakdown) is not a 120 FPS mode, at least not at launch.

GTA 6 on Xbox One (yes, really)

  • There's no native Xbox One version, but you can stream it through Xbox Cloud Gaming from 1 AM ET on November 19.
  • Cloud streams run at Xbox Series S settings.
  • You still have to buy the game. Xbox says cloud playtime can be bought without Game Pass from November 11 in supported markets.
  • Xbox CEO Asha Sharma called it the first step in a new 10-year partnership with Take-Two.

Then vs now

In 2013, GTA 5 launched on PS3 and Xbox 360 aiming for 30 FPS and often dipping below it. Its 2022 PS5 and Xbox Series version finally added a 60 FPS performance mode. Thirteen years later, GTA 6 is back at 30, but your old Xbox One gets to join the party.

Frequently asked questions

Is GTA 6 60 FPS on PS5 Pro?

No. Rockstar says GTA 6 targets 30 FPS on all consoles, including PS5 Pro. The Pro adds PSSR and ray-traced shadows.

Can you play GTA 6 on Xbox One?

Only through Xbox Cloud Gaming. There is no native Xbox One version, but streaming starts at 1 AM ET on November 19.

Do you need Game Pass to stream GTA 6?

You need to buy the game. Xbox says cloud playtime can be bought without Game Pass from November 11 in supported markets.
