GTA 6 has more than 20 side activities, from fishing, hunting, golf tournaments and street races to full video games you can play inside the game. The review copy also shows a zombie wave-survival shooter Jason can play on his TV, a top-down sci-fi shooter called Time Jackers: Rewind and a lightgun bar game called Bang For Your Buck.

This page lists every activity seen so far and where each one comes from: Rockstar's own footage, outlets that played the review copy, or launch-stream gameplay. GTA 6 unlocks on November 19 (release time in your region), and we'll add locations and rewards as we play.

Labels: OFFICIAL = Rockstar's own footage or words. REPORTED = outlets that played the review copy or Rockstar's press previews. SEEN = launch-stream gameplay we watched, with the streamer credited. OUR TAKE / THEORY = our read.

The video games inside GTA 6

This is the surprise of the review copy. Rockstar built several full video games you can play inside GTA 6, and Creative Bloq's reviewer says some of them "feel more complete than some real games" he has played.

Zombie wave-survival shooter (on Jason's TV)

SEEN: launch-stream gameplay by Dalek, Nov 18 (we watched it at full size)

In Dalek's stream, Jason sits down at his TV and plays a top-down zombie wave-survival game set in a neon city street at night. Between waves you choose a perk from three cards: Auto Shotgun (Chaos), Trigger Happy (Combat) and Blood Money (Economy, more XP from kills). There are also on-screen objectives such as collecting gears. We don't know the game's name or whether it gives any reward in GTA 6 yet; we'll add both once we've played it.

Time Jackers: Rewind

REPORTED: Creative Bloq review (Ian Dean, 5/5, Nov 18), played on the PS5 review copy

A top-down sci-fi shooter, per Creative Bloq. Where you play it (a console at home or an arcade cabinet) wasn't stated.

Bang For Your Buck

REPORTED: Creative Bloq review (Ian Dean, 5/5, Nov 18), played on the PS5 review copy

A lightgun, score-chasing bar game, so look for it in Leonida's bars. Same idea as the arcade cabinets in older GTA games, but Creative Bloq says it's a full game.

Tenebrae Lux

REPORTED: Creative Bloq review (Ian Dean, 5/5, Nov 18), played on the PS5 review copy

Rockstar's satire of moody indie walking sims: black-and-white, minimal and full of overwritten prose, as Creative Bloq describes it.

Sports and games

Golf and mini golf

REPORTED: Creative Bloq review (Ian Dean, 5/5, Nov 18), played on the PS5 review copy

Creative Bloq lists golf tournaments among the side activities, so full golf is back after GTA 5. Mini golf was confirmed earlier through Game Informer, and a Rockstar press shot shows Cal Hampton holding a putter on what looks like a gator-themed course (GamesRadar+).

Pool, basketball and wrestling

OFFICIAL footage / REPORTED: Rockstar's GTA 6 Extended Look and Game Informer's preview, as rounded up by GamesRadar+ (Emma-Jane Betts, updated Nov 13)
  • Pool: Creative Bloq relaxed with "a game of pool and a Patriot beer". Billiards was also confirmed via Game Informer.
  • Basketball: Rockstar's Extended Look shows Jason shooting hoops outside his beachfront safehouse.
  • Backyard wrestling: the Extended Look shows Jason getting thrown around a backyard ring.
  • Street fights: Creative Bloq says if you "look at someone too long" they'll demand a fight.

Gym and shooting range

OFFICIAL footage / REPORTED: Rockstar's GTA 6 Extended Look and Game Informer's preview, as rounded up by GamesRadar+ (Emma-Jane Betts, updated Nov 13)

Lucia lifts weights with button prompts in the Extended Look, and what you eat changes how Jason and Lucia look, so the gym matters. The shooting range has targets popping up from behind cover, a good place to try guns before a job (our gun system guide explains how the new weapon wheel and carry limit work).

Racing, stunts and driving

REPORTED: Creative Bloq review (Ian Dean, 5/5, Nov 18), played on the PS5 review copy
  • Street races are back, and Creative Bloq says drivers on the motorway will lean out of their car and challenge you to a race as a random event.
  • Track racing: the Extended Look shows laps on a circuit against other cars. Dirt bike races are shown too (GamesRadar+).
  • Stunts are listed in the review as optional content.
  • "The State's sports and races" can be taken on as you explore.

Outdoors: fishing, hunting and treasure

REPORTED: Creative Bloq review (Ian Dean, 5/5, Nov 18), played on the PS5 review copy
  • Fishing: "chilled fishing" trips plus fishing challenges, per Creative Bloq. Jason fishes from a yacht in the trailers, and GamesRadar+ notes freshwater fishing inland.
  • Hunting: Creative Bloq hunted deer through Jason's rifle scope and went to "hunt a swamp thing". Mount Kalaga's forests and the Grassrivers swamps look like the hunting grounds.
  • Deep-sea treasure hunts: a new one for the series, listed in the review. Scuba diving with manta rays is in the Extended Look.
  • Sailing: "you can go sailing", per the review.

Water and air

OFFICIAL footage / REPORTED: Rockstar's GTA 6 Extended Look and Game Informer's preview, as rounded up by GamesRadar+ (Emma-Jane Betts, updated Nov 13)

The Extended Look shows airboating through crocodile water, jet skis, kayaking in the ocean and Lucia base jumping off a tower before opening her parachute. Our map regions guide shows where each kind of water is.

Money activities

REPORTED: Creative Bloq review (Ian Dean, 5/5, Nov 18), played on the PS5 review copy

Creative Bloq's list includes crypto rug pulls (details unknown yet) and hidden collectable cards that you can sell. Robbing an armoured car and following it to the thieves' hideout is also something the reviewer did as a random event. For the fastest cash, see our money guide.

Downtime: TV, clubs and the zoo

OFFICIAL footage / REPORTED: Rockstar's GTA 6 Extended Look and Game Informer's preview, as rounded up by GamesRadar+ (Emma-Jane Betts, updated Nov 13)
  • TV: in-game shows and ads, including a fast-food parody advert and a nature documentary in the Extended Look.
  • Radio and talk shows: 80s FM hits, dance stations and a shock-jock talk show, per Creative Bloq.
  • Clubs, strip clubs and bars: a GTA staple. Drinks can give a stat bonus, and bar conversations tease secrets about the world (Creative Bloq).
  • Zoo: confirmed via Game Informer; how visits work isn't known yet.

Side missions vs activities

Activities are things you can do any time. Side missions (strangers, odd jobs, Randy's car list) are separate and are covered in our every side quest guide. Creative Bloq notes there's "no Ubisoft-style checklist": many side stories start from posters, flyers and random people you add to your phone contacts. Some activities may count toward trophies, see the trophy list.

OUR TAKE: Try the in-game video games early. If Rockstar follows its own history, there may be a trophy or reward tied to a high score, but nothing like that has been reported yet.

Frequently asked questions

What minigames are in GTA 6?

Reported so far: golf tournaments, mini golf, pool, basketball, backyard wrestling, the gym, shooting ranges, street and track races, fishing, hunting, deep-sea treasure hunts and several playable video games inside the game.

Can you play video games inside GTA 6?

Yes. Creative Bloq's review names Time Jackers: Rewind (a top-down sci-fi shooter), Bang For Your Buck (a lightgun bar game) and Tenebrae Lux. Launch-stream gameplay also shows Jason playing a zombie wave-survival game on his TV.

Is there golf in GTA 6?

Yes. Creative Bloq lists golf tournaments in its review, and mini golf was confirmed earlier via Game Informer.

Is there fishing and hunting in GTA 6?

Yes. The review mentions fishing trips, fishing challenges and hunting, including deer in the forests.
