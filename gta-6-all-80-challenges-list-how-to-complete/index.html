GTA 6 has 80 challenges, 10 in each of 8 categories: Combat, Criminal, Exploration, Fitness, Shopping, Sports, Survival and Vehicle. All of them are open from the start, any 50 unlock the One Last Thing trophy, and any 70 count for the 100% Completion checklist, so you can skip the 10 hardest. The full list with every requirement is below.

REPORTED: list and requirements from PowerPyx's All Challenges guide (Nov 18), cross-checked against AlcastHQ and Power Up Gaming, which also count 80. Rockstar hasn't published an official list
Where to see them in game: Progress > Challenges. Each one gets a Completed checkmark when it's done. Nothing on the list is missable.

Which 10 challenges to skip

OUR TAKE: these are our picks for the 10 skips, based on time and luck. Rockstar doesn't mark any as optional

You only need 70, so pick the 10 that cost you the most time. These are the strongest candidates because they need a huge count, a strict timer or pure luck:

  1. Basketball Pro: all 100 hoops in Leonida.
  2. Underwater Treasures: 85 treasures.
  3. Helipad Flyovers: 41 helipads at 100 feet or lower in under 10 minutes.
  4. Road Trip: every city and town in one day, out of the car only to refuel.
  5. Double Hole In One: two aces in a row.
  6. 8-Ball Break: sink the 8-ball on the break.
  7. Pro Angler: every fish species.
  8. Strength Regime or Cardio Regime: both characters at Level 4 for 5 days straight.
  9. Rally Winner: 3 race wins in a row at 3 tracks in one day, no restarts.
  10. Train Landing: a plane on a moving train.

If one of these happens naturally (you might catch every fish while chasing Big Fish and Angler), keep it and skip a different one.

Tips to finish challenges faster

  • Stack "in one day" challenges. Several need things done within one in-game day (Stick Ups, Mugging Spree, Grocery Shopper, Foot Travel, Pro Cyclist, Pro Swimmer, Marksman). Plan a day around two or three of them.
  • Save before the no-death ones. Many Criminal and timed challenges fail if you die or get arrested. Use a manual save first.
  • Let shopping happen. Most Shopping challenges complete while you upgrade guns, cars and clothes anyway.
  • Do the story first for money. Nest Egg, Serious Modder and Heavily Armed are much easier once story payouts come in.
  • Check Progress > Challenges often. Some tick off without you noticing, and the menu shows what's left.

Frequently asked questions

How many challenges are in GTA 6?

80, split into 8 categories of 10: Combat, Criminal, Exploration, Fitness, Shopping, Sports, Survival and Vehicle (per PowerPyx and other guide sites).

Do you need all 80 challenges for 100% in GTA 6?

No. Any 70 of the 80 count for the 100% Completion checklist, so you can skip 10.

How many challenges for the One Last Thing trophy?

Any 50 challenges unlock One Last Thing.

Where do I see my challenges in GTA 6?

In the pause menu under Progress > Challenges. Completed ones get a checkmark.

Are any GTA 6 challenges missable?

No. All 80 are available from the start and none are reported as missable.
