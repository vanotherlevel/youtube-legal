43 Soldiers Re-Enlisted in the Army for a GTA 6 Four-Day Pass

43 US Army soldiers re-enlisted for two to six more years and got a four-day pass to play GTA 6 at launch. They're all from the 9th Brigade Engineer Battalion at Fort Stewart, Georgia, the only unit that ran the offer, and 43 of its 130 eligible soldiers took it before the November 14 deadline.

The story first broke in August, when only 20 soldiers had signed. Task & Purpose, which first reported it, published the final number on November 18, the day before GTA 6 came out.

OFFICIAL: the 43-soldier count and the offer come from the Army, via Task & Purpose (Patty Nieberg, Nov 18, 2026) and Fox News. Quotes are from soldiers interviewed by Task & Purpose.

Who took the deal

Task & Purpose spoke to soldiers in the battalion. The best-known is Sgt. Jamire Smith, a combat engineer who had planned to leave the Army. He re-enlisted for six more years, and because the Army wants to keep his skills on heavy breaching vehicles, he also qualified for a bonus of about $60,000.

His plan for the four days is simple. "I'm all about the heist," Smith told Task & Purpose, adding that he's "all campaign". He reckons he has a good 24 hours to put into it.

Not everyone signed because of the game. Staff Sgt. Floyd Maxwell, a network systems specialist with four years in, re-enlisted for three more and said the real reason was his late father. The Army's line is that the pass was a bonus on top of reasons soldiers already had, not the thing that made them stay.

Whose idea was it?

The pitch came from Staff Sgt. Brittany Wolfer, the battalion's new career counselor, after the unit had gone about eight months without one. She told Task & Purpose she went to basic training the week GTA 5 came out, and called the GTA 6 pass an "eye grabber". She doesn't get the four days off herself.

The Army's senior career counselor, Sgt. Maj. Celeste R. Spencer, had floated the same idea in a TikTok video, and the battalion commander signed off on it. The 3rd Infantry Division's spokesperson said in August the goal was to get soldiers excited to re-enlist. The Army now says the offer "sparked positive conversations" among soldiers.

Why the Army story fits GTA 6

There's a neat link to the game itself. Rockstar's own character page says Jason Duval spent a short time in the Army before drifting into crime in the Leonida Keys. Fans think a "Marauders" tattoo points to a Ranger unit, but that part is fan theory, not Rockstar.

THEORY: Jason's exact unit has not been confirmed by Rockstar.

It's also one more sign of how big launch week is. Fox News, citing Sensor Tower, puts pre-orders at more than 17 million copies and about $1.65 billion by Wednesday (REPORTED), up again from the numbers on our pre-orders page. Workers, students and now soldiers are clearing their calendars, as we covered in everyone taking GTA 6 launch day off.

Frequently asked questions

Did the US Army really give soldiers time off to play GTA 6?

Yes, one battalion did. The 9th Brigade Engineer Battalion at Fort Stewart, Georgia offered a four-day pass for GTA 6's launch to soldiers who re-enlisted between August 1 and November 14, 2026. The Army confirmed it.

How many soldiers re-enlisted for GTA 6?

43 of the battalion's 130 eligible soldiers took the pass, up from 20 in mid-August, according to Army figures reported by Task & Purpose and Fox News.

How long did they sign up for?

Between two and six more years. Sgt. Jamire Smith signed for six and also qualified for a bonus of about $60,000 for his heavy breaching vehicle skills.

Is the whole Army offering GTA 6 leave?

No. Officials said the offer applied only to this one battalion, and its deadline passed on November 14.
