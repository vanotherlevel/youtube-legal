GTA 6's Australia and New Zealand Version Is Missing One File, and It's Only 704 KB

By vAnother Level · Updated 2026-11-12
Home › GTA 6 Guides

The GTA 6 'censored' file is smaller than a photo on your phone. Dataminer TexFunz2 listed the PS5 preload's 32 PlayGo chunks and found one, core_restofworld001, missing from the Australia and New Zealand version.

Datamine by @TexFunz2, reported by VGC. Not confirmed by Rockstar

"core_restofworld001" is confirmed missing from #GTAVI Australia & New Zealand version.

— @TexFunz2 2026-11-12

The chunk is about 704 KB. A bigger one, core_restofworld (about 1.28 GB), is what TexFunz2 suggested holds content cut from some regional versions, likely Japan's. Nobody outside Rockstar knows what either contains.

Then vs now

Australia has a long history with GTA ratings edits: GTA 3 (2001) and GTA: San Andreas (2004) both had content changed for the Australian release. This time, whatever's missing fits in under a megabyte.
