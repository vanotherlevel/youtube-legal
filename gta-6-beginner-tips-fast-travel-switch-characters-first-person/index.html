The most important GTA 6 beginner tip: you have no bank account until the mission Buck Me, so any cash you carry is lost if you die or get arrested. Hide it under the loose floorboards in Jason's bedroom. After Buck Me you also unlock RydeMe, the taxi app that works as GTA 6's fast travel.

This page collects every early-game answer reported by VGC, whose Jordan Middler played the full review copy and published guides on November 18: fast travel, when you can switch between Jason and Lucia, whether there's a first-person mode, how to change hair, and six things to know before your first hours in Leonida. 1. Save your cash (no bank until Buck Me)

REPORTED: VGC (Jordan Middler), who played the review copy, Nov 18

Early on, GTA 6 is cash only. VGC says bank accounts don't exist for you until the mission Buck Me. Until then, everything you carry is at risk: die or get arrested and you lose the cash on you.

  • Go to Jason's bedroom and check the loose floorboards. It's a stash spot for money, and you'll also find pictures about Jason's backstory there.
  • Drop your cash off often, especially before a risky job or a police chase.
  • After Buck Me, the bank account also unlocks RydeMe fast travel (next section).

Money matters a lot in this game. Rockstar has said some story progress depends on having enough cash, which we covered in GTA 6 missions and money gates, and the best ways to grow it are in every purchase that makes you richer.

2. How fast travel works in GTA 6 (RydeMe)

REPORTED: VGC (Jordan Middler), who played the review copy, Nov 18

Yes, GTA 6 has fast travel, and it's an app. VGC describes RydeMe as the game's version of Uber. It unlocks during Buck Me, because it needs the bank account you get there.

  1. Press up on the D-pad to open your phone.
  2. Open RydeMe.
  3. Pick a destination: your current safehouse, your map marker, or New Destination (opens the map so you can choose a spot).
  4. Wait for the car. If it doesn't stop right in front of you, check the minimap for it. If a taxi can't reach where you're standing, the ride is cancelled, so walk to a road.
  5. Choose how to ride: sit through the trip in real time, pay extra to speed it up, or skip the ride for an extra $100 on top of the fare.

VGC doesn't list the base fare or the speed-up price, so we don't either. Earlier previews also mentioned buses, trains and shared rides, but VGC's fast travel guide only covers RydeMe, so treat the rest as unconfirmed until we test it.

3. How to switch between Jason and Lucia

REPORTED: VGC (Jordan Middler), who played the review copy, Nov 18

Press right on the D-pad to open the swap menu, but only once both characters are free in the open world. That takes a while. Here is the order VGC reports:

4. Does GTA 6 have first-person mode?

REPORTED: VGC (Jordan Middler), who played the review copy, Nov 18

Not a full first-person mode like GTA 5 on PS4/PS5. VGC says GTA 6 doesn't have a traditional one. You still get first person in some vehicles, and you aim down sights with some weapons. To look around in first person while standing still:

  1. Hold L2 to focus on someone or zoom in slightly.
  2. Click L3 + R3 together. You're now in first person with full camera control.
  3. Move, and you drop back to third person.
Careful: pressing L3 + R3 without holding L2 turns on Focus Mode instead. It drains your focus meter and highlights things in the world.
THEORY: GTA 5 added first person on new consoles a year after launch, so a later update is possible, but Rockstar hasn't said anything about it.

5. Day and night matter more than before

REPORTED: VGC (Jordan Middler), who played the review copy, Nov 18
  • Days and nights are much longer than in older GTA games.
  • Check the time on your phone (up on the D-pad).
  • Some shops close at night, and some missions need you to visit in the day or at night.
  • Police react differently at night.
  • Sleep at Jason's house to skip the night and change the time.

6. Use the minimap to find hidden rooms and enterable buildings

REPORTED: VGC (Jordan Middler), who played the review copy, Nov 18
  • Inside a shop, walk up to the back doors. The minimap shows if there's more to explore, including hidden rooms and hidden staircases when you're close.
  • Use Focus Mode on a door: white = you can go in, red = it's alarmed.
  • On the world map, buildings come in two blues. Darker blue = has an entrance or interior. Lighter blue = no way in.
  • Near a building with an interior, the minimap marks the door you can use, usually with a white line.

7. Feed Dolores for an early Focus buff

REPORTED: VGC (Jordan Middler), who played the review copy, Nov 18

Jason has a pet snake called Dolores. Feed her and watch her eat her mouse, and you get an early Focus buff. It's quick and it helps from the first hour.

8. Unlock roadside repairs with Just A Scratch

REPORTED: VGC (Jordan Middler), who played the review copy, Nov 18

Finish the mission Just A Scratch and you can call your mechanic to fix your car wherever you are (in most cases). VGC says it isn't cheap but it's worth it, especially before a chase or a long drive.

How to change hair in GTA 6

REPORTED: VGC (Jordan Middler), who played the review copy, Nov 18
  • Jason: barbers and stylists around Leonida. Pay for a cut or dye, and change his facial hair and hair colour.
  • Lucia: the same barbers and stylists for hair. Beauty parlours (the lip icon on the map) do her makeup and nails.
  • Each character has a set of signature hairstyles plus standard ones. Some styles are only at certain shops.
  • Pre-ordered? The Vice City Style pack's haircuts, facial hair and makeup can be applied at these shops (what's in each edition).

Prices for cuts and dyes weren't listed, so we'll add them after launch.

Our take: what to do in your first hour

OUR TAKE
  1. Feed Dolores for the Focus buff.
  2. Stash your cash under Jason's floorboards and keep doing it until Buck Me.
  3. Push the story to Buck Me for the bank account and RydeMe, then to Just A Scratch for roadside repairs.
  4. Learn the two blues on the map and the white/red door check before you rob anything.
  5. Save the late-game cheat hunt for later. Cheats only work near the end of the story (every cheat).

Frequently asked questions

Does GTA 6 have fast travel?

Yes. VGC reports the RydeMe taxi app on your phone is GTA 6's fast travel. It unlocks during the mission Buck Me, once you have a bank account. You can ride in real time, pay to speed it up, or skip the ride for an extra $100.

When can you switch between Jason and Lucia in GTA 6?

You can play Lucia in the open world after Chapter 1 ends, and free switching opens in Chapter 2. Press right on the D-pad to open the swap menu.

Does GTA 6 have a first-person mode?

Not a full one like GTA 5. Some vehicles switch to first person and some weapons aim down sights. Standing still, hold L2 and click L3 + R3 for a first-person look; moving ends it.

How do you not lose money in GTA 6?

Until the mission Buck Me you have no bank account, and dying or being arrested loses the cash you carry. Stash it under the loose floorboards in Jason's bedroom.
