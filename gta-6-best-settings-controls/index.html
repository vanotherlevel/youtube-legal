The best GTA 6 settings to change first are Targeting (Lock-On, Partial or Free Aim), Aim Assist Strength, the Control Scheme (Default puts sprint on L3, Classic moves it back to X), Motion Blur and Camera Shake, plus Mute Licensed Music if you stream. Every one of them is in Pause > Settings, and you can change them at any time.

We read the menus frame by frame from two launch-day streams: GTA Series Videos went through every tab before the opening, and MELOO checked Gameplay and Display later. Below is every option we saw, its default, and what we'd set. More help on our GTA 6 guides hub.

Labels: ON SCREEN = we read it in a launch stream recording. REPORTED = launch-day outlet guides (credited). OUR TAKE = our recommendation. Menus may change with patches; this is the launch version.

The first-boot setup screens

ON SCREEN: seen in GTA Series Videos' launch stream
  1. Screen Narration toggle on the boot screen (Off).
  2. Navigation Experience, with three choices. We saw Minimap & Compass (Dynamic) here and Minimap Always On in a second launch stream we logged.
  3. UI Text Size (4 sizes) and Subtitles (4 choices, including Small, Medium and Large).
  4. Brightness calibration: turn the slider until the dark Rockstar logo is barely visible.
  5. Finish Setup?: the card that says the default controls now use L3 to run and sprint, "but can be changed at any time". Press Options to open Settings first.
  6. Legal Terms: tick Privacy Policy and Terms of Service, then Confirm.

OUR TAKE: pick Dynamic for a clean screen (the minimap appears when it's needed or when you press the D-pad), or Always On while you learn the map. You can change it later under UI > Navigation.

Control scheme and the L3 sprint change

ON SCREEN: seen in GTA Series Videos' launch stream

The Control Scheme tab has a scheme picker (Default, 1 of 3) and a preview list of 9 entries, including On Foot, Aiming, Melee, Land / Water Vehicles, Aircraft, Bicycle, Swimming and Parachute. This is the Default On Foot map we saw on a DualSense:

REPORTED (Insider Gaming, GamingBolt, GamesRadar)

The three schemes are Default, Classic and Left-Handed. Per Insider Gaming's full button tables: Classic puts Run back on X and moves Crouch to L3; Left-Handed puts Run on R3 and Look Behind on L3. GamesRadar's guide says the same: open Settings and switch the scheme from Default to Classic. We didn't see a button-by-button remap option in the tab, so the three schemes (plus your console's own system remapping) look like what you get.

OUR TAKE: should you switch?
  • Give Default an hour. One L3 click to run beats GTA 5's tap-X mashing.
  • Switch to Classic if muscle memory wins, or your left stick's click is worn.
  • Turn on Stand & Sprint (Gameplay > Movement) either way. Its on-screen description: it "stands you up automatically when activating sprint instead of staying in a crouch". It's Off by default.

Aim assist, lock-on and combat options

ON SCREEN: seen in GTA Series Videos' launch stream

Gameplay > Combat holds every aiming option. The default is Targeting: Lock-On (1 of 3). Switch to Free Aim (3 of 3) and the menu says "Aiming will never lock on to a target", and the two lock-on rows (Lock-On Time and Lock-On Target Switch) vanish, which takes the Gameplay tab from 27 options to 25. MELOO's Gameplay tab also showed 25 options.

REPORTED (GamingBolt, GameRant)

Both name the middle mode Partial: it slows your reticle near a target without locking on. GameRant says Shooting Assist still gives a quick lock-on to very close targets even in Free Aim, so turn it off too if you want no help at all. GamingBolt puts Aim Assist Strength at 0-5. In the stream we watched, the bar looked full at 4, so check your own slider.

OUR TAKE: best aim settings
  • New to GTA: Lock-On, Aim Assist at max, Lock-On Time 4+, Lock-On Target Switch On.
  • Skill without snapping: Partial, Aim Assist 2-3, Shooting Assist On.
  • Hardcore: Free Aim, Shooting Assist and Drive-By Assist Off, plus UI > Persistent Center Dot On.
  • Leave Quick Draw On. GamingBolt notes L2 and R2 then draw your last-used weapon.

Best settings for comfort and performance

ON SCREEN: seen in GTA Series Videos' launch stream

Camera > Comfort holds Camera Preferences (On), Weapon Sway (On), Camera Shake (On), Camera Bob (10) and Phone Display (Default, "choose the point of view when using the phone"). Camera > Cinematic holds Cinematic Kill Shot Frequency (3), Cinematic Vehicle Crash Frequency (3) and Idle Camera (On), which "switches to cinematic views when you've been inactive for a while".

We saw no graphics or frame-rate mode in any tab. For how it runs on each console, see our Digital Foundry PS5 vs PS5 Pro breakdown.

OUR TAKE: best comfort settings
  • Motion Blur 0-1. Blur smears every fast camera turn. The GTA Series Videos streamer dropped it from 3 to 1 on his way through the menus.
  • Camera Shake Off, Camera Bob 4-6 if you get motion sick or play close to a big screen. GamingBolt recommends Camera Shake off as well.
  • Cinematic Kill Shot / Vehicle Crash Frequency 1-2. Fewer slow-motion cuts in fights and chases (GamingBolt says the sliders go up to 5).

UI, reticle and progress notifications (animals, collections, stunts)

ON SCREEN: seen in GTA Series Videos' launch stream

The UI tab is the biggest one at 70 options. The highlights:

  • Readability: Language (12), UI Text Size (Small, 1 of 4), Color Adjustments, Map / Minimap Blip Size, In-World Marker Size.
  • Subtitles: Mission Only (2 of 3) by default, plus Closed Captions, Size, Speaker Names (Off) and Background.
  • Navigation: Minimap and Compass (both Dynamic, 2 of 3), GPS Route, Door and Interaction Markers, Altimeter.
  • HUD: Help Text, Objective, Phone Notifications, Area Names (On), Street Names (Off), Wanted Stars, Heat Indicators, Money and more.
  • Reticle: Persistent Center Dot (Off), Hit Markers (On), Reticle Shape (Complex, 1 of 4), Projectile Arc (Off (Toggle On), 1 of 4).
  • Units: Imperial, Pounds and Fahrenheit by default, each 2 of 2, so metric is there too.

Progress notifications

The toggles are Progress Notifications, Weapons, Vehicles, Animals ("Displays a notification when a new animal is discovered"), Collections, Stunts, Basketball, Challenges and Map Updates. Weapons, Vehicles and Animals were Off when the streamer first reached them, and he switched them on a few seconds later. Challenges was Off. So GTA 6 tracks animal discoveries, collections and stunts.

OUR TAKE: turn Animals, Weapons and Vehicles On. If you're chasing the platinum, our trophy list shows what counts.

Audio: Mute Licensed Music and the best streaming setup

ON SCREEN: seen in GTA Series Videos' launch stream

Audio > Setup: Mono Audio (Off), Tinnitus Reduction (Off), Controller Speaker (Off), GPS Voice (Off, 4 of 4), Mute Licensed Music (Off by default) and Vehicle Radio (Default, 1 of 3). Volume has Master, SFX, UI, Dialogue and Music (all 10).

Mute Licensed Music "mutes any licensed music", and its note adds that captions still show for music when they're on. The streamer switched it On in setup and changed it again mid-opening, so you can flip it any time.

OUR TAKE: best settings for streaming and YouTube
  1. Mute Licensed Music On before you go live or record. Radio songs are what get VODs muted or claimed. GamingBolt recommends the same for creators.
  2. Subtitles and Speaker Names On for viewers watching muted.
  3. A lower Blood & Gore level and Dismemberment Off if your platform or sponsors are strict about graphic violence.
  4. Idle Camera On for free B-roll while you read chat.

Recording rules are separate: see Rockstar's spoiler video policy.

Camera settings and the pause menu

ON SCREEN: seen in GTA Series Videos' launch stream / ON SCREEN: seen in MELOO's launch stream

The Camera tab (24 options) sets height and follow speed on foot, in vehicles, in aircraft and underwater. OUR TAKE: if the driving camera fights you, lower Horizontal Follow Speed (Driver) from 10 to 6-7.

MELOO's pause menu: Map, Game, Store, Help, Photo Studio, Settings. In Settings, Restore Default (Square) resets the highlighted option, so experiment freely.

Frequently asked questions

How do you change sprint from L3 in GTA 6?

Pause > Settings > Control Scheme, then switch Default to Classic. Insider Gaming and GamesRadar report that Classic puts Run back on X (A on Xbox) and moves Crouch to L3. Left-Handed puts Run on R3.

Can you turn off dismemberment in GTA 6?

Yes. Settings > Display > Visual Effects has a Dismemberment toggle (On by default) and Blood & Gore Intensity (High by default, 3 levels). We read both on screen in two launch streams.

What is the best aim assist setting in GTA 6?

Our take: Lock-On with max aim assist if you're new, Partial with 2-3 for a fairer feel, Free Aim with Shooting Assist off for hardcore. Default is Lock-On, Aim Assist 4.

Does GTA 6 have a streamer mode?

There's no mode by that name, but Audio > Mute Licensed Music (Off by default) mutes all licensed music, and captions still show for songs. Turn it on before you stream or record.

Does GTA 6 have difficulty settings?

No difficulty setting was in the menus. The closest options are Reduced Vehicle Damage, Accessible Combat, Simplified Mini Games, Tap Assist and the aim options.
