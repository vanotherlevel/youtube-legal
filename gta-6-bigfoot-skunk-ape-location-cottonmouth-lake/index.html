Bigfoot Is Real in GTA 6: Skunk Ape Found at Cottonmouth Lake

Yes, Bigfoot is in GTA 6. Players found him within hours of launch: it's Florida's own version, the Skunk Ape, and the easiest sighting so far is at Cottonmouth Lake, just south of Hamlet, in Leonida's swamps.

You don't need to get far in the story. GameRant says the encounter opens after your first mission, with no time-gating, so you can drive there as soon as free roam starts.

REPORTED: Insider Gaming, GameRant, VGTimes, Sportskeeda (Nov 18, 2026). Rockstar hasn't commented.

Where to find Bigfoot in GTA 6

  1. Finish your first mission so free roam opens up.
  2. Drive to Cottonmouth Lake, just south of Hamlet. GameRant describes it as across from the Chapter 1 starting area at Key Lento.
  3. Listen for a deep, guttural growl. Insider Gaming says that's the sign you're in the right spot.
  4. Look for the creature over an animal carcass near the road. GameRant says he's eating a deer up a footpath.
  5. Follow him into the marsh. He runs off and disappears. You can't catch him, and GameRant says bullets don't hurt him.
  6. Find the effigy tied to a tree in the woods and inspect it.

What happens when you find him

Inspecting the effigy is what triggers the real moment. Jason blacks out, and a short secret scene plays while he drifts in and out of consciousness. You see only the creature's big, hairy feet as it walks up to him.

The reports differ on one detail. Insider Gaming says nearby flowers put you to sleep. GameRant says the effigy knocks you out and Bigfoot carries you back to your vehicle. Either way, you wake up without a clear look at him.

Want a proper look? VGTimes says to run right up to him before he flees and open Photo Mode. That freezes the moment so you can rotate the camera around him.

Why it's a Skunk Ape, not a Sasquatch

Leonida is Rockstar's version of Florida, and Florida's swamp legend is the Skunk Ape, a smellier, swamp-dwelling cousin of the Pacific Northwest Sasquatch. VGTimes points out that Rockstar picked the local legend instead of the classic Bigfoot.

Frequently asked questions

Is Bigfoot in GTA 6?

Yes. Players found the Skunk Ape, Florida's version of Bigfoot, near Cottonmouth Lake on launch day (reported by Insider Gaming, GameRant and VGTimes).

Where is Bigfoot in GTA 6?

At Cottonmouth Lake, just south of Hamlet in Leonida's swamps. Listen for a deep growl and look for him over an animal carcass near the road.

When can you find Bigfoot in GTA 6?

After your first mission. GameRant reports no time-gating, so you can go as soon as free roam opens.

Can you kill Bigfoot in GTA 6?

No. GameRant says bullets don't injure him and you can't catch him. He runs into the marsh and leaves an effigy behind.
