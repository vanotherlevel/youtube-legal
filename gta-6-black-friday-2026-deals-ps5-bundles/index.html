GTA 6 has no official Black Friday discount. The Standard Edition is still $79.99 in the US and £69.99 on the UK console stores. Savings so far are mostly from UK retailers, console bundles and controllers.

REPORTED: prices below were listed by deal guides published or updated by Nov 26, 2026. We did not check each store live, so confirm the price and stock before you pay.

Is GTA 6 cheaper anywhere?

In the US, the guides we checked found no price below $79.99 for the code-in-box edition at the big retailers. In the UK there are lower prices:

Sony has no official GTA 6 PS5 bundle. These are retailer bundles:

Controllers and accessories

A PlayStation Blog sale, as quoted by gta6-life, takes $20 off the DualSense and $30 off the DualSense Edge through Nov 30. Amazon US was listed at $169 for the Edge (normally $199.99), and Amazon UK at £149.99 (normally £199.99). We found no discount on the limited GTA 6 DualSense ($84.99 / £74.99).

Should you wait?

THEORY (our take): with the game this new and no official sale announced, waiting for a US price cut over this weekend is unlikely to pay off. If you want it now, buy it now; if you need a console too, compare the bundle against the parts bought separately.

Check you have room first: guides put the install at a little over 200 GB. New players can start with our beginner tips, and the editions are compared on our Standard vs Ultimate page.

Frequently asked questions

Is GTA 6 on sale for Black Friday 2026?

There is no official discount. It is still $79.99 in the US. Some UK retailers list it below £69.99.

Is there an official GTA 6 PS5 bundle?

No. The bundles listed come from retailers such as EE and Very in the UK.

When do these deals end?

Most listings give no end date. The EE digital bundle and Sony's accessory sale are listed through Nov 30.
