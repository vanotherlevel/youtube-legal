Has GTA 6 already made its money back? On sales, probably yes. On profit, nobody outside Take-Two can say yet. Reported pre-orders of about $1.65 billion already sit above the most-cited $1 billion to $1.5 billion development estimates, but Rockstar has never revealed the real budget, and pre-order revenue is not the same as profit.

REPORTED: ABC News, Sensor Tower data via GameSpot/Yahoo Finance, analyst estimates via Moneywise and Gadget Review

The question took off on launch night after Australia's ABC News wrote that GTA VI is believed to have recouped its investment already. Here is what that claim is built on, what is a real number and what is a guess. More launch coverage on our GTA 6 news tab.

What ABC News reported

In its launch report (Fergus Gregg, ABC Arts, Nov 19 AEDT), ABC called GTA VI the most expensive game ever developed, with an estimated budget of more than $2.2 billion. The ABC writes in Australian dollars: in the same piece it converts the reported US$1.5 billion in pre-sales to about $2.21 billion, so the budget figure is roughly US$1.5 billion too.

Former ABC Good Game: Spawn Point host Angharad Yeo told the ABC that Rockstar had likely already made its money back, pointing to those pre-sales. That is her read of the reported numbers, not a statement from Rockstar or Take-Two.

What GTA 6 has already brought in

  • About 17.07 million pre-orders worth about $1.65 billion at the New Zealand unlock, per Sensor Tower data reported by GameSpot, Yahoo Finance and Forbes. Full breakdown on our pre-orders page. REPORTED.
  • Take-Two's own guidance: $8.0 billion to $8.2 billion in net bookings for fiscal 2027, built largely on GTA VI. OFFICIAL (company guidance).
  • GTA V for scale: about $800 million in its first 24 hours and about $1 billion in three days, per Guinness World Records.

Forecasts for the first weekend and first year are on our opening weekend sales page.

So has it really paid for itself?

OUR TAKE

On raw sales, the reported $1.65 billion in pre-orders already tops the $1 billion to $1.5 billion development range most analysts use. But that is gross revenue. Sony, Microsoft and retailers keep a share of every copy, marketing on this scale costs extra, and we don't know the true budget. So 'it already made its money back' is a fair headline on sales, and an unproven one on profit.

The real answer comes when Take-Two reports its next quarterly results, which will include launch-window sales. We'll update this page when it does.

Frequently asked questions

How much did GTA 6 cost to make?

Rockstar and Take-Two have never said. Analyst estimates mostly fall between $1 billion and $1.5 billion for development, with wider estimates up to about $2 billion.

Is GTA 6 the most expensive game ever made?

Very likely, going by every published estimate. Call of Duty: Black Ops Cold War's $700 million lifecycle cost from court documents is the biggest confirmed figure we know of, and GTA 6 estimates are well above it.

Has GTA 6 already made its money back?

On sales, reportedly yes: Sensor Tower put pre-orders at about $1.65 billion at launch, above most budget estimates. Profit is a different question, because platforms and retailers take a share and the real budget is unknown.

Why does ABC say $2.2 billion?

The ABC reports in Australian dollars. Its $2.2 billion is roughly US$1.5 billion.
