Can You Pet Dogs in GTA 6? Yes, and Day-One Players Found a Lot More

Yes, you can pet dogs in GTA 6. Walk up to a dog, hold L2, and you get three prompts: Pet, Scold and Study. On day one, players also posted clips of petting cats, and one found an Easter egg at the top of the game's tallest radio tower.

OFFICIAL: the Pet / Scold / Study prompt is shown in Rockstar's 26-minute Extended Look gameplay (Jason pets a dog before the elevator drug meet), as described by Shacknews

How petting works

  • Pet: Jason gives the dog a pat. Shown in Rockstar's own gameplay.
  • Scold: tells the dog off. Also on the prompt in the Extended Look.
  • Study: looks at the animal more closely. Shacknews compares it to Red Dead Redemption 2, where studying animals filled the compendium. How deep it goes in GTA 6 is not confirmed yet.

What players found on day one

Review copies and the New Zealand launch put the game in players' hands on November 18. Kotaku's launch live blog rounded up the first animal clips posted on X:

  • Petting dogs and cats
  • Hitting a dog with a car door, and throwing a cat out of a moving car (yes, really)
  • A dog that rescues the player on a jet ski
REPORTED: clips by individual X users (credited by Kotaku as @SMGxPrincessirl, @ibxtoycat, @ChumBucketDrama, @gtasix_ and @ooctwts), via Kotaku's live blog, Nov 18 12:56 PM ET

The radio tower Easter egg

Player @xGarbett climbed the tallest radio tower in Leonida, a climb of about six minutes, and found an egg in a nest at the top. Throwing the egg off the tower plays a small cutscene. Kotaku's Ethan Gach says he found the egg himself last week but never saw the cutscene. We're not spoiling it here.

REPORTED: Kotaku live blog, Nov 18 12:55 PM ET

Dog poop, and the lottery dog

Before launch, Rockstar North's Rob Nelson told YouTuber TGG that if a dog does its business and the owner bags it, you can knock the owner so they drop the bag, pick it up and throw it at people. Kotaku also cited an IGN interview with Nelson about a dog that can somehow predict winning lottery numbers.

REPORTED: Rob Nelson via TGG's hands-off preview and IGN, as written up by Kotaku (Rebekah Valentine, Aug 28)

Can you own a pet in GTA 6?

Not confirmed. Rockstar has shown no adoption, pet ownership or pet care for Jason and Lucia. Kotaku noted that one trailer scene hints they own a dog or live with someone who does, and GTA 5 had Chop, but that isn't a pet system.

THEORY: a pet at a safehouse is a fan hope, not a confirmed feature. We'll update this page if players find one
