In GTA 6 you can't keep a stolen car until Chapter 2: from then on you register it with Moses (no tracker) or, from early Chapter 3, at any Pay 'n' Spray (standard tracker), and it gets a garage slot. Before that, Jason's '70 Vapid Ganado is your car, and the first mod shop, Coastal Auto Care, fixes and resprays it for free during the Chapter 1 mission "Just A Scratch".

We read every mod-shop menu, price and car name at full size in three launch streams. For buying garages, see our GTA 6 garages page; more on the GTA 6 guides hub.

Labels: ON SCREEN = we read it in MELOO's, Ben-Gun's or Dalek's launch stream. REPORTED = launch-day guides, credited per section. OUR TAKE = our read. Some prices were mission discounts; we say when.

How to save and keep cars in GTA 6

REPORTED (PowerPyx "How to Get New Personal Car", Nov 18)
  1. Chapter 1: only your personal car (Jason's '70 Ganado) stays. PowerPyx warns that upgrades bought for it are lost when you replace it later.
  2. Chapter 2: the main mission "Moses (Take A Vehicle to Moses)" unlocks registration. Bring Moses a car with no tracker and pick Register in his garage in Port Gellhorn.
  3. Check the tracker first with the Waink phone app. Scan the car before you steal it.
  4. You start with 1 garage slot. When it's full you must sell a car to make room. Later in Chapter 2 the Vice City Real Estate app lets you buy more garages (prices and locations here).
  5. Early Chapter 3: you can sell stolen cars and register cars with Standard Trackers at any Pay 'n' Spray.
ON SCREEN: Ben-Gun's and MELOO's launch streams

Here's what a Waink scan looks like. Ben-Gun scanned the red Imponte '77 Phoenix in the police impound: Access: LOCKED, Security: ALARMED, Tracker: NONE. MELOO got the same "WAINK IT" prompt on R1. Walking up to a car also shows its make and model, like the Declasse '74 Vamos in the same lot.

REPORTED (PowerPyx, Cultured Vultures)
  • Tracker tiers: no-tracker cars go to Moses, standard-tracker cars to Pay 'n' Spray, and high-end cars with advanced trackers need the Key Cloner (mid Chapter 3). PowerPyx says advanced trackers alert the police when you slow down in the car.
  • Registration cost: PowerPyx puts a re-registration at "a couple thousand dollars". Cultured Vultures says Moses registers your first car for free.
  • Scooter Bros app: Cultured Vultures reports it can deliver a registered car to you for a fee, or $12,000 up front for three free deliveries a day. It can also recover a destroyed or impounded car, "for quite the premium" (no exact price given).
OUR TAKE: in Chapter 1, spend only what the story gives you on the Ganado. Save the real mod money for the first car you register in Chapter 2. To make that money, see our GTA 6 money guide (Moses car runs, Randy's supercars).

Car customization: the real mod shop menus and prices

ON SCREEN: MELOO's stream (~2:16:30) and Dalek's stream (~1:21:20), Coastal Auto Care

Your first visit to a mod shop comes in "Just A Scratch" (Chapter 1, after "Gone Fishing" per PowerPyx). Jason drives the rusty Ganado to Joey at Coastal Auto Care. A pop-up opens first: "REPAIR FOR FREE? You'll need to repair your vehicle before making modifications." So the rule is simple: a damaged car has to be repaired before you can mod it.

The shop menu has six tabs: PRO BUILDS, COSMETICS, BODYWORK, PERFORMANCE, WHEELS, INTERIOR. Every screen shows the car's stats on the right. For Jason's '70 Ganado (stock) they read:

The EXCLUSIVE tag on the Retro Build and the Blade livery most likely marks edition-bonus content (one launch guide lists the Ganado Retro Build as an Ultimate Edition item). Not yet checked on a Standard Edition copy.

Other mod shops

REPORTED (PowerPyx Ultimate Edition rewards guide, Nov 18)
  • Coastal Auto Care (Key Lento, Mariana County): the story mod shop above.
  • Rideout Customs (Ultimate Edition): donk-style custom rims and interiors.
  • One-Eyed Willie's Auto Shop (Ultimate Edition, Lake Leonida): off-road modifications and hand-painted artwork.
  • Pay 'n' Spray (19 shops): repairs, resprays, and from Chapter 3 selling and registering. More below.

On screen, MELOO's game showed Joey's tip after the mission: "Call Joey from your Contacts if the Ganado needs to be repaired or delivered." VGC reports that calling your mechanic repairs the car wherever you are, but that it's "not cheap".

Pay 'n' Spray: repair, respray and losing the cops

ON SCREEN: Ben-Gun's stream (~1:24:15), MELOO's (~1:04:30), Dalek's (~1:01:55)

All three streamers used a Pay 'n' Spray in the same early impound mission. Ben-Gun's menu read PAY 'N' SPRAY - HAMLET, with REPAIR: "Repairs engine and collision damage." for the Imponte '77 Phoenix. It was FREE in the mission, with $440 shown crossed out as the normal price. The respray screen (SERVICES / RESPRAY) offered three colours. The car's current red was locked with the note "Too similar to current color", so you can't respray a car the same colour to trick the cops.

  • MELOO's hint: "Pay 'n' Sprays can respray vehicles so the cops can't recognize them."
  • Ben-Gun's hint after the respray: "Cops will recognize you faster in areas with heat if you haven't changed your appearance and clothing." The wanted HUD shows three icons under the stars: clothes, face and car. A respray only clears the car one.
  • Dalek cycled red, grey and blue before driving out in blue.
REPORTED (PowerPyx Pay 'n' Spray locations, Nov 18)

There are 19 Pay 'n' Sprays, marked with an orange spray-can icon once the map is unfogged (Map > Vehicles > Pay 'n' Sprays has a counter): 4 in Mariana County (including Key Lento), 10 in Vice-Dale County (including Hamlet, Downtown, Ocean Beach and Washington Beach), 1 in Leonard County, 2 in Kelly County (Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia) and 2 in Lummox County.

The police impound: how the Phoenix mission plays

ON SCREEN: all three streams (Ben-Gun ~1:16:28, Dalek ~57:22, MELOO ~55:30)

The early mission "Unwanted Attention" (map brief: "Work with Cal to recover a car from a police impound lot for Brian.") sends Jason into a police impound for a red Imponte '77 Phoenix. Cal sums it up: "high school weed dealer energy". Ben-Gun's lot sits next to the Hamlet Police Department. Tips the game gives you along the way:

  • "Gates can be opened quickly by shooting the button controls." (Ben-Gun)
  • "Lose the cops by hiding or escaping their search area." (Dalek)
  • "Try going off-road or along back streets to lose pursuing cops." (MELOO)
  • Then "Take the Phoenix to the Pay 'n' Spray", respray it, and "Deliver the Phoenix to Brian's garage".

How it goes wrong: Ben-Gun tried towing the Phoenix out with a tow truck at 3 stars, got an "Engine damaged" warning and was wasted beside the police station. Dalek smashed out through the gate into a cruiser (the Phoenix's plate read ASS MAN). OUR TAKE: get in the car before the shooting starts, and use the gate buttons instead of a slow tow truck. Later, in free roam at night, Dalek used a BREAK OPEN prompt on a Key Lento POLICE IMPOUND fence and ended up busted.

  • Private jet: Dalek walked onto the apron at the Vice City international airport and took a Lapwing Nimbus (~2:33:25). With 4 wanted stars he flew it over the city, then bailed out with the PARACHUTE prompt over the islands (~2:36:04).

  • Your own plane: GameSpot says the planes from story missions aren't kept. The keepable one is Kyle's Mallard, at Kyle McKittrick's farm in East Kelly County, after his side questline.

Vehicle damage and the "Reduced Vehicle Damage" setting

ON SCREEN: MELOO's stream, Settings > Gameplay (~5:53)

Under Settings > Gameplay there's a toggle called Reduced Vehicle Damage: "Reduces damage taken by your vehicle. Warning: This will significantly change the balance and experience of gameplay." It was Off on MELOO's console. The same list has other driving options: Nitrous (Hold, 1/2), Auto Drive (Restricted, 1/2), Auto Equip Helmet (Off), and Simplified Mini Games, which "simplifies the interactions needed for situations such as exercising, hotwiring, and basketball."

Damage matters, which is why the setting exists. In the streams, crashes crushed bonnets and threw up "Engine damaged" warnings. Kotaku's review says crashes can make a car harder to steer or accelerate, and Shacknews reports that engine damage hurts fuel retention. OUR TAKE: together with Accessible Combat and Simplified Mini Games, it's one of the main switches that make GTA 6 easier.

OUR TAKE: turn it on if Randy's "perfect condition" supercar deliveries keep failing, or if you just want to cruise. Leave it off if you want the handling to fall apart after a crash.

Frequently asked questions

How do you save a car in GTA 6?

Register it. From Chapter 2 (after the mission "Moses (Take A Vehicle to Moses)"), bring Moses a car with no tracker and pick Register. From early Chapter 3 you can also register standard-tracker cars at any Pay 'n' Spray. Each registered car needs a garage slot, and you start with one.

Where is the first mod shop in GTA 6?

Coastal Auto Care in Key Lento, which you reach in the Chapter 1 mission "Just A Scratch". It repairs and resprays Jason's '70 Ganado for free in that mission. Its menu has Pro Builds, Cosmetics, Bodywork, Performance, Wheels and Interior tabs.

How much does car customization cost in GTA 6?

On screen at Coastal Auto Care (Jason's Ganado): $50 to restore the engine bay, $100 to restore the stock engine (discounted from $200), $185 to restore the finish, $200 to restore the bodywork, $590 for a Custom Wing spoiler, $740 for the Blade livery and $1,535 for the Retro Build - Standard kit.

Does Pay 'n' Spray remove your wanted level in GTA 6?

It changes your car's colour so the cops can't recognize the car, but they also track your face and clothes. The game's hint warns that cops in heat areas recognize you faster if you haven't changed your appearance and clothing. There are 19 Pay 'n' Sprays (PowerPyx).

What does Reduced Vehicle Damage do in GTA 6?

It's a toggle in Settings > Gameplay that "reduces damage taken by your vehicle". The game warns that it will significantly change the balance and experience of gameplay.
