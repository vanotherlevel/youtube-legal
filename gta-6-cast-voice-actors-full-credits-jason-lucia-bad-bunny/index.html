Rockstar has published the full GTA 6 credits, and they confirm the leads: Dylan Rourke plays Jason Duval and Manni L. Perez plays Lucia Caminos. The official cast list on rockstargames.com runs to roughly 400 character credits, with surprise names like Bad Bunny, Tyreek Hill, Jason Sudeikis and John Leguizamo.

OFFICIAL: Rockstar Games credits page, rockstargames.com/VI/credits, live Nov 19, 2026

The credits went live on launch day. On the same day the two lead actors broke their silence. Below are the main cast, the celebrity cameos, and what the leads said.

Who plays Jason and Lucia in GTA 6?

Jason Duval is played by Dylan Rourke. Lucia Caminos is played by Manni L. Perez. They are the first two names in the MAIN CAST section of Rockstar's credits. Fans had guessed Rourke from the trailers for a long time, but this is the first official confirmation (PlayStation Universe).

REPORTED: the actors' joint statement, via GTABoom, GameSpot, GamesRadar+ and VGTimes

According to GTABoom, Rourke and Perez posted the same message on Instagram and changed their profile pictures to their characters. They signed it "Jay" and "LU". GamesRadar+ quotes them calling the developers "the hardest working and most skilled artisans gaming has to offer". VGTimes reports the sign-off: "See y'all in Leonida."

Familiar GTA names in the credits

Two credits jump out for long-time fans. Jimmy De Santa is credited to Danny Tamberelli, the actor who voiced Jimmy in GTA V. Dave Norton, the FIB agent from GTA V, is credited to Julian Gamble.

THEORY: how big these roles are is unknown. Credits list names, not screen time, and they could be TV, radio or phone cameos.

Radio, TV and writing credits

  • The credits have a full GTA TV & Radio writing team, plus cast sections for shows. Macca (from Macca & Friends on WEAZEL) is voiced by Darius Marquis Johnson.
  • Composers listed include Bobby Krlic and Andrew Kierzenbaum.
  • Under GTA TV & Radio, the writing directors are DJ Pooh and Joey Henson. The "Additional Writing By" list there includes "Weird Al" Yankovic, Arca, Billy Corben, Brian Posehn, Doug Stanhope and Lil Duval.
  • Rockstar studios named include Rockstar New England, Toronto, India and London, on top of the core team.

How to see the GTA 6 credits yourself

  1. Open Rockstar's official page: https://www.rockstargames.com/VI/credits.
  2. Scroll past the studio teams to MAIN CAST. Character names are in quotes, with actors under them.
  3. Keep scrolling for the full cast, the radio and TV casts, and Special Thanks.
