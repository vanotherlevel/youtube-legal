Is GTA 6 Censored in Japan or Australia? What We Know

Nobody has proven that GTA 6 is censored anywhere yet. The Japanese PS5 version is about 1.19 GB smaller than the global one and its CERO Z rating has no sexual content icon, while the Australia and New Zealand build is missing one 704 KB file. Rockstar and Take-Two have not explained either difference.

THEORY: censorship is a guess based on file sizes and rating icons. Rockstar has not confirmed any content changes

GTA 6 unlocked in New Zealand at midnight local time (6 AM ET, November 18), and the rest of the world follows on November 19. Players thinking about a region switch to play early keep asking the same thing: is the version I get cut? Here is what is actually known.

Japan: smaller file, missing icon

Japan's rating board, CERO, gave GTA 6 a CERO Z rating, its strictest 18+ tier. The Japanese listing shows content icons for violence and crime, but not for sexual content. In the US and Europe, the ESRB flags strong sexual content and nudity, and PEGI marks sexual content. Australia (R18+) and New Zealand (R18) both list sex scenes and nudity.

REPORTED: CERO Z with violence and crime icons only, via GTA BOOM, IBTimes UK and Automaton

Dataminer TexFunz2, who listed the PS5 preload's data chunks (reported by VGC), found a chunk called core_restofworld of about 1.28 GB. TexFunz2 suggested it may hold content that some regions, likely Japan, don't get. That size is close to Japan's gap, but nobody outside Rockstar can open the encrypted files to check.

THEORY: that core_restofworld is cut Japan content. Not confirmed

Why fans expect Japan cuts

History. The Japanese release of GTA V softened its sex scenes and made a torture scene non-playable, even though it also had a CERO Z rating. CERO Z still bars explicit sex and the goriest content, so publishers often edit games for Japan.

REPORTED: GTA V Japan edits, via GTA BOOM

Australia and New Zealand: one tiny file

The Australia and New Zealand PS5 build is missing core_restofworld001, a chunk of about 704 KB. That's smaller than a phone photo. It could be a texture, an image or one setting. Australia rated GTA 6 R18+ with no changes listed, which is why most outlets think nothing big is cut there. We covered the find when it broke: GTA 6's Australia and New Zealand version is missing one 704 KB file.

Datamine by @TexFunz2, reported by VGC. Contents unknown

Australia does have a record here: GTA 3 and Vice City had content removed there, and GTA Online's casino features weren't available in Australia. No source links the missing 704 KB file to gambling or to any feature.

Should you region-switch to play early?

That's the real question behind all this. New Zealand accounts get the game hours before everyone else (see our unlock times and NZ trick page). Based on the reports, the NZ build differs from the global one by under a megabyte, so a New Zealand account is very unlikely to cost you real content. A Japanese account is the one with the 1.19 GB question mark.

OUR TAKE: based on the file sizes above, not on playing both versions

Region switching also has its own risks: purchases and store settings can get messy, and there are reports of accounts running into trouble. Check your console's rules first.

What happens next

Now that New Zealand players have the game, side-by-side comparisons of scenes are the only way to settle it. Kotaku asked Take-Two what differs between the regional versions. When Rockstar, Take-Two or a solid comparison answers it, we'll update this page. Follow every update on our GTA 6 news hub.

Frequently asked questions

Is GTA 6 censored in Japan?

Not confirmed. The Japanese PS5 build is about 1.19 GB smaller and its CERO Z rating has no sexual content icon, but Rockstar hasn't said any content was changed.

Is GTA 6 censored in Australia or New Zealand?

Probably not in any big way. The AU/NZ build is missing one file of about 704 KB, and Australia rated it R18+ with no listed changes. What the file holds is unknown.

Will a New Zealand account give me a cut version of GTA 6?

Based on reported file sizes, the NZ build differs from the global one by under 1 MB, so it's very unlikely to cost you real content. A Japanese account is the one with a bigger gap.

Has Rockstar commented on GTA 6 censorship?

No. Neither Rockstar nor Take-Two has publicly explained the regional file size or rating differences.
