GTA 6 has no character creator, but you can change Jason and Lucia's hair, beard, clothes and accessories, and you can build their stats in the gym. Haircuts are sold at barbers like Barnacle Beauties (we saw cuts from $34 to $66 on launch day), clothes come from your safehouse wardrobe and from stores such as Hamlet Thrift, and workouts raise your Health and Stamina.

We went through three launch-day streams frame by frame and wrote down every menu, name and price we could read at full size. This page lists them, then adds what launch-day guides from PowerPyx, Insider Gaming and GTABase report about stats, tattoos and the Ultimate Edition stores. The last section explains why your outfit matters when the cops are after you.

Labels on this page: ON SCREEN = we read it ourselves in MELOO's, Ben-Gun's or Dalek's launch stream. OFFICIAL = Rockstar's own words. REPORTED = outlet guides written by people who have played the game. OUR TAKE = our own read. Prices are what the streamers saw early in the story and may change later.

Barbers: every hairstyle and price we saw

ON SCREEN: seen in MELOO's launch stream

In MELOO's launch stream, Jason sits down at Barnacle Beauties and the barber menu has four tabs: Hairstyle, Hair Color, Facial Hair Style and Facial Hair Color. You preview each cut live on Jason's face, then press BUY. Each item shows "Applied" or "Not applied" under it, and the cost comes out of your cash: MELOO had $1,480 before his $35 Buzz Cut and $1,445 after it.

The Hairstyle tab had 9 styles. Here they are with the prices on screen:

The Facial Hair Style tab had 10 options. We could read three of the prices: Short Stubble $20, Medium Growth $27 and Heavy Growth $30. We didn't see the Hair Color or Facial Hair Color menus open, so we can't list those colors or prices yet.

Early story tip (ON SCREEN, Ben-Gun's launch stream): before Lucia gets out, Jason's map lists an optional task called Get the Chop: "Visit Barnacle Beauties to change your hairstyle or tidy up your beard." It's marked "Lucia will appreciate this", so the haircut also helps your relationship with her. The same list has Pick Up Groceries, Buy Lucia a Necklace (from the jewelry store The Siren's Chest) and Buy Flowers.

The bathroom mirror at your safehouse

ON SCREEN: seen in Dalek's launch stream

You don't always need a barber. In Dalek's stream the MIRROR menu at home has Hairstyle, Facial Hair Style and Body Hair, and Body Hair includes Shaved Chest (option 1 of 2). No price was shown on that screen. Insider Gaming reports that the mirror starts with only a few options unlocked.

Sara's Unisex Salon (Ultimate Edition)

OFFICIAL (Rockstar editions page)

Rockstar's editions page lists Sara's Unisex Salon as Ultimate Edition content. Dexerto and Insider Gaming report that it sells hairstyles for both characters, facial hair for Jason, and makeup and nails for Lucia. Standard Edition players still get the regular barbers like Barnacle Beauties.

Clothes: the wardrobe, outfits and the trunk

ON SCREEN: seen in MELOO's and Ben-Gun's launch streams

Your safehouse WARDROBE has five tabs: Outfits, Tops, Bottoms, Footwear and Accessories. In MELOO's launch stream the Outfits tab shows 11 entries. These are the ones we could read across MELOO's and Ben-Gun's menus:

  • Saved Outfits (your own combinations)
  • Big Shots Workwear
  • Military Shirt & Jeans
  • Hawaiian Shirt & Shorts
  • Flannel Shirt & Jeans
  • T-Shirt & Board Shorts
  • Tourist Tank & Cutoffs
  • Workout Wear
  • Swimwear
  • Underwear and Nightwear (seen in Ben-Gun's list)
  • Big Shots Tactical Gear (seen in Ben-Gun's list)

The Accessories tab splits into Headwear, Eyewear, Jewelry and Watches, and Bottoms splits into Pants and Shorts. The button bar reads ADD TO TRUNK (1/5), so you can keep up to five outfits in your car's trunk. A tip on screen confirms it: "You can save custom outfits and add them to your trunk from your wardrobe."

OUR TAKE: put a spare outfit in your trunk before every big job. It's the fastest way to change clothes after a chase, and as the wanted section below explains, the cops track what you're wearing.

Tattoos in GTA 6

OFFICIAL (Rockstar editions page)

Rockstar's editions page lists the Electric Fang Tattoo Parlor as Ultimate Edition content. Dexerto and Insider Gaming report it's in Stockyard, with over 50 tattoos for Jason and Lucia designed by the art collective FAILE.

None of the three streams we watched visited a tattoo parlor, so we haven't seen a tattoo menu, the body zones or any prices. Jason already has his own arm tattoos when the game starts. Whether Standard Edition players get other tattoo parlors is not confirmed yet, and we'll update this section as soon as we see one in the game.

Gym and stats: Health, Stamina and Focus

ON SCREEN: seen in MELOO's, Ben-Gun's and Dalek's launch streams

The top-left HUD has three bars: pink heart (health), green lightning bolt (stamina) and purple eye (focus). The first gym tip in the game puts it plainly: "Strength training and cardio impacts your health and stamina. Track progress in the Fitness app." Another tip says "Working out regularly will help improve your physique and stats."

Jason's house has a small gym on the dock. All three streamers used it in Chapter 1. Each machine has its own rep target:

ExerciseReps per setControlsWhat popped up
Bench press5Hold L2 + R2 to lift, release slowly to lower; O to rerackStrength Workout +11% (MELOO)
Kettlebell swings14L2 + R2 to swing up, release at the topStrength Workout +3% (MELOO)
Squats5Lift / lower with L2 + R2, O to rerackWorkout Complete: Time to rest and recover (MELOO, Dalek)

The in-game phone page on Strength explains the rules. Weight training and pull-ups improve your health and build muscle. You have to hit the gym during your workout window, and "if you miss a window, or don't complete a full workout, you'll lose your gains." A popup mid-session repeats this: "Finish the workout to lock in your gains." Afterwards, eat some protein and rest: the game says "consuming protein after a workout will reduce the recovery period by 50%." Eating and resting before a workout also helps, and being tired hurts your stamina recovery.

Cardio has its own window. MELOO got a "Cardio Workout: Workout window open!" popup at home, and Ben-Gun got a "Stamina buffed" note. Ben-Gun's Jason also hits a punching bag under the house. For Focus, the game tells you "Thoughtful actions will increase your focus bar depending on your criminal profile," and Ben-Gun's meditation on the dock ends with "Focus buffed".

REPORTED (PowerPyx, Insider Gaming, GTABase, Nov 18)
  • Where the stats live: PowerPyx says you can see Strength (Health), Cardio (Stamina) and Focus by scrolling down a character's profile in the Jason & Lucia menu.
  • Strength = max health, Cardio = max stamina: PowerPyx. Cardio also goes up from treadmills and bikes at fitness studios, or from running, cycling and swimming in the open world.
  • Fitness studios: later you can pay a membership fee to join fitness studios (PowerPyx). GTABase says you buy the membership at the front desk, and that three exercises finish the day's workout.
  • Train with your partner: at fitness studios, while one character works out, the other finishes their workout automatically (PowerPyx).
  • Focus: controls how long you can use the slow-motion focus (L3 + R3). PowerPyx recommends the Market Range Beginner challenge at Rodgers Ranch & Shooting Range in Kelly County ($5 waiver pass). Insider Gaming adds basketball, fishing, golf, pool and meditation, and says your criminal profile decides which kills help: non-lethal takedowns for some profiles, headshots for others.
  • Your body changes: GTABase reports that eating affects your weight and exercise builds and tones muscle. We haven't tested this ourselves yet.
OUR TAKE: on the map there's a dumbbell filter, and Dalek's map showed blue dumbbell icons around Ocean Beach. Those look like the fitness studios. Until you unlock them, use the free dock gym at Jason's house every workout window, then grab a protein shake from the fridge.

Why your clothes matter with a wanted level

ON SCREEN: seen in Ben-Gun's and MELOO's launch streams

When you're wanted, three small red icons sit under the stars: a clothes hanger (your outfit), a person (your face and hair) and a car (your vehicle). MELOO's chase shows all three lit at 2 stars. Each icon means the cops have that part of your description.

Right after a Pay 'n' Spray respray in Ben-Gun's launch stream, the game spells out the rule: "Cops will recognize you faster in areas with heat if you haven't changed your appearance and clothing." A respray only fixes the car icon. The person and hanger icons stay until you change your look.

There's a quick fix in the item wheel. Ben-Gun's wheel has Baseball Cap and sunglasses slots that switch ON/OFF, with the hint "Adjust your hat and glasses to lower recognition". There's also a WEAR STYLE option for the cap.

REPORTED (GTABase, pre-launch)

GTABase says changing your outfit clears what the police know about your clothing, but changing just one accessory may not be enough. Changing your hairstyle, or covering up with a hat or mask, deals with your appearance. Our full chase breakdown is in the GTA 6 wanted level and police guide.

  1. Lose sight of the cops first.
  2. Respray or swap the car (car icon).
  3. Put on a trunk outfit or change at a wardrobe (hanger icon).
  4. Flip your hat and glasses in the item wheel, or get a haircut if you're near a barber (person icon).

Frequently asked questions

Is there character creation in GTA 6?

No. You play as Jason and Lucia, but you can change their hair, facial hair, body hair, clothes and accessories, and build their Health, Stamina and Focus through workouts and activities.

How much is a haircut in GTA 6?

At Barnacle Beauties we saw nine hairstyles from $34 (Caesar Cut) to $66 (Wavy Slick Back), and facial hair from $20 (Short Stubble). Your safehouse mirror also changes hairstyle, facial hair and body hair, but it starts with fewer options (no price was shown on it).

How do you change clothes in GTA 6?

Use the wardrobe at your safehouse (Outfits, Tops, Bottoms, Footwear, Accessories) or buy new items at a clothing store. You can save custom outfits and keep up to five of them in your car's trunk.

Can you get tattoos in GTA 6?

Rockstar lists the Electric Fang Tattoo Parlor in Stockyard as Ultimate Edition content, reported to have 50+ tattoos designed by FAILE. We haven't seen a tattoo menu on launch streams yet, and other tattoo parlors for the Standard Edition aren't confirmed.

How do you increase stats in GTA 6?

Strength workouts (bench press, squats, kettlebells) raise max health, cardio raises max stamina, and focus comes from activities like the shooting range, meditation, fishing or pool. Finish the full workout during your workout window, then eat protein: it cuts recovery time by 50%.

Do cops recognize your clothes in GTA 6?

Yes. The wanted HUD tracks your outfit, appearance and car separately, and the game warns that cops recognize you faster if you haven't changed your appearance and clothing. Change outfits, swap your hat and glasses, and respray the car.
