Yes, GTA 6 has cheats, but you have to earn them. According to PC Gamer, Game Rant and VGC, who played review copies, cheats only work after you reach the late game. Each one is unlocked by finishing a matching challenge, like 100 workouts for Body Armor. Two outlets list 61 cheats, and button inputs are known for 17 of them so far.

This page puts every reported cheat in one table: what it does, how to unlock it, its input if anyone has found it, and which outlet the input comes from. Where the outlets disagree, we say so. Rockstar hasn't published a cheat list, so everything here is REPORTED until the game is out on November 19 and players can test it.

Labels on this page: OFFICIAL = Rockstar's own words. REPORTED = outlets that played the game before launch (PC Gamer, Game Rant, VGC). THEORY = our own read. We'll test every input ourselves after launch and update this page.

Does GTA 6 have cheats?

REPORTED (PC Gamer, Game Rant, VGC, Nov 18): yes, unlocked through challenges

Yes. Three outlets reported cheats on November 18, the morning the review embargo lifted. All three say the same basic thing: you don't type a code from day one like in GTA 5. You finish a challenge in the world, and the matching cheat unlocks.

  • PC Gamer (Sean Martin): cheats open up only after you finish the main story, which it puts at 91 story missions. It loaded an earlier save to test, and cheats didn't work there.
  • VGC (Jordan Middler): cheats work after a certain late-game mission, but you can finish the unlock challenges earlier. It says the cheats live in the Progress menu, not the phone.
  • Game Rant (Jake Fillery): lists the same cheats with more button inputs. Several cheats are tied to finishing the main story.

How to use cheats in GTA 6

REPORTED: the two outlets describe it differently

This is the one point where the reports clash. PC Gamer's steps, on PS5:

  1. Press ↑ on the D-pad to open the phone.
  2. Hold L1 and press R1.
  3. Enter the cheat's input.

VGC says cheats are not on the phone and are found in the Progress menu once that option unlocks. Both could be true: the Progress menu may list what you've unlocked, and the phone may be where you enter it. That's our guess (THEORY). We'll confirm the exact steps on launch day. On Xbox, LB and RB should replace L1 and R1, but no outlet has said so yet.

Save warning (REPORTED, PC Gamer): once you use a cheat, you can't save from that point on. So cheats can't help you finish side missions or challenges. Quit without saving to turn them off. Use them in a session you don't mind losing.

Which cheats to unlock first

OUR TAKE: a plan, not a fact
  • Do workouts while you play the story. 100 workouts gives you Body Armor, and fitness challenges give the BMX and the '65 Dominator.
  • Shooting ranges early. One set of medals feeds three cheats: Max Ammo, Infinite Ammo and No Reload.
  • Pick up the watches as you see them. 20 watches give you both time cheats.
  • Leave the Lazer for last. It needs every challenge in the game.

Do cheats work in GTA 6 Online?

No outlet has said, and GTA 6 Online isn't out yet (see what Rockstar has confirmed about Online). In GTA 5, cheats only ever worked in story mode, and that's very likely here too (THEORY).
