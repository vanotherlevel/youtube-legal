GTA 6's main collectibles are 18 sets of collectible cards (480 cards), WAiNK phone scans of landmarks and street art, 125 Cocaine Flamingos, 33 underwater discoveries and 397 stunts. On top of that you can study 255 animals with binoculars, catch 35 fish species and hunt for Bret Vogel's 15 Target Decks. Those counts come from PowerPyx's launch-day progress database. Rockstar hasn't published its own list.

This page explains how each type works, which ones count toward trophies and 100%, and what we saw on screen in the launch streams: the animal notification setting, a tiger shark you fight with a speargun, and the fishing-themed gun shop. Exact locations for every item are a separate job. Where we don't know a spot yet, we say full map coming.

Labels on this page: ON SCREEN = seen in a launch stream (MELOO's, Dalek's or GTASeries'), read at full size. REPORTED = launch-day guides (mainly PowerPyx, plus GTABase and The Big Lead). OUR TAKE = our own read. Not seen yet = not confirmed yet.

The game tracks all of it for you (settings we saw)

ON SCREEN: seen in GTASeries' launch stream, Settings > UI (about 3:15 in)

GTA 6 has a PROGRESS NOTIFICATIONS block in the UI settings, with a toggle for each type: Weapons, Vehicles, Animals, Collections and Stunts. The help text for Animals reads: "Displays a notification when a new animal is discovered." On that stream, Animals, Weapons and Vehicles were switched Off, while Collections and Stunts were On. We don't know if that's the default or the streamer's choice.

OUR TAKE: turn Animals and Vehicles on early. Studying animals and scanning cars are the two grinds people forget, and a pop-up for every new one tells you instantly when you've found something you still need.

To check progress, PowerPyx points to the pause menu: Progress > Vehicles / Animals for discoveries, Progress > Activities > Stunts for stunts, and the Collections list for cards.

Collectible cards (18 sets, 480 cards)

REPORTED (PowerPyx, All Collectible Card Sets, Nov 18)
  • Where they are: inside buildings, usually lying on surfaces or stuck to walls. Outside, look in yellow phone boxes on walls and toolboxes.
  • Colour versions count: each colour or tier of the same card (Bronze/Silver/Gold, or colour variants) is a separate card for set completion.
  • You can't buy or farm them. Cards only come from pickups, so the supply is limited.
  • Sell duplicates: any Pawn Shop buys cards, from $20 to over $1,000 each. Selling doesn't remove a card from your Collections list, so you can sell before a set is finished.
  • How many you need: 3 sets for Don't Touch My Deck, 15 sets for 100%. Fifteen sets means at least 372 of the 480 cards, depending on which sets you pick.

WAiNK scans, flamingos, meteorites and the rest

REPORTED (PowerPyx trophy guide and database; The Big Lead collectibles guide, Nov 18)
  • WAiNK scans: WAiNK is the scanner app on Jason and Lucia's phone. Hold D-Pad Up to open it fast. You scan 31 Landmarks, 35 Curiosities, 23 Sculptures and 23 No Flusher Tags, and any 100 of them unlock You WAiNKer! (PowerPyx: "it doesn't matter which 100").
  • Styles from magazines: The Big Lead says magazines inside homes let you WAiNK-scan new makeup, hairstyles and tattoos (examples it names: Sunset Blush, Goth Garnet). PowerPyx counts 79 Styles, including 34 Luxury Watches.
  • Cocaine Flamingos (125): found in gardens and on beaches across several regions. GTABase's 100% checklist asks you to destroy 100.
  • Meteorite chunks and shards: The Big Lead found them on beaches, under beds, under piers and underwater, and says they glow in the dark, so search at night. A full count isn't confirmed yet.
  • Underwater discoveries (33): all 33 are needed for 100%, per GTABase. OUR TAKE: expect to dive for these, so read the shark section below first.
  • Unique animals to scan: The Big Lead lists a Festive Poodle and a Cartoon Labrador as unique scannable animals. Where they are: full map coming.

Animals: how to study all 255

REPORTED (PowerPyx, How to Study Animals & Scan Vehicles, Nov 18)
  1. Equip the binoculars from the weapon wheel.
  2. Zoom in with D-Pad Up.
  3. Hold R1 / RB on the animal to study it. It's then logged under Progress > Animals.

You don't have to kill or skin anything, unlike Red Dead Redemption 2. Every breed of dog and cat counts as its own entry. You need 100 of the 255 for 100% completion, and PowerPyx says that's enough for every trophy too.

Fishing: how it works

REPORTED (PowerPyx trophy guide and database; GTABase 100% checklist)
  • Cal is your fishing teacher. After the Chapter 1 main mission Feng Shui (Meet Cal), Cal texts you a fishing spot, marked with a purple icon on the map. Follow his fishing questline and beat him in a challenge for Baited My Tackle.
  • 35 fish species are listed in the database, both under Animals and under Fishing.
  • For 100%: there's no separate fish count. GTABase says Cal's fishing challenges count toward the 100 optional tasks on the checklist.
  • Just Like Paradise (the hidden fishing trophy): PowerPyx says it pops automatically when you finish Gone Fishing, the first main mission of Chapter 1. You can't miss it.
REPORTED (pre-launch: Rockstar's Rob Nelson, as reported by GameSpot)

Fishing is catch and release. You don't keep or sell the fish. It's there for fun, challenges and the checklist, not money. For money, see hunting below or our money guide.

The fishing control setting

ON SCREEN: seen in MELOO's launch stream, Settings > Gameplay > Accessibility (about 5:53 in)

Settings has a Fishing option with 3 choices. It was set to Rotate (1 of 3) on MELOO's stream and on GTASeries'. Per Game8's settings guide (REPORTED), the three ways to reel are rotating the right stick, holding or tapping Square (X on Xbox), or pulling the right stick back. If stick-spinning hurts your thumb, change it before Cal's challenges.

Chuck's Tackle & Shot (it's a gun shop)

ON SCREEN: seen in MELOO's launch stream (about 45:30 to 47:00 in); also Ben-Gun's

An early story stop has a giant leaping bass on its sign: Chuck's Tackle & Shot. Don't be fooled by the name. The banner under the sign says it's fully stocked and supplied by Ammu-Nation, the tip on screen says "Purchase, modify, and manage your weapons at Ammu-Nations across Leonida", and inside it's a gun counter with rifles on the wall. We haven't seen fishing gear for sale there yet. The story itself opens with fishing jokes: on the boat, a sticker reads "BORN TO FISH / FORCED TO WORK!" and Cal says "Fishing. I love fishing."

Hunting: Bret Vogel and the 15 Target Decks

REPORTED (PowerPyx, Veteran Hunted (Meet Bret) walkthrough and trophy guide, Nov 18)

Hunting is Jason-only and comes from Bret Vogel, an old army friend who runs a hunting business called Killtelligence. Some time after the Chapter 2 mission Coffee Break (while you're saving $15,000 for "Prepare for Vice City"), Bret texts Jason and marks Veteran Hunted (Meet Bret) in the far north of Lummox County. Lucia can't be with you.

  1. Track: press L3 + R3 to see a white scent line. It turns yellow as you get close.
  2. Scout: look through a sniper scope or binoculars and hold R1 to study the animal.
  3. Shoot clean: one shot to the heart or head with the hunting rifle keeps the carcass in "pristine condition". Extra shots lower the quality.
  4. Haul it: pick up the carcass, load it into Bret's truck, then unload it at the processing area.
  5. Get paid: the first job pays $500 (via BuckMe) and the unique weapon variant Killtelligence SM-69.

After that, Bret marks more hunts with purple icons, each one after a specific animal. Track them under Map > Activities > Target Deck. There are 15 Target Decks, all 15 are needed for 100%, and Fair Game (hunt a unique animal for Bret) comes along the way. Which animal is the "unique" one isn't confirmed yet. Sharks: yes, and you fight them with a speargun

ON SCREEN: seen in MELOO's launch stream (about 2:07:45 in) and Dalek's (about 3:19:26 in)

On a story dive with a group of divers, a tiger shark starts circling over the reef. MELOO's subtitles read "Uh... is that a shark?", Jason has a speargun out (ammo counter 1 / 8), and a "SHARK" (L2) prompt appears to aim at it. Another diver yells at him to use the speargun. Dalek's stream shows the same tiger shark among schools of red fish, with the line "Yeah, shoot it, Jason!" The dive HUD shows an oxygen bar top left and DIVE (R1) / SURFACE (X) prompts.

What we don't know yet: whether sharks attack you in free roam, whether you can hunt them for Bret, and which of the 18 Aquatic animals they count as. The 33 underwater discoveries mean you'll be in shark water sooner or later, so OUR TAKE: keep a speargun handy when you dive for them.

Stunts (397)

REPORTED (PowerPyx trophy guide, Nov 18)

Stunts come in four kinds: 130 Stunt Jumps, 202 Under the Bridges, 40 Knife Flights and 25 Base Jumps. Any 250 unlock Cunning Stunts and 300 count for 100%, and you can mix categories. PowerPyx says Under the Bridges are the quickest. The Stunts notification (On in the stream we saw) tells you each time one counts.

Where to start

OUR TAKE
  1. Chapter 1: turn on the Animals and Vehicles notifications, put the binoculars on your weapon wheel, and do the meditation at Jason's house (it may be missable).
  2. After Feng Shui: start Cal's fishing questline as soon as the purple icon shows up.
  3. Every new area: pick up the cards you pass, WAiNK anything that looks like a landmark, and scan cars at busy junctions.
  4. Chapter 2 onward: take Bret's hunts with Jason as they come; they're needed for 100% and they pay.
  5. Endgame: finish stunts (Under the Bridges first), flamingos and underwater discoveries by plane and boat.

Frequently asked questions

How many collectibles are in GTA 6?

There's no single number. PowerPyx's launch database lists 480 collectible cards in 18 sets, 112+ WAiNK scan spots, 125 Cocaine Flamingos, 33 underwater discoveries, 27 magazines and books, 18 meditation spots and 397 stunts, plus 255 animals to study and 557 vehicles to scan.

How do you study animals in GTA 6?

Equip the binoculars, zoom in with D-Pad Up and hold R1 (RB on Xbox) on the animal. You don't need to kill it. There are 255 animals and 100% needs 100, per PowerPyx.

How do you unlock fishing in GTA 6?

After the Chapter 1 mission Feng Shui (Meet Cal), Cal texts you a fishing spot marked with a purple icon. His questline leads to the fishing challenge for the Baited My Tackle trophy. Fishing is catch and release.

How do you unlock hunting in GTA 6?

Some time after the Chapter 2 mission Coffee Break, Bret Vogel texts Jason and marks Veteran Hunted (Meet Bret) in northern Lummox County. Only Jason can hunt. There are 15 Target Decks, and all of them are needed for 100%.

Are there sharks in GTA 6?

Yes. Launch streams from MELOO and Dalek show a tiger shark circling Jason on a story dive, with a speargun and a SHARK aim prompt. Whether sharks attack in free roam isn't confirmed yet.
