GTA 6 Criminal Profile Explained: Ranks and How to Stay Clean

The Criminal Profile is GTA 6's record of how Jason and Lucia commit crimes, and it is separate from your wanted stars. Careful, low-violence play keeps a character Professional; heavy violence pushes them toward Irredeemable, which reportedly cannot be undone.

REPORTED: rank names, triggers and effects below come mainly from PowerPyx's guide dated Nov 18. Rockstar has not published a rulebook, and the game may be patched.

Why Rockstar added it

In an interview with Eurogamer, as reported by Dexerto on Nov 26, Rockstar head of development Rob Nelson said the team wanted what you do in the open world to sit closer to how Jason and Lucia are portrayed in the story. He described wanting to build up the sophistication of the NPC population while allowing more player choice.

His example was a robbery: "you don't have to beat them to death to get their money." Dexerto's write-up says the studio built on Red Dead Redemption 2's Honor system, and that player opinion on the new system has been mixed. Pre-launch coverage also reported Rockstar stressing that the profile tracks how you operate and is not a morality system.

The ranks, in order

RankWhat pushes you there (PowerPyx)
ProfessionalThreaten staff without hurting anyone, destroy the security tapes, avoid or disable alarms, and leave before police arrive
HardenedShoot enemies in the legs or shoulders without killing them
ViolentHeadshot kills, finishing off downed enemies, killing civilians or police
PsychoKilling dogs or cats, killing police and civilians in large numbers, and some especially brutal acts
IrredeemableContinuing Psycho-level violence for long enough without moving back toward Professional

Outlets have not all used the same names. Some early reports listed an "Aggressive" state and did not mention every rank above, so treat the in-game label in your own menu as the final word.

What your rank changes

Police

PowerPyx reports that at Psycho and Irredeemable, police are more likely to open fire on sight and you can no longer surrender. That turns a small mistake into a full chase or a restart.

Focus

Focus is the slow-motion aiming meter. The game itself tells you that thoughtful actions raise your Focus bar depending on your Criminal Profile. PowerPyx says Professional and Hardened characters build it with stealth takedowns, Violent and Psycho characters with headshots, and Irredeemable has no passive way to grow it.

Between Professional and Psycho, PowerPyx describes few other direct gameplay effects and calls the profile mostly a way of tracking your habits.

A wanted level is not the same thing

ON SCREEN: in-game hint, seen in a launch stream

During the impound mission the game says: "Getting a wanted level doesn't affect your criminal profile, but how you choose to deal with the cops will." Stealing a car and driving away is a very different choice from shooting every officer at the gate. For losing stars, use our wanted level and police guide.

How to check your profile

  1. Open the map.
  2. Press L1 (LB on Xbox) to move to the Jason & Lucia tab.
  3. Select a character. The current rating is shown at the top.

PowerPyx also says holding right on the D-pad during play shows the profile icon in the bottom-right corner. Jason and Lucia are tracked separately, so check both.

How to stay Professional

  • Threaten, don't attack. Keeping staff and bystanders unharmed is the core of a clean robbery.
  • Deal with the cameras. Destroy the tapes in the store's backroom. PowerPyx says getting in needs the Snap Gun, unlocked in Chapter 3.
  • Handle the alarm, and leave before police get there.
  • Use stealth takedowns where you can. They also build Focus at this rank.
  • Run instead of fighting police when escaping is possible.

Step-by-step robbery help, including safes, is in our safe codes and robberies guide.

Before you go on a rampage

Irredeemable is reported to be permanent. At least one YouTube title claims a player undid it; we have not verified that, so do not count on it. If you want to cause chaos, make a manual save first and keep it untouched, so you can return to a character who can still surrender. Our saving guide explains manual slots and autosave.

THEORY (our advice)

On a first playthrough, aim to keep at least one character careful. You keep the option to surrender, your options for building Focus stay open, and you avoid locking yourself out of anything that depends on a clean profile. Our endings guide (full spoilers) lists Professional profiles among the conditions for one ending.

Does it make GTA 6 less fun?

That depends on how you play. The profile does not stop you doing anything; it makes the game respond to a pattern. If you enjoy planning a quiet score, it rewards that. If you mostly want chaos, the main cost shows up late: police who shoot first and a surrender option that disappears. Keeping a spare save is the simplest way to enjoy both.

Frequently asked questions

What are the Criminal Profile ranks in GTA 6?

PowerPyx lists five: Professional, Hardened, Violent, Psycho and Irredeemable, tracked separately for Jason and Lucia.

Can you reset an Irredeemable profile?

PowerPyx reports it is permanent. Keep an earlier manual save if you want a way back.

Does a wanted level change your Criminal Profile?

Not by itself. The in-game hint says how you choose to deal with the police does.

How do I see my Criminal Profile?

Open the map, press L1 or LB to reach the Jason & Lucia tab, and select a character.
