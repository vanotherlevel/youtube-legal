What's NOT in GTA 6: Darts, Bowling, Disney World & Why Rockstar Cut Them

GTA 6 has no darts and no bowling, and Leonida has no version of Disney World, Universal Studios or Orlando. Rockstar has confirmed all of these on the record. Its developers say the cuts were deliberate: a feature that can't reach their standard gets left out, because, as GTA 6 producer Rob Nelson told VGC, "We don't have unlimited time."

Now that the game is out (November 19, 2026), players are finding out what made it in and what didn't. Some missing activities are series staples. Darts, for example, was in GTA 4 and GTA Online. Below is every cut or absent feature that Rockstar's own staff have talked about, who said it, and why. Rumours are labelled separately.

Why there are no darts or bowling in GTA 6

OFFICIAL: Rob Nelson, GTA 6 producer and Rockstar North co-studio head, to VGC (Nov 19, 2026)

Darts is the cut fans keep asking about. Nelson told VGC's Jordan Middler that "the darts one is a contentious one", and explained that even a studio the size of Rockstar has limits. When a feature can't be tuned to the level the team wants, they would rather leave it out than ship a weak version. On darts specifically, he said the team "didn't have the resources", and that this is a factor on every Rockstar game.

VGC notes bowling is also absent. It was one of GTA 4's best-known activities, alongside darts and pool.

  • The bigger reason: starting with Red Dead Redemption 2, Rockstar moved away from separate minigames and towards "activities": things that feel like an extension of the game's normal mechanics and copy the real activity authentically.
  • Examples Nelson gave: weightlifting at the gym, which mimics real lifting, and basketball, where you can run around and shoot hoops but can't play a full game.
  • Pool came up as an example of an activity done properly, with real physics. VGC's report uses it to explain the approach.
  • Chess was mentioned as something that was hard to get into Red Dead Redemption 2, even with Dutch's chessboard in camp. VGC's report doesn't say whether chess is in GTA 6.

If you want to know what you can do between missions, our GTA 6 activities and minigames list covers every side activity found so far, and the 100% completion checklist shows which ones count.

Is Disney World or Orlando in GTA 6?

OFFICIAL: Paul MacPherson, Rockstar senior VP of environment art, to Game Informer (Sep 29, 2026), via Dexerto

No. Leonida is based on Florida, but Rockstar confirmed it has no version of Orlando or its big theme parks, Walt Disney World and Universal Studios. Game Informer asked whether central Florida would appear in some form. MacPherson explained that the team picks locations by balancing landscapes, towns and landmarks against what the game needs, and said "everything we have is geared to getting that full Florida experience!"

So the map you get is Vice City (Rockstar's Miami), the Leonida Keys, the Grassrivers wetlands, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia and Mount Kalaga National Park. There's no inland theme-park city. Our map regions guide walks through all six areas.

Kotaku's launch-day live blog summed up both cuts the same way: Disney World and darts were left out for narrative reasons and for time.

Why you can't restock your fridge in GTA 6

OFFICIAL: Rob Nelson to Famitsu, reported by GamesRadar and GamingBolt (Aug 31, 2026)

At one point in development, GTA 6 had a system where you could buy groceries in stores and stock Jason and Lucia's fridge to use later. Rockstar removed it. Nelson told Famitsu it was cut "because this would have complicated the mechanics", and that the studio is always balancing realism against how easy the game is to play.

Food and healing still exist. They just don't need grocery runs. See how healing works in GTA 6 for health, armor and consumables.

Locked for now: casinos, zoo and graveyard

OFFICIAL: Rockstar has called these areas 'future' content (see our full report)

Some places aren't cut so much as closed. Players found locked casinos, a zoo and a graveyard on the map, and Rockstar's answer pointed to the future. We've collected everything on that in GTA 6 locked casinos, zoo and graveyard, and every expansion tease in our GTA 6 DLC page.

Not missing, just later: PC

GTA 6 launched on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only. A PC version hasn't been dated by Rockstar. Our GTA 6 PC release date page tracks the reports and what's actually confirmed.

Will darts or bowling be added later?

THEORY: our read, not confirmed by Rockstar

Rockstar hasn't said. Nelson's comments were about not finishing those activities to standard for launch, not about ruling them out forever. GTA Online has added darts before, and Rockstar has already pointed to future content for the locked areas. So a later update or the online mode adding them isn't impossible. Treat any claim that darts or bowling are "coming" as a rumour until Rockstar announces it.

What Rockstar's cuts tell you about GTA 6

All three explanations point the same way. Rockstar would rather have fewer things that all feel real than a long checklist of mini-games. That's why the gym makes you actually lift, the basketball court is for shooting around, and grocery shopping was cut once it started getting in the way. If something you loved from GTA 4 or GTA 5 is missing, that's most likely the reason.

Frequently asked questions

Is there darts in GTA 6?

No. GTA 6 producer Rob Nelson told VGC darts was left out because the team didn't have the resources to make it meet Rockstar's standard.

Can you go bowling in GTA 6?

No. VGC reports bowling, a GTA 4 favourite, isn't in GTA 6 either.

Is Disney World in GTA 6?

No. Rockstar's Paul MacPherson told Game Informer that Orlando, Walt Disney World and Universal Studios have no version in Leonida.

Can you play basketball in GTA 6?

You can shoot hoops and run around the court, but Rob Nelson says full basketball games aren't playable.

Why can't you buy groceries in GTA 6?

Rockstar removed a fridge-restocking system during development because it would have complicated the mechanics, Rob Nelson told Famitsu.
