Cyberleek Claims a 209 GB GTA 6 Xbox Build Leak: What's Real

By vAnother Level · Updated 2026-11-20

Leak group Cyberleek claims it released a 209 GB Xbox build of GTA 6 on November 19, but without the executable, so it can't be played. No outlet has verified the files, Rockstar hasn't commented, and this is not a PC version of GTA 6. Cyberleek says it will only release the missing file when a countdown runs out or its crypto token hits a target.

REPORTED (Neowin, Notebookcheck, Wolf's Gaming Blog, Nov 19): Cyberleek's claims, not confirmed by Rockstar or Take-Two

Neowin reports that Cyberleek also said it may go after a PS5 build if the Xbox one can't be made to work. Several outlets say the countdown points to around November 23. We don't link to the files and won't.

Is it real?

Nobody has confirmed it. Neowin and Notebookcheck call the build "reportedly" and "allegedly" leaked. Wolf's Gaming Blog says people who checked the files told it they look genuine apart from the missing EXE, but the writer didn't download them. Screenshots of a file structure are circulating on social media, and those alone don't prove anything.

THEORY: even if the files are real, a build without its executable isn't a playable leak

Can you play GTA 6 on PC now?

No. GTA 6 launched on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only, and Rockstar hasn't announced a PC version. Notebookcheck points out there's no working Xbox Series X emulator, because the console runs its own custom system. Wolf's Gaming Blog adds that recompiling a console game for PC usually needs code the leak doesn't include. Our GTA 6 PC release date page covers what's actually been reported about a PC launch.

Who is Cyberleek?

  • Early October: the group posted roughly 25 minutes of GTA 6 footage it said came from a full build (Wolf's Gaming Blog).
  • October: it threatened to release a fully playable build if its meme-coin hit a $30M market cap. It also demanded a physical disc release and an apology over digital-only plans (Neowin).
  • October 14: after the coin missed the target, it posted "The End of the Road", saying the leaks would stop "for now" (Wolf's Gaming Blog).
  • October: Kaspersky documented a CyberLeek shop selling a "full game" for $1,000 or a 24-hour demo for $100 (via Digital Trends).
  • Now: the target is cut to $15M. Cyberleek's own line is that "30 million was too ambitious".
REPORTED (Neowin): the token's creator reportedly cashed out over $250,000 in trading fees in August, crashing its value

Stay safe: don't download it, don't buy the coin

Every outlet covering this urges caution, and Wolf's Gaming Blog says plainly not to give the group money. Fake GTA 6 PC installers, "pre-loads" and cracks are already a major malware trap: Kaspersky counted 27,416 GTA VI-themed malware and unwanted-software detections between June and November 2026. A 209 GB torrent of unknown origin is exactly the kind of file attackers use. Downloading pirated games is also illegal in most countries.

Our fake GTA 6 PC and malware warning page lists the scams to watch for. In separate news, the hacker behind the 2022 GTA 6 leak is due to be sentenced on November 23. See our Arion Kurtaj page. More launch-week stories are on our GTA 6 news hub.

Frequently asked questions

Did GTA 6 leak on Xbox?

Leak group Cyberleek claims it released a 209 GB November 2026 Xbox build of GTA 6 on November 19. No outlet has verified it, and the executable is missing, so it can't be played.

Is the Cyberleek GTA 6 leak playable?

No. Cyberleek held back the executable and says it will release it when a countdown ends or its crypto token reaches a $15M market cap. Nothing playable has been shown.

Can I play GTA 6 on PC with the leaked build?

No. GTA 6 is out only on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, there's no working Xbox Series X emulator, and Rockstar hasn't announced a PC version.

Is it safe to download the GTA 6 leak?

No. Torrents and fake installers are a common way malware spreads, and Kaspersky has logged over 27,000 GTA VI-themed malware detections in 2026. Pirating the game is also illegal in most countries.
