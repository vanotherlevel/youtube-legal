GTA 6 Digital Foundry Tech Review: PS5 vs PS5 Pro, 1440p, 30 FPS and the 23 FPS Dip

Digital Foundry says PS5 Pro is "the best way to play" GTA 6 by far. Its tech review, reported on November 18, finds both PS5 and PS5 Pro render at roughly 1440p and target 30 FPS. The Pro holds that 30 almost perfectly, while the base PS5 can fall to 23 FPS in the heaviest explosions.

REPORTED: Digital Foundry's findings as relayed by TweakTown and AltChar, Nov 18 2026

Digital Foundry also called GTA 6 "the single most visually striking game of the generation" and summed up console performance as "both acceptable yet a little disappointing". Here's what that means for you on each console.

The 23 FPS dip, explained

The scary number comes from one stress test: blowing up street vehicles with a grenade launcher. On base PS5 the frame rate dropped to 23 FPS and stayed below 30 until the player left the area or died. Digital Foundry stressed it's a worst case, and average performance sat close to 30. If your plan is mostly chaos and explosions, though, that's the kind of performance you might see.

The Pro was tested for 50+ hours and showed no dips in normal play. In the same explosion scene it touched 24 FPS for a moment before recovering.

Why there's no 60 or 40 FPS mode

In a DF Direct episode the day before, Richard Leadbetter said the game is CPU-bound, not GPU-bound. Leonida is dense and systemic, and the PS5 Pro's CPU is only modestly faster than the base console's. He called 60 FPS "not viable" and said even 40 FPS would need "a huge amount of optimization or cutbacks", because each frame would get 25 ms instead of 33.3 ms.

OFFICIAL: Rockstar's Nov 10 Newswire confirmed a 30 FPS target on every console, PS5 Pro included

We covered that announcement, plus Xbox One cloud play, in GTA 6 is 30 FPS even on PS5 Pro. Want to see what 30 actually feels like? Try our 15 vs 30 vs 60 FPS comparison.

THEORY: a later patch could add a performance mode. Rockstar hasn't said anything about one

What about Xbox?

Update (Nov 18, afternoon): Digital Foundry's Xbox results are now out via Kotaku (REPORTED). Series X runs at 1440p with stability similar to PS5 Pro; Series S runs at 720p and dips to about 25 FPS in combat, 20 in heavy carnage and briefly 18-19 FPS. Rockstar's Marc Chambers also reportedly told Kotaku the PS5 Pro isn't powerful enough for 60 FPS. Full details: GTA 6 Series S vs Series X performance.

Our take: which console should you play on?

  • Have a PS5 Pro? Play there. Cleaner image, extra ray-traced shadows and a much steadier 30.
  • On base PS5? It still looks great and mostly holds 30. Expect a few dips in big fights.
  • On Xbox One or no console? Cloud streaming runs at Series S settings (how it works).

Frequently asked questions

Is GTA 6 better on PS5 Pro?

Yes, per Digital Foundry. Both render at about 1440p and 30 FPS, but the Pro adds PSSR upscaling that looks close to native 4K, ray-traced shadows and a much steadier 30 FPS.

Does GTA 6 drop below 30 FPS on PS5?

Sometimes. Digital Foundry saw small dips in normal play and a worst case of 23 FPS when blowing up cars with a grenade launcher. Average performance was close to 30.

Will GTA 6 get a 60 FPS mode?

Nothing is announced. Digital Foundry says the game is CPU-bound, calling 60 FPS not viable and 40 FPS very hard without big cutbacks.

What resolution is GTA 6 on PS5?

Digital Foundry estimates about 1440p internal on both PS5 and PS5 Pro. Rockstar hasn't published official resolutions.
