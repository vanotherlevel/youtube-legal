GTA 6 DLC and Story Expansions: Everything Confirmed So Far

The only GTA 6 add-on content confirmed so far is the Ultimate Edition ($99.99) and the pre-order bonuses. Rockstar has not announced any story expansion. Here's everything that is confirmed, and what history says about what comes next.

Ultimate Edition content

OFFICIAL: Rockstar store copy
  • The Hawk and Little Morgan revolvers, 'sourced from the Vercetti Estate' (the Tommy Vercetti clues).
  • A '95 Grotti Cheetah, 'an ode to Shore Drive'.
  • A raid on a Southside Vice City gang compound.
  • Wyman's abandoned classic cars to track down and restore (side quests).

Pre-order bonuses

OFFICIAL
  • The Vintage Vice City Pack, including a weapon pattern 'inspired by Tommy Vercetti's iconic palm tree button-up'.
  • A '55 Vapid Stanier and a garage by Ocean Beach.

Will GTA 6 get story DLC?

HISTORY, NOT A PROMISE

GTA IV got two full story episodes (The Lost and Damned, The Ballad of Gay Tony). GTA V got none: its updates went to GTA Online. RDR2 also put its updates into Red Dead Online. So the safe bet is that most new GTA 6 content follows GTA 6 Online, but nothing is announced.

Locked casinos, a zoo and a graveyard (Nov 18)

OFFICIAL quote via Kotaku

Launch reviewers found two locked casinos, a detailed zoo, a large graveyard and a northern town the story never uses. Rockstar VP of design Stephen Donkin told Kotaku these areas "give us options in the future". No DLC is announced. Full story.

Sources: Rockstar Games store and pre-order copy (via Gematsu). We update this page after every Rockstar announcement and title update.

Frequently asked questions

Does GTA 6 have DLC?

So far only the Ultimate Edition and pre-order bonuses. No story expansion has been announced.

What does the GTA 6 Ultimate Edition include?

Extra items such as the Hawk and Little Morgan revolvers, a '95 Grotti Cheetah, a gang compound raid and Wyman's classic car jobs.
