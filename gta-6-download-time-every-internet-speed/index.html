How Long GTA 6 Takes to Download on Every Internet Speed

By vAnother Level · Updated 2026-11-09
Home › GTA 6 Guides

GTA 6 is up to 212 GB, and your internet plan decides whether you're playing at launch or waiting until the weekend. We did the math for every common speed. These are best-case times at your plan's full speed. Real downloads are usually slower because of Wi-Fi, other devices on your network and PlayStation Network traffic on launch week.

212 GB is Rockstar's figure. Times calculated by vAnother Level

The chart

  • 25 Mbps: 18 hours 51 minutes
  • 50 Mbps: 9 hours 25 minutes
  • 100 Mbps: 4 hours 43 minutes
  • 300 Mbps: 1 hour 34 minutes
  • 500 Mbps: 57 minutes
  • 1 Gbps: 28 minutes
  • 2 Gbps: 14 minutes

How we worked it out: 212 GB × 8 = 1,696 gigabits, divided by your speed. For example, 1,696,000 megabits ÷ 100 Mbps = 16,960 seconds, which is 4 hours 43 minutes. One more thing: the PlayStation Store listing that dataminers read shows a 223 GB package, so on PS5 add about 5% to every time.

How to download it faster

  1. Use an Ethernet cable instead of Wi-Fi if you can. It's the biggest free speed-up.
  2. Pause other downloads and streaming in the house while it installs.
  3. Leave the console in Rest Mode overnight with automatic downloads on.
  4. Spectrum customers can get free Gig speeds until November 30: see the Spectrum speed boost.
