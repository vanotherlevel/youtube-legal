The best GTA 6 easter eggs we've seen in the released game so far are a licence plate that reads "ASS MAN", two full video games you can play on Jason's TV (a zombie shooter and a black-and-white puzzle game called Tenebrae Lux), and a prison dialogue wheel with the options Venus, Mars and Uranus. Add neon Vice City-style opening credits, a meditation minigame and a pressure washer, and day one already has plenty of Rockstar's usual jokes.

Everything below comes either from footage we watched at full size in the launch streams (with the stream and the second, so you can check it), or from launch-day reviews and guides that we credit by name. Story spoilers stop at the first chapter. For the rest of our launch coverage, see the GTA 6 guides hub.

Labels on this page: ON SCREEN = we saw it ourselves in a launch stream recording (MELOO, Ben-Gun, Dalek or one more launch stream) and read it at full size. REPORTED = from a review or launch guide by a named outlet (Kotaku, PowerPyx, GTABase, GameSpot and others). THEORY = our own read of what a joke or name refers to. Rockstar hasn't explained any of these.

The opening credits: neon Vice City lettering

ON SCREEN: seen in [Ben-Gun's launch stream](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lOGyysf6pDo), 75-300 s

The game opens with credits laid over the first cutscenes, the way GTA V and Vice City did. The job titles are in a pink handwritten neon script, and the names sit underneath in a chunky pink-and-white font. At 75 s the "Character Art Direction" card glows over Jason's face during the opening police stop, while he says "We were fighting over who was gonna drive."

Between scenes, full-screen collage cards fill silhouettes with Vice City art. Two are worth pausing on:

  • 285 s, "Additional Score: Overmono": the silhouette is a man in a white jacket, filled with palm trees and a neon "OCEAN VIEW" hotel sign. The Ocean View was Tommy Vercetti's first safehouse in Vice City (2002).
  • 240 s, "Open World Writing / In Game Media": the In Game Media list includes DJ Pooh, a long-time Rockstar collaborator since the San Andreas days.

In another launch-night stream we recorded the "Executive Producer: Sam Houser" credit appears over the prison yard full of women in orange "INMATE" jumpsuits, around 390 s.

THEORY: the white-jacket silhouette under the Ocean View sign looks like a deliberate Tommy Vercetti nod. It isn't Tommy, and nobody has confirmed a cameo. We weigh every clue on our page [Is Tommy Vercetti in GTA 6?](/is-tommy-vercetti-in-gta-6-every-clue/).

Prison prologue jokes: Venus, Mars, Uranus

ON SCREEN: seen in [another launch-night stream we recorded](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Tu1GuAAt5mU), 495 s

The first time you get control, Lucia is in her cell with her cellmate Yolanda, who sits doing a puzzle at a blue table. The prompt bottom right first says TALK / YOLANDA. Hold L2 and it opens a choice list: VENUS (triangle), MARS (X), URANUS (square), with YOLANDA underneath. Rockstar used the very first choice in the game for a planet pun.

THEORY: Uranus is the oldest joke in the solar system, and putting it in the first prompt of the game sounds like a statement of intent. We haven't seen the three lines side by side yet. Both Ben-Gun and Dalek just pressed TALK.ON SCREEN: seen in [Ben-Gun's launch stream](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lOGyysf6pDo), 465 s

A few seconds later the guard walks Lucia past cell block A102. In another stream the line just before is "Shit, you haven't heard? Counselor Reigns got canned." Then the subtitle at 465 s reads: "Word is he got caught "counseling" with his hands down an inmate's pants." The quote marks around "counseling" are in the subtitle itself.

The "ASS MAN" licence plate

ON SCREEN: seen in Dalek's launch stream, 3442-3445 s

In the job where Jason steals his red car back from the Hamlet Police Department impound, the rear plate is clear on screen as he shoots his way out: a white Leonida plate that reads ASS MAN, with a pink flamingo between the two words. The objective box at 3445 s reads "Lose the cops by hiding or escaping their search area", with 3 stars and the face, outfit and car icons under them.

The plate shows up again in Ben-Gun's launch stream at 5056 s, when Jason pulls the same car into a Pay 'n' Spray. It rolls out green, and the objective calls the car the Phoenix ("Deliver the Phoenix to Brian's garage", 5072 s). So it isn't a random plate: it's on a story car that every player drives.

THEORY: "ASSMAN" is the vanity plate Kramer ends up with in a famous Seinfeld episode. The '77 Phoenix has always been GTA's take on the Pontiac Firebird Trans Am, the car from Smokey and the Bandit (1977). Both look like nods, and neither has been confirmed.

Two full video games inside GTA 6

In the Chapter 1 mission "Feng Shui" (named on the pause menu in Ben-Gun's stream at 8960 s), Jason and Cal sit on the couch and play games on the TV. The camera cuts to full screen, and you play them yourself.

The zombie shooter: "DAY 54" and perk cards

ON SCREEN: seen in [Ben-Gun's launch stream](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lOGyysf6pDo) (9064-9076 s), Dalek's launch stream (13374-13392 s) and [MELOO's launch stream](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=akMuwfhCOQg) (9285 s)

It's a top-down survival shooter set on a burning city street at night. The intro card reads "DAY 54 / KILL ZOMBIES TO GET XP AND UPGRADE YOUR CHARACTER." It has its own HUD (health, stamina, LV.6, a 17:59 countdown), then the objective "COLLECT 6 GEARS" comes up.

Tenebrae Lux: a moody puzzle game with a lighthouse

ON SCREEN: seen in [Ben-Gun's launch stream](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lOGyysf6pDo) (8740 s) and [MELOO's launch stream](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=akMuwfhCOQg) (8700-8740 s)

MELOO's stream shows how it starts: Cal says "Okay. Yeah, let's just play the game." Next comes a loading screen that says "USE CLEF HEADPHONES FOR THE BEST EXPERIENCE" (in-world product placement inside a game inside a game), then a grainy black-and-white title screen: "JOHANNES ZUCH PRESENTS / TENEBRAE LUX", with a lighthouse on a cliff and NEW GAME / CONTROLS. A "GØITR VISIONS" studio logo plays before it. The controls card mentions inspecting objects and "panel puzzles".

REPORTED: a "Tenebrae Lux" poster was already spotted in pre-launch material (MovieWeb), which some fans linked to Clair Obscur: Expedition 33THEORY: Tenebrae Lux reads as a jab at arty, auteur-led indie games: Latin title, "presents" credit, lighthouse, black and white.

Minigames that are basically jokes

Meditation

ON SCREEN: seen in [Ben-Gun's launch stream](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lOGyysf6pDo), 16580 s

Jason sits cross-legged on the dock at sunrise while a pink dream filter washes over the screen. He says "Okay. My mind is clear. I am at peace." You use L and R to "center your attention", and L2 and R2 to "let go of the thought". About 20 minutes later in Ben-Gun's playthrough (17822 s), a deal on an airport runway turns into a shootout.

REPORTED: PowerPyx lists 18 meditation spots as a collectible

Power-washing

ON SCREEN: seen in Dalek's launch stream, 21094-21100 s

At the boat yard, Jason pressure-washes a filthy speedboat on its trailer, hull number LN 7402 27. The prompts read "Hold L2 to aim. Hold R2 to begin washing", and the grime strips off in real time to show the white and teal paint underneath. The boat is a Shitzu with "Squa..." on the side.

THEORY: Shitzu is GTA's long-running boat brand, and the Squalo is a speedboat name that goes back to Vice City. It's also PowerWash Simulator inside GTA.

Shooting champagne bottles off a yacht

ON SCREEN: seen in [Ben-Gun's launch stream](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lOGyysf6pDo) (19588-19594 s) and [MELOO's launch stream](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=akMuwfhCOQg) (20140-20145 s)

At a yacht party off the Vice City skyline, bottles are thrown into the air and Lucia shoots them with a pistol. The objective reads "Shoot the champagne bottles", with a focus prompt. It's the image at the top of this page.

New finds since launch (updated Nov 20)

Two new easter eggs went around on launch day: an RX-Men comic that turns Marvel's X-Men into a drug-ad parody, and a Western novel in a Millport bar that ties GTA 6 to Red Dead Redemption. Neither has been confirmed by Rockstar, and we haven't filmed either one yet, so both are labelled REPORTED.

RX-Men #13: the X-Men parody comic

REPORTED: ComingSoon and SuperHeroHype (Nov 19-20), plus Fiction Horizon

According to ComingSoon, players found a comic on sale in the game called RX-MEN #13, from a publisher named ED Comics, priced at $1.25. The joke is in the title: the X becomes Rx, the prescription symbol. Reports say the heroes include Capsule, Side Effect, Diagnosis and Mr. I (a play on MRI), and the villain is Dr. Placebo.

  • Where: the magazine rack inside the 24/7 store in Key Lento, Mariana County (Fiction Horizon).
  • Timing: it's doing the rounds just as the live-action X-Men return in Avengers: Doomsday.

A Red Dead Redemption book in Millport

REPORTED: GameSpot's GTA 6 easter eggs guide (one outlet so far)

GameSpot found a readable Western novel inside Jacob's Tavern in Millport. Walk up to it and interact to read excerpts. The book is about a cowboy in New Austin, the region from Red Dead Redemption. GTA and Red Dead have dropped nods to each other before, and GameSpot reads this as another sign that the two series share one universe. Other guides say there are more copies around the map.

THEORY: a book is a safe way to nod to Red Dead without any direct crossover. Don't expect John Marston or Arthur Morgan to show up.

  • Snapmatic is back as the in-game Instagram.

  • Under the Bridges (202) and Knife Flights (40), GTA V's stunt-flying collectibles, return (PowerPyx).

  • 125 Cocaine Flamingos to collect. THEORY: Vice City's hidden packages, given a Florida twist.
  • Vercetti Estate: Rockstar's official Ultimate Edition text says the Hawk & Little Morgan revolvers are "sourced from the Vercetti Estate". More on our Tommy Vercetti clue page.

Crazy NPC details

ON SCREEN: seen in Dalek's launch stream, 3445 s, and [Ben-Gun's launch stream](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lOGyysf6pDo), 5072 s

During a police chase, the wanted HUD shows the stars plus three red icons (a clothes hanger, a person, a car). The cops know your outfit, your face and your car separately, and you have to change each one to shake them. Reviewers describe the rest of the crowd system:

REPORTED: The Outer Haven, Kotaku, PSU and Digitally Downloaded reviews (Nov 18)
  • NPCs comment on your outfit, and tell you that you smell or look a mess if you skip showers or sleep for days (The Outer Haven).
  • You can Greet or Taunt anyone. In tense moments the options become Defuse / Provoke. One reviewer greeted a sleeping man, who woke up and started a fight (The Outer Haven).
  • Police respond only when someone witnesses or reports a crime, or a camera catches it. Witnesses give descriptions, and you can delete a store's security tapes so the police have nothing (Kotaku).
  • A random event has a polar bear escape from a zoo truck after protesters stop it. It roams the city, and the radio gives updates later (Kotaku, Digitally Downloaded).
  • An alligator turned up to eat a body left nearby (PSU). Kotaku saw a giant snake carrying a dead body in the swamp.
THEORY: myth check: "NPCs film you on their phones" and "NPCs remember you days later" come from fan sites and old leaks. No review we read describes either, and Rockstar says the game uses no generative AI for NPC chat. We'll test both on camera

Frequently asked questions

Is there an X-Men easter egg in GTA 6?

Yes, reportedly. Players found a comic called RX-Men #13 (ED Comics, $1.25), a pharmaceutical parody of X-Men with heroes like Capsule and Side Effect and a villain called Dr. Placebo. It's reported on the magazine rack of the 24/7 store in Key Lento (ComingSoon).

Is there a Red Dead Redemption easter egg in GTA 6?

GameSpot reports a readable Western novel about a cowboy in New Austin, Red Dead's region, inside Jacob's Tavern in Millport.

What is the ASS MAN licence plate in GTA 6?

It's the rear plate on Jason's red Imponte '77 Phoenix, seen clearly in the police impound job (Dalek's launch stream, 3442 s): a Leonida plate reading ASS MAN with a flamingo in the middle. Rockstar hasn't explained it. Our guess is a nod to the Seinfeld "ASSMAN" plate.

Can you play video games inside GTA 6?

Yes. In the Chapter 1 mission "Feng Shui", Jason plays a top-down zombie shooter with perk cards ("DAY 54 / KILL ZOMBIES TO GET XP...") and a black-and-white puzzle game called Tenebrae Lux on the TV. We saw both in Ben-Gun's and MELOO's launch streams. PowerPyx also lists 4 arcade video games as a collectible.

Is Tommy Vercetti in GTA 6?

Not as far as anyone has seen. No review mentions him. The clues are the official "Vercetti Estate" revolver text and Vice City callbacks like the Ocean View hotel card in the opening credits. Our Tommy Vercetti page goes through every clue.

What do Venus, Mars and Uranus mean in GTA 6?

They're the options on the first dialogue wheel in the prologue, when Lucia talks to her cellmate Yolanda. It's the new interaction system, with a planet pun (Uranus) on its very first prompt.
