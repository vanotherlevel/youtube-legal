GTA 6 has two editions: the Standard Edition at $79.99 and the Ultimate Edition at $99.99, both on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. There's no Deluxe or Collector's Edition. Every pre-order, Standard or Ultimate, also gets the free Vintage Vice City Pack, and digital pre-orders on PlayStation Store or Microsoft Store add one free month of GTA+.

So the real question is whether the extra $20 for Ultimate is worth it. Below is everything in each edition, item by item, what the pre-order bonus actually gives you, the deadline, and what's still unconfirmed. Every claim is labelled and sourced at the bottom. What's in the GTA 6 Standard Edition

OFFICIAL

The Standard Edition is the full game: Jason and Lucia's whole story, all of Leonida, every mission and activity that isn't tied to the Ultimate extras. Nothing story-related is locked behind the more expensive edition. Rockstar describes the Ultimate items as extras "threaded across" the story, not extra story.

It costs $79.99 in the US (other regions vary by store). You can buy it digitally on PlayStation Store or Microsoft Store, or as a physical box that holds a download code, not a disc. Both versions can pre-load, so you're ready to play the second the game unlocks. If you're still pre-loading, check our download time at every internet speed.

Everything in the GTA 6 Ultimate Edition

OFFICIAL: item list from Rockstar's announcement, via Gematsu and Game Informer

For $99.99 you get the game plus a big bundle of extras. Here's every item Rockstar listed, grouped so it's easier to judge:

Vehicles

  • '95 Grotti Cheetah, a classic sports car
  • '67 Vapid Dominator Buggy plus a garage
  • Shitzu Squalo speedboat
  • Jason's Safehouse vehicles: a Dinka Enduro motorcycle and a Crest Kayak
  • Ganado Retro Build mod kit
  • Classic Car Collection special commission (Rockstar's art calls it the Wyman car collection)

Weapons

  • Hawk & Little Morgan Revolvers
  • Personalized pistols for Jason and Lucia (Game Informer names them as the Girardi ES9 and Klose K17)

We cover both in our every new GTA 6 weapon guide and how carrying guns works in the GTA 6 gun system explained.

Shops and places (Ultimate only)

  • Rideout Customs, a mod shop
  • One-Eyed Willie's, a second mod shop
  • Sara's Unisex Salon, with exclusive hairstyles
  • Stock 305, a clothing store
  • Electric Fang Tattoo, with 50+ exclusive tattoos (Game Informer credits the designs to the art duo FAILE)
  • PTT YOUNGIN$ compound, a gang compound with an illegal goods store

Clothes and style

  • Vice City Style: themed outfits, tattoos and looks
  • Goodtime Gear apparel collection

Vintage Vice City Pack: the pre-order bonus

OFFICIAL

Everyone who pre-orders or buys before November 20, 2026 gets the Vintage Vice City Pack, in either edition. It contains:

  • '55 Vapid Stanier sedan plus the Shore Court garage, which comes with a weapon locker and a fencing drop-off
  • Outfits and hairstyles for Jason and Lucia
  • Channel the Original Kingpin, a tropical weapon pattern
REPORTED: deadline

Game Informer reports the cutoff as before November 20, so a launch-day purchase on November 19 should still get it. A few guides say November 19 instead. If you're buying late, check the store listing for the pack before you pay.

The free month of GTA+

OFFICIAL

Digital pre-orders on PlayStation Store or Microsoft Store get one month of GTA+. The perks are for GTA Online (the GTA 5 one): a monthly GTA$500,000 deposit, bonus GTA$ on Shark Cards, free and discounted vehicles, plus the GTA+ Games Library. GTA 6 itself launches as a single-player game, so none of this touches Jason and Lucia's story. More on that in our GTA 6 Online page.

Watch the renewal: after the free month, GTA+ auto-renews at the normal monthly price until you cancel. It's one redemption per account, and it has to be redeemed before an expiry date. Set a reminder if you don't want to pay.

Can you upgrade from Standard to Ultimate?

REPORTED

GTABase reports an Ultimate Edition Upgrade that Standard owners can buy separately at any time, including physical owners after they redeem their code. Rockstar hasn't published an upgrade price that we could find. The gap between the two editions is $20, but don't assume the upgrade costs exactly that until you see it in the store.

Is the GTA 6 Ultimate Edition worth it?

OUR TAKE (opinion, not confirmed)

Everything in Ultimate is extras: cars, a boat, two weapons, outfits, tattoos and five businesses to shop at. None of it is extra story. That makes it a good deal if you:

  • want a fast car, a bike and a boat from the first hours instead of earning them
  • spend a lot of time on customising cars, clothes and tattoos (that's three of the five exclusive shops)
  • like the Morgan revolver and want it early

Skip it if you only care about the story and prefer to earn your garage. Our guess is that most of the vehicles' value fades once the in-game money starts flowing. Our purchases that make you richer page explains how. Either way, buy before November 20 so you don't miss the free Vintage Vice City Pack.

Still unconfirmed

  • Upgrade price: not published by Rockstar yet
  • Ultimate as a physical box: Rockstar only lists the Standard code-in-a-box; GTABase reports Ultimate as digital only
  • PC version: GTA 6 launches on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only, with no PC date announced
  • A Collector's Edition: none announced

Frequently asked questions

How much is GTA 6?

The Standard Edition is $79.99 and the Ultimate Edition is $99.99 in the US, on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.

Is there a GTA 6 Deluxe or Collector's Edition?

No. There are only two editions, Standard and Ultimate. No Deluxe or Collector's Edition has been announced.

Does the GTA 6 physical copy have a disc?

No. The physical Standard Edition is a box with a download code inside, and it can pre-load like the digital version.

What is the GTA 6 pre-order bonus?

The Vintage Vice City Pack: a '55 Vapid Stanier with the Shore Court garage, outfits and hairstyles for Jason and Lucia, and the Channel the Original Kingpin weapon pattern. Digital pre-orders also get one month of GTA+.

Can I still get the Vintage Vice City Pack after launch?

Game Informer reports it's included if you pre-order or buy before November 20, 2026, so a launch-day purchase should still count.
