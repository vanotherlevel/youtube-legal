Huge spoiler warning: this page explains the final mission, the final choice, every ending and the post-credits scene of GTA 6. Stop here if you haven't finished the story.

GTA 6 has at least nine endings on screen so far (counting two epilogue variants), and the director says there are more than ten. Three are decided on the bridge at the end of the final mission, where you pick Swim For It (Triangle) or No Surrender (R2): No Surrender kills Jason and Lucia together, Swim For It usually means Lucia jumps and escapes while Jason goes to prison, and if Jason walks out holding his pistol both are shot. A 4th, Happily Ever After, also comes from Swim For It: Jason is jailed, then two years later a $2.5 million payment buys him out and the family drives off together. The other three split off earlier, at the truck standoff before the final mission, when you wait for Take Deal: the Swamp Ending (two skulls in the swamp), the Sacrifice (Jason dies, Lucia lives) and Defend Jason (both die on the tower roof). The 8th and 9th are a new Two Years Later epilogue, seen on 20 November after No Surrender and after the pistol Swim For It, when $2 million is saved.

Rockstar hasn't explained it

Every player who got Two Years Later also had a strong relationship with Lucia.

Why the final mission happens: Giorgio's hard drive

The ending starts in Chapter 5. Giorgio has a hard drive and tells Jason and Lucia it can get the three of them a pardon "from the very top". In the same chapter, Niko Bellic from GTA IV is waiting in Giorgio's chair: Giorgio lost a shipment worth $2 billion, and Niko warns him he has one more chance. Niko then leaves Leonida with his dog Rocco. Full details are on our Niko Bellic page.

That leaves Giorgio holding the drive, and the final job is Jason and Lucia taking it from him. The mission card is cut off on screen as "The End of the R...".

THEORY: the full mission name is most likely "The End of the Road"

The final mission, step by step

  • The marina: it starts at Brian's marina in the Keys at sunrise. The whole crew is there and Cal has the hacking gear. You pick weapons at the armory and board a helicopter.
  • Giorgio's tower: a curved glass skyscraper in downtown Vice City. You come in from the air at dusk and shoot down the balconies floor by floor, with 5 stars from the first shot.
  • The files: inside the penthouse, the hacker laptop goes onto Giorgio's computer and you defend the room while the download bar fills.
  • The collapse: the tower is hit, the floor gives way and Jason and Lucia wake up in burning rubble. The files are downloaded, but the drive is still in the office, so you go back for it.
  • The escape: you switch between Jason and Lucia covering each other, snipe guards on the next floor, then take one of Giorgio's cars down the tower's spiral ramps while a helicopter shoots at you.
  • The crash and the lift: the car crashes into the building site next door. They ride a construction lift down the outside of the unfinished tower while police search the site.
  • The last drive: a convertible down the Keys highway in the morning, to the house in the Keys. Someone tipped off the police, so it's back onto the bridge, straight into a roadblock.

The final choice: Swim For It or No Surrender

At the roadblock Jason and Lucia kiss, the screen freezes grey and you get two buttons: Triangle = Swim For It, R2 = No Surrender. The trophy list has one called Actions Speak Louder ("choices made, for better or worse").

Listen to the dialogue right before the choice, because it explains the endings. Jason holds up the drive and says Lucia can use it. Lucia answers that the deal was only for her. Jason says at least one of them can make it out.

No Surrender ending: Jason and Lucia die

Press R2 and the world turns purple and slows down. Jason and Lucia go out shooting, standing up in the convertible against the whole roadblock. They break through but they're hit, and the car slams into the guardrail. Jason dies slumped over the wheel, still holding his pistol, and Lucia dies beside him. A deputy says: "Yeah, they're gone."

The credits roll over an aerial shot of the bridge. Between the names, everyone else's life goes on: Lucia's mother at the door with a toddler, Cal on a fishing boat, kids playing in a yard, family photos on a dresser. Our full breakdown: No Surrender ending + post-credits.

Swim For It ending: Lucia escapes, Jason goes to prison

Press Triangle and Jason lays out a plan: don't draw your weapon, get to the edge of the bridge, jump, swim to a sandbar, take a boat and go all the way to Cuba. He gives Lucia his phone, and they walk to the rail with their hands up.

On the count of three Lucia jumps. Jason doesn't. Two deputies grab his legs on the rail and pin him to the road. The last you see of Lucia is her falling toward the water. The sandbar, the boat and Cuba are never shown; they only exist in Jason's plan.

The Swim For It epilogue

  • Lucia signs a deal at a desk with an American flag behind her.
  • Jason gets a mugshot: Vice-Dale Police Department, number 00468723.
  • Lucia packs an orange suitcase and drives north, crying, home to her mother's door.
  • Jason ends up in an orange LDC jumpsuit: a fight in the prison canteen, the prison yard.
  • The last scene: Jason, now with long hair and a beard, reads a book called The Cowboy's Prayer Is To Die Young. A guard says he has a visitor. Behind the glass are Lucia and a young boy, who presses his hand to the glass. Jason presses his against it.

The hidden third ending: Swim For It gone wrong

Sirloin replayed the finale and pressed Swim For It again, but this time Jason's first line was different: "Don't do anything stupid" instead of "don't draw your weapon". Same plan, same sandbar, same boat. Then Jason steps out of the car with one hand up and his pistol still in the other. The deputies open fire. Jason goes down on the road, Lucia is shot in her seat, and a deputy says he's gone. The credits open on the shot-up car with Jason's body on the road.

THEORY: the visible difference between the two Swim For It runs is the gun in Jason's hand. Whether earlier story choices change his line or the outcome is not confirmed

We'll update this page if Rockstar or a datamine explains the trigger.

The 4th ending: the Swamp Ending, and how to get it

The fourth ending was found on 20 November, a day after launch, in BabyZone's playthrough. It is the only ending that never reaches the bridge. It splits off before the final mission: after Lucia breaks Jason out of the precinct, the two of them have one of the task force officers on his knees behind a truck. He offers them a cut of the whole city if they let him live.

How to unlock the Swamp Ending

  1. Play the story until Lucia breaks Jason out of the precinct.
  2. Keep going to the standoff at the truck, right before The End of the Road. The officer is on his knees.
  3. Do not press R2 (Shoot). At first only Shoot is on screen. Hold your fire and wait, it can take up to about 15 seconds.
  4. When TAKE DEAL appears under it, press X / Cross. The story now heads for the swamp, not the bridge.
SEEN IN-GAME: the Shoot / Take Deal prompt and the full Swamp Ending in BabyZone's footageTHEORY: the exact wait before Take Deal shows up may vary; about 15 seconds is what we saw

What happens in the Swamp Ending

Taking the deal puts Jason and Lucia on the same side as the task force that has hunted them since Chapter 1. They gear up at its hangar, pull the hard drive job on the tower, and call Mendez to come and get them. He does come: SWAT drags them out of the crane cage and he tells everyone they are his suspects, saying they are putting on a show "for the optics". That show ends at dawn, in the swamp.

Lucia asks to call their son. Mendez tells them to sit tight, he is taking them right where they need to go. Then the credits roll, and the epilogue finds two skulls in the mud beside an alligator in the Grassrivers swamp. The game never shows the shots. It doesn't need to.

That's why this one hits hardest. Every other ending is decided on the bridge, in one moment. This one is decided at that truck, the first time they trust the wrong cop. The man who killed his own officers in Chapter 1 was never going to let two witnesses walk away. Full breakdown: GTA 6 4th Ending Explained.

THEORY (our take): the Swamp Ending is the worst ending, because it is the only one decided by trusting the task force instead of each other

The 5th ending: Happily Ever After, and how to get it

The fifth ending surfaced on 20 November in Shirrako's playthrough and was confirmed the same evening on Ben-Gun's stream. It starts like the normal Swim For It ending: Lucia jumps and Jason is pinned on the bridge and jailed. Then the screen says Two Years Later. Lucia gets a text from Abe: "It's done." Her bank app shows Payment Sent -$2,500,000, Jason walks out of prison, and Jason, Lucia and their son drive off together at sunset.

How Ben-Gun got Happily Ever After

  • Relationship with Lucia at 4/4 (Hot Together).
  • $2,000,000 in Family Savings.
  • Both criminal profiles at Professional.
  • Shoot the officer at the truck standoff (don't take the deal).
  • At the bridge, pick Swim For It and keep Jason empty-handed.
SEEN IN-GAME: these settings are on screen in Ben-Gun's run, and the ending is shown in full by Shirrako and Ben-GunTHEORY: nobody has confirmed which of these are required. They are what one run had, not an official checklist

The 6th ending: the Sacrifice (Stay Down)

The sixth ending was found by BabyZone. It follows the Take Deal route, the same one as the Swamp Ending, up to the construction tower. There Jason tells Lucia he'll draw them off. He walks out with his hands up but still holding his pistol, asks for the state prosecutor, and the SWAT line shoots him. The game greys out and gives Lucia a new prompt: R2 Defend Jason or Square Stay Down.

Pick Stay Down and Lucia lives. Jason dies on the roof, and the epilogue follows Lucia to her mother in Liberty. It is the only ending so far where Lucia survives free and Jason doesn't. Cal's post-credits scene plays as usual.

SEEN IN-GAME: the Defend Jason / Stay Down prompt and the Sacrifice ending, in BabyZone's footage, plus a second run on N3on's stream

The 7th ending: Defend Jason

The seventh ending is the other side of that prompt, and it was found the same night (BabyZone, uploaded 20 November at 8:19 PM ET). Everything is the same up to the tower roof: Jason surrenders with the pistol and is shot down against the barrier. This time Lucia picks R2 Defend Jason.

  • Lucia gets up with Jason's pistol, and the game hands you control: a short playable shootout against the SWAT line, with the wanted level maxed out.
  • Lucia is hit. Jason, still on the ground, fires one last time.
  • They die together on the roof, Lucia lying across Jason with the pistol still in his hand. A voice on the radio says "Yeah, they're gone."
  • The credits roll over the roof, followed by the same both-dead epilogue as No Surrender (the world goes on without them), then Cal on the dock.
SEEN IN-GAME: the prompt, the shootout and the ending in BabyZone's footage (single source so far)

So the tower prompt decides who lives: Stay Down saves Lucia, Defend Jason means neither of them makes it. In both, Jason dies trying to buy her time.

The 8th ending: No Surrender, Two Years Later

The eighth one is a variant of No Surrender, found in Ben-Gun's upload on 20 November (7:57 PM ET). The finale plays exactly like the normal No Surrender: the purple slow-motion shootout at the roadblock, and Jason and Lucia both dead in the car. The usual epilogue follows (Lucia's mother at the door with the toddler, Cal fishing). Then something new appears in the credits.

  • A TWO YEARS LATER card over the mother's house in autumn (5:24 in Ben-Gun's video).
  • A man in a suit and glasses arrives with a briefcase. The woman raising their son cries and hugs him.
  • They sign papers at the kitchen table, and an older Tommy peeks into the room.
  • A big white house with a SOMMERS REAL ESTATE - SOLD sign, kids running through it, and a family photo taken in the backyard.
  • It ends on a framed photo of Jason and Lucia.
SEEN IN-GAME: the Two Years Later section in Ben-Gun's No Surrender run (single source so far; we checked the frames ourselves)

So even when both of them die, the money they saved can still reach their family. Ben-Gun's save had $2,000,000 in Family Savings and the relationship at 4/4. Two other No Surrender runs did not get this section: Sirloin's had $2M but the relationship at 3/4, and Gamer's Little Playground's had 4/4 but not the $2M.

THEORY: Two Years Later needs BOTH $2M saved and a 4/4 relationship. That fits the three runs we compared, but Rockstar hasn't confirmed it

The 9th ending: Swim For It gone wrong, Two Years Later

The ninth one showed up in Zanar's upload on 20 November (8:48 PM ET), about an hour after the 8th. It plays like the pistol Swim For It ending: Jason steps out of the car with his hands up and the pistol still in his right hand, the deputies fire, Jason dies on the road and Lucia dies in her seat. The usual both-dead epilogue follows. Then the same TWO YEARS LATER card appears (4:09 in Zanar's video).

  • A man in a suit with a briefcase comes to the door, and the woman raising Tommy cries and hugs him.
  • Papers are signed at the table while an older Tommy peeks in.
  • The family moves into a new house with a SOLD sign, and a family photo is taken on a tripod.
  • It ends on a framed photo of Jason and Lucia, then the credits, Cal and STORY COMPLETE.
SEEN IN-GAME: the Two Years Later section after the pistol Swim For It in Zanar's run (single source so far; we checked the frames ourselves)

Zanar's description says the couple had saved over $2 million but their criminal profile was too high, so they die. It also says Tommy grows up with his aunt Lorena Caminos and grandmother Mia Caminos, and the money buys the family a new property. So the Two Years Later section isn't tied to one ending: it has now appeared after two different death endings.

THEORY: any ending where both of them die may get Two Years Later if enough money is saved. Nobody has shown it yet after the Swamp, Sacrifice or Defend Jason endings

Are there more GTA 6 endings?

Almost certainly. Rockstar's head of development Rob Nelson told IGN Spain on 18 November that there are more than ten endings, some only subtly different and others radically different, reportedly tied to neglecting the relationship between Jason and Lucia. Rockstar's English line so far is only "a lot more than two".

REPORTED: "more than ten endings" (Rob Nelson to IGN Spain, a single Spanish-language source)RUMOURS with no footage yet: Jason goes free while Lucia is killed or jailed, and low-relationship or 'Psycho' profile endings. We'll add them only when they are on screen

Cal's post-credits scene

Every ending has the same post-credits scene. Cal sits on a dock in a Hawaiian shirt and yellow cap, waiting for Giorgio's drive to finish decrypting. He opens the files, reads, and says "No. Fucking. Way." before it cuts to black. The game never says what's on the drive.

THEORY: the drive is a setup for future story DLC. See our [GTA 6 DLC page](/gta-6-dlc-story-expansions-what-we-know/)

What happens after the ending

The screen says Story Complete and puts you back before the finale, so you can finish loose ends, go for 100% and replay the choice. The final mission's gold trophy is You Gotta Know When to Get Off. In the No Surrender run we covered, the stats read 100% story but only 59% overall after 39 h 27 min, with $2 million in family savings. The post-game also opens a cheats menu where each cheat is earned with a challenge.

What the endings mean

The clues were there all along. The trophy for 100% completion is called A Long Road, the first trailer used "Love Is a Long Road", and the last mission's trophy is You Gotta Know When to Get Off. In No Surrender neither of them gets off. In Swim For It, Lucia gets off the bridge and Jason doesn't. The book Jason reads in prison, The Cowboy's Prayer Is To Die Young, points straight at the other ending.

Rockstar doesn't mark any ending as canon. Only Swim For It has a next scene, though: a visitor and a hand on the glass. Which one did you pick? Tell us under the video. More story pages are on our GTA 6 story hub.

Frequently asked questions

How many endings does GTA 6 have?

At least nine are on screen so far: No Surrender (both die), Swim For It (Lucia escapes, Jason goes to prison), Swim For It with Jason holding his pistol (both shot), the Swamp Ending, Happily Ever After (Jason bought out two years later), the Sacrifice (Jason dies, Lucia lives), Defend Jason (both die on the tower), and two 'Two Years Later' variants where both die but the money they saved gives their family a new life (after No Surrender and after the pistol Swim For It). Rockstar's Rob Nelson told IGN Spain there are more than ten.

How do you get the Two Years Later ending in GTA 6?

It is a credits section that follows an ending where both of them die. Ben-Gun got it after No Surrender with $2,000,000 in Family Savings and the relationship at 4/4; Zanar got it after the pistol Swim For It with over $2 million saved. No Surrender runs with only $2M or only 4/4 did not get it, so the money is likely the key, together with the relationship, but Rockstar hasn't confirmed it.

How do you get the GTA 6 Swamp Ending (4th ending)?

At the truck standoff after Lucia breaks Jason out of the precinct, don't press R2 to shoot. Wait (up to about 15 seconds) until Take Deal appears, then press X / Cross. The story skips the bridge and ends in the Grassrivers swamp.

What does Defend Jason do in GTA 6?

On the tower roof, after Jason is shot, Lucia gets the prompt R2 Defend Jason or Square Stay Down. Defend Jason starts a short playable shootout, and both of them die on the roof. Stay Down is the Sacrifice ending: Jason dies and Lucia lives.

What is the good ending in GTA 6?

Happily Ever After is the only ending found so far where Jason, Lucia and their son end up free together: Jason is jailed, then bought out for $2.5 million two years later. In Ben-Gun's run it came from a 4/4 relationship, $2M saved, Professional profiles, shooting the officer at the truck and Swim For It without the gun. Rockstar doesn't call any ending canon.

Can you replay the GTA 6 ending?

Yes. After the credits the game returns you to before the finale, so you can replay the final choice.

What does Cal find on the drive?

The game doesn't say. In the post-credits scene Cal decrypts Giorgio's drive, reads it and says "No. Fucking. Way." before it cuts to black.

What is the GTA 6 final mission trophy?

You Gotta Know When to Get Off, a gold trophy that pops when the story is complete.
