Huge spoiler warning: this page explains the final mission, the final choice, every ending and the post-credits scene of GTA 6. Stop here if you haven't finished the story.

GTA 6 has two ending choices and three outcomes. On the bridge at the end of the final mission the game asks you to pick Swim For It (Triangle) or No Surrender (R2). No Surrender kills Jason and Lucia together. Swim For It usually means Lucia jumps and escapes while Jason goes to prison, but in one run Jason walks out holding his pistol and both of them are shot.

SEEN IN-GAME: the choice, all three outcomes, the epilogues and Cal's post-credits scene are all shown in full playthrough footage (MELOO, Sirloin)THEORY: what exactly triggers the third outcome. Rockstar hasn't explained it

Why the final mission happens: Giorgio's hard drive

The ending starts in Chapter 5. Giorgio has a hard drive and tells Jason and Lucia it can get the three of them a pardon "from the very top". In the same chapter, Niko Bellic from GTA IV is waiting in Giorgio's chair: Giorgio lost a shipment worth $2 billion, and Niko warns him he has one more chance. Niko then leaves Leonida with his dog Rocco. Full details are on our Niko Bellic page.

That leaves Giorgio holding the drive, and the final job is Jason and Lucia taking it from him. The mission card is cut off on screen as "The End of the R...".

THEORY: the full mission name is most likely "The End of the Road"

The final mission, step by step

  • The marina: it starts at Brian's marina in the Keys at sunrise. The whole crew is there and Cal has the hacking gear. You pick weapons at the armory and board a helicopter.
  • Giorgio's tower: a curved glass skyscraper in downtown Vice City. You come in from the air at dusk and shoot down the balconies floor by floor, with 5 stars from the first shot.
  • The files: inside the penthouse, the hacker laptop goes onto Giorgio's computer and you defend the room while the download bar fills.
  • The collapse: the tower is hit, the floor gives way and Jason and Lucia wake up in burning rubble. The files are downloaded, but the drive is still in the office, so you go back for it.
  • The escape: you switch between Jason and Lucia covering each other, snipe guards on the next floor, then take one of Giorgio's cars down the tower's spiral ramps while a helicopter shoots at you.
  • The crash and the lift: the car crashes into the building site next door. They ride a construction lift down the outside of the unfinished tower while police search the site.
  • The last drive: a convertible down the Keys highway in the morning, to the house in the Keys. Someone tipped off the police, so it's back onto the bridge, straight into a roadblock.

The final choice: Swim For It or No Surrender

At the roadblock Jason and Lucia kiss, the screen freezes grey and you get two buttons: Triangle = Swim For It, R2 = No Surrender. The trophy list has one called Actions Speak Louder ("choices made, for better or worse").

Listen to the dialogue right before the choice, because it explains the endings. Jason holds up the drive and says Lucia can use it. Lucia answers that the deal was only for her. Jason says at least one of them can make it out.

No Surrender ending: Jason and Lucia die

Press R2 and the world turns purple and slows down. Jason and Lucia go out shooting, standing up in the convertible against the whole roadblock. They break through but they're hit, and the car slams into the guardrail. Jason dies slumped over the wheel, still holding his pistol, and Lucia dies beside him. A deputy says: "Yeah, they're gone."

The credits roll over an aerial shot of the bridge. Between the names, everyone else's life goes on: Lucia's mother at the door with a toddler, Cal on a fishing boat, kids playing in a yard, family photos on a dresser. Our full breakdown: No Surrender ending + post-credits.

Swim For It ending: Lucia escapes, Jason goes to prison

Press Triangle and Jason lays out a plan: don't draw your weapon, get to the edge of the bridge, jump, swim to a sandbar, take a boat and go all the way to Cuba. He gives Lucia his phone, and they walk to the rail with their hands up.

On the count of three Lucia jumps. Jason doesn't. Two deputies grab his legs on the rail and pin him to the road. The last you see of Lucia is her falling toward the water. The sandbar, the boat and Cuba are never shown; they only exist in Jason's plan.

The Swim For It epilogue

  • Lucia signs a deal at a desk with an American flag behind her.
  • Jason gets a mugshot: Vice-Dale Police Department, number 00468723.
  • Lucia packs an orange suitcase and drives north, crying, home to her mother's door.
  • Jason ends up in an orange LDC jumpsuit: a fight in the prison canteen, the prison yard.
  • The last scene: Jason, now with long hair and a beard, reads a book called The Cowboy's Prayer Is To Die Young. A guard says he has a visitor. Behind the glass are Lucia and a young boy, who presses his hand to the glass. Jason presses his against it.

The hidden third ending: Swim For It gone wrong

Sirloin replayed the finale and pressed Swim For It again, but this time Jason's first line was different: "Don't do anything stupid" instead of "don't draw your weapon". Same plan, same sandbar, same boat. Then Jason steps out of the car with one hand up and his pistol still in the other. The deputies open fire. Jason goes down on the road, Lucia is shot in her seat, and a deputy says he's gone. The credits open on the shot-up car with Jason's body on the road.

THEORY: the visible difference between the two Swim For It runs is the gun in Jason's hand. Whether earlier story choices change his line or the outcome is not confirmed

We'll update this page if Rockstar or a datamine explains the trigger.

Cal's post-credits scene

Every ending has the same post-credits scene. Cal sits on a dock in a Hawaiian shirt and yellow cap, waiting for Giorgio's drive to finish decrypting. He opens the files, reads, and says "No. Fucking. Way." before it cuts to black. The game never says what's on the drive.

THEORY: the drive is a setup for future story DLC. See our [GTA 6 DLC page](/gta-6-dlc-story-expansions-what-we-know/)

What happens after the ending

The screen says Story Complete and puts you back before the finale, so you can finish loose ends, go for 100% and replay the choice. The final mission's gold trophy is You Gotta Know When to Get Off. In the No Surrender run we covered, the stats read 100% story but only 59% overall after 39 h 27 min, with $2 million in family savings. The post-game also opens a cheats menu where each cheat is earned with a challenge.

What the endings mean

The clues were there all along. The trophy for 100% completion is called A Long Road, the first trailer used "Love Is a Long Road", and the last mission's trophy is You Gotta Know When to Get Off. In No Surrender neither of them gets off. In Swim For It, Lucia gets off the bridge and Jason doesn't. The book Jason reads in prison, The Cowboy's Prayer Is To Die Young, points straight at the other ending.

Rockstar doesn't mark any ending as canon. Only Swim For It has a next scene, though: a visitor and a hand on the glass. Which one did you pick? Tell us under the video. More story pages are on our GTA 6 story hub.

Frequently asked questions

How many endings does GTA 6 have?

Two choices and three outcomes seen so far: No Surrender (both die), Swim For It (Lucia escapes, Jason goes to prison), and Swim For It with Jason holding his pistol (both are shot).

What is the good ending in GTA 6?

Swim For It with Jason unarmed is the only outcome where both survive: Lucia escapes and takes a deal, and Jason goes to prison. Rockstar doesn't call any ending canon.

Can you replay the GTA 6 ending?

Yes. After the credits the game returns you to before the finale, so you can replay the final choice.

What does Cal find on the drive?

The game doesn't say. In the post-credits scene Cal decrypts Giorgio's drive, reads it and says "No. Fucking. Way." before it cuts to black.

What is the GTA 6 final mission trophy?

You Gotta Know When to Get Off, a gold trophy that pops when the story is complete.
