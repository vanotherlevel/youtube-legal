Rockstar has officially named only three street-level gangs in GTA 6: San4San and PTT Youngin$ in Vice City, and an unnamed local biker gang in Ambrosia whose patches, according to fan databases, read Final Chapter. Around them sit the criminal crews of the story cast: Boobie Ike's club and record label, Brian Heder's smuggling boat yard in the Keys, and Raul Bautista's bank robbers.

This guide explains every gang, crew and criminal group known so far: where they operate, who leads them, where they appear in the official footage, and what they mean for you as a player. It also separates the real groups from the rumours, leak names and social-media clubs that fill most online gang lists.

GTA 6 releases November 19, 2026. This page uses only Rockstar's own material (Trailer 1, Trailer 2, the Launch Trailer, An Extended Look from August 27, 2026, and the official character and edition pages) plus named press previews, which were hands-off. No leaked footage. Extended Look and trailer timestamps match Rockstar's YouTube uploads.

How we label each gang

  • Confirmed: Rockstar named it or said it, or the name appears on screen.
  • Preview: reported by press previews, interviews, or fan databases citing official screenshots.
  • Speculation: leak claims, fan art, fan comparisons and anything Rockstar has not backed up.

Which gangs has Rockstar confirmed?

Rockstar footage and store copy
  • San4San: the name is an on-screen interaction prompt in An Extended Look at 1:00.
  • PTT Youngin$: named in the Ultimate Edition store copy as a raidable gang compound in Southside Vice City.
  • The local biker gang: Rockstar's own words for Ambrosia's club. The name Final Chapter comes from patches in official screenshots, as reported by fan databases.

Just as important is what Rockstar has not confirmed: no gang territories, no turf wars, no recruiting gang members like San Andreas, and no gang reputation meter. Names like M7 or the Guardia Brothers come from leak claims and fan art; they are covered near the end, clearly labelled.

San4San: the first gang you meet

Name on screen, Extended Look 1:00

Where: a run-down apartment block in Vice City, at night, in the opening minutes of An Extended Look. Jason and Lucia cross a dark lot toward the entrance with men loitering by the door (from 0:09). Inside, five or six men hold the lobby: a big man with a towel over his shoulder, a man in a red vest, tattooed arms and graffiti on every wall (0:24).

At the lift, the interface offers two choices: RAYMOND? and SAN4SAN. You can literally ask for the gang by name. Breakdowns of the scene list more interaction options on gang members: Greet, Antagonize, Defuse and Rob, so every encounter could go friendly or very wrong.

In the graffiti-covered lift (1:28), a heavy-set member in a white tee and gold chains checks his phone; his pendant looks like an S4S. Upstairs: dim corridors, debris, crew members posted in the halls. There is even a prompt to pet the dog. Then the red door, with a pair of eyes in the slot (2:18): nobody walks in without being looked over.

Raymond, the drug lab and Boobie Ike

Game Informer, Dexerto, GTABase

Behind the door is the gang's business: a drug lab. Previews say it is run by Raymond, a San4San member; the fan database GTABase lists another member, Ernesto. Game Informer calls the mission a drug pickup on behalf of music mogul and entrepreneur Boobie Ike. So the first gang contact in GTA 6 is a supplier for someone else's empire, and Boobie's own Rockstar bio says the drug money pays for it all.

Smaller details from breakdowns: a member's shirt reportedly advertises Righteous Slaughter: Hood Ops, a fake in-universe video game, and one blog spotted a lieutenant in a Sound4Sound shirt, read as the gang's own music label (blog-level, take it lightly).

The Mendez raid

Game Informer, Kotaku, Dexerto

Then it goes wrong. Game Informer says someone close to the operation betrays them to the police, leading to a massive shootout with the invading officers through the building. Previews name the officer leading it as Mendez (Kotaku spells it Menendez). One breakdown calls his unit a rogue squad that shoots gang members on sight; that is a blogger's read, not Rockstar's.

Around 4:12, a figure bursts through drywall (one breakdown says a San4San member tackling an officer). Jason fires back from cover, grabs a shotgun whose pickup prompt reads Moreland 850 Shotgun (4:28), and hauls a red duffel bag the whole way out. Previews say the raid kills San4San members, and that Raymond helps them escape through a window onto a rooftop.

Trivia: GTA Vice City Stories (2006) had the Mendez Cartel run by Armando and Diego Mendez. Same surname; no connection confirmed.

Where San4San comes from

Fan and press comparisons

Fan databases place San4San in La Perle, read as Vice City's version of Miami's Little Haiti. In Trailer 2 at 0:47, Jason drives past a mural with the Haitian coat of arms and portraits while Vice City police make a street stop. Fan guides say the mural reads S4S Life; we could not read that in our frame, so it stays reported.

Fans and press compare San4San to Zoe Pound, a Haitian-American street gang formed in Miami in the early 1990s. Fans read the name as blood for blood. Rockstar has explained neither. Claims that San4San wears blue bandanas are unbacked; the lobby crew we see wears none. This is also not Rockstar's first Haitian gang: GTA Vice City (2002) had the Haitians under Auntie Poulet, and after protests in 2003 Rockstar apologised and removed lines from that game.

PTT Youngin$ and the gang compound raid

Ultimate Edition store copy

PTT Youngin$ is the gang most players have never heard of, and the only one Rockstar has said you can hit. The name comes from the store page for the Ultimate Edition (about $99.99), listed in June 2026: Gang Compound: PTT Youngin$ Illegal Goods Store. The copy says you raid the compound of one of Southside Vice City's loudest and most socially active gangs and escape safely to score special items and distinct contraband.

That makes it the one confirmed gang-versus-player activity in the game so far. With San4San you are a customer; with PTT you are the threat. What we do not know:

  • exactly where Southside is (Rockstar has not put it on a map)
  • whether the raid is repeatable, or whether the contraband can be sold
  • whether standard-edition players see PTT at all outside the raid

Fan databases describe an official image of members filming a dirt-bike stunt outside a Southside building at night. Neon gang colours and a rivalry with San4San are fan speculation.

The Final Chapter MC: Ambrosia's bikers

Rockstar: the local biker gang

Rockstar's Ambrosia copy reads: the Allied Crystal sugar refinery provides the jobs, while the local biker gang provides almost everything else. No name. In a company town, “almost everything else” suggests a club that runs the town, not just a corner of it.

Name from patches, per fan databases

The name Final Chapter comes from fan databases reading the patches in Rockstar's official Ambrosia screenshots: a reaper-style patch, an Ambrosia rocker and a founding year of 1982. We could not find the patch in our copies of the trailers, and sites disagree on the logo (hooded skeleton with a book, grim reaper, winged skull). One database lists their colour as black, their bikes as the Avarus and Sovereign, and a Mustang .357 revolver as a sidearm. All reported, none from Rockstar's text.

Footage is thin: this Trailer 2 biker (no readable patch, so not proven to be a member), a pack of bikes at night, and a brief biker in the Launch Trailer. Fans compare the patches to the Bandidos, the Florida presence to the Warlocks, and Ambrosia to Clewiston, Florida, America's sugar town. In GTA 5 the biker role belonged to The Lost in Blaine County; in GTA 6 it passes to a club in a sugar town.

Boobie Ike's empire: Jack of Hearts and Only Raw Records

Rockstar character page

Not a street gang, an empire. Rockstar calls Boobie Ike a local Vice City legend who turned his time in the streets into a legitimate business spanning real estate, a strip club and a recording studio. The club is the Jack of Hearts (tagline: it's all about heart). The label is Only Raw Records, which he runs with Dre'Quan Priest; now they just need a hit.

The key line on his page: the club money pays for the studio, and the drug money pays for it all. Drugs fund the club, the club funds the studio, the studio is the clean face on top. And previews report that the first Extended Look mission is Boobie's pickup from the San4San lab, so Jason and Lucia start as his delivery drivers. The real estate side has no addresses, buildings or missions yet.

Rockstar's Rupert Humphries told Dazed the team drew on Miami bass, Overtown house parties, the Booby Trap club on the river, and artists like Kodak Black and Sexyy Red. Fans compare the Jack of Hearts to Miami's King of Diamonds; that is a fan comparison only.

Dre'Quan Priest and the Real Dimez

Rockstar character page

Rockstar says Dre'Quan was always more of a hustler than a gangster: he dealt on the streets, but music was the goal, and he used to book acts into Boobie's strip club. His signing is the Real Dimez, Bae-Luxe and Roxy, friends since high school who turned shaking down local dealers into cash via spicy rap tracks. They had an early hit with a local rapper called DWNPLY and are now signed to Only Raw.

Rockstar's page links Dre'Quan to Grassrivers and the Real Dimez to Port Gellhorn; those are website link labels, not confirmed home turf. The Launch Trailer duo in pink and purple balaclavas with a gold AK is widely read as the Real Dimez; Rockstar has not said so. Is this a gang in the San Andreas sense? No colours, no turf, no war. It is a crew with a record deal, funded by drugs, run out of a strip club.

Brian Heder's smugglers in the Leonida Keys

Rockstar character page

Rockstar calls Brian Heder a classic drug runner from the golden age of smuggling in the Keys, still moving product through his boat yard with his third wife, Lori, and around long enough to let others do his dirty work. His page caption brags he hauled so much grass in a plane he could make Leonida levitate.

The crew: Brian at the top, Lori beside him, Jason doing the work (Rockstar says he lives rent-free in one of Brian's places, so long as he helps with local shakedowns), and Cal Hampton, a fellow associate who spends his time snooping on Coast Guard radio. Game Informer calls Brian Jason and Lucia's boss and primary mission giver. Where: Brian's Boat Works & Marina in the Keys, seen in Trailer 2 at 1:25 and at the start of the Launch Trailer.

Fans and press compare it to the 1970s and 80s Florida Keys, when smugglers ran weed by boat and plane. Trailer 2 also shows a boat chased through the swamp, and the Extended Look has a night boat chase with spotlights. Smuggling in GTA 6 comes with a tail.

Raul Bautista's heist crew

Rockstar character page

Rockstar describes Raul Bautista as a seasoned bank robber with confidence, charm and cunning, always on the hunt for talent. The warning: his recklessness raises the stakes with every score, and sooner or later his crew will have to double down or pull their chips from the table. Fans believe the older man in the bandana in Trailer 2 is him; Rockstar has not labelled the shot.

Game Informer, Engadget

In the Extended Look, previews report Jason and Lucia as getaway drivers for Raul (from about 13:30). Lucia drives, Jason leans out with a carbine, police SUVs and a helicopter behind; Game Informer says the wanted level hit four stars. They hide in a garage tunnel, then Lucia burns the getaway car with a Molotov and the crew switches to a maroon van (16:12), splitting the cash in the back.

The police and the Criminal Profile

Rob Nelson, Rockstar North, to IGN

Every crew shares one enemy, and it is the best-armed group in the state. Fan databases report several forces: Vice City PD, Vice Beach and Port Gellhorn departments, the Leonida Highway Patrol and county sheriffs (reported). Rob Nelson confirmed a Criminal Profile that tracks what kind of criminal you are: consistent actions may change how the world reacts to you. Names like professional, aggressive, violent or psycho are reported by outlets, not confirmed by Rockstar.

  • Crimes get reported only when someone sees them or an alarm goes off.
  • Police remember your outfit, vehicle, and whether you were alone or with a partner. Change clothes or cars to become a different suspect.
  • Wanted levels reportedly go up to six stars (GTA 5 topped out at five).
  • Not everyone runs. Nelson said in previous GTA games you were a bit all-powerful, and GTA 6 is not like that.

That last point is visible at Extended Look 12:14: after Jason and Lucia rob a Food Mart, the clerk follows them outside with a shotgun and keeps firing.

Mount Kalaga radicals and groups that are not gangs

Rockstar region copy via PC Gamer

Rockstar's Mount Kalaga copy mentions hillbilly mystics and paranoid radicals in the national park's backwoods. No name, no leader, no logo. Militia or survivalist readings are speculation. The Launch Trailer's armed men with night vision wading a swamp at sunset are not labelled as Kalaga; they could be police, a crew or something else.

Many online lists get this wrong. These are not gangs:

  • Thrillbilly Mud Club: the watermark on a Trailer 1 mud-bog party clip. It is an in-world social media channel.
  • High Rollerz Lifestyle: a Trailer 1 lifestyle clip with a logo, beside a green lowrider. No crimes, no territory.
  • Dirt-Bike Dirt-Bags: a Weazel News headline about riders in Trailer 1, not a gang name.

Our rule: if Rockstar names it as a gang, or it is a crew working crimes in the story, it is on this list. A car meet or mud party is Leonida flavour.

GTA 6 gang rumours: M7, Guardia Brothers, militia and cartels

Leak lists and fan wikis only
  • M7: described as an MS-13-style gang with M7 tattoos. Only a fan wiki leaked tag and fan art; fans link it to GTA 5's Marabunta Grande.
  • Guardia Brothers: a leak-list biker crew. Fan art only.
  • A far-right militia in Grassrivers or Mount Kalaga: a guess stacked on Rockstar's paranoid radicals line.
  • Cartels: no cartel is confirmed; a Cuban cartel is a popular theory. The drug trade is confirmed through Brian's bio; a cartel running it is not.
  • Old Vice City names (Forellis, Diaz, Vercetti, Cubans): none appear in GTA 6 material.
  • Ballas and Families in GTA 6 Online: a single leak-based report.

If a listicle gives you gang names nobody else has, they were probably made up.

GTA 5 vs GTA 6 gangs

  • Vice City (2002): Cubans vs Haitians, Ricardo Diaz's empire, the Forellis, and businesses that paid out once Tommy owned them.
  • San Andreas (2004): Grove Street (green), Ballas (purple), Vagos (yellow), Aztecas. Real gang wars: you took turf by surviving three waves of attacks.
  • GTA 4 / The Lost and Damned (2008-09): The Lost MC vs the Angels of Death, mafia families, Russian mob.
  • GTA 5 (2013): Families, Ballas, Vagos, Aztecas, Marabunta Grande, The Lost, the Madrazo cartel. Territory was hostile ground only.
  • GTA 6 (so far): fewer gangs, each specific. San4San is woven into the story; no gang colours are confirmed; the rest of the underworld is crime as a business around the story cast.

Will GTA 6 have gang wars or territories?

Not announced

Rockstar has not confirmed territories, turf wars, recruiting or a gang reputation system. What is real: the PTT Youngin$ compound raid (Ultimate Edition), the Greet / Antagonize / Defuse / Rob prompt on gang members, the Criminal Profile, and the Paradise Garage, which Rockstar describes as a secure place to deposit stolen goods to be fenced. That is crime as a business, not turf. Whether gangs react to your Criminal Profile is not stated.

GTA 6 Online: Rockstar has said multiplayer is planned but not at launch, with no gang features announced. GTA Online history (the 2016 Bikers update, CEO organisations) suggests crews arrive through updates. A 2027 date from Twitch's CEO is a report, not a Rockstar date.

Frequently asked questions

What gangs are in GTA 6?

Rockstar has named San4San and PTT Youngin$ in Vice City, plus the local biker gang in Ambrosia (Final Chapter on their patches, per fan databases). Story crews include Boobie Ike's empire, Brian Heder's smugglers and Raul Bautista's heist crew.

Is San4San in GTA 6?

Yes. SAN4SAN appears as an on-screen prompt next to a man called Raymond at 1:00 in An Extended Look, during the first mission.

Will GTA 6 have gang wars?

Not confirmed. Rockstar has not announced territories, turf wars, recruiting or gang reputation. The only confirmed gang activity is raiding the PTT Youngin$ compound in the Ultimate Edition.

Who are PTT Youngin$ in GTA 6?

A Southside Vice City gang from the Ultimate Edition store copy, described as one of the area's loudest and most socially active gangs. You raid their compound for special items and contraband.

Is there a biker gang in GTA 6?

Yes. Rockstar says the local biker gang provides almost everything else in Ambrosia. Fan databases read Final Chapter and 1982 on the patches in official screenshots; Rockstar's own text does not name them.

Are there cartels in GTA 6?

No cartel has been officially confirmed. The drug trade is confirmed through characters like Brian Heder and Boobie Ike.

Is M7 a real gang in GTA 6?

There is no credible source. M7 comes from a fan wiki leak label and fan concept art.
