GTA 6 adds 274 new mechanics we could verify before launch, from a six-star wanted level with heat icons to robbable stores, a Criminal Profile and Focus slow-motion aiming. Below is every one, numbered and grouped, with what changed from GTA 5 and how sure we are about it.

This page is the written version of our two-hour video. GTA 6 launches November 19, 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, and nobody outside Rockstar's press previews has played it yet, so this is a list of what was shown and reported, not a review. Start with the latest GTA 6 news or the GTA 6 hub for everything else.

Labels on this page: OFFICIAL (44) = Rockstar said it (Newswire, rockstargames.com or a Rockstar developer interview). SEEN in footage (66) = visible in Rockstar's own gameplay footage. REPORTED (164) = described in press previews (Edge, IGN, VICE, TGG and others) but not shown. Where sources disagree we say so. No leaked GTA 6 material is used to count anything.

How many new mechanics does GTA 6 have?

We counted 274. The video title says 245+ because we kept finding more while making it. The count only includes things with a named source: 44 are official, 66 can be seen in Rockstar footage and 164 come from hands-on previews. Anything that only traces back to the 2022 leak, a retailer listing or a single unsourced site is left out and listed separately at the bottom.

The ten new mechanics that change everything

OUR OPINION

Most of the list is small. These are the ten we think matter most. Each one takes something GTA 5 handled for you and makes it your problem, or your choice.

  1. Six stars with heat icons: the game tracks what the police know about you, not just how angry they are.
  2. No police radar blips: you have to actually look for them.
  3. Robberies in stores you can walk into: a new crime game on its own.
  4. Car tiers need better tools: stealing anything you see is over.
  5. Tyres, engines and fuel tanks as targets: every chase becomes a shooting problem, every getaway car a choice.
  6. Lock-on reported off by default: VICE says an option may remain.
  7. Focus with red and yellow zones: choose a fatal or non-lethal shot.
  8. Criminal Profile: Rockstar says it records how you operate and is not a morality system.
  9. Jason and Lucia's bond: Edge reports four levels raised through walks, cycling, drives and gym trips, and missions can change with it.
  10. The map: reportedly hundreds of places to enter, so the doors matter as much as the roads.

What we can't confirm (not counted)

UNCONFIRMED / NOT COUNTED

You have probably heard these. None of them made the list, because they trace back only to the 2022 leak, an Amazon Brazil store listing, a single site with no attribution, or one creator's reading of one clip.

  • 700 explorable establishments: from a leak; VICE says it isn't confirmed.
  • Drag strips, ovals and a Gellhorn raceway: tied to an alleged leaked map.
  • A social network for viral videos, weather using the physics system, NPC work and sleep routines, unique abilities per lead: only from the Amazon Brazil listing.
  • Surrendering to police, looting everyone, multi-till stores, stopping witnesses: 2022 leak only, no preview says it.
  • Spear guns, golf clubs, flashbangs, tracking jammers, crowbars, lockpicks: named only in leak text. The tool Rob Nelson actually names is the key cloner.
  • 600,000 NPC animations: hedged by the outlet that printed it, with no named Rockstar source.
  • Bullet travel time, piercing car parts, cutting alarm power: one breakdown video's reading, no source.
  • No dual wielding, prone being cut: unattributed or leak-based.

If any of these turn out real after launch on November 19, we will add them here and mark the page updated.

How many new mechanics are in GTA 6?
