Rockstar has not published a GTA 6 weapons list, but across four trailers, the Ammu-Nation commercial, the cover art and the official screenshots it has shown about 28 to 31 different weapons, many with real in-game maker names like the Girardi ES9, Klose K17, Moreland 850 and Duke 5.56. Our day one estimate is 40 to 50 weapons, most likely around 45.

This page goes through every weapon Rockstar has actually shown, which ones are new to the series, the new carry rules, and how likely everything else is. It's the written version of our video below. GTA 6 launches November 19, 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, and we'll update this page once we've played it.

Labels on this page: OFFICIAL = Rockstar's trailers, screenshots, cover art or edition details. REPORTED = press previews and outlets. THEORY = our own prediction. Anything that only comes from the 2022 leaks is called a rumour, and no leaked footage is used.

GTA 6 pistols and revolvers with real names

OFFICIAL: Extended Look, cover art, Ultimate Edition
  • Girardi ES9: Jason's full-size 9mm sidearm, compared by the press to the Beretta 92. Rockstar names it in the Ultimate Edition description, and the Extended Look shows 17 rounds in the magazine.
  • Klose K17: Lucia's Glock-style pistol. That's new: in GTA 5, Glock-style pistols only appeared in artwork and on police holsters.
  • Nipper .38: only on the cover art, printed on the gun in Lucia's hand. "Nipper" is old slang for a small pickpocket, so it's a joke hidden on the box. The closest GTA 5 match is the SNS Pistol.
  • Hawk & Little Morgan revolvers: a his-and-hers pair in the Ultimate Edition, "sourced from the Vercetti Estate", Tommy Vercetti's old mansion. Hawk & Little made the Heavy Revolver and Homing Launcher in GTA Online.
  • Mustang .357: stamped on a biker's revolver in an official screenshot. It's a Colt Python look-alike, just like Vice City's .357 in 2002.
  • Capo pistol: the name appears on Jason's pistol at the end of the first trailer, a classic 1911 shape.

The Extended Look also shows Lucia robbing a jewellery store with her pistol, and a customer goes straight for her: people in Leonida fight back. More on the Vercetti link in our Tommy Vercetti clues page.

Shotguns and rifles: Moreland and the Duke family

OFFICIAL: Extended Look and screenshots
  • Moreland 850: a pump-action based on the Remington 870. The pickup prompt shows its name on screen, and Lucia fires it one-handed while carrying a bag of cash.
  • Duke 5.56: an M4-style rifle Jason takes off a gunman, again named on screen. Duke is a brand-new gun maker for the series.
  • Duke carbine and a special ops carbine with a suppressor and a 40-round magazine.
  • Duke assault sniper: in a hunter's hands in one official screenshot. GTA 5 had an "Assault Sniper" that was cut before release.

Heavy weapons: grenade launcher and belt-fed LMG

OFFICIAL: trailers 2 and launch trailer

The launch trailer shows a woman leaning out of a car with a six-shot revolving grenade launcher, and in the second trailer a red-haired woman uses one on the police cars chasing her. Rockstar planned a grenade launcher for Vice City in 2002 and cut it, so 24 years later one is finally being fired in Vice City.

The Ammu-Nation commercial in trailer 2 is basically a shopping list: a belt-fed light machine gun that looks just like an M249 (the gun GTA 5's Combat MG was based on), a wall of rifles and shotguns, and a box with the Shrewsbury logo, the maker of GTA 5's launchers.

SMGs, AKs, snipers and paint jobs

OFFICIAL: trailers and screenshots
  • An MP5-style SMG in the trailer 2 bank robbery and a Mac-10-style SMG with the pre-order paint job.
  • A gold Uzi in the newest screenshots, a nod to the gold SMG from The Ballad of Gay Tony (2009), plus a gold AK in the launch trailer.
  • AK-pattern rifles carried by mercenaries in the Extended Look.
  • A bolt-action sniper rifle in the launch trailer, and hunters with scoped rifles and a break-action shotgun in the screenshots.
  • The pre-order bonus gives most guns a tropical pattern based on Tommy Vercetti's palm-tree shirt.
REPORTED: PC Gamer

PC Gamer thinks the hunter's break-action is a double barrel. That gun has only appeared in Chinatown Wars and GTA Online, so it would be a first for a main story game.

Melee and throwables

OFFICIAL: trailers, screenshots and captions
  • Baseball bat, pool cue and mini golf club.
  • A knife, shown twice in the November screenshot gallery (a man by a swamp campfire, and a guy pulling a knife on Jason). Before that gallery it only existed in the leaks.
  • A Molotov sitting in the throwable slot of the weapon wheel, then thrown seconds later.
  • A Rockstar caption mentions a switchblade, last seen in GTA 4 and GTA Online, never in GTA 5's story.

How many guns can you carry in GTA 6?

OFFICIAL: Rockstar's Rob Nelson

You can't carry everything any more. You get two concealed handguns and two long guns: one slung, one in your hands. The rest goes in your car's trunk, a garage weapon locker or an Ammu-Nation locker, and the Extended Look shows Lucia pulling a rifle out of a trunk. A rifle on your back is fine, but walking around holding one makes people move away. Ditching the gun you used, changing clothes and leaving the area lowers your heat. Full details in our GTA 6 gun system explained guide.

REPORTED: previews
  • The weapon wheel survives, now split into a weapons tab and an items tab, after Rockstar reportedly spent years trying to replace it.
  • A shooting range with targets and a timer, plus suppressors, red dots and scopes.
  • Suppressive fire: shoot near people to scare them instead of hitting them.
  • Lock-on is no longer default, aim is freer and tunable, rifles kick harder than in GTA 5, and limb shots can take people out of a fight or disarm them.

Shooting from cars, and people shoot back

OFFICIAL: Extended Look

Jason fires a carbine from the window sill of a getaway car while a helicopter follows, Jason and Raul lean out of opposite windows together, and Lucia fires her pistol backwards from a red convertible until the police SUV behind her flips. The world shoots back too: in a store robbery the clerk aims a pump shotgun at you in the security mirror, then follows the robbers outside and fires.

What other weapons will be in GTA 6? Our predictions

THEORY: our estimate, not confirmed
  • Very likely: hunting rifle (hunters in Grassrivers already carry them), a player knife, rocket launcher, grenades (in every 3D and HD GTA), micro SMG.
  • Likely: sticky bombs (GTA 5 heist staple, and the Ultimate Edition boat comes with an explosives crate), crowbar, flashbang, taser (still only a rumour, though Rockstar is pushing non-lethal play).
  • Possible: machete (a deleted age rating mentioned decapitation and dismemberment), minigun (fights the carry limits), M60, flare gun, speargun (leak-only, so a rumour).
  • Long shots: chainsaw (last in Chinatown Wars 2009; only a deleted ex-Rockstar LinkedIn post hinted at prop labels), katana, screwdriver, flamethrower, bow.

How many weapons will GTA 6 have on day one?

GTA 5 launched with about 30 weapons in the standard edition and Vice City had 35. Rockstar has already shown 28 to 31, and staples like grenades, a rocket launcher, sticky bombs and a crowbar haven't been seen yet. Our estimate: 40 to 50, most likely around 45, with fewer silly weapons, more real ones, and every one with its own name. We'll rank every single one after launch: see the full list so far on our every GTA 6 weapon page and the latest GTA 6 news.

Frequently asked questions

How many weapons are in GTA 6?

Rockstar hasn't published a list. About 28 to 31 different weapons have been shown in trailers, screenshots and the cover art. Our estimate for day one is 40 to 50, most likely around 45 (theory).

What new guns are in GTA 6?

Officially shown names include the Girardi ES9, Klose K17, Nipper .38, Hawk & Little Morgan revolvers, Mustang .357, Moreland 850 shotgun and the Duke 5.56 rifle family, plus a revolving grenade launcher and a belt-fed LMG.

Is there a knife in GTA 6?

Yes, a knife appears twice in Rockstar's November screenshot gallery, held by NPCs. Before that it was only in the leaks.

Is the weapon wheel in GTA 6?

Yes, according to reports it survives, split into a weapons tab and an items tab, but you can only carry two handguns and two long guns at once.
