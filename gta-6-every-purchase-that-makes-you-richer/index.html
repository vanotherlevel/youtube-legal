The purchases that make you richer in GTA 6 are the ones that make crime pay more: tools that open better cars, gear that lets you rob safely, and vehicles that carry more loot out of every job. Flashy buys like mod shops and gym memberships cost money and earn nothing back.

This guide covers every purchase Rockstar has shown so far that could feed your bank balance: where it appears in the official footage, what it costs (if anything is known), how fast it pays you back, and how sure we are. At the end, every item gets a place on a payback tier list, matching the board in our video.

GTA 6 launches November 19, 2026. Nobody outside Rockstar has played it yet. Everything here comes from Trailer 1, Trailer 2, the Launch Trailer, An Extended Look (August 27, 2026) and named press previews, which were hands-off (Rockstar played, press watched). Almost no in-game prices have been shown, so we never invent one. Unknown costs say not shown yet.

How we label every claim

  • Confirmed: on screen in Rockstar footage, or printed in Rockstar store or site copy.
  • Preview: reported by named press who watched Rockstar's demo, or said by Rockstar staff in those interviews.
  • Speculation: our own logic, or GTA 5 / Red Dead Redemption 2 precedent.

Extended Look timestamps match Rockstar's official upload (the slim jim prompt is at 6:07). Trailer 1 and 2 times match Rockstar's YouTube uploads.

How money works in GTA 6

On screen in An Extended Look

Open the weapon wheel at 15:59 in An Extended Look and two numbers sit in the top right: $15,150 beside a purple icon and $3,146 beside a green wallet icon. At 17:52 the items wheel shows $15,150 and $700.

Press reading, not labelled by Rockstar

Press who watched the demo read these as money in the bank and cash on hand. Rockstar has not labelled them yet. Previews say the difference matters:

  • Cash on hand is lost if you die or get caught. Banked money is safe.
  • Stolen valuables are not cash yet. You sell them to a fence first, usually at a discount.
  • Some story missions are locked behind money. IGN's preview says certain missions will not start until you have raised a certain amount of capital. Amounts are not shown.

ATMs are in the world. In Trailer 2 at 0:42, Jason leaves a Leonida Keys corner store with a six-pack, past an ATM sign and machine. Another ATM sits inside Hank's Waffles during the Extended Look robbery (about 11:52). A single source says cash must be deposited at an ATM to be safe; not confirmed.

Cars you can steal, flip, keep or buy

The fastest money with zero spending is cars. Here is every car-related way to earn (or spend) that has been shown or previewed.

1. Parked car theft: slim jim or smash window

Prompt on screen, Extended Look 6:07

Where: Extended Look 6:07, a Vice City strip-mall lot with a huge American flag and eagle mural on the building behind. Jason walks up to a parked red classic sedan and two prompts appear: Slim Jim (Triangle) and Smash Window (Circle). Seconds later Lucia is doing burnouts in it.

How it pays: smashing is fast but loud and leaves broken glass. The slim jim is quiet and leaves the car clean, and press say damage lowers a car's value. Cost: nothing. Payback: instant.

2. Carjacking

Edge and other previews

Previews say some drivers fight back or are armed, passengers can resist, and a carjacked car can come with a tracker or a near-empty tank. Free and fast, but loud. A full car is a bad target.

3. Selling to a car fence

Rockstar's Rob Nelson to IGN

This is where stolen cars become money. Rockstar has not named a fence or shown where one is. Previews say fences ask for certain models and the drop-off blocks trackers, but you must arrive without a police tail. Rob Nelson of Rockstar North told IGN: “The car fence will give you less money for it if it's damaged.” Working for fences over time unlocks extra services. Payouts: not shown yet.

4. Pay and Spray

Previews only

Previews say it can buy your stolen car, repaints cars to cut police heat, and does basic repairs and colours. Access reportedly depends on fence work and story progress, so it is an unlock, not a day one store. Locations and prices: not shown.

5. Repairs before you sell

Nelson's demo

In the press demo, Nelson repaired a damaged supercar before delivery because a damaged car sells for less. Where repairs happen is not stated; even the strip mall Lucia walks into at Extended Look 17:57 has a “We Do Repairs” sign. If the repair costs less than the value lost, it pays for itself (our logic, not Rockstar's numbers).

6. Car registration

RockstarINTEL citing IGN

You can reportedly pay to register a stolen car and keep it as a personal vehicle, whose trunk carries every weapon you have bought. Fee: not shown. Slow payback, but it builds the car you will do every job in.

7. The police impound job

IGN interview

Nelson stole an Infernus supercar from a police impound lot after Jason suggested it while driving past. It had an advanced tracker that pinged when he drove slowly, so he kept his speed up. Fences can reportedly hand you impound access codes. High value, no outlay, high risk. Not shown on screen.

8. Edition and pre-order vehicles

Rockstar editions page and Take-Two copy
  • '95 Grotti Cheetah (Ultimate Edition): mid-90s sports car, arrives later in the story per Kotaku.
  • '55 Vapid Stanier + garage near Ocean Beach (Vintage Vice City Pack, every pre-order).
  • '67 Vapid Dominator Buggy (UE), kept at the Paradise Garage in Watson Bay.
  • Dinka Enduro + kayak at Jason's safehouse, and the Shitzu Squalo speedboat at Washington Beach with an explosives crate (UE).
  • Classic Car Collection (UE): a commission for the fixer Wyman to track down abandoned classics. Cash reward not stated.

The Ultimate Edition costs about $99.99 in real money. None of these items has confirmed in-game income.

9. Mod shops

Ultimate Edition content

The UE adds Rideout Customs in Vice City, One-Eyed Willie's in Lake Leonida and a retro build for Jason's Vapid Ganado. Nothing says mods raise a fence payout. Payback: none known.

10. Scooter Bros and impound recovery

Edge, November 2026 issue

The Scooter Bros phone app delivers your own car within seconds for a small fee, weapons in the trunk. Fee: not shown. If you abandon your car, police can impound it, and you pay more to get it back (or steal it back).

The car money loop: steal clean (slim jim), keep it undamaged (repair if needed), lose the police, deliver it (fence or Pay and Spray).

Car theft tools: the purchases that open better cars

Slim jim: how to use it

On screen; controls per Postmode

Jason already has a slim jim in the Extended Look. Press Triangle at the driver's door, then sweep the right stick from 3 o'clock to 9 o'clock in one motion. It opens older, less secure cars with no broken glass and no smashed-window alarm. Whether you buy it is not shown, and neither is a price. Payback: fast.

Key cloner

Nelson via Engadget; GamesRadar

Nelson said fancier vehicles with electronic locks need key cloners, and GamesRadar quoted Rockstar saying you acquire certain tools to get in. How you unlock it: not shown (one guide says fence work, a single source). Postmode says the Extended Look shows key cloning later; we could not find that moment, so we will not fake it. Fun detail: a stock car in the 17:46 race carries an IMOFF Immobilizer Bypass decal.

Car scanner app (WAINK)

Nelson's walkthrough, not on screen

Press call it WAINK. It is not shown on screen; previews say it tells you if a car is locked, alarmed or tracked (and the tracker tier), which tool you need, what the car is worth at a fence or Pay and Spray, what registration costs, and which models fences want (Edge). Free. Payback: instant. Scan first, then steal only what you can open cleanly and sell.

Tracker removal

Single hands-off source

Higher-tier trackers beep and send your position when you slow down. In the preview build, removal was locked behind fence work. Cost: not shown. A lockpick and snap gun sold in back rooms appear on one fan wiki only; treat them as unverified.

Garage space: where to keep the cars that earn

RockstarINTEL citing IGN

Jason and Lucia can each own multiple personal vehicles, if they have the garage space. One preview says you can buy a garage, stash a stolen car and register it later. No garage locations or prices have been shown.

The Vice City apartment

Where: a small pink apartment block in Vice City, Extended Look 6:20 to 7:25. After Lucia leaves prison the pair share it: flamingo wallpaper, a fridge, a TV and a carport out front. Rockstar has not said whether homes cost anything.

Jason's stilt house (rent free)

Rockstar character bio

The green wooden stilt house in the Leonida Keys (Trailer 2 0:08). Rockstar says Brian Heder lets Jason live there rent free, so long as he helps with local shakedowns. Free rent is real money saved.

Paradise Garage and the pre-order garage

Ultimate Edition copy via Kotaku

The UE's Paradise Garage in Watson Bay has a weapon locker and a secure place to deposit stolen goods to be fenced. Loot you carry can be lost, so a safe drop point means less risk. It is the only garage Rockstar's copy ties to fencing, but it costs real money. The pre-order garage near Ocean Beach is called the Shore Court Garage by fan databases, which also list a locker and deposit there; Rockstar's text does not, so treat that as unconfirmed.

Safehouses and a $1 locker sign

Nelson told IGN safehouses feel closer to the camps in Red Dead Redemption 2 than old GTA safehouses: fridge, TV, even home haircuts, which helps because police remember how you look. Buying new safehouses is not confirmed. At Extended Look 10:20 in Vice City, near the Effluvia rooftop bar scene, a sign reads Public Locker Rentals From Only $1, the one real price in the footage. What lockers do is not shown.

Robbery gear: what to buy before you hit a store

Weapons (Ammu-Nation)

Ammu-Nation ad in Trailer 2; buying per Nelson

In Trailer 2 (2:09 to 2:13), Jason's TV plays an Ammu-Nation ad with a wall of guns. Nelson told IGN “all your weapons that you've purchased will always be available in the back of your personal vehicle”, so guns are bought. Named guns include the Moreland 850 shotgun (pickup prompt, Extended Look about 4:28) and the Duke 556 assault rifle (about 25:09). Prices and store locations: not shown. No gun, no robbery, so payback is fast.

Your trunk is your armoury: at Extended Look 9:11, masked Jason checks a rifle at an open trunk. Trailer 1 also passes a Pawn & Gun shop at 1:11; its function is not shown.

Masks, outfits and haircuts

Items wheel on screen

Jason and Lucia mask up at the door of Hank's Waffles (Extended Look 11:29). The items wheel has a Face Wrap mask slot, sunglasses, a cap and an outfit hanger. During the robbery the wanted HUD shows what police know: camera footage, the duo, your outfit, your face and your weapon. Nelson says police remember your outfit and vehicle; change them and you shake the search.

Clothes come from local stores and salons cut hair (IGN); the UE adds Stock 305, Sara's Unisex Salon and Electric Fang tattoos. Mask prices: not shown. Also note: clerks fight back. One fires a shotgun through a hole in the door, then follows you outside.

The getaway car (and a second one)

Nelson and Edge: put a car out front first

Nelson said he should really prepare by putting a vehicle out front. The Extended Look bank job (about 13:30 to 16:40) shows how: Lucia drives, Jason fires at police with a helicopter overhead, then in a car park Lucia burns the getaway car with a Molotov. They switch to a maroon van, roll through a toll plaza, and split the cash in the back. Molotov price: not shown.

Fan wiki relaying hands-on reports

Bags: a fan wiki says a bike carries what fits on your back, a car a couple of bags, and a van several. If that holds, a van pays for itself.

Explosives

IGN preview: greed versus speed

Blow a safe fast and you damage some valuables; find the combination and you keep full value, but police may arrive. A fence may text you a safe code. Explosive prices: not shown. Nelson told Insider Gaming you can rob pretty much anything: a headphone shop, a payday loan place, banks. The UE's Hawk and Little Morgan revolvers are pure style; nothing says they earn more.

Paid activities: do they pay?

Activities shown; no payouts shown

Racing: a stock car race (lap 1 of 2, 8th of 16, Extended Look 17:47), a dirt bike race (about 18:36) and night street racing. Postmode names Ambrosia Raceway Park. Entry fees and prizes are not shown; GTA 5 had paid races, but that is precedent, not proof.

Gambling: the Trailer 2 store has a lottery machine prop, but no casino or betting has been shown, and Rockstar's rating listing has no Simulated Gambling descriptor. Shooting range (about 18:33): targets and a timer, no payout. Gym: Edge says you need a membership to work out. It earns nothing.

Fishing, hunting, jet skis, scuba, pool, darts, golf, tennis, basketball, skydiving and backyard wrestling all appear with no payout shown. Jason buffs a boat at Brian's marina in the Launch Trailer, but whether it is paid is not shown; Rockstar says he works for local drug runners.

Can you buy businesses or invest in GTA 6?

Nothing announced

Rockstar has not announced any business, property or investment you can buy for income in GTA 6 single player, and no stock market app has appeared. What has been shown belongs to other people:

  • Boobie Ike's empire: real estate, the Jack of Hearts strip club and Only Raw Records. Rockstar's caption: the club money pays for the studio, and the drug money pays for it all.
  • Brian's Boat Works & Marina in the Keys (Trailer 2 1:25, with a Rent a Rack banner in the Launch Trailer). Rockstar says Brian moves product through it. Jason works for it.
  • PTT Youngin$ Illegal Goods Store (UE, Southside Vice City): a gang compound you raid, not a shop you own.
  • Private jets and penthouse towers: you visit them; nothing shows you can buy them.
GTA 5 precedent only

GTA 5 story mode let you buy properties and play the stock market. None of that is announced for GTA 6. For now, the businesses that make you money are the ones you rob.

Purchases that unlock new ways to earn

Think of these as keys, not trophies: story capital (some missions need money first), the key cloner (prestige cars), fence work (tracker removal, Pay and Spray, impound and safe codes), registration, repairs, weapons, explosives, masks, garages and the Ultimate Edition (the PTT compound raid and Wyman's commission). Almost every real earner is a crime, and almost every tool makes the crime pay more. Previews say both Jason and Lucia can steal and own cars.

Best return on your money: the payback tier list

The ranking judges one thing: how fast each purchase pays you back, using only what Rockstar has shown. Almost no prices are public, so each item is ranked by what it unlocks, and how safely. Bottom up:

  • D: buyable businesses and gambling. Neither is confirmed; zero evidence either pays.
  • C: gym membership, UE mod shops, Scooter Bros, race entry. Money you spend for fun or convenience. Race entry could climb if prize money appears.
  • B: car registration, garage space, Paradise Garage, explosives. Useful but slow, real-money, or lossy.
  • A: repairs before sale, masks and outfits, the getaway van. They protect the payday: full fence value, less police heat, more bags.
  • S: the top three. Weapons, because every store, payday lender and bank job starts with one. The key cloner, because it opens Leonida's most valuable cars. And the slim jim: Jason already has it, so there is no outlay, and clean cars get full fence value.

Frequently asked questions

What should I buy first in GTA 6?

A gun and a mask. Every robbery needs a weapon, and masks and outfit changes help you keep the loot. Jason already has a slim jim for clean car thefts.

Can you buy businesses in GTA 6?

Not as far as anyone knows. Rockstar has not announced any business, property or investment you can buy for income in single player, and no stock market app has been shown.

How do you make money fast in GTA 6?

Based on previews: steal cars cleanly, keep them undamaged and sell them to a car fence, and rob stores with a mask and a getaway car ready. Fence payouts are not shown yet.

Is there a casino or gambling in GTA 6?

None has been shown. Trailer 2 has a lottery machine prop, but Rockstar's rating listing has no Simulated Gambling descriptor.

What is the slim jim in GTA 6?

A car theft tool shown in An Extended Look at 6:07. It opens older parked cars quietly without breaking the window, so the car keeps its value.

Do you lose money when you die in GTA 6?

Previews say cash on hand is lost if you die or get caught, while banked money is safe.

How much do things cost in GTA 6?

Almost no prices have been shown. The only clear one is a Public Locker Rentals From Only $1 sign in Vice City.
