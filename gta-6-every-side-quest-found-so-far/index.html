GTA 6 has five kinds of side quest shown so far: random events, strangers, gang jobs, odd jobs (robberies) and a car list from a fence called Randy. Rockstar is also blurring the line between side content and story missions on purpose, so some of these will feel like full missions. Here is everything shown before the November 19, 2026 launch, ranked from five to one and lined up against GTA 3, Vice City, GTA IV and GTA V.

Labels used on this page: OFFICIAL = Rockstar's own footage or words. PREVIEW = press previews and interviews (IGN, Famitsu). MY READING = our own read of the footage. We update this page after launch with real mission names and locations.

Side quest vs side activity: what's the difference?

An activity is something you do: the gym, mini golf, scuba diving, the zoo. A side quest is somebody who wants something from you: a person, a job, a story and a payout. GTA 6 has plenty of both, but the previews say the open world and the missions are no longer cleanly split.

PREVIEW (IGN): mini golf and the zoo are in

5. Random events

In GTA 5, random events were blue dots on your radar: somebody robbed, somebody stranded, and you chose to help or drive past. In GTA 6, Rockstar's Rob Nelson told Famitsu that interactive random events happen all over Leonida, and gave the example of a lawyer living in a dumpster.

PREVIEW (FAMITSU): 'Interactive random events occur in all sorts of places'

In the Extended Look, Lucia runs into a guy hanging off the trunk of a moving car while a livestream chat rolls on screen: a social-media stunt you can stumble into.

OFFICIAL: the trunk challenge

4. Strangers

GTA IV had random characters on the street and GTA V had Strangers and Freaks, like the Epsilon Program. GTA 6 hasn't named its strangers yet, but previews say people now notice you: stand too close, follow them or listen in, and they turn around and ask what you want.

My reading: that's how strangers become side quests in GTA 6. Nobody marks them on the map; you just find them.

3. Gang jobs

The GTA 6 Ultimate Edition lists a raid on the compound of one of Southside Vice City's loudest gangs: get in, grab the goods, get out. For who those gangs are, read every gang in GTA 6 explained.

OFFICIAL (Ultimate Edition): raid a Southside Vice City gang compound

2. Odd jobs and robberies

GTA 3 had taxi, vigilante, paramedic and firefighter jobs, and Vice City had the Pizza Boy. In GTA 6, Rob Nelson told IGN that robberies are 'a huge amount of what you can and need to be doing' between story missions, and sometimes the story won't move until you raise the cash.

Robberies come with choices: find the combination and crack the safe for the full take, or blow it open with explosives and lose part of it.

1. The car list: Randy the fence

The one side job in every single GTA. GTA 3 had the Import/Export garage at the docks, plus a crane that only wanted emergency vehicles. Vice City made you buy Sunshine Autos and fill its lists one car at a time. GTA IV had Stevie texting you photos of the cars he wanted.

GTA 6 has a car fence named Randy who wants very specific vehicles. In Nelson's playthrough, the fence handed over a door code for a police impound lot, and Jason spotted the lot himself and suggested the job. Damage the car and the fence pays less.

The Ultimate Edition also adds a collector named Wyman who wants abandoned classics tracked down and restored.

OFFICIAL (Ultimate Edition): Wyman's abandoned classics

Verdict: side quests become missions

In GTA 6, a side quest isn't an icon you drive to. It's a person who wants something, and the open world turns it into a mission. Nelson even says a perfect tyre shot, or a last-second dash over a train crossing, can make a free-roam escape feel like a scripted mission. The car list has survived 25 years, and our bet is it's the first side quest you'll do on November 19.

Frequently asked questions

Does GTA 6 have side missions?

Yes. Rockstar has shown random events, gang raids, robberies and a car fence called Randy who wants specific vehicles. Full mission names come at launch on November 19, 2026.

Does GTA 6 have Strangers and Freaks?

Not by that name yet. Previews say NPCs react when you follow or crowd them, which is how strangers are expected to turn into side quests.

Who is Randy in GTA 6?

A car fence who asks for very specific vehicles and pays less if they're damaged. In one preview he gave Jason a door code to a police impound lot.

Who is Wyman in GTA 6?

A classic-car collector from the Ultimate Edition who wants abandoned classics tracked down and restored.
