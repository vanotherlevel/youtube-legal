GTA 6 has 196 weapons in total: 53 guns, 9 melee weapons, 4 explosives and 130 variants, according to PowerPyx's progress database. You buy them at 24 gun stores across Leonida. Only the 6 Expanded stores sell snipers, light machine guns and launchers, and prices run from $350 for the Moreland 850 shotgun (on sale at your first store) to $16,000 for the H-AT rocket launcher.

This page covers every gun class with the real in-game names. Wherever we could, we read the price and stats off the store menu in launch-day streams. It also covers the carry rules, attachments, every store, the free guns, and the best guns to buy first. More guides are on our GTA 6 guides hub.

How to read the labels: ON SCREEN means we read it ourselves from the store menu in MELOO's launch stream, Ben-Gun's launch stream or Dalek's launch stream. REPORTED means it comes from a launch-day guide (PowerPyx, playday.one, Insider Gaming). Stats are out of 10. Where a price says "not confirmed yet", nobody has shown it on screen.

Carry limit and loadout rules

ON SCREEN: tutorial tips in MELOO's launch stream

GTA 6 stops you carrying everything at once. The game's own tips say it plainly: "Openly carrying weapons can be dangerous in Leonida. Manage them at any weapon locker and your vehicle's trunk." A second tip says Ammu-Nations "will expand their stock as you purchase more weapons".

REPORTED (TheSixthAxis, Insider Gaming, launch day)
  • What you carry: 2 sidearms, 2 long guns, some explosives, 1 melee weapon and your equipment.
  • Long guns show on your back. Police get suspicious when they see one, and people around you react to a gun in your hand.
  • Everything else waits in storage, in the trunk of a car you're keeping or the locker at any Ammu-Nation. You can dump a gun to lose the cops and get it back from there later.
  • Ammo, armor and explosives you buy go straight to your owned car's trunk, so park it close before a job.
  • Icon colours: blue means a gun you own, white means one you picked up from an enemy, and gold means a unique weapon. You can't modify picked-up guns, and they're gone when you quit unless you store them.
  • Lockers swap variants. You change between a gun's standard and alternate versions at the locker.

How the gun store menu works

ON SCREEN: MELOO and Ben-Gun at Chuck's Tackle & Shot, MELOO at Big Shots
  • Three tabs: Sidearms, Longarms, and Ammo & Equipment.
  • Four stats per gun: Damage, Accuracy, Handling and Range, each out of 10. Press R3 for a second page with Mag Size, Held Ammo, Fire Style, Brand and Caliber.
  • Two kinds of money. A green wallet icon means you can pay in cash. A purple icon means the gun needs a bank account ("Requires a bank account"), so you can't buy it with the cash in your pocket.
  • Locked stock. Some guns say "Buy 2 more guns to unlock" or "Buy 4 more guns to unlock". The more you buy at a store, the more it opens up.
  • Ammo refills are listed per gun. A full refill for the Moreland 850 at Big Shots showed "Fill Ammo ($15)".

Melee, explosives and equipment

  • Body armor: $250 (ON SCREEN, Dalek's stream). It's payable in cash or from the bank and comes in Black plus three other finishes. It shows as a blue bar over your health, and you need a new vest to refill it (REPORTED, PowerPyx).
  • Hammer: in "Gone Fishing", behind the building where Donnie is held (REPORTED, VGC). Fists have block and counter.
  • Explosives: Sticky Bombs (sold at Ammu-Nation and, after his quests, Yasmani's shop), grenades and Molotovs. The fourth explosive is not confirmed yet.
  • Harpoon gun (ON SCREEN, MELOO at about 2:07:45): Jason pulls one underwater when a shark circles the bachelor-party dive. We didn't see its name on screen.
  • Parachutes are sold at Ammu-Nation, and parachute bags come in finishes (one is "Ginger Pinstripe").

Attachments: every slot and what it does

ON SCREEN: Moreland 850 Modify menu, MELOO about 2795-2810 s and Ben-Gun about 7766-7785 s

Pick a gun you own and choose Modify to get two tabs, Attachments and Cosmetics. On the Fortune & Son Moreland 850, the Attachments list counts 1 / 11 to 11 / 11, so that shotgun has 11 attachment slots:

  1. Barrels ("Improves ranged damage"; the standard part is the 18" Standard, 1 of 2)
  2. Foregrips ("Improves recovery speed after firing")
  3. Forends
  4. Iron Sights
  5. Lights
  6. Magazines
  7. Muzzles & Suppressors
  8. Optics
  9. Pistol Grips
  10. Saddles
  11. Stocks ("Improves stability while aiming")

Every part shows what it does to the stats before you buy it. In Ben-Gun's stream, the $250 Treadbound Compact foregrip (1 of 4 foregrips) took the Moreland from Accuracy 4.2 and Handling 5.6 to Accuracy 4.5 (+0.3) and Handling 5.3 (-0.3), with Damage 7.5 and Range 2.5 unchanged. With the Treadbound Tactical fitted, it read Accuracy 4.7 and Handling 5.1. So most attachments trade one stat for another.

Slot counts differ by gun: the Hobo sawed-off's menu shows a single mod icon, while the Mediator's shows seven. Andrew, the Chuck's clerk, offers "a few bucks off an upgrade" on your first visit.

REPORTED (Insider Gaming)

You can take suppressors and flashlights on and off in the field. Every other change needs a store visit, and bigger stores carry more parts.

Chuck's Tackle & Shot (your first gun store)

ON SCREEN: Ben-Gun about 7712 s, MELOO about 2538-2810 s

Jason's first gun store is Chuck's Tackle & Shot in Key Lento, a bait-and-tackle shop whose sign reads "stocked and supplied by Ammu-Nation". The quest card reads "Working for Brian will require some gear from Ammu-Nation. Pick up a longarm at Chuck's Tackle & Shot." ("Pick Up Another Gun"; PowerPyx lists the mission as "Gear Up"). The clerk (Andrew, per PowerPyx) calls Jason "Marauder" in the menu: "Take your pick, Marauder." The store also sells eyewear and fishing clothes.

Its stock: 4 pistols (Nipper $550, ES9 $700, K17 $975, Duke 1922 $1,050) and 4 longarms, two of which we saw priced: the Moreland 850 at $350 (down from $700) and the Mediator at $450 (down from $900). PowerPyx says you can probably only afford the shotgun at this point. In MELOO's stream, $407 cash dropped to $57 after the Moreland.

Big Shots (Hamlet, Expanded)

ON SCREEN: MELOO about 4116-4166 s, Dalek about 4040-4076 s

Big Shots in Hamlet is one of the 6 Expanded stores. Most of its longarms need a bank account. It has two exclusive variants: The Silver 1922 ($2,210) and the Sand Storm ES9 ($1,470), each marked "A unique variant exclusive to Big Shots." Body armor here costs $250.

Free weapons and how to unlock the rest

  • Enemy drops: you can pick up any gun you see, but it stays white, can't be modified, and is lost on quit unless you store it.
  • Killtelligence SM-69 sniper: free from Bret (the "Veteran Hunted" questline, Chapter 2, Jason only). His 9th quest unlocks the Shrewsbury SMS-01 to buy (REPORTED, PowerPyx).
  • Ultimate Edition: the Duval Signature ES9 (Jason, in any Ammu-Nation locker from Chapter 1) and the Caminos Signature K17 (Lucia, Chapter 2). They look different but have the base guns' stats and can't be upgraded. MELOO's stream shows the tip: "A new Ultimate Edition unique weapon variant is available at any Ammu-Nation weapon locker."
  • Pre-order bonus: a tropical weapon pattern on every edition (REPORTED).
  • Unique (gold) weapons: one-off finds around Leonida. Nobody has published a full list of where they are yet.
  • Cheats (only after the story; using one turns off trophies and saving): blow up 100 vehicles for the H-AT, land 100 explosive hits for the explosive Shortstraw, and win 25 bronze, silver or gold medals at the shooting ranges for Max Ammo, Infinite Ammo and No Reload. See our cheats list.

How to get a sniper rifle and launchers

REPORTED (PowerPyx)
  1. Cheapest sniper: as Jason, start Bret's hunting quests in Chapter 2 and finish the first one for the free Killtelligence SM-69.
  2. Buy one: go to any of the 6 Expanded stores. The Laird 350 at $950 is the cheapest.
  3. Launchers: the Hillinger Boomer ($12,000), Deever Tech GLR40 ($15,500) and Hauners H-AT ($16,000) are sold only at Expanded stores. Save up with vault and car jobs first (see our money guide).
  4. Free rocket launcher: the H-AT cheat after the story (100 vehicles destroyed).

Frequently asked questions

How many weapons are in GTA 6?

196 in PowerPyx's progress database: 53 guns, 9 melee weapons, 4 explosives and 130 variants. GTABase says 50 of the 66 standard weapons count toward 100% completion.

How many guns can you carry in GTA 6?

Two sidearms and two long guns, plus some explosives, one melee weapon and your equipment. Everything else stays in your car's trunk or an Ammu-Nation weapon locker.

How many Ammu-Nations are in GTA 6?

24 gun stores, per PowerPyx. Six are Expanded stores (Woodhatch, Big Shots, Phil's, Black Eagle, Iron & Oak and Prestige), the only ones selling snipers, LMGs and launchers. Your first one is Chuck's Tackle & Shot in Key Lento.

What is the best early gun in GTA 6?

The Fortune & Son Moreland 850 pump shotgun. Chuck's Tackle & Shot sells it for $350 (down from $700) with 7.5 damage. The Hawk & Little Mediator ($450 there) is the best second pick for range.

How do you get a sniper rifle in GTA 6?

As Jason, finish Bret's first hunting quest for the free Killtelligence SM-69. Or buy one at an Expanded store: the Laird 350 at $950 is the cheapest, per PowerPyx.
