Rockstar has shown 34 weapons and items in GTA 6 so far: 7 pistols and revolvers, 1 submachine gun, 8 rifles, 5 shotguns, 2 sniper rifles, 2 heavy weapons, and 9 throwables, melee weapons and gadgets. Only a handful have official names on screen: the Moreland 850 shotgun and the Duke 556 assault rifle, plus Jason's Girardi ES9 and Lucia's Klose K17 from Rockstar's own copy.

This guide lists every one of them with exactly where it appears in the official footage, how sure we are, how the new weapon system works, how you will get guns, and our early predictions for the best weapons at launch.

GTA 6 releases November 19, 2026. Everything here is from Rockstar's Trailer 1, Trailer 2, the Launch Trailer, An Extended Look (August 27, 2026), official screenshots and named hands-off press previews. No leaked footage. Extended Look and Trailer 1/2 timestamps match Rockstar's YouTube uploads; Launch Trailer times are approximate.

How we label each weapon

  • Confirmed: Rockstar named it, or its name appears on screen.
  • Preview: shown on screen but unnamed, reported by previews, or seen only in official screenshots.
  • Speculation: leak-derived fan lists. Never treated as fact here.

How weapons work in GTA 6

Hands-off previews; Rob Nelson of Rockstar North

The biggest change: no more unlimited pockets. In GTA 5 you carried every gun you owned. In GTA 6, previews say you carry two concealed handguns plus two long guns, one slung on your back and one in your hands. Everything else lives in your car's trunk; your personal vehicle keeps the full loadout, and some garages and safehouses have weapon lockers.

So ditching your car in a chase means leaving your arsenal behind. People also notice what you carry: a rifle on your back gets looks, a rifle in your hands makes people move away, and police may warn you before they escalate.

The weapon wheel

On screen, Extended Look 15:59

The wheel is back, redesigned with two tabs, Weapons and Items, switched with R1. At 15:59 it shows a pistol, a Molotov in the throwable slot, fists and binoculars. Bloomberg's Jason Schreier reported Rockstar spent a long time trying to replace the wheel with something more realistic before returning to it. The Items tab now holds healing items.

Ammo, aiming and the police

  • Ammo HUD (top right): magazine and reserve, such as 6 / 94 on a shotgun and 30 / 50 on the Duke 556.
  • Aiming: hard lock-on is reportedly off by default.
  • Hit markers (reported): white for a hit, yellow for an enemy down but alive, red for a kill. Limb shots can drop enemies without killing them.
  • Weight: rifles reportedly carry more recoil; pistols fire as fast as you pull the trigger. Combat looks closer to Red Dead Redemption 2.
  • Movement: crouch and shoot outside cover, lean out of a window for drive-bys while your partner drives, and (per previews) hand weapons to your partner.
  • Police memory: a gun icon beside the wanted stars means witnesses described your weapon. Changing or hiding it helps you shake the heat.

Every pistol and revolver in GTA 6

  1. Girardi ES9 (confirmed): Jason's personal sidearm per Rockstar. A full-size handgun; his pistol shows 17 rounds in the Extended Look drug-lab shootout (about 4:16). It also appears in Trailer 1's robbery montage.
  2. Klose K17 (confirmed): Lucia's personal sidearm per Rockstar, a polymer striker-fired pistol. She uses it in the jewellery store robbery (about 18:00), where a customer grabs at it.
  3. Red-dot pistol (seen): a gunman in the Launch Trailer fires a pistol with a red dot sight, so handguns can take optics.
  4. Police service pistol (seen): an officer's sidearm in Trailer 1's bodycam shot (0:49).
  5. Hawk & Little Morgan revolvers (confirmed): a matched his-and-hers pair, one scoped and stainless, one engraved and blued, from the Ultimate Edition. Not in the trailers.
  6. Mustang .357 (preview): the name is stamped on the barrel in an official Ambrosia screenshot, in a biker's hand.
  7. Crew pistols (seen): the many other handguns carried by crews and robbers. Pistols are the most common weapon in Rockstar's footage.

Because you can carry two concealed handguns, the pistol is your always-on weapon: armed in town without anyone noticing.

Submachine guns

Seen in Trailer 2, unnamed

8. MP5-style SMG. The only SMG shown so far: in Trailer 2's bank job (1:16), Lucia in a skull mask pushes it into a bank manager's face. Rockstar has not named it. SMGs have always been GTA's drive-by king, so expect more at launch.

Every assault rifle and carbine

  1. Duke 556 assault rifle (confirmed): Jason takes it off a downed gunman in the office shootout and the pickup prompt reads Duke 556 Assault Rifle (Extended Look about 25:09). HUD: 30 in the magazine, 50 in reserve. Duke boxes also appear in the Ammu-Nation ad, so expect a Duke family.
  2. AR-pattern carbine (seen): fired by Jason leaning out of the window in the police chase (about 14:20). The HUD shows 40 in the magazine, possibly an extended mag.
  3. M4-style carbine (seen): masked Jason checks it at the trunk (9:11), and it returns in the Launch Trailer.
  4. Lucia's flag-pattern rifle (seen): at the same trunk, with a blue flag-style finish. A strong hint that weapon finishes are back.
  5. Scoped carbine (seen): carried by a masked gunman and used by Lucia at the shooting range (about 18:34), where she quick-swaps to her pistol.
  6. AK-pattern rifle (seen): carried by the white-suited mercenaries in the skyscraper siege (about 24:00). Lucia texts that they have auto weapons and explosives and sound military.
  7. Gold AK (seen): two women in pink and purple balaclavas pose with a gold-detailed AK in the Launch Trailer.
  8. Suppressed tactical carbines (seen): a night vision squad wades through the mangroves with silenced rifles in the Launch Trailer.

Rifles are numbers 9 to 16 on our list. Attachments are confirmed by sight: suppressors, red dots and scopes. There is no attachment menu yet, so how you add them is unknown.

Every shotgun

  1. Moreland 850 (confirmed): the name pops up on screen when Jason picks it up in the drug-lab raid (4:28). Pump action, 6 in the tube and 94 in reserve, with heavy recoil on every shot.
  2. One-handed shotgun (seen): Lucia fires and pumps a shotgun one-handed while hauling a duffel bag (about 9:22), with its own slower animation. Carrying loot changes how you fight.
  3. Shopkeeper's shotgun (seen): rob the Food Mart and the clerk chases you outside firing (about 12:14).
  4. Double-barrel shotgun (preview): in an official Grassrivers screenshot, not in any trailer yet.
  5. The Ammu-Nation wall (seen): Trailer 2's TV ad (2:11 to 2:13) shows racks of pump shotguns, AKs and ARs behind the counter, with Shrewsbury and Duke boxes.

Shotguns are numbers 17 to 21 on our list.

Sniper rifles

  • 22. Sniper rifle (seen): in the Launch Trailer, Jason lines up a long scoped rifle over the night skyline.
  • 23. Duke assault sniper (preview): a Duke-branded semi-automatic sniper in an official Grassrivers screenshot.

Heavy weapons

  • 24. Grenade launcher (seen): a revolving drum-fed launcher. In the Launch Trailer, Lucia fires it off an overpass onto police cars; a red-haired accomplice uses one in Trailer 2 (about 1:58).
  • 25. Belt-fed light machine gun (seen): the Ammu-Nation owner hefts one with an ammo box in the Trailer 2 ad. The first machine gun in official footage.

Throwables, melee and gear

  • 26. Molotov cocktail (seen): Lucia selects it from the throwable slot and burns the getaway car to destroy evidence (16:01).
  • 27. Fists (seen): unarmed has its own wheel slot, and street fights use contextual prompts.
  • 28. Knuckle rings (seen, unclear): a man flexes a hand of linked rings in the Launch Trailer. Weapon or jewellery is unclear.
  • 29. Baseball bat (preview): official Port Gellhorn screenshot.
  • 30. Hammer (preview): reported from Trailer 1, but we could not find it in our copy.
  • 31. Pool cue and 32. Mini golf club (preview): both in Cal Hampton screenshots.
  • 33. Switchblade (confirmed name): named beside the Morgan revolvers in Rockstar's caption. It may be a prop.
  • 34. Binoculars (seen): in the wheel's gadget slot with their own zoom view, ideal for casing a job.

Every weapon brand so far

  • Duke: the Duke 556, the Duke assault sniper, and Duke boxes in Ammu-Nation. GTA 6's main rifle maker so far.
  • Moreland: the 850 pump shotgun.
  • Girardi and Klose: the leads' handguns.
  • Shrewsbury (back from GTA 5): boxes in the Ammu-Nation ad.
  • Hawk & Little (back from GTA 5): the Ultimate Edition revolvers.
  • Mustang: the .357 revolver.

Expect each brand to cover a family of weapons at launch, the way Vom Feuer and Shrewsbury did in GTA 5.

How to get weapons in GTA 6

  1. Ammu-Nation (confirmed back via the Trailer 2 ad). Rockstar's Rob Nelson told IGN that all weapons you have purchased are always available in the back of your personal vehicle. Reports say stores sell weapons and body armour; attachments are unclear. Prices and store locations are not shown yet.
  2. Enemy drops: Jason takes the Duke 556 and the Moreland 850 straight off downed gunmen, with a named pickup prompt each time.
  3. Police cars: Edge reported patrol cars carry shotguns or rifles you can take from an unattended cruiser. Risky, but free.
  4. Storage: your trunk and weapon lockers in some garages and safehouses are where you swap loadouts.
  5. Editions: the Vintage Vice City pre-order pack includes a weapon finish; the Ultimate Edition adds the Hawk & Little Morgan revolvers and engraved guns for Jason and Lucia.

GTA 5 vs GTA 6 weapons: what changed

  • Carry limit: every gun in your pocket in GTA 5; two handguns and two long guns in GTA 6.
  • Aiming: GTA 5 leaned on lock-on; GTA 6 reportedly turns hard lock-on off by default, with heavier recoil and three-colour hit markers.
  • World reactions: GTA 5 pedestrians mostly ran; in GTA 6 they notice guns, clerks shoot back, customers grab your weapon, and police track what you used.
  • The wheel stayed, now with a separate items tab.

Every gunfight Rockstar has shown

  1. The drug-lab raid (Extended Look about 4:10 to 5:50): Jason picks up the Moreland 850 mid-fight and fires from cover and outside it.
  2. The heist prep (9:11): gearing up at the trunk, then Lucia's one-handed shotgun escape with the bag.
  3. The Food Mart robbery (about 12:00): the clerk chases them out with a shotgun, and a weapon icon joins the wanted stars.
  4. The police chase (about 14:00): Raul's job, Jason leaning out with the 40-round carbine while a police car flips.
  5. The jewellery store (about 18:00): Lucia holds the room with her K17, and a customer grabs at it.
  6. The skyscraper siege (about 23:00 to 25:30): white-suited mercenaries with AK-pattern rifles; Jason fights floor by floor and picks up the Duke 556.
  7. The swamp squad (Launch Trailer): night vision and suppressed rifles, pointing to stealthy night missions.
  8. The overpass (Launch Trailer): Lucia's grenade launcher on police cars, beside the gold AK duo.
  9. The Trailer 2 bank job: the MP5-style SMG pushed into a manager's face.

Rumoured weapons (not confirmed)

Leak-derived fan lists

Fan databases list weapons from the 2022 leak: a Micro Draco, MAC-11, SCAR-style rifle, combat machine gun, taser, sticky bombs, RPG and more. Instagram posts claim a speargun, and one wiki lists flashbangs and smoke grenades in Trailer 2; we checked Trailer 2 frame by frame and found neither. Claims that dual wielding is gone, or that the carry limit is two long guns, one pistol and one melee, trace back to unofficial footage and conflict with the previews.

Best weapons in GTA 6 (our predictions)

Nobody has played GTA 6 yet, so these are predictions from what Rockstar has shown, building up to the one we think matters most:

  • Most underrated: the Molotov. Burning your getaway car to lose the heat could be the smartest move in the game.
  • Best for police chases: the grenade launcher. Nothing ends a six-star pursuit faster than a drum of grenades into a roadblock.
  • Best sidearm: whichever pistol you keep concealed. With two hidden handguns allowed, it is the gun you will always have, even after losing your car.
  • Best close range: the Moreland 850. So much of GTA 6 happens indoors, in stores, bars and apartments, that a hard-hitting pump shotgun will win most of those fights.
  • Best rifle: the Duke 556. It is named, enemies drop it, and assault rifles will be your main long gun in almost every big shootout.

We will update this list on launch day with every gun in the game and where to find it.

Frequently asked questions

How many weapons are in GTA 6?

Rockstar has shown 34 weapons and items so far in its trailers, An Extended Look and official screenshots. The full list will only be known at launch on November 19, 2026.

How many guns can you carry in GTA 6?

Previews say two concealed handguns plus two long guns, one slung and one in your hands. The rest are stored in your car trunk or weapon lockers.

Is Ammu-Nation in GTA 6?

Yes. Trailer 2 shows an Ammu-Nation TV ad with a wall of guns, and Rockstar's Rob Nelson confirmed weapons are purchased.

What is the Duke 556 in GTA 6?

An assault rifle named on screen by a pickup prompt in An Extended Look, around 25:09. It holds 30 rounds in the magazine.

What guns do Jason and Lucia use?

Rockstar names Jason's personal pistol as the Girardi ES9 and Lucia's as the Klose K17.

Does GTA 6 have weapon attachments?

Suppressors, red dots and scopes are visible in official footage, but no attachment menu has been shown, so how you add them is unknown.
