GTA 6 Fast Food Tie-Ins: KFC's Chicken 'Disc', a Cluck n' Bell Car and Popeyes' Spice City

Fast food chains turned GTA 6's launch week into a menu. KFC Spain handed out a fried chicken "disc" to fans who brought in their GTA 6 case, KFC UK & Ireland delivered meals in a hot pink Cluck n' Bell Cadillac, and Popeyes launched a double-chicken Spice City Sandwich in the US that runs until today, November 22.

None of these is an official Rockstar collaboration as far as the reports say. Here's what each chain did, where and when, with the source for every claim.

KFC Spain: 'The Replacement Disc'

REPORTED: Dexerto (Dylan Horetski, Nov 19) and Que.es (Nov 18)

GTA 6's physical edition ships with a download code instead of a disc, and KFC Spain made a joke of it. Its promotion, "The Replacement Disc", gave fans a limited-edition fried chicken disc in a case with Colonel Sanders on the cover, styled like a video game box.

  • Where: ten KFC restaurants in Madrid, Barcelona, Valencia, Zaragoza and Málaga.
  • When: November 19 and 20, from 4 PM to 8 PM local time.
  • How: bring your original GTA 6 case. Free while supplies lasted.
  • Why: KFC Spain framed it as a response to the industry moving away from physical media.

The promotion has ended. Dexerto doesn't say Rockstar was involved or commented.

KFC UK & Ireland: a real Cluck n' Bell car in London

REPORTED: Dexerto (Dylan Horetski, Nov 17); "not an official collaboration with Rockstar Games"

KFC UK & Ireland borrowed the vibe of GTA's Cluckin' Bell for "Cluck n' Bell", a launch-day delivery in a hot pink 1959 Cadillac convertible. The driver wore a Hawaiian shirt and sunglasses, opened the trunk at the winner's door and served the food on a custom tray.

  • Who could win: London residents aged 18+, through a competition form linked from KFC's Instagram on November 17. Winners were contacted November 18 and the deliveries happened on launch day, November 19.
  • The box: Tenders Bucket, Tower Burger, Twister Wrap, Mini Fillet Burger, fries, an Iced Coffee Kwench and Kwench Cloudy Lemonade.
  • Everyone else: free KFC delivery on Just Eat across the UK from 6 PM on November 19.

KFC's Phoebe Syms said the idea is "keeping one hand on the controller and the other the chicken" (via Dexerto).

Popeyes: the Spice City Sandwich (last day today)

REPORTED: Popeyes press release via Dexerto (Michael Gwilliam, Nov 12)

In the US, Popeyes launched the Spice City Sandwich for GTA 6 week: two crispy chicken breast fillets on a toasted brioche bun with barrel-cured pickles and classic or spicy mayo. In short, the regular chicken sandwich with double the chicken. It's available nationwide November 19 to 22 on the Popeyes app and online. Dexerto didn't list a price, so check the app.

Don't mix it up with the London 'Cluckin' Bell' shop

REPORTED: Gameranx (Ryan Parreno, April 22, 2026), citing Dexerto

Earlier this year a London takeaway put up a Cluckin' Bell sign with a copy of the game's logo, and people wondered if it was a Rockstar stunt. It wasn't: Gameranx reported the logo came down, the sign was changed to "The Cluckin' B", and Dexerto found the business had registered as "The Cluckin Bite, Ltd". Take-Two and Rockstar didn't comment.

Frequently asked questions

Is KFC still giving away the GTA 6 chicken disc?

No. KFC Spain's 'Replacement Disc' ran only on November 19 and 20, from 4 to 8 PM, at ten restaurants in Spain, while supplies lasted.

Is the Cluck n' Bell car an official Rockstar collab?

No. Dexerto reports KFC UK & Ireland's Cluck n' Bell delivery was not an official collaboration with Rockstar Games.

How long is the Popeyes Spice City Sandwich available?

November 19 to 22, 2026, in the US, ordered online or in the Popeyes app, per the Popeyes press release reported by Dexerto.
