The fastest car in GTA 6 found so far is the Pegassi Infernus '96, with a Speed rating of 7.1 out of 10, ahead of the Grotti Cheetah '95 (6.6) and the Pfister Comet '88 (6.1). That's the ranking on GTABase's list, built from the in-game vehicle stats and last updated November 19, launch day.

One big catch: GTA 6 doesn't show a real top speed in mph or km/h. The number in the stats card is a rating, and GTABase says it's still testing each car on the road to measure real speeds. So treat this as the best ranking we have three days after launch, not the final word. Below: the full top 30, the fastest car in each class, the two fast cars you get free with the Ultimate Edition, and how the rating compares with Jason's own Ganado.

Labels: REPORTED = from GTABase's fastest cars list (updated Nov 19, 2026). ON SCREEN = stats we read ourselves in launch-stream footage. OUR TAKE = our read. Rockstar has published no official top speeds.

What is the fastest car in GTA 6?

REPORTED: GTABase, in-game Speed rating, Nov 19

The Pegassi Infernus '96 sits at #1 with a 7.1 Speed rating, half a point clear of anything else on the list. It's a Sports Classic, so it's a 90s supercar rather than a modern hypercar, which fits a game set in today's Leonida where the flashiest cars on the street are often older icons. GTABase lists how to get it as "Coming Soon", so its spawn spot or dealer isn't confirmed yet.

#2 is the Grotti Cheetah '95 (6.6), and this one you can own straight away if you have the Ultimate Edition, since GTABase lists it as an Ultimate Edition car. #3 is the Pfister Comet '88 (6.1), the fastest car in the Sports class.

Muscle cars fill the middle of the list: eleven of the top 30 are Muscle class, which suits Leonida's long straight highways and the Keys bridges. If you're doing a Moses car run or one of Randy's deliveries, a fast Muscle car is the easiest thing to find on the street.

Ultimate Edition: two fast cars for free

REPORTED: GTABase lists both as Ultimate Edition cars
  • Grotti Cheetah '95: #2 overall at 6.6, the fastest car you can own without hunting for it.
  • Sirius Transgressor '67: #30 at 4.5. It's part of Wyman's classic-car questline that starts with the Ultimate Edition Dominator Buggy (see our Ultimate Edition guide).

Is Jason's Ganado fast?

ON SCREEN: Ganado stats card in launch-stream footage

Not really. We read the stock '70 Ganado's stats card ourselves in launch-stream footage for our cars guide: Speed 4.7, Acceleration 5.3, Asphalt Handling 3.6. GTABase lists the same 4.7, which puts it at #21 of 30. The Infernus '96 is rated about 50% higher. Upgrades at a mod shop change the stats, so a tuned Ganado will climb this list.

Why the Speed rating isn't a real top speed

REPORTED: GTABase

GTABase points out that GTA 6 shows no top speed figure anywhere, and that the Speed stat is a rating rather than mph or km/h. A higher rating should mean a higher top speed, but two cars with the same rating can still feel very different once acceleration, handling and braking come in. That's why GTABase says it's driving each car in-game to measure real top speeds, and why its ranking "isn't final yet".

Watch out for exact mph numbers. Some sites already list GTA 6 cars at 130-155 mph. Rockstar hasn't published any, and the game doesn't show any, so a number with no test video behind it is a guess. We'll add measured speeds here only when a tester shows the run on video.
OUR TAKE: for missions and getaways, the Speed rating matters less than you'd think. In GTA 6 the cops track your car and your face (see our wanted level guide), so a fast car with a tracker you can't remove can be worse than a slower clean one. For pure speed runs across the Keys, the Infernus '96 and the Cheetah '95 are the two to chase.

Frequently asked questions

What is the fastest car in GTA 6?

So far, the Pegassi Infernus '96, with a Speed rating of 7.1 out of 10 on GTABase's list (updated Nov 19, 2026). The Grotti Cheetah '95 (6.6) and the Pfister Comet '88 (6.1) are next. The ranking isn't final because GTABase is still testing real top speeds in-game.

What is the top speed of cars in GTA 6 in mph?

The game doesn't show one. GTA 6 only gives a Speed rating out of 10, and Rockstar hasn't published mph or km/h figures, so any exact mph number online is an estimate until someone measures it on video.

What is the fastest Ultimate Edition car in GTA 6?

The Grotti Cheetah '95, #2 on the list with a 6.6 Speed rating. The Ultimate Edition also includes the Sirius Transgressor '67 (4.5).

How fast is Jason's Ganado in GTA 6?

The stock '70 Vapid Ganado has a Speed rating of 4.7 (#21 on GTABase's top 30). Mod shop upgrades raise its stats.
