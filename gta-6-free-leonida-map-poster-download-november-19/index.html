GTA 6 Free Leonida Map + Poster: How to Get It on November 19

GTA 6 does not come with a paper map, but Rockstar is giving one away for free. On November 19, 2026, launch day, Rockstar says it will add an official printable Grand Theft Auto VI cover art poster and map of Leonida as a free digital download on its official site. A double-sided physical poster follows on the Rockstar Store shortly after launch.

OFFICIAL: Rockstar Newswire, 'Grand Theft Auto VI Pre-Load Begins on November 10' (Nov 3, 2026)

What Rockstar announced

Rockstar slipped the news into its pre-load post on November 3. The key line, as shared by GTAVIoclock and quoted by Nerd's Chalk: "On November 19 we will be adding an official printable Grand Theft Auto VI cover art poster and map of Leonida as a free digital download to the official site. A double-sided physical version of this poster will also be made available shortly after launch."

You don't need to own or pre-order GTA 6 to grab the printable files. They're free downloads on Rockstar's site. Etsy and other shops already sell unofficial 'GTA 6 map' posters, so wait for the free official one.

The $0.99 physical poster

REPORTED: price and free-with-purchase detail via Nerd's Chalk and THM Magazine (via GTAVIFRANCE)

Rockstar's own wording only promises a double-sided physical version of this poster "shortly after launch". Press reports add that it costs $0.99 (€0.99) on its own at the Rockstar Store and comes free with any Rockstar Store purchase. Shipping costs and which regions it ships to haven't been confirmed, so check the store page before you order.

Is there a map in the GTA 6 box?

No. The physical GTA 6 box holds a download code, not a disc, and reports list no paper map inside. We opened one ourselves in GTA 6 code in a box, unboxed. That's why the free download matters: it brings back the paper map that came in the box with older GTA games.

OUR TAKE

Old-school fans always had a map in the box. A free print-at-home file plus a $0.99 poster is Rockstar's cheap way to keep that tradition alive while the game itself is a code.

What we still don't know

  • How much of the state the map shows. Rockstar only says "map of Leonida". It could be the whole state or one area.
  • Whether it's annotated with region names and landmarks, or is clean art.
  • File size and print quality. That decides how big you can print it.
  • Whether the physical poster's map side matches the printable map.

Until the file lands, our GTA 6 map regions explained page covers all six official Leonida areas, and how big the GTA 6 map is next to GTA 4, GTA 5 and RDR2 covers the size.

How to get it on launch day

  1. On November 19, open Rockstar's official GTA VI site (rockstargames.com/VI).
  2. Find the poster and Leonida map downloads. Rockstar hasn't shown the exact button yet.
  3. Save both files. Print at home on A4/Letter, or take the file to a print shop for A2/A3 if the resolution holds up.
  4. Want the real thing? Order the double-sided physical poster from the Rockstar Store once it's listed (reported $0.99, free with any order).

We'll update this page with the direct link, file size and a look at the map as soon as it goes live. Every GTA 6 launch story is on our GTA 6 news hub, and all release times are in GTA 6 release time in every region.

Frequently asked questions

Does GTA 6 come with a map?

Not in the box. The box holds a download code. Rockstar is adding a free printable map of Leonida and a cover art poster to its official site on November 19, 2026.

Is the GTA 6 Leonida map free?

Yes. Rockstar says the printable poster and map are free digital downloads. A double-sided physical poster is reportedly $0.99, or free with any Rockstar Store order.

Do I need to own GTA 6 to download the map?

No purchase has been mentioned. Rockstar describes it as a free download on the official site.

When can I get the physical GTA 6 poster?

Rockstar says shortly after launch, on the Rockstar Store. No exact date has been given.
