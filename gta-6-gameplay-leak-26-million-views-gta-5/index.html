That 'GTA 6 Gameplay Leak' With 26 Million Views? Look Closer

By vAnother Level · Updated 2026-11-17

A 16-second clip captioned "GTA 6 GAMEPLAY LEAK" has passed 26 million views on X since November 15, days before GTA 6 launches on November 19. It shows a Lucia lookalike shooting at police from a rooftop. It isn't GTA 6 footage: the account calls itself a parody account, and the city in the clip is GTA 5's Los Santos.

GTA 6 GAMEPLAY LEAK

— GameJoker (@GameJoker_) November 15, 2026
NOT OFFICIAL: parody account

How we know it's not GTA 6

  • The account says so. GameJoker's bio reads "#1 Source for PARODY Gaming News/Leaks".
  • The map is GTA 5's. The roads, crossings and rooftops match Los Santos, not Leonida's Vice City.
  • The HUD is GTA 5's. The wanted stars and minimap style are from GTA 5, not the HUD Rockstar has shown for GTA 6.
  • A mod watermark is visible, and the top replies are already calling it out.

How to spot a fake GTA 6 leak

  1. Check the account bio for words like parody, satire or fan-made.
  2. Compare the map with Rockstar's official trailers: Leonida has Vice City's neon, swamps and Keys, not Los Santos's hills.
  3. Look for watermarks from modders or editors.
  4. Check rockstargames.com/newswire: real GTA 6 news comes from Rockstar first.
  5. Wait 48 hours: GTA 6 is out on November 19, 2026, so real gameplay is days away.
