GTA 6 has 10 garages you can buy or unlock: eight cost between $4,000 and $40,000, and two are free (one from Moses in the story, one with the Ultimate Edition). You buy them in the Vice City Real Estate app on your phone or by walking up to them. Safehouses are different: you can't buy a house, they come with the story.

This page lists every garage with its price and where it is, the cheapest and best ones to buy first, how vehicle storage works, and what we know about safehouses and businesses. GTA 6 unlocks on November 19 (release time in your region).

Labels: OFFICIAL = Rockstar's own words. REPORTED = outlets that played the review copy or Rockstar's press previews. OUR TAKE / THEORY = our read. We'll check every garage ourselves after launch and update this page.

The pre-order garage (Shore Court)

OFFICIAL: Rockstar's pre-order bonus announcement (via Gematsu and Game Informer, June 24)

If you pre-ordered, there's one more. The Vintage Vice City Pack pre-order bonus includes a '55 Vapid Stanier and the Shore Court garage, which comes with a weapon locker and a fencing drop-off for stolen goods. GTABase places it on Shore Drive in Ocean Beach. It isn't in GameSpot's list of 10, so treat it as an extra on top. Full details of both bonus packs are in our editions guide.

How to buy a garage in GTA 6

REPORTED: GameSpot (Joey Carr) garage guide, Nov 18, matched by Sportskeeda (Jagaran Dutta) the same day
  1. Open your phone (up on the D-pad) and go to the Vice City Real Estate app, or drive to the garage and buy it in person.
  2. Pick a garage. Prices depend on location: GameSpot says garages near Vice City or in areas with pricier cars cost the most, and ones in the middle of nowhere cost the least.
  3. Pay. You need the cash in your bank, and you don't get a bank account until the story mission Buck Me (beginner tips).

GameSpot also spotted garages that only show up on the map when the game automatically stores a vehicle for you. You can't buy those, and they tend to sit near wherever you're living in the story.

Why garages matter: the 3-car limit

REPORTED / PREVIEW: Rockstar's August 2026 press previews, as collected by GTABase (Aug 31)
  • Each character can own several personal vehicles, kept at your safehouse, but you start with room for only 3 cars. Buying garages adds space.
  • Stolen cars are temporary until you register them at a Pay 'N' Spray. Unregistered cars eventually despawn. A garage lets you keep one without paying for it, GameSpot says.
  • Personal and stolen cars double as mobile storage for spare guns, gear and outfits.
  • Leave a personal car behind with police around it and it gets impounded. You can sneak in to take it back, or pay a fee later.
  • High-end cars have trackers that a fence has to remove before you can register or sell them. Our money guide covers the car-selling side.

Can you buy houses or safehouses in GTA 6?

REPORTED / PREVIEW: Rockstar's August 2026 press previews, as collected by GTABase (Aug 31)

No. In the August previews, Rockstar told press that residential safehouses can't be bought. They unlock through the story as Jason and Lucia's living situation changes, a bit like the camps in Red Dead Redemption 2. This came from press previews, not a Rockstar announcement, so we label it REPORTED.

What a safehouse does, per the previews and GTABase:

  • Save your game, change outfits and store your personal vehicles.
  • Trim or shave hair and facial hair (new hairstyles still need a salon).
  • Jason's bedroom has loose floorboards to stash cash before you have a bank (VGC).

Can you buy businesses in GTA 6?

UNCONFIRMED: no outlet has reported a business you can buy for passive income

Not as far as anyone has reported. GTA 5's story let you buy businesses for weekly income, but nothing like that has shown up in the review-copy guides yet. Money in GTA 6 comes from robberies, fences and car jobs instead, and GTABase's preview recap says every store in Leonida can be robbed. If a property you can buy for income turns up after launch, we'll add it here.

Frequently asked questions

How many garages are in GTA 6?

GameSpot and Sportskeeda both found 10: eight you buy for $4,000 to $40,000 and two free ones (Port Gellhorn Station from Moses in Chapter 2, and Paradise Garage with the Ultimate Edition). Pre-orders also get the Shore Court garage.

What is the cheapest garage in GTA 6?

Mitchel Auto Center in central Kelly County, east of Port Gellhorn, for $4,000 (REPORTED by GameSpot).

How do you buy a garage in GTA 6?

Use the Vice City Real Estate app on your phone or go to the garage in person. You need money in the bank, which opens after the mission Buck Me.

Can you buy a house in GTA 6?

No. Rockstar told press in its August previews that safehouses can't be bought; they unlock through the story. Garages are the property you can buy.

How many cars can you own in GTA 6?

You start with space for 3 personal vehicles, per Rockstar's previews. Buying garages adds more storage.
