GTA 6 Giant Mutant Crab & the Real Easter Egg on the Tallest Tower

Two new GTA 6 secrets went viral on launch day. One is a giant mutant crab that attacks Jason in a rare cutscene. The other is a literal Easter egg in a bird's nest at the top of the game's tallest radio tower, and throwing it off plays its own little scene.

Both come from players, not Rockstar. Here's what has been shown on video, what the outlets report, and what's still a guess.

REPORTED: Kotaku launch live blog, GameSpot, IBTimes UK (Nov 18-19, 2026). Rockstar hasn't commented.

The giant mutant crab

The clip that started it came from chumBucket (@ChumBucketDrama) on X, posted Nov 18 as "GTA 6 RARE MONSTER CRAB CutScene". Kotaku put it in its launch live blog and in its roundup of animal encounters, simply calling it a giant enemy crab.

Kotaku also says it is less clear how the player reached this encounter. Nobody has posted a confirmed step-by-step trigger yet.

THEORY / UNVERIFIED
  • A WikiGames entry says the crab hides in a house in Gullet Marsh and ambushes you when you go inside. It cites no source, so treat it as unconfirmed.
  • The same entry says you can fight back if you react fast enough. No reward has been reported.
  • YouTube creators call it a radioactive or mutant crab and link it to Florida B-movies. That's fan theory, not something the game has said.

The real Easter egg on the tallest tower

This one has much more detail. xGarbett (@xGarbett) on X posted it on Nov 18, and GameSpot, IBTimes UK and Kotaku all wrote it up.

  1. Find the tallest radio tower. Outlets don't agree on where it is: GameSpot says Vice City, GamerHour says Vice-Dale County, and IBTimes UK says the exact spot isn't confirmed yet.
  2. Climb to the top. It takes about six to seven minutes (Kotaku says about six, GameSpot and IBTimes UK say about seven).
  3. Find the bird's nest. It holds several brightly painted eggs. Interact with it to pick one up.
  4. Throw the egg off the tower. A short cutscene plays: the egg hits the windscreen of a car driving below.

GameSpot describes the driver as a scummy-sounding real estate agent or lawyer of some kind, and other write-ups say realtor, so that detail differs between reports. IBTimes UK adds that you can also talk to an influencer at the top.

Kotaku's writer says they found the egg the week before launch but never thought to throw it. Grab one before you climb back down.

Why this matters

Leonida is clearly full of secrets. In under 48 hours players have found Bigfoot at Cottonmouth Lake, a zombie game inside the game, and now a giant crab and a tower Easter egg. We're adding every confirmed one to our GTA 6 easter eggs list and the GTA 6 news hub.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a giant crab in GTA 6?

Yes. A player clip shared on X on Nov 18 shows a giant mutant crab cutscene, and Kotaku reported it. How to trigger it isn't confirmed yet.

Where is the giant crab in GTA 6?

One unsourced wiki says a house in Gullet Marsh. That hasn't been confirmed by Rockstar or a major outlet.

How do you get the Easter egg in GTA 6?

Climb the tallest radio tower (about 6-7 minutes), take a painted egg from the bird's nest at the top, and throw it off. A cutscene shows it smashing a car's windscreen.

Did Rockstar confirm these secrets?

No. Both are player discoveries reported by Kotaku, GameSpot and IBTimes UK.
