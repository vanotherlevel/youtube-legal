Police Want GTA 6 Out So Everyone Stays Home and They 'Catch a Break'

By vAnother Level · Updated 2026-11-09

  2. Then vs now: from Hot Coffee to 'please release it'
  3. The police are in the game too

Even the police are counting down to GTA 6. The Greensboro Police Department in North Carolina posted on X that Rockstar's game needs to "hurry up and get here" so people stay home and officers can "catch a break". The post passed 1 million views, and Dexerto picked it up today.

Real post from the official @GSO_Police account (November 6, 2026)

The post

GTA 6 needs to hurry up and get here so y'all can stay at home and let us catch a break.

— Greensboro Police Department (@GSO_Police) November 6, 2026

When we checked it on November 9, it had about 39,000 likes, 3,100 reposts and 300 replies. It's clearly a joke, and that's how the replies took it. Nobody is claiming a video game release changes crime numbers.

Then vs now: from Hot Coffee to 'please release it'

Here's why this one is funny if you've followed the series for a while. Twenty years ago, Grand Theft Auto was the game politicians and lawyers lined up to attack:

  • 2005: the "Hot Coffee" scandal hit GTA: San Andreas. Then-Senator Hillary Clinton called for a Federal Trade Commission investigation into the game.
  • 2000s: attorney Jack Thompson ran years of lawsuits and campaigns against GTA. He was permanently disbarred in Florida in 2008, the same year GTA IV came out.
  • 2026: a US police department is publicly asking for GTA 6 to come out sooner.

The police are in the game too

The joke works both ways. Rockstar's trailers are full of Leonida's version of the news: police body-cam clips, a corrections mugshot and a TV segment titled "Dirt-Bike Dirt-Bags". Our hero shot is an officer from Trailer 2.

When does GTA 6 come out?

November 19, 2026, on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, so Greensboro's officers have 10 days to wait. PS5 owners are already pre-loading: read GTA 6 Is Pre-Loading on PS5. To plan your first hours, see Every Gang Explained and the GTA 6 hub.
