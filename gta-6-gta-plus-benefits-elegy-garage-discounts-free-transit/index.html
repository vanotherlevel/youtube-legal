Yes, GTA+ now does something inside GTA 6's story mode. Members reportedly get a widebody '90 Annis Venturas BLVD Elegy and a Crosstown garage from Chapter 3, 10% off at shops across Leonida, 10% more when they sell cars, and free rides on public transit, rental scooters, bikes and Seasharks.

The list surfaced on November 18, when players in New Zealand got into the game first, and was written up by GameSpot, RockstarINTEL, TrueAchievements, GamesRadar and TweakTown. Rockstar hasn't published a GTA 6 benefits page of its own yet, so we label the perks REPORTED below.

RockstarINTEL has the fullest list. GameSpot calls the Elegy a free supercar, and TweakTown first picked up the discounts and free rentals from a post on r/GTA6.

How much GTA+ costs

REPORTED: RockstarINTEL

RockstarINTEL lists GTA+ at $7.99 / £6.99 / €7.99 a month on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Digital pre-orders on PlayStation Store and Microsoft Store came with one free month, which Rockstar's support pages say you redeem from your account. After that it renews at the normal price unless you cancel.

The perks only work while you're subscribed. Cancel, and RockstarINTEL says the boosts and the subscriber items stop working. Don't plan your money or your garage around them if you only have the free month.

How to turn GTA+ boosts off

REPORTED: RockstarINTEL

Some players don't want discounts and boosts in a single-player run. You can keep the membership and switch them off:

  1. Pause the game.
  2. Open the Account tab.
  3. Scroll to GTA+ Boosts.
  4. Toggle them off. You can turn them back on the same way.

Why this is a change

Until launch, GTA+ was a GTA Online membership: a monthly GTA$500,000 deposit, member Shark Card bonuses, free cars and the GTA+ Games Library. When Rockstar announced the free month for GTA 6 pre-orders, nothing said it would touch Jason and Lucia's story, and our editions guide said so at the time. We've updated that page.

It also lands in the same week as the anger over the $99.99 Ultimate Edition, whose extras unlock chapter by chapter, with the Cheetah only arriving in Chapter 5. A monthly subscription that adds a car and discounts to a single-player game will get the same questions. Our full chapter list is in where to find every Ultimate Edition bonus.

OUR TAKE (opinion)

None of these perks are big. 10% off and free scooters won't change how fast you get rich, and the Elegy arrives in Chapter 3, when you can already afford good cars. If you're short on cash early, our money guide does more for you than a subscription. The one perk worth having is the Crosstown garage, because it can fence stolen goods.

  • What GTA+ will include once GTA 6 Online launches (no date yet: see our GTA 6 Online page)

Frequently asked questions

Does GTA+ work in GTA 6 story mode?

Yes, reportedly. Members get a '90 Annis Venturas BLVD Elegy and a Crosstown garage from Chapter 3, 10% shop discounts, 10% more on car sales and free transit and rentals, per RockstarINTEL and GameSpot. Rockstar hasn't posted its own list yet.

How much is GTA+ for GTA 6?

RockstarINTEL lists $7.99 / £6.99 / €7.99 a month. Digital pre-orders got one month free.

What happens if I cancel GTA+ in GTA 6?

The perks only work while you're subscribed, so the discounts, boosts and subscriber items stop working when the membership ends.

Can I turn off GTA+ boosts in GTA 6?

Yes. Pause, open the Account tab, scroll to GTA+ Boosts and toggle them off. You keep the membership.
