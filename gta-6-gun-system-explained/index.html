In GTA 6, your guns can't disappear any more. Rockstar North's Rob Nelson says it is the first time in the series that weapons can't be hidden: you carry two concealed handguns plus two visible long guns, and everything else lives in the trunk of your personal vehicle or an Ammu-Nation locker.

That one rule changes everything around a gun: how people on the street react, how the police describe you, how it sounds, how it's held, and what a bullet does to a body. This guide breaks down all five systems from our video, with every claim labelled and sourced. GTA 6 launches November 19, 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, and we will update this page once we have tested it in the real game.

Labels on this page: OFFICIAL = Rockstar's own footage, slides or interviews with Rockstar staff. PREVIEW = press previews (Edge, IGN, Famitsu, creators). THEORY / MY READING = our own read. No leaked GTA 6 material is used.

How many guns can you carry in GTA 6?

OFFICIAL: Rob Nelson to TGG and Famitsu
  • Two concealed handguns, always on you and hidden.
  • Two long guns (rifles, shotguns, SMG-size weapons), which are visible: one on your back, one in your hands.
  • Everything else goes in the trunk of your personal vehicle or a locker at Ammu-Nation. Nelson called the old system an "invisible arsenal hidden away".

In GTA V you could pull a shotgun out of nowhere in front of a crowd and nobody reacted. Nelson told TGG that just doesn't work any more with how sophisticated Leonida's population is. He also told Famitsu the team debated it for a long time, worried they were losing the fun of pulling a rocket launcher out of thin air, before deciding that more choices make the game more engaging.

PREVIEW
  • Any car can hold a long gun for a while, but only your personal vehicle keeps your full loadout.
  • Previews say an in-game app can deliver your car to you with the arsenal already in the trunk.
  • Jason is seen stealing a shotgun from the boot of an unattended police car, so guns can be found in the world.
  • Jason and Lucia can reportedly hand guns to each other. Dual wielding was not seen.

Do NPCs react to your guns?

OFFICIAL: Rob Nelson

Yes. According to Nelson, a long gun slung on your back should be fine, but a gun in your hand makes people uneasy and they start moving away from you. In the Extended Look, Jason and Lucia even hide their guns behind their backs before walking into a store. Nelson compared it to Red Dead Redemption 2, where Arthur could keep his gun holstered or point it at someone mid-conversation, and said GTA 6 takes that idea to an entire city.

PREVIEW: Edge

The police build a description of you that includes your weapon, and dropping an identifiable gun helps you shake them off. The wanted display also shows whether the police know your face, your clothes and whether you're armed. The wanted level now goes up to six stars instead of five, which the Extended Look confirmed.

Shoot to miss: GTA 6 lets you not kill

OFFICIAL: Rob Nelson to Famitsu

Nelson told Famitsu there's a new mechanic that lets you control the direction of your gunfire, so instead of shooting directly at the police you can deliberately miss. The goal was to give players the choice not to kill police or civilians while getting away. His example: shooting the tyres of a police car flipped it over and no officers died. Shooting the front or the windows of a car changes its condition and how the driver reacts.

PREVIEW

Previews say this feeds into how the game judges you: threatening or incapacitating someone counts differently from killing them, and firing back at someone who shot first is judged less harshly than a random spree.

Red and yellow targeting, hit markers and the kill cam

OFFICIAL: Extended Look

The Extended Look shows a slow-motion aim that marks the body: red for lethal shots (head, chest) and yellow for joints and limbs, where a shot can incapacitate or disarm instead of kill. GTA 6 also has a kill cam, a descendant of the one Rockstar built for Max Payne 3 in 2012.

PREVIEW
  • Coloured hit markers: white = hit, yellow = incapacitated, red = kill.
  • The kill cam can reportedly be switched off.
  • Aim leans toward free aim, with less lock-on by default and aim assist you can tune in the options.

Red Dead Redemption 2's Dead Eye was the warm-up: you slowed time, painted shots and, once upgraded, saw an enemy's weak spots.

Does GTA 6 still use Euphoria physics?

MY READING

Rockstar has used NaturalMotion's Euphoria since GTA IV (2008): instead of a canned death animation, a body's motion is built in real time based on where and how it was hit. It has been in Rockstar's engine through Red Dead Redemption, Max Payne 3, GTA V and Red Dead Redemption 2. NaturalMotion later stopped licensing Euphoria to other studios, but Rockstar kept it in its own engine. Rockstar hasn't named it for GTA 6, so treat it as the same family of tech, not a confirmed feature name.

How GTA 6 gun sounds work

OFFICIAL: Rockstar North GDC 2014 + Famitsu

At GDC 2014, Rockstar North's audio team showed that GTA V's world changes sounds instead of just playing them: distance, occlusion, reflections, reverb, resonances and shockwaves. Close shots are sharp and punchy; distant ones are muffled and reverberant. A gunshot in GTA IV was already around ten separate components, and from GTA IV to GTA V the ambient audio zones grew from 86 to 949 and the rules from 57 to 1,210.

For GTA 6, Nelson told Famitsu the controller's haptic feedback is tuned per weapon, and in the preview demo the assault rifle had more recoil, more sound and more impact than its GTA V version. Rockstar hasn't published a technical breakdown of GTA 6's audio yet.

Reloads, animations and the Take-Two recoil patent

OFFICIAL
  • GTA V's first-person mode needed around 3,000 new animations for the weapons alone (Rob Nelson, then animation director).
  • Red Dead Redemption 2 reloaded one round at a time. Nelson admitted they went deeper than they should have; testers found some reloads too slow, so they were dialled back.
  • Art director Aaron Garbut said Leonida's people have around 600,000 animations.
  • Nelson says GTA 6 retargets animations on the fly, so characters of every height and shape work together.
MY READING

Take-Two patent US 2025/0095261 A1 describes adjusting animations at runtime for different body sizes and objects, and specifically mentions weapon aiming and weapon recoil retargeting. Rockstar hasn't linked the patent to GTA 6, but it describes exactly what Nelson talks about.

OFFICIAL: Extended Look

Handling changes you can see in the footage: Lucia swaps between two equipped guns without the weapon wheel, Jason grabs an enemy's Duke 556 off the floor, and Lucia fires a shotgun one-handed while carrying a bag.

Verdict: did Rockstar kill the fun?

MY READING

Rockstar took away the rocket launcher in your pocket. In exchange, every gun has a sound mixed to the street you're on, hands built for whoever holds it, a body that reacts to where it's hit, and a city that remembers what you carried. Frequently asked questions

How many weapons can you carry in GTA 6?

Two concealed handguns plus two visible long guns (one on your back, one in your hands). The rest of your arsenal stays in your personal vehicle's trunk or an Ammu-Nation locker, per Rockstar's Rob Nelson.

Can you play GTA 6 without killing people?

Partly. Rob Nelson told Famitsu you can deliberately miss and aim at tyres or limbs to escape without killing police or civilians, and red/yellow targeting marks lethal vs disabling shots. Whether every mission allows it is unknown until launch.

Does GTA 6 have lock-on aim?

Previews report less lock-on by default and a lean toward free aim, with aim assist you can tune in the options.

Can you turn off the kill cam in GTA 6?

According to previews, yes. The kill cam is shown in the Extended Look and can reportedly be switched off.
