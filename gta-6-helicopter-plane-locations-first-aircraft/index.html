The fastest free aircraft in GTA 6 are in Southwind, about 2.5 miles southwest of Jason's house in Key Lento: Mammoth Dodo seaplanes sit at a small hangar by the dock, and a Buckingham Maverick helicopter sits on a dock right next to it, with nobody guarding either. After that, every county has unguarded airstrips and helipads, and any rooftop marked with an (H) icon on the map almost always has a helicopter on it.

Below: the exact Southwind route, every helicopter and plane spot reported so far, county by county, why you should skip Vice City International Airport, and how to get a fighter jet out of Mile Zero Air Station alive. Everything comes from guide writers playing the released game, all credited at the bottom.

Where to find your first plane in GTA 6

REPORTED: PC Gamer (Christopher Livingston, Nov 18), launch-week guide

You start the story in the Keys, and the first plane is closer than the game makes it look. The story points you north toward Hamlet and Vice City. Go south instead.

  1. From Jason's house in Key Lento, drive southwest through Key Lento.
  2. Keep going west through Abismo Key, then west and north into Southwind. You stay on the Sunbelt Highway (Route 1) the whole way; you know you're close after two long bridges.
  3. As you enter Southwind the highway bends north. On your right there's a concrete barrier beside the road. Where it ends, turn right into the parking lot at the sign for SBW (Sherwin Boatworks) and Chicki's Tiki Bar.
  4. Face the water (east). On your right (south) is a small hangar with a few Mammoth Dodo seaplanes inside and around it, and often a couple more moored off the dock.
  5. Drive one into the water and take off, or steal one straight from the dock.

PC Gamer reports there's never anyone around, so taking one doesn't draw attention. Seaplanes aren't the fastest planes in the game, but they're the best first plane: they land on water and on land, and they need almost no room to get airborne. That makes them ideal for exploring the Keys and the swamps, where runways are rare.

Where to find your first helicopter in GTA 6

REPORTED: PC Gamer (Christopher Livingston, Nov 18)

Same trip, same parking lot. Instead of looking south toward the hangar, look straight east from the parking lot entrance. An open gate leads out to a dock where a Buckingham Maverick helicopter is parked. Ignore the no-trespassing sign, run onto the dock and get in. No wanted level, no fight.

Southwind sits right next to Mile Zero Air Station, GTA 6's military base. It also has planes and helicopters, but they're heavily guarded. Grab the free seaplane or the Maverick first; the base is a separate job (below, and in our military base guide).

Rooftop helipads in Vice City

Zoom in on the map and look for the (H) icon. PowerPyx reports there's almost always a helicopter on a marked helipad. In Vice City most of them are on skyscraper roofs: walk into the building's lobby and take the elevator to the roof. DualShockers adds that Tequesta, just south of Downtown, is full of helipads, with at least two holding helicopters, which matters for the jet escape below.

Why you should skip Vice City International Airport

REPORTED: PowerPyx (Nov 18)

The airport looks like the obvious place to steal a plane, but the jets there carry trackers. PowerPyx reports that stealing one gets you a wanted level with fighter jets sent to shoot you down. Every spot in the table above is unguarded, so there's no reason to start a war for a plane. If you do end up wanted, our wanted level guide covers how to lose the heat.

How to get a fighter jet in GTA 6

REPORTED: DualShockers (Adam Beck, Nov 19)

DualShockers reports there's only one place on the whole map with fighter jets: Mile Zero Air Station, next to Southwind, the same town as your first seaplane. It's the most heavily guarded location in the game, with armored vehicles, tanks and fighter jets on site. Staying undetected is described as almost impossible.

  • Front entrance (near Southwind): heavily guarded. DualShockers' trick is to get into the armored vehicle usually parked by the entrance and drive it in.
  • From the sea (stealth): the port on the western side of the base is still guarded but has no fences, so you have much more room to move. A seaplane or boat from Southwind gets you there.
  • Once you're airborne, the base puts you on radar straight away and sends fighter jets to intercept. Expect a 5-star wanted level.

The jet escape trick

  1. Fly the stolen jet north to Downtown Vice City or Tequesta.
  2. Climb as high as you can. The pursuing jets follow you up.
  3. As soon as you level off, bail out. You need a parachute for this, so buy one before you fly.
  4. Free-fall until you're level with the skyscrapers, then open the chute and steer onto a Tequesta helipad with a helicopter on it.
  5. While the jets are still busy high above, fly the helicopter out of the wanted zone.

DualShockers calls it tricky but doable. Their jet was damaged beyond repair on the way, so treat the jet as a joyride, not a vehicle you keep. Full base details, including the tank, are on our Mile Zero military base page.

Best aircraft for each job

OUR TAKE, based on the reports above
  • Exploring the Keys and swamps: a Southwind seaplane. Water landings mean you can touch down almost anywhere south of Vice City.
  • Clearing map fog: the Key Lento airstrip plane (long fuel, per PowerPyx), then swap at Port Gellhorn or central Kelly County when it runs dry.
  • Getting around Vice City: a rooftop helicopter. Land on any (H) roof and you're at street level by elevator.
  • Crossing the whole map: the central Kelly County airstrip puts you within quick reach of everything, which is why PowerPyx rates it.
  • Pure chaos: the Mile Zero fighter jet, with the parachute escape ready.

Frequently asked questions

Where is the first helicopter in GTA 6?

In Southwind, about 2.5 miles southwest of Jason's house in Key Lento. Turn into the SBW / Chicki's Tiki Bar parking lot, look east, and go through the open gate to the dock: a Buckingham Maverick is parked there, unguarded (PC Gamer).

Where can I find a plane in GTA 6 early?

Mammoth Dodo seaplanes sit at the small hangar in the same Southwind parking lot, and PowerPyx reports a plane on the airstrip at Key Lento near Jason's house.

Can you steal a plane from the GTA 6 airport?

Yes, but PowerPyx reports the jets at Vice City International Airport have trackers, so you get a wanted level and fighter jets come after you. Use an unguarded airstrip instead.

Where is the fighter jet in GTA 6?

DualShockers reports fighter jets are only at Mile Zero Air Station next to Southwind. Getting one out means a 5-star wanted level; their escape is to bail out with a parachute over Tequesta and take a rooftop helicopter.

How do I find helipads in GTA 6?

Zoom in on the map and look for the (H) icon. There's almost always a helicopter on one, and in Vice City you reach rooftop helipads by the building's elevator (PowerPyx).
