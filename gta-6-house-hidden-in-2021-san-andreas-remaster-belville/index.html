A GTA 6 House Was Hiding in the 2021 San Andreas Remaster

By vAnother Level · Updated 2026-11-19

A house that appeared in GTA San Andreas: The Definitive Edition back in 2021 has been matched to a real house in GTA 6, in the Belville area of Leonida. The match was found by Jared from the GTA VI Mapping server and shared by the GTA Wiki on X on November 18, 2026, the day before launch.

REPORTED: GTA Wiki on X, covered by VGC and Notebookcheck. Rockstar hasn't commented.

If the match holds, fans may have had a look at a piece of GTA 6 two years before Trailer 1, inside a remaster almost nobody was studying for clues.

Players had long called it "the GTA 6 house" because the image only existed in the remaster, so people guessed it came from Rockstar's next game. Launch week is the first time anyone could check it against the real map.

Which came first?

THEORY

There are two ways to read it. Either Rockstar's GTA 6 team built the house first and it slipped (or was planted) into the 2021 remaster as an easter egg, or the remaster asset was later reused and rebuilt for GTA 6. Notebookcheck leans toward GTA 6 coming first but notes there's no way to prove it. Rockstar hasn't said.

Our take: the match is too close (same lawn, same two palms) to be a coincidence, but "first look at GTA 6" is still a fan reading, not a Rockstar statement. We'll update this page if Rockstar or a developer comments.

How to see it yourself

  1. Open the GTA Wiki's Belville page or its Nov 18 post on X for the side-by-side images.
  2. In GTA 6, head to Belville on the Leonida map and look for the single-storey house with two palm trees on the front lawn.
  3. Use Photo Mode to line up the same angle as the 2021 image.

Frequently asked questions

What is the GTA 6 house in San Andreas Definitive Edition?

A house added in GTA San Andreas: The Definitive Edition (2021) that wasn't in the 2004 original. On Nov 18, 2026 the GTA Wiki matched it to a house in GTA 6's Belville area.

Where is the house in GTA 6?

In Belville, Leonida, according to the GTA Wiki. It has the same structure, front lawn and two palm trees as the 2021 version.

Who found the GTA 6 house match?

Jared from the GTA VI Mapping server, as part of the GTA Wiki's GTA 6 Mapping Project. The GTA Wiki shared it on X.

Did Rockstar confirm it was a GTA 6 tease?

No. Rockstar hasn't commented. That it was a deliberate early look at GTA 6 is a fan theory.
