To heal in GTA 6, get out of combat and let your health regenerate, or use a healing item from the ITEMS tab of the weapon wheel: hold the wheel button, press R1/RB to switch tabs, then Square/X for the health item and Triangle/Y for Adrenaline. For tougher fights, wear body armor bought at Ammu-Nation and carry Zombix for instant health.

Healing in Leonida also ties into a light RPG layer: food, sleep and the gym change how your Health, Stamina and Focus bars behave. Below is everything reported so far, from Rockstar's own gameplay footage, Rockstar North's Rob Nelson, and launch-week guides. Every claim is labelled, because Rockstar has not published an official healing guide.

How to use healing items from the weapon wheel

OFFICIAL: seen in Rockstar's Extended Look gameplay

GTA 6 puts guns, gear and consumables on one radial wheel with two tabs, WEAPONS and ITEMS. In Rockstar's own extended gameplay footage, the wheel shows a red pill bottle with a heart icon (health) on Square and an energy shot (adrenaline) on Triangle, each with a stock count such as x2. You use them without leaving the wheel, and the body armor slot sits on the same wheel.

  1. Hold the weapon wheel button (L1 on PS5, LB on Xbox Series X|S).
  2. Press R1/RB to switch from WEAPONS to ITEMS.
  3. Press Square/X to use your health item, or Triangle/Y for Adrenaline.
  4. Watch the count: at x0 the button does nothing until you restock.
The wheel does NOT pause the game. Opening it only blurs the background, so get behind cover before you heal. (Seen in the Extended Look footage, reported by AllThings.How.)

Zombix vs Adrenaline: which one to use

REPORTED: PowerPyx trophy guide (Nov 18), Beebom (Nov 19)

Zombix is the straight heal: it restores health on the spot. Adrenaline products give a short health boost and, according to PowerPyx, extend how long your combat Focus lasts, GTA 6's slow-motion aiming ability that highlights enemy weak points. Focus drains the purple bar under your health and stamina in the top-left of the HUD.

PowerPyx's advice for the hardest robbery trophy is to go in with an armored vest, sticky bombs and Zombix/Adrenaline equipped. Both are reported to be sold in shops. Exact prices and which store chains stock them haven't been confirmed by a big outlet yet, so we'll add them once we've checked in-game.

Body armor: where to buy it

REPORTED: PowerPyx (Nov 18)

Body armor is bought at Ammu-Nation. PowerPyx counts 24 Ammu-Nation gun stores across Leonida and recommends wearing armor so you can walk out of cover and close in on enemies, especially for the Gunslinger and Retail Therapy trophies. The same stores sell sticky bombs, and their weapon lockers hold special guns you unlock in missions.

Every gun, attachment and Ammu-Nation price we've confirmed is on our GTA 6 every weapon page, and the gun mechanics are explained in GTA 6's gun system.

Does health regenerate in GTA 6?

REPORTED: Beebom (Nov 19)

Yes. Staying out of combat slowly regenerates health. No outlet has published the exact rate or the cap yet. In GTA 5, natural regeneration stopped at half health, but we have not seen that confirmed for GTA 6, so don't count on it.

Sleep, food and the gym: how they change your bars

OFFICIAL: Rockstar North's Rob Nelson (preview interviews, Sept 2026)

Rob Nelson, co-studio head at Rockstar North, said an in-game health app tracks whether Jason and Lucia are well rested. If they aren't, it affects the regeneration of certain attributes. Rockstar hasn't said which ones. He also said stamina is raised through exercise, and overall health improves by working out.

REPORTED: TGG preview via GamesRadar+, Insider Gaming and GTA Intel; PowerPyx
  • Gym: buy a membership at a fitness club. Completing three exercises in one session finishes the day's workout and helps raise health; three strength exercises reportedly give a timed health boost.
  • Cardio: treadmills, bikes and calisthenics raise stamina for longer sprinting, swimming and climbing.
  • Protein shakes and bars: a temporary strength and stamina boost.
  • Food and drinks: can push Health, Stamina and Focus above their normal maximum for a short time. PowerPyx also says eating, drinking or showering at home buffs Focus.
  • Weight: Rockstar has confirmed eating too much or too little changes your weight, and skipping sleep makes Jason and Lucia look run-down.
  • Training together: while you work out with one character, the other completes their training automatically (PowerPyx).

The gym is optional and counts toward completion. See our 100% completion checklist and the barber, clothes, tattoos and gym guide.

Our take: a simple healing loadout

OUR TAKE: not from Rockstar
  1. Before any robbery or big shootout, buy armor at the nearest Ammu-Nation.
  2. Keep at least two Zombix and two Adrenaline on the ITEMS tab. Restock whenever you pass a shop.
  3. Eat or drink at home before a mission for the temporary bar boost.
  4. Sleep in your safehouse bed to refill Health and Stamina, and save right after. Our how to save guide explains the 15 slots.
  5. Hit the gym early. Higher health and stamina make every chapter easier, and it's needed for 100%.

Frequently asked questions

How do you heal in GTA 6?

Stay out of combat to regenerate, or open the weapon wheel, press R1/RB to switch to the ITEMS tab, and press Square/X for your health item (Zombix) or Triangle/Y for Adrenaline. First aid kits, food and sleeping in your bed also restore health.

Where do you buy body armor in GTA 6?

PowerPyx reports body armor is sold at Ammu-Nation gun stores, of which there are 24 across Leonida.

What is Zombix in GTA 6?

Zombix is a consumable that restores health directly. Guides report it's bought in shops and used from the ITEMS tab of the weapon wheel.

Does sleep matter in GTA 6?

Yes. Rockstar's Rob Nelson said a health app tracks whether Jason and Lucia are well rested, and poor sleep affects the regeneration of certain attributes. Sleeping in your bed also refills Health and Stamina.
