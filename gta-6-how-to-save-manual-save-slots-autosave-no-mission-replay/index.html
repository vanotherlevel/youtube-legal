How to Save in GTA 6: 15 Slots, Autosave & No Mission Replay

To save in GTA 6, open the pause menu (Options on PS5, Menu on Xbox Series X|S), go to Game, then pick Save Game and choose one of the 15 manual save slots. You can do it anywhere in free roam, but not during the prologue and not while a mission is active.

That sounds simple, but GTA 6 has two traps that can cost you hours: there is no mission replay, and loading an older save overwrites your single autosave. Below is how saving works, what blocks it, how cheats affect it, and the 15-slot plan the guide sites recommend. Everything comes from guides published on launch eve (November 18) by outlets with early access, and every claim is labelled.

How to save your game in GTA 6 (step by step)

REPORTED: GameRant, PowerPyx, KeenGamer (Nov 18)
  1. Finish the prologue first. Manual saving unlocks once the opening ends and Jason reaches his house at the marina, which guides say usually happens after the first job for Brian.
  2. Make sure no mission is active. The Save option is greyed out during missions.
  3. Press Options (PS5) or Menu (Xbox Series X|S) to open the pause menu.
  4. Go to the Game tab and select Save Game.
  5. Pick an empty slot, or overwrite one you no longer need.
  6. Wait for the save to finish writing before you quit to the main menu or close the game.
You do NOT need a safehouse or a bed to save in GTA 6. Unlike older GTA games, the reports agree you can save anywhere in free roam from the pause menu.

How many save slots does GTA 6 have?

REPORTED: GameRant, PowerPyx, KeenGamer

Some guides describe the autosave as a hidden 16th slot; others call it 15 manual slots plus one autosave. Either way it works the same: one autosave that keeps replacing itself, and 15 slots that only change when you save over them.

When you can't save in GTA 6

  • During the prologue. You rely on autosave until Jason reaches his marina home (REPORTED: GameRant, KeenGamer).
  • During any active mission. Finish the mission or leave it, then open the pause menu again (REPORTED: PowerPyx, KeenGamer).
  • After using a cheat. PowerPyx and KeenGamer report that any cheat disables saving (and trophies/achievements) for the rest of that session. Fully close and relaunch the game to save again. Cheats only unlock late in the game anyway; see our GTA 6 cheats list.

Autosave: when it saves and the overwrite trap

REPORTED: PowerPyx, GameRant, KeenGamer

GTA 6 autosaves after you complete missions and open-world events. There is only one autosave, so each new one wipes the previous one.

The trap: loading an older manual save overwrites your autosave (PowerPyx). If you load a save from two hours ago just to check something, the autosave from a minute ago is gone. Before you load anything, make a fresh manual save in a spare slot.

GameRant also warns that leaning only on autosave can lose you things like a rare vehicle, or lock in a story choice you didn't want. KeenGamer's advice is to treat the autosave as crash protection and keep your real checkpoints in manual slots.

Does GTA 6 have mission replay?

REPORTED: PowerPyx mission replay guide (Nov 18)

No. PowerPyx reports GTA 6 has no mission replay and no chapter select at launch. That covers main story missions and the Strangers & Freaks side missions, and there are no mission gold medals. Some optional missions also become unavailable as the story moves on, and safehouses change between chapters.

So your manual saves are the only way back. If you want to replay a mission you loved, revisit an old safehouse, or catch a side mission you missed, you need a save from before that point. Our missions and chapters page covers the 5-chapter structure.

THEORY

GTA 5 and Red Dead Redemption 2 both had mission replay, so Rockstar could add it in a later patch. Nothing official says so yet.

Safehouses and beds

REPORTED: KeenGamer

You don't need them to save, but safehouses still matter. KeenGamer reports Jason's first one is a stilt house in Key Lento in the Leonida Keys, which Brian lets him live in rent-free. Sleeping in the bed skips the night and refills Health and Stamina, and loose floorboards in Jason's bedroom work as a cash stash until bank access unlocks. Dying or getting arrested costs the cash you're carrying.

Frequently asked questions

How do you save in GTA 6?

Open the pause menu (Options on PS5, Menu on Xbox), go to Game, then Save Game, and choose one of 15 slots. It works anywhere in free roam, after the prologue and outside missions.

How many save slots does GTA 6 have?

Guides report 15 manual save slots plus one autosave slot that overwrites itself.

Can you replay missions in GTA 6?

No. PowerPyx reports there is no mission replay or chapter select at launch, so keep manual saves at the start and end of each chapter.

Why can't I save in GTA 6?

You're either still in the prologue, in an active mission, or you used a cheat this session. Cheats disable saving until you restart the game.
