How to Steal Cars in GTA 6: Slim Jim, Key Cloner & Trackers

In GTA 6 you can still smash a window and hotwire a car, but most cars are locked and alarmed, and keyless cars can't be hotwired at all. You unlock three tools through the story: the Waink It scanner app in Chapter 1, the slim jim in Chapter 2 and the key cloner in Chapter 3. Then you have to beat the car's tracker and sell it or keep it.

REPORTED: everything on this page comes from PC Gamer's hands-on guides published Nov 18 (Christopher Livingston, Andrea Shearon), written from review copies. Rockstar hasn't published a car-theft guide

Method 1: smash the window and hotwire

Walk up to the driver's door and press the interact button. The game tells you whether the car is locked (most are), alarmed (nearly all are) and whether it's keyless.

  1. Choose to smash the window.
  2. Jason or Lucia climbs in. Hotwiring takes a second or two: move the control sticks to find the "sweet spot".
  3. Drive off. On an alarmed car the alarm goes off, which can draw attention, so leave fast.

This doesn't work on keyless cars. You can break the window, but you can't hotwire one or drive it away. You need the key cloner for those (below).

Method 2: carjack a driver

You can still pull people out of their cars, but many fight back. You have to mash a button to win. If you lose, they slam your head against the car and drive off. And plenty of Leonida drivers are armed and will shoot.

  • You can't slim jim a car someone is sitting in. If the door is locked, you have to break the window.
  • Carjacked cars can still have trackers, so check the warning before you get comfortable.

Scan first: the Waink It app

In Chapter 1's sixth story mission, Unwanted Attention, Cal helps you install Waink It on your phone. Point it at a car to see:

  • Make and model
  • Locked, alarmed, keyless, and what kind of tracker it has
  • The car's condition and how much it sells for
  • What it costs to register it at a Pay 'n' Spray (that's how you make a stolen car yours)
  • Whether Moses or Randy will buy it
Scan before you steal. It takes a second and tells you if the car is worth the heat: cheap old car for Moses, flashy car for Randy, or a car nobody wants.

Method 3: the slim jim (quiet, no alarm)

You get the slim jim in Chapter 2's fifth mission, "Moses", right after Lucia's Girls' Trip mission. Moses, an auto mechanic, gives it to you and shows you how it works.

  1. Walk up to a locked car door and select Slim Jim.
  2. Find the sweet spot with the analog stick for about a second.
  3. The door pops open: no broken glass and no alarm. It's a little slower than your elbow.
  • Check the trunk: a slim-jimmed car lets you pop the trunk without the alarm. PC Gamer says trunks can hold weapons, drugs or cash.
  • Some cars only need unlocking, then you hotwire them as usual.
  • The slim jim won't start a keyless car.
OFFICIAL: Rockstar's Extended Look gameplay shows the Slim Jim and Smash Window prompts at a parked car (6:07). The unlock mission is REPORTED by PC Gamer

Method 4: the key cloner (keyless cars)

Keyless cars are new to the series and can't be hotwired. You get the key cloner in Chapter 3, mission 16, "Steal the Zorruso for Randy". It's a long wait, but it's the best tool in the set.

  1. Stand next to the keyless car's door and press interact.
  2. Select Clone Key. It runs on your phone.
  3. Find the sweet spot with the control stick to speed it up.
  4. The car unlocks and starts on its own. Drive.

The catch: keyless cars usually come with trackers.

Trackers: standard vs advanced

NoneNothingOlder cars. Good for Moses

PC Gamer's tip: you have to drive pretty fast to keep an advanced tracker quiet, so getting the car to a Pay 'n' Spray or Randy's garage without wrecking it is the real challenge. Plan a route on wide roads before you clone the key.

Bonus: the snap gun (doors, not cars)

Not a car tool, but people search for it with the others. Yasmani gives you the snap gun in Chapter 3, after the jewelry heist in "Dubious Provenance". Equip it at a locked door, pick Use snap gun, find the sweet spot with the left stick and hold the right trigger until the meter fills.

  • It gets you through the door without tripping the alarm, but the alarm is still armed. Kicking other doors, smashing display cases or a store worker can still set it off.
  • Use it on a store's back door to reach the office and switch the alarm off completely.

Our quick plan

OUR TAKE: based on the reported systems above, not a tested route
  1. Chapter 1: scan everything with Waink It. Smash and hotwire only cheap, non-keyless cars.
  2. Chapter 2: switch to the slim jim. Quiet entries, check every trunk, sell old cars to Moses.
  3. Chapter 3: with the key cloner, go after Randy's flashy cars. Skip advanced-tracker cars if you're far from a Pay 'n' Spray.
  4. Use your 3 Pay 'n' Spray sales a day, and keep the best car in a garage.

Frequently asked questions

How do you steal a locked car in GTA 6?

Smash the window and hotwire it (loud, sets off alarms), or from Chapter 2 use the slim jim to open it quietly. Keyless cars need the key cloner from Chapter 3.

When do you get the slim jim in GTA 6?

Chapter 2, in the fifth mission "Moses", right after Lucia's Girls' Trip mission. Moses gives it to you, per PC Gamer.

How do you get the key cloner in GTA 6?

It unlocks in Chapter 3's 16th mission, "Steal the Zorruso for Randy". It unlocks and starts keyless cars, which can't be hotwired.

How do you beat an advanced tracker in GTA 6?

Keep driving fast. When you slow down it beeps, and you have a few seconds to speed up before it sends your location to the police.

How many cars can you sell a day in GTA 6?

Three per day at Pay 'n' Spray, per PC Gamer. Moses buys older low-end cars and Randy buys certain expensive ones.

What is the Waink It app in GTA 6?

A phone app Cal installs in Chapter 1 (Unwanted Attention). It scans cars for locks, alarms, keyless ignition, trackers, condition, sale value and registration cost.
