How to Unlock Hurricanes in GTA 6: Freddie's Bunker Guide

To unlock hurricanes in GTA 6, break into Freddie's bunker on Lake Leonida twice, finish the hidden side mission Retest Freddie's Defenses, and choose Leave when the hurricane warning appears. After that, guides report that hurricanes join the normal weather system and can turn up at random.

REPORTED: VICE, iGeeksBlog and Output Lag (all Nov 26, 2026). Rockstar hasn't published a guide to this.

Where is Freddie's bunker?

The compound is in North Leonard County, by Lake Leonida. Output Lag puts it on the northwest shore, just below the North Leonard County label on the map and close to the North Kelly County line. iGeeksBlog describes it as north of the lake. Nothing marks it, so set your own waypoint.

Output Lag's landmarks: a dirt road over a rail crossing, a toppled water tower on your right and a tall electric fence covered in yellow warning signs. The front gate stays shut. Freddie is a doomsday prepper and podcaster, and outlets identify him with the rapper Freddie Gibbs.

What to bring

  • A rifle or other automatic weapon. Drones attack on both visits and explode when they reach you.
  • Healing items. Both visits have proximity landmines.
  • Focus mode (L3 + R3 on PS5, per iGeeksBlog) to spot the mines.

First visit: the tunnel

  1. From the gate, walk right along the fence to a yard with stacked shipping containers, one orange container and a yellow bulldozer.
  2. Get in the bulldozer and push the orange container aside. A hatch and ladder are underneath.
  3. Follow the tunnel under the fence and climb up inside the compound. The alarm goes off.
  4. Shoot or walk around the landmines and shoot the drones down early.
  5. Use the intercom at the arched concrete bunker. Freddie won't let you in yet. He says he'll upgrade his defenses and text you.

Waiting for Freddie's text

The second visit starts when Freddie contacts you. Output Lag says this takes at least a few in-game days and can take much longer, that sleeping doesn't seem to speed it up, and that the text may only arrive when you're near the bunker. If nothing shows up, drive back to North Leonard County and check your phone. Make a manual save before going back in.

Second visit: Retest Freddie's Defenses

  1. The tunnel no longer works. Push the orange container against the fence with the bulldozer, climb the blade and then the container, and jump over. You can also climb the water tower beside the fence and drop onto a roof inside.
  2. Clear the yard slowly. There are more mines, and a second wave of drones.
  3. Go to the bunker door. This time Freddie lets you in.

Choose Leave to see the hurricane

Inside, Freddie gives a tour and puts you on his podcast. Then his screen shows a hurricane warning and he asks whether you're staying or going. Pick Leave. Your character takes Freddie's Dubsta (a G-Wagon style SUV) out of the garage straight into the storm.

Output Lag reports that choosing Stay plays a short party montage, skips a week of story time and opens onto clear weather. It says you get the same cash and the same Dubsta either way.

Reward

  • Hurricane weather, which can then occur at random.
  • Freddie's Dubsta. Output Lag says to register it if you want to keep it, like any other car. Our garage guide covers how.
  • Cash: Output Lag reports roughly $5,000 to $7,500, varying between saves. If you drive off before he pays, it says the money arrives on BuckMe with a text.
NOT SETTLED: guides disagree slightly on where on the lake the compound sits, and no one has shown a way to switch hurricanes off again.
Sources: VICE (credits @BlueThunderReal on X) · iGeeksBlog · Output Lag. More in our GTA 6 guides.

Frequently asked questions

Does GTA 6 have hurricanes?

Yes, but guides report they don't happen until you finish the hidden side mission Retest Freddie's Defenses.

Where is Freddie's bunker in GTA 6?

By Lake Leonida in North Leonard County. It isn't marked on the map. Output Lag places it on the northwest shore.

Why hasn't Freddie texted me?

It can take several in-game days or longer. Output Lag suggests driving back near the bunker and checking your phone.

Should I leave or stay during the hurricane warning?

Choose Leave if you want to drive into the storm.
