GTA 6 has 39 Illegal Gang Operations spread across 9 gangs: hideouts, stash houses and fronts you can raid for safe cash, narcotics and key valuables. They show up as a yellow icon once you walk into the area, they respawn after a cooldown, and robbing one from every gang unlocks the Pest Control trophy. All 39 are also one of the full-count lines on the 100% checklist.

Below: how to find them, what counts as "fully robbed", the gang-by-gang list with every name found so far, the best fence for the drugs you pick up, and a quick route for Pest Control. Everything comes from two guide sites playing the released game, both credited at the bottom.

How to find Illegal Gang Operations

REPORTED: PowerPyx (Nov 18) + KeenGamer (Rafly Wibowo, Nov 19), launch-week guides
  • They are not on the map until you physically enter the area. Then a yellow icon appears and stays.
  • Unvisited sites are labelled "Robbery Opportunity"; visited ones show their real name (for example Kingpin Bowling or Casa Guardia).
  • Filter the map: Map > Robberies (money bag icon) > Illegal Operations.
  • Track progress in Progress > Gangs, which shows discovered and completed counts for each gang.
  • Hover the icon on the map to see the security level and the key valuables inside before you go in.
  • PowerPyx's 100% guide reports that 19 of the 39 get marked through story progress; the rest you find by exploring.

How to rob one (and make it count)

REPORTED: PowerPyx (Nov 18) + KeenGamer (Rafly Wibowo, Nov 19), launch-week guides

An operation only counts as robbed when you take every key valuable: the safe plus each item marked on the icon, such as unique weapon variants and luxury watches. Killing the guards and grabbing the drugs is not enough. If a gang still shows 0 completed after a raid, you missed a valuable or the safe.

  1. Hover the icon to check security and the valuables list.
  2. Bring a suppressed weapon. Police are only called if the crime is seen or heard.
  3. Take guards out one at a time from cover.
  4. Find the randomized safe code somewhere on the premises or on a downed guard. Focus Mode (L3 + R3 on PS5) highlights useful objects. Our safe codes guide covers this in detail.
  5. Loot the safe, every key valuable and the narcotics.
  6. Leave the area and sell the drugs at a fence (table below).
Heat: KeenGamer reports gang hideouts draw less police heat than banks, stores or jewelry jobs, because cops respond slowly and only come if a witness reports you or the fight gets loud. A long shootout still brings patrols and a normal wanted level. Heat also lingers in the area after you lose your stars: change clothes and ditch a car the police logged. More in our wanted level guide.

Best fence for gang narcotics

REPORTED: KeenGamer fence table (Nov 19)

Respawns: can you rob them again?

REPORTED: PowerPyx (Nov 18) + KeenGamer (Rafly Wibowo, Nov 19), launch-week guides

Yes. A completed operation turns grey, then respawns after a cooldown and can be robbed again for more cash and drugs. Neither guide gives an exact timer yet. That makes your favourite hideout near Tyrone a repeatable money run once the story opens up Vice City.

Pest Control trophy: fastest route

REPORTED: KeenGamer route; trophy name and rule also in PowerPyx

Pest Control (Bronze) = rob one Illegal Operation from each of the 9 gangs. KeenGamer's suggested order:

  1. Grassrivers: a Kingfishers site plus La Polilla Negra's Palmer Fishing Warehouse.
  2. Port Gellhorn: a Kolts & Associates operation.
  3. Ambrosia: a Final Chapter operation.
  4. La Perle: a San4San operation, then sell the drugs to Tyrone.
  5. Southside: one of the two PTT Youngin$ operations.
  6. Finish with M7, The Guardia Brothers and Other Criminals, checking Progress > Gangs as you go.

Our take

OUR TAKE

Gang operations are the best mix of money and progress in GTA 6: each raid moves the 39/39 line, the $100,000 narcotics line and Pest Control at the same time, with less heat than a bank. Go in suppressed, look for the safe code before the shooting starts, and save your drugs for Tyrone. For the full picture see the 100% completion checklist and the GTA 6 guides hub.

Frequently asked questions

How many Illegal Gang Operations are in GTA 6?

39, across 9 gangs, reported by PowerPyx and KeenGamer. The Ultimate Edition's bonus PTT Youngin$ store is extra and doesn't count.

Why doesn't my gang operation count as completed?

You have to take every key valuable: the safe and each marked item such as unique weapons and watches. Killing guards and taking drugs isn't enough.

Do Illegal Operations respawn in GTA 6?

Yes. They turn grey after you rob them and come back after a cooldown, so you can rob them again.

Where should I sell gang narcotics in GTA 6?

Tyrone in La Perle, Vice City pays the most (from Chapter 3) and stays after the story, per KeenGamer.

How do you get the Pest Control trophy?

Rob one Illegal Operation from each of the 9 gangs. Cheats disable trophies for that session.
