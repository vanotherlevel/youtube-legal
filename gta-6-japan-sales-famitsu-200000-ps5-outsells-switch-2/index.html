GTA 6 Sells 200,759 Boxed Copies in Japan as PS5 Passes Switch 2

By vAnother Level · Updated 2026-11-27

GTA 6 sold 200,759 physical copies in Japan in its first four days, going straight to No. 1 on Famitsu's chart for November 16 to 22. The same week, the PS5 family sold 41,472 consoles and moved ahead of Nintendo Switch 2, which sold 36,817. Digital store purchases aren't in the game figure.

The software chart

REPORTED: Famitsu estimates, via VGChartz, PlayStation Universe and Pro-Gamer (Nov 26)
RankGameUnits that week
1Grand Theft Auto VI (PS5)200,759
2The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time (Switch 2)35,295
3Nintendo Switch Sports Resort (Switch 2)10,486
4Rhythm Heaven Groove (Switch)7,830
5Tomodachi Life: Living the Dream (Switch)4,570

GTA 6 sold almost six times as many boxes as the game in second place. It went on sale on Thursday, November 19, so the total covers four days.

PS5 hardware jumped

REPORTED: Famitsu hardware estimates
  • PS5 family: 41,472. Of those, 40,362 were the Digital Edition, 1,031 the PS5 Pro and 79 the standard model.
  • Switch 2: 36,817.
  • Switch family: 4,362.
  • Xbox Series X|S: 751.

Pro-Gamer reports that the PS5 sold 11,136 units the week before, against 42,402 for Switch 2. That makes this week's PS5 total a rise of about 272%. The same outlet says it's the first week PS5 has beaten Switch 2 in Japan since Switch 2 launched in June 2025.

Pro-Gamer also points to a Japan-exclusive Midnight Black PS5 Digital Edition released on November 19 as a driver of the spike. That's the outlet's analysis: a new console model and a huge game arrived on the same day, so the figures alone can't say how much each one added.

What the number leaves out

Famitsu tracks retail. Copies bought directly from the PlayStation Store aren't counted, so 200,759 is not GTA 6's total sales in Japan. Boxed copies of GTA 6 contain a download code, not a disc, as our UK physical chart story explains.

How it compares with GTA 5

Pro-Gamer puts GTA 5's Japanese debut on PS3 in October 2013 at 360,115 physical copies, well above GTA 6's boxed figure.

OUR TAKE

It isn't a like-for-like comparison. Far more players buy digitally now than in 2013, and Famitsu's retail count doesn't see those purchases. The lower box number doesn't show that fewer people in Japan are playing.

The bigger picture

Japan is one retail market. For the worldwide launch, the company's own estimate is $3.79 billion in retail sales on day one; see why Take-Two shares still slipped. Japan also gets a censored CERO Z version, covered in our Japan and Australia censorship page. More on the GTA 6 news tab.

Sources: VGChartz, William D'Angelo: Japan weekly chart, week ending Nov 22 · PlayStation Universe, Michael Harradence (Nov 26) · Pro-Gamer: Famitsu charts (Nov 26). All figures are Famitsu estimates.

Frequently asked questions
