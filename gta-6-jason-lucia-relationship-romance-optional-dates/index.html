GTA 6 Jason & Lucia Romance: Optional, Dates & What Changes

Yes, GTA 6 has a romance system for Jason and Lucia, and it's completely optional. Rockstar North's Rob Nelson says the relationship grows when you spend time together in Leonida, and you can ignore it with no penalty: the two stay partners in crime either way.

Here is everything Rockstar has said about it, the date activities reported so far, what happens if you neglect Lucia (or Jason), and what we still don't know before the worldwide launch on November 19. Every claim is labelled.

Is the GTA 6 romance optional?

OFFICIAL: Rob Nelson, head of development and co-studio head at Rockstar North, to IGN (via TechRadar)

Yes. Nelson told IGN the romance is up to you. If you're into it, he says it "feels great". If you're not, don't bother. The story itself keeps the pair together for most of the game, so skipping the romance doesn't break anything.

"It's not something that we want to foist upon players." - Rob Nelson, Rockstar North, to IGN

He also called the player "a steward of their relationship", and said that at the very least, Jason and Lucia "will get through most things as partners in crime." In a Guardian studio visit, he summed it up in one line: "The relationship is yours to nurture or yours to lose."

How the relationship system works

REPORTED: IGN interview and demo, Famitsu interview, The Guardian visit (via VICE, MIXED, GTA BOOM)
  • Time together builds it. Nelson describes quality time in the open world. Even robbing a bank together counts, according to VICE's summary of the IGN interview.
  • Texts matter. You can switch between Jason and Lucia for much of the game. When they're apart they can call or message each other. Nelson's example: a goodnight text arrives while you play as the other character. Ignore it and the relationship takes a small hit. A quick reply is enough.
  • You can ask to meet up. Playing as Jason, you can text Lucia to meet, then head out together.
  • Hold hands with R2. In the demo shown to IGN, the pair reunited in free roam, held hands by holding R2, and shared a quick kiss.
  • You can check the status. Famitsu reports you can check Jason and Lucia's relationship status in-game at any time. How it's shown (a meter, a menu tab or something else) hasn't been confirmed.

What happens if you neglect the relationship

REPORTED: IGN interview via VICE

Neglect changes how the two act around each other. If the bond gets weak enough, the hand-holding and kissing options can disappear. Spending time together again rebuilds it. Nelson has also said how much you nurture the relationship affects how the story plays out, but Rockstar hasn't described every possible outcome.

THEORY: our read, not confirmed

Rockstar keeps saying "no penalty" for skipping the romance and also says it shapes how the story plays out. The likely meaning is different scenes and dialogue, not a failed mission or a locked ending. We'll update this page once players confirm it after launch.

Why Rockstar built it this way

GTA 6 is a two-lead story built around a couple, and Rockstar has described it as a Bonnie and Clyde story. Nelson told IGN that playing both characters together, whether escaping the police or waiting for each other, "feels very cool", and that making those moments work took complicated AI systems. So the relationship plays out in gameplay as well as in cutscenes. Who the pair are and who they meet is covered in our GTA 6 story explained guide.

Frequently asked questions

Is the GTA 6 romance optional?

Yes. Rockstar North's Rob Nelson told IGN you can ignore it with no penalty. Jason and Lucia stay partners in crime either way.

Can you date in GTA 6?

Yes, as Jason and Lucia together. Reported activities include beach walks, the zoo, kayaking, billiards, mini golf, scuba diving, parachuting and robberies together.

What happens if you ignore Lucia's texts in GTA 6?

Reportedly a small relationship hit. A quick reply avoids it. Long neglect can remove hand-holding and kissing until you rebuild the bond.

Can Jason and Lucia break up in GTA 6?

Rockstar hasn't said. The story keeps them together for most of the game, and they stay partners in crime even without the romance.
