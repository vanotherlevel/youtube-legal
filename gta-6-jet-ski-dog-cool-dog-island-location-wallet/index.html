GTA 6 Jet Ski Dog: Where to Find Cool Dog & Dog Island ($1,218)

To find the jet ski dog in GTA 6, take a boat out into open water (Gambit Bay in the Leonida Keys is the most reported spot), jump in, swim away from the boat and wait: after a few minutes you hear barking, and a dog in sunglasses rides up on a jet ski to rescue you. Ride on the back without getting off, and he takes you to a small dog island where he digs up a wallet worth about $1,200 (reported as $1,218).

Players are calling him Cool Dog or Lucky Dog; the game hasn't given a name anyone agrees on yet. Below: the exact steps, the two bays where he's been seen, what's on Dog Island, how to get the cash, and the nearby SOS Island secret. Everything comes from players and guide writers on the released game, credited at the bottom.

How to trigger the jet ski dog rescue

REPORTED: Insider Gaming (Tom Henderson), PlayDay.One, GamesHorizon, Nov 19
  1. Get a boat. Insider Gaming takes the boat from the dock at your house. Any boat works: GamesHorizon says you can steal one from the docks in the Keys, the Atlantic side or the Gulf side.
  2. Drive into Gambit Bay in the Leonida Keys (near Key Lento). PlayDay.One's writer also found him in Partridge Bay near Lummox County.
  3. Stop well away from shore, other boats and islands. Open water is the point: the event is about being stranded.
  4. Jump into the water and swim away from your boat. You don't need to dive, and you don't need scuba gear.
  5. Wait. Insider Gaming says about five minutes. Listen for barking: if he doesn't seem to show up, he may already be behind you.
  6. When the jet ski arrives, climb on the back and let the dog drive.
Don't get off mid-ride. GamesHorizon and Prodigy Gamers both say that if you jump off before the jet ski fully stops, you're stranded at sea and miss Dog Island. One PlayDay.One writer also ran out of fuel on the first try, so be patient and try again if it happens.

No source has found a story requirement. Insider Gaming says game progress doesn't matter, so you can do this in the first hour, while you're still in the Keys with Jason. No one has confirmed a time-of-day rule or an exact swim distance, and the spawn may be random, which is why two different bays are reported.

If you start the game in the Keys, Gambit Bay is the easy choice. If he doesn't come after five or six minutes, swim further from shore before you give up, then try the other bay.

What's on Dog Island

Stay on until the jet ski fully stops. The dog drops you at a small island that PlayDay.One and GamesHorizon describe the same way:

  • Several friendly dogs living on the island.
  • Toys everywhere, including fetch toys. Insider Gaming says you can throw him a ball and interact with him.
  • A pair of sunglasses in a box near an upturned boat, which you can loot (GamesHorizon). Yes, they look like his.
  • A trail into the brush. When the dog runs off, follow him. He leads you to a dead body and digs up a wallet.

You can question, praise or scold the dog through the interaction prompts, according to GamesHorizon, though you won't understand his answers. To leave, take his jet ski back to the mainland.

How much money is in the wallet?

REPORTED: amounts differ slightly between sources

GamesHorizon says the wallet holds $1,218, added to your cash automatically. Insider Gaming puts it at $1,200. PlayDay.One repeats the $1,218 figure but says its writer hadn't confirmed it personally. Either way it's an easy early-game top-up, worth doing before your first big purchase. Insider Gaming found no hidden achievement tied to the dog.

Bonus: SOS Island and the 'Send Beer' sign

REPORTED: GamesHorizon (Kirthana K), Nov 19

While you're out on the water: GamesHorizon describes a second island far out in Leonida Bay with SOS written in the sand and a second sign that reads "Send Beer". Walk to the first S to find a phone; watch the video on it to see what happened to whoever was stranded there. There's no cash there, just a good joke.

GamesHorizon also says the Hemming Rugger boat at the Leonida Bay docks in Mariana County comes with free scuba gear, handy if you want to explore underwater on the same trip.

Where did the jet ski dog come from?

The dog went viral before most of the world could even play. On November 18, during the New Zealand early unlock, Out of Context Media (@ooctwts) posted a clip of streamer TGG left speechless as a dog rescued him on a jet ski. Kotaku featured it in its launch live blog, and Windows Central put jet-skiing with dogs in its roundup of the funniest early clips.

THEORY: our read, not confirmed by Rockstar

The sunglasses, the upturned boat and the body near the wallet all suggest a story: the dog's owner was shipwrecked, and the dog now rescues every swimmer it finds. Rockstar hasn't explained it, and none of our sources say anything in the game confirms it. Watch the island closely and decide for yourself.

Frequently asked questions

How do you get the jet ski dog in GTA 6?

Take a boat into open water, Gambit Bay in the Leonida Keys is the most reported spot, jump in, swim away from the boat and wait about five minutes. Listen for barking; a dog in sunglasses arrives on a jet ski and rescues you.

Where is Dog Island in GTA 6?

It's a small secluded island you can only reliably reach by riding the dog's jet ski all the way there. No source has published exact map coordinates yet, so stay on the jet ski until it fully stops.

How much money does the GTA 6 jet ski dog give you?

The dog digs up a wallet next to a body on Dog Island. GamesHorizon reports $1,218; Insider Gaming says about $1,200.

Is there an achievement for the jet ski dog?

Insider Gaming found no hidden achievement or trophy linked to it. Check our trophy list page if that changes.
