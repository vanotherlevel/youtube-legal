GTA 6 JoyStation: All 4 Playable Games Inside GTA 6

Yes, you can play real video games inside GTA 6. The game has an in-world console called the JoyStation, and reports name four playable parody games: Time Jackers: Rewind, Tenebrae Lux, Street Trash 3: DeZombifier and the bar arcade shooter Bang For Your Buck.

Rockstar's head of production Rob Nelson told Kotaku these games come from "an internal dev team within the dev team". They have their own cutscenes, menus and multiple stages, so they play more like small full games than throwaway mini-games.

What Rockstar said

OFFICIAL: Rob Nelson (Rockstar) to Kotaku, Nov 19
  • A small team inside Rockstar designs and builds the in-game games. Nelson called it "an internal dev team within the dev team".
  • GTA 5's arcade games, like Go Go Space Monkey and Let's Get Ready to Bumble, couldn't simply be ported over, so Rockstar made new ones.
  • Tenebrae Lux pokes fun at pretentious indie games, and Nelson said it was made with the "greatest respect and admiration".
  • On the team's attitude: "you couldn't make that without loving it, but we definitely had fun making these."

Time Jackers: Rewind

A top-down sci-fi action game with a cyberpunk look. VGTimes compares it to Hotline Miami, with a much slower pace.

Tenebrae Lux

The joke game of the bunch. It sends up slow, pretentious indie puzzle games. VGTimes notes the name of its fictional creator, Johannes Zuch, appears on screen and in the credits again and again, which it reads as a jab at big-name auteur directors. Kotaku says it's a favourite in its office.

Street Trash 3: DeZombifier

A zombie shooter with runs you replay to level up. Kotaku's reviewer says they played it often, and VGTimes calls it the most well-rounded of the four.

Bang For Your Buck

The only one that isn't on the home console. It's a light-gun arcade game on cabinets in bars, complete with plastic rifles. You shoot bucks and other woodland critters, spare the does, and work through several stages with power-ups.

Where exactly each bar cabinet is, and whether any of these games tie into trophies, hasn't been confirmed yet. We'll update this page once verified locations are out. For every other side activity, see our activities and minigames list.

Why it matters

Rockstar has hidden games inside its games before, but GTA 6 goes further: four separate titles, each with its own style and joke target.
