GTA 6 Launch Bugs So Far: Falling Through the Map, Box Codes & Fixes

GTA 6 launched with no confirmed game-breaking bugs, but players have reported a few glitches: Lucia falling through the map in the prologue, characters clipping through furniture, and a missing door animation in Gone Fishing. The bigger launch-week headaches were outside the game: retail boxes with missing or unreadable codes, and PSN "services are busy" sign-in errors on November 18.

REPORTED: from players on X and outlets (Sportskeeda, egamers.io, VICE, AltChar, TweakTown). Rockstar hasn't acknowledged any of these bugs publicly yet

In-game glitches players found

  • Falling through the map (prologue). X user @BaylenLatona shared footage of Lucia dropping through the ground near the locker or during a flashback. Sportskeeda says she usually respawns where she was a moment later. If she doesn't, restart the game and replay the mission.
  • Clipping through walls and objects. Streamer MichaelDoesLife posted a clip of Jason and a police officer sinking into a couch, and other players reported the same. It's cosmetic, not game-breaking.
  • Missing plane door animation (Gone Fishing). X user @rathor7_ showed Jason clipping straight into the pilot's seat after rescuing Donnie, with no door-opening animation.
  • Performance. Digital Foundry found base PS5 and Series X mostly hold 30fps, while Series S can dip toward 20fps during heavy vehicle destruction. Full breakdown in our Digital Foundry tech review page.

egamers.io's launch tracker (George Tsagkarakis, Nov 19) found no verified mission-breaking bug with a source behind it. That matches what we're seeing: funny glitches, nothing that blocks progress.

Missing or broken box codes

Since November 16, some physical buyers have reported boxes with no code inside, codes they can't read, or codes that won't redeem. TweakTown rounded up reports citing Dexerto and social posts, including a Best Buy customer with no code. RockstarINTEL reportedly couldn't see the code on its own US and UK PS5 copies, and some PEGI boxes have a black strip over it. VideoCardz reports some affected buyers got replacements.

REPORTED: Rockstar hasn't confirmed a widespread box-code problem, and nobody knows how many copies are affected

Fix: go back to the retailer you bought it from. PlayStation Support says code problems are the store's job first. On Xbox, redeem the code on the account that will own the game, because codes can't move between accounts.

PSN "services are busy" sign-in errors

On November 18, as New Zealand unlocked, PS5 players got a "services are busy" message even though their connection test passed. AltChar counted 876 Downdetector reports around 18:00 GMT and over 1,000 by 19:00 GMT. VICE linked many of them to North American players trying the New Zealand region trick. Sony didn't comment, and PlayStation's status page showed everything normal. AltChar found no matching Xbox outage. The full timeline is on our PSN outage page.

Quick fixes that work

  1. Fell through the map: wait a few seconds for the respawn. If it doesn't come, quit to the menu and reload your last save. Our save guide explains how to keep a manual save handy.
  2. Sign-in error: check the PlayStation status page and wait a few minutes rather than spamming retry.
  3. Download stuck at 0%: pause and resume it. If that fails, delete it under Downloads/Uploads and download it again from your Game Library (PlayStation Support steps).
  4. Storage: keep more than 212 GB free. Rockstar lists up to 212 GB for the install, and updates like 1.002.001 come on top.
  5. Gameplay bugs: report them to Rockstar Support. PlayStation sends all patch and bug questions there.
THEORY: small clipping and fall-through bugs like these are the kind usually fixed in early patches. No GTA 6 fix has been announced yet
We'll update this page as Rockstar responds or a patch lands. Every launch story is on our GTA 6 news hub.

Frequently asked questions

Does GTA 6 have bugs at launch?

A few minor ones are reported: falling through the map in the prologue, clipping through objects, and a missing plane door animation in Gone Fishing. No verified game-breaking bug so far (REPORTED).

What do I do if I fall through the map in GTA 6?

Wait a moment, since Lucia usually respawns. If not, restart and reload your last save or replay the mission.

My GTA 6 box has no code. What do I do?

Contact the retailer you bought it from. Some buyers have reportedly received replacements.

Is GTA 6 down?

PSN had sign-in errors on Nov 18 as New Zealand unlocked. Check the PlayStation or Xbox status page and Rockstar Support for the latest.
