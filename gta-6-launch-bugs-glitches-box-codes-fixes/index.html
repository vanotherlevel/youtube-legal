Install GTA 6 update 01.002.002 first: Rockstar says it fixes several crashes, autosave problems, phone calls that didn't trigger and a wanted level you couldn't lose. Some PS5 players still report crashes as of November 25, including error CE-108255-1, so follow PlayStation's official steps below if the update doesn't solve it.

OFFICIAL: fix list from Rockstar Support. Player bug reports below are REPORTED

What update 01.002.002 fixes (November 23)

  • Crashes: several issues that caused the game to crash. Rockstar doesn't say which ones.
  • Weather: the weather not changing for long stretches.
  • Autosave: the game not autosaving correctly.
  • Phone calls: calls not triggering, including calls that move the story forward or unlock mission content.
  • Wanted level: being unable to lose your wanted level.
  • Gym: being unable to use the deadlift bar.
  • Fences: partners not handing over bags so you can sell them.

Sizes, rollout and earlier versions are on our patch notes tracker.

Still crashing after the update? (November 25)

REPORTED: Gizmodo, Kyle Barr, November 25

Gizmodo reports that some players still call the game unplayable on PS5, with crashes at startup or mid-game, and some Reddit users cite CE-108255-1 and other error codes. Nobody knows how many people are affected, and neither Rockstar nor Sony had answered Gizmodo's request for comment.

In-game glitches players found

  • Falling through the map (prologue). X user @BaylenLatona shared footage of Lucia dropping through the ground near the locker or during a flashback. Sportskeeda says she usually respawns where she was a moment later. If she doesn't, restart the game and replay the mission.
  • Clipping through walls and objects. Streamer MichaelDoesLife posted a clip of Jason and a police officer sinking into a couch, and other players reported the same. It's cosmetic, not game-breaking.
  • Missing plane door animation (Gone Fishing). X user @rathor7_ showed Jason clipping straight into the pilot's seat after rescuing Donnie, with no door-opening animation.
  • Performance. Digital Foundry found base PS5 and Series X mostly hold 30fps, while Series S can dip toward 20fps during heavy vehicle destruction. Full breakdown in our Digital Foundry tech review page.

At launch, egamers.io's tracker (George Tsagkarakis, Nov 19) found no verified mission-breaking bug with a source behind it. Rockstar's November 23 notes have since confirmed that some phone calls failed to trigger, including ones that progress the story.

Jason locked out in Chapter 3 (Missing Persons)

REPORTED: IBTimes UK (Yari Chen, November 24), citing GAMINGbible and the GTA6BUGS community tracker. Not confirmed by Rockstar

Some players say they can't progress after the final warehouse meeting in the Missing Persons side mission in Kelly County, early in Chapter 3. Control switches to Lucia, but Jason stays greyed out on the character wheel: he can't be selected and won't answer calls. Some also say Lucia has no story mission to start.

  • Still there after the patch? Players kept reporting it after 01.002.002, and it isn't on Rockstar's list of seven fixes.
  • Possible workaround: the GTA6BUGS tracker suggests loading a manual save made before the warehouse meeting. Rockstar hasn't confirmed that it works, and it needs an earlier save.
  • Before you try that side mission: make a manual save first. Our save guide shows how.
  • Platforms: the tracker lists reports from PS5 and Xbox Series X|S players.

"Checking Out" won't start at the end of Chapter 2

REPORTED: GTA6BUGS community tracker (reported November 21, updated November 24), from Reddit player posts. Not confirmed by Rockstar

Some PS5 and Xbox Series X|S players say that after Afternoon Delight, the move to Vice City scene at the Starlet Motel never triggers, so the next mission, Checking Out, doesn't start. They describe no call, no map marker and no prompt at the motel door.

Check the money first. In the normal flow there is a $15,000 savings target before the move (the tracker cites PowerPyx). If you've met it and nothing happens, these are the player-reported things to try, none of them guaranteed:

  • Restart the console, load a manual save and drive around for a while.
  • Call Cal as Jason, then restart the game.
  • Close the game completely, reopen it and wait for the messages.
  • Do the optional Billy dirt bike mission first.

The tracker says no single fix works for everyone, and as of November 24 it listed no official patch for this one. Keep a separate manual save before you experiment.

Missing or broken box codes

Since November 16, some physical buyers have reported boxes with no code inside, codes they can't read, or codes that won't redeem. TweakTown rounded up reports citing Dexerto and social posts, including a Best Buy customer with no code. RockstarINTEL reportedly couldn't see the code on its own US and UK PS5 copies, and some PEGI boxes have a black strip over it. VideoCardz reports some affected buyers got replacements.

REPORTED: Rockstar hasn't confirmed a widespread box-code problem, and nobody knows how many copies are affected

Fix: go back to the retailer you bought it from. PlayStation Support says code problems are the store's job first. On Xbox, redeem the code on the account that will own the game, because codes can't move between accounts.

PSN "services are busy" sign-in errors

On November 18, as New Zealand unlocked, PS5 players got a "services are busy" message even though their connection test passed. AltChar counted 876 Downdetector reports around 18:00 GMT and over 1,000 by 19:00 GMT. VICE linked many of them to North American players trying the New Zealand region trick. Sony didn't comment, and PlayStation's status page showed everything normal. AltChar found no matching Xbox outage. The full timeline is on our PSN outage page.

GTA 6 crashing on PS5 (error CE-108255-1)

Some PS5 players say GTA 6 crashes with error CE-108255-1 within minutes of starting, often during the opening or just after it. VGTimes (Arkadiy Andrienko, Nov 20) reports that reinstalling, restoring licenses and rebuilding the database didn't fix it for them, while lighter PS5 games still run fine on the same consoles.

After the November 23 patch: Notebookcheck (Adam Corsetti, November 24) reports that CE-108255-1 is still happening on the base PS5, Slim and Pro, with one player still crashing after a few minutes despite reinstalling and rebuilding the database.

REPORTED (VGTimes, from player posts on X): no count of affected players. Rockstar and Sony hadn't commented when it was published

Why would only GTA 6 crash? The suspicion is heat. GTA 6 pushes the console harder than most games, so a PS5 that's clogged with dust, or has worn cooling, may only fail under that load.

THEORY: overheating (dust, or cooling wear on the chip) is a suspected cause, not a confirmed one. Nobody has proven it

What PlayStation's official GTA VI help page says to try

OFFICIAL: PlayStation Support's 'Grand Theft Auto VI game troubleshooting' page
  1. Update your PS5: Settings > System > System Software > System Software Update and Settings.
  2. Restore Licenses: Settings > Users and Accounts > Other > Restore Licenses.
  3. Check free space: Settings > Storage. Compare it with the size shown on the game's View Product page.
  4. Clear Cache and Rebuild Database: start the PS5 in Safe Mode and pick that option. It doesn't delete your saves.
  5. Download stuck? Pause and resume it. If that fails, use Cancel and Delete, then download again from the Game Library.
  6. Still broken on the console itself? The page sends hardware problems to PlayStation Repairs.

If the crash keeps happening at the same point, it's worth the free checks first. Give the PS5 room to breathe: don't put it in a closed cabinet, and clean dust from the vents. Then see whether other demanding games crash too. If only GTA 6 crashes, note where it happens and contact Rockstar Support. Back up your saves before any full reinstall; our save guide shows how saving works.

Don't use Reset PS5 or Reinstall System Software to fix a single game. Those options can wipe the console's data, and neither PlayStation nor Rockstar lists them as a GTA 6 fix.

Quick fixes that work

  1. Update the game: make sure you're on 01.002.002 (1.0.2.2 on Xbox) before trying anything else.
  2. Fell through the map: wait a few seconds for the respawn. If it doesn't come, quit to the menu and reload your last save. Our save guide explains how to keep a manual save handy.
  3. Sign-in error: check the PlayStation status page and wait a few minutes rather than spamming retry.
  4. Download stuck at 0%: pause and resume it. If that fails, delete it under Downloads/Uploads and download it again from your Game Library (PlayStation Support steps).
  5. Storage: keep more than 212 GB free. Rockstar lists up to 212 GB for the install, and updates like 1.002.001 come on top.
  6. Gameplay bugs: report them to Rockstar Support. PlayStation sends all patch and bug questions there.
  7. Crashes on PS5 (CE-108255-1): update the console, Restore Licenses, then Clear Cache and Rebuild Database in Safe Mode. Check the vents for dust and airflow too. [Full steps above](#ps5-crash).
THEORY: Rockstar's notes don't mention the clipping or fall-through glitches above, so they may still happen

Frequently asked questions

Does GTA 6 have bugs at launch?

Yes. Rockstar's 01.002.002 notes confirm fixes for crashes, autosave, phone calls, weather and wanted level bugs. Players have also reported glitches such as falling through the map and clipping.

Why can't I switch to Jason in GTA 6?

Some players report Jason staying greyed out after the final warehouse meeting in the Missing Persons side mission. Rockstar hasn't confirmed it. A community tracker suggests loading a manual save made before that meeting.

Why won't Checking Out start in GTA 6?

Some players report the Starlet Motel move scene not triggering after Afternoon Delight. Check the $15,000 savings target, then try loading a manual save after a console restart or calling Cal as Jason. These are player-reported, not official, fixes.

Why does GTA 6 keep crashing on PS5?

Rockstar says update 01.002.002 fixes several crash causes without naming them. Some players still reported crashes on November 25. Update the game and PS5, restore licenses, then clear cache and rebuild the database in Safe Mode.

Will reinstalling GTA 6 fix crashes?

Not always. Players who get CE-108255-1 say a reinstall didn't help. Back up your saves first, and never use Reset PS5 for a single game.

What do I do if I fall through the map in GTA 6?

Wait a moment, since Lucia usually respawns. If not, restart and reload your last save or replay the mission.

My GTA 6 box has no code. What do I do?

Contact the retailer you bought it from. Some buyers have reportedly received replacements.

Is GTA 6 down?

PSN had sign-in errors on Nov 18 as New Zealand unlocked. Check the PlayStation or Xbox status page and Rockstar Support for the latest.
