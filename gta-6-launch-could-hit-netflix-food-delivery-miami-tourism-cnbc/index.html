GTA 6 Could Hurt Netflix and Boost Food Delivery, Says PwC

GTA 6's launch is big enough that analysts expect it to show up outside gaming: PwC told CNBC that food delivery companies and Miami tourism could get a boost, while video streaming platforms could lose viewers to Leonida. Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick told CNBC that "our expectations were high and they are being vastly exceeded so far."

REPORTED: CNBC (Julia Boorstin and Stephen Desaulniers), Nov 14, updated Nov 16. Forecasts are analyst projections, not results.

Rockstar declined CNBC's interview request, so everything below comes from Take-Two, analysts, Twitch, HR data and PwC. Here's the full picture now that the game is out.

What Take-Two's CEO said

OFFICIAL: Take-Two CEO quote to CNBC

Zelnick's line on demand was short: "our expectations were high and they are being vastly exceeded so far." He has been careful not to call GTA 6 the biggest launch ever before sales are in, so this is about as far as Take-Two has gone in public.

Who wins and who loses, according to PwC

Food delivery: up

PwC projected that food delivery companies could see a surge as players stay home. It didn't put a number on it. Brands are already leaning in, which we covered in our launch-day off work and school roundup.

Miami tourism: up

PwC said Miami, the real city behind Vice City, could benefit from tourism. Its comparison: foreign visitors to Seoul's Bukchon Hanok Village rose 118% in the four weeks after Netflix's KPop Demon Hunters came out. The Miami Heat already ran a Vice City night this week.

Streaming: down

Bart Spiegel, who leads PwC's Global Entertainment & Media practice, said the game could "pull eyeballs away from video streaming platforms." He expects analysts to point at GTA 6 on Q1 2027 earnings calls to explain weaker numbers.

THEORY (PwC forecast)

These are predictions. We'll know if they came true when delivery apps and streamers report their next quarter.

Twitch and creators

Twitch's chief product officer Mike Minton called the launch a "sustaining opportunity" for streamers to grow and keep their audience. Creator Deansocool (375,000 Twitch followers) went further: "This game is going to make a lot of millionaires in 2027." The launch already broke records: see GTA 6's Twitch record. GTA V is still among Twitch's five most-watched games this year, 13 years after it came out.

Frequently asked questions

What did Take-Two's CEO say about GTA 6 sales?

Strauss Zelnick told CNBC that "our expectations were high and they are being vastly exceeded so far." Take-Two hasn't released an official launch sales number yet.

Will GTA 6 hurt Netflix and streaming?

PwC's Bart Spiegel said it could "pull eyeballs away from video streaming platforms" and expects it to be cited on Q1 2027 earnings calls. That's a forecast, not a result.

How many people are taking time off for GTA 6?

BambooHR data in CNBC's report says PTO requests for Nov 19 are about 13% higher than the same days in 2024 and 2025, and nearly one in five workers plan time off.

How many copies will GTA 6 sell?

Morgan Stanley forecasts 40 million by March 2027. Sensor Tower estimated over 12 million pre-orders by Nov 13. Neither is an official figure.
