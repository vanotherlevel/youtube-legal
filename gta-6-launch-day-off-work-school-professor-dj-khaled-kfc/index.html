Everyone Is Taking GTA 6 Day Off: Professor, DJ Khaled, KFC

The world is clearing its calendar for GTA 6. In the last few days a professor in Spain cancelled homework and moved an exam for launch week, Xbox turned DJ Khaled into "Dr. XBOX" handing out joke doctor's notes, and a KFC in Australia is holding a fake funeral so fans have an excuse to stay home on November 19.

Here is every day-off story so far, what is real, what is a joke, and where each one comes from. GTA 6 unlocks at local midnight on November 19 (times for every region), and New Zealand already has it.

A professor cancelled homework for GTA 6 week

REPORTED: email shared on X Nov 16, reported by Dexerto. Not independently verified

An email from a professor in Spain went viral after it was shared on X on November 16 (one of the posts was by creator ElBuni, @therealbuni). According to Dexerto's translation, the professor told students:

  • No homework, projects or deadlines in his subject during GTA 6's launch week.
  • An exam moved from the 23rd to the 30th, "so you can organize yourselves better and have more free time during those days."
  • An apology for the heavier workload the week before, which he said was to get ahead on the syllabus. He warned they'd need to focus again after launch week.
  • A reminder to play responsibly, since some of the game's content isn't suitable for their age.

The line that made it spread: "When GTA V came out in 2013, I was your age... I had my moment with GTA V, and I was excited for you to have yours with GTA VI." Dexerto notes the professor's name, school and subject aren't public and it couldn't verify the email itself.

Xbox and DJ Khaled: 'Dr. XBOX' doctor's notes

OFFICIAL: Xbox Wire campaign, Nov 16 2026 (via Dexerto, TweakTown, Windows Report)

Xbox launched a campaign on November 16 with DJ Khaled playing "Dr. XBOX". At DrXBOX.com you type your name and get a personalised note "prescribing" time off for a made-up "Vitamin G deficiency", with symptoms like controller cramps and late-night gaming dreams. It's free and reportedly needs no account.

Xbox's post opened with: "A big week in gaming is coming, and there's a serious case of Vitamin G deficiency around the world." Khaled's own message: "Take. The. Day. Off."

It's a joke note. Xbox says the notes have no medical validity and it isn't responsible if your employer doesn't have a sense of humour. The Xbox Wire post doesn't name GTA 6, but outlets tied the timing to the launch. There's no sign DJ Khaled appears in the game.

KFC's fake funeral for 'Ann Alibi'

REPORTED: KFC Robina, Queensland, Australia, via Dexerto Nov 16

A KFC in Robina, Queensland is holding a mock funeral for a fictional woman called "Ann Alibi", timed for launch day, so fans have a "funeral" to attend instead of work. Dexerto says it comes with a hearse, a coffin, flowers, a condolence book, an order of service and a eulogy from a surprise guest, plus free fried chicken for anyone who turns up. The invite says the service falls on the same day "something 13 years in the making finally arrives." No exact time or sign-up method was given.

Companies closing for the day

  • Burger Motorsports (California car tuning company): announced on Instagram in June a "company-wide operational pause" on launch day because staff would be "in Vice City". Work resumes once they've "finished at least one mission, and returned to reality." (REPORTED via Dexerto)
  • Logitech: gave staff a real day off to play games before GTA 6 (our story).
  • Greensboro Police joked GTA 6 should hurry so people stay home and officers "catch a break" (our story).

1.5 million sick days and $1 billion?

ESTIMATE: economist's prediction, not a measured number

Economist José García Montalvo (Pompeu Fabra University, Barcelona) estimated about 1.5 million Americans could miss work on November 19, costing around $1 billion in lost productivity, assuming a $70,000 average salary (reported by International Business Times, via Dexerto, Oct 22). Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick said back in April that "a lot of people will be calling in sick on November 19." Treat the figures as a rough guess. We'll only know how many people really stayed home if someone measures it after launch.

Frequently asked questions

Did a professor really cancel exams for GTA 6?

An email from a professor in Spain, shared on X on Nov 16 and reported by Dexerto, says he cancelled homework and deadlines for launch week and moved an exam from the 23rd to the 30th. His name and school aren't public, and Dexerto couldn't verify it independently.

Is the DJ Khaled Xbox doctor's note real?

The campaign is real (Xbox, Nov 16), and the notes are free at DrXBOX.com, but the note itself is a joke with no medical validity. Xbox says so itself.

Where is the KFC GTA 6 fake funeral?

At KFC Robina in Queensland, Australia, on launch day, according to Dexerto. Attendees reportedly get free fried chicken.

How many people will skip work for GTA 6?

Nobody knows yet. One economist estimated about 1.5 million Americans and roughly $1 billion in lost productivity, but that is a prediction, not a measurement.
