GTA 6 Launch: How Cyberpunk, The Witcher and The Sims Congratulated Rockstar

Half the games industry stopped to say congratulations when GTA 6 launched on November 19, 2026. Cyberpunk 2077, The Witcher, Kingdom Come developer Warhorse, The Sims, Rebellion, Microsoft Flight Simulator and more all posted GTA-themed messages, and the Cyberpunk post alone passed about 1.2 million views (VGTimes roundup).

The S.T.A.L.K.E.R. developers also reacted with their own take on GTA 6 set in the Zone, which VGTimes covered separately. A Looney Tunes cover with Bugs Bunny and Lola went around too, but VGTimes notes it was a parody shared by ToonHive, not an official Warner Bros. post.

The fan art that went viral

  • Code Veronica on X: Leon Kennedy and Ada Wong from Resident Evil on a GTA cover with palm trees and a yacht, captioned "PEAK" (about 183K views).
  • Chris Jones: a Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles x Grand Theft Auto VI cover (5K+ views).
  • FleurashDesign (Artist Ash): Spider-Man and Black Cat with a convertible, "Taking the spider-buggy out for a spin."
  • VideoArtGame / Sergei Surmenko: GTA VI redrawn as a 2001 GTA III-style PS2 case, ESRB rating and all.

Not everyone was only cheering: Warren Spector

Deus Ex creator Warren Spector congratulated the team on LinkedIn and called GTA 6 possibly one of the biggest achievements in games and business. He also said this kind of budget shouldn't become the norm: "I'd rather have hundreds of games and full employment," adding that he hopes GTA 6 "is the last of its kind."

REPORTED: Spector's LinkedIn post via VGTimes (Aleksandr Manin, Nov 19, 2026)

And Xbox still won't say the name

Xbox marked launch day without writing "GTA 6". Its Xbox Wire promo with DJ Khaled's doctor's notes says players have "a serious case of Vitamin G deficiency", and the November 3 X-Bite alligator console was another wink. One exception: the November 10 post about GTA 6 on Xbox Cloud Gaming did name the game.

THEORY: Eurogamer suggested a PlayStation marketing deal with Rockstar explains it. Nobody has confirmed that

Want to know how big launch day really was? The pre-load alone broke internet traffic records, and people took the day off work for it.

Frequently asked questions

Which studios congratulated Rockstar on GTA 6?

Cyberpunk 2077 (CD Projekt Red), The Witcher, Warhorse Studios, Rebellion, tinyBuild, The Sims, Microsoft Flight Simulator 2024, The Expanse: Osiris Reborn, MOUSE: P.I. For Hire and the S.T.A.L.K.E.R. team, plus the Clarkson's Farm account.

What did Cyberpunk 2077 post for GTA 6?

"From Night City to Vice City, sending some love coast to coast" with a Panam GIF. It reached about 1.2 million views.

Why doesn't Xbox say GTA 6?

Xbox hasn't explained it. Eurogamer suggested a PlayStation marketing deal, but that's unconfirmed. Xbox did name the game once, in its Nov 10 cloud gaming post.

What did Warren Spector say about GTA 6?

He congratulated Rockstar on LinkedIn but said he'd rather have hundreds of games and full employment, and hopes GTA 6 is the last game of its kind.
