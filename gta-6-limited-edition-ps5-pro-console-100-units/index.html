GTA 6 PS5 Pro Console: Only 100 Made, and You Can't Buy It

No, you can't buy the custom GTA VI PS5 Pro. The console is reported to be limited to 100 units that are being given to selected athletes, influencers and content creators, and Twisted Voxel reports that Rockstar has confirmed it will not be sold at retail.

REPORTED: Twisted Voxel, Notebookcheck and VGTimes. The 100-unit count is traced to the account Ben/videotech; we have not found a Rockstar or Sony announcement page for it.

What the console looks like

  • Side plates: dark plates with palm tree silhouettes and the Grand Theft Auto VI logo.
  • Centre stripes: a teal-to-magenta gradient, a nod to Vice City nights.
  • Serial number: each unit carries its own number under the Rockstar logo.
  • Case: a custom hard flight case.

What comes in the case

According to Twisted Voxel, each package holds the console, two matching DualSense controllers with VI branding and a copy of Grand Theft Auto VI. Notebookcheck notes that it is not confirmed whether the units sent to content creators include the same controllers as the earlier ones.

The Heat link is not random: the team ran a Vice City themed night around launch. We covered it on our Miami HEAT Vice City night page.

What you can still get

The console and its plates have no announced retail version. The themed DualSense controllers are a separate product that has been sold in shops, though Notebookcheck says they are often sold out. If a listing offers the 100-unit console, treat it carefully: check the serial number, the seller's history and the payment protection, because pictures of the real unit are public and easy to copy.

THEORY (our take): with Sony reporting record player numbers at launch, a retail GTA 6 console or faceplate would be an easy holiday product. Nothing like that has been announced.

Frequently asked questions

Can I buy the GTA 6 PS5 Pro console?

No. It is reported as a 100-unit promotional item that will not be sold at retail.

How many were made?

Reports say 100, each with its own serial number. The count is traced to the account Ben/videotech.

Who got one?

Reported recipients include several Miami Heat players and streamers and creators such as Davy Jones.

What is included?

Reportedly the console, two VI-branded DualSense controllers, a copy of the game and a flight case.
