GTA 6 Locked Casinos, Zoo & Graveyard: Rockstar Says 'Future'

Yes, GTA 6 has places on its map that the game doesn't use yet: at least two big casinos you can't enter, a detailed zoo, a large graveyard and a small northern town that the story never sends you to. Rockstar's VP of design Stephen Donkin told Kotaku the map was "built for the long term" and that these areas "give us options in the future", but no DLC or update has been announced.

Here is exactly what was found, what Rockstar said word for word, why there's no gambling in GTA 6, and what these spots could become. Everything is labelled so you know what's official and what's a guess.

What Rockstar said

OFFICIAL: Stephen Donkin, Rockstar VP of design, speaking to Kotaku
"The map has been built for the long term and with many factors in mind... there are a few areas on the map that give us options in the future should we have a gameplay or narrative need for them." - Stephen Donkin, Rockstar

Note what he did not say. He gave no date, no format (story DLC, free update or Online) and no promise. Rockstar also hasn't announced any single-player DLC or post-launch plan. So the honest read is: the doors are locked on purpose, and Rockstar has left itself room.

Why there's no casino gambling in GTA 6

OFFICIAL: Donkin via Kotaku

Donkin explained why the casinos stay shut: "Jason and Lucia's story didn't feel like the right place for us to introduce gambling," especially "with the fine balance we sought throughout the economy." Kotaku notes Rockstar calls GTA 6 the first GTA where money really matters, and a casino could break that. Our money guide shows how tight the early cash is.

REPORTED: Dexerto (Oct 6)

This lines up with the age ratings. Dexerto reported in October that GTA 6's ESRB rating lists blood and gore, intense violence, nudity, strong sexual content, drugs and more, but no gambling descriptor, and PEGI didn't list gambling either. RDR2, Red Dead Online and the PS5 version of GTA 5 all carry one.

Will these areas become DLC?

THEORY: our read, not confirmed
  • Casinos = GTA Online. GTA 5's casino was planned for story mode and ended up as GTA Online's Diamond Casino in 2019. An online mode is the most likely place for gambling in GTA 6 too, and areas with real rules on money fit Online better than Jason and Lucia's story.
  • Zoo and graveyard = missions or story DLC. A detailed zoo and a big cemetery are classic mission sets (a chase through the enclosures, a funeral gone wrong). They could be saved for a story expansion like GTA 4's The Lost and Damned.
  • The northern town = a future story hub. A whole town the story skips is the kind of place an expansion builds around.
  • Or nothing. GTA 5 had unused interiors that never opened. Rockstar said 'options', not plans.

Frequently asked questions

Can you go in the casinos in GTA 6?

No. Kotaku reports at least two large casinos with locked doors that aren't used in the story or side missions. Rockstar says there's no gambling in Jason and Lucia's story.

Is there a zoo in GTA 6?

Yes, there's a detailed zoo on the map, but it isn't part of the story (REPORTED via Kotaku). Rockstar says some areas are kept for possible future use.

Is GTA 6 getting DLC?

Nothing is announced. Rockstar's VP of design said the map has areas that 'give us options in the future', but gave no date or format.

Why is there no gambling in GTA 6?

Rockstar's Stephen Donkin said the story 'didn't feel like the right place' for gambling, given the balance of the game's economy. The age ratings also list no gambling.
