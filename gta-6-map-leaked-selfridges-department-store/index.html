GTA 6's Map Seemingly Leaked a Week Early, From a Department Store Display

By vAnother Level · Updated 2026-11-12
Home › GTA 6 Guides

After 13 years of trailers, datamines and theories, the GTA 6 map may have leaked from... a shop window. Photos from the GTA 6 collection at Selfridges in the UK appear to show a stylised version of the game's map, a week before launch.

Reported by VGC, November 12, 2026. Unconfirmed by Rockstar

The clearest picture came from an Instagram account that has since been deleted. A second person shared a photo of what looks like the same map, folded up in a display at Selfridges in Manchester, and said whoever set it up must have seen it fully.

Selfridges in Manchester Exchange Square have got the #GTA6 Collectors Set on display and the Map folded up in the display too..

— @GazzaMontana 2026-11-12

According to VGC, the map appears to be part of the GTA 6 Collector's Edition, which Selfridges isn't selling; it was a display item for the store's Rockstar partnership. The Collector's Edition ships in time for November 19.

Then vs now

Paper maps have come in GTA boxes since GTA 3, and GTA 5's 2013 poster map was the last one fans unfolded on launch day. This time the map got out a week early. We already compared the map's rumoured size here: GTA 6's map vs GTA 4, GTA 5 and RDR2.
