GTA 6's Map Is Nearly as Big as GTA 4, GTA 5 and Red Dead Redemption 2 Combined, Rockstar Says

By vAnother Level · Updated 2026-11-11
Home › GTA 6 Guides

Take GTA 4's Liberty City, GTA 5's Los Santos and Blaine County, and Red Dead Redemption 2's whole frontier. Put them together. That's roughly how big GTA 6's Leonida is, according to Rockstar in Edge magazine's November 2026 cover feature.

Rockstar to Edge magazine; reported by PC Gamer and GamesRadar

What Rockstar actually said

  • Leonida's total landmass is nearly the same as the playable maps of GTA 4, GTA 5 and RDR2 combined.
  • It has "an order of magnitude more interiors" than before (PC Gamer's report of the interview).
  • Rockstar devs say they're still discovering places in it they've never seen.
  • Not confirmed: an official size in square miles or kilometers. Numbers you see online are fan estimates.

Quick reality check: "landmass" isn't the same as "stuff to do", and GTA 5's map had a lot of empty hills. But with that many interiors, Leonida sounds dense, not just big.
