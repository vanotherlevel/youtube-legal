GTA 6's map is the fictional state of Leonida, Rockstar's take on Florida, and Rockstar's official site splits it into six areas: Vice City, the Leonida Keys, Grassrivers, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia and Mount Kalaga. Vice City is the big neon city, and the other five are the islands, swamps, rundown coast, industrial heartland and national-park wilderness around it.

Below is each region in order, what Rockstar has actually said, what outlets have reported from the trailers and previews, and what's still guesswork. Everything is labelled, and every source is linked at the bottom. GTA 6 launches November 19, 2026, so the full map is only hours away. We'll update this page with real in-game detail once it's out.

Vice City

OFFICIAL

Vice City is the heart of the map and the place the series is named around this time. Rockstar's own tagline for Leonida is "Home to the neon-soaked streets of Vice City and beyond", and most of both trailers is set here: high-rise skyline, beach strips, marinas, clubs and highways. It's Rockstar's modern Miami, the same city the 2002 game set in the 80s, now rebuilt for the present day with social media, viral clips and influencers baked into the setting.

Several of the official characters are tied to the city. Rockstar calls Boobie Ike a "local Vice City legend" with a strip club, real estate and a recording studio, and his label Only Raw Records is part of the music side of the story. See every named character in our GTA 6 story explained page.

Why it matters for you: expect most story missions, shops, property and the densest traffic here. If you want to know whether a familiar face turns up, our Tommy Vercetti clue page covers what's real and what's fan theory.

Leonida Keys

OFFICIAL: Jason's bio places him in the Keys

The Leonida Keys are a string of tropical islands joined by long bridges, Rockstar's version of the Florida Keys. This is where Jason Duval is when the story finds him: his official bio says he's living in the Keys and working for local drug runners. Brian Heder, the veteran smuggler with a boat yard, is the other official character tied to this kind of life.

Trailer 2 and the official art show seaplanes, speedboats, shallow turquoise water and small island towns. Outlets such as Insider Gaming describe it as the region for boating, reefs and waterfront properties.

THEORY

Because Jason starts here, the Keys are likely one of the first areas you'll see, but Rockstar hasn't said which region the game opens in or whether the map is locked at the start.

Grassrivers

REPORTED

Grassrivers is Leonida's swamp country, the game's answer to the Everglades. Rockstar's art shows an airboat tearing across sunset wetlands, and reporting from the trailers and previews describes marshland, waterways and wildlife, alligators included. Insider Gaming notes Jason and Lucia are seen crossing it by fanboat in gameplay footage.

Expect this to be the area for airboats, hiding from the cops in the reeds and the wildlife moments the trailers love. Our 274 new mechanics guide lists the animal and vehicle details seen so far.

Port Gellhorn

REPORTED

Port Gellhorn is the opposite of Vice City's glamour. Rockstar's art shows a water tower, rail lines and a working port under a stormy sky, and outlets describe it as Leonida's "forgotten coast": a former holiday spot now full of rundown motels, empty strip malls and crime. Insider Gaming calls it an industrial coastal settlement with a rundown, commercial feel.

THEORY

Fans and sites like GTABoom link it to Panama City and Florida's Panhandle. Rockstar hasn't confirmed a real-world model.

Ambrosia

REPORTED

Ambrosia is Leonida's industrial heartland. The official art is all smokestacks and refinery towers under an orange sky, and outlets describe factories, rail yards and heavy industry, with a big sugar refinery dominating the skyline beside Lake Leonida.

THEORY

The sugar refinery and lake have led fans to compare it to the Clewiston and Lake Okeechobee area of Florida. That comparison comes from fans and fan sites, not Rockstar. Details here are still light, so this is the region most likely to surprise people on launch day.

Mount Kalaga

REPORTED

Mount Kalaga is the wild one: a national park of thick forest, rivers, rocks and hills, shown in Rockstar's art with sunlight breaking through the trees. Reports describe it as the most rural part of the map, with hunting and fishing as activities, and Insider Gaming calls it a rural retreat full of wildlife.

Real Florida has no mountains, so this is where Rockstar bends the setting the most. It's the closest GTA 6 gets to Red Dead Redemption 2's wilderness, which fits Rockstar's line that the map rivals RDR2 and GTA 5 combined.

What's still not confirmed

  • The exact map size in square miles. Only the Edge comparison exists.
  • Which region you start in, and whether any area is locked early in the story.
  • County names and borders. Some fan maps use extra county names; Rockstar's official list is the six above.
  • Interiors per region. Rockstar says there are far more than before, with no count.
  • GTA 6 Online's map. No official word on whether Online uses the full state at launch. See GTA 6 Online release date.

Frequently asked questions

How many regions are in the GTA 6 map?

Six, per Rockstar's official site: Vice City, Leonida Keys, Grassrivers, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia and Mount Kalaga, all in the state of Leonida.

Is GTA 6 set in Florida?

It's set in Leonida, Rockstar's fictional version of Florida. Vice City is its version of Miami, the Keys mirror the Florida Keys, and Grassrivers is widely reported as its Everglades.

Where does Jason start in GTA 6?

Rockstar's official bio says Jason is living in the Leonida Keys, working for local drug runners. Rockstar hasn't confirmed the exact opening mission location.

How big is the GTA 6 map?

Rockstar told Edge the landmass is close to GTA 4, GTA 5 and Red Dead Redemption 2's maps combined. There is no official square-mile figure yet.
