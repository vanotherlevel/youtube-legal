GTA 6 Map Size: How Long to Walk, Run, Drive & Fly Across Leonida

Walking from the bottom of GTA 6's map to the top takes about 3 hours 21 minutes. Running takes 1 hour 41 minutes, driving the highways takes under 11 minutes, and a prop plane does it in under 6. Those are the first real timed tests of Leonida, run by Kotaku's Zack Zwiezen on November 18, 2026, the day before launch, after 130+ hours with a review copy.

Below: every time in one table, how it compares to GTA 5, how far one tank of fuel gets you, what Rockstar has actually said about the map's size, and why the numbers still undersell it. New to the map? Start with our GTA 6 map regions guide.

How long does it take to cross the GTA 6 map?

REPORTED: Kotaku review-copy test, Nov 18, 2026
MethodTime to cross LeonidaRoute / vehicle
Walking3 h 21 min 20 sSouthernmost reachable land, as straight north as possible (detours around alligators and cliffs)
Running (sprinting)1 h 41 minSame route in reverse, sprinting whenever stamina allowed
Driving10 min 53 sMid-range sedan as Lucia, southernmost road to northernmost dirt road, mostly highways
Flying5 min 51 sProp plane, southernmost to northernmost land

Kotaku ignored speed limits and weaved through traffic on the drive, so a careful drive will be slower. The walk started on highways across the southern islands before heading inland and north.

GTA 5 figures are the ones Kotaku quotes for comparison. The odd one out is running: GTA 6 takes more than three times longer, while walking is only about 35% longer. That gap isn't just map size.

Why running takes so much longer in GTA 6

  • Stamina is back. Jason and Lucia have a stamina bar that caps full-speed sprinting. In GTA 5 you could sprint forever.
  • They seem slightly slower. Kotaku says the sprint itself feels a little slower than Michael, Franklin or Trevor.
  • Wildlife gets in the way. The run meant dodging alligators and other animals in the swamps.
Want to run longer? Cardio raises max stamina. Our character customization, gym & stats guide covers which workouts help.

How far does one tank of fuel get you?

REPORTED: Kotaku fuel test, Nov 18, 2026

Cars in GTA 6 burn fuel, and electric cars drain battery. The game warns you at half a tank and again near empty, and marks the nearest station on the compass. Kotaku drove normally until each car died:

VehicleFull tank / charge lastedMap crossings (approx.)
Older pickup truck29 min 8 sAbout 2
Banshee sports car18 min 31 sAbout 1, with fuel left
Tesla Model 3-style EV20 min 23 sAbout 1.5
  • Refuel at any gas station, or a charging station for EVs. Kotaku says stations are plentiful.
  • Owned cars can be summoned with the in-game Scooter Bros app, and Kotaku's summoned car came with a little extra fuel.
  • In 130+ hours Kotaku ran dry only twice, both times in stolen cars that were already part-empty. Check the gauge before a getaway.
  • Fuel is more of a hassle early in the story, when money is tight and you have few cars.

Fuel prices were not listed in the test. For car buying, storing and repairs, see our GTA 6 cars and vehicles guide.

How big is the GTA 6 map officially?

OFFICIAL: Rockstar developer quotes, reported by outlets
  • About twice GTA 5 and three times RDR2: Rockstar's Rob Nelson to YouTuber TGG at Rockstar North, reported by VGC (Aug 28, 2026).
  • Nearly GTA 4 + GTA 5 + RDR2 combined in landmass, with "an order of magnitude more interiors": Rockstar to Edge magazine, reported by PC Gamer and GamesRadar. Our earlier write-up: GTA 6's map is nearly three maps combined.
THEORY: no official square-kilometre figure exists

Rockstar has never given an area in km² or square miles. Fan and outlet estimates range widely depending on whether water is counted, so treat any exact number you see as a guess. The crossing times above are the most concrete measure available right now.

Why the crossing times undersell Leonida

A straight line north misses most of the map. Kotaku points out that the times ignore the buildings you can enter, the underwater areas and the wilderness that spreads out on every side of the route. After 100 hours and most main and side missions, the tester says there were still areas they hadn't visited, and that GTA 5 now "feels so tiny" by comparison.

OUR TAKE: an 11-minute drive sounds short next to the fan predictions of 25-30+ minutes, but those were trailer-era guesses. Density matters more than distance, and the official "order of magnitude more interiors" line is the part that should change how big Leonida feels.

Fastest ways to get around

  1. Fly. A prop plane crosses the map in under 6 minutes.
  2. Fast travel with RydeMe. Reported to unlock after the Buck Me mission; see our beginner tips.
  3. Drive the highways. Under 11 minutes end to end, with fuel for one or two crossings.
  4. Run only short hops. Stamina runs out, so sprint in bursts.

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to walk across the GTA 6 map?

About 3 hours 21 minutes from the southernmost land to the northernmost point, per Kotaku's review-copy test. GTA 5 took about 2.5 hours.

How long does it take to drive across GTA 6?

Kotaku drove it in 10 minutes 53 seconds in a mid-range sedan using highways and ignoring speed limits. A prop plane did it in 5 minutes 51 seconds.

Is the GTA 6 map bigger than GTA 5?

Yes. Rockstar's Rob Nelson reportedly said it is about twice the size of GTA 5 and three times Red Dead Redemption 2. No official square-kilometre figure has been released.

Do cars run out of gas in GTA 6?

Yes. In Kotaku's test a pickup lasted 29 minutes, a Banshee 18.5 minutes and an EV 20 minutes on a full tank or charge. Refuel at gas or charging stations.
