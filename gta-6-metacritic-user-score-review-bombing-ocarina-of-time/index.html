GTA 6 Metacritic User Score: Why a 99 Game Sits Around 5/10 (Review Bombing Explained)

By vAnother Level · Updated 2026-11-22

GTA 6 has a 99 critic score on Metacritic, but its user score fell to roughly 5 out of 10 in launch week. Outlets reported numbers between 4.9 and 5.6, depending on the day they checked, and most of them blame review bombing: piles of 0/10 scores, some from people who say they never played the game.

Not every low score is bad faith, though. Some players are unhappy with the 30fps cap on consoles and launch bugs. Here's what each outlet reported, with sources, so you can tell the noise from the real complaints.

The numbers: 99 vs about 5

REPORTED: snapshots from Tech4gamers, Dexerto, Shiftdelete and GTA Base, launch week. The user score is still moving.

The critic score didn't move: GTA 6 is still at 99, which we covered on our GTA 6 reviews page. The user score changes every hour as new ratings come in, so check Metacritic itself for the live number.

Why the user score dropped

REPORTED: Dexerto, Technobezz, Fortress of Solitude, KeenGamer
  • Zero-score floods. Outlets describe a wave of 0/10 user reviews posted right after launch.
  • People who never played it. Fortress of Solitude reports that some reviewers openly admit they haven't played the game.
  • An Ocarina of Time feud. Dexerto reports that GTA 6 and the Ocarina of Time remake got caught in a review bomb war between fan groups. Technobezz quotes one user who gave GTA 6 a 4 while calling it excellent, saying the score was payback for attacks on Ocarina of Time.
  • Real complaints too. Dexerto notes some players dislike the 30fps cap on consoles, frame-rate drops and open-world bugs. Those are covered on our launch bugs and fixes page and the Digital Foundry tech review page.
THEORY: how much of the score is bad faith is unknown. KeenGamer points out there is no confirmed evidence of how many ratings were submitted in bad faith.

What buyers say: the PlayStation Store score

REPORTED: GGRecon

On the PlayStation Store, only people who own the game can rate it. GGRecon reports GTA 6 sits at 4.84 out of 5 there, from about 170,000 ratings. That's a lot higher than the Metacritic user score, which anyone with an account can rate.

Why it matters (and why it doesn't)

A gap like this is mostly a fight about the score, not the game. Rockstar reported a record launch (see our opening weekend sales page), and the buyer-only score is near the top. Still, the 30fps cap and the launch bugs are real, and they're the part of the low scores worth listening to. If Rockstar patches performance, the honest low reviews could change; our patch notes tracker will show it.

Frequently asked questions

What is GTA 6's Metacritic user score?

Launch-week reports put it between 4.9 and 5.6 out of 10 (Tech4gamers 4.9, Dexerto 5.0, Shiftdelete 5.4, GTA Base 5.6). It changes constantly, so check Metacritic for the live number. The critic score is 99.

Is GTA 6 being review bombed?

Several outlets say yes: floods of 0/10 scores, some from people who admit they haven't played it, plus a fan feud with the Ocarina of Time remake. KeenGamer notes there's no confirmed count of how many ratings are in bad faith.

What do actual buyers rate GTA 6?

GGRecon reports the PlayStation Store, where only owners can rate, shows 4.84 out of 5 from about 170,000 ratings.
