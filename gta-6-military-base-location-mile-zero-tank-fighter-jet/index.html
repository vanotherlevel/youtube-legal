The GTA 6 military base is Mile Zero Air Station, on Goose Key in Mariana County, the island in the far south-west corner of the Leonida map. The tanks are parked in the south of the base. The fastest way in is the stunt jump at the south-east entrance, which carries you over the fence. Stealing a tank unlocks the Challenging Tank trophy and gives you a wanted level straight away.

This is GTA 6's version of GTA 5's Fort Zancudo: a restricted military airfield full of armour and aircraft that you can visit early in the story, because it sits fairly close to Jason's first home. Below is everything reported in the launch-week guides from PowerPyx, PC Gamer and NowGame, with every claim labelled. Rockstar hasn't published an official guide to the base.

Where is the military base in GTA 6?

REPORTED: PowerPyx (Nov 18), NowGame (Nov 18), PC Gamer

Mile Zero Air Station takes up most of Goose Key, an island at the south-western edge of Leonida in Mariana County. That's the end of the Leonida Keys, the chain of islands south of Vice City (see our map regions guide).

NowGame says you can drive there from Key Lento across the long bridges that link Leonida's southern islands, or arrive by boat, plane or helicopter. PC Gamer describes the base as being near the starting area, so it's one of the first big-ticket places you can reach in free roam.

Name check: Mile Zero is a nod to Key West's real 'Mile 0' marker, where US Route 1 starts. That's our observation, not something Rockstar has said.

How to get into Mile Zero Air Station

REPORTED: PowerPyx, NowGame

You have two options. You can drive straight through the main gate, but the checkpoint is guarded. The cleaner route is the base's own stunt jump:

  1. Drive to the south-east corner of the base, using the access road south of the facility.
  2. Weave past the concrete barricades without stopping. NowGame warns that soldiers may shoot on some stretches.
  3. Build up speed and hit the large dirt ramp in front of the barbed-wire fence.
  4. You land inside the base. NowGame notes it also counts as the 'Mile Zero Air Station Southeast' stunt jump, which helps toward 100% (see our 100% checklist).
Jumping the fence doesn't make you invisible. As soon as soldiers spot Jason or Lucia in the restricted area, you get a wanted level and the military opens fire almost at once (NowGame). Pick a fast car with some armour, and have your target vehicle in mind before you land.

How to steal a tank in GTA 6

REPORTED: PowerPyx (Nov 18), NowGame

The tanks are Hauners Rhino models (NowGame, which also lists the Hauners APC parked in several parts of the base). PowerPyx says the tanks are in the south of the air base, behind the southern hangar, and they're unlocked, so you just walk up and get in.

  • Wanted level: PowerPyx reports an instant 3-star wanted level the moment you take a tank. Its video guide shows it climbing to 6 stars, the top of GTA 6's wanted scale, once you start fighting back.
  • Trophy: taking the tank unlocks Challenging Tank (Bronze, 10 Gamerscore). PS5Trophies' video says to fight in it until the notification pops if it doesn't trigger right away.
  • Keeping it: you can't. PowerPyx says tanks don't fit in your garage, so they can't be saved as personal vehicles. Enjoy it, then lose the cops (see our wanted level guide).

Several creators, including HarryNinetyFour and MrBossFTW, have posted videos of grabbing a fighter jet from the base on launch day (credited below). If the tank is the trophy, the jet is the escape. Taking off from Mile Zero is the quickest way to leave a high wanted level behind.

Want a passenger jet instead? Try VCIA

PC Gamer's guide hub points to the VCIA airport tarmac in Vice City as the place to steal a commercial jet. Our trophy list has Catch Me If You Can (Bronze) for making an 'unscheduled departure at VCIA'. A bigger plane is a lot less agile than a fighter jet, but it's a separate trophy.

Tips for a clean military base run

  • Save first. GTA 6 has 15 manual slots; save in a free slot before you go in (see how to save).
  • Bring armour and healing. Buy body armour at Ammu-Nation and carry Zombix for the run back out (see how to heal).
  • Go straight to your vehicle. NowGame's advice: get to the vehicle you want and leave as fast as you can. Don't stand and fight the soldiers on foot.
  • Do the trophy first. Take a tank for Challenging Tank, then go back another time for a jet if you want the flight escape.

Frequently asked questions

Where is the military base in GTA 6?

Mile Zero Air Station, on Goose Key in Mariana County, the far south-western corner of the Leonida map at the end of the Keys (REPORTED by PowerPyx, NowGame and PC Gamer).

Where is the tank in GTA 6?

Rhino tanks are parked in the south of Mile Zero Air Station, behind the southern hangar. They're unlocked, so you can just get in (REPORTED, PowerPyx).

How do you get the Challenging Tank trophy?

Steal a tank from Mile Zero Air Station. If it doesn't pop right away, fight in the tank until the notification appears (REPORTED, PowerPyx and PS5Trophies).

Can you keep the tank in GTA 6?

No. PowerPyx reports that tanks are too big for your garage, so they can't be saved as personal vehicles.

Is there a fighter jet in GTA 6?

Yes. Launch-day videos show fighter jets being stolen from Mile Zero Air Station. NowGame lists the model as the Hauners P996-Lazer (REPORTED).
