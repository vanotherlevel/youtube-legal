GTA 6 has 91 main story missions across five chapters, counting the Prologue, according to the mission lists guide sites built from the launch build (REPORTED). The main story takes about 50 hours. Below you'll find the chapter names, the mission count per chapter, every Chapter 1 and 2 mission in order, and why some missions need cash first.

This page sorts every number by how solid it is, from Rockstar's own words to press reports. Rockstar hasn't published an official mission list, so the list below is labelled REPORTED and credited. Chapters 3 to 5 show counts only, to keep the story unspoiled. For the people you'll be doing these missions with, see GTA 6 Story Explained. Everything else is on our GTA 6 hub.

Labels on this page: OFFICIAL = Rockstar's own footage, images or words. REPORTED = press reports and previews (Insider Gaming, Edge, IGN). THEORY = our own read. Nothing here is from the 2022 leak.

GTA 6 mission list: all 5 chapters

REPORTED (Gamer Tweak, Beebom, Nov 18): 5 chapters, 91 main missions

Two guide sites published full lists on launch week, and they agree on the five chapter names and the order of the core story missions. They disagree on the total. Gamer Tweak counts 91 main missions (Prologue included). Beebom counts 118, because it also lists short in-between steps such as "Deposit Cash Into BuckMe" and "Save for the Future" as their own entries. We use the 91 count below. THEORY: the cash steps are the money gates

Our read: the extra entries in Beebom's list (depositing cash, saving for the future, paying fees) are the money gates Rockstar talked about in August, shown as their own objectives. That fits Insider Gaming's report that the hurdles are minor.

How many missions are in GTA 6?

REPORTED (Insider Gaming, Nov 15): nearly 100 missions in 5 chapters

On November 15, Insider Gaming's Tom Henderson reported nearly 100 missions across five chapters. He based this on people who played the game before launch. TweakTown and other outlets ran the same numbers, all citing Henderson. Rockstar has not given a mission count.

Here's how that compares with Rockstar's last two big story games:

The 50-hour figure is for the main story only. Side activities come on top. The RDR2 and GTA 5 averages are the same figures TweakTown and Insider Gaming used for their comparison.

THEORY: 'about 20 missions per chapter' is just maths, not a report

Some sites say each chapter has about 20 missions. That's 100 divided by 5. Nobody has reported how the missions are spread across the chapters, and RDR2's chapters varied a lot in size. Treat that number as a guess.

A '201 missions in 9 chapters' post is also going around. It came from an unverified social media account and no outlet has backed it. We're not counting it.

How long is the GTA 6 story?

There are two numbers going around, and they measure different things:

  • About 50 hours: Insider Gaming's 'consensus average' for the main story, from pre-launch players. REPORTED.
  • 80 hours: Rob Nelson, co-studio head at Rockstar North, told The New York Times his February playthrough took about 80 hours. That run included optional goals that change the story, and a lot of wandering. It's one developer's run, not an average. REPORTED (quoted interview).

Put together: if you go straight through the story, plan on about 50 hours. If you play like Nelson, doing optional goals and exploring between missions, 80 is realistic. Mission lengths also vary, and Henderson notes skill changes the total a lot.

The 200-hour Platinum

REPORTED (Insider Gaming): about 200 hours for Platinum

Henderson's report puts the PS5 Platinum at about 200 hours, more than double GTA 5. Insider Gaming says the biggest time sink is scanning 170 animal species across Leonida, a feature revealed in September 2026. Henderson also expects some RNG-based trophies, like RDR2's Zoologist. TweakTown notes an earlier datamine found 55 PS5 trophies, Platinum included.

Do you need money to progress the GTA 6 story?

OFFICIAL (Rockstar, August): you must raise money between some missions

Yes, sometimes. In August, Rockstar confirmed that you'll have to gather money between missions before some jobs unlock, as Dexerto reported. IGN's preview says the same: now and then you have to raise a set amount before the next part of the story opens up. Rockstar has not said how much money, or which missions.

REPORTED (Insider Gaming): the hurdles are minor

Insider Gaming says the cash hurdles are minor, and that players who want to rush the story still can. Its examples for quick money: rob a shop, or deliver a particular car to a fence. That fence is very likely Randy from our side quests guide, the one who wants specific cars and pays less for damaged ones.

Pauses between missions

REPORTED (Edge preview): 'natural gating' adds pauses between missions

Edge magazine's preview says the story has natural gating that puts pauses between missions. In GTA 5, the next mission marker usually appeared the moment you finished one. Nelson told Edge that when the team tried GTA 5's pacing, everything felt too fast for a world this detailed. Dexerto compares it to RDR2, where you sometimes had to wait or sleep until the right time of day.

Are there heists in GTA 6?

Yes. Robbery is the centre of the story, which Rockstar has framed around Jason and Lucia's crime spree since Trailer 1. GamesRadar's coverage of the Extended Look says the robbery mechanics have been greatly expanded. It also says much of the robbery footage shown was the open world, not scripted missions. So the line between a story heist and a robbery you plan yourself is blurrier than in GTA 5.

THEORY: the big set-piece heists sit at chapter ends

Our read is that the bank job from the launch gallery is a story set piece, and that big scores like it will close out chapters, the way GTA 5's heists marked turning points. That's a guess until launch. We'll confirm or bin it on November 19.

Is the open world locked behind missions?

No. Edge says the map is open to explore and parts of it are not locked off. Rockstar has said the map is too big to show everything through story missions, so a lot is left for you to find. Our map regions guide covers all six areas of Leonida.

Frequently asked questions

How many missions are in GTA 6?

91 main story missions in five chapters, counting the Prologue, according to Gamer Tweak's list (reported). Beebom counts 118 because it includes short in-between steps. GMTreks lists 57 optional missions on top.

What are the GTA 6 chapter names?

Chapter 1 Just Like Paradise, Chapter 2 Paradise Lost, Chapter 3 Tits Ass Drug Money, Chapter 4 Vice City, Baby, and Chapter 5 Top of the World, Ma.

What is the first mission in GTA 6?

The Prologue, Lucia's counselling meeting. Gone Fishing, a smuggling run with Jason, follows.

How long does it take to beat GTA 6?

About 50 hours for the main story (Insider Gaming, reported). Rockstar North's Rob Nelson said his own run took about 80 hours, including optional goals and exploring.

Do you need money to do GTA 6 story missions?

Sometimes. Rockstar confirmed in August that you'll need to raise money between some missions. Insider Gaming says the hurdles are minor, for example robbing a shop or delivering a car to a fence.

How long is the GTA 6 Platinum trophy?

About 200 hours, reportedly. Scanning 170 animal species is the biggest chunk of it.
