The fastest way to make money in GTA 6 is robbing large bank vaults. PowerPyx reports they pay up to $205,000 for about 5 minutes of work. They don't open up until the end of Chapter 4, though. Before that, the best money is selling stolen cars (to Moses, Pay 'n' Spray, then Randy) and Yasmani's jewelry store jobs, which pay $10,000-$25,000 each.

This guide goes chapter by chapter: what pays, when it unlocks, roughly how much, and the catch with each method. It also covers why you shouldn't bother grinding in Chapter 1, and how cash and loot work so you don't lose a big score on the way home.

Labels on this page: OFFICIAL = Rockstar's own words. REPORTED = guide writers and outlets who have played the game (PowerPyx's launch-day guide is the main source here). THEORY / OUR TAKE = our own read. The game is out November 19, so we'll check every payout ourselves and update this page.

Chapter 1: don't grind yet

REPORTED (PowerPyx)

You can rob stores (threaten the clerk, take the register), do gang operations and run races in Chapter 1. PowerPyx's advice is simple: don't farm money here, because the cash you earn in Chapter 1 is lost when the chapter ends. Play the story and save the grind for Chapter 2.

Small stores were never going to make you rich anyway. Before launch, GTABase (from Rockstar's own descriptions) put local store robberies at "a couple of thousand dollars at most".

Chapter 2: sell stolen cars to Moses

REPORTED (PowerPyx)

Moses opens up after the main quest "Moses (Take A Vehicle to Moses)". From then on you can bring him stolen cars for $1,000+ each. A run takes 1-2 minutes, which makes this the best steady earner in Chapter 2.

  • No trackers: Moses only takes cars without a tracker.
  • Cooldown: his garage needs about 2 minutes after each sale.
  • Don't steal next door: he refuses cars stolen too close to his garage. PowerPyx suggests taking them off the main road in Port Gellhorn instead.
  • OUR TAKE: use the 2-minute cooldown to scout the next car, so you're never just waiting.

Chapter 3: jewelry jobs, trackers and Randy

Yasmani's jewelry store robberies ($10,000-$25,000)

REPORTED (PowerPyx)

Early in Chapter 3, "Yasmani's Jewelry Jobs" introduces jewelry store robberies. Each one pays $10,000-$25,000, and you can hit several stores in a row. Open the backroom safe for the bigger take, then leave fast before the police lock the area down. Yasmani pays well for the stolen jewelry.

Pay 'n' Spray: cars with Standard Trackers (~$4,000+)

Also early in Chapter 3, you can sell cars fitted with Standard Trackers at Pay 'n' Spray. They're worth more than the trackerless cars Moses takes: around $4,000+ for a good car, per PowerPyx.

Randy's Wish List supercars ($30,000+ each)

About halfway through Chapter 3, "Clonin' Keys, Makin' GS" gives you the Key Cloner. That opens Randy's Wish List: high-end sports cars spread around Vice City that pay $30,000+ each if you bring them in perfect condition. Randy texts you tips about where the cars are, under the phone's Reminders section.

How many cars? Sources disagree. PowerPyx says 18, Japanese guide site Nettoge says 16. We'll count them in-game and update this. Finishing the list and Randy's final mission unlocks the Supply & Demand trophy (see our trophy list).

$120,000 story payout

At the end of Chapter 3 the story pays you $120,000 automatically, per PowerPyx. You don't need to do anything extra for it.

Chapter 4 and after: large bank vault robberies (up to $205,000)

REPORTED (PowerPyx)

This is the big one. At the end of Chapter 4, before the ending questline (the point of no return), start the optional quest "That's Where The Money Is... (Wait at the Cafe)". It works as the tutorial. After that the method repeats:

  1. Find a security van. Follow one, wait at a bank, or buy a tip from Yasmani for $10,000. His tips only open after you've robbed 2 Payday Loans, if you skipped his earlier quests.
  2. Follow the van to the bank and get into the vault while it's open.
  3. Expect 4 stars instantly. A vault robbery gives you a 4-star wanted level the moment it starts.
  4. Escape on a fast motorcycle to cut through traffic, then switch vehicles so you're not recognised.
  5. Drop the cash off with Yasmani or at one of your garages.

Once the story is done, PowerPyx puts repeat vault runs at around $100,000-$200,000 every 5 minutes, by far the best money in the game.

Stealing ATMs with a tow truck (the trick everyone is sharing)

REPORTED (Game Rant + Power Up Gaming, Nov 21)

Yes, you can rip an outdoor ATM out of the wall in GTA 6 with a tow truck or any truck fitted with a winch, then break it open for the cash. Clips of it went viral on X this weekend. Here's how it works, per the two guides that tested it.

  1. Get a hook. Steal a tow truck (police impound lots are the most reliable spot, per Game Rant: Chapter 1's impound is south of Nifty Threads Island Wear in Key Lento, Chapter 2's is in Port Gellhorn by the Starlet Motel exit), or add a winch/towing hook to your own truck at a Mod Shop (Bodywork section). Power Up Gaming says flatbeds work well.
  2. Find an outdoor ATM. Game Rant says ATMs have no map icon and recommends the ATM Finder in the BuckMe app. Power Up Gaming says you can find towable ATMs on the in-game map. Indoor ATMs can't be pulled out.
  3. Back up flush to the ATM, lower the hook until it attaches, then drive away in the opposite direction to yank it out.
  4. Break it open. Use explosives, or keep driving and dragging it until it's destroyed. The cash compartment is your payout.
  5. Lose the cops. Power Up Gaming says police show up almost as soon as the ATM comes out: get out of the red area fast, then use a Pay 'n' Spray and change outfit.
Two catches. 1. Unlock: Power Up Gaming says ATM robberies open through Yasmani's ATM Jobs, which come after his Jewelry Jobs in Chapter 3. Game Rant doesn't list a requirement. 2. Marked bills: per Power Up Gaming the money comes as marked bills you have to sell to a fence (Yasmani pays the best rate), so it isn't spendable until fenced. Neither guide gives a per-ATM payout yet, so we won't guess one. Power Up Gaming also notes that 100% completion doesn't need ATM robberies, but it does need $250,000 in fenced marked bills.

What all this money is for

OFFICIAL (Rockstar, via Dexerto): some story missions need cash first

Rockstar has said parts of the story wait until you've hit cash goals. That's why money matters from Chapter 2 on (more in our missions and money gates page). PowerPyx also reports that the maximum Family Savings for "Save for the Future" costs $2 million. For spending it well, see our every purchase that makes you richer ranking.

How cash and loot work (don't lose a score)

REPORTED (GTABase, pre-launch, from Rockstar's previews)
  • Three kinds of money: bank money, cash in your pocket, and loot in a duffel bag.
  • Loot isn't money yet. Bag loot only becomes money when a fence or buyer takes it (Yasmani, Moses, Randy and Pay 'n' Spray all fit this).
  • Cash can be lost. GTABase reported a $20,000 carried-cash cap, and that pocket cash can be lost if you die. Bank it at an ATM.
  • OUR TAKE: after a big job, drop the money off before you do anything else risky.

Is there a money cheat?

Outlets with review copies report a late-game money cheat that you unlock with a challenge, like every other cheat in GTA 6. It's no shortcut early on, and cheats can affect saves and trophies. Details are on our GTA 6 cheats page.

Watch out for "money glitch" and "free money generator" sites. GTA 6 has no PC version and no Online mode at launch, and any site asking for your PSN or Xbox login is a scam.

Our money plan (OUR TAKE)

OUR TAKE
  1. Chapter 1: story only. The cash doesn't carry over.
  2. Chapter 2: Moses car runs between missions until the next money gate is covered.
  3. Chapter 3: jewelry jobs (always the backroom safe), then Randy's list once you have the Key Cloner. Pay 'n' Spray tracker cars to fill gaps.
  4. End of Chapter 4: do the vault tutorial before the point of no return, then farm vaults for big purchases and Family Savings.

Frequently asked questions

What is the fastest way to make money in GTA 6?

Large bank vault robberies. PowerPyx reports up to $205,000 for about 5 minutes. They unlock at the end of Chapter 4 through the optional quest "That's Where The Money Is... (Wait at the Cafe)".

How do you make money early in GTA 6?

In Chapter 2, sell trackerless stolen cars to Moses ($1,000+ each). In Chapter 3, do Yasmani's jewelry store jobs ($10,000-$25,000) and Randy's Wish List supercars ($30,000+ each). Don't grind in Chapter 1: that money is reported to be lost when the chapter ends.

How many cars are on Randy's Wish List in GTA 6?

Sources disagree: PowerPyx says 18 and Nettoge says 16. Each car pays $30,000+ in perfect condition, and you need the Key Cloner from mid-Chapter 3.

Is there a money cheat in GTA 6?

Outlets with review copies report a money cheat that unlocks late in the game through a challenge. See our GTA 6 cheats page for the details.

Can you steal ATMs in GTA 6?

Yes, outdoor ones. Per Game Rant and Power Up Gaming (Nov 21), you hook an outdoor ATM with a tow truck or a winch-fitted truck, drag it out of the wall, then blow it up or keep damaging it to get the cash. Police respond fast, and Power Up Gaming says the cash comes as marked bills you sell to a fence. Indoor ATMs can't be pulled out.
