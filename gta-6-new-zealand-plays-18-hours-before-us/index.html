New Zealand Gets GTA 6 About 18 Hours Before the US East Coast

By vAnother Level · Updated 2026-11-10
Home › GTA 6 Guides

If you're in the US, someone in New Zealand will have been playing GTA 6 for about 18 hours by the time your copy unlocks. The PlayStation Store listing shows GTA 6 unlocking at midnight local time on November 19, region by region, and New Zealand is the first big market to hit midnight.

Unlock times come from the PlayStation Store listing; Rockstar hasn't published a launch hour

The math

  • New Zealand: midnight November 19 (NZDT, UTC+13) = 11:00 UTC on November 18.
  • US (East Coast): midnight November 19 (EST, UTC−5) = 05:00 UTC on November 19.
  • Gap: 18 hours. Reports say the US and Canada PS Store may use a single midnight Eastern unlock, which is 9 PM Pacific on November 18.
  • UK: midnight November 19 (GMT) = 13 hours after New Zealand.

Then vs now

Back in 2013, GTA 5 hit stores on September 17 in most regions, but Japan waited until October 10. Now it's hours, not weeks: the whole world gets GTA 6 within one day.
