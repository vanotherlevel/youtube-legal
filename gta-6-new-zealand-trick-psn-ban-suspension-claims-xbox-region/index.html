GTA 6 New Zealand Trick: 2.1 Million Players Used It, Plus the PSN Ban Claims

By vAnother Level · Updated 2026-11-19

Nobody has confirmed a PSN ban wave for the GTA 6 New Zealand trick. Claims that Sony is suspending PlayStation accounts made in New Zealand to play early are circulating on launch eve, but Sony, Rockstar and Microsoft haven't said anything, and the one report with details ties the suspensions to payment methods, not the region.

REPORTED: VICE live coverage, Tech4Gamers, @SourceVI on X

The trick itself is simple: GTA 6 unlocks at midnight local time, and New Zealand is the first big market to hit midnight on November 19 (about 11:00 UTC on November 18). Players elsewhere switch their console or account to New Zealand to start hours early. It works very differently on Xbox and PS5, and that difference is where the risk is. All release times are on our GTA 6 release time page, and more launch news is on the GTA 6 news tab.

How many people used it? About 2.1 million, says Ampere

REPORTED: Ampere Analysis estimate, via Kotaku (Nov 19)

Roughly 2.1 million players pretended to live in New Zealand to start GTA 6 early, according to new estimates from data firm Ampere Analysis reported in Kotaku's launch live blog. These are estimates, not Rockstar, Microsoft or Sony figures.

  • About 1.5 million of them were Xbox players in the US, who got in roughly 18 hours before their own midnight unlock.
  • About 600,000 were players in the UK who switched their console region.
  • Kotaku notes the PS5 route was more complicated, and one player reported that their New Zealand save didn't show up on their US copy afterwards (a single user report, not confirmed).
Our take: those numbers fit what we wrote before launch. The Xbox location swap was the easy, popular route; PS5 needed a second account and a second copy, so far fewer people went that way. Total launch sales estimates are on our GTA 6 sales page.

What the PSN ban claims actually say

REPORTED / UNCONFIRMED
  • VICE wrote in its GTA 6 live coverage that the trick is "reportedly" letting US Xbox players in early, and that claims of PlayStation account suspensions are circulating. VICE didn't say who made the claims and had no Sony or Rockstar statement.
  • Tech4Gamers (Nov 18) points to posts on X such as @SourceVI. Players say they lost access to their PSN account and digital purchases, with error code WS-116331-5 and a terms-of-service violation message.
  • Tech4Gamers says those suspensions are reportedly linked to suspicious payment methods, and that it's unclear whether they have anything to do with the New Zealand trick. It also says no official statement or widespread verified reports exist yet.
  • Some sites have turned this into "Sony is banning GTA 6 early players". We haven't found a single verified case that proves the region switch alone caused a ban.
Bottom line: treat any "confirmed ban wave" headline as unproven until Sony comments. But the risk isn't zero, see the next section.

OFFICIAL: PlayStation terms of service

Sony's PSN terms let it suspend or close accounts created with false information, and a suspended account can lose access to the games already bought on it. Buying on a new account usually also means a payment method from another country, the exact thing Tech4Gamers says the reported suspensions are linked to. So the PS5 version of the trick is the one we'd skip.

THEORY: our read, not confirmed

Swapping the Xbox console location is a normal setting that players have used for years, most recently for Forza Horizon 6. That makes it lower risk, but neither Rockstar nor Microsoft has said it's allowed for GTA 6.

Is it worth it?

The trick only gets you a few hours. Midnight in New Zealand is 6 AM ET on November 18 in the US, and your own midnight launch follows later that day. VICE also reports that a PS5 system update is required to play, so set that up first wherever you are. On PS5, risking your main account and every game on it for a few hours is a bad trade. If you want to start strong instead, read our GTA 6 tips and tricks and the best settings guide while you wait.

Frequently asked questions

How many people used the GTA 6 New Zealand trick?

About 2.1 million, according to Ampere Analysis estimates reported by Kotaku: roughly 1.5 million US Xbox players and 600,000 in the UK. These are third-party estimates, not official numbers.

Can you get banned for the GTA 6 New Zealand trick?

Not confirmed. Suspension claims are circulating, but Sony hasn't commented, and the one detailed report links them to payment methods rather than the region. PS5 accounts made with a false country can be suspended under PSN's terms, so that's the riskier route.

Does the New Zealand trick work on Xbox?

It's reported to. Guides say you change the console's Location to New Zealand, launch your pre-loaded copy, then change it back. It's unofficial and Microsoft hasn't commented.

Does the New Zealand trick work on PS5?

Only with a separate New Zealand PSN account and a second copy of the game, which is where the ban risk comes from.

What time does GTA 6 unlock in New Zealand?

Midnight NZDT on November 19, which is about 11:00 UTC / 6 AM ET on November 18.
