Is Niko Bellic in GTA 6? Yes, as 'The Hitman' (Spoilers)

Spoiler warning: this page names a late-story mission and a cameo from Chapter 5. Stop here if you want to go in blind.

Yes, Niko Bellic is in GTA 6. Michael Hollick, Niko's voice actor from GTA IV, is in Rockstar's official GTA 6 credits as "The Hitman". Players who reached the late story say the Hitman is an older, grey-haired Niko. He's a short cameo, not a playable character.

OFFICIAL: Rockstar's GTA 6 credits list Michael Hollick as "The Hitman"REPORTED (GTA BOOM, Beebom, from playthroughs): The Hitman is Niko Bellic. Rockstar hasn't named Niko anywhere

Where Niko Bellic appears in GTA 6

According to Beebom, Niko turns up in Chapter 5, "Top of the World, Ma", during the story mission "Taking Liberties" at Giorgio's penthouse in Vice City. Giorgio is a shipping magnate, and Niko works for him as an enforcer, chasing a missing shipment.

  • How many scenes: Beebom counts two cutscenes. In the second, Niko comes back to collect the shipment after Jason and Lucia finish the job.
  • His look: older, with grey hair. Both GTA BOOM and Beebom describe him this way.
  • His line: Beebom quotes Niko saying "I was beginning to like it down here."
  • Playable? No. It's a story cameo.

The reports don't fully agree on the shipment. Beebom calls it Giorgio's cocaine. GTA BOOM only mentions Niko's "Balkan crime dealings". We'll update this page if Rockstar or more outlets add detail.

Why the credits say 'The Hitman' and not Niko

The credits went up on launch day. Listing Hollick as "The Hitman" means that anyone skimming the cast list can't spoil the cameo for themselves. The Niko connection comes from people who played the mission, not from Rockstar's wording. Our full cast page has the rest of the GTA 6 voice actors from the official credits.

Does this mean a GTA IV remaster?

THEORY: fans are reading Niko's return as a hint at a GTA IV remaster. Rockstar hasn't announced or teased one

Niko's cameo has brought the GTA IV remaster talk back. For now it's just a nod to fans. Rockstar has said nothing about a remaster, and the GTA 6 credits are the only official source for this story. Frequently asked questions

Is Niko Bellic in GTA 6?

Yes. His GTA IV actor, Michael Hollick, is in Rockstar's official credits as "The Hitman", and players report that the character is an older Niko.

What mission is Niko Bellic in?

Beebom says "Taking Liberties" in Chapter 5, "Top of the World, Ma", at Giorgio's Vice City penthouse. He shows up again in a later cutscene.

Can you play as Niko in GTA 6?

No. He's a story cameo in cutscenes. Jason and Lucia are still the only playable characters.

Is Jimmy De Santa in GTA 6?

Yes, per GTA BOOM. He's voiced by Danny Tamberelli and appears as an influencer at the Jack of Hearts. His full role hasn't been detailed yet.
