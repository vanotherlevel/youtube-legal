GTA 6 Is One Week Away. Kids Born the Day GTA 5 Came Out Are Now 13

By vAnother Level · Updated 2026-11-11
Home › GTA 6 Guides

One week to go. GTA 6 launches November 19, 2026. And here's a number that makes everyone feel old: a baby born on GTA 5's launch day, September 17, 2013, is now 13 years old.

Dates from Rockstar; the math is ours

The math

  • GTA 5 to GTA 6: 4,811 days.
  • GTA 5 babies: 13 years and 2 months old on November 19.
  • GTA 5 was rated M (Mature 17+) by the ESRB, so those kids are still about four years away from that age rating.
  • Trailer 1 to launch: almost three years. The first trailer dropped in December 2023.
