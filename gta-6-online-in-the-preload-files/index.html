Is GTA 6 Online Already in the Files? What the Preload Data Hints at

By vAnother Level · Updated 2026-11-09
Home › GTA 6 Guides
On this page
  1. What's in the files
  2. What Rockstar actually says
  3. Then vs now

Everyone wants to know about GTA 6 Online, and the preload files just added fuel. PlayStation metadata from the GTA 6 preload includes multiplayer activity entries. Meanwhile, Rockstar's own PlayStation Store FAQ says "Grand Theft Auto VI is a single-player experience." Both things are true at once, so here's how they fit.

Hinted, not confirmed: Rockstar hasn't announced GTA 6 Online

What's in the files

  • Multiplayer activity strings: `launchMultiplayerActivity` sits next to `joinSession` and `launchActivity` in the game's PlayStation metadata (spotted by @PlaystationSize).
  • A split install: dataminers say the story content is kept apart from a shared base install, which would let an online mode arrive later as its own download instead of a patch.
  • A missing regional file: a file called `core_restofworld001` is reportedly missing from the Australia and New Zealand build. Nobody knows why yet; it could be a localisation quirk or nothing at all.

What Rockstar actually says

  • Rockstar's PlayStation Store FAQ: "Grand Theft Auto VI is a single-player experience."
  • The PS5 store listing reportedly labels the game "offline play only".
  • At Take-Two's September 2026 shareholder meeting, GTA 6 was again framed as a single-player release, with continued support for the existing GTA Online.
  • GTA 6 launches November 19, 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. No online mode has been announced for launch.

Then vs now

GTA 5 launched on September 17, 2013, and GTA Online followed on October 1, 2013, just two weeks later. Red Dead Redemption 2's online mode took about a month after launch. GTA 6 is the first time Rockstar has publicly called the launch version single-player, so if an online mode is coming, it looks like it will be its own thing, on its own schedule.

Frequently asked questions

Does GTA 6 have online at launch?

No online mode has been announced. Rockstar's PlayStation Store FAQ calls GTA 6 a single-player experience.

Is GTA 6 Online in the preload files?

There are multiplayer-related metadata strings, which hints at online support, but the files are encrypted and Rockstar hasn't confirmed anything.

When did GTA Online come out after GTA 5?

Two weeks later: GTA 5 launched September 17, 2013 and GTA Online on October 1, 2013.
