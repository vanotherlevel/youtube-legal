GTA 6 Online Release Date: What Rockstar Has (and Hasn't) Confirmed

By vAnother Level · Updated 2026-11-17

Rockstar has not announced a release date for GTA 6 Online. GTA 6 launches November 19, 2026 as a story game. Every online date you see is a report or an estimate, so here's what's actually known, clearly labelled.

What's confirmed

OFFICIAL
  • GTA 6 launches November 19, 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.
  • Rockstar has not published GTA 6 Online modes, a date or a price.

What's reported

REPORTED / DATAMINED

Some outlets cite a Bloomberg report placing GTA 6 Online in 2027. Our own look at the pre-load files found multiplayer strings: read is GTA 6 Online already in the files? and the preload datamine. Strings in files are not a launch date.

Frequently asked questions

Is GTA 6 Online out at launch?

Rockstar hasn't announced GTA 6 Online for launch day. GTA 6 releases on November 19, 2026 as a story game.

When will GTA 6 Online come out?

No official date. Reports point to 2027; GTA V's online came two weeks after the story, RDR2's about a month after.
