Analysts expect GTA 6 to make around $2 billion in its opening weekend, about double GTA V's record of $1 billion in three days in 2013. Bank of America calls $1.9 billion the "passing grade" for Take-Two. The first real sales number could come as soon as Monday, November 23.

REPORTED: every number on this page is an analyst forecast, not a sales result. Rockstar and Take-Two have not published a sales target.

The $2 billion weekend forecast

REPORTED: Joost van Dreunen's SuperJoost PlayList newsletter, via iXBT Games and VGTimes

Games analyst Joost van Dreunen says GTA VI could earn Take-Two more than $2 billion over its opening weekend, and that Take-Two could announce a number above $2 billion by Monday, November 23. His reasoning is simple: pre-orders already cover roughly a billion dollars, and the launch weekend itself should add another billion.

  • First year: he expects about 38 million copies sold, against 35 million for GTA V over the same stretch.
  • The long game: he notes the bigger gap may come later, from how much each player spends in the years after launch (GTA Online-style money).

Bank of America's $1.9 billion 'passing grade'

REPORTED: BofA note of Nov 11 by Omar Dessouky and Arthur Chu, via BigGo Finance and Proactive Investors

Bank of America set the bar for investors. Its analysts say GTA VI needs at least $1.9 billion in opening sales to keep Take-Two's fiscal 2027 expectations intact. Anything under $1.5 billion would suggest unit sales are falling short.

  • The bank kept its Buy rating and a $330 price target on Take-Two.
  • The median investor expectation it cites is about 50 million units in the fiscal year.
  • Timing tells: BofA expects the first figures by Nov 23. A number as early as Nov 20 would point to huge day-one demand. Silence until the 23rd could hint at a softer start.
What this means for you: nothing changes in the game. But a big sales number usually means Rockstar puts even more into GTA Online and future updates, so these forecasts are worth watching.

Newzoo's $5.2 billion first week

REPORTED: Newzoo projection (July 2026), resurfaced by Variety on Nov 12

Newzoo is the most bullish. It projects $3.25 billion to $5.2 billion in the first week, with about 51 million copies at the centre of its model. It got there by scaling early US and European pre-order spending (about $180 million) to a global figure. That number covers a whole week, so it isn't directly comparable to the weekend forecasts above.

What Take-Two has actually said

OFFICIAL: Take-Two Q2 earnings call, Nov 9, 2026

Take-Two's own guidance is $8.0 billion to $8.2 billion in net bookings for the fiscal year ending March 31, 2027, about 21% up on last year. CEO Strauss Zelnick said pre-orders are running ahead of internal projections and that strong Ultimate Edition demand is built into that guidance. Take-Two has not given a launch-weekend target.

See our pre-order tracker page for the Sensor Tower numbers, and Standard vs Ultimate for what each edition costs ($79.99 / $99.99).

Our take: will GTA 6 beat $2 billion?

THEORY: our own read of the numbers above

If Sensor Tower is right, $1.45 billion is already sitting in pre-orders before a single disc is sold in a shop. Physical copies, Ultimate upgrades and launch-day buyers only need to add about $550 million for the weekend to clear $2 billion. That looks very likely. The real question is whether Rockstar posts a number on Nov 20, which would mean it went far past that.

Frequently asked questions

How much will GTA 6 make on its opening weekend?

Analysts forecast around $2 billion. Joost van Dreunen says more than $2B, and Bank of America calls $1.9B the passing grade. These are forecasts, not results.

When will Rockstar announce GTA 6 sales?

Take-Two hasn't said. Analysts expect a first figure by Monday, November 23, possibly as early as November 20 if launch day is huge.

How much did GTA 5 make at launch?

GTA V made $800 million on day one and passed $1 billion in three days in September 2013.

How many GTA 6 pre-orders are there?

Sensor Tower estimated 14.94 million digital pre-orders worth about $1.45 billion as of November 16. Take-Two hasn't confirmed a figure.
