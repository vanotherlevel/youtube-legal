GTA 6 has started unlocking, and New Zealand got it first. Rockstar releases Grand Theft Auto VI at midnight local time on November 19, so the rolling launch began in Auckland at midnight NZDT. That was 6 AM ET / 3 AM PT / 11 AM UK on Wednesday, November 18. Everyone else unlocks at their own local midnight over the next day.

OFFICIAL: Rockstar's release schedule says GTA 6 unlocks at midnight local time on Nov 19 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S

The day is already busy. The review embargo lifted at the same moment with a 99 Metascore. New Zealand's tourism board is joking about it, and the "New Zealand trick" is everywhere. Here's what's real, when it unlocks for you, and what to watch out for.

When GTA 6 unlocks where you live

These are the times New Zealand's midnight falls in other places (Vice's breakdown). Your own copy normally unlocks at your local midnight on November 19. In the US, digital PS5 copies are reported to unlock together at midnight ET (9 PM PT on Nov 18). Full list: GTA 6 release time in every region.

Tourism New Zealand joined in

New Zealand's official tourism account got involved. On its Pure New Zealand Instagram, Tourism New Zealand answered players joking about "moving" there for an early copy: "One perk of seeing the sun first? Getting GTA first too." It added that it would love to have them. VGC spotted it. It's the second government-adjacent GTA 6 joke this autumn: last month Australia's Fair Work Ombudsman posted a guide to taking leave for the game.

OFFICIAL: Tourism New Zealand post, reported by VGC Nov 18

Does the New Zealand trick work?

On Xbox, outlets say it should. On PS5 it's much harder and riskier. Neither Rockstar nor Microsoft has confirmed that it works for GTA 6.

Xbox Series X|S

  1. Finish your pre-order and the full pre-load first (up to 212 GB).
  2. Go to Settings > System > Language & location and set Location to New Zealand.
  3. Restart the console and launch your pre-loaded digital copy.
  4. If it stays locked, switch your location back and wait for your normal unlock.
REPORTED: steps via Insider Gaming, GamesRadar, GameSpot and Vice. GamesRadar warns that switching before you pre-order can show prices in NZ dollars

The catch: Microsoft's policy says you should only change region if you have actually moved, and GameSpot advises weighing that before you try. PC Gamer notes some past launches blocked the trick, so it is not guaranteed.

PS5

Changing your PS5's clock or settings won't unlock a US or UK pre-order early. You'd need a New Zealand PSN account and a copy bought from the New Zealand PlayStation Store. GameSpot reports that some players who changed their PS5 region have had their accounts suspended without warning. Our advice: on PS5, just wait for midnight.

REPORTED: GameSpot (PS5 suspensions), Vice and Dexerto (NZ account requirement)
Before midnight: install the required PS5 system update (update 14.23 guide) and the GTA 6 1.002.001 patch so nothing downloads at the worst moment.

Watch out for fake reviews and fake leaks

VGC reports that Metacritic already removed fake GTA 6 user reviews posted before launch and blocked new ones. Metacritic usually holds user reviews until a game has been out for about 36 hours. The 99 critic score is untouched. With the game out in one country, expect a wave of "leaked ending" clips. Mute the words you care about if you want to stay clean.

REPORTED: VGC, Nov 17

Frequently asked questions

Is GTA 6 out now?

Yes in New Zealand, since midnight NZDT on Nov 19 (6 AM ET Nov 18). Everywhere else unlocks at local midnight on Nov 19, with US digital PS5 copies reported at midnight ET / 9 PM PT.

Does the New Zealand trick work for GTA 6?

Outlets say it should work on Xbox by setting your console location to New Zealand, but Rockstar and Microsoft haven't confirmed it. On PS5 you need an NZ account and an NZ store copy, and GameSpot reports some region-changers were suspended.

Can I get banned for using the New Zealand trick?

Microsoft's policy says to change region only if you've moved. On PS5, GameSpot reports account suspensions for some players who changed region. It's safest to wait for your normal unlock.

What time does GTA 6 unlock in the UK?

At midnight UK time on Thursday, November 19 (00:00 GMT).
