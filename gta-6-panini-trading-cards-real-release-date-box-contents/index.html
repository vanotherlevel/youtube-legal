Real GTA 6 Trading Cards: Panini Release Date & Box Contents

GTA 6's collectible sports cards are becoming real: Panini America and Rockstar Games announced an official GTA VI sports trading card set on November 24, due in December 2026. No price has been announced and, when the reports below were published, there was no way to pre-order it.

ANNOUNCED: Panini America and Rockstar Games, November 24 (post embedded by VGC)

Release date

The announced window is December 2026. The trade site ICv2 gives a retail date of December 18, but the other coverage we checked only says December, so don't plan around the 18th until Panini publishes it.

Those per-box figures are averages, not guarantees, and pack odds haven't been published.

Rare cards to chase

Outlets report Silver, Pink, Teal and Light Blue colour parallels. GTA BOOM also lists team-colour versions. The numbered Legend cards are the scarce ones:

  • Bronze: numbered to 49
  • Silver: numbered to 25
  • Gold: one copy each

Players and teams shown so far

GTA BOOM names Dartanyon Pearson (Liberty City Wrath, number 84) as the first card shown, along with Maleek Rivers (Vice City Manatees), Carl Kasey (Vice City Narcos) and Mario Rugama (Liberty City Swingers). All four are fictional athletes from the game's world.

How big is the set?

This isn't settled. ICv2 describes a 70-card set: 30 normal, 9 illustrated, 9 retro, 10 rookie and 12 wrestling cards, with the wrestling cards printed in smaller numbers. GTA BOOM, writing on announcement day, said Panini had not yet set a full checklist. Treat the 70-card breakdown as reported, not final.

Real cards vs in-game cards

The physical product is much smaller than the hunt inside the game. PowerPyx counts 18 card sets and 480 cards in GTA 6, including non-sports sets. In the game you find them as pickups and can sell duplicates at Pawn Shops. The real set is a sports collection bought in packs.

Nothing in the coverage we checked says the real cards unlock anything in the game, so don't buy them expecting a bonus. For the in-game hunt, see our collectibles guide and 100% checklist.

Video above: all 18 in-game card sets, by How to Everything on YouTube.

Before you buy

  • Wait for a real listing. With no pre-orders announced, treat any early "pre-order" or single-card sale with suspicion.
  • Check availability where you live. The set comes through Panini America, and we found no details about other countries.
  • Watch for the checklist and odds before paying for boxes to chase a specific card.
