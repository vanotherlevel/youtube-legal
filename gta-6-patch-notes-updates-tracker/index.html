GTA 6 update 01.002.002 (1.0.2.2 on Xbox), released November 23, 2026, fixes several crashes, autosave problems, phone calls that didn't trigger (including story calls), stuck weather and a wanted level you couldn't lose. It's about 603 MB on PS5 and 1.6 GB on Xbox Series X|S. It's the first update with a published fix list.

GTA 6 update 01.002.002 patch notes (November 23)

OFFICIAL: Rockstar Support, summarised in our own words

Rockstar's list is short. The update fixes:

  • Crashes: several issues that caused the game to crash. Rockstar doesn't say which ones.
  • Weather: the weather not changing for long stretches.
  • Autosave: the game not autosaving correctly.
  • Phone calls: calls not triggering, including calls that move the story forward or unlock mission content.
  • Wanted level: being unable to lose your wanted level.
  • Gym: being unable to use the deadlift bar.
  • Fences: partners not handing over bags so you can sell them.

What to do after updating

  • A mission never unlocked? If you were waiting on a phone call, check again after updating (GTABase's advice, not Rockstar's).
  • Autosave: it's fixed in this version, but a manual save before big missions still costs nothing. See our save guide.
  • Still crashing? Gizmodo reported on November 25 that some PS5 players still say the game crashes. Work through our crash fixes.
  • Rollout: it went out in stages: 33% of PS5 players first and everyone about 6 hours later, according to the PS Store tracker PlayStation Game Size.

Did update 01.002.002 fix everything? (checked November 27)

No. The fan bug tracker GTA6BUGS compared its player "Me too" reports before and after the patch in a November 25 article. We found no newer patch as of November 27.

Update 01.002.000 (November 13)

The first update appeared on November 13, during the pre-load window. MP1st reported it through the PS Store tracker PlayStation Game Size: roughly 1 GB on PS5, and at first only for about 50% of players. The rest got it around 11 hours later. Red Bull Gaming lists it as about 1.07 GB on PS5 and 2.18 GB on Xbox Series X|S, rolled out in two waves according to a data miner.

  • What it changed: unknown. Rockstar didn't release a changelog because the game wasn't out yet.
  • Files that grew (datamined, via iGrandTheftAuto.com and MP1st): the AU/NZ and Japan packages grew by about 35 MB, including a story data file (+15.75 MiB) and an Americas level file (+15.625 MiB).
  • Firmware: MP1st reported the patch needs PS5 system software 14.22.00.00.
THEORY: MP1st expects it handles performance, stability and security. That's an educated guess, not a confirmed change list

Update 01.002.001 (November 16)

Three days later a second, smaller patch arrived: about 602 MB on PS5 and about 1.6 GB on Xbox Series X|S (Red Bull Gaming, sizes from data miners). It also went out in phases, so some players saw it hours before others. Kotaku called it "only 602MB." Again, no official notes.

This is the version GTA 6 launched on at midnight local time on November 19. Since November 23 the current version is 01.002.002, so if your game still shows 01.002.001, check for an update.

The PS5 system update you need

OFFICIAL: PlayStation Support US

On November 17, PlayStation Support US posted that Sony released a PS5 system software update that is required before playing Grand Theft Auto VI, and told players to download it before midnight on November 19. Kotaku noted that Sony didn't say what the update is for. If GTA 6 won't start, check this first: Settings > System > System Software > System Software Update and Settings > Update System Software.

Game crashing on PS5 with CE-108255-1? Update the console and the game first, then follow the steps in our GTA 6 launch bugs and fixes page.

Why the earlier updates have no patch notes

The November 13 and November 16 updates arrived before release, and Rockstar didn't publish change lists for them. The first post-launch update is the first one with official notes.

How to update GTA 6

  1. PS5: highlight GTA VI on the home screen, press Options, then choose Check for Update.
  2. Xbox Series X|S: open My games & apps > Manage > Updates and install anything listed for Grand Theft Auto VI.
  3. Updates roll out in waves. If a friend has it and you don't, wait a few hours and check again before reinstalling.

Frequently asked questions

What is the latest GTA 6 update?

Version 01.002.002 (1.0.2.2 on Xbox), released November 23, 2026: about 603 MB on PS5 and 1.6 GB on Xbox Series X|S.

Are there GTA 6 patch notes?

Yes, for 01.002.002. Rockstar Support lists fixes for crashes, weather, autosave, phone calls, wanted level, the gym deadlift bar and partners not handing over bags. The two pre-launch updates have none.

Did GTA 6 have a day-one patch?

Two patches arrived before launch (Nov 13 and Nov 16), and outlets call them day-one patches. Rockstar hasn't confirmed that label.

Do I need a PS5 system update to play GTA 6?

Yes. PlayStation Support said a PS5 system software update is required before playing Grand Theft Auto VI. MP1st reported the patch needs firmware 14.22.00.00.

Did update 01.002.002 fix GTA 6 crashes?

It fixes several crash causes, but Gizmodo reported on November 25 that some PS5 players still say the game crashes.

Did update 01.002.002 fix the Chapter 3 Jason softlock?

Not according to players: GTA6BUGS reported on November 25 that it is still being reported, and Rockstar's patch notes don't mention character switching.
