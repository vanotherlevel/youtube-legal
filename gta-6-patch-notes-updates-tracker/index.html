GTA 6 has had two updates so far, both before launch: version 01.002.000 on November 13 (about 1.07 GB on PS5) and 01.002.001 on November 16 (about 602 MB on PS5, about 1.6 GB on Xbox Series X|S). Rockstar hasn't published patch notes for either one, and as of November 20 no post-launch patch has been reported. We'll update this page the moment a new version or official notes appear.

REPORTED: version numbers and sizes come from PS Store trackers, data miners and player reports via MP1st, Red Bull Gaming and Kotaku. Rockstar hasn't confirmed sizes or contents

Update 01.002.000 (November 13)

The first update appeared on November 13, during the pre-load window. MP1st reported it through the PS Store tracker PlayStation Game Size: roughly 1 GB on PS5, and at first only for about 50% of players. The rest got it around 11 hours later. Red Bull Gaming lists it as about 1.07 GB on PS5 and 2.18 GB on Xbox Series X|S, rolled out in two waves according to a data miner.

  • What it changed: unknown. Rockstar didn't release a changelog because the game wasn't out yet.
  • Files that grew (datamined, via iGrandTheftAuto.com and MP1st): the AU/NZ and Japan packages grew by about 35 MB, including a story data file (+15.75 MiB) and an Americas level file (+15.625 MiB).
  • Firmware: MP1st reported the patch needs PS5 system software 14.22.00.00.
THEORY: MP1st expects it handles performance, stability and security. That's an educated guess, not a confirmed change list

Update 01.002.001 (November 16)

Three days later a second, smaller patch arrived: about 602 MB on PS5 and about 1.6 GB on Xbox Series X|S (Red Bull Gaming, sizes from data miners). It also went out in phases, so some players saw it hours before others. Kotaku called it "only 602MB." Again, no official notes.

This is the version GTA 6 launched on at midnight local time on November 19. If your game shows 01.002.001, you're up to date.

The PS5 system update you need

OFFICIAL: PlayStation Support US

On November 17, PlayStation Support US posted that Sony released a PS5 system software update that is required before playing Grand Theft Auto VI, and told players to download it before midnight on November 19. Kotaku noted that Sony didn't say what the update is for. If GTA 6 won't start, check this first: Settings > System > System Software > System Software Update and Settings > Update System Software.

Game crashing on PS5 with CE-108255-1? Update the console and the game first, then follow the steps in our GTA 6 launch bugs and fixes page.

Why there are no GTA 6 patch notes

Both updates landed before release, when patch notes would have spoiled content or mattered to nobody yet. Rockstar usually posts notes on its Support site and Newswire once a game is live, as it does for GTA Online and Red Dead Online title updates. The first post-launch patch is the one most likely to come with an official change list.

How to update GTA 6

  1. PS5: highlight GTA VI on the home screen, press Options, then choose Check for Update.
  2. Xbox Series X|S: open My games & apps > Manage > Updates and install anything listed for Grand Theft Auto VI.
  3. Updates roll out in waves. If a friend has it and you don't, wait a few hours and check again before reinstalling.

Frequently asked questions

What is the latest GTA 6 update?

Version 01.002.001, released November 16, 2026 (about 602 MB on PS5, about 1.6 GB on Xbox Series X|S). It's the version GTA 6 launched on. No post-launch patch has been reported as of November 20.

Are there GTA 6 patch notes?

Not yet. Rockstar hasn't published notes for 01.002.000 or 01.002.001. Only datamined file changes are known, and those aren't confirmed.

Did GTA 6 have a day-one patch?

Two patches arrived before launch (Nov 13 and Nov 16), and outlets call them day-one patches. Rockstar hasn't confirmed that label.

Do I need a PS5 system update to play GTA 6?

Yes. PlayStation Support said a PS5 system software update is required before playing Grand Theft Auto VI. MP1st reported the patch needs firmware 14.22.00.00.
