Paul Walker's car in GTA 6 is a lime-green Mitsubishi Eclipse lookalike from the first Fast and Furious film, parked in a villa's garage in the middle of Vespucci Island, just off Vice City. You can get it right at the start of the game with no mission: break into the house, open the garage, break the car's window, hotwire it and drive away.

It's not a licensed Mitsubishi, and Rockstar hasn't said a word about it, but the paint, the giant wing and the plate that reads HAD YOU leave little doubt who it's for. Below: the exact route, what to do at the house, the Toy Story secret in the trunk, and every detail players have spotted. We showed the whole thing in our Short, embedded above.

Where is Paul Walker's car in GTA 6?

REPORTED: Insider Gaming, Jalopnik, HotCars, plus player footage (Millzaa on X), Nov 19
  1. Head to Vespucci Island, the island just off Vice City. From Jason's house it's roughly a 5-mile drive.
  2. Drive toward Vice Beach. Jalopnik's directions: the house is in the middle of the island, and it's on your left as you head toward the beach.
  3. Look for the villa with two cars parked out front. That's the front of the house in the footage. Some players climbed the tall privacy hedge (TikToker @schlayerr did); others found the driveway gate already open.
  4. Break into the house. Insider Gaming says there's a door inside the villa you have to break to get into the garage.
  5. Find the Eclipse. It's the only car in that garage.
  6. Break the window and hotwire it (Insider Gaming: with the L and R sticks), then drive it out. It's yours.
Car not there? Jalopnik says some players didn't see it on their first visit and had to leave and come back before it spawned. Drive a few blocks away and return before you give up.

No source has found a story lock. Insider Gaming says it's available very early, as soon as the opening sequences are done and the open world opens up, and HotCars sums it up as no heist, no mission, no catch. That makes it one of the best early cars you can grab: Insider Gaming calls it much faster than Jason's default car.

The wheel, wing and tyre differences come from Jalopnik, which calls it a very impassioned dead ringer rather than an exact copy. HotCars adds that it even has underglow, like the film car.

THEORY: our read, not confirmed by Rockstar

The HAD YOU plate reads like a nod to the first film's famous street-race exchange, where Brian insists he almost had Dom. Rockstar hasn't explained the plate, and HotCars points out the studio hasn't confirmed the car is a deliberate tribute at all, so officially it's just an easter egg. Paul Walker died in 2013, the year GTA 5 came out, which is why so many players are treating the garage as a memorial.

The snake in the trunk (Toy Story easter egg)

There's a second joke hidden in the same car. Break open the boot (trunk) of the Eclipse and a huge snake attacks you. Insider Gaming and the player footage both tie it to Woody's "There's a snake in my boot!" line from Toy Story. Expect a fight: in the clip the snake is far bigger than anything you'd expect in a Vice City garage.

Frequently asked questions

Where is Paul Walker's car in GTA 6?

In a villa's garage in the middle of Vespucci Island, just off Vice City. Drive toward Vice Beach and the house is on your left; it has two cars parked out front. Break into the house, then the garage, and the green Eclipse is the only car inside.

Do you need to finish a mission to get the Fast and Furious car?

No. Insider Gaming, Jalopnik and HotCars all say there's no mission. You can grab it very early, as soon as the opening sequences are done and you can explore.

What does the Paul Walker car's licence plate say in GTA 6?

The orange plate reads HAD YOU, with GL.USA across the top. Rockstar hasn't explained it; it reads like a nod to the first film's street race.

What's in the trunk of the Eclipse?

A huge snake that attacks you when you break the boot open, a Toy Story joke on Woody's "There's a snake in my boot!" line.
