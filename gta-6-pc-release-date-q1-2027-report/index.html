GTA 6 PC Release Date: Is It Really Coming in Q1 2027?

No, GTA 6 is not on PC at launch, and Rockstar has not announced a PC release date. The game unlocks November 19, 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only. The best report so far, from GamesBeat, says the PC version is planned for Q1 2027 (January to March), only a few months after consoles.

OFFICIAL: GTA 6 releases November 19, 2026 on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. No PC version has been announced

With reviews out and the game about to unlock, "is GTA 6 on PC?" is one of the most asked questions this week. Here's what is confirmed, what is reported, and what is just a guess.

The Q1 2027 report

On October 13, 2026, GamesBeat's Alexander Lee reported that Rockstar plans to release the PC version of GTA VI in the first quarter of 2027. He cited four industry sources who asked not to be named. The report says the PC version still has to meet the same quality bar as the console version before it ships.

REPORTED: GamesBeat, four anonymous sources, Oct 13 2026. Rockstar did not respond to GamesBeat's request for comment

Comicbook.com, KitGuru and Tom's Hardware all relayed the report. None of them confirmed it independently. KitGuru called it "possible but unproven".

What Take-Two has said

Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick has never confirmed a PC version, but he has explained why consoles come first. In May 2026 he framed it as "serving the core consumer" (via PC Gamer). He also said PC can be a big share of a major game's sales, which is why most outlets read his comments as "PC is coming, just not yet".

REPORTED: Zelnick's comments as quoted by PC Gamer, May 2026. Not a PC announcement

A Q1 2027 PC launch would be by far Rockstar's shortest wait ever. That's why many fans are sceptical. Rockstar often moves dates late in development, and GTA 6 itself was delayed twice before landing on November 19.

Why it could come sooner this time

  • FiveM and roleplay. Rockstar bought Cfx.re, the team behind FiveM, in 2023. The huge GTA roleplay scene lives on PC, and GamesBeat links Rockstar's hinted creator tools to it.
  • GTA 6 Online. Twitch CEO Dan Clancy told Bloomberg GTA 6's online mode would launch "next year" (via GamesBeat). A PC launch alongside Online would make sense, but nothing links the two officially.
  • Sales. PC is a big market, and Take-Two is targeting record revenue this fiscal year.
THEORY: these are reasons outlets give for an earlier PC port. Rockstar hasn't stated any of them as its plan

Can you play GTA 6 on PC right now?

Not natively. Rockstar's Xbox Cloud Gaming support covers Xbox Series X|S and Xbox One consoles, and Dexerto reports Xbox's Matthew Ball confirmed it won't stream to PC, at least at launch. The real option today is Remote Play from a PS5 or Xbox you own to your PC. Those viral "GTA 6 PC" clips are exactly that: GTA 6 PC videos are fake: it's Remote Play. See also GTA 6 release time, every region and Xbox Cloud.

Be careful with "GTA 6 PC download" sites. There is no official PC build, so any file claiming to be one is fake and likely malware.

Our take

If you only play on PC, we'd plan for 2027, with Q1 as the best-case reported window, not a promise. The moment Rockstar posts a PC date on its Newswire, we'll update this page. Until then, check our GTA 6 news hub and the Digital Foundry console tech review to choose your console.

Frequently asked questions

Is GTA 6 on PC?

Not at launch. GTA 6 releases November 19, 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only. Rockstar hasn't announced a PC version or date.

When is the GTA 6 PC release date?

There's no official date. GamesBeat reported in October 2026, citing four sources, that Rockstar plans a PC release in Q1 2027 (January to March). Rockstar hasn't confirmed it.

Can I play GTA 6 on PC with Xbox Cloud Gaming?

Not at launch. Rockstar lists Xbox Cloud Gaming on Xbox Series X|S and Xbox One consoles, and Dexerto reports Xbox's Matthew Ball said it won't stream to PC, at least at launch. Use Remote Play from your own console instead.

How long did GTA 5 take to come to PC?

About 19 months. GTA V launched on consoles on September 17, 2013 and on PC on April 14, 2015. Red Dead Redemption 2 took about a year.
