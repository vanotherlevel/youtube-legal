Yes, GTA 6 and Phantom Blade Zero are both nominated for Game of the Year at The Game Awards 2026. Grand Theft Auto VI picked up 8 nominations, the most of any game this year, and Phantom Blade Zero picked up 5, all announced on November 17, two days before GTA 6 is even out.

Below is every category each game is in, the full Game of the Year list, why GTA 6 qualifies by a single day, how both games stack up, and our own picks for December 10. It goes with our video, which covers the moment the nominees were revealed.

Labels on this page: OFFICIAL = The Game Awards, Rockstar or S-Game's own words. REPORTED = press, trackers and analysts. MY READING = our opinion, not a prediction from anyone inside the show.

Game of the Year 2026 nominees

OFFICIAL: The Game Awards, Nov 17
  • 007 First Light
  • Control Resonant
  • Grand Theft Auto VI
  • Phantom Blade Zero
  • Resident Evil Requiem
  • The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time

The Game Awards posted the six nominees on X with a link to start your ballot. The show is on December 10, 2026.

So the two games meet head to head in four categories: Game of the Year, Best Game Direction, Best Art Direction and Best Score & Music. They avoid each other in the action categories, because GTA 6 is in Action/Adventure and Phantom Blade Zero is in Best Action.

Why GTA 6 is eligible before it's even out

OFFICIAL: The Game Awards eligibility rule (via Insider Gaming)

To count for 2026, a game has to be commercially available on or before Friday, November 20, 2026, at 9 PM PT. GTA 6 launches on November 19, so it gets in by one day (Insider Gaming, GamingBolt).

The catch: nominees were announced on November 17, two days before launch. Whether the jury had early copies hasn't been confirmed. From launch to the show on December 10 there are 21 days to play one of the biggest games ever made. Next year it would be too late, so this is GTA 6's only shot.

Why Phantom Blade Zero earned its spot

Our video makes the case in five reasons. Here is the short version:

  1. The world. S-Game calls the style "kung fu punk". Soul, the main character, has 66 days to live, and "66 Days" is even the name of a difficulty mode.
  2. The bosses. Every fight is a duel. Hua, the Deadly Betrothed, was hard enough that S-Game's Nov 4 patch reduced her grab attacks on Wayfarer and Pathbreaker difficulty (DSOGaming).
  3. The builds. You carry weapons and Phantom Edges at the same time and switch mid-combo. We ranked every one of them in this video.
  4. The combat. Parries, dodges and counters chain like a kung fu film. The fights were choreographed with Kenji Tanigaki.
  5. The players. S-Game says it sold over 4 million copies in 72 hours, on PC and PS5, with no Xbox version.

How action games and Rockstar have done at the show

  • Sekiro won Game of the Year in 2019, and Elden Ring in 2022.
  • Black Myth: Wukong was nominated for Game of the Year in 2024 and won Best Action and the Players' Voice.
  • GTA V won Game of the Year at the 2013 VGX awards, the year before The Game Awards began.
  • Red Dead Redemption 2 lost Game of the Year to God of War in 2018, but won four awards, including Best Narrative and Best Performance (Roger Clark as Arthur Morgan).

Our picks for December 10

MY READING
  • Best Action Game: Phantom Blade Zero. Nothing else on that list moves as fast, and it's the category Wukong won.
  • Best Art Direction: Phantom Blade Zero. One frame and you know what game it is. This is a straight fight with GTA 6.
  • Players' Voice: Phantom Blade Zero's best shot, if its fans vote the way Wukong's did.
  • Game of the Year: GTA 6 is the favourite on name alone. But a jury that hasn't finished it is making a bet, and Phantom Blade Zero is the game they've actually played.
Players' Voice is the one award decided by the fans. Vote at thegameawards.com. Our video says fan voting closes December 9; check the site for the exact time.
