Press up on the D-pad to open Jason's iFruit phone in GTA 6. The home screen has 10 app icons, and the ones we read by name on screen are WhatUp (messages), Contacts, Gallery and Snapmatic, plus a Reminders widget. For the radio, hold left on the D-pad in a car and switch stations with L1/R1. We saw Cocoteo FM, Dirty South Classics, Emotion 98.3, Flash FM, Gloriana Blues Radio, Honey and Kaleidoscope on the station bar.

Everything below comes from frames we read at full size in launch streams, plus launch-day guides from big outlets (credited in each section). This page covers every app, how texting works (yes, you can text while you drive or fly), contacts and calls, the settings menu, the radio controls, and every station we know of. More apps are reported, but we only list one as seen when we've read its name ourselves.

Labels: ON SCREEN = we read it in a launch stream (MELOO, Ben-Gun, Dalek, GTASeries, or the Adin Ross clip posted by ClipCloud). OFFICIAL = Rockstar's own words. REPORTED = launch-day outlet guides. OUR TAKE = our read. If an app or station isn't here, it hasn't been confirmed yet.

Apps reported by launch-day guides

REPORTED (outlets, Nov 18)
  • RydeMe: the taxi app that works as fast travel. You set a marker, pay a fare that rises with distance, and can pay $100 to skip the ride. It opens in Chapter 2, because it needs the bank account you get in the mission Buck Me (PowerPyx, VGC). Full steps are in our beginner tips.
  • Vice City Real Estate: where you buy garages ($4,000 to $40,000) from your phone, per GameSpot. Our garages guide lists all 10.
  • WAiNK: a scanner app. GTABase's 100% guide says you use it to scan vehicles as they drive by (200 of 277 base models count), 100 WAiNK Locations (landmarks and sculptures), and 45 magazine tattoos.
  • Paradise Taxi (a contact, not an app): before RydeMe opens, PowerPyx says to go to Contacts > All Contacts > Paradise Taxi. The flat fee to skip the ride is $100.

We haven't read RydeMe, Vice City Real Estate or WAiNK on a phone screen in our footage yet. They may sit behind the two unlabeled icons, or open later in the story. We'll update the table when we see them.

Texting in GTA 6 (WhatUp)

ON SCREEN (Adin Ross clip, MELOO, Dalek)

Yes, you can text while driving in GTA 6. The car keeps going while Jason holds the phone in one hand. Adin Ross's reaction to it went viral on launch morning, and MELOO's stream goes further: at about 16170 s Jason reads Brian's texts while flying a plane, with the cockpit gauges still moving. Dalek reads a thread while riding a scooter.

Messages work like a real chat app. Your inbox is split into Suggested (threads with something new, marked with a red dot) and Messages (everything else). Each thread shows a "New message" divider and your replies in blue bubbles. The things you can do from a thread:

  • Location pins: a contact can send a place as a card. Press Set Destination and it becomes your waypoint. Examples we read: Cal sends "Casa De Duval" ("Beers in your fridge and me in your pad."), and Kenton sends three wreck dive spots: Nuestra Señora de la Ética Laxa, Nuestra Señora de la Noche and Nuestra Señora del Ocio.
  • Expired pins: old pins turn grey and say "Map Pin Expired", like Brian's "Cal's fishing spot" in the Adin Ross clip.
  • Item cards: Brian's thread also shows a "Corsair Key" card next to Jason's reply "Dropped that stuff from the boutique".
  • Photos: Cal sends a photo of the red Phoenix you have to steal, captioned "Help her rise from the flames" (MELOO, ~3276 s).
  • News links: Cal shares a news story card ("Leonida Governor compares...") with the red newspaper icon.
  • Money hints: Donnie texts that he "Left a taste of the profits 4u in yr mailbox", and a hint pops up: "Collect cash from completed jobs from your mailbox" (MELOO, ~2590 s). His thread also has a Pelican Valley pin. Brian also texts that he left your pay "in your mailbox" (MELOO, ~15330 s).
OUR TAKE: pins in texts are the quickest way to set a route. Open the thread, put the cursor on the pin, press Cross, and close the phone. You never have to open the full map.

Contacts and calls

ON SCREEN (Adin Ross clip, MELOO ~4440 s)

Open Contacts and you get an All Contacts button at the top and Suggested people under it. When a mission wants a call, the objective says so ("Call Brian") and that person sits in Suggested. All Contacts is sorted A to Z with letter tabs across the top (Jason's early list shows B, C, D, J, L and P). Pick a name and a menu pops up with three choices: Call, Open WhatUp and Open Snapmatic. So every contact has a chat thread and a Snapmatic profile.

Phone settings: wallpaper, ringtones and the cop scanner

ON SCREEN (MELOO, ~3246-3268 s)

The settings screen is headed with Jason and iFruit, the game's phone brand. It has three entries:

  • Wallpaper: a grid of coloured wave designs. The one MELOO picks is called "Orange Circles".
  • Ringtone: Silent, Wind Chimes, Dreamy, Beeps, Retro, Beam Me Up, Bugging, Echo, Samba Nights and Digital Raindrops (cut off at the bottom), with more below. Dreamy was set before he changed it to Retro.
  • Third-Party Plugins: this is where cop_scanner_v3.1.9 lives.

The cop scanner plugin (police dispatch)

Early in the story, Cal says "let me put this thing on your phone" and installs it. The hint reads: "You can now listen in on cop radio frequencies and will be notified of dispatch areas." The plugin page says "Version 3.1.9" and calls it an "encrypted radio scanner that picks up frequencies from police comms". It's a big help for escaping the cops: see our wanted level guide.

Frequently asked questions

How do you open the phone in GTA 6?

Press up on the D-pad. Select with Cross, scroll with the right stick, and back out with Circle. You can use it while driving, on a scooter or even flying a plane.

What apps are on the GTA 6 phone?

The home screen has 10 icons. The ones we read by name are WhatUp (messages), Contacts, Gallery and Snapmatic, plus a Reminders widget and a settings screen with a cop scanner plugin. Outlets also report RydeMe (fast travel), Vice City Real Estate (garages) and WAiNK (scanning).

Can you text while driving in GTA 6?

Yes. Jason holds the phone in one hand and the car keeps going. We also saw him read texts while flying a plane in MELOO's launch stream.

How many radio stations are in GTA 6?

The total isn't confirmed. Rockstar officially revealed six (Flash FM, Cocoteo FM, Back Country Radio, AfroBank FM, The Chamber 106.6, Dirty South Classics). In the game we also saw Emotion 98.3, Gloriana Blues Radio, Honey and Kaleidoscope.

How do you change the radio station in GTA 6?

Hold left on the D-pad in a vehicle to open the radio and vehicle controls, then press L1 or R1 to scroll through stations. Press Cross to turn the radio off.
