GTA 6 PlayStation Record: 39M PS5 Players in 4 Days

By vAnother Level · Updated 2026-11-26

GTA 6 is the biggest game launch in PlayStation history, with more than 39 million unique players on PS5 from launch on November 19 through November 22, Sony says. It also set a new PlayStation record for the most people playing one game at the same time.

OFFICIAL: PlayStation Blog post, Nov 23, 2026, covering the launch window that ended Nov 22.

Every number Sony shared

StatSony's figure
Unique players on PS5More than 39 million
Total PS5 playtime683 million hours
Average per player17.4 hours
Concurrent playersAll-time PlayStation record (no figure given)
Newly connected PS5 consolesBiggest single day since the console launched, outside end-of-year holidays
New PS5 consolesGTA 6 was the first game played on 70% of them since its launch

Sony says the concurrent record beats every earlier title on PlayStation, including multiplayer, free-to-play and single-player games. That stands out because GTA 6 launched with its story mode, not with a new online mode.

Players are not the same as copies sold

39 million is a player count, so it should not be read as 39 million PS5 copies sold. Unique players can include more than one person or account using the same copy or console. The sales figures that exist are separate:

  • Official (Take-Two, Nov 20): 32.5 million units sold before release across PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, and an estimated $3.79 billion in day-one retail sales.
  • Estimate (Alinea Analytics, Nov 23): 44.9 million copies sold through by the end of launch weekend, 33.7 million of them on PS5. This is an analyst estimate, not an official number.

The full breakdown is on our GTA 6 launch sales page.

What 17.4 hours means for your save

An average of 17.4 hours in the opening days shows how hard people went at launch. If you are behind that, there is no rush: see how long the story runs on our chapters and story length page, or pick your next goal from the 100% completion checklist.

THEORY (our take): Sony's post covers PlayStation only. We have not seen a matching player count from Microsoft, so the all-platform total is unknown.

Frequently asked questions

How many people played GTA 6 on PS5 at launch?

Sony says more than 39 million unique players from November 19 through November 22, 2026.

How many hours did PS5 players spend in GTA 6?

683 million hours in total, an average of 17.4 hours per player during the opening days.

Did GTA 6 set a concurrent player record?

Yes, the highest concurrent player count for any game on PlayStation. Sony did not publish the number.

Has GTA 6 sold 39 million copies on PS5?

Sony did not say that. 39 million is unique players, not copies sold.
