GTA 6 Broke Internet Records: 33 Tbit/s and ISPs Slowing Down

By vAnother Level · Updated 2026-11-19

Yes, GTA 6 broke internet traffic records, and it did it before launch. On November 10, the night the 212 GB pre-load opened, internet exchange DE-CIX hit an all-time global peak of 33.01 Tbit/s, and UK provider Virgin Media O2 reported its biggest data day ever.

OFFICIAL (DE-CIX press release, Virgin Media O2): record traffic peaks on Nov 10, 2026, the day GTA 6 pre-loads opened

DE-CIX CTO Dr Thomas King said the "sheer scale" of the surge is unmatched. For context, the Frankfurt exchange is Europe's biggest. DE-CIX normally sees spikes like this for big software updates and football finals.

IMPORTANT: DE-CIX reports total traffic. It has not said how much of the 33 Tbit/s was GTA 6 itself

Why GTA 6 hit the internet so hard

  • The size: the pre-load needs up to 212 GB, according to Rockstar's support FAQ.
  • Everyone at once: PS5 pre-loads opened at midnight local time on Tuesday, November 10. Xbox Series X|S opened at 12 AM GMT the same day. TechPowerUp reports the date was first November 12 and was moved up two days.
  • The demand: Sony says GTA 6 is the most pre-ordered game ever on the PlayStation Store. Our page on the reported 14.94M pre-orders has the numbers.

Some ISPs slowed down

Not every network coped. GGRecon reports that UK provider YouFibre emailed customers about slower speeds, blaming a "network capacity issue" from GTA 6 downloads. CEO Jeremy Chelot said customers around Yorkshire saw congestion while the company rebalanced its traffic.

REPORTED (GGRecon, Dexerto): temporary congestion at some providers, not a wider outage

In the US, Spectrum went the other way. It offered a free "Gig Speed Lift" to eligible customers from November 9 to 30, upgrading qualifying plans to 1 Gig, or to 2 Gig in some areas, if activated in the My Spectrum app by November 30. Restrictions apply.

Frequently asked questions

Did GTA 6 break internet records?

Yes. On November 10, 2026, when pre-loads opened, DE-CIX hit an all-time global peak of 33.01 Tbit/s and Virgin Media O2 reported record UK data traffic. DE-CIX hasn't said how much of that was GTA 6 alone.

How big is the GTA 6 download?

Up to 212 GB, according to Rockstar's support FAQ.

Why is my internet slow with GTA 6?

Some providers, like UK ISP YouFibre, reported temporary congestion from GTA 6 downloads. Downloading overnight or over a wired connection usually helps.

How long does GTA 6 take to download?

Deutsche Telekom estimates about 28 minutes at 1,000 Mbit/s, 9 h 25 min at 50 Mbit/s and over 47 hours at 10 Mbit/s.
