GTA 6 Sales: $3.79 Billion Day One, 32.5M Sold (Official)

UPDATED Nov 20, official: GTA 6 made an estimated $3.79 billion in day-one retail sales worldwide and sold 32.5 million units before release, Take-Two and Rockstar announced on Friday, Nov 20. Rockstar calls it "the biggest entertainment launch in history", a new record for entertainment in any medium.

OFFICIAL: Take-Two / Rockstar press release (Business Wire), Nov 20, 2026

The official GTA 6 numbers

FigureWhat it countsSource
$3.79 billionDay-one retail sales, company estimate through the end of Nov 19 worldwideTake-Two press release
32.5 million unitsSold before release, a new record across all platformsTake-Two press release
99 on MetacriticCalled the highest-rated game of all time in the releaseTake-Two press release
  • Where it sold: PlayStation Store, Microsoft Store, Rockstar Games Store and retailers worldwide. The release doesn't give a PS5 vs Xbox split.
  • Sam Houser (Rockstar founder and president) said in the release that the team is grateful and humbled by the response from players worldwide.
  • For scale: the same release says GTA 5 has sold in nearly 235 million units, and the GTA series nearly 480 million.
Retail sales are not profit. $3.79 billion is Take-Two's estimate of what players spent at retail on day one. It is not Take-Two's booked revenue. The company will report its own revenue in its next earnings.

Official vs the estimates

The official figure is far above every outside estimate. Sensor Tower's launch-evening estimate (below) was about 24.58 million copies and $2.36 billion, but it counted only digital console sales. Take-Two's $3.79 billion also counts boxed copies and every storefront. Compared with GTA 5's $815.7 million in its first 24 hours (Guinness World Records), $3.79 billion is roughly 4.6 times as much. That's our own math, and the two figures are measured differently.

REPORTED: everything below this line is the earlier third-party tracking, kept for the timeline

Before the official numbers (Nov 19, launch evening): GTA 6 has reportedly sold about 24.58 million copies worth roughly $2.36 billion on PS5 and Xbox by the end of its first day, counting months of pre-orders. That's Sensor Tower's Thursday-evening estimate, reported by GameSpot and TweakTown. It's up from 19.1 million ($1.84 billion) that same morning, and roughly three times the money GTA 5 made in its first 24 hours. Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick said on TBPN the company will share official sales data soon; until then, nothing here is official.

On the morning estimate, FOX worked it out at about 2.25 times GTA 5's money. On the evening figure it's closer to 2.9 times ($2.36 billion vs $815.7 million) and about 13 million more copies. Keep one thing in mind: GTA 6's number includes months of pre-orders, while GTA 5's record is a 24-hour sales figure. Sensor Tower also says GTA 6 crossed $1 billion a week before launch.

REPORTED: analysts, via FOX and Reuters
  • Wedbush Securities expects about $3 billion and 30 million copies by the end of the year, as reported by the New York Times (via FOX).
  • TD Cowen's Doug Creutz told Reuters the timing and size of the next GTA Online is "the most important question for the stock", more than GTA 6 unit sales.
  • Some analysts warn sales could slow faster after launch, since so many copies were pre-ordered (FOX).
  • Take-Two shares were down about 1% on Thursday after closing 6% higher the day before (Reuters).

Take-Two's own figures could arrive as early as Friday, FOX reports, without naming a source, and Strauss Zelnick said on TBPN that official GTA 6 sales data is coming soon (GameSpot). On the November earnings call he said the company isn't "ready to declare victory" yet. We'll update this page when official numbers land.

How big is that?

In its own November report, Sensor Tower said GTA 6 was at roughly double the pre-order revenue of the previous record holder, Call of Duty: Modern Warfare II, nine days before launch. It also said GTA 6 passed $300 million in its first week of pre-orders, and that Hogwarts Legacy, Diablo IV and Marvel's Spider-Man 2 were each below $200 million at the same point.

Take-Two stock jumps 6% as reviews land

REPORTED: Stocktwits, Nov 18

Take-Two (TTWO) shares were up 6% in pre-market trading on Wednesday, Nov 18, after the reviews dropped, according to Stocktwits (Arnab Paul). Earlier that morning Stocktwits had the stock up 2.6% after an 11% two-day fall, and it says TTWO is still down about 17% in 2026. Take-Two still hasn't given an official pre-order figure; at its last earnings report it only said demand was "far in excess of expectations."

2.46 million people watched on Twitch

REPORTED: TwitchTracker via Stocktwits

Once New Zealand unlocked, GTA VI drew roughly 2.46 million concurrent Twitch viewers, about 66% of everything being watched on Twitch at that moment, per TwitchTracker figures quoted by Stocktwits. For where to watch the big streams, see our 24-hour launch livestream guide.

The only real limit: consoles

REPORTED: Yahoo Finance (Daniel Howley), Circana's Mat Piscatella

Yahoo Finance reports about 75% of pre-orders are on PS5. Circana's Mat Piscatella told it the install base of PS5 and Xbox Series consoles is "the only real limitation" on the game's reach, and consoles cost far more now than at GTA 5's launch:

ConsoleLaunch pricePrice now (Yahoo Finance)

For comparison, Piscatella said a PS3 cost about $250 and an Xbox 360 about $230 when GTA 5 launched in 2013, roughly $350 after inflation.

TweakTown also reports GTA 6 is now the most pre-ordered game ever on both the PlayStation and Xbox stores, by Sensor Tower's tracking. For what analysts expect next, see our opening weekend sales forecast. The reviews landed at 99 on Metacritic.

Sensor Tower: other games will feel it

FORECAST: Sensor Tower prediction

Sensor Tower predicts that daily players on everything that is not GTA 6 will collectively fall by more than 10% in the month after launch. Wccftech reports Roblox is expected to be the exception. Sensor Tower based it on recent big launches, where other PlayStation games dipped about 5% on average (Monster Hunter Wilds -7.6%, Oblivion Remastered -6.0%, Resident Evil Requiem -4.7%), and notes seasonality also plays a part.

What happens next

  • Nov 18 (done): reviews landed at 99 on Metacritic, and New Zealand unlocked first.
  • Nov 19 (OFFICIAL): GTA 6 unlocks at midnight local time. See the release time in every region.
  • After launch: Take-Two is the only source for real sales. Until it reports, every number on this page is an estimate.

Frequently asked questions

How many copies has GTA 6 sold?

Officially, 32.5 million units before release, per Take-Two's Nov 20 press release. Day-one retail sales reached an estimated $3.79 billion through the end of Nov 19.

How much money did GTA 6 make on day one?

About $3.79 billion in retail sales worldwide, by Take-Two's own estimate. That is what players spent, not Take-Two's profit or booked revenue.

Is GTA 6 the biggest launch ever?

Rockstar says yes: it calls GTA 6 the biggest entertainment launch in history and a new record for entertainment across any medium.

Did GTA 6 beat GTA 5's launch record?

Yes, by a wide margin. GTA 5 made $815.7 million in its first 24 hours; GTA 6's official day-one retail figure is $3.79 billion, about 4.6 times as much (measured differently).

Why was Sensor Tower's estimate lower?

Sensor Tower tracked only digital console sales (about 24.58 million copies, $2.36 billion). The official figure also includes boxed copies and all storefronts.

Is GTA 6 the most pre-ordered game ever?

By Sensor Tower's tracking, yes: it says GTA 6 was at about double Call of Duty: Modern Warfare II's pre-order revenue at the same point. It is an estimate, not an official record.

Do more people pre-order GTA 6 on PS5 or Xbox?

PS5, by a lot. Sensor Tower puts PlayStation at about 74-75% of pre-orders and Xbox at about 25-26%.

Do these numbers include physical copies?

No. They are Sensor Tower estimates of digital console pre-orders only, net of refunds.
