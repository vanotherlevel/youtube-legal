GTA 6 Preload Datamined: 120Hz on PS5 Pro, Multiplayer Strings and a 223 GB Package

By vAnother Level · Updated 2026-11-10

The GTA 6 preload went live, and dataminers got to work within hours. The game files themselves are encrypted, so nobody has seen the map, story or gameplay. What they can read is the PlayStation metadata and file listings, and that's already turned up a few things Rockstar hasn't talked about.

Update, November 10: Rockstar has now confirmed GTA 6 targets 30 FPS on all consoles, including PS5 Pro, with PSSR and ray-traced shadows as the Pro extras. So the 120Hz flag below is not a 120 FPS mode at launch. Full story.

Unconfirmed: none of this is confirmed by Rockstar or Sony

1. A 120Hz mode on PS5 Pro

Longtime Rockstar dataminer Tex2 (@TexFunz2) says the metadata lists 120Hz / High Framerate support on PS5 Pro, but no VRR. Careful: 120Hz output doesn't mean 120 FPS. It's often used to run a 40 FPS mode smoothly, but Tex2 says whether that happens "is up to Rockstar." The store page only says "PS5 Pro Enhanced."

2. A newer PSSR upscaler

The files list a PSSR version of 13.60. Recent PS5 Pro games like Cyberpunk 2077 and Assassin's Creed Shadows list 13.00A, so GTA 6 seems to ship with a newer build of Sony's upscaler. There's also a flag matching PS5's High CPU Frequency Mode.

3. Multiplayer strings

Metadata spotted by @PlaystationSize includes `launchMultiplayerActivity` next to `joinSession`. Dataminers also say the story content sits apart from a shared base install, which could let an online mode arrive as its own download later. Rockstar hasn't announced GTA 6 Online.

4. The real file size

Rockstar says up to 212 GB. The PlayStation package listing shows 223.09 GB in 53 files, and reports say a PS5 system update is required. See how long that takes on your internet.

Then vs now

Back on GTA 5, dataminers dug GTA Online heist content out of the PS3 and Xbox 360 update files long before heists finally launched in 2015. This time dataminers have the whole game on their drives ten days early, but it's locked tight, so all they can read is the label on the box.

Frequently asked questions

Does GTA 6 have a 120 FPS mode?

Unconfirmed. Dataminer Tex2 found 120Hz output support for PS5 Pro in the metadata, which isn't the same as 120 FPS. Rockstar hasn't announced performance modes.

Does GTA 6 have online multiplayer?

Rockstar hasn't announced it. Dataminers found multiplayer-related metadata strings, which suggests an online mode may come later as a separate download.

How big is the GTA 6 preload on PS5?

Rockstar says up to 212 GB; the PlayStation package listing shows about 223 GB in 53 files.
