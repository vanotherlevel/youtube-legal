GTA 6 Preload Not Downloading on PS5? Here's the Fix

By vAnother Level · Updated 2026-11-09
Home › GTA 6 Guides
On this page
  1. Fix 1: check the time in your region
  2. Fix 2: digital pre-order, right account
  3. Fix 3: storage, auto-download and Rest Mode
  4. Fix 4: still stuck or stuck at 0%

If your GTA 6 preload isn't downloading on PS5 tonight, there's a good chance nothing is broken. The PS5 preload opens at midnight local time on November 10, and it rolls out region by region, starting with New Zealand. That's why some players are already showing finished installs while yours still shows a countdown. Xbox works differently: it opened at once worldwide at midnight GMT, which was 7 PM ET on November 9.

Preload dates from Rockstar; GTA 6 launches November 19, 2026

Fix 1: check the time in your region

Open GTA 6 in your Game Library. If you see a countdown or a pre-order page instead of a Download button, the preload simply hasn't opened where you live yet. No setting will make it start early. In the US, that means midnight on the night of November 9 to 10, in your own time zone.

Fix 2: digital pre-order, right account

Only digital pre-orders can preload. Boxed PS5 copies come with a download code instead. You also have to be signed in to the PSN account that bought the game. If someone else in your house pre-ordered it, the preload won't show on your profile. You can check the order under Transaction History on PlayStation Store.

Fix 3: storage, auto-download and Rest Mode

  • Free up at least 212 GB. That's Rockstar's figure. Some players report the PS5 install showing a little more (around 223 GB), so leave extra room.
  • Turn on automatic downloads so the preload starts on its own.
  • Leave the PS5 in Rest Mode, not fully off. Rest Mode keeps downloading, but switching the console off stops it.

Fix 4: still stuck or stuck at 0%

  1. Find GTA 6 in your Game Library and press Download by hand.
  2. Open Downloads/Uploads in the Control Center and make sure it isn't paused, and that the PS5 is online.
  3. Restart the PS5 from the Power menu. Choose Restart, not Rest Mode.
  4. Go to Settings > Users and Accounts > Other > Restore Licenses, then try again.
  5. Still nothing? Cancel and delete the stuck download and start it again from the Game Library.

On launch week millions of people are downloading at once, so if PlayStation Network itself is having problems, waiting is the only real fix. Check the PSN service status page before you start deleting things.

Frequently asked questions

When does the GTA 6 preload start on PS5?

At midnight local time on November 10, 2026, rolling out region by region starting with New Zealand. On Xbox Series X|S it started worldwide at midnight GMT (7 PM ET on November 9).

Why is my GTA 6 preload not showing on PS5?

Usually because it hasn't opened in your time zone yet, the game was bought as a boxed copy, or you're signed in to a different PSN account from the one that pre-ordered it.

How much space does the GTA 6 preload need?

Rockstar says up to 212 GB. Some players report the PS5 install a little larger, so free up extra space to be safe.
