GTA 6 Costs $79.99. Adjusted for Inflation, GTA 5 Cost About the Same

By vAnother Level · Updated 2026-11-10
Home › GTA 6 Guides

$79.99 feels like a lot for a game. But GTA 5 cost about the same. GTA 5 launched at $59.99 in September 2013. Adjusted for US inflation, that's roughly $80 to $86 in 2026 money, depending on which inflation measure you use. GTA 6's standard edition is $79.99, and the Ultimate Edition is $99.99.

Prices from Rockstar / PlayStation Store; inflation range from CPI-based estimates

The numbers

  • GTA 5 (2013): $59.99.
  • That $59.99 today: about $80 to $86 (US CPI-based estimates differ by month and method).
  • GTA 6 (2026): $79.99 standard, $99.99 Ultimate.
  • What's different: wages haven't always kept up with prices, so it can still feel more expensive even if the inflation math is close.

And you get a lot of game for it: up to 212 GB of it. See how long that takes to download, and if you're on an old console, GTA 6 is playable on Xbox One through the cloud.
