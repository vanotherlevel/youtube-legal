GTA 6 Is Pre-Loading on PS5, and Fans Just Heard the Main Menu Theme

By vAnother Level · Updated 2026-11-09

  5. Get ready for launch

GTA 6 is going onto PS5 consoles right now. Ten days before the November 19 launch, players are posting their pre-loads finishing, and the first thing everyone noticed was the main menu theme. One clip of it passed 3 million views in a few hours.

Launch date November 19, 2026 (Rockstar)

First listen: the GTA 6 main menu theme

GTA 6 Updates (@gtasix_) posted the first recording of the theme that plays on the PS5 home screen once the game is installed. It's the first new Grand Theft Auto menu music in 13 years.

BREAKING: First listen to the GTA 6 main menu theme on PS5.

— GTA 6 Updates (@gtasix_) November 9, 2026

Other players backed it up with their own installs, including @NikTek ("GTA 6 is actually being installed on my console") and @GTASixInfo, who posted the moment theirs finished.

Then vs now: 18 years of GTA home-screen music

Here's the angle most posts skipped. Every HD Grand Theft Auto has had its own console home-screen theme, and each one set the tone before you pressed a button. @GTAVI_Countdown lined all three up side by side:

GTA HD Trilogy PlayStation Home Screen Music: GTA 4 (2008), GTA 5 (2013), GTA 6 (2026)

— GTA 6 Countdown (@GTAVI_Countdown) November 9, 2026

The detail nobody noticed: 4 hidden 'Extra' add-ons

@iGrandTheftAuto spotted that GTA 6's PS5 pre-load menu changed overnight. It used to show only "Grand Theft Auto VI". Now it lists the main game plus four add-ons named Extra2, Extra3, Extra4 and Extra5.

What the Extras are is unknown

Rockstar hasn't said what they are. Extra install chunks like this are often language packs, optional data or launch content, but nothing is confirmed, so treat any guess you see as a guess.

Official: GTA VI: The Festival in Miami

Rockstar also made it official today: Grand Theft Auto VI: The Festival is a live event on November 21 at Mana Wynwood in Miami, two days after launch, with artists from the game and its official soundtrack.

Grand Theft Auto VI: The Festival, a special live event on November 21 at Mana Wynwood in Miami to celebrate the launch of GTAVI, featuring a star-studded lineup of artists from the game and the official soundtrack.

— Rockstar Games (@RockstarGames) November 9, 2026
  • Pre-sale ticket lottery: sign up at gtavi-thefestival.miami and set up a dice.fm account by 4 p.m. ET on Tuesday, November 10.
  • Tickets: a chance at tickets on Wednesday at 12 p.m. ET, per Rockstar.

Frequently asked questions

Can you pre-load GTA 6?

Yes, on PS5. Players began posting finished pre-loads on November 9, 2026, ten days before launch. Rockstar hasn't posted a separate pre-load announcement.

When does GTA 6 come out?

November 19, 2026, on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.

What is GTA VI: The Festival?

A live launch event on November 21, 2026 at Mana Wynwood in Miami, with artists from the game and its soundtrack. Tickets go through a pre-sale lottery on gtavi-thefestival.miami.

What are the Extra add-ons in the GTA 6 pre-load?

Unknown. The PS5 pre-load menu lists Extra2 to Extra5 next to the main game, but Rockstar hasn't explained them.
