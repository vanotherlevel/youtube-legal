PSN Went Down Hours Before GTA 6: What Happened and What to Do

Yes, PlayStation Network went down on Wednesday afternoon, November 18, just hours before GTA 6 unlocks at midnight in the US. Players got a "services are busy" message when signing in. By about 3:15 PM ET Sony's status page showed everything running normally again.

REPORTED: Newsweek, XDA Developers, VICE

Sony hasn't said what caused it, and nobody has confirmed a link to GTA 6. It still landed on the worst possible day, with the game already live in New Zealand and millions of PS5s about to hit PSN at midnight. More launch news on our GTA 6 news tab.

What broke

Newsweek listed the services players had trouble with:

  • Signing in to PlayStation Network (consoles stalled after connecting)
  • Online multiplayer
  • PlayStation Store
  • Party chat
  • Launching games, apps and network features

The error most people saw said PlayStation services were "busy". XDA noted that some players could still log in, so it wasn't a full blackout for everyone.

Was it caused by GTA 6?

OFFICIAL: Sony has not given a cause

Sony hasn't explained the outage, and Newsweek points out that nothing ties it to the game's launch. Newsweek and XDA both contacted Sony; no answer was reported.

THEORY

XDA's Patrick O'Rourke suspects GTA 6's staggered worldwide launch overloaded the service. VICE's Evan Roberson thinks many of the affected players were North Americans using the New Zealand trick to start early, and expects PSN to "likely go down again within the next few days". Both are guesses, not confirmed.

How to be ready for midnight

You can't stop PSN from wobbling, but you can make sure nothing on your side needs a server at 11:59 PM:

  1. Install PS5 system update 26.07-14.23.00 now. PlayStation Support reportedly said you need it before midnight. Full steps in our PS5 update 14.23 guide.
  2. Finish the GTA 6 pre-load and any game update, so there's nothing left to download at unlock.
  3. Sign in to PSN tonight, before the midnight rush, and stay signed in.
  4. Digital copy? On the PS5 you play on, turn on Settings > Users and Accounts > Other > Console Sharing and Offline Play. This standard PS5 setting lets your console run your digital games without checking your licence online.
  5. If it fails at midnight, check status.playstation.com before restarting over and over.
OUR TAKE
The step that matters most is getting the update and pre-load done now. An outage during the unlock hurts the people still downloading 212 GB the most.

When GTA 6 unlocks for you

GTA 6 unlocks at midnight local time on November 19 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, so the load comes in waves, region by region. US East Coast players get in three hours before the West Coast. Every region's time is in our GTA 6 release time guide.

Frequently asked questions

Is PSN down right now?

PSN had a sign-in outage on the afternoon of November 18. Sony's status page showed all services normal again by about 3:15 PM ET. Check status.playstation.com for the live status.

Was the PSN outage caused by GTA 6?

Sony hasn't said. Some outlets think launch traffic from New Zealand and the NZ trick played a part, but that isn't confirmed.

Can I play GTA 6 if PSN is down?

If the game is fully downloaded and, for digital copies, your PS5 has Console Sharing and Offline Play turned on, your console doesn't need to check your licence online. Sign in and finish all downloads before midnight to be safe.

What time does GTA 6 unlock in the US?

Midnight local time on November 19, 2026. East Coast players get in three hours before the West Coast.
