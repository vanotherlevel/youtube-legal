GTA 6 Release Time: When It Unlocks in Every Region (and Xbox Cloud)

By vAnother Level · Updated 2026-11-17

On this page
  1. Console: midnight local time
  2. Xbox Cloud Gaming: 1 AM ET
  3. Before launch night

GTA 6 unlocks at midnight local time on November 19, 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S if you own the game and have it pre-loaded. Xbox Cloud Gaming is different: press reports say streaming starts at 1 AM ET, a single global time, so cloud players in some regions start later than console players.

Console: midnight local time

OFFICIAL: Rockstar's release-time page

The installed version unlocks at midnight in your own time zone, which is why New Zealand gets in first. We worked out the gap in New Zealand gets GTA 6 about 18 hours before the US East Coast.

Xbox Cloud Gaming: 1 AM ET

REPORTED: Digital Citizen, Dexerto, Pure Xbox
WhereCloud start (reported)
US East (ET)1:00 AM, Nov 19
US West (PT)10:00 PM, Nov 18
UK (GMT)6:00 AM, Nov 19
  • Streams on Xbox One and Series X|S with Series S settings.
  • You still have to buy GTA 6: Cloud Gaming access is not ownership.
  • Monthly cloud hours are capped by tier (reported: Ultimate 15 h, Premium 10 h, Essential 5 h); extra playtime can be bought.
  • No PC streaming for GTA 6 at launch.

Before launch night

Make sure the 212 GB pre-load hit 100% (fix if it's stuck on PS5), and check how long the download takes on your connection.

Sources: Rockstar Games (release-time page), Digital Citizen, Dexerto. We'll update this page if Rockstar or Xbox change the times.

Frequently asked questions

What time does GTA 6 unlock?

Midnight local time on November 19, 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.

Can I play GTA 6 on Xbox One?

Only through Xbox Cloud Gaming, reportedly from 1 AM ET on November 19, and you still need to own the game.

Can I stream GTA 6 on PC?

No. PC streaming isn't supported for GTA 6 at launch.
