GTA 6 Reviews Reportedly Drop Nov 18: Embargo Time in Every Region

The first GTA 6 reviews are reported to go live on Wednesday, November 18, 2026, one day before the game launches. Insider Gaming reports the embargo lifts at 6:00 AM Eastern / 3:00 AM Pacific / 11:00 AM GMT, but Rockstar has not announced a review date or time.

REPORTED: not confirmed by Rockstar

Where the November 18 date comes from

  • October 26: insider NateTheHate posted that Rockstar had started sending review codes to media and that the "review embargo is November 18th".
  • November 17: Insider Gaming reported it "understands from metadata and other means" that the embargo lines up with marketing beats set for midnight New Zealand time, and published a full time table.
  • Still unconfirmed: VICE notes Rockstar "has not confirmed" a review embargo date and hasn't said anything public about reviews.

What else happens on November 18

  • Bloomberg Q&A: Bloomberg has a live Q&A with reporter Jason Schreier set for 8 AM PT on November 18. VICE points out the listing doesn't say whether he has played the game or whether Bloomberg will publish a review.
  • Vice City Night: the Miami Heat play as "Vice City" on the eve of launch. Read our Vice City Night page.
  • Launch: GTA 6 unlocks at midnight local time on November 19 (OFFICIAL), starting in New Zealand.
THEORY: what the score will be

Fans have already made up their minds. When Metacritic asked for GTA 6 Metascore predictions, the replies were mostly "100" (our round-up). GTA 5 scored 97 on PS3. We'll update this page once reviews are actually live.
