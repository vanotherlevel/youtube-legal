GTA 6 reviews are out, and they're about as good as reviews get. When the embargo lifted at 6 AM ET on November 18, Grand Theft Auto VI landed a 99 Metascore on PlayStation 5 from 109 critic reviews, and every one of them counts as positive. That ties The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time for the highest Metascore on record.

What reviewers loved

  • Leonida itself. VGC calls the open world one of the most immersive ever built, packed with content. Wccftech calls it the most detailed crime simulation in the series.
  • Jason and Lucia. Both VGC and Wccftech single out the two leads. VGC says the relationship system really shapes the story, which feels personal rather than political.
  • Looks and sound. Industry-leading visuals, a strong soundtrack and great voice acting come up again and again.
  • The satire is still sharp. The classic GTA humour is back, aimed at modern Florida-style life.

The catch: 30 FPS and a few dips

OFFICIAL: per the VGC and Wccftech reviews

Nobody scored it low, but the reviews agree on the weak spots:

  • 30 FPS on every console. Wccftech says there's no performance mode. On PS5 Pro it holds almost all the time, with PSSR upscaling plus ray-traced lighting and shadows.
  • Dips in the busiest places. VGC saw slowdowns in the densest areas, but rarely, and says they didn't spoil anything.
  • Melee and races. Wccftech finds hand-to-hand combat shallow next to Red Dead Redemption 2, and AI rubberbanding is still there in races.

We broke down what 30 FPS really looks like in GTA 6 at 15 vs 30 vs 60 FPS and GTA 6 30 FPS on PS5 Pro, Xbox and cloud.

How long is GTA 6, according to reviews?

REPORTED: reviewers' own playtimes, which vary with side content

VGC puts the main story at about 60 hours without side content. Wccftech needed about 90 hours to reach the ending. Both fit the 50-hour-plus story reports we covered in GTA 6 missions, chapters and story length.

Our take

OUR TAKE

A 99 from 109 reviews is a huge result, and this early count could still dip by a point as late reviews arrive. Either way, the hype has gone straight into the reviews. The only real complaint is the 30 FPS cap. With BofA's sales forecasts already near $2 billion for launch weekend (our breakdown), these scores only add fuel. Frequently asked questions

What is GTA 6's Metacritic score?

99 on PS5 from 109 critic reviews (all positive) as of 6:15 AM ET on November 18, 2026. The Xbox Series X|S page also shows 99 from 9 reviews. The score can still change slightly as more reviews come in.

Is GTA 6 the highest-rated game ever?

It's tied. A 99 Metascore matches The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time (N64), the highest on Metacritic's record, as GamesRadar+ reported.

Did GTA 6 beat GTA 5 and Red Dead Redemption 2?

Yes, so far. GTA 5 and RDR2 both peaked at 97 on Metacritic, and GTA 4 at 98.

What do reviews criticise in GTA 6?

Mainly the 30 FPS cap on every console with no performance mode, rare frame-rate dips in busy areas, shallow melee and rubberbanding in races.
