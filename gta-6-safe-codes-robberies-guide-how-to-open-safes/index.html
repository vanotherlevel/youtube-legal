There are three ways to open a safe in GTA 6: find the code on the premises (desks, back offices, a guard or employee), get it from someone (a fence can text it to you, or you can pressure staff for it), or blow the safe open, which works but damages part of the loot inside. Story-mission safes give you their code. Codes for free-roam robberies are reported to be randomized, so a fixed code list won't work for every store.

Below is everything reported so far about safes, robberies and getaways in Leonida, from Rockstar North's own preview comments (Rob Nelson), launch-week guides (GameSpot, PowerPyx, PlayStationInfo) and preview sessions. Every claim is labelled. Rockstar has not published an official robbery guide. More guides live on our GTA 6 guides hub.

Jewelry job safe codes (Yasmani, Chapter 3)

REPORTED: PlayStationInfo, citing PowerPyx (Nov 18)

These are the codes reported for the three stores in Yasmani's jewelry jobs in Chapter 3. They apply to these story safes only. Free-roam robbery safes use different codes.

StoreCodeTarget itemWhere the safe is
Casa de Empeño1680Buffalo Skull NecklaceSmall office in the back
Richardson Jewelry6815Diamond RingNortheast corner of the middle room
Donovan Banks & Co.0104Amethyst NecklaceElongated room at the rear
  • Do Casa de Empeño first. Delivering that haul to Yasmani is reported to unlock the Snap Gun, which opens locked back doors without setting off the alarm.
  • Richardson Jewelry: a control panel can cut the fuse box. Use the back entrance.
  • Donovan Banks & Co.: the back office has the security controls, and you can wipe the camera footage there. Enter all four digits, including the leading zero.
If a code doesn't work in your game, search the office for the written code instead. Launch-week code lists can differ between sources.

The practice safe (Safe Word mission)

REPORTED: PowerPyx walkthrough (Nov 18)

In Safe Word, a Chapter 3 mission from Nicole at Raul's compound in Stockyard, Jason and Lucia winch a safe out of a Peacock Estates mansion and drag it away behind a truck. Back at the compound you practise on it, and the moves match the bank job at the end of the chapter:

  1. Drill: hold the drill steady on the right gauge while the left gauge climbs. Don't let the drill light turn red.
  2. Cable: steer the camera cable with the left stick and look around with the right stick. Reach the gold ring without touching the cable or the walls.
  3. Brace the door: hold the aim and brace buttons and mash the prompt while Nicole drills and enters the combination.

Cracking the practice safe is reported to give $1,500 and a variant pistol, Gil's Kalinga .50. You can repeat the practice as often as you like.

What you can rob in GTA 6

REPORTED: Rob Nelson (Rockstar North) via Famitsu and TGG
  • Stores, including ones with no robbery icon on the map.
  • Payday loan stores and cash vans out in the world.
  • Jewelry stores and small banks, alone or with your partner.
  • Gang hideouts. The map can show how much cash and drugs are inside.
  • Some targets come from map opportunities or tips, some you find by chance, and big jobs can show the potential take before you commit.

Rockstar has not said how many robbery targets there are, how payouts compare, or how often a store can be hit again.

Stealth vs loud

Stealth

  • Use the Snap Gun on the back door. Kicking it in sets off the alarm.
  • Shoot out cameras and disable the security system so the alarm stays silent.
  • Scout for side and back entrances first.
  • Nelson said a clean job means "nobody will know what happened there."

Loud

  • It's faster, but a crime gets reported as soon as someone sees it or an alarm goes off. A passer-by who spots your gun can call it in.
  • Staff and customers can rush you, grab for your gun, or pull their own (TGG preview).
  • Wiping the CCTV stops them identifying you, but one store in a preview kept its footage in the cloud (Kind of Funny Games).

Loot, fences and the getaway

REPORTED: preview sessions and Rockstar North comments
  • Loot goes into duffel bags. There's a limit on how many you can carry and how much each holds. A bike is faster; a van or truck carries more bags.
  • Stolen goods show as a separate value under your cash until you sell them. Drugs, jewelry and cars go to different fences.
  • Police response at jewelry stores is "severe," Nelson said: grab what you came for and leave.
  • To shake the description, take off the mask, switch cars and change clothes. Our wanted level guide covers what cops remember.
  • With both characters on the job, one drives while the other shoots. You don't have to kill anyone: shooting a cop car's tyres can flip it. Violence still feeds your Criminal Profile.
THEORY: our take

Since codes for free-roam safes are reported to be random, the fastest repeatable method is likely to pressure an employee for the code rather than search the building. It's quick, and it keeps the loot intact. We'll update this page once more players test it.

Frequently asked questions

Are GTA 6 safe codes random?

Reportedly yes for free-roam robberies (GameSpot). Story-mission safes, like Yasmani's jewelry jobs, have fixed codes or give you the code in the mission.

How do you find a safe code in GTA 6?

Search desks and back offices (Focus Mode helps), loot a guard, pressure an employee with the "Safe Code?" prompt, or get it texted by a fence.

Can you blow open safes in GTA 6?

Yes, but blowing a safe damages part of the loot inside and draws more attention, so it's best as Plan B.

What are the jewelry store safe codes in GTA 6?

Reported codes for Yasmani's Chapter 3 jobs: Casa de Empeño 1680, Richardson Jewelry 6815, Donovan Banks & Co. 0104 (PlayStationInfo, citing PowerPyx).
