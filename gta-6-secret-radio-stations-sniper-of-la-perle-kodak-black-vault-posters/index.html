GTA 6 Secret Radio Stations: All 5 Posters (Kodak Black & The Vault)

GTA 6 has two secret radio stations, called Unique Playlists, and you unlock both by scanning 5 posters with the WAiNK phone app: 4 posters for Kodak Black's The Sniper of La Perle and 1 poster for The Vault Radio. All five are in Vice City, inside shops and clubs in La Perle, Crosstown and Downtown, and both playlists count toward 100% completion.

Players found Kodak Black's hidden show on launch day, and guide writers at PowerPyx, CULTR and Vice have since mapped every poster. Below: each exact poster spot, how to scan, how to listen once they're unlocked, what's on the tracklists, and the one catch that trips people up (the game doesn't tell you which posters you've already scanned). Sources are credited at the bottom.

How to scan a poster with WAiNK

The posters are WAiNK scans, the same phone camera app you use for the 108 WAiNK collectibles. According to CULTR, you hold Up on the D-pad to bring up the phone, aim the camera at the poster, and press R1 on PS5 or RB on Xbox to scan it. If the scan works, the poster is logged. Our video above walks through how WAiNK scanning works across the whole map.

  1. Walk into the shop or club and find the poster (spots in the table above).
  2. Hold Up on the D-pad to open the phone and switch to WAiNK.
  3. Aim at the poster until it's centred and press R1 (PS5) / RB (Xbox).
  4. Repeat for all four Sniper posters, then the single Vault poster.
The catch: the game does NOT show which of the four Sniper of La Perle posters you've already scanned (PowerPyx and CULTR both note this). If the playlist doesn't unlock, you missed one, and the only fix is to revisit all four. Do them in one trip, in the order above, so you know exactly which ones are done.

The Sniper of La Perle (Kodak Black): all 4 poster locations

The Sniper of La Perle is an On Demand playlist hosted by Florida rapper Kodak Black. CULTR says it is presented by Haitian DJ and producer Michael Brun, and describes it as a celebration of Haitian konpa, Miami rap and Caribbean culture, which fits La Perle, the game's take on Miami's Little Haiti. Three posters are in La Perle and the last one is a short drive away in Crosstown.

Poster 1: Aunt Zola's Soul Food (La Perle)

Go inside the restaurant. The poster is beside the cashier at the checkout area. All three sources agree on this one.

Poster 2: TWA Ponya Tattoo (La Perle)

Inside the tattoo studio, the poster sits on the table directly beside the entrance. You can scan it almost as soon as you walk in (Vice describes it simply as near the entrance).

Poster 3: Drip Drops (La Perle)

Drip Drops is a clothing store. The poster is on the sales counter next to the clerk.

Poster 4: Jack of Hearts (Crosstown)

This is the one people miss. Enter the Jack of Hearts club in Crosstown and go up to the second floor, into the upstairs studio area. The poster is in the eastern recording booth, on a crate (PowerPyx and CULTR). Vice only says it's in the upstairs studio, so if you're looking around downstairs, you're on the wrong floor.

How to listen to The Sniper of La Perle

Once all four posters are scanned, open On Demand > The Sniper of La Perle. It plays through any car radio, or on foot through your phone with headphones/earbuds.

The Sniper of La Perle tracklist (22 songs)

CULTR and Vice both list 22 tracks. Highlights include three Kodak Black songs ("300 Blackout", "Haitian Scarface" and "Tete Kale Bobis" with Michael Brun), "Haiti" by John Wicks featuring Kodak Black, Michael Brun's "Jessica" with J Balvin and SAINt JHN, Wyclef Jean's "Guantanamera" with Ms. Lauryn Hill and Celia Cruz, plus konpa from Carimi, Djakout, T-Vice, Zenglen and Barikad Crew. CULTR notes Zenglen's "Child Support" is the song heard in Trailer 2. The full list is on Vice.

The Vault Radio: the single poster

The second Unique Playlist is much quicker. Go to The Vault nightclub in Downtown Vice City. CULTR places it just south of the Jack of Hearts and west of the NINE1NINE club, near a big four-way overpass, so you can do it right after poster 4. Just inside the front entrance, scan the red poster with a gas mask and "The Vault" printed on it. That one scan unlocks the station.

Listen under Radio > The Vault (it sits with the regular stations, not On Demand), in a car or with earbuds on foot. CULTR says it's hosted by DJ Pretzel and plays 24 underground house, techno and rave tracks from inside the club, including Dom Dolla & Solomun "Damn", Barry Can't Swim "Return To Bhibo", DJ Koze "XTC" and a Solomun remix of Lana Del Rey's "West Coast". CULTR counts it as the 23rd full station on top of the 22 on the main dial.

REPORTED: host and tracklist from CULTR only; the other guides don't name The Vault's host

Do the secret radio stations count for 100%?

Yes. Both playlists are the 2/2 Unique Playlists line in the 100% list. PowerPyx says your progress shows under Progress Menu > Collections > Exploration > Unique Playlists. See every other requirement on our GTA 6 100% completion checklist. None of our sources name a trophy tied only to the playlists, so we don't list one; check our GTA 6 trophy list for everything that is confirmed.

Best route: all 5 in one trip

  1. Start in La Perle: Aunt Zola's Soul Food (cashier), then TWA Ponya Tattoo (table by the door), then Drip Drops (sales counter).
  2. Drive to the Jack of Hearts in Crosstown and go straight upstairs to the eastern recording booth (crate).
  3. Check On Demand: if The Sniper of La Perle is there, you're done with that set. If not, go back through the La Perle three.
  4. Head a little south to The Vault in Downtown and scan the red gas-mask poster at the entrance.
  5. Check Radio > The Vault and the Unique Playlists counter (2/2).
THEORY: no source gives a story chapter or mission you must reach first; if a club is locked when you visit, come back later in the story

Frequently asked questions

How do you unlock Kodak Black's radio station in GTA 6?

Scan 4 posters with the WAiNK app: Aunt Zola's Soul Food, TWA Ponya Tattoo and Drip Drops in La Perle, and the upstairs eastern recording booth at the Jack of Hearts in Crosstown. Then play it under On Demand > The Sniper of La Perle.

How many secret radio stations are in GTA 6?

Two Unique Playlists: The Sniper of La Perle (4 posters) and The Vault Radio (1 poster), 5 posters in total. Both count toward 100% completion.

Where is The Vault poster in GTA 6?

Just inside the front entrance of The Vault nightclub in Downtown Vice City: a red poster with a gas mask and 'The Vault' on it. Scan it with WAiNK.

Why didn't The Sniper of La Perle unlock?

You missed one of the four posters. The game doesn't show which ones you've scanned, so revisit all four, especially the Jack of Hearts one, which is upstairs on a crate in the eastern recording booth.
