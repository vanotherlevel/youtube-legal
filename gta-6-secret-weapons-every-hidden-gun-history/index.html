GTA 6 hides its special weapons in three places: store-exclusive variants that only one gun shop sells, gold "unique" weapons you find around Leonida, and guns you unlock by finishing questlines or challenges. Big Shots in Hamlet, for example, sells The Silver 1922 ($2,210) and the Sand Storm ES9 ($1,470), and the menu marks each one as a unique variant you can't buy anywhere else.

That's the newest version of a trick Rockstar has used for 25 years. It has hidden guns in a Scarface bathroom, on top of bridges, in glove boxes, next to nuclear warheads and at the end of a serial killer's trail. Below is what the first launch streams show in GTA 6, then every secret weapon in the series, game by game, so you know what to look for.

Labels on this page: ON SCREEN = read from real GTA 6 launch-stream menus. OFFICIAL = Rockstar's own words, art or listings. REPORTED = guide sites and press (PowerPyx, VGC, Famitsu). THEORY = our own prediction. GTA series history comes from the games themselves, checked against the GTA Wiki. The full gun list with prices is in our GTA 6 every weapon guide.

GTA 6's secret and unique weapons so far

ON SCREEN: MELOO and Dalek launch streams, Big Shots
  • Store exclusives: every gun store has at least one unique variant you can't buy anywhere else (REPORTED, PowerPyx). At Big Shots (Hamlet, one of the 6 Expanded stores) it's The Silver 1922 heavy pistol for $2,210 and the Sand Storm ES9 for $1,470. Both need a bank account, not pocket cash.
  • Gold icon = unique weapon. In your weapon wheel, blue marks a gun you own, white marks one you picked up from an enemy, and gold marks a unique weapon.
  • Ultimate Edition guns: the Duval Signature ES9 (Jason, in any Ammu-Nation locker from Chapter 1) and the Caminos Signature K17 (Lucia, from Chapter 2). They look special but keep the base gun's stats and can't be upgraded.
REPORTED: PowerPyx
  • Questline guns: as Jason, Bret's "Veteran Hunted" quests in Chapter 2 give you the Killtelligence SM-69 sniper for free, and his 9th quest unlocks the Shrewsbury SMS-01 to buy.
  • Challenge unlocks: destroy 100 vehicles for the H-AT rocket launcher, land 100 explosive hits for the explosive Shortstraw, and win 25 medals at the shooting ranges for Max Ammo, Infinite Ammo and No Reload. See our cheats list.
  • Gold uniques in the world: one-off finds around Leonida. As of November 18, nobody has published a full list of where they are. We'll add locations here as they're verified.
OFFICIAL: Rob Nelson (Rockstar North) to Famitsu

Before launch, Rockstar North's Rob Nelson told Famitsu that Ammu-Nation sells special guns exclusive to that store, and that players who "take a bit of time and explore" will be rewarded. The launch streams now back up the first part. The second part is the hunt that starts on November 19.

GTA 3, Vice City & San Andreas: hidden packages

In GTA 3 the rule was simple: find hidden packages and Rockstar pays you in weapons. Every 10 packages put a new gun at every safehouse, for good, and the 100th paid out a rocket launcher plus $1,000,000.

Vice City turned it into a treasure trail. 20 packages gave you the chainsaw, 40 the flamethrower and 60 the minigun. The best part was where they were hidden in the world: the chainsaw in a blood-stained Ocean Beach bathroom (straight from the Scarface chainsaw scene), the flamethrower by a pool shaped like the Rockstar logo, and the minigun on the top floor of a ruined drug factory.

San Andreas went bigger. There's a minigun in an Area 69 control room next to what look like nuclear warheads, another on top of the Kincaid Bridge arch (jetpack, parachute or helicopter only), a katana, and a purple dildo melee weapon in the Los Santos police HQ showers.

GTA IV, The Lost and Damned & The Ballad of Gay Tony

  • Little Jacob's trunk: become friends and he sells you discount guns out of his car, the series' first gun car.
  • Terry's van (Lost and Damned) did the same for Johnny, and finally made the grenade launcher playable. Its files were already on the 2002 Vice City disc.
  • Gay Tony added explosive shotgun shells, sticky bombs and the Gold SMG, hidden in the glove box of Yusuf's car only the first time you get in after one specific mission.

GTA V & GTA Online: rooftops, the Railgun and treasure hunts

  • Story mode rooftops: a minigun on a Fort Zancudo control tower (four stars the moment you walk in), a rocket launcher on the NOOSE building and a grenade launcher on the La Mesa bridge arch.
  • The Railgun: first a returning-player bonus on the newer consoles, later sold at Ammu-Nation for $250,000, and there's a free one inside a sunken cargo plane north of Stab City.
  • Double-Action Revolver: a treasure hunt from a strange photo to a hidden chest. 50 headshots with it unlocked a gold version in Red Dead Redemption 2, months before RDR2 came out.
  • Navy Revolver: five clues on a serial killer's trail across Blaine County, and at the end the killer hunts you.
  • Stone Hatchet, Up-n-Atomizer, Widowmaker, Unholy Hellbringer and the gold Perico Pistol (stolen from El Rubio's office drawer during the heist). Today's rarest guns sit in the moving Gun Van.

Red Dead Redemption 2: guns with names

RDR2 is the template GTA 6 seems to follow. Rare guns there have an owner and a story: Otis Miller's revolver at the end of a treasure map, the legendary gunslingers' guns (Flaco, Granger, Midnight, Calloway) you lose if you don't loot them, a hermit's rare shotgun, and Micah's revolver on a frozen corpse. Named, story-driven and missable, which is exactly what GTA 6's named signature and store-exclusive variants look like.

Which old secret weapons could return in GTA 6?

THEORY: our predictions, not facts
  1. Store-exclusive hunt: now confirmed on screen. The game is in finding which of the 24 stores sells which unique.
  2. Named legend guns, RDR2-style: the gold unique weapons are the strongest sign. Very likely.
  3. A treasure-map weapon: Rockstar has done it three times (Otis Miller, Double-Action, Navy Revolver). Likely.
  4. A minigun or launcher on a military base or rooftop: in every era of the series. Possible, but GTA 6 already sells launchers at Expanded stores.
  5. The Vice City chainsaw: a fan favourite for a game set in Vice City. Not seen yet.
  6. Railgun and ray guns: they don't fit GTA 6's grounded tone at launch. Maybe later in GTA 6 Online.

Frequently asked questions

Does GTA 6 have secret weapons?

Yes. Every gun store has at least one exclusive variant (Big Shots sells The Silver 1922 and the Sand Storm ES9), gold unique weapons are hidden around Leonida, and some guns unlock from questlines and challenges.

What does a gold weapon icon mean in GTA 6?

Gold marks a unique weapon. Blue is a gun you own, white is one you picked up from an enemy, which can't be modified and is lost on quit unless you store it.

How do you get a free sniper in GTA 6?

As Jason, start Bret's 'Veteran Hunted' quests in Chapter 2. Finishing the first gives you the Killtelligence SM-69, per PowerPyx.

Is the Railgun in GTA 6?

It hasn't been seen. The Railgun was a GTA V and GTA Online weapon, and we think it's unlikely at launch given GTA 6's grounded tone.
