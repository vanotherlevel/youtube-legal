GTA 6's First Plagiarism Claim: Was Chase's Custom SMG Decal Traced?

GTA 6 is facing its first plagiarism claim, two days after launch: fans say the character decal on a custom SMG was traced from a 2021 illustration by artist GabyT. It's an allegation, not a ruling. Rockstar hasn't commented, the artist hasn't publicly responded in the reports we checked, and other players say the two images barely line up.

REPORTED (Dexerto, GameRant, Notebookcheck, Nov 21): a fan allegation, not confirmed by Rockstar, Take-Two or the artist

Where the SMG comes from

The gun is a reward from an optional encounter with Chase, a character in a fox costume who is cornered by police. You break through the blockade, lose the cops, and get Chase and a friend back to their compound. GameRant reports the reward is a choice: $15,000 cash or the custom SMG. The decal in question is the fox character art on that SMG. Outlets haven't named the base gun model.

What the comparison shows, and what it doesn't

  • Same pose details: Dexerto notes both images show a head turned to the side, a half-lidded eye and white fur around a dark muzzle.
  • Differences: the in-game version has muddier colours and the character holds a bone in its mouth.
  • GameRant's check: it says much of the facial structure lines up when the images are overlaid, with the art appearing flipped horizontally.
  • The other side: some players say the outlines barely match and call it only minor similarity. Fox character art shares a lot of common shapes.
THEORY: whether this is tracing, reference, coincidence or licensed art is unknown. Nothing public shows whether Rockstar contacted GabyT.

Is it actually illegal?

Not automatically. GameRant points out that tracing alone doesn't prove copyright infringement. Under US law the question is whether protected parts of the art were copied without permission, and whether fair use applies. Because it was a commission, the artist usually keeps the copyright unless a written agreement transferred it, and those terms aren't public. Rockstar may also have licensed it. Until Rockstar or the artist says something, treat it as an open question.

It's not the only real-world look-alike in the Chase encounter: Dexerto notes fans compared Chase's costume to a real fursuit associated with FoxiFelix, which GameRant reads as an intentional parody. Frequently asked questions

Did Rockstar plagiarise art in GTA 6?

That's not proven. Fans allege the fox decal on a custom SMG was traced from a 2021 illustration by artist GabyT. Rockstar hasn't responded, and some players say the images barely match.

How do you get the custom SMG in GTA 6?

It's a reward from the optional Chase encounter: help the fox-costumed Chase escape a police blockade and reach the compound, then pick the custom SMG instead of $15,000 cash (per GameRant).

Who is the artist in the GTA 6 plagiarism claim?

GabyT (@Gabitigress18 on X), who posted the commissioned illustration on December 8, 2021, according to Dexerto and GameRant.
