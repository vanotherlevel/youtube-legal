GTA 6 Extended Look Songs: Which Tracks Blew Up on Spotify

The biggest reported jump from the GTA VI Extended Look was Captain & Tennille's "But I Think It's a Dream", up 130,730% in global Spotify streams. "Inner Light" by Bob Moses and Elderbrook, up 1,070%, kept going: the Associated Press reported in November that it had gained more than 50 million additional streams since the late-August trailer.

REPORTED: Spotify data given to EDM.com and NME, as summarised by RockstarINTEL (Sept 4); Associated Press via CTV News (Nov 13)

The reported Spotify jumps

These figures measure the days after the Extended Look in late August, not the game's November 19 launch.

A percentage rise says how far a song climbed from where it started, not how many total plays it has. A little-played track can post a far bigger percentage than a well-known one.

Playlists and younger listeners

  • Playlist adds: up 885,400% for "But I Think It's a Dream", 4,155% for "Inner Light" and 700% for "People Are People".
  • Gen Z: Spotify told NME that Gen Z discovery of Prefab Sprout's "Cars and Girls" rose 530%, and of Cliff Richard's "Devil Woman" 325%.
  • Shazam: "Devil Woman", "Inner Light" and "Se Me Nota" entered the US top 10.

How to find the songs

RockstarINTEL reports that Spotify put all 15 tracks from the Extended Look into a playlist called Grand Theft Auto Radio. Those licensed songs are separate from GTA VI: The Album, which is original music made for the game. For the in-game side, see our phone apps and radio stations guide.

What we don't know

  • We found no official Rockstar breakdown of these streaming numbers.
  • The September figures and the later 50 million count cover different periods, so they can't be added or compared directly.
OUR TAKE

A trailer placement can revive an older song within days. "Inner Light", a 2021 release, shows the effect can also last for months.

Sources

Sources: RockstarINTEL: Extended Look songs' streaming growth (Sept 4) · Associated Press via CTV News: Bob Moses' viral hit (Nov 13)

Frequently asked questions

What is the song in the GTA 6 Extended Look that blew up the most?

By reported percentage, Captain & Tennille's "But I Think It's a Dream", up 130,730% in global Spotify streams.

Who sings Inner Light in the GTA 6 trailer?

Canadian duo Bob Moses with British musician Elderbrook. The song dates from 2021.

Is there an official playlist of the Extended Look songs?

RockstarINTEL reports that Spotify made a 15-track playlist called Grand Theft Auto Radio.
