GTA 6's story follows Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos, a criminal couple in the state of Leonida whose "easy score" goes wrong and drops them into a criminal conspiracy that stretches across the state. That is everything Rockstar has officially said about the plot. The rest of this page is what Rockstar's character profiles and trailers add, with every claim labelled so you know what is official and what is fan theory.

GTA 6 launches on November 19, 2026 (see the unlock time in every region). Once it's out, this page will be updated with what the opening hours actually show, without major spoilers up top. For everything else, see our GTA 6 hub.

The GTA 6 story in one paragraph

OFFICIAL: Rockstar's GTA VI site and Trailer 2 (May 6, 2025)

Rockstar's own tagline opens with "Jason and Lucia have always known the deck is stacked against them." The official summary, as reported by GamingBolt and VGC on Trailer 2 day, says an easy score goes wrong and the pair end up in the middle of a criminal conspiracy spanning Leonida, Rockstar's take on Florida. Locations named so far include Vice City and the Leonida Keys.

So the setup is a classic Rockstar crime story: two people who want out, one job that should have been simple, and a state full of people who want a piece of them.

Who is Jason Duval?

OFFICIAL: Rockstar character profile

Jason is one of GTA 6's two playable protagonists. According to Rockstar's profile, he grew up around grifters and crooks, joined the Army, and then ended up in the Keys working for local drug runners. Rockstar sums him up as a man who "wants an easy life, but things just keep getting harder."

  • Background: Army veteran. The Launch Trailer opens with him saying "I got in some trouble in the Army…" (OFFICIAL, Rockstar captions).
  • Where he lives: Brian Heder lets him stay rent-free in exchange for help (OFFICIAL, Brian's profile).
  • His friend: Cal Hampton, the paranoid one (OFFICIAL).
  • Lucia: his page says meeting her "could be the best or worst thing to ever happen to him" (OFFICIAL).

Who is Lucia Caminos?

OFFICIAL: Rockstar character profile

Lucia is the other playable lead and the first playable woman in the modern GTA series. Rockstar writes that her father taught her to fight as soon as she could walk, and that fighting for her family landed her in the Leonida Penitentiary. Sheer luck got her out, and her profile says she has "learned her lesson — only smart moves from here."

  • Family: she wants the good life her mom has dreamed of "since their days in Liberty City" (OFFICIAL). Lucia's family came from Liberty City, GTA's New York.
  • Prison: Trailer 1 opens on her in an orange jumpsuit. Asked why she's there, she answers "Bad luck, I guess" (OFFICIAL, Rockstar captions, 0:09).
  • Release: in Trailer 2, Jason arrives at the prison: "I'm here for Lucia Caminos. She's supposed to be getting out" (OFFICIAL, 0:50).
  • Jason: her page says "A life with Jason could be her way out" (OFFICIAL).

THEORY

Fans have compared the pair to Bonnie and Clyde since Trailer 1, which ends on the two of them robbing a store together. Rockstar has never used that name. Whether the story ends well for both of them is pure guesswork until launch, and the "best or worst thing" line on Jason's page is the only official hint either way.

Cal Hampton

Cal is Jason's friend, happiest at home with a beer and a radio tuned to Coast Guard chatter. His profile line: "Casual paranoia loves company, but his friend Jason has bigger plans." That reads like Cal is the safe life Jason is about to walk away from.

Brian Heder

Brian is the old-school Keys smuggler. Jason lives on his property rent-free in return for help with the business, and Rockstar says "Brian's been around long enough to let others do his dirty work." He is the most likely source of Jason's early jobs.

Boobie Ike

Boobie is a Vice City legend who turned street money into real estate, a strip club and a recording studio. "Boobie's all smiles until it's time to talk business." Our gangs guide covers his empire in more detail.

Dre'Quan Priest

Dre'Quan went from dealing on the street to running a label: breaking into music was always the goal. He is tied to Only Raw Records, the same label as Real Dimez.

Real Dimez (Bae-Luxe and Roxy)

Bae-Luxe and Roxy are childhood friends who hustled local dealers before going viral as a rap duo. Now signed to Only Raw, they are "hoping lightning can strike twice." Expect them to be the social media side of the story.

Raul Bautista

Raul is the professional: a seasoned bank robber "always on the hunt for talent ready to take the risks that bring the biggest rewards." If GTA 6 has big heist missions, he is the obvious crew boss.

THEORY

Raul recruiting Jason and Lucia for the "easy score" that goes wrong is the most popular fan theory, but Rockstar has not linked him to the pair. The same goes for any returning characters: see every Tommy Vercetti clue for what's real and what's wishful.

Frequently asked questions

What is the GTA 6 story about?

Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos pull what should be an easy score in Leonida, it goes wrong, and they end up in a criminal conspiracy that spans the state. That is all Rockstar has officially said about the plot.

Who are the main characters in GTA 6?

The two playable protagonists are Jason Duval, an Army veteran working for drug runners in the Keys, and Lucia Caminos, who has just got out of the Leonida Penitentiary.

Who are the supporting characters in GTA 6?

Rockstar has profiled Cal Hampton, Brian Heder, Boobie Ike, Dre'Quan Priest, Raul Bautista and the rap duo Real Dimez (Bae-Luxe and Roxy).

Why was Lucia in prison in GTA 6?

Rockstar says fighting for her family landed her in the Leonida Penitentiary, and sheer luck got her out. The exact crime hasn't been revealed.
