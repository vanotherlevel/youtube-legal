GTA 6 Was Watched for 125 Million Hours in Its First 24 Hours

People watched 125.47 million hours of GTA 6 livestreams in the game's first 24 hours. That's according to analytics firm Streams Charts, which also tracked a peak of about 7.91 million people watching at the same time across Twitch, YouTube, Kick and other platforms. IShowSpeed was the most-watched streamer, with 4.47 million hours.

REPORTED (Streams Charts day-one recap, Nov 19; CNBC, Nov 20): third-party tracking figures, not numbers from Rockstar or the platforms

The biggest single-moment crowd belonged to Spanish streamer IlloJuan, who peaked at 562K viewers. Indonesian YouTuber Windah Basudara hit 392.5K, and Brazil's PAULINHO O LOKO reached 279.3K, also on YouTube. The list shows how global the launch was: Spain, Brazil and Indonesia sit right next to the US names.

IShowSpeed's run made headlines beyond the numbers: clips of his reaction to the ending spread across X. Our GTA 6 endings explained page covers all three endings (spoilers).

REPORTED (CNBC): Twitch tracker CastAccord logged about 2 million hours watched over roughly 36 hours of Kai Cenat's launch stream

Sales: 19.1M copies early, $3.79B official

CNBC reports that Sensor Tower estimated about 19.1 million copies sold, pre-orders included, by Thursday morning (Nov 19), worth roughly $1.84 billion. That estimate is already out of date. Take-Two's own press release on November 20 put day-one retail sales at $3.79 billion, with 32.5 million units sold before release.

OFFICIAL (Take-Two/Rockstar press release, Nov 20): $3.79B day-one retail sales, 32.5M units pre-release

Our GTA 6 sales page tracks every figure, from pre-orders to the official launch numbers.

The GTA 6 clip economy

CNBC's piece also looks at the money around the streams. Clip agencies paid editors to cut and post highlights. One campaign around KreekCraft paid $1 per 1,000 views, up to $400 per clip. Agency Clipped & Shipped put up a $10,000 prize pool for editors pushing Kai Cenat clips across YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Instagram and X.

  • Small channels went viral too: a GTA 6 Short from a channel with about 1,220 subscribers drew 1.5 million views, according to CNBC.
  • Using AIR Media-Tech's US Shorts benchmark, CNBC estimates 1.5 million Shorts views earn only about $490. Big views don't always mean big money.
  • An X user claimed an AI-assisted clipping operation earned $21,000 overnight. CNBC says it could not verify that.
THEORY: CNBC's earnings figures are benchmark estimates. The creators' real payouts weren't verified

What analysts say comes next

Wedbush analyst Alicia Reese told CNBC that in-game purchases could bring Take-Two "years of high-margin recurring revenue". She expects a PC version and then an online mode to follow the console launch, as GTA 5 did. Investor Ross Gerber called GTA 6 "more than just a game" and said the series could grow into film and TV.

THEORY (analyst view): Rockstar has not announced GTA 6 for PC or a GTA 6 Online date

Frequently asked questions

How many hours of GTA 6 were watched on day one?

125.47 million hours across Twitch, YouTube, Kick and other tracked platforms in the first 24 hours, according to Streams Charts.

What was GTA 6's peak viewership on Twitch, YouTube and Kick?

Streams Charts tracked a peak of about 7.91 million concurrent viewers across all platforms on launch day.

Who was the most-watched GTA 6 streamer?

IShowSpeed, with 4.47 million hours watched in the first 24 hours across YouTube and Twitch. IlloJuan had the highest single peak, at 562K viewers.

How much has GTA 6 sold?

Take-Two officially reported $3.79 billion in day-one retail sales and 32.5 million units sold before release. An earlier Sensor Tower estimate, reported by CNBC, was 19.1 million copies by the morning of Nov 19.
