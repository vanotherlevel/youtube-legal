Why Take-Two Stock Slipped After GTA 6's $3.79B Launch

GTA 6 made an estimated $3.79 billion in worldwide retail sales on November 19, but Take-Two shares still slipped, by almost 4% on launch day. One analyst's explanation, reported by Fortune on November 25: investors care more about when the next GTA Online arrives, and how big it is, than about how many copies GTA 6 sells.

What's confirmed

OFFICIAL: Take-Two / Rockstar company figures
  • $3.79 billion in worldwide retail sales on release day. This is the company's own estimate.
  • Take-Two kept its fiscal 2027 net bookings outlook at $8 billion to $8.2 billion, with $3.35 billion to $3.5 billion expected for the October to December quarter, as listed by Fortune.
  • For scale, Take-Two's fiscal 2026 net revenue was $6.66 billion (its SEC filing, via Fortune).

Retail sales and Take-Two's own revenue aren't the same number. Retail sales are what buyers paid; stores and platform holders keep part of it, and a company books revenue on its own accounting schedule. So $3.79 billion in one day doesn't mean $3.79 billion lands in this quarter's results.

Why the shares fell

REPORTED: Fortune, November 25

Fortune reports that Take-Two shares fell almost 4% on November 19. TD Cowen analyst Doug Creutz wrote that the timing and size of the next GTA Online matter more to the stock than GTA 6 unit sales. The report doesn't give a date Creutz expects, or a price target.

The logic is easy to follow even without a forecast. A game sale happens once. An online mode can earn for years, as GTA Online did after 2013. A huge launch was widely expected, with pre-orders reported long before release, so the open question for investors is what comes next. That last part is our reading, not Creutz's words, and a one-day share move can have more than one cause.

What about copies sold?

ESTIMATE: Alinea Analytics, not confirmed by Take-Two

Research firm Alinea Analytics estimates 44.9 million copies sold by the end of opening weekend, grossing more than $4 billion. Treat it as an outside estimate: Take-Two hasn't confirmed a unit figure for the weekend.

When is GTA 6 Online coming?

Rockstar hasn't announced a release date. Everything with a date on it is a report or an estimate. We keep the confirmed and reported details apart on our GTA 6 Online release date page.

The launch in jokes

The launch spilled well outside the games world. On November 20, Mark Zuckerberg posted an edited GTA 6 screenshot on X, a prompt about picking up groceries for "Priscilla", asking which version he got. Elon Musk replied with a laughing emoji.
