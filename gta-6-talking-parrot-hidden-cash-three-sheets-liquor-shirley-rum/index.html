GTA 6 Talking Parrot Cash: Three Sheets Liquor Stash (~$2,000)

By vAnother Level · Updated 2026-11-19

The GTA 6 talking parrot is a red macaw in the exotic-pet lot behind Three Sheets Liquor in Key Lento, and if you talk to it, it squawks out where the owner hides his money: a vent in the wall next to the generator. You reach it through Shirley's rum errand early in the story, and with the store's small safe, the trip is reported to pay a little over $2,000, one of the biggest early cash grabs in the game.

Players found it on launch day, and guide writers at GTABoom, Insider Gaming and Screen Hype have all done it on the released game. Below: when the errand unlocks, the route in, the fight, the exact spot, how much money each part pays, and the deadline that makes it missable. Sources are credited at the bottom.

Missable: GTABoom says Shirley's errand disappears for good once you start the Chapter 1 mission Tourist Trap. Do it before then.

How to unlock the parrot cash (Shirley's rum errand)

REPORTED: GTABoom, Insider Gaming, Screen Hype, Mashable (via World Today News), Nov 19
  1. Play the first four story missions. GTABoom and Mashable list them as Gear Up, Lab Fees, Just a Scratch and Unwanted Attention. Finishing them unlocks Pipework.
  2. Visit Shirley, Jason's neighbour. Screen Hype places her house on the big corner property east of Jason's place on Peach Palm Drive. Insider Gaming says she's just down the road from Jason's safehouse.
  3. She asks for rum. Screen Hype says she wants it for a mimosa; Insider Gaming names the bottle as Salt Squid Rum. The store, Three Sheets Liquor, gets marked on your map.
  4. Drive to Three Sheets Liquor in Key Lento. Screen Hype puts it off the Sunbelt Highway, north of the Key Lento Pay 'n' Spray and south of the shoe store Flip Flop House.
  5. Ask for Shirley's rum at the counter (it's a dialogue option). Screen Hype says she has a tab, so you don't pay.

GTABoom also says you can simply drive to the store in free roam and it still works, as long as Tourist Trap hasn't started. Insider Gaming describes the follow-up a little differently: after you hand Shirley the rum and play for a while, a yellow mission icon appears at the liquor store (the mission it calls Strange Noises). Either way, the cash is in the same place.

Where is the talking parrot?

While you're inside, you hear the owner telling a room full of animals to be quiet. That's the hint. Behind the store there's an exotic-pet lot, and GTABoom gives the route in:

  • Go down the alley on the right side of the building.
  • At the end, look at the left wall for a broken section of chain-link fence and slip through.
  • The parrot is a red macaw (Screen Hype calls it red and blue) in the central cage, near the back of the lot.

The fight: deal with the thugs first

You won't have the yard to yourself. Every source says armed men are waiting, and the parrot even comments on the fight, according to Insider Gaming. Screen Hype's writer counted five: one in the back room, three more who run in from outside, and a last one in a red shirt. They fought with fists and won, so you don't need a big gun, but bring armour if you have it. Our heal and armour guide covers how to patch up between waves.

Where the money is: the generator vent

Once it's quiet, talk to the parrot. It squawks about cash and a generator (Mashable's description), which points you to the hiding spot:

  1. Stand facing the parrot's cage.
  2. Look to the back-left corner of the outdoor area. Insider Gaming puts the generator on the left side of the building.
  3. Next to the generator there's an interactable vent (Mashable calls it a grate in the wall). Open it to take the cash.

Frequently asked questions

Where is the talking parrot in GTA 6?

In the exotic-pet lot behind Three Sheets Liquor in Key Lento, Leonida Keys. Go down the right-side alley and through the broken chain-link fence on the left wall; the red macaw is in the central cage.

How much money does the GTA 6 parrot give you?

Reports range from about $1,000 to $1,400 in the generator vent, plus roughly $900-1,000 in a small safe inside, for a little over $2,000 total (GTABoom). Insider Gaming says nearly $2,500 with everything.

Is the parrot cash missable?

Yes, according to GTABoom: Shirley's rum errand disappears once you start the Chapter 1 mission Tourist Trap.

When can you do Shirley's rum errand?

After the first four story missions (Gear Up, Lab Fees, Just a Scratch, Unwanted Attention) unlock Pipework, and before Tourist Trap.
