GTA 6 Makes Driving Harder When You Text (No Pause)

By vAnother Level · Updated 2026-11-19

Yes, you can text while driving in GTA 6, but the game makes the car harder to control while your phone is out. Bloomberg's Jason Schreier spotted it on launch night, Kotaku's reviewer confirmed it, and because the phone never pauses the game, the safe move is to let your partner drive or pull over first.

REPORTED: from players and outlets who played GTA 6 on Nov 18. Rockstar hasn't explained the mechanic

What Jason Schreier found

On Nov 18, Bloomberg reporter Jason Schreier posted on X that he wasn't joking: GTA 6 really does make the driving controls harder while you're using your phone. He joked it could teach a whole generation not to text and drive.

Kotaku included the post in its GTA 6 launch live blog, and its reviewer Zack confirmed the penalty from his own playthrough. Kotaku also points out the way around it: switch to the passenger seat and let your partner drive.

Why it hits harder in GTA 6

  • The phone doesn't pause the game. Game Rant's phone guide (Nov 18) says it works like GTA 5: traffic, cops and enemies keep moving while you scroll. It advises against opening it while driving or in a fight.
  • You open it with D-Pad Up, per Game Rant. Apps include WhatUp texts, RydeMe, BuckMe, Camera, Snapmatic and the WAiNK car scanner.
  • The phone is a physical object in the world, not a flat menu, according to Rockstar's Extended Look and the Dazed interview with Rob Nelson (via GTA Intel).
  • Partner texts matter. GTA Intel says ignoring your partner's texts can hurt the relationship, so there's a reason to reply mid-drive.
THEORY: we don't know if the penalty is a steering wobble, slower input or just looking away from the road. Nobody has measured it yet

How to text without crashing

  1. Let the other character drive. Switch seats with your partner and use the phone as the passenger (Kotaku, GTA Intel).
  2. Pull over or stop at a red light before you open WhatUp.
  3. Never open it during a chase. The game keeps running, and a crash plus a wanted level costs more than a late reply. Our police guide shows how to lose the cops if you do.
  4. Use RydeMe for long trips (it costs money) and text from the back seat.

Frequently asked questions

Can you text while driving in GTA 6?

Yes. The phone opens while you drive, but players and Kotaku's reviewer report the car is harder to control while it's out (REPORTED).

Does the phone pause the game in GTA 6?

No. Per Game Rant, opening the phone doesn't pause anything, just like GTA 5.

How do you open the phone in GTA 6?

Press D-Pad Up, per Game Rant's Nov 18 guide.

How do you text safely while driving in GTA 6?

Switch to the passenger seat and let your partner drive, or pull over first.
