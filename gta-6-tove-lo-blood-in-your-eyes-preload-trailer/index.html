GTA 6's New Pre-Load Trailer Debuts a Tove Lo Song: "Blood In Your Eyes"

By vAnother Level · Updated 2026-11-10
Home › GTA 6 Guides

GTA 6 just got its first big new song of launch week. Rockstar's Pre-Load Now video, released November 10 as preloads opened, is set to "Blood In Your Eyes" by Tove Lo. The track is also on GTAVI: The Album, and it's out now on all major streaming services.

Announced by Rockstar Games on X, November 10, 2026

"Blood In Your Eyes" by @ToveLo — featured in today's Pre-Load Now video and also on GTAVI: The Album.

— Rockstar Games (@RockstarGames) November 10, 2026

The short Pre-Load Now video shows previously unseen side characters and new sequences rather than Jason and Lucia, according to early coverage.

Then vs now

Grand Theft Auto has always sold itself with music. Vice City's 1980s radio stations became a soundtrack box set in 2002, and GTA 5 came with original songs and a full score in 2013. GTA 6 is the first in the series with a standalone album rolling out before the game is even playable.
