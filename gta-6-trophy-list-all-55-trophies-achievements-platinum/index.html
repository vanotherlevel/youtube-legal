GTA 6 has 55 trophies and achievements: 1 Platinum, 2 Gold, 6 Silver and 46 Bronze, worth 1,000 Gamerscore on Xbox. The Platinum is called Vice Got a Price!, and on Xbox it's the new Mythic achievement for unlocking everything else. Every trophy is single-player, so you don't need GTA 6 Online or any DLC.

Below is every trophy in one place, sorted into groups so you can plan your run: story, side characters, activities, crime, exploration and the secret ones. You'll also find the one trophy that may be missable, how long the Platinum takes and whether cheats lock you out. More guides live in our GTA 6 guides hub.

Labels on this page: OFFICIAL = Rockstar's own words. REPORTED = the list as published on Nov 18 by GTABase, Press Start and PowerPyx (all three match name for name). THEORY = our own read. Rockstar hasn't posted the list itself. Light spoiler warning: the hidden story trophies name chapters and late missions.

The GTA 6 Platinum: Vice Got a Price!

REPORTED: Vice Got a Price! = unlock every other trophy (Xbox: Mythic achievement, 0G)

You earn it by unlocking the other 54. GTA 6 is the first big game to use Xbox's Mythic achievement, which works like a PlayStation Platinum. Nothing needs Online, a second playthrough or a difficulty setting (GTA 6 has no difficulty options, per PowerPyx).

Is any GTA 6 trophy missable?

REPORTED (PowerPyx): potentially 1 missable, Positive Mental Attitude

PowerPyx says Positive Mental Attitude (meditate at every spot) may be missable. One meditation spot is at Jason's house in Chapter 1, and it reportedly can't be reached after Chapter 1 ends. Meditate there before you move on, and you're safe. Everything else can be done after the story.

How long does the GTA 6 Platinum take?

REPORTED (PowerPyx): about 200 hours, difficulty 4/10, 1 playthrough

PowerPyx puts it at about 200 hours with a 4/10 difficulty. Other estimates go as low as 80+ hours. The difficulty isn't the issue; the sheer amount of checklist is. Their roadmap, in short:

  1. Story and optional missions (60-80 hours): play the story, do side missions, scan vehicles and study animals as you go. Do the Chapter 1 meditation at Jason's house.
  2. 100% checklist (about 100 hours): clear the completion list and 70 of the 80 challenges. Most trophies pop here.
  3. Strangers & Freaks and cleanup (about 10 hours): finish leftover strands and any trophy still locked.
OUR TAKE (THEORY): do the partner robberies (Hot Together, Retail Therapy) early, because they also bring in cash for Treat Yo Self and Little Boxes. Leave Cunning Stunts (250 stunts) and You WAiNKer! (100 locations) for last; they're grinds you can chip away at while exploring.

Do cheats disable trophies in GTA 6?

REPORTED (PowerPyx): yes, cheats disable trophies and saving

Yes. PowerPyx says using a cheat turns off trophies and saving. If you want to try them, quit without saving afterwards. Every reported cheat is on our GTA 6 cheats list.
