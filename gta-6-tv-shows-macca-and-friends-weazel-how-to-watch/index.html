GTA 6 TV Shows: Macca & Friends on WEAZEL and How to Watch

Yes, you can watch real TV shows in GTA 6. The headline show is Macca & Friends, a 2D animated cartoon about Macca the pink gator, and it airs on the in-game network WEAZEL. On launch day, players found full episodes, about 8 minutes each, playing on the TV in Jason's home.

OFFICIAL: Rockstar revealed Macca & Friends with a 30-second teaser on Nov 13, 2026, premiering Nov 19 on WEAZEL

What is Macca & Friends?

Rockstar showed the first teaser on November 13, 2026, six days before launch. It came out exclusively through DiscussingFilm. The teaser is about 30 seconds long, with a full voice cast and 2D animation. Earlier GTA games mostly had short looping 3D clips on TV.

  • Macca: a pink alligator, already GTA 6's unofficial mascot. He's also on the Leonida maps and in Rockstar's Collector's Box.
  • His friends: a talking Sun in sunglasses, and a crab landlord. Outlets disagree on the crab's name: Insider Gaming says Pinchy, Beebom says Pinche.
  • The plot in the teaser: Macca needs money so his landlord won't evict him. That's the same "make money, stay free" theme as the main story, as GamingBible points out.
  • Where it's set: Vice City and the Grassrivers, the swamp country of Leonida.
  • The tone: an adult comedy. GamingBible compares it to BoJack Horseman, and Push Square to Adult Swim shows.

Full episodes are in the game

REPORTED (Kotaku launch live blog, Nov 19, 11:26 AM): a full 8-minute Macca & Friends episode found playing on Jason's TV

Before launch nobody knew how much of the show would really be in the game. Now we know it isn't just the teaser. Kotaku's launch live blog says GTA 6's TV is "basically a replacement for shortform video apps", with animation, news and reality programming on it. Their example is a full 8-minute episode of Macca & Friends spotted on Jason's TV.

How to watch TV in GTA 6

  1. Go to a safehouse with a TV. Jason's starting home in the Leonida Keys has one.
  2. Walk up to the TV and use the prompt to turn it on.
  3. Change channels with the on-screen prompts until you find WEAZEL. Kotaku's tip: just sit there. It's real content, not a 10-second loop.
  4. Want to flip between channels? GTABoom's safehouse guide says you can change channels, or radio stations, while you're using them.
Watching TV is optional and isn't a mission. It isn't on the 100% completion checklist as far as we know. The radio side, including Weird Al's DJ slot, is in our phone and radio guide.

GTA TV, then and now

  • GTA IV (2008): three channels: Weazel, CNT and a public broadcasting channel.
  • GTA V (2013): two channels, Weazel and CNT. Shows included Fame or Shame and the cartoons Republican Space Rangers and Kung Fu Rainbow Lazer Force.
  • GTA 6 (2026): full-length animated episodes, plus news and reality TV. Rockstar reportedly used an outside animation studio (Kinda Funny).

Frequently asked questions

Can you watch TV in GTA 6?

Yes. Safehouses like Jason's home have working TVs. Kotaku found animation, news and reality programming, including full 8-minute episodes of Macca & Friends.

What channel is Macca & Friends on?

WEAZEL, GTA 6's in-game TV network. Rockstar's teaser said it premieres on Nov 19, 2026, launch day.

Who is Macca in GTA 6?

Macca is a pink cartoon alligator who stars in Macca & Friends. He's also on the Leonida map and in Rockstar's Collector's Box.

How many Macca & Friends episodes are there?

Rockstar hasn't said. Kotaku found at least one full 8-minute episode on launch day. We'll update this page when the count is known.
