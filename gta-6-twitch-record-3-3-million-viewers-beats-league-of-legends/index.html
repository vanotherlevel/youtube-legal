GTA 6 Breaks Twitch Record: 3.3 Million Viewers, Beats LoL

By vAnother Level · Updated 2026-11-18

Yes, GTA 6 just set the record for the most-watched single game in Twitch history. On November 18, the Grand Theft Auto VI category peaked at about 3.29 million concurrent viewers, passing League of Legends' roughly 3.11 million from the 2022 World Championship final.

REPORTED: Insider Gaming, TwistedVoxel, LibertyCity, using Streams Charts and TwitchTracker data

Twitch itself hasn't announced the record yet. The figures come from third-party trackers, and they differ a little from site to site. The game unlocked in New Zealand first, so all of this happened before most of the world could even play. More launch news on our GTA 6 news tab.

One category still sits higher: TwitchTracker lists Twitch's Special Events category at about 3.92 million, but that isn't a game. Among individual games, GTA 6 is now number one.

Who drew the most viewers?

LibertyCity named the two biggest Twitch streams it saw:

  • HoneyMad (Russia): about 576,000 viewers
  • alanzoka (Brazil): about 313,000 viewers

Many other streamers set personal records too. YouTube's own 24-hour launch marathon starts tonight, so the combined total could climb again. Times and creators are in our YouTube 24-hour livestream guide.

Will the record go higher?

OUR TAKE

Probably. The 3.29 million peak came before the game unlocked in the US, UK and Europe, where most of the big English-language streamers are. Midnight in the Americas on November 19 is the next big test. Pre-orders are already past 17 million copies, per Sensor Tower: see our pre-orders page.

Frequently asked questions

How many people watched GTA 6 on Twitch?

The GTA 6 category peaked at about 3.29 million concurrent viewers on November 18, 2026, per Streams Charts and TwitchTracker data reported by Insider Gaming and TwistedVoxel.

Is GTA 6 the most-watched game on Twitch ever?

Reportedly yes, for a single game. It passed League of Legends' roughly 3.11 million from the 2022 Worlds final. Twitch hasn't confirmed it officially.

How many people watched GTA 6 across all platforms?

Preliminary Streams Charts data put the combined Twitch, YouTube and Kick peak at roughly 7.2 to 7.6 million, depending on the report.

Who was the biggest GTA 6 streamer on launch day?

LibertyCity reported HoneyMad at about 576,000 viewers and alanzoka at about 313,000.
