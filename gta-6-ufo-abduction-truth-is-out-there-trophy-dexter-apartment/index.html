GTA 6 UFO Abduction: The Truth Is Out There Trophy + Dexter's Apartment

Yes, GTA 6 has a UFO. It's a night-time abduction event in Kelly County, on the back roads east of Port Gellhorn, and finishing it unlocks a hidden bronze trophy called The Truth Is Out There. On the same launch day, players also found a Vice City apartment block that looks almost exactly like Dexter Morgan's building from the TV show Dexter.

Both are player discoveries, written up by outlets on Nov 18-19. Here's how to see the UFO yourself, what's known about the Dexter building, and what's still unconfirmed.

REPORTED: GamesHorizon, VGTimes, Grandtheft.gg, Dexter Daily, GameSpot (Nov 18-19, 2026). Rockstar hasn't commented on either.

Where is the UFO in GTA 6?

Reports agree on the area: a dirt road in Kelly County, east of Port Gellhorn, a few hundred metres from the Starlet Motel safehouse. The event plays out in the woods just off that road.

  • When it unlocks: after the story's first chapter. VGTimes says finish Chapter 1; GamesHorizon says reach Chapter 2 while you're around Port Gellhorn. Both point to the same moment.
  • Time of day: night only. GamesHorizon's writer saw it after 2:00 AM in-game, and says it can also show up shortly after midnight.
  • Who can trigger it: Jason or Lucia. GamesHorizon reports no real difference in dialogue or outcome between them.

How to trigger the UFO event (step by step)

REPORTED: steps from GamesHorizon (Kushal Bhattacharyya) and VGTimes (Hennadiy Chemeris)
  1. Drive the dirt roads east of Port Gellhorn at night. Look for a car parked facing a trail into the woods, with its trunk open and its lights flickering. VGTimes adds there are clothes scattered nearby.
  2. Stop and turn on your flashlight.
  3. Follow the faint trail into the trees toward a blue light that keeps getting brighter.
  4. Reach the misty clearing. A UFO is beaming a person up into the sky.
  5. Talk to the abductee. They lash out and faint. Talk to them again and offer to help.
  6. Go back for their car. You get an objective to fetch the car from the start of the trail. When you walk up to it, the UFO comes back overhead and beams you.
  7. Trophy pops. Your character reacts, passes out, and wakes up three days later. The bronze trophy is The Truth Is Out There, with the in-game description "What the hell just happened?"
The trophy is not needed for 100% story completion, per VGTimes. Save before you head out, since your character loses three in-game days. Our GTA 6 save guide shows how to make a manual save.

Viral clips on X back this up. Tech Ultimatum (@TechieUltimatum) shared a Kelly County night clip of a saucer lifting an NPC in a light beam, with Lucia asking what on earth she's looking at. It's a nice nod to GTA 5's famous Mount Chiliad UFO mystery.

Dexter Morgan's apartment in Vice City

Fans first suspected it back in August, when a building with the same shape showed up in the background of Rockstar's extended gameplay look, in the shot where Lucia parachutes off a rooftop. Now that the game is out, players who finished the opening missions have driven Jason to the building and posted the footage, mostly on TikTok.

  • How close is it? Fan site Dexter Daily calls the in-game complex "roughly 90% faithful" to the real Miami condo used in the show.
  • What matches: the overall building and the white railings from the original series.
  • What's different: the columns are light blue, like the real complex looks today, not white as in the show. Rockstar seems to have mixed details from different years of the building's history.
  • Where exactly? It's in Vice City. GameSpot has a step-by-step guide to finding it.
THEORY: Rockstar has not said it's a Dexter tribute

Some headlines say the easter egg is "officially confirmed", but no outlet cites a Rockstar statement. The confirmation is players finding the building in the game. With a show set in Miami and a game set in a Miami parody, it's an easy nod to believe in, but it's still a fan reading until Rockstar says otherwise.

Leonida's secrets so far

Less than a day in, players have found a UFO, Bigfoot at Cottonmouth Lake, a giant mutant crab and a real Easter egg on the tallest tower, and the jet ski dog and Dog Island. We keep adding the confirmed ones to our GTA 6 easter eggs list and the GTA 6 news hub.

Frequently asked questions

Are there aliens in GTA 6?

Yes, in one secret event. At night in Kelly County, east of Port Gellhorn, a UFO abducts a person in the woods and then beams your character, unlocking the bronze trophy The Truth Is Out There.

How do you get The Truth Is Out There trophy in GTA 6?

After Chapter 1, go to the dirt roads east of Port Gellhorn after midnight (2 AM is most reliable), find the parked car with an open trunk and flickering lights, follow the trail to the blue light, help the abductee, then walk back to their car.

Is the UFO trophy needed for 100%?

No. VGTimes reports it isn't required for 100% story completion.

Is Dexter's apartment in GTA 6?

Players found a Vice City apartment complex that closely matches Dexter Morgan's building from the show. Rockstar hasn't confirmed it's a deliberate tribute.
