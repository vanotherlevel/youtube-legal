GTA 6 Tops UK Physical Chart With No Disc in the Box

Yes, GTA 6 debuted at number one in the UK retail chart, even though the boxed edition holds a download code and no disc. The chart covers the week ending November 21, 2026, and is based on GfK data.

REPORTED: GfK chart as published by VGChartz, Nintendo Life and Games Asylum. No UK unit figure has been made public in these reports.

GTA 6 lifted other games too

Christopher Dring of The Game Business said 36 of the top 40 games in the physical UK chart sold substantially more during GTA 6's launch week. These are the rises Dring listed, as quoted by VGChartz:

GameWeekly rise
Ghost of Yotei+790%
Astro Bot+730%
Marvel's Wolverine+400%
Elden Ring+350%
Spider-Man 2+165%

Dring also pointed out that this was a Black Friday build-up week, so GTA 6 is probably not the only reason. Discounts and early holiday shopping were happening at the same time.

Is there a disc in the GTA 6 box?

No. The retail edition is code-in-a-box: you redeem the code and download the game. Games Asylum notes that the boxes went on sale on November 12, ahead of the November 19 launch. If your code or download gives you trouble, our launch bugs and box codes fixes page walks through it.

What the chart does not tell you

A first place here is a ranking of boxed sales, not a total. It does not include digital store purchases and does not say how many boxes were sold. For the bigger numbers, Take-Two's official figures were 32.5 million units sold before release and an estimated $3.79 billion in day-one retail sales worldwide. Those are global, not UK. See the launch sales breakdown.

THEORY (our take): a No.1 for a disc-less box suggests many buyers still like picking up something from a shop, or giving it as a gift.
