The biggest UK estimate says 887,515 workers could call in sick for GTA 6 on November 19. It comes from a Uswitch survey of 1,000 UK gamers, scaled up to 2.56 million people taking some kind of time off, but the survey measured what people said they might do, not real absences.

REPORTED: Uswitch/OnePoll figures via Advanced Television and IBTimes UK. These are survey intentions and estimates, not confirmed absences

GTA 6 unlocks at midnight local time on November 19, a Thursday. Four different surveys have tried to guess how many Brits will skip work for it. Here's what each one actually says.

The Uswitch survey in detail

Uswitch, the broadband comparison site, commissioned OnePoll to ask 1,000 working UK gamers about launch day. 438 of them said they plan to play GTA 6. Among GTA 6 players:

  • 54% plan to change their working schedule on release day
  • 30% of home workers may use some working time to play
  • 17% would consider annual leave
  • 14% may take unpaid leave or make other arrangements
  • 9% may call in sick without being ill
  • 21% expect to be more tired at work, and 21% expect to be distracted

Scaled up to the UK workforce, Uswitch's figures come to 2.56 million workers taking time off: about 1.68 million on annual leave and 887,515 on sick leave. Using the £19.67 UK median hourly pay, that's 20.5 million working hours worth around £403.5 million, of which £139.7 million is the possible sick leave.

REPORTED: the 2.56M / 887,515 / £403.5M scaling appears in Advanced Television's write-up. IBTimes UK notes the calculation behind the UK-wide figure isn't set out in the published research, so treat it as a rough estimate

Max Beckett, Uswitch's broadband expert, said GTA VI "is one of the biggest gaming releases in years", and his main tip was about the 212 GB download, not the sick day. See our download time for every internet speed.

BrightHR: 83,000 workers already booked it

Absence tracker BrightHR's data, reported by The Sun, says 83,000 UK workers requested days off across November and December to play GTA 6, costing UK businesses about £12 million, with roughly £3 million of that on launch day alone. About 5% of holiday requests for November 19 give a GTA-related reason.

REPORTED: BrightHR data via The Sun, as relayed by Tech4Gamers (Nov 9). Booked leave, not sick days

Why the numbers are so far apart

  • Different questions. Uswitch asked what gamers might do. Opinium asked who plans to play on day one. BrightHR and BambooHR count real leave requests.
  • Different groups. Uswitch only asked working gamers, then scaled to the whole workforce. Opinium asked all UK adults.
  • Booked leave isn't a sickie. BrightHR's 83,000 asked properly. Uswitch's 887,515 is the guess for people who wouldn't.
  • Cause isn't proven. BambooHR itself says the 8.3% UK rise doesn't prove GTA 6 caused every extra request.
OUR TAKE: the real number of UK sick days is unknowable until absence data comes out. The leave-request counts (BrightHR, BambooHR) are the hardest evidence; the 887,515 figure is the most quoted but the softest

The same story in the US

In the US, an economist's estimate put the launch at 1.5 million workers and $1 billion in lost productivity, and BambooHR's survey found 18% of 1,530 full-time US employees plan to take launch day off. Frequently asked questions

How many UK workers will call in sick for GTA 6?

Uswitch's survey-based estimate is 887,515 UK workers taking sick leave on November 19, out of 2.56 million taking some time off. It's based on what 1,000 gamers said they might do, so it's an estimate, not a count.

How many people have booked time off for GTA 6 in the UK?

BrightHR data reported by The Sun says 83,000 UK workers requested days off in November and December to play GTA 6, and about 5% of holiday requests for November 19 are GTA-related.

How much will GTA 6 cost UK businesses?

Estimates range from about £12 million (BrightHR, booked leave) to £403.5 million (Uswitch's scaled estimate of all time off, including £139.7 million in possible sick leave).

What day does GTA 6 come out in the UK?

Thursday November 19, 2026, at midnight local time on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.
