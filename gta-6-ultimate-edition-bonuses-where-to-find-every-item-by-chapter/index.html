Your GTA 6 Ultimate Edition bonuses don't all unlock at the start. They unlock as the story goes: most shops and Jason's vehicles appear in Chapter 1, the Morgan Revolvers, Dominator Buggy and Classic Car Collection in Chapter 2, Vice City Style and the Shitzu Squalo boat in Chapter 4, and the '95 Grotti Cheetah only in Chapter 5.

Below is every item, the chapter it unlocks in and exactly where to collect it, based on launch-day guides from Shacknews and VICE (Nov 18, 2026), plus the backlash from players who paid $99.99 and still have to wait. Rockstar hasn't published its own unlock list, so the chapter data is labelled REPORTED.

Chapter 1 bonuses: Jason's vehicles and the five shops

REPORTED

Chapter 1 gives you the most. Jason's Dinka Enduro and Crest Kayak wait at his house, and the Ganado Retro Build is applied at Coastal Auto Care in Key Lento. Jason's personal Girardi ES9 shows up in the Variants section of the Ammu-Nation weapon locker.

All five Ultimate-only businesses open in this chapter too: Rideout Customs and One-Eyed Willie's (mod shops), Stock 305 (clothes), Sara's Unisex Salon (hair) and Electric Fang Tattoo. They're all in the map index, so you don't need to drive around looking.

Use them while you can: VICE and PlayStation LifeStyle report that the Dinka Enduro, Crest Kayak and Ganado Retro Build can't be used after Chapter 1. Rockstar hasn't explained why. Our guess is that it's because the story moves you away from Jason's place, but that is THEORY.

The PTT Youngin$ Compound is the illegal-goods store Rockstar listed as an Ultimate exclusive side mission. Shacknews says you find it by exploring Southside, and it then appears in the map index under Robberies / Illegal Operations. (Shacknews spells it "Pit Youngin$"; Rockstar's own art says PTT.)

Chapter 2 bonuses: Lucia's pistol, Morgan Revolvers, Dominator Buggy

REPORTED

Chapter 2 starts once Lucia joins the story properly. That's when her Klose K17 and the Hawk & Little Morgan Revolvers land in the Ammu-Nation locker. Read how those fit your loadout in our GTA 6 gun system explained and every GTA 6 weapon pages.

For the '67 Vapid Dominator Buggy and its garage, visit Mud Club with Wyman, then take the task from the Exploration section of the map index. Once the Buggy is yours, Wyman texts you on WhatUp to start the Classic Car Collection side job. Jason and Lucia's signature looks also unlock at Sara's Unisex Salon here.

Chapter 4 bonuses: Vice City Style and the Shitzu Squalo

REPORTED

Nothing from the Ultimate Edition is reported for Chapter 3. In Chapter 4 you get Vice City Style: the outfits go into your wardrobe, the tattoos appear in the Special section of most tattoo parlours, and the hair and beards are at Sara's Unisex Salon. The Shitzu Squalo speedboat is at a canal dock in Washington Beach and shows in the map index under Personal.

Chapter 5 bonus: the '95 Grotti Cheetah

REPORTED

The car on the Ultimate box art is the last thing you get. The '95 Grotti Cheetah unlocks in Chapter 5 at the Pegassi Residences building (map index > Exploration). GTA 6 has five chapters in total, per our missions and story length guide, so this is late-game.

Pre-order bonus: '55 Vapid Stanier (Chapter 3)

REPORTED: Shacknews

The Vintage Vice City Pack is the pre-order bonus for both editions, not an Ultimate item. Shacknews reports the '55 Vapid Stanier and its garage unlock in Chapter 3 (map index > Exploration). Collecting the Stanier then unlocks the pack's outfits and hairstyles and the Channel the Original Kingpin weapon pattern (Cosmetics / Wraps at any Ammu-Nation).

Why players are calling it "scam of the year"

REPORTED: PlayStation LifeStyle (Mintu Kumar Tomar), Nov 18

PlayStation LifeStyle rounded up the anger on X. One player called the Ultimate Edition the "scam of the year". Others asked why paying $100 still means 25+ hours of waiting, or compared the chapter unlocks to a battle pass. The article says Rockstar hasn't responded and hasn't confirmed the unlock times.

OUR TAKE (opinion)

Tying the items to the story makes sense for some of them: a Chapter 1 Cheetah would make the early money grind pointless. But players paid for the Ultimate Edition up front, and some of its best items (the Cheetah, the Squalo and Vice City Style) only arrive 40-60 hours in. If you haven't bought yet and only want the extras early, know that you won't have them early. Our Standard vs Ultimate guide has the full price breakdown.

Bonus missing? Quick checks

  1. Check your chapter first: anything above your current chapter in the table won't be there yet.
  2. Open the map index in the pause menu and look under Vehicles, Style, Exploration and Personal.
  3. For weapons, open an Ammu-Nation weapon locker and check the Variants tab.
  4. Make sure the Ultimate Edition (or the Ultimate upgrade) is installed on your console as an add-on.
  5. Still missing? Contact Rockstar Support, and don't buy from "unlock" websites or YouTube comments, which are scams.

Frequently asked questions

Do GTA 6 Ultimate Edition bonuses unlock straight away?

No. They're reported to unlock by story chapter: most shops and Jason's vehicles in Chapter 1, the revolvers and Dominator Buggy in Chapter 2, Vice City Style and the Squalo in Chapter 4, and the '95 Grotti Cheetah in Chapter 5.

Where is the Grotti Cheetah in GTA 6?

Shacknews reports it unlocks in Chapter 5 at the Pegassi Residences building, listed in the map index under Exploration.

Can I lose Ultimate Edition items in GTA 6?

VICE and PlayStation LifeStyle report that Jason's Dinka Enduro, Crest Kayak and the Ganado Retro Build can't be used after Chapter 1. Rockstar hasn't commented.

How long does it take to unlock all GTA 6 Ultimate Edition content?

Early estimates say about 25+ hours to reach Chapter 2 and about 60 hours to reach Chapter 5. These are reported estimates, not Rockstar figures.

Where is the '55 Vapid Stanier pre-order car?

Shacknews reports it unlocks in Chapter 3, in the map index under Exploration. Collecting it also unlocks the Vintage Vice City outfits and the Kingpin weapon pattern.
