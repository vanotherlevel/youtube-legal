GTA 6 Unofficial PC Ports: Take-Two Reportedly Hits 3,007 GitHub Repos

By vAnother Level · Updated 2026-11-27

Take-Two Interactive has reportedly issued a DMCA takedown covering 3,007 GitHub repositories tied to unofficial GTA 6 PC ports. Tom's Hardware reported it on November 26, citing VideoCardz. There is still no official PC version and no announced PC date.

REPORTED: Tom's Hardware, citing VideoCardz (Nov 26). We have not seen the takedown notice ourselves

What was reportedly removed

According to Tom's Hardware, the action is the biggest Take-Two has taken so far to protect GTA VI. It reportedly came a day after a recompilation project run by a 15-year-old was shut down on both Discord and GitHub.

"Recompilation" here means converting the console game's code so it can run on a Windows PC. Nothing about these projects comes from Rockstar, and a takedown notice is a copyright claim, not a court ruling.

The Discord server: Project Leonida

REPORTED: Insider Gaming, Mike Straw (Nov 24). No statement from Rockstar or Take-Two

Insider Gaming reports that a Discord server working on a PC port, called Project Leonida, was shut down after Rockstar learned of the work. The project had reportedly reached the point where the game started booting on some PCs.

  • How it was shut down: the report does not say whether Discord removed the server, a legal notice was sent or the organisers closed it.
  • The project lead: posted that they had received a two-year Discord ban and were quitting. That is their own account, as relayed by Insider Gaming.
  • Not given: how many members the server had, or any statement from Rockstar, Take-Two or Discord.

The projects haven't all stopped

REPORTED: Tom's Hardware, citing the tracker PortWatch VI and a Reddit post
  • Development is reportedly moving into private spaces. PortWatch VI, a site that tracks the unofficial ports, was following 30 projects.
  • One build reportedly ran for more than 90 minutes on an RTX 3050 without crashing, at an unplayable 1 to 3 frames per second.
  • A Reddit post claims a port can already reach 30 FPS. That is an unverified claim from its own poster.

Tom's Hardware also points to AI-assisted reverse engineering tools as a reason these projects have appeared so quickly, while noting the work still takes skill and effort.

Should you download one?

OUR ADVICE

No. These builds are unauthorized copies of a game Rockstar hasn't released on PC. Files that claim to be "GTA 6 PC" are also a natural hiding place for malware, as we covered in fake GTA 6 PC gameplay and the Remote Play malware warning. A video of a port running proves nothing about whether a download with the same name is safe. We don't link to any of these projects.

When is the official PC version?

Rockstar has not announced one. GamesBeat reported a Q1 2027 target from anonymous sources in October, which remains a report, not a date. Our GTA 6 PC release date page separates that report from the newer rumours.

Not the first time

Take-Two has used GitHub takedowns on fan projects before: in 2021 it targeted re3 and reVC, reverse-engineered versions of GTA III and Vice City. TorrentFreak also reported takedowns against unofficial mobile and web ports of GTA V shortly before GTA VI's launch. Those are separate from the GTA 6 action reported here. Frequently asked questions

Is GTA 6 out on PC?

No. It released on November 19, 2026 for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Rockstar has not announced a PC version date.

What did Take-Two take down?

Tom's Hardware, citing VideoCardz, reports a DMCA takedown covering 3,007 GitHub repositories connected to unofficial GTA 6 PC port work.

Are the unofficial GTA 6 PC ports safe?

We advise against them. They are unauthorized, none comes from Rockstar, and fake downloads can carry malware.

What was Project Leonida?

A Discord server working on an unofficial GTA 6 PC port. Insider Gaming reported on November 24, 2026 that it was shut down; the report does not say by what mechanism.
