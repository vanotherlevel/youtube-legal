GTA 6 Update 1.002.001: 602 MB on PS5, 1.59 GB on Xbox

GTA 6 has a second pre-launch update: version 1.002.001, rolling out since Monday, November 16, 2026. It's reportedly 602.5 MB on PS5 and about 1.59 GB on Xbox Series X|S, and Rockstar has published no patch notes for it.

REPORTED: sizes from RockstarINTEL and the PlayStation Game Size tracker on X, not from Rockstar

If you pre-loaded, it should download on its own. If it hasn't shown up, you're probably in a later rollout wave, not stuck.

Why you might not have it yet

On PS5 the update is reportedly going out in waves, not to everyone at once. GGRecon's schedule, citing RockstarINTEL:

  • 5 AM ET Monday (Nov 16): about one third of PS5 pre-loaders eligible.
  • 5 PM ET Monday: about two thirds eligible.
  • 5 AM ET Tuesday (Nov 17): everyone who pre-loaded should have it.

On Xbox, RockstarINTEL says players got it straight away instead of in waves, while ComicBook described a staggered rollout on both platforms. The reports don't agree, so if your Xbox doesn't have it yet, check again in a few hours.

To force a check on PS5: highlight GTA 6 on the home screen, press Options → Check for Update. On Xbox: My games & apps → Manage → Updates. Leave the console in Rest Mode with internet on so it can finish on its own.

Is this the day-one patch?

OFFICIAL: Rockstar hasn't announced a day-one patch

Some headlines called 1.002.001 the "day-one patch", but Rockstar hasn't confirmed one, or its size, contents or timing. It follows 1.002.000, which arrived on November 13 at about 1 GB, also with no patch notes.

THEORY

GGRecon wrote that a third update before Thursday "would not be a shock". The size gap between PS5 and Xbox (about 1 GB) hasn't been explained by Rockstar either. Treat any "leaked patch notes" you see as fake until Rockstar posts them.

PS5 owners: you need the system update too

This game update is separate from Sony's PS5 system software 26.07-14.23.00, which PlayStation Support reportedly said to install before midnight on November 19. Our page on PS5 update 14.23 has the install steps.

MP1st linked Rockstar's patch timing to the PS5 homebrew scene, but it later corrected its headline, saying the latest firmware had not been cracked. There's no proof the GTA 6 updates are anti-piracy patches.

When GTA 6 unlocks

Rockstar's support page says GTA 6 unlocks at midnight local time on November 19, 2026 in every region. Xbox Cloud Gaming streams from 1 AM ET. Times for every country are on our release time page, and every launch story is on the GTA 6 news hub.

Sources: TechTimes (Terrence Hill), Nov 17 · MP1st (Alex Co), Nov 16 · RockstarINTEL · GGRecon on update 1.002 · ComicBook and Vice via TechTimes · Rockstar Support (unlock times).

Frequently asked questions

What is GTA 6 update 1.002.001?

A second pre-launch update released November 16, 2026, reportedly about 602.5 MB on PS5 and 1.59 GB on Xbox Series X|S. Rockstar hasn't published patch notes.

Why don't I have GTA 6 update 1.002.001 yet?

On PS5 it reportedly rolled out in waves: everyone who pre-loaded should have had it by 5 AM ET on November 17. Use Options → Check for Update to force a check.

Is 1.002.001 the GTA 6 day-one patch?

Rockstar hasn't said so. No day-one patch has been officially announced, and another update before launch is possible.

No. Rockstar hasn't published notes for 1.002.000 or 1.002.001.
