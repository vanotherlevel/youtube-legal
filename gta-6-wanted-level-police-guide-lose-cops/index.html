To lose a wanted level in GTA 6, get out of the red search area on the map and stay out of sight until the stars stop. The game says so itself: "Lose the cops ★ by hiding or escaping their search area." Police also remember your outfit, your face and your car, shown as three red icons under the stars. Changing any of them (a Pay 'n' Spray respray, a new outfit, a different car) makes you harder to spot next time.

Everything below comes from two kinds of source. First, what we read on screen, frame by frame, in three launch-day streams (MELOO, Ben-Gun and Dalek). All three play the same early mission, Unwanted Attention, where you steal a car back from a police impound. Second, launch-day guides from PowerPyx, VGC and Cultured Vultures. More guides are on our GTA 6 guides hub.

The wanted stars: how the HUD works

ON SCREEN: seen in MELOO's, Ben-Gun's and Dalek's launch streams

The stars sit top right, with a row of six slots. In MELOO's launch stream, two are lit and four are dark during the impound getaway. Dalek reaches 3 stars while shooting at an officer by the impound gate. Ben-Gun's tow truck fight beside the Hamlet Police Department also hits 3 stars, and he gets the red wasted screen there.

The stars change color. They're white while the cops are chasing you and red once you've broken away and they're searching. The game spells this out in a hint with a red star next to it: "The cops are searching for you ★ and they have your description."

The search area (heat zone)

ON SCREEN: seen in MELOO's launch stream

When you break away, the pause map shows a big red-shaded zone. In MELOO's stream the tooltip on it reads: "The cops are actively looking for you in this area." Under it are three lines: VEHICLE KNOWN, APPEARANCE KNOWN and OUTFIT KNOWN. Your minimap also turns red while you're inside it.

The objective changes to "Lose the cops", and the game gives you two tips in a row:

  • "Lose the cops ★ by hiding or escaping their search area." (ON SCREEN, Dalek and MELOO)
  • "Try going off-road or along back streets to lose pursuing cops." (ON SCREEN, MELOO)
REPORTED (Cultured Vultures, Nov 18)

Cultured Vultures adds that if the cops don't see you for a while, the search radius shrinks until they give up. Reviews (PSU, COGconnected, TheSixthAxis) say the area stays "hot" for a while after a chase. If you go back too soon looking the same, they'll pick you up again.

How police remember you: outfit, face and car

ON SCREEN: seen in all three launch streams

Under the stars there's a row of red icons: a clothes hanger (your outfit), a head (your appearance) and a car (your vehicle). Each one means the police have that part of your description. The icons only show what they actually know. In Ben-Gun's stream, Jason is on foot during the tow truck fight and only the outfit and face icons are lit. Once he climbs into the tow truck and the cops see him drive, the car icon lights up too.

The game warns you that this sticks. After the respray in Ben-Gun's stream, a hint says: "Cops will recognize you faster in areas with heat if you haven't changed your appearance and clothing." So a new paint job only clears the car. Your clothes and face still give you away.

REPORTED (reviews) + PRE-LAUNCH
  • Witnesses matter. Police come when someone sees the crime, a victim reports it, or an alarm or camera catches it. Witness descriptions are read out over the police radio (Kotaku, TheSixthAxis, Wccftech reviews).
  • Other icons exist. Pre-launch guides also list a weapon, a CCTV camera and a two people icon (police looking for Jason and Lucia as a pair). We've only seen the outfit, face and car icons on screen so far.
  • Trackers. Some stolen cars have trackers that ping the police (Press Start, Kotaku reviews). When Jason scans cars in the impound with his phone, each one gets a card listing Access, Security and Tracker (ON SCREEN, Ben-Gun).
  • Hair counts. Rockstar's previews showed a new haircut breaking your description. Per the same previews, a hat or glasses alone may not be enough.

Criminal Profile: the other system

ON SCREEN: in-game hint in Ben-Gun's launch stream

Stars and your Criminal Profile are separate. The game tells you so during the impound fight: "Getting a wanted level doesn't affect your criminal profile, but how you choose to deal with the cops will." Stealing the car is fine. Gunning down every officer at the gate is what counts.

REPORTED (PowerPyx, Nov 18)

OUR TAKE: at the moment that hint appears in Ben-Gun's stream, while he's shooting at officers, a small purple devil icon pops up on the right of the screen. We think it marks his profile moving toward the violent end, but no source has confirmed what it means yet.

PowerPyx reports that at Psycho and Irredeemable the cops are more likely to shoot on sight and you can't surrender anymore. Jason and Lucia have separate profiles. To check yours, open the map, press L1 to reach the Jason & Lucia tab, and pick a character.

How to lose the cops, step by step

  1. Break line of sight. Turn off the main road. The game's own hint says off-road or back streets (ON SCREEN). Stars go red once you're out of sight.
  2. Get out of the red search area. Check the pause map. It's the zone that says "The cops are actively looking for you in this area" (ON SCREEN).
  3. Split up if you're a duo. Cultured Vultures dropped from 3 stars to 2 this way. Hold right on the d-pad and press Triangle while nearly stopped (REPORTED).
  4. Change what they know. Respray or ditch the car, switch outfits from your trunk (Loadout > Items), and take off or put on a cap and sunglasses (REPORTED, Cultured Vultures).
  5. Don't come straight back. The area stays hot. Come back with a different look, or wait (REPORTED, reviews).
OUR TAKE: the order matters. A Pay 'n' Spray inside the search area is no use to you (see below), so escape first, then change your look, then clean up the car.

The police impound (Unwanted Attention mission)

ON SCREEN: seen in MELOO's, Ben-Gun's and Dalek's launch streams

The game teaches all of this in Unwanted Attention. Its brief reads: "Work with Cal to recover a car from a police impound lot for Brian." The car is an Imponte '77 Phoenix (red, with a Leonida plate that reads "ASS MAN"), held at the Police Impound next to the Hamlet Police Department.

  • Cop scanner first. Cal sets Jason up with a cop scanner on the way. The hint reads: "You can now listen in on cop radio frequencies and will be notified of dispatch areas." (ON SCREEN, MELOO)
  • Scan the lot. The objective is "Search the impound for the Phoenix". Your phone shows each car's Access, Security and Tracker (ON SCREEN). VGC says you get in over a gap in the wall on the left side, and the Phoenix is chained to a tow truck you have to release (REPORTED, VGC).
  • The gate. After Ben-Gun was wasted, the retry hint read: "Gates can be opened quickly by shooting the button controls." (ON SCREEN). Dalek shoots his way out past the officers at the gate.
  • Keep the car alive. VGC says the mission fails if the Phoenix gets wrecked. Ben-Gun's screen flashed "Engine damaged" during the shootout, shortly before he went down.
  • Finish the job. Take the Phoenix to the Pay 'n' Spray, then deliver it to Brian. VGC says to grab the cash from the back, buy any outfit at the gun shop to clear your heat, and the mission ends once the heat is gone (REPORTED, VGC).

Pay 'n' Spray: what it does and what it costs

ON SCREEN: seen in Ben-Gun's, Dalek's and MELOO's launch streams

The hint is clear: "Pay 'n' Sprays can respray vehicles so the cops can't recognize them." On the map, the one used in the mission is listed as PAY 'N' SPRAY - HAMLET, "Garage offering repairs and resprays to lower recognition", next to Boone Speedway. It's a garage under a "Used Auto Parts" sign.

Inside, the menu has two services:

ServiceIn-game descriptionPrice seen
Repair"Repairs engine and collision damage."FREE in this mission, with a crossed-out list price: $440 on Ben-Gun's Phoenix, about $610 on Dalek's (read through the strike line)
Respray"Change the vehicle color to reduce recognition." A tag underneath reads "This vehicle has heat"FREE in this mission. Normal price not seen yet
  • You can't respray the same color. Red was locked with "Too similar to current color". Ben-Gun went green, Dalek went blue (ON SCREEN).
  • Red stars are fine. Both drove in with red (searching) stars still showing (ON SCREEN).
  • But not inside the search area. Cultured Vultures says you can't enter a Pay 'n' Spray inside the red search boundary, so set your route to one outside it (REPORTED).
  • It only fixes the car. Your outfit and face are still known, so change clothes too (ON SCREEN hint, above).

Normal Pay 'n' Spray prices: not confirmed yet. The two repair prices we saw were different, so OUR TAKE is that repairs cost more the worse the damage. No outlet has published respray prices outside the mission. We'll add them once we've seen them. Pay 'n' Spray also buys cars with Standard Trackers from Chapter 3 (PowerPyx; see our money guide).

Wasted and Busted

ON SCREEN: Ben-Gun's launch stream

The red wasted screen is back. Ben-Gun got it during the 3-star tow truck fight at the Hamlet impound. Because it was a mission, he restarted at the impound gate. We haven't seen a Busted screen on stream yet. Rockstar's previews showed one, and PowerPyx's note that surrender goes away at Psycho means surrendering is possible before that. Pre-launch reports also say any cash you're carrying can be lost when you're wasted or busted (Vice), so bank it once you can (see our beginner tips).

Tips for dealing with the police

OUR TAKE, built on the ON SCREEN and REPORTED facts above
  • Watch the icons, not just the stars. Each lit icon is something to change. Car icon: Pay 'n' Spray or a new car. Hanger: new outfit. Head: haircut or mask.
  • Keep spare outfits in your car's trunk for a quick change after a chase (REPORTED, Cultured Vultures and reviews).
  • Pick the right getaway car. Cultured Vultures found a Coquette struggles on muddy beach ground and suggests the Kamacho for its off-road grip. Bank jobs start at 4 stars, so plan the route first.
  • Delete the tapes. Destroying security tapes in a store's backroom means no CCTV description, and PowerPyx counts it toward a Professional profile.
  • Use limb shots, not kills, if you care about your profile. Kills push you toward Violent and Psycho, and those make cops shoot on sight.

Frequently asked questions

How do you lose a wanted level in GTA 6?

Break line of sight and get out of the red search area on the map. The in-game hint says: "Lose the cops by hiding or escaping their search area." Back streets and off-road routes help, and splitting up Jason and Lucia can drop a star.

How many wanted stars does GTA 6 have?

Six. The HUD shows six star slots, top right. Launch streams show 2 and 3 stars during the early impound mission. Bank vault robberies are reported to start you at 4.

Does Pay 'n' Spray remove your wanted level in GTA 6?

It changes your car's color "to reduce recognition", which clears the car from your description. Your outfit and face are still known. Cultured Vultures reports you can't enter one inside the red search area.

How much does Pay 'n' Spray cost in GTA 6?

It's free during the Unwanted Attention mission. Crossed-out repair prices of $440 and about $610 showed on two streams. Normal respray prices are not confirmed yet.

Does a wanted level affect your criminal profile in GTA 6?

No. The game says getting a wanted level doesn't affect your criminal profile, but how you deal with the cops does. Killing police pushes you toward Violent and Psycho.
