Weird Al Is a GTA 6 Radio DJ: Meet DJ Al on Vice City FM

Yes, that's "Weird Al" Yankovic you hear on the GTA 6 radio. He voices DJ Al, one of the hosts of Vice City FM, the station that plays 1980s hits. Kotaku's Zack Zwiezen recognised the voice while driving and confirmed it with Rockstar.

CONFIRMED BY ROCKSTAR (to Kotaku, Nov 18, 2026): Weird Al is a host on Vice City FM

What we know about DJ Al

  • Station: Vice City FM, the 80s hits station. You can pick it up while driving anywhere in Vice City and the wider state of Leonida.
  • Two hosts: Kotaku says the station has two hosts and Weird Al is one of them. The second host hasn't been named in the reports.
  • His act: a loud, silly on-air persona. He yells about songs and tells stories about his past. Consequence notes a song bringing back "memories... repressed memories", and DJ Al promising to keep playing 80s music until the 2080s.
  • Voice only: DJ Al isn't a character you can meet in the world. He's only on the radio.

Hear him for yourself in this in-game clip posted by Kotaku's Zack Zwiezen:

Are Weird Al's songs in GTA 6?

No, not that anyone has found. Kotaku says none of his originals or parodies are on the in-game radio, and "Dare to Be Stupid" isn't there either. Kotaku's writer hopes his music gets added if GTA 6 comes to PC.

THEORY: Rockstar has added songs to GTA radio after launch before (GTA Online updates), so a Weird Al track later isn't impossible. Nothing is announced

He teased it before launch

Kotaku points out that earlier in launch week Weird Al hinted on Bluesky that he was part of a big upcoming game. Now we know which one. Consequence picked up the story on launch day (Nov 19, 11:10 AM ET), crediting Kotaku.

How to hear DJ Al

  1. Get in any vehicle with a radio.
  2. Hold left on the D-pad to open the radio wheel, then switch stations with L1/R1 (LB/RB on Xbox).
  3. Pick Vice City FM and wait for the talk breaks between songs.
  4. On foot, buy headphones or earbuds and the radio plays through your phone (see our phone guide).
