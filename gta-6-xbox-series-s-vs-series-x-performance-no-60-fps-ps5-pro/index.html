GTA 6 on Xbox Series S Drops to 18 FPS, and Rockstar Says No 60 FPS on PS5 Pro

By vAnother Level · Updated 2026-11-18

GTA 6 runs at about 720p on Xbox Series S and can fall to 18-19 FPS in big explosive shootouts, while Series X holds roughly 1440p at a mostly stable 30 FPS. On the same day, Rockstar's technology production director Marc Chambers reportedly said the PS5 Pro is "not powerful enough" to run the game's simulation at higher frame rates, so there's no 60 FPS mode on any console.

REPORTED: Xbox numbers from Digital Foundry's analysis via Kotaku (Kenneth Shepard), Nov 18 2026

That fills the gap from this morning, when Digital Foundry's PS5 numbers were out but Xbox wasn't. Here's every console side by side, what Rockstar said, and which version you should play tomorrow if you have a choice. Full PS5 breakdown: Digital Foundry tech review.

Xbox Series S: what you lose

The Series S version renders at 720p, with some details showing up less often and lower-resolution textures. It aims for 30 FPS but rarely holds it once things kick off: regular combat and police chases sit around 25 FPS, big carnage pushes it to about 20 FPS, and Digital Foundry caught a brief dip to 18-19 FPS in one explosive shootout.

It's still the full game, same map, same story, same missions. Kotaku's practical advice for Series S owners: avoid stacking huge on-screen destruction if you want it to stay smooth.

Series S owner with a choice? If you also have access to a Series X, PS5 Pro or even Xbox Cloud Gaming on a good connection, those will feel noticeably smoother in chases and shootouts.

Xbox Series X: basically PS5 Pro-level smoothness

Series X runs at 1440p and, per Digital Foundry via Kotaku, its stability is similar to the PS5 Pro, with the same short drops in car chases, crowded streets and big firefights. The Pro still has the edge in image quality thanks to PSSR upscaling and ray-traced shadows.

What Rockstar said about 60 FPS

In an interview with Kotaku, Marc Chambers, technology production director at Rockstar Games, explained why the PS5 Pro doesn't get a faster mode. The base version uses ray-traced reflections and global illumination; the Pro adds ray-traced shadows and sharper image quality through PSSR. But the Pro's CPU is only slightly better than the base PS5's, and he said it "is not powerful enough to run the game's complex simulation at higher frame rates."

REPORTED: Chambers quote via Kotaku's interview, as relayed by GamingBolt, VGChartz and VGC, Nov 18 2026OFFICIAL: Rockstar's Nov 10 Newswire already confirmed a 30 FPS target on every console, PS5 Pro included

VGC's summary is that Rockstar has suggested 60 FPS won't come post-launch either. Rockstar hasn't promised a future performance patch, and no outlet we checked quotes a firm "never".

THEORY: a 60 FPS option is most likely to appear first in the PC version, which GamesBeat reports for Q1 2027 (not confirmed by Rockstar)

Which console should you play GTA 6 on?

  1. PS5 Pro: best image (PSSR + RT shadows) and the steadiest 30.
  2. Xbox Series X: very close on stability, a little softer image.
  3. PS5: same 1440p, but more frequent dips below 30.
  4. Xbox Series S: 720p and the roughest frame rate, but the whole game is there.

Frequently asked questions

What FPS does GTA 6 run at on Xbox Series S?

It targets 30 FPS at about 720p, but Digital Foundry measured around 25 FPS in normal combat, about 20 FPS in heavy carnage and a brief 18-19 FPS dip (reported via Kotaku).

Does GTA 6 have a 60 FPS mode on PS5 Pro?

No. Rockstar's Marc Chambers reportedly told Kotaku the PS5 Pro's CPU isn't powerful enough for the game's simulation at higher frame rates. Every console targets 30 FPS.

Is GTA 6 better on Xbox Series X or PS5?

Both render around 1440p at 30 FPS. Digital Foundry found Series X about as stable as the PS5 Pro, while the base PS5 dips below 30 more often (reported).

Will GTA 6 get a performance patch for 60 FPS?

Rockstar hasn't announced one, and VGC reports Rockstar suggested it won't come post-launch. The rumoured PC version is the likeliest place for 60 FPS.
