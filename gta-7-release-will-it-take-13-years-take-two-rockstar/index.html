Will GTA 7 Take 13 Years? What Take-Two and Rockstar Actually Said

By vAnother Level · Updated 2026-11-19

GTA 7 has not been announced, and there is no release date or window. But Rockstar's own studio heads have said the next game won't take as long as GTA 6 did, because the engine, tools and team built for it can be reused. Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick also says the "nature and velocity" of GTA content will look "dramatically different over the next decade".

GTA 6 launched on November 19, 2026, a little over 13 years after GTA 5 (September 17, 2013) and about eight years after Rockstar's last game, Red Dead Redemption 2 (October 26, 2018). So with launch day here, the question everyone is asking is how long the wait for the next one will be. Here's exactly what has been said, and what it does and doesn't mean.

OFFICIAL: no GTA 7 announcement, title or date from Rockstar or Take-Two

Rockstar: 'We're not going to rip it apart'

REPORTED: Bloomberg's GTA 6 making-of feature, via VGC (Oct 14, 2026)

In Bloomberg's making-of feature on GTA 6, Rockstar's studio heads were asked about the next game. Phil Hooker said: "The engine, the tools and the team we've built during this project will allow us to do things more quickly." He added that he's confident the studio can "go more sideways than upwards to build more compelling content".

Aaron Garbut put it more bluntly: "We foundationally changed a lot of stuff on this project. But now it's done, and we're not going to rip it apart." Coverage of the feature summed it up as Rockstar's next game not taking another eight years to make.

  • What they said: the GTA 6 tech is a foundation to build on, not something to rebuild from scratch.
  • The comparison Bloomberg drew: GTA Vice City came out about a year after GTA 3, because it was built on GTA 3's groundwork.
  • What they didn't say: what the next game is, or when it comes. VGC notes nothing suggests a Vice City-style one-year turnaround.

Take-Two's CEO: 'dramatically different over the next decade'

REPORTED: Zelnick to IGN alongside Take-Two's Q2 FY2027 earnings, via Insider Gaming and TweakTown (Nov 9, 2026)

Asked about that Bloomberg report, Zelnick told IGN that "the nature and velocity of the content we'll see from our iconic franchises, including GTA" will "look dramatically different over the next decade".

That line got turned into a lot of "GTA 7 confirmed faster" headlines, but read it carefully. "Velocity of content" doesn't have to mean a new numbered game. It could just as well mean bigger and more frequent updates, story expansions or online content for GTA 6. Insider Gaming made the same point: Zelnick did not promise a shorter cycle for a sequel, and did not name GTA 7 at all.

Why GTA 6 took so long

Between GTA 5 and GTA 6, Rockstar shipped Red Dead Redemption 2 in 2018 and kept GTA Online running with big updates for over a decade. TweakTown, citing the same reporting, notes Rockstar grew from 2,441 staff at Red Dead 2's launch to 4,711 across 12 offices. GTA 6 also rebuilt a lot of the core tech: Rockstar says it "foundationally changed" things this time, which is exactly the work it says it won't need to redo.

So when could GTA 7 come out?

THEORY: our read, not confirmed by Rockstar or Take-Two

Nobody outside Rockstar knows, and anyone giving you a year is guessing. Our read of the quotes: the first thing to come faster is probably more GTA 6 content (expansions and online), since that's the most literal meaning of "velocity of content". We've tracked what's already teased, like the locked casinos, zoo and graveyard Rockstar calls 'future', in our GTA 6 DLC page. A new numbered GTA built on the same engine would still be a huge project, but going "sideways" on finished tech means a gap closer to the old 3-5 years is at least possible, rather than another 13.

Remember Rockstar also has other series. Hooker's comment was about Rockstar's next game, which isn't guaranteed to be a GTA.

Frequently asked questions

Is GTA 7 announced?

No. Rockstar and Take-Two have not announced GTA 7, its name, or any release window.

Will GTA 7 take 13 years?

Rockstar says it shouldn't. Its studio heads told Bloomberg the engine, tools and team built for GTA 6 will let them work faster, and reports summed that up as the next game not taking another eight years.

What did Strauss Zelnick say about the next GTA?

He told IGN the 'nature and velocity' of content from Take-Two's franchises, including GTA, will look 'dramatically different over the next decade'. He gave no GTA 7 date.

How long did GTA 6 take?

GTA 6 came out on November 19, 2026, just over 13 years after GTA 5 (September 2013) and about 8 years after Red Dead Redemption 2.
