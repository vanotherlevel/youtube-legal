GTA Outside America? Rockstar's GTA 6 Lead Says 'Never Say Never', but GTA Already Did It in 1999

By vAnother Level · Updated 2026-11-11
Home › GTA 6 Guides

Could a future GTA leave America? "Never say never," says Rob Nelson, co-studio head of Rockstar North and one of GTA 6's leads, in an interview with The Guardian published today. He added the team still has "so much to do in America", so don't pack your bags for Tokyo yet.

Quotes from The Guardian's interview, as reported by TheGamer (November 11, 2026)

Nobody remembers: GTA already left America

  • 1999: Grand Theft Auto: London 1969. An expansion pack for the first GTA on PC and PlayStation, set in swinging-'60s London.
  • 1999: GTA: London 1961. A free PC add-on that went even further back.
  • Since then: every GTA city has been American: Liberty City, Vice City, San Andreas, and now Leonida in GTA 6.

So technically, a GTA outside the US wouldn't be a first. It would be the first in 27 years.

GTA 6 itself launches November 19 in Leonida, Rockstar's take on Florida. See when it unlocks in your time zone and our GTA 6 hub.

Frequently asked questions

Will GTA ever leave America?

Rockstar's Rob Nelson said 'never say never' but that there's still 'so much to do in America'. Nothing has been announced.

Has GTA ever been set outside the US?

Yes. GTA: London 1969 and GTA: London 1961 (both 1999) were set in London.
