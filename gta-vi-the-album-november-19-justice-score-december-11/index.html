GTA VI: The Album, the 34-song soundtrack from Rockstar Games and Atlantic Records, is out now, and it reportedly had Spotify's biggest opening day ever for a soundtrack: 29.85 million streams. The game's actual score, composed by Justice, is a separate album, Grand Theft Auto VI: Original Score, out December 11, 2026.

The Spotify record: 29.85 million streams on day one

Reports this week say GTA VI: The Album drew 29.85 million Spotify streams across its 34 songs on its first day, the biggest opening day for any soundtrack on the service. The number comes from the music account Pop Base and was picked up by Push Square and GGRecon.

REPORTED: Pop Base figure via Push Square and GGRecon. Neither Spotify nor Rockstar has published its own numbers

Treat it as an opening-day figure only. It shows how many people were curious in the first 24 hours, not how the album will hold up over the coming weeks. It also covers The Album alone, not the licensed songs on the in-game radio stations.

The Album: out now

OFFICIAL: Rockstar Games x Atlantic Records

A joint release from Rockstar Games and Atlantic Records. Reported artists include Travis Scott, Keith Richards, Cardi B, Morgan Wallen and Rauw Alejandro. Vinyl and CD editions were announced alongside streaming. It was set for launch week; outlets give November 19 or 20 as the streaming date.

Justice's Original Score: December 11

OFFICIAL: Rockstar music page

The French duo's score lands three weeks after the game. The single 'Pusher' (feat. Gesaffelstein) is already streaming. Justice played at GTA VI: The Festival in Miami on November 21.

The pre-load trailer already debuted a Tove Lo song: read Blood in Your Eyes.

Streaming vs physical: the album is streaming now. Physical copies were on a slightly different schedule: the official store listed a November 20 ship date for the limited vinyl, and Blood Records said it aimed to ship on or close to the 19th.

Don't mix the two records up: The Album (34 licensed original songs) is not the score. Justice's GTA VI: Original Score is 30 tracks plus digital bonus tracks, out December 11 on streaming, CD and vinyl.

Frequently asked questions

When does the GTA 6 soundtrack come out?

GTA VI: The Album is out now, released for launch week (outlets give November 19 or 20). Justice's Original Score releases December 11, 2026.

Did the GTA 6 soundtrack break a Spotify record?

Reportedly, yes: 29.85 million streams on its first day, the most for a soundtrack, according to Pop Base as reported by Push Square and GGRecon. Spotify and Rockstar haven't published their own figures.

Who made the GTA 6 score?

Justice, the Grammy-winning French electronic duo.

How many songs are on the GTA 6 album?

34 original tracks. The full list (reported by DJ Mag and VICE) opens with Justice feat. Michael McDonald, 'Starting From Zero', and ends with 'Overtown Lights' by Stanley Clarke, Yussef Dayes & Robert Glasper.

Is the GTA 6 vinyl sold out?

When checked on November 18, the $124.98 / £108.99 limited liquid-filled vinyl was reported sold out in the UK and EU, while the standard vinyl ($49.98) and CD ($19.98) were still listed. Stock changes often, so check the store.

Is the GTA 6 album the same as the radio stations?

No. The Album is 34 original songs made for the game. The in-game radio has many more licensed tracks, and Justice's score is a separate album out December 11.
