Two GTA 6 records, two dates. Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album, 34 original songs made for the game, releases on November 19, 2026, launch day. The game's actual score, composed by Justice, is a separate album: Grand Theft Auto VI: Original Score, out December 11, 2026.

The Album: November 19

OFFICIAL: Rockstar Games x Atlantic Records

A joint release from Rockstar Games and Atlantic Records. Reported artists include Travis Scott, Keith Richards, Cardi B, Morgan Wallen and Rauw Alejandro. Vinyl and CD editions were announced alongside streaming.

Justice's Original Score: December 11

OFFICIAL: Rockstar music page

The French duo's score lands three weeks after the game. The single 'Pusher' (feat. Gesaffelstein) is already streaming. Justice is also on the bill for GTA VI: The Festival in Miami on November 21.

The pre-load trailer already debuted a Tove Lo song: read Blood in Your Eyes.

Full GTA VI: The Album tracklist (all 34 songs)

REPORTED: DJ Mag (Nov 16, after vinyl images leaked, confirmed by sources close to the game) and VICE (Nov 18). Rockstar has not posted the full list itself.

Justice appear twice:'Starting From Zero' with Michael McDonald is track 1, and 'Flower In A Hurricane' with Tame Impala, SZA and King Gizzard & The Lizard Wizard is track 6. Six songs (marked ✅) have been streaming since September 17.

Streaming vs physical: the album hits streaming on November 19, launch day (OFFICIAL, Rockstar Newswire). Physical copies may land a day later: the official store lists a November 20 ship date for the limited vinyl, and VICE gives November 20 as the release date. Blood Records says it aims to ship on or close to the 19th.

Don't mix the two records up: The Album (34 licensed original songs) is not the score. Justice's GTA VI: Original Score is 30 tracks plus digital bonus tracks, out December 11 on streaming, CD and vinyl.

Frequently asked questions

When does the GTA 6 soundtrack come out?

GTA VI: The Album releases November 19, 2026. Justice's Original Score releases December 11, 2026.

Who made the GTA 6 score?

Justice, the Grammy-winning French electronic duo.

How many songs are on the GTA 6 album?

34 original tracks. The full list (reported by DJ Mag and VICE) opens with Justice feat. Michael McDonald, 'Starting From Zero', and ends with 'Overtown Lights' by Stanley Clarke, Yussef Dayes & Robert Glasper.

Is the GTA 6 vinyl sold out?

The $124.98 / £108.99 limited liquid-filled vinyl is reported sold out in the UK and EU (and per VICE). The standard vinyl ($49.98) and CD ($19.98) were still listed when checked.

Is the GTA 6 album the same as the radio stations?

No. The Album is 34 original songs made for the game. The in-game radio has many more licensed tracks, and Justice's score is a separate album out December 11.
