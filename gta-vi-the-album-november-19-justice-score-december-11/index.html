GTA VI: The Album Drops Nov 19; Justice's Original Score Follows Dec 11

By vAnother Level · Updated 2026-11-17
Home › GTA 6 › News
GTA 6News47Guides13Story & Characters4Missions1DLC & Updates1GTA 6 Online2Videos35

Two GTA 6 records, two dates. Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album, 34 original songs made for the game, releases on November 19, 2026, launch day. The game's actual score, composed by Justice, is a separate album: Grand Theft Auto VI: Original Score, out December 11, 2026.

The Album: November 19

OFFICIAL: Rockstar Games x Atlantic Records

A joint release from Rockstar Games and Atlantic Records. Reported artists include Travis Scott, Keith Richards, Cardi B, Morgan Wallen and Rauw Alejandro. Vinyl and CD editions were announced alongside streaming.

Justice's Original Score: December 11

OFFICIAL: Rockstar music page

The French duo's score lands three weeks after the game. The single 'Pusher' (feat. Gesaffelstein) is already streaming. Justice is also on the bill for GTA VI: The Festival in Miami on November 21.

The pre-load trailer already debuted a Tove Lo song: read Blood in Your Eyes.

Sources: Rockstar Games: GTA VI music, Gematsu, MusicRadar, Inven Global.

Frequently asked questions

When does the GTA 6 soundtrack come out?

GTA VI: The Album releases November 19, 2026. Justice's Original Score releases December 11, 2026.

Who made the GTA 6 score?

Justice, the Grammy-winning French electronic duo.
