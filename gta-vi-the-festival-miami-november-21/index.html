GTA VI: The Festival Set Times: Cardi B 7:45 PM, Travis Scott 12:40 AM (Miami, Nov 21)

GTA VI: The Festival is Saturday, November 21, 2026, at Mana Wynwood in Miami. Doors open at 4 PM ET, Cardi B is on at 7:45 PM, and Travis Scott closes the night at 12:40 AM Sunday with Don Toliver, Kodak Black and SoFaygo. Rockstar says every dollar from ticket sales goes to Miami charities.

OFFICIAL: event announced by @RockstarGames and PlayStation (Nov 9); set times and charity pledge posted by @RockstarGames (Nov 19-20)

*Most reports, including AllHipHop's write-up of Rockstar's post, put Metro Boomin on the second stage at midnight; a few list him on Stage 1. The Ca7riel & Paco Amoroso slot comes from festival set-time round-ups, not from Rockstar's post as quoted, so treat it as reported.

REPORTED: exact end times and the Ca7riel & Paco Amoroso slot come from set-time round-ups (TalkEsport, Stupid Dope), not a Rockstar quote

Every ticket dollar goes to Miami charities

Rockstar's set-time post ended with: "As a thank you to Miami, all proceeds from ticket sales will go to local Miami charities." A follow-up named nine groups, including The Miami Foundation, Hermanos de la Calle, Live Healthy Little Havana, the Martin Luther King Economic Development Corporation, the Coconut Grove Crisis Food Pantry and Miami Bethany Community Services, plus three linked to local firefighters and police. Rockstar has not said how the money is split.

Can you still get in? Is there a livestream?

  • Tickets: entry was by lottery through DICE (17+, two-ticket limit, non-transferable). The lottery closed November 10. Rockstar's page still lists a waitlist, but joining it does not guarantee entry.
  • Livestream: none announced. Rockstar has not said the show will be streamed, so the clips that fans post on X and TikTok are your best bet.
  • Merch: Rockstar says The Store at Mana Wynwood will carry special GTA VI gear on the day.
  • Venue: Mana Wynwood, 318 NW 23rd Street, Miami (reported ticket-holder entrance on NW 5th Avenue).

Why Miami, why now

GTA 6 launched worldwide on November 19 and Rockstar says it did $3.79 billion in day-one retail sales. The festival is the third real-world Vice City moment of launch week, after the Miami HEAT's Vice City night on November 18 and the release of GTA VI: The Album. It was put together with PlayStation and the team behind Miami's III Points festival, and most of the acts are on the game's soundtrack.

Sources: Rockstar Games on X (set times and charity post), AllHipHop, TalkEsport, Stupid Dope, Sportskeeda, Push Square, Shacknews.

Frequently asked questions

What time is Travis Scott at the GTA 6 festival?

12:40 AM ET on Sunday, November 22 (the late slot of the Nov 21 show), sharing the stage with Don Toliver, Kodak Black and SoFaygo, per Rockstar's set times.

What time does Cardi B perform at GTA VI: The Festival?

7:45 PM ET on Stage 1. Doors open at 4 PM.

Is GTA VI: The Festival livestreamed?

No livestream has been announced by Rockstar or PlayStation.

Where does the ticket money go?

Rockstar says all proceeds from ticket sales go to local Miami charities, including The Miami Foundation and Hermanos de la Calle.
