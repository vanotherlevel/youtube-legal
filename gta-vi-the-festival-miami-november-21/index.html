GTA VI: The Festival Hits Miami Nov 21 With Travis Scott and Cardi B

By vAnother Level · Updated 2026-11-17
Home › GTA 6 › News
GTA 6News43Guides12Story & Characters4Missions1DLC & Updates1GTA 6 Online2Videos35

Two days after launch, Vice City goes real. Grand Theft Auto VI: The Festival, presented by Rockstar Games and PlayStation, is a one-day live music event at Mana Wynwood in Miami on November 21, 2026, with two stages of artists from the GTA 6 soundtrack.

OFFICIAL: announced by @RockstarGames, Nov 9

Lineup

  • Travis Scott
  • Cardi B
  • Justice
  • Metro Boomin
  • Don Toliver
  • More artists from GTA VI: The Album

Tickets

Entry was by lottery, 17+ only. The ticket-purchase lottery reportedly closed November 10 at 4 PM ET, so if you didn't enter, you'll be watching the clips like the rest of us.

It's the second real-world Vice City night of launch week: the Miami HEAT play as Vice City on November 18.

Sources: Push Square, Shacknews, TweakTown, Inven Global.

Frequently asked questions

When is the GTA 6 festival?

November 21, 2026, at Mana Wynwood in Miami.

Who is performing at GTA VI: The Festival?

Reported performers include Travis Scott, Cardi B, Justice, Metro Boomin and Don Toliver.
