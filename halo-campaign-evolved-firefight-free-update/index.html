Halo: Campaign Evolved Is Getting Firefight in a Free Winter Update

By vAnother Level · Updated 2026-11-12
Home › Halo: Campaign Evolved

Firefight is back. Xbox is adding the fan-favourite wave mode to Halo: Campaign Evolved, the remake of the first Halo's story, as a free update this winter.

Announced at Xbox FanFest, reported by VGC, November 12, 2026
  • 4-player PvE: you and three friends hold out against waves of Covenant.
  • Free for everyone who owns the game.
  • No date yet: more details at Halo Fest, December 12-13. FanFest attendees can play it today.

Then vs now

Firefight debuted in Halo 3: ODST in 2009 and became a series staple in Reach. The original 2001 Halo never had it, so this remake is getting a mode the real thing never did. Campaign Evolved launched in July 2026, and VGC notes it may be the only game released under the Halo Studios name, after Xbox moved Halo development to Activision.
