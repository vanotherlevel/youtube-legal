Ex-Tekken Boss Katsuhiro Harada Calls Xbox Series S a 'Slightly Weaker Child'

By vAnother Level · Updated 2026-11-11
Home › Xbox Series S

Katsuhiro Harada does not hold back. The former Tekken boss, now head of SNK-owned VS Studio, said at the Console Game Developers Conference in Seoul that he wishes hardware like the Xbox Series S "wouldn't be released", calling it "a device so similar to Series X while having awkwardly lower performance".

Translated by Inven Global, reported by VGC (November 11, 2026)

The line everyone's quoting: making games for Series S is like doing "extra work for a slightly weaker child." He said it as a joke, with a senior Xbox representative in the audience.

Then vs now

Developers have grumbled about Series S since it launched in 2020 at $299 next to the $499 Series X, mostly about its smaller memory. Some games even launched later on Series S. It still gets the big ones, though: GTA 6 comes to Xbox Series X|S on November 19, and Rockstar has said it targets 30 FPS on every console.

Quotes translated by Inven Global, reported by VGC. Watch more on our YouTube channel: vAnother Level.

Frequently asked questions

What did Harada say about Xbox Series S?

He said he wishes it hadn't been released and compared optimizing for it to 'extra work for a slightly weaker child' (translated by Inven Global).

Yes, GTA 6 launches on Xbox Series X|S and PS5 on November 19, 2026.
