About the Application

AF YouTube Shorts Automation is a secure content management tool built for creators to streamline video publishing workflows, format metadata, and manage uploads efficiently using official Google APIs.

Features & Functionality

  • Automates short-form video tagging and title formatting.
  • Connects directly with authorized YouTube creator channels.
