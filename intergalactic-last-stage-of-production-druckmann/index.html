Intergalactic Is Playable and in Its 'Last Stage of Production', Says Neil Druckmann

By vAnother Level · Updated 2026-11-12
Home › Intergalactic: The Heretic Prophet

Naughty Dog's next game is further along than you think. Neil Druckmann says Intergalactic: The Heretic Prophet is "at the last stage of production, where we can play it, and we're refining and polishing it".

Comic Creators Block Party 2026, reported by VGC, November 12, 2026

He described it as a mix of three eras of the studio: the sci-fi and fantasy of Jak and Daxter, the pulp action of Uncharted and the serious drama of The Last of Us, with deeper gameplay systems than before. A full reveal is planned for next year.

Then vs now

Naughty Dog hasn't made sci-fi since the Jak and Daxter games of the 2000s. Intergalactic was first teased at The Game Awards in December 2024.

Quotes from Neil Druckmann, reported by VGC. Watch more on our YouTube channel: vAnother Level.

Frequently asked questions

When is Intergalactic coming out?

No release date yet. Druckmann says it's in the last stage of production, with a full reveal planned for next year.

Is Intergalactic playable?

Yes, internally. Druckmann says the team can play it and is polishing it.
