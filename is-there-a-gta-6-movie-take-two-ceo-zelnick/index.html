Is There a GTA 6 Movie? Take-Two's CEO Answers at Launch

No, there is no GTA 6 movie or TV show. Nothing has been announced, and Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick says the company is in no rush: he told Bloomberg it doesn't rule one out, but is "very, very selective".

With GTA 6 launching to record pre-orders and a story many reviewers called cinematic, the question is everywhere this week. Here's what Take-Two has actually said.

OFFICIAL: no GTA film or series has been announced by Rockstar or Take-Two

What Zelnick said on Bloomberg

REPORTED: The David Rubenstein Show (Bloomberg, Nov 12, 2026) via Notebookcheck

On The David Rubenstein Show, published by Bloomberg on YouTube on Nov 12, Zelnick was asked about a Grand Theft Auto movie or series. His answer: "We don't rule it out, but we're very, very selective."

  • Any adaptation would have to meet Take-Two's own creative standards, so a famous name alone isn't enough.
  • He did not say whether any film or TV project is being discussed inside the company.
  • Notebookcheck's write-up notes that even if one were in the works, it would likely be years away.

Launch week: 'We prefer to control our own destiny'

REPORTED: Variety, GTA 6 launch week

In a launch-week interview with Variety, Zelnick explained why GTA has never been adapted for film or TV, summed up in the headline quote: "We prefer to control our own destiny." The point is the same one he's made for years: Take-Two would rather keep full control of how its games are portrayed than hand GTA to a studio.

The closest thing to a GTA film so far is The Gamechangers, a 2015 BBC drama about the making of the series and the legal fights around it, starring Daniel Radcliffe. Rockstar and Take-Two had nothing to do with it, and Take-Two took legal action against the BBC over it in 2014.

Could GTA 6 become a movie one day?

THEORY: our take, not confirmed

GTA 6 is the most story-driven game in the series: two leads, a relationship you can shape and a Bonnie-and-Clyde arc across Leonida. That's exactly the kind of story studios want. But Take-Two's answer hasn't changed in seven years, and with GTA 6 likely to earn billions on its own, there's no money pressure to license it. If a show happens, expect it years from now, made on Take-Two's terms.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a GTA 6 movie?

No. Rockstar and Take-Two have not announced any GTA 6 film or TV series.

Will GTA ever get a movie?

Maybe one day. Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick told Bloomberg in November 2026 that he doesn't rule it out but is 'very, very selective', and any adaptation would have to meet Take-Two's creative standards.

Why has GTA never been made into a movie?

Take-Two wants full creative control. Zelnick told Variety the company prefers to 'control our own destiny', and in 2019 said full control would mean financing a film itself.

What is The Gamechangers?

A 2015 BBC drama about the making of GTA, starring Daniel Radcliffe. It was not made with Rockstar or Take-Two.
