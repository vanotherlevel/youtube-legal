Rockstar has not announced Tommy Vercetti for GTA 6. But it has put his name in its own store copy, his shirt on a gun skin, his first hotel in the pre-order art, and Vice City's release date on Lucia's revolver. Here is every clue counted down from ten to one, the rule that says Tommy never existed in this universe, the fan theories (labelled) and our honest odds.

Labels: OFFICIAL = Rockstar's own words or footage. SEEN = found in Rockstar's files by us. THEORY = a theory, not fact.

Where the series left Tommy

Tommy was a made man in the Forelli family. In 1971 he walked into an ambush, killed eleven men and got fifteen years in prison. Vice City is set in 1986: he gets out, is sent south to oversee a drug deal, and the deal is ambushed. By the end he has killed Ricardo Diaz, taken his mansion on Starfish Island and turned it into the Vercetti Estate, then killed Lance Vance and Sonny Forelli and taken over Vice City.

Ray Liotta voiced him, and Vice City sold more than 17.5 million copies. Tommy never dies in any game: in San Andreas (1992), Ken Rosenberg calls the Vercetti office and Tommy won't take his calls. That's the last time a GTA game says his name.

The rule: why he can't come back

Rockstar splits GTA into two universes. GTA 3, Vice City and San Andreas are one world; GTA IV, V and VI are another. Before GTA 5, Dan Houser said the old leads would be 'mythical characters' in the new world who never existed. The closest the new world got was a GTA IV graffiti wall: 'Remember Tommy, you're still my hero.'

Ray Liotta died in May 2022, and Rockstar posted a tribute calling him the iconic voice of Tommy Vercetti. Dan Houser, who wrote Tommy, left Rockstar in 2020 and says he wrote none of GTA 6.

Every clue, ten to one

  1. 10. Phil's Ammu-Nation. A Trailer 2 TV ad shows a man in a Phil's tank top. Vice City's Phil Cassidy lost an arm in 1986; this Phil has two. A son, or just the name.
  2. 9. The prison. Vice City opens with Tommy fresh out of prison; Trailer 1 opens with Lucia inside one. Trailer 2 is scored to 'Hot Together', a 1986 song: the year Tommy arrived.
  3. 8. The old map. Highway signs in the Extended Look point to Washington Beach and Ocean Beach, two district names from Tommy's map.
  4. 7. Brian Heder. Rockstar calls him a drug runner from the golden age of smuggling in the Keys, Tommy's era, and he wears a palm print shirt.
  5. 6. The Cheetah. Trailer 1 shows a white Cheetah with a 'CH33TAH' plate on Ocean Drive; the Ultimate Edition's '95 Grotti Cheetah is called an ode to Shore Drive.
  6. 5. The gecko. In Trailer 2's gift shop there's a blue metal gecko with white flowers, the pattern of Tommy's shirt. Rockstar has never commented: a strong maybe.
  7. 4. Ocean Beach. The pre-order pack gives a '55 Vapid Stanier and a garage by Ocean Beach, where Tommy's story started.
  8. 3. The Ocean View Hotel. The pre-order art puts Jason and Lucia under a pink neon 'Ocean View Hotel' sign: Tommy's first safehouse.
  9. 2. The original kingpin. The Vintage Vice City Pack's weapon pattern is 'inspired by Tommy Vercetti's iconic palm tree button-up': the first time GTA 6 material says his name.
  10. 1. The Vercetti Estate. The Ultimate Edition revolvers, the Hawk and Little Morgan, are described as 'sourced from the Vercetti Estate'. Not inspired by: sourced from.

The serial numbers

Lucia's revolver is engraved L Caminos with serial 291002: October 29, 2002, the day Vice City came out. Jason's is engraved J Duval with serial 311006: October 31, 2006, the day Vice City Stories came out. Kotaku called Jason's scoped revolver a lore mistake because it resembles a Vice City Stories gun Tommy never owned, but the serial is that exact game's release date.

What's missing

There's no Vercetti name on screen in any trailer, and no line where Jason or Lucia mentions Tommy. Some wikis claim a Starfish Island road sign in the Extended Look; we checked every highway sign and couldn't find one. A site claiming Rockstar ruled Tommy out has no Rockstar source behind it.

The theories

THEORY
  • The Vercetti Estate is in the game but someone new owns it.
  • The old games exist as movies inside this world, which would explain a two-armed Phil without breaking Rockstar's rule.
  • Jason is related to Tommy: around since Trailer 1, nothing official behind it.
  • If alive in 2026, Tommy would be about 76.

Frequently asked questions

Is Tommy Vercetti in GTA 6?

Not announced. Rockstar's own store copy names him for a weapon pattern and says the Ultimate Edition revolvers were 'sourced from the Vercetti Estate', but he hasn't appeared in any trailer.

Is the Vercetti Estate in GTA 6?

Rockstar's Ultimate Edition text says the revolvers come from it, so the place exists in GTA 6's world. Whether you can visit it is unconfirmed until launch.

Who voiced Tommy Vercetti?

Ray Liotta, who died in May 2022.

Are GTA 6 and Vice City in the same universe?

No. GTA 3, Vice City and San Andreas are one universe; GTA IV, V and VI are another.
