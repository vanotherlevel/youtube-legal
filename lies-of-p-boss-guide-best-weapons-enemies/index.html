Lies of P: How to Beat Every Boss, Best Weapons & Enemies

By vAnother Level · Updated 2026-11-12
Home › Guides › Lies of P

  3. Normal enemies

This guide covers every boss in Lies of P in the order you meet them, from the Parade Master to the Nameless Puppet, plus every optional mini-boss and all eight bosses of the Overture DLC (June 2025). For each fight you get where it happens, its elemental weaknesses and resistances, the attacks that end runs, and how to answer each one with a Perfect Guard, a dodge or positioning. After the bosses you'll find a weapon tier list with where to get each pick, the best Legion Arms, and a table of the normal enemies worth respecting.

Five rules that win most Lies of P fights. 1) Perfect Guard (block at the last moment) negates damage, builds the boss's stagger and slowly breaks its weapon; a normal guard still loses some HP, which you get back by hitting the enemy. 2) Fury attacks (the boss glows red) can't be blocked with a normal guard: dodge them or Perfect Guard them. 3) When a boss's HP bar gets a white outline it is groggy: land a charged heavy attack to knock it down, then press the Fatal Attack. 4) Match the element: Electric Blitz for puppets and machines, Fire for Carcass flesh, Acid for many armoured late-game bosses. Abrasives (Fire, Electric Blitz, Acid) let you add an element to any weapon. 5) Use the Specter at the summoning pool before each boss; it splits the boss's attention and makes punish windows far bigger.

Parade Master

Where: Krat Central Station Plaza, the first real boss, just after you leave the station. Weak to Electric Blitz, resists Fire. If you chose the Electric Coil Stick or picked up an Electric Blitz Abrasive, use it here.

  • Phase 1: punches, smashes, a forward charge and a belly flop. Watch his arm pull back before each swing and guard or sidestep. The body slam has a long recovery: sidestep it and hit him with a full combo.
  • Charge: he draws back then lunges. Dodge sideways, not backwards; it covers a lot of ground.
  • Stomp: dodge as the leg lifts, then punish while the foot is planted.
  • Don't stand directly behind him - his wide swipe still reaches you there.
  • Phase 2 (about half HP): he drops the cage and swings a huge makeshift mace. The range jumps a lot, so stay just outside the swing between your attack strings. The mace can be broken with repeated Perfect Guards (around eight).
  • Fury attack (red glow): back off or Perfect Guard it. Don't hold normal block.
  • Punish window: when his HP bar turns white, use a charged heavy attack to knock him down, then take the Fatal Attack.

Mad Donkey

Where: Alchemist Bridge on the way to Krat City Hall; the nearest Stargazer is Inside the House on Elysion Boulevard. He's a human Stalker with a big sword and no elemental weakness. He drops the Krat City Hall Key, so you need this win to continue.

  • Downward slash: sidestep either way or Perfect Guard it; it's the easiest hit to learn the parry timing on.
  • Wide horizontal swing: the biggest hitbox he has. Step back rather than dodging into it.
  • Rising uppercut: dodge backwards as he pulls the blade back.
  • Three-hit advancing combo: keep backing off or block it out, then hit him once it ends.
  • Fury attack: dodge or Perfect Guard. He has no armour to speak of, so dodging behind him after a combo sets up easy hits.
  • Approach: be patient. Two or three hits after each of his combos, then guard. He's a skill check for the Perfect Guard system more than a damage race.

Scrapped Watchman

Where: Krat City Hall Courtyard. Weak to Acid (and Strike), strongly resists Electric Blitz - leave the Coil Stick in the bag. Beating him gives the Overcharged Storage Battery; give it to Eugenie at Hotel Krat for the Fulminis Legion Arm.

  • Phase 1: long arm swings, sweeps, a jumping lunge and a grab. The arm pulls back clearly before each swing: guard or step out of the arc.
  • Grab: slow wind-up; step away or guard. Don't panic-dodge early.
  • Jump lunge: back off, let him land, then punish the recovery.
  • Phase 2 (about half HP): he electrifies. Melee hits can now Shock you, and he adds shock pulses around his body, ranged shock blasts and an electric rain.
  • Shock pulse AoE: back out of the radius as soon as the sparks gather.
  • Ranged blast: dodge left or right, not back.
  • Electric rain: the safest spot is underneath and behind him - get in close and attack his back while it falls.
  • Tip: a Shot Put or charged heavy attack when his bar goes white ends phase 2 fast. Bring Acid throwables or the Acid Abrasive if you have them.

King's Flame, Fuoco

Where: the furnace room at the centre of Venigni Works, after the Control Room; there's a summoning pool before the fire. Weak to Electric Blitz and Acid, heavily resists Fire. He drops the High-powered Flame Amplifier, which crafts the Flamberge Legion Arm.

  • Uppercut / uppercut smash: block or Perfect Guard the first punch, then step out of the smash.
  • Spinning fist lunge: it's directional - dodge to the side.
  • Flamethrower: stay close; it covers the area in front of him at range.
  • Overheat punch: dodge away from the impact zone.
  • Phase 2 Molten Flood: when he enters phase 2 he floods the room with molten metal. Hide behind one of the pipes, or run to the far side of the room if no pipe is near. Learn where the pipes are during phase 1.
  • Oil spill: stay off the oil puddles; his fire ignites them.
  • Fire cannon: keep moving or use pipes for cover. Once you're in melee range again you get free hits.
  • Approach: an Electric Blitz weapon or abrasive and a fire-resistance item make this much shorter. Overheat buildup is your real enemy, so don't trade hits inside the flames.

Fallen Archbishop Andreus

Where: Archbishop's Altar in the St. Frangelico Cathedral Library; the Stargazer is right before the doors. Weak to Fire and Pierce, resists Acid and Strike. His attacks inflict Decay, which also wears down weapon durability. Bring a Fire Abrasive, Thermite and a Decay cure.

  • Phase 1: a huge creature with claw combos, lunges, a body slam and a tongue whip.
  • Double and triple claw combos: block or Perfect Guard, then counter. The triple combo travels far, so don't assume you're safe at mid range.
  • Double claw lunge: dodge back or sideways, or Perfect Guard it.
  • Leg stomp (Fury): fast; dodge it or Perfect Guard.
  • Body slam: never stand under him.
  • Tongue whip: guard it; it builds Decay.
  • Phase 2: Andreus himself climbs out of the creature's back, giving the boss two 'fronts'. The creature keeps its moves and Andreus adds staff swings, a wing swipe, a chest blast and acid shots.
  • Chest blast: run from the first blast, then dodge the slam that follows.
  • Staff glows: a radial blast follows. Get out of range immediately.
  • Approach: stay close and slightly to the side; the acid shots are rare when you're tight on him.

Eldest of the Black Rabbit Brotherhood

Where: Malum District Town Hall. The leader of the Brotherhood swings a huge cleaver. No elemental weakness - every damage type is neutral.

  • Overhead smash (sometimes three in a row): guard and keep guarding until he stops; watch for the blade rising again.
  • Near-360 sideways swings: back away as soon as he starts swinging; the fast three-swing version catches people who counter early.
  • Uppercut swing: can knock you down. Block or step out, then counter.
  • Spin attack ending in an uppercut: back fully out of range.
  • Jump attack: dodge away from the landing zone the moment he leaves the ground.
  • Running charge from distance: sidestep it and hit his back.
  • Approach: he's slow to recover after big swings. Two hits and back to guard. Perfect Guards on the overhead smashes build stagger quickly.

King of Puppets and Romeo, King of Puppets

Where: Estella Opera House Stage, past the Opera House Stargazer and the burning chandelier hall; summoning pool before the arena. This is three health bars back to back: the King's two phases, then Romeo gets up and fights you himself.

  • King phase 1 - weak to Electric Blitz, Acid and Strike; resists Fire. Swipes, a low drag, a telegraphed wind-up smash (often twice) and a side jump. Step back from the smash and counter; don't stand under him when he jumps.
  • King phase 2 - weak to Acid and Strike (Electric Blitz drops to neutral). Drill hands into a Fury slam and sweep, air blasts, a tornado spin and delayed Ergo missiles.
  • Drill hands: either back off, or dodge through the opening strikes for free hits. The finishing slam is a Fury attack.
  • Tornado spin: stay close; the inside of the spin is safe and you can hit him.
  • Ergo barrage: the missiles explode after a delay - keep moving sideways rather than rolling into them.
  • Romeo - weak to Acid, Electric Blitz and Strike; resists Fire and Slash. A fast scythe fighter.
  • Scythe pull: his exposed eye glows red before he hooks your neck. Dodge sideways the instant you see the glow.
  • Scythe counter stance: he braces; don't hit into it. Use the pause to heal.
  • Meteor slash (Fury): he ignites the scythe, leaps and dives. Dodge backwards or roll through him.
  • Fire flurry (from half HP): three fiery triple-slash strings, then a big finisher. Run during the first three and parry the last, or interrupt the ignite with a Shot Put or Fable Art.
  • Approach: an Acid weapon or Acid Abrasive is the best single choice for all three bars. Strike weapons (Big Pipe Wrench, Krat Police Baton) also do well.

Champion Victor

Where: Grand Exhibition Gallery. Weak to Acid, absorbs Electric Blitz. The Acidic Crystal Spear (Path of the Pilgrim chest) is the classic answer here.

  • Charge: do not try to block it - a normal guard breaks and he follows with a heavy punch. Run out of its path or dodge at the last moment.
  • Hook swing and pummel: block or dodge; the pummel can turn into a Fury attack.
  • Stomp / jump stomp: dodge back or to the side and counter. In phase 2 the stomps come in pairs, so don't counter after the first one.
  • Jump punch: dodge towards him as he leaves the ground.
  • Five-hit beatdown: he walks forward clenching a fist first. Block the string or dodge each hit; punish after the fifth.
  • Grab: he raises both palms first - move away early. If caught, dodge right after the throw to avoid the finishing stomp.
  • Phase 2 (about half HP): he sheds his attachments and charges more. After you stagger him he has two 'fake' recoveries: one feint and one wild flurry. Wait until he actually falls or the Fatal Attack prompt appears before going in.

Green Monster of the Swamp and Puppet-Devouring Green Monster

Where: Barren Swamp Nest, past the Barren Swamp Bridge Stargazer. Two bars: the Green Monster, then it fuses with puppet parts and becomes the Puppet-Devouring Green Monster. Weak to Pierce, and to Fire in the first phase; resists Acid and Strike. Many guides recommend switching to Electric Blitz for the second form; the stat sheet doesn't show it, so treat that as community advice.

  • Decay everywhere: arm flings, antenna whips and bile spray all build Decay. Bring cures or the Attribute Resistance Ampoule.
  • Antenna whip (four lashes): block them, or dash in close so they land behind you.
  • Antenna spear: a thrust that pulls you in if it hits. Dash sideways.
  • Burrow: it digs under and erupts below you. Watch the trail and move off it.
  • Double-hand smash: get out of range or dodge behind it.
  • Charge (sometimes twice): run perpendicular to it; expect a second charge.
  • Bile spray: it turns slowly - circle behind it during the wind-up.
  • Approach: use the summoning pool. The Specter holds its attention and you can work its flank with Fire on phase 1.

Corrupted Parade Master

Where: Cerasani Alley, on the route to Hotel Krat, heading back from the Krat Central Station Lobby. The first boss returns as a Carcass-corrupted version. Weak to Fire and Pierce, resists Acid and Strike. Beating him (end of Chapter 9) also gives you the Star's Chrysalis, which unlocks the Overture DLC.

  • He keeps the original charge, stomp, swipe, body slam and mace patterns - the same answers work (sidestep the charge, punish the slam's long recovery, never stand behind him).
  • New: body-slam crawl - a slow crawl with repeated ground slams. Keep distance until it ends.
  • New: decay grab - he engulfs you, dealing big damage and Decay. Don't stand in front of him while he's winding up with nothing else happening.
  • New: exploding minions - kill or avoid them quickly; don't fight him with them near you.
  • Approach: Fire Abrasive or Thermite, a charged heavy when his bar turns white, then Fatal Attack. This fight hits harder than it looks; keep your guard stamina up.

Black Rabbit Brotherhood

Where: Relic of Trismegistus Combat Field, reached from the Relic of Trismegistus Entrance. A four-on-one: Eccentric (spear and grenades), Battle Maniac (sword, red scarf, pull string), Youngest (double blades) and finally the Eldest, who only arrives after the other three fall. No elemental weakness.

  • Separate them. Use the pillars and garbage piles to break line of sight so only one can reach you at a time.
  • Get all three low, then finish them close together. The Eldest joins after the third rabbit dies, and the fight becomes a much easier one-on-one.
  • Battle Maniac: dodge sideways from his pull string; block his tracking slash-and-thrust strings rather than rolling.
  • Youngest: dodge her leaping knife throw as soon as she jumps; Perfect Guard her double-blade charge.
  • Eccentric: guard his grenades, dodge to his side during spear combos for a backstab, and block his high-tracking jump attack.
  • Eldest: move out of range when the shockwave animation starts; after a stagger he flails wildly, so wait behind cover until the Fatal Attack window comes back.
  • Tools that help: a Specter, the Deus Ex Machina mine Legion Arm, and throwables while the Eldest is stuck on the garbage.

Laxasia the Complete

Where: the Prayer Room in Arche Abbey, next to a Stargazer. Weak to Acid, resists Electric Blitz (strongly in phase 2; Shock is effectively useless there). The Acidic Crystal Spear blade or the Acid Abrasive is the strongest answer.

  • Phase 1 (sword and shield): heavy, slow combos with long electric reach. Repeated hits break her shield.
  • Slam spam: run away, or chain Perfect Guards for huge stagger damage if you're confident.
  • Uppercut spam: sprint away, then dodge the final sweep.
  • Electric stab grab: after the taunt, dodge into the thrust.
  • 360 charged swing: back off to full range.
  • Phase 2: she sheds her armour, moves much faster and fights in the air with lightning.
  • Storm cloud lightning: dodge the bolts, or Perfect Guard them to send them back at her.
  • Electrical blast: it covers the whole arena close to her - get out early.
  • Approach: patience in phase 1, then stay mobile in phase 2 and only punish after her combos fully end.

Simon Manus, Arm of God and Awakened God

Where: Cradle of the Gods, at the top of Arche Abbey. Two bars. Weak to Electric Blitz and Acid, resists Fire. Phase 2 can inflict Disruption, which kills you outright when the bar fills, so carry a Disruption cure or resistance item.

  • Phase 1 (Arm of God): a giant mace. Horizontal swings are best handled by staying close and guarding.
  • Leap smash: watch where he jumps and get out of the landing area.
  • Overhead / fiery overhead: back off as he lifts the mace; the fiery version leaves a fire pool - circle behind him.
  • Circle of Doom (expanding ring): very high damage. Back away immediately.
  • Phase 2 (Awakened God): mostly ranged magic.
  • Pillar: a ghostly pillar fires along his facing - move off his line.
  • Celestial strikes: beams from a cloud - keep circling in one direction.
  • Celestial orbs: a row of orbs - find the gap and stay in it.
  • God's Smite: a giant hand from the sky. Leave the marked area completely.
  • Approach: chase him in phase 2. His ranged attacks are much harder to deal with at distance than up close.

Nameless Puppet

Where: Under the Abyss. You only fight it if you refuse to give Geppetto P's heart (required for the 'Free from the Puppet Strings' and 'Rise of P' endings). Weak to Electric Blitz and Acid, resists Fire. Many players call it the hardest base-game boss.

  • Phase 1 (longsword): fast combos, dashes and thrusts. Guard or Perfect Guard the swings; sidestep thrusts and move behind it. It can heal itself with Pulse Cells up to twice - so keep pressure on.
  • Jump AoE: back out of the blast circle when it lands.
  • Charge dash: get off its line; it knocks you down.
  • Phase 2 (two swords): much faster, adds triple energy beams, an energy wall and windmill strings.
  • Energy beams and wall: dodge sideways out of the lines or leave their range.
  • Bloody Scream: after you land a Fatal Attack, wait a moment before following up - an explosion triggers.
  • Approach: quick weapons and combos beat charged heavy attacks here. Electric Blitz Abrasive plus Fulminis is a strong pairing.

Overture DLC bosses

Overture DLC - released June 2025

How to start Overture: finish Chapter 9 by beating the Corrupted Parade Master to get the Star's Chrysalis, then travel by Stargazer to Path of the Pilgrim (Chapter V) and watch the cutscene. You can swap back to the main story at any time. The DLC has eight bosses; only the Premetamorphic Green Hunter is optional. A general rule for the DLC: its Carcass-type bosses are almost all weak to Fire and Pierce and resist Acid.

Tyrannical Predator (Overture)

Where: Krat Zoo, Savanna Experience Center - the first DLC boss. Weak to Fire and Pierce, resists Acid and Strike. Most of its attacks come out of the front, so play to its side.

  • Wide bite / rushing bite: strafe sideways, not back.
  • Sliding tail sweep (180 degrees): strafe right and move behind it.
  • Slam combo (jaw slam into body slam): the first hit is slow - Perfect Guard it to open the boss up.
  • Triple threat: lunge, two bites, then a delayed claw slam. Guard the first hits and Perfect Guard the slow last one.
  • Tail whip: comes from its right; stand near the head to avoid it.

Markiona, Puppeteer of Death (Overture)

Where: Krat Zoo, Greenhouse Interior. She fights together with her Arche Puppet. Markiona herself is weak to Acid and resists Electric Blitz; the puppet is weak to Electric Blitz and Acid and resists Fire. Focus Markiona - the puppet is mostly a distraction.

  • Puppet dash: a straight mace charge - strafe sideways.
  • Three magic swipes: block the first two, Perfect Guard the last to open her up.
  • Sphere barrage: use the first two waves to build stagger, then run from the third.
  • Golden Strikes: parry the puppet's slams. Each parry shrinks the final explosion; two or more parries make it small enough to keep attacking Markiona through it.
  • When the puppet's HP runs out it freezes and regenerates - that's your window to burst Markiona.
  • Below 50% HP both get aggressive and add repeated aerial dives. Strafe out of the dive line and block her sphere slam at the end.

Veronique, Leader of the Sweepers (Overture)

Where: Zelator Underground Facility, Coolant Control Room. A fast hammer fighter whose weapon catches fire in her second phase and builds burn. Her elemental weakness is not well documented; one guide recommends a Decay (Acid) build.

Weakness not confirmed
  • Rapid hammer strings: she chains strikes quickly. Learn the rhythm and block or Perfect Guard the whole string instead of dodging out mid-combo.
  • Leaps and slams: the impact footprint is small - sidestep and punish the landing.
  • Her hammer can be broken with Perfect Guards; Perfect Guard Grindstones speed this up.
  • Phase 2 flames: the burning hammer builds Overheat. Her flame-fan attacks leave gaps you can use to heal or attack.
  • Weapon choice: a heavy, high-stagger weapon helps. Guides suggest charging a Cataclysm shot while she walks in from the far end of the arena.

Two-Faced Overseer (Overture)

Where: Zelator Underground Facility, Elixir Research Zone. Weak to Fire and Pierce, resists Acid and Strike. A battle-axe brute with two states: green (calm) and red (enraged).

  • Green state: slow, basic axe swings. Perfect Guard them to build stagger; two staggers push it into the red state.
  • Red state: faster, longer strings. Two more staggers return it to green.
  • Axe stab: the most dangerous move - it impales and drags you across the arena. Dodge sideways when it raises its right hand.
  • Four-hit swing combo: Perfect Guard when it raises its left hand.
  • Stomp and six-slam sequence: retreat during the wind-up; if you're stuck close, block or Perfect Guard each slam.
  • Stab-and-pull moves: block the stab, then strafe sideways for the pull.

Premetamorphic Green Hunter (Overture, optional)

Where: from the Ancient Ruins Excavation Site, take the elevator down while facing the wall to spot a hidden walkway partway down the cliff; follow it until you drop into the arena. The only optional DLC boss. Weak to Fire and Pierce, resists Acid and Strike. It uses an evolved version of the Green Monster moveset, and its life-draining grabs heal it.

  • Body slam grab: it rears up, walks forward and slams - if it lands, it devours you. Strafe sideways as it stands up, or Perfect Guard.
  • Tentacle impale (fast double stab): strafe right or block both hits.
  • Fury swipes (five claws, red glow): Perfect Guard the telegraphed ones; don't just hold block.
  • Decay spray: the hitbox reaches its sides too - sprint out of range, don't hug its flank.
  • Burrow: run from the disturbed ground, then dodge as it erupts.

Anguished Guardian of the Ruins (Overture)

Where: the end of the Ancient Ruins Excavation Site, just after the Destroyed Tram Station. Weak to Fire and Pierce, resists Acid. Defeating it gives the Ergo that trades for the Pale Knight gunblade.

  • Phase 1: telegraphed claw sweeps and trident stabs. Break its two weak points (head horns and left hand) for short stuns.
  • Stomp: only hits its right side - strafe right.
  • Trident combo: block the sweep, then strafe right away from the two stabs.
  • Phase 2 (about 60% HP, after a cutscene): adds light beams from the markings on its body.
  • Charged beam: it sweeps slowly left to right, so approach while strafing right.
  • Consecrated Ground glyph: run out of the centre before it explodes.
  • Approach: stay close or behind it - almost everything it does is aimed forward.

Lumacchio, Leader of the Bastards (Overture)

Where: Shipwreck Exterior, Archon Fleet. A duellist with an umbrella in phase 1, then a decaying gastropod beast with a giant claw from around 40% HP. Phase 1 is a human - backstabs work and he staggers easily. Phase 2 is weak to Fire and Pierce and resists Acid and Strike, and inflicts Decay.

  • Laugh tells: a high laugh means three explosive canisters - move towards him. A low laugh means canisters followed by a lunge - close the gap and block the lunge.
  • Four-hit umbrella combo: Perfect Guard the whole string instead of rolling out.
  • Smoke bomb ambush: don't chase him into the smoke.
  • Transformation: he heals and is invincible while changing. Use the time to heal and apply a Fire Abrasive.
  • Phase 2 pouncing claw: Perfect Guard it to stagger him - the best opening of the phase.
  • Fury slam: drift to his side or behind. Spinning claw: keep your distance and let it whiff.
  • Approach: a fast weapon you can stop after one or two hits, Fire for phase 2, and Decay protection (the Piercing Hatred Amulet nullifies Decay).

Arlecchino, the Blood Artist (Overture final boss)

Where: Monad Charity House, Rose Garden Entrance - the DLC's final boss and, for many players, the hardest fight in all of Lies of P. Guides report he's vulnerable to Electric Blitz; we haven't seen a stat sheet confirming it. Beating him and finishing Overture rewards Monad's Rose Sword.

Weakness reported by guides, not confirmed by stats
  • Phase 1: sickle-and-sword slashes with a steady rhythm, including a repeated four-hit combo. Parrying works better than dodging; Perfect Guards build towards a big stagger window.
  • Phase 2: after his bar empties he revives with two sabres and blood magic.
  • Blood Barrage: a leap followed by burning blood projectiles. Move sideways across the spread rather than backwards.
  • Punish windows: he pauses often between combo sequences. Use those pauses for charged heavies and Fable Arts, not for healing greed.
  • Approach: use any summon or ally offered for the fight, an Electric Blitz weapon or abrasive, and keep your healing slots full going in.

Good blade and handle combos

Because the blade sets damage and the handle sets moveset and scaling, the strongest builds put a high-damage or elemental blade on a handle whose moveset you like. A few combinations that work well:

  • Acid build: Acidic Crystal Spear blade on the Puppet's Saber or Wintry Rapier handle, crank set to Advance. You keep a fast, safe moveset while Radiate stacks Acid damage. Swap the blade to the Electric Coil Stick for puppet-heavy areas.
  • Fire build: Salamander Dagger blade on the Booster Glaive or Dancer's Curved Sword handle for reach, Advance crank. Ideal for Andreus, the Corrupted Parade Master and most Overture Carcass bosses.
  • Strength build: Bone-Cutting Saw or Live Puppet's Axe blade on the Big Pipe Wrench handle with a Motivity crank. Slow, but every hit staggers and the Payback Swing handle art punishes after a guard.
  • Parry build: any strong blade on a handle with Guard Parry (Wintry Rapier, Dancer's Curved Sword, Black Steel Cutter). Guard Parry turns a timed deflect into a heavy counter.
  • Early game: buy the Electric Coil Stick on Elysion Boulevard and put its blade on your starting handle. It carries you through Parade Master, Fuoco and the puppet zones.

Normal enemies

Krat's enemies fall into a few families, and each family has a reliable weakness. Puppets are machines: hit them with Electric Blitz and Strike. Carcasses (including the Corrupted Puppets, which count as Carcasses) are flesh: use Fire. Alchemists are human fanatics who throw Disruption and Decay; kill the throwers first. Stalkers are human hunters with fast combos and no elemental weakness.

Frequently asked questions

How many bosses are in Lies of P?

The base game has 14 story boss encounters (several, like King of Puppets into Romeo, have multiple health bars) plus 11 optional mini-bosses. The Overture DLC adds 8 more bosses, one of which is optional.

What is the hardest boss in Lies of P?

In the base game most players name the Nameless Puppet (only fought if you refuse Geppetto) or Laxasia the Complete. In the Overture DLC, Arlecchino, the Blood Artist is widely called the hardest boss in the whole game.

How do I start the Overture DLC?

Finish Chapter 9 by beating the Corrupted Parade Master to get the Star's Chrysalis, then travel by Stargazer to Path of the Pilgrim (Chapter V) and watch the cutscene to begin. You can return to the main story at any time.

What are Fury attacks and how do I counter them?

Fury attacks are moves where the enemy glows red. A normal guard won't stop them, so either dodge them or land a Perfect Guard by blocking at the very last moment.

What does Perfect Guard do in Lies of P?

A Perfect Guard is a block timed just before a hit lands. It takes no damage, builds the enemy's stagger meter and wears down its weapon, which can break after enough Perfect Guards.

Which element should I use against bosses?

Electric Blitz is best against puppet bosses like Parade Master and Fuoco. Fire is best against Carcass bosses like Andreus, the Corrupted Parade Master and most Overture bosses. Acid is best against Champion Victor, Laxasia, Romeo and the Scrapped Watchman.

What is the best weapon in Lies of P?

For a first playthrough the Two Dragons Sword (Green Monster Ergo) and Holy Sword of the Ark (Fuoco Ergo) are top picks, and the Acidic Crystal Spear blade is the best elemental option. In Overture, Monad's Rose Sword is the standout reward.

Can I get both the weapon and the amulet from a boss Ergo?

No. Each boss Ergo can be traded once, for either the special weapon or the amulet.
