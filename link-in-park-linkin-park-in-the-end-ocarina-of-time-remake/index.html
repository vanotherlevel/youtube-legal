"Link-in Park": Someone Played Linkin Park's In the End on the Ocarina in the Zelda Remake

By vAnother Level · Updated 2026-11-08
Home › Zelda: Ocarina of Time Remake Guides

Three days after The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time remake launched on Nintendo Switch 2, one of its biggest moments online has nothing to do with dungeons. X user @StevenxTCG posted a short clip of Link standing in a field and playing Linkin Park's "In the End" on the ocarina, captioned "Ladies and gentlemen, I present Link-in Park".

Ladies and gentlemen, I present Link-in Park

— Steven (@StevenxTCG) November 6, 2026

As of November 8 the original post has passed 4.6 million views, with about 130,000 likes and 21,000 reposts. It was picked up by bigger accounts too: Culture Crave shared it (over 600,000 views) and ComicBook.com posted it as "Link in a park playing Linkin Park's In the End in Ocarina of Time."

How is this possible in Ocarina of Time?

The ocarina in Ocarina of Time has always had free play: you pull it out and every button is a note, so you can play anything, not just the songs Zelda and Sheik teach you. The remake keeps that, and as long as you keep pressing notes, Link just keeps playing whatever you input. That's how players have been recreating real songs since the remake launched on November 5.

Ocarina of Time's main notes are limited, so covers like this take practice: the player has to fit the melody into the ocarina's range and time each note by hand. Fans in the replies joked that "it starts with one" (a nod to the song's opening line), and plenty of people paid tribute to Linkin Park's late singer Chester Bennington.

Frequently asked questions

What song did Link play on the ocarina?

Linkin Park's "In the End" (2001). X user @StevenxTCG performed it with the free-play ocarina in the Ocarina of Time remake and called it "Link-in Park".

Can you play any song on the ocarina in the Ocarina of Time remake?

Yes. Free play lets you play any notes you press, so you can perform real songs as long as they fit the ocarina's notes.

When did the Ocarina of Time remake come out?

November 5, 2026, exclusively on Nintendo Switch 2.
