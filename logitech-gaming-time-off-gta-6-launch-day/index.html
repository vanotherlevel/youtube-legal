Logitech Is Giving Employees a Day Off to Play Games, Right Before GTA 6 Launches

By vAnother Level · Updated 2026-11-11
Home › GTA 6 Guides

Your boss might not let you skip work for GTA 6. Logitech's will. Logitech has rolled out "Gaming Time Off": an extra day of holiday for employees to play a new game of their choice, just ahead of GTA 6's November 19 launch. The company said that if staff spend it exploring Vice City on launch day, "you won't hear any complaints from us."

Reported by Fortune and VGC, November 10-11, 2026

What Logitech said

  • CEO Hanneke Faber (to Fortune): "It's time to retire the stereotype that gaming is a waste of time."
  • Chief people officer Elaine Laird: "Play is not the opposite of work."
  • What it is: one extra day off to play a new game. Reports don't say whether every employee in every country gets it.
