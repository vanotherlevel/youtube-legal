Madden 27 Begs You "Don't Delete!" Before GTA 6's 212 GB Lands

By vAnother Level · Updated 2026-11-18

EA is asking Madden NFL 27 players not to uninstall the game to make room for GTA 6. An in-game message reads "Don't delete! The crew is assembling for a high-stakes run" and told players to wait for a signal on 11/17. That's two days before GTA 6 needs up to 212 GB on PS5 and Xbox.

OFFICIAL: the in-game message and the Madden NFL 27 social post are EA's own words, as shared by players and reported by Push Square, CharlieIntel and GGRecon

What the Madden 27 message says

Players started sharing screenshots on X on November 14. The notice greets you when you boot Madden NFL 27:

  • "Don't delete! The crew is assembling for a high-stakes run."
  • "Await the signal from our insider on 11/17."
  • "Do not leak this transmission!"

It wasn't the first hint. On November 10, the day GTA 6 pre-loading started, the official Madden NFL 27 account posted: "Don't let 210GB get between you and Your League." The heist wording and the "insider" line are a clear nod to GTA, and the "do not leak" joke lands in the same week the GTA 6 hacker pleaded guilty.

What happened on 11/17: The Heist

REPORTED: program details via MUT.GG (Nov 17, 3 PM ET). EA hasn't said outright that this is the 11/17 signal

On November 17, a limited Madden Ultimate Team program called The Heist went live, themed around the GTA VI hype. That fits Push Square's guess that the teaser was for Ultimate Team.

Why EA is worried: GTA 6's file size

Rockstar says the GTA 6 pre-load needs up to 212 GB, depending on your console. GGRecon worked out that this is about a quarter of a 1 TB PS5 (around 842 GB usable). On the original 825 GB PS5 it's closer to a third. Datamined preload files point to an install of about 223 GB once day-one data lands, and update 1.002.001 already added up to 1.59 GB on Xbox.

Madden NFL 27 came out on August 13. Players in Push Square's comments put its install at about 50-55 GB, which makes it one of the first games people look at deleting. That figure is REPORTED, and it changes with every patch.

Need space but want to keep Madden? On PS5, check Settings > Storage > Console Storage > Games and Apps and sort by size. Captured video clips and old PS4 copies of games you also own on PS5 are often the easy wins. Deleting a game is never permanent: you can download it again from your library, with its latest patch.

Our take

It's a fun bit, and it's also a real problem. As Push Square points out, keeping players once GTA 6 is out is a genuine headache for live-service games, and every one of them is fighting for the same SSD space and the same free evenings. A Field Pass that ends on November 25 means you have to keep Madden installed through GTA 6's first week to get the Urlacher card. That's probably the whole point.

Frequently asked questions

Why does Madden 27 say "Don't delete"?

EA added an in-game message asking players to keep Madden NFL 27 installed ahead of GTA 6, which needs up to 212 GB. It teased a 'high-stakes run' with a signal on November 17.

What was the Madden 27 11/17 signal?

A limited Ultimate Team program called The Heist went live on November 17, with a 92 OVR Brian Urlacher as the top reward. It ends November 25 at 6 PM ET. EA hasn't formally confirmed it was the teased signal.

How much space does GTA 6 need?

Rockstar says the pre-load needs up to 212 GB depending on the console. That's about a quarter of a 1 TB PS5 and about a third of the original 825 GB PS5.

If I delete Madden 27 for GTA 6, do I lose it?

No. You can download a game again from your library at any time. You'll just need to download its latest update again too.
