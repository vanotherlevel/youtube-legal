The Miami HEAT Will Become "Vice City" the Night Before GTA 6 Launches

By vAnother Level · Updated 2026-11-10
Home › GTA 6 Guides

The night before GTA 6 comes out, Miami turns into Vice City for real. Rockstar announced "A Night in Vice City": on Wednesday, November 18 at 7 PM Eastern on the NBA on ESPN, the Miami HEAT "trade Miami for Vice City" when they host the Milwaukee Bucks.

Announced by Rockstar Games; details at HEAT.com/ViceCity

A Night in Vice City. On the eve of the release of Grand Theft Auto VI, tune in to the NBA on ESPN Wednesday, November 18 at 7PM Eastern to see the HEAT trade Miami for Vice City when they host the Milwaukee Bucks.

— Rockstar Games (@RockstarGames) November 9, 2026

Then vs now

Vice City has always been Miami in disguise, all the way back to GTA: Vice City in 2002, which leaned on 1980s Miami Vice style. Now the real Miami team is wearing the fictional name, a day before players get to drive through Leonida's version of it. Rockstar's launch week also includes GTA VI: The Festival in Miami on November 21.

More launch week: New Zealand plays 18 hours early, the Tove Lo song in the new trailer, GTA 6 hub.

Announced by Rockstar Games and the Miami HEAT. Watch more on our YouTube channel: vAnother Level.
