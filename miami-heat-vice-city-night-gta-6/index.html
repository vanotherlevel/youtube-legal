The night before GTA 6 comes out, Miami turns into Vice City for real. Rockstar announced "A Night in Vice City": on Wednesday, November 18 at 7 PM Eastern on the NBA on ESPN, the Miami HEAT "trade Miami for Vice City" when they host the Milwaukee Bucks.

Announced by Rockstar Games; details at HEAT.com/ViceCity

A Night in Vice City. On the eve of the release of Grand Theft Auto VI, tune in to the NBA on ESPN Wednesday, November 18 at 7PM Eastern to see the HEAT trade Miami for Vice City when they host the Milwaukee Bucks.

— Rockstar Games (@RockstarGames) November 9, 2026

The Vice City court has been revealed

OFFICIAL: court shown by the Miami HEAT, Nov 17 (via ESPN)

The one-night court is pink, white and blue hardwood with "Vice City" at centre court, "I ♥ Vice City" on a baseline, and a GTA VI logo near the dunker spot on each side. It's only being used for this one game.

The Vice City jersey

OFFICIAL: unveiled Nov 6 (Heat x Rockstar x Cactus Jack)
  • A black reworked version of the HEAT's "Vice" alternates, with pink and white accents
  • "Vice City" across the chest and a VI nod
  • Travis Scott's flipped Nike Swoosh, with six pink stars (the GTA wanted level) and Cactus Jack branding at the hem
  • A jock tag listing Rockstar Games, the HEAT and Cactus Jack
  • On-court and Swingman versions go on sale in limited quantities on game day (Nov 18)

What's at the arena on game night

  • The "Vice City" collection goes on sale two hours before tip-off
  • GTA VI-inspired cars on the West Plaza, plus photo spots around the arena and East Plaza
  • Official GTA VI music playing in the building
  • Cluckin' Bell and other GTA-series fast food at select concession stands
  • The "Welcome to Vice City" sign lit on the Kaseya Center roof

The Heat store's Court Culture "Vice City" collection (tees from about $45, hoodie $100) was already on sale before the game, according to GTA Intel's price list. Check MiamiHeatStore.com for current stock.

REPORTED: prices from GTA Intel's Oct 6 list

The players are in on it

Giannis Antetokounmpo, now with Miami and facing his old Bucks team, told ESPN: "I heard that we have special jerseys and special court." Klay Thompson, a long-time GTA player, joked about streaming GTA VI in the jersey.

Then vs now

Vice City has always been Miami in disguise, all the way back to GTA: Vice City in 2002, which leaned on 1980s Miami Vice style. Now the real Miami team is wearing the fictional name, a day before players get to drive through Leonida's version of it. Rockstar's launch week also includes GTA VI: The Festival in Miami on November 21.

More launch week: New Zealand plays 18 hours early, the Tove Lo song in the new trailer, GTA 6 hub.

Frequently asked questions

When is the Miami HEAT Vice City game?

Wednesday, November 18, 2026 at 7 PM Eastern on the NBA on ESPN, against the Milwaukee Bucks.

What does the Vice City court look like?

Pink, white and blue hardwood with Vice City at centre court, I ♥ Vice City on a baseline and a GTA VI logo near each dunker spot.

Can I buy the Miami HEAT Vice City jersey?

Yes, in limited quantities from game day, Nov 18. It's black with pink and white accents, Travis Scott's flipped Swoosh and six pink stars.

Is GTA 6 out the night of the HEAT game?

Not yet. GTA 6 launches November 19; the game is the night before.
