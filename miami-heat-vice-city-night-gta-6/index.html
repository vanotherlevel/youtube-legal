The night before GTA 6 came out, Miami turned into Vice City for real. On Wednesday, November 18, the Miami HEAT hosted the Milwaukee Bucks on the NBA on ESPN in one-night-only uniforms, on a one-night-only GTA VI court, in a full Rockstar takeover of Kaseya Center called "A Night in Vice City".

Here's everything that was confirmed: the court, the difference between the jersey the players wore and the one fans could buy, what Rockstar gave the team, and the numbers behind the Heat's Vice City merch.

Announced by Rockstar Games; details at HEAT.com/ViceCity

A Night in Vice City. On the eve of the release of Grand Theft Auto VI, tune in to the NBA on ESPN Wednesday, November 18 at 7PM Eastern to see the HEAT trade Miami for Vice City when they host the Milwaukee Bucks.

— Rockstar Games (@RockstarGames) November 9, 2026

The Vice City court has been revealed

OFFICIAL: court shown by the Miami HEAT, Nov 17 (via ESPN)

The one-night court is pink, white and blue hardwood with "Vice City" at centre court, "I ♥ Vice City" on a baseline, and a GTA VI logo near the dunker spot on each side. The three-point arcs are neon pink with the GTA VI logo and palm trees, and the court was used for this one game only.

The Vice City jersey

OFFICIAL: HEAT x Rockstar Games x Nike x Cactus Jack (details via SportsLogos.Net, Nov 18)

There's been no word on an online release of the fan jersey yet, per Yahoo Sports, so anyone who missed the game is stuck with the resale market for now. We'll update this page if the Heat put it online.

What's at the arena on game night

  • The "Vice City" collection went on sale two hours before tip-off
  • GTA VI-inspired cars on the West Plaza, plus photo spots around the arena and East Plaza
  • Official GTA VI music played in the building all night
  • Cluckin' Bell and other GTA-series fast food at select concession stands
  • The "Welcome to Vice City" sign lit up on the Kaseya Center roof

NBC Miami adds that Burger Shot and Cluckin' Bell stands had already been showing up at Heat games for weeks (arena tour guests keep asking if Burger Shot is open: it's only open on game and event nights), and a special halftime show was planned. Miami-Dade County reportedly struck a $975,000 deal to put the "Vice City" sign on top of the arena, where airport travellers can see it.

The Heat store's Court Culture "Vice City" collection (tees from about $45, hoodie $100) was already on sale before the game, according to GTA Intel's price list. Check MiamiHeatStore.com for current stock.

REPORTED: prices from GTA Intel's Oct 6 list

The players are in on it

Giannis Antetokounmpo, now with Miami and facing his old Bucks team, told ESPN: "I heard that we have special jerseys and special court." Klay Thompson, a long-time GTA player, joked about streaming GTA VI in the jersey.

REPORTED: NBC Miami, Nov 18

On Tuesday, Rockstar gave every Heat player a custom GTA VI console and controller plus a copy of the game. The Heat say they spent more than two years planning the collab.

  • Erik Spoelstra (Heat coach): "I embrace it. I've loved Vice for this organization."
  • Bam Adebayo (Heat captain): "The culture of Miami is special and getting to see everyone experience that through the game is what's so exciting."
  • Jennifer Alvarez (Heat SVP of brand): "We're all just running on pure adrenaline."

The Vice City merch, by the numbers

  • The Heat's Vice City collection launched on October 2
  • About 30% of orders came from outside the US
  • It ships to 60 countries
  • This was the Heat's fourth Vice-themed court; the Vice uniforms started in 2017-18 (SportsLogos.Net)

It doesn't stop at the arena: NBA 2K27 has a Vice City Edition with Bam Adebayo on the cover. See our NBA 2K27 x GTA 6 page.

Then vs now

Vice City has always been Miami in disguise, all the way back to GTA: Vice City in 2002, which leaned on 1980s Miami Vice style. Now the real Miami team is wearing the fictional name, a day before players got to drive through Leonida's version of it. Rockstar's launch week also includes GTA VI: The Festival in Miami on November 21.

More launch week: New Zealand plays 18 hours early, the Tove Lo song in the new trailer, GTA 6 hub.

Frequently asked questions

When was the Miami HEAT Vice City game?

Wednesday, November 18, 2026, the night before GTA 6 launched, against the Milwaukee Bucks on the NBA on ESPN.

What does the Vice City court look like?

Pink, white and blue hardwood with Vice City at centre court, I ♥ Vice City on a baseline and a GTA VI logo near each dunker spot.

Can I buy the Miami HEAT Vice City jersey?

The "Vice City" fan jersey was sold only at the Nov 18 game, in limited numbers, one per ticket holder. There is no word on an online release yet. The players wore a different black "Heat" jersey.

Is GTA 6 out now?

Yes. GTA 6 launched worldwide on November 19, 2026, the day after the HEAT game.

What did Rockstar give the Miami HEAT players?

Custom GTA VI consoles and controllers plus a copy of the game, handed out on November 17 (NBC Miami).
