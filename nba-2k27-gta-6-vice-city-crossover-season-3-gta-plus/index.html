NBA 2K27 x GTA 6: Vice City Takes Over Season 3 on Nov 27

The first GTA 6 crossover is official: NBA 2K27 Season 3 is a Vice City takeover, running from November 27, 2026 to January 15, 2027 (11 AM ET). And if you have GTA+ on PS5 or Xbox Series X|S, you can play the NBA 2K27 Standard Edition at no extra cost for that whole window.

OFFICIAL: dates, GTA+ offer and Pro Pass rewards from 2K's NBA 2K27 Season 3 page

2K announced it on November 16, three days before GTA 6 unlocks. It's Take-Two putting its two biggest series in one place, and it lands eight days after launch day.

What's in NBA 2K27 Season 3: Vice City

OFFICIAL: Season Pro Pass rewards (2K)
  • Macca the Gator Green Release
  • Nike x Cactus Jack "Vice City" Swingman Jersey
  • Vice City Pink T-Shirt
  • Cluckin' Bell Employee Outfit
  • MyCAREER rewards from Level 1 to 40, plus new Vice City cards and a Premier Exchange card in MyTEAM
REPORTED: from Complex and TweakTown's write-ups of the announcement
  • A Vice City-style park in The City: beachfront courts, neon hotels, supercars and a Macca the Gator statue.
  • The NBA Arena becomes the Sahara Arena for the season.
  • Vice City and Los Santos jerseys, plus Burger Shot and Cluckin' Bell outfits.
  • Events: Conquer Leonida, the timed Vice Run, Wear & Earn, and Neon vs Noir, a 5v5 Vice City vs Los Santos event that Complex says has a four-point "Wanted Star" spot late in each quarter.

The NBA 2K27 Vice City Edition

REPORTED: 2K's Season 3 page doesn't list the edition's price

Complex reports a digital NBA 2K27 Vice City Edition for $39.99 (an introductory price) on PS5, Xbox Series X|S, Nintendo Switch 2 and PC, sold from November 27 to January 15. Bam Adebayo, the Miami HEAT center, is on the cover. Reported contents: the Season 3 Pro Pass, a Vice City T-shirt, boosts, and an instant 95 OVR Adebayo.

One outlet quoted an $80 price instead, so check your store before you buy.

GTA+ members: NBA 2K27 at no extra cost

OFFICIAL: 2K's terms
  1. Nov 27 to Jan 15 (11 AM ET): GTA+ members on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S can play NBA 2K27 Standard Edition at no additional cost.
  2. Same window: buy the Standard Edition at 20% off on the platform tied to your GTA+ membership, if you want to keep it. The discount can't be combined with other offers.
  3. Dec 17 to Mar 31, 2027 (noon ET): claim the GTA+ Bonus Pack: a blue Boat Hat and Oversized T-Shirt for MyCAREER, 30 Skill Boosts (5 games) and 15 Gatorade Boosts (5 games).
Season Pro Passes and Hall of Fame Passes are sold separately. 2K says they need the base game, an internet connection and an NBA 2K account, aren't available in South Korea, Belgium or Brazil, and reset at the end of the season.

It was teased back in August

Players spotted it early: a Season 3 arcade cabinet with a Rockstar logo showed up in the NBA 2K27 Season 1 trailer in August. It also lines up with the Miami HEAT's Vice City night on November 18, where the HEAT wear the same Nike x Cactus Jack Vice City uniforms.

THEORY

Neither 2K nor Rockstar has said whether any of this links to rewards in GTA 6 itself or GTA 6 Online. Until they do, assume the crossover is NBA 2K27-only. More launch-week stories are on the GTA 6 news hub.

Frequently asked questions

When does the NBA 2K27 GTA 6 crossover start?

Season 3, the Vice City takeover, runs from November 27, 2026 to January 15, 2027 at 11 AM ET, according to 2K.

Is NBA 2K27 free with GTA+?

Yes, for the season. From November 27 to January 15, GTA+ members on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S can play NBA 2K27 Standard Edition at no additional cost, and can buy it at 20% off to keep it.

How much is the NBA 2K27 Vice City Edition?

Complex and TweakTown report $39.99 digital, with Bam Adebayo on the cover. 2K's Season 3 page doesn't list the price.

Does the NBA 2K27 crossover give rewards in GTA 6?

Nothing official says so. The announced rewards are all inside NBA 2K27.
