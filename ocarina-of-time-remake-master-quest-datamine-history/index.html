Master Quest Is Hiding in the Ocarina of Time Remake's Files, and It's Been 'Coming' Since the 64DD

By vAnother Level · Updated 2026-11-08
Home › Zelda: Ocarina of Time Remake Guides
On this page
  1. What the dataminers found
  2. Master Quest has been 'coming' for 25+ years
  3. Should you wait for it?

The Ocarina of Time remake launched on Nintendo Switch 2 on November 5 without Master Quest, the harder remixed version of the game. Three days later, dataminers say it's sitting in the game's files. If that sounds familiar, it should: Master Quest has a habit of showing up in places it wasn't announced.

Datamine, not confirmed by Nintendo

What the dataminers found

Dataminer MrCheeze posted on Bluesky that the remake contains Master Quest references, including strings like `_MQ`, `IsMQModeExist`, `NewGameMQ` and `ReleaseMQ`. Fellow dataminer OatmealDome was also reported looking into the files.

More details are surfacing about the Zelda: Ocarina of Time Master Quest datamine, including that it's unlocked by owning DLC rather than being an update, world is mirrored, and dungeons are modified

— Nintendo Everything (@NinEverything) November 7, 2026
  • Unlocked by DLC ownership, not a free update, according to the reports.
  • Mirrored world, like the 3DS version's Master Quest.
  • Modified dungeons, which is the whole point of Master Quest.
  • The data is reportedly incomplete, so it may not be finished.

Before launch, series producer Eiji Aonuma didn't rule Master Quest out, but said he didn't think they would announce anything specific at this time. Kotaku confirmed the remake launched without it. So right now this is code in the files, not an announcement.

Master Quest has been 'coming' for 25+ years

Here's the part almost nobody is talking about. Master Quest has never once arrived the normal way:

EraWhat happened
Late 1990s, Nintendo 64DDNintendo announced Ura Zelda, a 64DD disk that would patch Ocarina of Time with remixed dungeons. The 64DD flopped in Japan and Ura Zelda was never released.
2003, GameCubeIt finally appeared as Master Quest on a bonus disc (Ocarina of Time + Master Quest), given away as a Wind Waker pre-order bonus and in some GameCube bundles. It was never sold in stores.
2011, Nintendo 3DSOcarina of Time 3D included Master Quest, now mirrored, but you had to beat the main game first to unlock it.
2026, Switch 2 remakeLaunched without it. Days later, the files suggest a mirrored Master Quest tied to DLC.

Fans still argue about whether the GameCube Master Quest was the real Ura Zelda or a quicker level remix made to sell pre-orders. Either way, it's been a bonus, an unlock, and now possibly DLC, but never just part of the game.

Should you wait for it?

No. Master Quest has always been something you play after finishing the normal game, and nothing is announced. Play the remake now, and if Nintendo announces Master Quest DLC, it'll be a second run through harder, mirrored dungeons. Frequently asked questions

Is Master Quest in the Ocarina of Time remake?

Not playable yet. The game launched without it, but dataminers found unfinished Master Quest code that appears to be unlocked by owning DLC. Nintendo hasn't announced anything.

What is Master Quest?

A harder version of Ocarina of Time with remixed dungeons. It started as the cancelled 64DD expansion Ura Zelda, came out on a 2003 GameCube bonus disc, and returned mirrored in Ocarina of Time 3D (2011).

Will Master Quest be free?

Unknown. The datamine reportedly ties it to DLC ownership rather than a free update, but nothing is confirmed.
