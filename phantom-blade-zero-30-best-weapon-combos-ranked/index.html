The 30 best weapon combos in Phantom Blade Zero, ranked from 30 to 1. Every entry is either two main weapons together or a main weapon with a Phantom Edge. Soul Tempered weapons count as their own weapon, so Shadowless Edge gets its own spots, separate from the Sanguine Twin it's made from.

The top 5

#1 Scarlet Wraithblade + Jagged Steel (weapon + weapon). Every swap refills your Sha-Chi.

#2 White Serpent + Aureate (weapon + weapon). My S tier combo.

#3 Wild Oriole Sword + Armybreaker (weapon + weapon). Both phases in a little over three minutes.

#4 Shadowless Edge + Baishan Stormswords (weapon + weapon). Thunder for pressure, red blades for the kill.

#5 Freezing Chaos + Whiteshadow (weapon + weapon). Freeze him, swap back, and burst.

Four of the top five have their own full test videos on the channel: White Serpent + Aureate, Wild Oriole Sword + Armybreaker, Shadowless Edge + Baishan Stormswords and Infernal Rouge + Blazing Edge at #13.
