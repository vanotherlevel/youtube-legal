Phantom Blade Zero: How to Beat Every Boss, Best Weapons & Enemies

By vAnother Level · Updated 2026-11-12

This guide covers every boss in Phantom Blade Zero in the order you meet them: all 56 distinct bosses across 62 fights, including the optional duels, the Long Pillar trials and the true-ending finale. Each one has its location, the attacks that kill people and the counter to each. After the bosses come the best weapons and Phantom Edges with where to get every one, then every normal enemy type and its weakness.

Phantom Blade Zero is S-GAME's kung fu action RPG. It launched on PS5 and PC on October 29, 2026, after a delay from the original September 9 date. Everything here assumes the November 4 patch (PC version 1.0302256). That patch made Hua's grab attacks come less often on Wayfarer and Pathbreaker but did not change weapon numbers, so the weapon advice still holds.

The five rules that beat every boss: 1) Blue flash = parry. Wait for the glint to fade, then press. A clean parry costs you no Sha-Chi and drains theirs. 2) Red flash = dodge, right away. It can't be blocked or parried. Dodge on time and you Ghoststep behind the boss with a free punish window. 3) Black or grey screen flash = dodge, never block. Blocking or parrying it can wipe out your whole Sha-Chi bar. 4) Break the Sha-Chi bar, then swap weapons mid-combo. The swap extends the combo and refills your own Sha-Chi. 5) Phases checkpoint. On most multi-phase bosses, dying in phase two puts you back at phase two, so a long fight is never a total loss.
Don't miss these: grab the Reflex Device early (Valley Sha-Chi Tower Base bell, Weeping Creek Valley: put the Furnace Inspection Crank in the furnace). It widens your parry and perfect-dodge windows by 15%, or 25% at +2. Beat You Loong before Flame. Find all five of Mu Xiaokui's ribbons before her fight in the Village of Lu. For the true ending, beat Zhu Yuan and Bi Feng and clear all five Long Pillars. Enemies and bosses don't respawn, so no drop can be farmed in one playthrough.

About the weakness lines: the element weaknesses below come from one fan database (metabot.gg's boss and enemy tables) and we haven't checked them all in game. Treat them as a useful hint, not gospel. Elements come from weapons and Phantom Edges: Fire (Flaming Mane, Fire Meteor, Sanguine Reach), Frost (Whiteshadow, Freezing Chaos), Lightning (Thunder Spear, Baishan Stormswords), Poison (Flying Dagger). You Loong's Deluge makes enemies Wet, which makes them weaker to Lightning and Ice.

1. Zuo Shang, the Order's Shadow Sovereign (Prologue)

Where: the opening tutorial duel. Required. The fight is scripted to end in a cutscene, so you can't really lose. Use it to learn the rhythm. Don't swing into his sword strings. Let each string play out, parry the hits you can read, then counter once he stops. Learn here which flashes are blue and which are red, because every later boss is built on that.

2. The Hunt, Hunter of the Order (both fights)

Where: first on Old Man Gen's moving cart in the Prologue (scripted), then for real at the Great Waterfall in Weeping Creek Valley. Required. Weakness (data): Lightning +30%.

  • Cart fight: just survive. Guard or parry when he closes in and dodge the grab. Don't chase him around the cart.
  • The hook grab is by far his biggest damage. It's a red flash: dodge it every single time, then punish the recovery. The Dread Axe from Peng Meng is a great punish here.
  • Phase two, The Hunt, Sixfold Scythe: six scythes and much faster chained combos. Treat each link on its own: parry the blue ones, dodge the red ones, and don't try to dodge a whole string at once.
  • Approach: stay put at the start and let him come to you. Phase two checkpoints, so if you die there you restart at phase two.
  • Drop: Hunter's Chain Sickle (S-tier Phantom Edge that also grapples across gaps), 20,000 Remnant Dust.

3. Jagged Steel, the Frenzied Brute

Where: Weeping Creek Valley, near the Rustblade Pavilion bell. Required. Weakness (data): Lightning +16%. He's the parry and perfect-dodge teacher. His heavy overhead and sweeping swings are slow and readable, so stay close and never trade hits during a swing. Dodge late, then punish the long recovery. Use his red flashes to practise Ghoststeps; a few clean parries break his Sha-Chi. Drop: Jagged Steel, a Bleed sword that stays S tier all game.

4. Peng Meng, the Axemaster

Where: Weeping Creek Valley, The Axemaster's Fall bell. Required. Weakness (data): Poison +30%. He teaches the flash system better than anyone.

  • Blue axe combos: parry every hit. This is where you build his Sha-Chi damage.
  • Red grab: it lands about 0.6 seconds after the flash. Dodge sideways and you Ghoststep behind him for a free combo.
  • Low diving lunge: backing straight off doesn't work. Sidestep as he lands.
  • Ground-slam combo: the first swing can't be parried, so dodge it, then parry the rest of the combo.
  • Drop: Dread Axe, your first Phantom Edge and the best one in the game.

5. Aberrant Three-Faced Puppet (optional)

Where: Weeping Creek Valley, inside a Challenge Rift. Optional. Weakness (data): Fire +30%. You can find it early, but on a first visit it's far too tanky. Come back with upgraded weapons. It moves through mirrors and portals, so watch where it comes out before you commit, then parry its strings. It's a long fight: safe parries beat a damage race. Drop: Three-Headed Doll (story item). Its end-game quest, Triple Threat, leads you to Yucong (boss 12) and the Freezing Chaos.

6. Hua, Deadly Betrothed (optional)

Where: Weeping Creek Valley, Flesh Lily Fields under the first Sha-Chi Tower. Look for the hidden opening in the tower machinery and slide down the wooden bridge. Optional, and one of the hardest fights in the game for how early it is. Weakness (data): Fire +30%.

  • Four-hit string: it always ends on a blue finisher. Block or parry the first three hits and perfect-parry the fourth.
  • Red grab: dodge. The November 4 patch made Hua's grabs less frequent on Wayfarer and Pathbreaker, but not on Hellwalker or 66 Days.
  • Flesh Lilies: they explode. Walk or dodge clear before you re-engage, and never fight next to one.
  • Long-reach cut and low sweep: dodge these rather than blocking.
  • Don't over-commit: she has a counter-swing, and she gets more dangerous as her health drops. Take two or three hits, then reset.
  • Drop: Scarlet Wraithblade (A-tier two-hander), Flesh Lily.

7. The Gold-Masked One (Long Pillar duels 1 to 5, optional)

Where: five Long Pillar statues across the map. Optional, but all five are needed for the perfect true ending, which makes him effectively required if you want everything. Most players rank him the hardest boss in the game. He only takes real damage after a perfect parry, a perfect dodge or a Sha-Chi break, and he gets faster at each pillar.

  • Sword and polearm strings: heavy use of both flashes. Parry blue, dodge red, and play reactively.
  • The red grab sets up a lethal follow-up. Never block it, always dodge it.
  • Never get greedy after one parry. Take the punish the game gives you, then defend again.
  • Bring sustain: the Outlaw Warblade heals on every hit, which makes long attempts much more forgiving.

8. Hua Qianxiao, the Sword Fanatic

Where: Crimson Forest, Pangzhen Bridge Overlook. Required. Weakness (data): Lightning +16%. His sword combos are fast and long, so this is a stamina fight. Hold block through his strings to make him burn stamina, and parry only the hits you're sure of. When he puts up his bubble move, smash it with the Dread Axe. Once his stamina runs out, finish the Sha-Chi bar and unload. Drop: The Fanatic's Broken Blade, which you can restore into the Drunken Sword later.

9. Jing Ye, the Outlaw

Where: Crimson Forest, near the Outlaw Trap bell. Required. Weakness (data): Frost +16%. The fight opens on horseback and runs several phases (three, by one guide's count), and progress saves between them. If you're caught in front of a mounted charge you get flattened: dodge sideways, then hit him as he turns around. Only punish in the gaps between his attack sequences. Drop: Outlaw Warblade, an S-tier two-hander that heals you on every hit.

10. Stone Hand Iron Fever

Where: Crimson Forest. Required. He turtles behind a heavy guard, and light attacks bounce off it. Don't spam lights. Parries drain his Sha-Chi faster than any combo, and heavy or Sha-Chi combos crack the guard open. Drop: Iron Gauntlets, an S-tier Phantom Edge whose punches boost your main weapon's damage.

11. Bai Chilian, Lady of the Twin Serpents

Where: Crimson Forest, the chapter finale. Required. Weakness (data): Fire +16%. Phase two is named the Sacrificial Serpent.

  • Blue twin-sword strings: a parry dance. Perfect-parry every hit for big Sha-Chi damage.
  • Red serpent strikes: they go straight through your block. Dodge on time and Ghoststep behind her.
  • Her patterns change, so long combos get you caught. Two or three hits, then reset.
  • Phase two checkpoints.
  • Drop: White Serpent & Crimson Viper (S-tier dual swords), Resonance Talisman.

12. Yucong, No. 34 Twisted Puppet (optional)

Where: Crimson Forest, Threadbound Great Tree (the Mirrorwood). It only appears when you return during the Mirage Estate chapter, through the Triple Threat quest. Optional. Weakness (data): Fire +30% (the database lists Steel, Frost and Chaos forms of Yucong). Its red slash strings are fast, and it punishes early swings. Wait them out: parry blue, dodge red, then punish. Drop: Freezing Chaos (S-tier sword), Chaos Iron Fragment II, and it completes The Four Freed.

13. Zhuo Bi, the Gatekeeper

Where: Village of Lu, guarding the gate into the village. Required. Weakness (data): Lightning +30%. His giant shield soaks everything from the front and blocks arrows, so don't waste ranged attacks. Parry the shield slam as it comes down, then use heavies and the Dread Axe to smash his guard. Swap weapons mid-combo once it breaks. Drop: Bastion (shield Phantom Edge).

14. Master Bi Yuan of the Tianxuan Sect

Where: Village of Lu, Statue Grounds, in a burning courtyard. Required. Weakness (data): Fire +16%. He's a true swordmaster, and almost every chain can be parried.

  • Close sword chains, including the green crescent sweep: parry each cut.
  • Sidestep lunge (around 800 damage): dodge it as he turns back in.
  • Teal forward-dash cuts: parry the weak cut, dodge the strong one.
  • Flying slash (about 12 metres): go sideways, not backwards.
  • Heavy teal wave: dodge the moment he pulls back to charge it.
  • Approach: heal right after a dodge, before his next chain. Don't race his health bar.
  • Drop: Tianxuan Bronze Sword (A tier, blocks cheaply but can't parry).

15. Master Bi Hai of Kuye Temple

Where: Village of Lu. Required. Weakness (data): Fire +16%. He summons monks, and if you ignore them they surround you. Kill or split up the monks first, then keep controlled pressure on Bi Hai's guard, break his Sha-Chi and swap-combo. Drop: Kuye Temple Prayer Beads (A-tier summon edge: three bronze monks with over 14,000 health between them).

16. Dusk, the Puppeteer (optional)

Where: Village of Lu, the ravine past the Broken Puppet Pit Entrance. Optional; needs six Memory Flowers to unlock. His puppets pile up fast and the fight turns into a crowd brawl. Control the summons first, then work Dusk down with parries, break his Sha-Chi and swap-combo. Drop: Puppeteer's Bell (A-tier summon edge), Illusory Pipa Head.

17. Yuzan, No. 3 Musician Puppet

Where: Village of Lu, Puppet Testing Grounds inside the well (Beneath the Well bell). Required. Weakness (data): Fire +30%. It fights with music projectiles. Attack during a pattern and you get shot. Evade the pattern, close the distance in the gap, and hit back between patterns, never during them. Drop: Heartrend Pipa (keep it: it repairs into the A-tier Illusory Pipa).

18. Mu Xiaokui, Young Mistress of the Order

Where: Village of Lu, the chapter finale. Required. Phase two's bar reads Queen of Iron and Jade. A third phase only happens if you found all five of her ribbons before the fight. Each phase plays like a different boss, so reset your approach every time.

  • Delayed heavy: built to catch early dodges. Wait for it to actually come down, then dodge.
  • Fast blade chains: dodge each hit on its own timing.
  • Lunge: dodge it, or block or parry only if you're already close.
  • Spinning crimson rings: get out of them. Don't trade hits inside.
  • Perfect dodges open her biggest Sha-Chi breaks. Heal only when she backs away.
  • Phase three (ribbons route): Iron Fiend and Jade Dancer join her. Keep both guardians on screen and hit every button cue in the final scene; miss one and the scene restarts.
  • Drops: Wild Oriole Sword at the end of the lakeside dock after the fight (missable, grab it before you leave). Phase three adds the Fiend Saws, Fist Fragment: Core and Puppet No. 44's Eye.

19. Acolyte Yanru (optional)

Where: Village of Lu, the scarecrow below the Terraces. Optional. Weakness (data): Frost +30%. His one trick is a fire blast that catches anyone rushing in mid-attack. Wait for the blast to finish, then parry his strings. A few parries break him. Drop: Yanru's Blazing Mask, one of the four Acolyte Masks for the Four Guardians' Mirror.

20. Blaze, Shadow Warden Captain

Where: Pangzhen, South Gate Bridge. Required. Weakness (data): Fire +16% in phase one. Phase two's bar reads Phosphor Consciousness. His meteor hammer owns mid range: stay close enough to read him, or fully out of chain range, never in between. Counter during the recovery after his fire attacks. Phase two checkpoints. Drop: Fire Meteor (A-tier edge), Crimson Flesh Lily.

21. Cavalrybane, Elite Shadow Warden

Where: Pangzhen, Pavilion of Knowledge Crossroads, the red-eyed rider across a rainy stone plaza. Required. Weakness (data): Fire +16%. His strikes are so predictable the fight turns into a parry farm. Every parry chips his Sha-Chi. Break it, then swap-combo. Drop: Armybreaker, our number one weapon in the game.

22. Wu Xuan, the Fallen General

Where: Pangzhen, just past the Backstreet Archway bell. Required. Weakness (data): Chaos +50%, the biggest weakness listed for any boss. His defensive stance is the twist: heavy attacks break it open. Outside the stance, read each flash and answer it, and once he breaks, a weapon swap stretches the combo. Drop: Wolfjaw (lifesteal mace edge).

23. Gu Wanyue, the Wailing Wraith

Where: Pangzhen, Pond of Fading Song. Required. Her red flashes come often and none of them can be parried. Dodge every one, and only counter when it's safe. The loop is survive, break her Sha-Chi, punish, repeat. Drop: Fanatic's Fragment: Fixation, half of the upgrade for the Fanatic's Broken Blade.

24. Corpse-Eating Xue (optional)

Where: Mandate Encampment, behind a locked door. Optional. Weakness (data): Frost +20%. His fire breath covers a long line in front of him. Don't combo into it. Step off the line, wait for a clean opening, then parry and punish. Reward: no item, but beating him starts the tale Xue, Admirer of Heroes, which pays out the Sha-Chi Headscarf accessory.

25. Wan Jun, the Coppermaul (optional)

Where: Mandate Encampment, behind a chained gate (break the chain with the Dread Axe). Optional. Weakness (data): Poison +30%, so the Flying Dagger is a great edge here. His hammer is slow and very heavy. Dodge it rather than trading parries, then punish the long recovery. A few cycles and his Sha-Chi bar breaks. Drop: Bashpole (S-tier edge that also smashes weak floors).

26. Red, the Mysterious Swordswoman (optional)

Where: Mandate Encampment, on a rooftop. Optional. Her attacks come suddenly, so react; don't guess or pre-dodge. The rooftop edges make every step count, so keep your back away from the drop. Reward: no item; it advances the Daughter of the Dark Sect tale.

27. Chief Disciple of the Seven-Stars Formation (optional)

Where: Mandate Encampment. Optional. Six disciples fight with him. Stand in the middle and you get surrounded, so fight at the edge of the arena where nobody can get behind you. Thin out the disciples first, then focus the Chief. Phase two (bar: Sword of Despair; data lists Fire +16%) checkpoints. Drop: Swordsman's Summon (edge), Training Grounds Key.

28. Wu Lengshuang, the Soft Sword (optional)

Where: Mandate Encampment, near the Path of the Baishan's Fall bell. Optional. One of the hardest bosses in the game, and fair with it. She punishes aggression. Parry, take one or two hits, then reset. Those small punishes add up. Break her Sha-Chi, then go all in. Drop: Soft Snake Sword (A tier), Blackstone Fragment.

29. Yubi, No. 21 Swordfighter Puppet (optional)

Where: Mandate Encampment, Training Grounds at the far end, during the Illusory Pipa quest. Optional. Chipping its guard with light attacks does nothing. Parry its sword strings, then break the guard with Sha-Chi combos. Short punishes only, then reset. Drop: Baishan Stormswords (S tier after our level-30 tests), Broken Vessel, an Illusory Pipa part.

30. Xie Qiudong, the Divine Healer

Where: Pangzhen part 2, Green Serpent House. Required. Weakness (data): Fire +30%. His gimmick is healing: back off and his health bar climbs back up. Keep constant pressure so the heal never fires, and after that he's a plain parry check. Remember the Long Pillar deadline above: guides report Pillars 1 to 4 must be done before you open the Green Serpent House door. Drop: Everflow (medicine).

31. Chang Hong, the Thunder Spear

Where: Pangzhen part 2, Ancestral Shrine Square. Required. Weakness (data): Frost +16% in both phases. His charge out-ranges a back-step, so dodge sideways and hit him on every turnaround. Phase two (the Thunder Lancer) is mounted with bigger charges: same answer, sideways every time. Drop: Thunder Spear (A-tier lightning edge).

32. You Loong, Leader of the Blackwater Crew (both fights, optional)

Where: Pangzhen part 2, then a frenzied rematch on Meigu Lake at the Blackwater Crew Dock island. Optional, but beat him before Flame. Weakness (data): Frost +30%. It's a solid sword duel. Punish his openings, never mid-string, break his Sha-Chi and extend with a swap. Drops: You Loong's Deluge, the water-cannon edge that counters Flame. The rematch drops the Loong Trident and the High-Power Pump.

33. Flame, the Mask-Bound Lion Dance Grandmaster

Where: Pangzhen part 2, Lion Dance Hall Depths. Required. Weakness (data): Lightning +30% in both phases. Phase one alone has a huge health pool (around 88,830 on Wayfarer), so play it safe.

  • Bring You Loong's Deluge. The water cannon hits him hard and triggers Scalding Steam on a burning Flame for bonus damage. Wet targets also take more Lightning, so a Deluge into Thunder Spear combo makes sense here (our inference from the two effects, not tested).
  • Pole leaps: he perches on fire poles and leaps down. Dodge the landing, then punish it.
  • Fire: his fire covers huge areas. Don't stand in it to finish a combo.
  • Phase two, The Lion Dance Grandmaster's Final Dance: on foot with the lion head. Dodge the leap, then punish. Phase two checkpoints.
  • Drop: Dormant Flaming Mane. Have it repaired at Jingyang Hall into the Flaming Mane (A-tier fire edge).

34. Acolyte Lei Ting (optional)

Where: Pangzhen, below Old Man Gen's House, available after Flame. Optional. Weakness (data): Water +16%, so open with the Deluge. The basics end it quickly: parry blue, dodge red, break his Sha-Chi, swap weapons. Drop: Leiting's Thunder Mask (Acolyte Mask).

35. Soulclaimer, Elite Shadow Warden

Where: Pangzhen part 2, Dockside Warehouse courtyard on the northeast docks. Required. Weakness (data): Fire +16%. Attacking into his guard gets you nowhere. Build Sha-Chi damage with parries, guard-break him, then swap mid-combo. Drop: Parting Hooks (A-tier counter weapon).

36. Zhu Yuan, the Searing Blade (optional, true ending)

Where: Pangzhen, at the bottom of the Sha-Chi Tower. Optional, but required for the true ending. Weakness (data): Frost +16%. A fast, fair blade duel: parry blue, dodge red, punish every Ghoststep. Break the bar and stretch the combo with a swap. Drop: Scorching Flames, half of the Flaming Grip that tempers the Sanguine Reach into the Blazing Edge.

37. Grief-Stricken Jin

Where: Meigu Lake, Village of Lu Shallows Dock. Required. It's a group fight. Lock on to Jin and ignore the others, and they hit you from behind. Clear the adds first, then turn on Jin: one-on-one he's a basic parry, break and swap duel. Drop: Embroidered Shoes (Soul Temper material for the Scarlet Wraithblade). Fight him during A Brother Once More and you also get the Rouge Box accessory.

38. One-Eyed Archer (optional)

Where: Meigu Lake, One-Eyed Isle Dock, the easternmost island. Optional. The whole fight is a chase. Dodge the arrows to close the distance, then stay glued to him, because he has very little up close. Drop: One-Eyed Bow (edge). Loot the ruin behind the arena for the Sharpshooter's Eye accessory.

39. Yan Qiansui, the Zither Maiden (optional)

Where: Meigu Lake, the Sunken Opera House (Meigu Songhouse Dock). Optional; needs all six Yan'er Memory Flowers. Weakness (data): Fire +16%. Her notes flood the arena, and swinging through them gets you hit. Dodge the notes, punish her pauses and break her Sha-Chi in the gaps. Drop: Fanatic's Fragment: Attachment (the other half of the Drunken Sword upgrade), Illusory Pipa Strings.

40. Heartpiercer, Elite Shadow Warden

Where: Meigu Lake, inside the White Serpent House. Required. Weakness (data): Fire +16%. The same Warden formula as Cavalrybane and Soulclaimer: read the flash, answer it, break the Sha-Chi bar, swap weapons. Drop: Sundering Dual Axes, White Serpent Eye.

41. Acolyte Guhe (optional)

Where: Meigu Lake, the scarecrow on the East Dock island. Optional. Weakness (data): Fire +16%. His only danger is trading hits with you, so never swing into an attack. Parry, punish, reset. Drop: Guhe's Venom Mask. With all four Acolyte Masks, the Four Guardians' Mirror opens the hidden realm for Long Pillar 4.

42. Blizzard Blademaster (optional)

Where: Meigu Lake, the frozen lake by the Whiteshadow's Fall bell. Optional. Weakness (data): Fire +16%, so bring the Flaming Mane. Rest at the bell first so your gourd is full.

  • Tall ice crescent: sidestep it; it's not a parry.
  • Short ground ice burst: it can lock you in place. Back off.
  • Red-flash strike: dodge for a Ghoststep.
  • High leap and slam: dodge the landing, then punish.
  • Don't block his chains: frost builds up and freezes you. The Frostward accessory halves Chill and Freeze.
  • Drop: Whiteshadow (A-tier frost two-hander). Puppet No. 99 appears on the lake afterwards.

43. Iron Butcher, the Frenzied Brute (optional)

Where: Mountain of Imprisonment, near The Lady's Path bell. Optional. Weakness (data): Lightning +16%. It's Jagged Steel's fight again with bigger numbers. Never trade with his huge cleaver swings: dodge late and punish the long recovery. Drop: Iron Butcher Mask (Soul Temper material for Jagged Steel).

44. Bi Feng, Giant Saber Commander (optional, true ending)

Where: Mountain of Imprisonment, Cavern Passage bell. Optional, but required for the true ending. Stay just outside the giant saber's sweep, step in right after it passes, then work the guard. Once the bar breaks, swap weapons to stretch the combo. Drop: Juggernaut (greatsword that stacks Red Edge on parries).

45. Kui Yanyan, the Deranged (optional)

Where: Mountain of Imprisonment, Shrine Outer Gate. Optional. Watch one full combination before you do anything, then counter at its end. Every clean counter pushes his Sha-Chi bar closer to breaking. Drop: Missing Lift Crank, Sha-Chi Amber Fragment.

46. Jade-Face (optional)

Where: Mountain of Imprisonment, The Lady's Shrine bell. Optional, but he guards the way to Huangxing. Swing before one of his strings ends and the rest of it lands on you. Wait, parry the blue flashes, and each opening chips the Sha-Chi bar. Break it, then go all in. Drop: Seamless Death (dual ring weapon). Check the building to the right of the arena for the Desperate Stand accessory.

47. Huangxing, the Kunlun Renegade (optional)

Where: Mountain of Imprisonment, The Lady's Shrine, past Jade-Face. Optional. Weakness (data): Frost +16% in phase one, Lightning +30% in phase two.

  • Chain-ball launch: it knocks you back even through a block. Dodge it.
  • Long bladed-pole thrust and the skid across the hall: dodge both.
  • Thin red flash: still a dodge. Don't mistake it for a blue.
  • On Hellwalker his AI adapts to your last action, so vary your defence instead of repeating the same dodge.
  • Phase two, the Whirling Executioner, moves to a new location and checkpoints. Weapon-swap to refill your Sha-Chi when it runs low.
  • Drop: Flying Guillotine (A-tier edge). Talk to Inanis afterwards to start the quest for Inanis' Radiant Fan (S-tier edge).

48. The Puppet Scavenger and Acolyte Hansui (optional)

Where: Crimson Manor, the Scavenger Pens in the depths of the manor. Optional. Weakness (data): Hansui Fire +30%. It's two bosses at once. Keep both on screen and focus one down; once one falls, the rest is a normal duel. Drop: Alloyed Scythe Blade (Hunter's Chain Sickle upgrade), Crystal Puppet Eye [Yang], Hansui's Frost Mask (Acolyte Mask).

49. Yuding, No. 12 Crescent Blade Puppet

Where: Mirage Estate, Training Grounds bell. Required. Weakness (data): Lightning +16%. Dodge the wide green crescent sweep, then strike during the recovery. Break the Sha-Chi bar between sweeps and swap-combo. Drop: Crescent Blade (polearm).

50. Qiu Feng, the Crimson Boneblade

Where: Mirage Estate, near the Mourning Hall bell. Required. A precise duel: perfect parries first, damage second. Let the Sha-Chi bar fill from parries, then swap-combo the break. Drop: Wraith Mask (accessory: Ghoststeps spawn attacking masks).

51. Lei Dang, the Order's Envoy

Where: Mirage Estate, above the Perfect Vessel Pond bell. Required. Weakness (data): Frost +16% in both phases. Most of his damage is ranged, so fight him up close.

  • Red aim line across the arena: a far cannon shot follows. Slip sideways off the line.
  • Mid-range volley with red streaks: dodge it.
  • Overhead cannon slam: dodge, then use the big punish window.
  • Phase two, the One-Armed Tiger: he leaps back and the cannon glows red before a screen-filling blast. Dodge on the fire, then cut back in. Phase two checkpoints.
  • Drop: Tiger Cannon (A-tier edge that also breaks weak walls). The NPC behind the door where Lei Dang waits repairs the Dormant Flaming Mane.

52. Mo Yuan, Leader of the Bastion of Mo (optional)

Where: Mirage Estate, tied to the last Long Pillar. Guides disagree on the details: some list him as the fifth Long Pillar opponent, others as a separate fight, and one puts it at the Weeping Creek pillar. What they agree on is that the reward is the Bastion of Mo Mask. Optional. It's a pure dodge fight. Almost every big move is a red flash, so each one is a Ghoststep chance. Red flash as he rises means the leaping kick, then a leaping red crescent: dodge both. His fast long-blade cuts with red streaks are dodges too. Punish the recovery after his overhead downward strike. Drop: Bastion of Mo Mask (Soul Temper material that turns the Sanguine into Infernal Rouge).

53. The Saw and the Crusher

Where: Mount Soulrend, The Giant's Lair on a frozen lake. Required. Two giants at once. Let them stack up and you eat both attacks, so pull them apart wherever you can. Phase two is The Crusher, Earthshattering Form (weakness, data: Poison +30%). He slams his iron ball into the ice and disappears into a snow cloud. Don't charge in. Wait two or three seconds until he reappears, then dodge clear of the slam and drag. Phase two checkpoints. Drop: Bonesaw (two-hander). You also get the Baishan Headband if Cui's tale is done.

54. Leng Tu, Herald of the Veil

Where: Mount Soulrend, the summit past the Soulrend Cliff bell. Required. His red threads fill the arena: dodge them, then punish the opening. Break the Sha-Chi bar and end it with a swap combo. Arrive healed, because Meng Hua starts right after. Drop: Herald's Lingering Threads (turns the Puppeteer's Bell into the Abyssal Bell).

55. Meng Hua, the Merciful Lady of the Blossom Veil

Where: Mount Soulrend, Soulrend Cliff, straight after Leng Tu. Required; the normal-ending final boss.

  • Red lances rain across the arena: dodge them.
  • Fan strings are the parries. This is where you build her Sha-Chi damage.
  • Only attack during real openings; she punishes everything else.
  • Later, lightning and frost join in. Every elemental pillar is a dodge, never a block.
  • Drop: The Lady's Fan (A-tier dual weapon), The Lady's Crown.

56. Zuo Shang, true final boss (The True Long)

Where: Mount Soulrend, through the gold portal atop Soulrend Cliff. Optional and missable: true-ending route only. The portal needs the true-ending requirements, which include beating Zhu Yuan and Bi Feng; guides add the five Long Pillars for the perfect ending. Phase two, The True Long, fills the arena with golden attacks, so movement beats parries.

  • Broad golden sweep: stand still to parry it and you're erased. Dodge.
  • Golden beam along the water: sidestep it.
  • Expanding golden ring: dodge through it or get clear.
  • Falling gold blades: keep moving the whole time.
  • Blade lock: mash the on-screen prompt to win it.
  • Drop: Aureate (A-tier sword), Broken Long Body, Radiant Heart, and the true ending.

Normal enemies

Normal enemies follow the same flash rules as bosses, and most of them die to the same loop: parry, Ghoststep, break the Sha-Chi bar, execute. Executions and assassinations are your friends: with Endless Renewal they restore HP and Sha-Chi. Enemies don't respawn, so clearing an area properly keeps it clear. One guide counts 102 ordinary enemy types across the Prologue and eight regions (unverified). The game groups them into four families. Their weaknesses below come from metabot.gg's enemy tables (single source).

Family weaknesses at a glance: Puppets are almost all weak to Fire (+30%), and Bleed and lifesteal don't affect them without the Livingwood Talisman. Most Maskwalkers (the brutes, madwomen and bronze, iron and gold monks) are weak to Frost (+16%), but bandits and thieves are weak to Poison. Order soldiers and Shadow Wardens lean towards Fire (+16%), the Blackwater Crew to Frost (+30%). Animals are weak to Fire (+30%).

Frequently asked questions

How many bosses are in Phantom Blade Zero?

56 distinct bosses across about 62 fights. The extra fights are repeats: the Gold-Masked One at the Long Pillars, plus two fights each with You Loong and The Hunt.

What is the hardest boss in Phantom Blade Zero?

The Gold-Masked One at the Long Pillars. He only takes real damage after perfect parries, perfect dodges or a Sha-Chi break, and his red grab leads into a lethal follow-up. Wu Lengshuang, Hua, Deadly Betrothed, and Yubi are the hardest of the rest.

Which bosses do I need for the true ending?

Beat Zhu Yuan (bottom of the Pangzhen Sha-Chi Tower) and Bi Feng (Cavern Passage bell, Mountain of Imprisonment). Guides also list all five Long Pillars for the perfect ending: Pillars 1 to 4 before the Green Serpent House door, Pillar 5 before The Last Chance bell. A gold portal then opens atop Soulrend Cliff, leading to Zuo Shang.

What is the best weapon in Phantom Blade Zero?

The Armybreaker from Cavalrybane in Pangzhen. Its Thrust turns every weapon swap or blue-flash parry into a free gap closer. The Fiend Saws and Wild Oriole Sword are close behind, and both are missable in the Village of Lu.

What is the best Phantom Edge?

The Dread Axe. Its charged cleave smashes guards and shielded groups, and you get it early from Peng Meng in Weeping Creek Valley.

How do I beat Flame, the Lion Dance Grandmaster?

Beat You Loong first and equip You Loong's Deluge. The water cannon hits Flame hard and triggers Scalding Steam while he burns. Punish the landings from his pole leaps, and in phase two dodge the lion-head leap, then punish.

Does Phantom Blade Zero have DLC bosses?

Not yet. No DLC or expansion has been announced as of November 2026; the Deluxe Edition only adds outfits, an art book and the soundtrack.

What does the red flash mean in Phantom Blade Zero?

A red flash marks an attack you can't block or parry, so dodge it. A perfectly timed dodge triggers a Ghoststep behind the enemy. Blue flashes are parries, and a black or grey screen flash is a killer move that drains your Sha-Chi if you block it.
