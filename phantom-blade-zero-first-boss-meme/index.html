Everyone complains about Phantom Blade Zero's first boss. Then something clicks, and ten seconds later Jagged Steel, the Frenzied Brute is getting taken apart. We had to make it a meme.

Who is Jagged Steel?

Jagged Steel, the Frenzied Brute, is the first boss you fight in Phantom Blade Zero. He's the wall where a lot of players learn that button-mashing doesn't work and reading his swings does. Once you learn that, the fight turns into the clip above.
