Phantom Blade Zero Came 1% Short of Golden Joystick Game of the Year

By vAnother Level · Updated 2026-11-13
Home › Phantom Blade Zero Guides

One percent. That's how close Phantom Blade Zero came to winning Ultimate Game of the Year at the Golden Joystick Awards 2026. Resident Evil Requiem took the award with 17% of the public vote, Phantom Blade Zero finished second on 16%, and Crimson Desert was third on 15%.

Results from the 44th Golden Joystick Awards, London, November 11, 2026

Why it's a big deal

Ultimate Game of the Year is decided by fans, not critics. Phantom Blade Zero launched on October 29, just one day before voting closed on October 30, so almost all of those votes came from people who hadn't even played it yet, or had played it for a single day.

It was also up against Capcom's biggest night: Resident Evil Requiem also won Console Game of the Year and Best Game Trailer, and Capcom was named Studio of the Year. For a new action game from S-GAME, a Chinese studio on its first big console release, second place out of 12 nominees is a huge result.

Reviews agree

Critics like it too: Phantom Blade Zero sits around 88 on Metacritic and 90 on OpenCritic.
