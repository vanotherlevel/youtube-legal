You told me I had Shadowless Edge and the Baishan Stormswords way too low on my tier list. So I maxed both to level 30 and took them into real boss fights: Cavalrybane, Stone Hand Iron Fever, Wan Jun the Coppermaul, The Hunt and Mu Xiaokui. Short answer: you were right.

Shadowless Edge

Shadowless Edge is the Soul Tempered Sanguine Twin.

  • Stats: 1264 damage, 468 Guard Break.
  • Shadow Form: summons a Phantom Double for 20 seconds, with a 10-second cooldown.
  • Phantom Requiem: costs 1 orb, an unblockable crow sweep.
  • Lv 5: Chi-Infused Blade.
  • Lv 15: Relentless Pursuit.
  • Lv 25: Blades at Will.
  • Lv 30: Phantom Triple Slash (needs more than 50% Sha-Chi and no weapon switch for 15 seconds).

The chain-combo loop

This is why Shadowless Edge feels broken: Sha-Chi combo → Relentless Pursuit → 6 light + 3 heavy → Blades at Will, then go again. Done right, the chain loops almost forever.

Baishan Stormswords

The Baishan Stormswords run on wind and thunder.

  • Stats: 1217 damage, 553 status effects.
  • Wind Gauge fills into Wind Sword; Thunder Gauge fills into Thunder Sword.
  • Both at once: Baishan's Blessing (attack up for 20 seconds, 30 seconds at Lv 25), Lightning Storm and the unblockable Wind-Thunder Slash.
  • Lv 5: Centered Breath.
  • Lv 15: Wind Infusion (parries build wind).
  • Lv 30: Elemental Confluence.

Frequently asked questions

Is Shadowless Edge good in Phantom Blade Zero?

Yes. Soul tempered to level 30 it hits 1264 damage with 468 Guard Break, Shadow Form adds a Phantom Double, and its chain combos loop almost forever. We rate it S tier.

How do the Baishan Stormswords work?

Attacks fill a Wind Gauge and a Thunder Gauge for Wind Sword and Thunder Sword; with both active you get Baishan's Blessing, Lightning Storm and the unblockable Wind-Thunder Slash. We rate them S tier.
