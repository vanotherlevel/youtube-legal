Phantom Blade Zero

Phantom Blade Zero's Laziest Build: 3 Puppets and a Clone Fight the Boss for You

By vAnother Level · Updated 2026-11-12 · Watch on YouTube
Home › Phantom Blade Zero Guides

Five against one, and you barely swing. This Phantom Blade Zero build puts three Elite Puppets and a Phantom Double of you on the field at the same time, then lets them do the work. We maxed it out and took it into real boss fights.

Tested on our own save at level 30, numbers read off the in-game menus

The build

  • Abyssal Bell (Soul Tempered Puppeteer's Bell): summons a Sword-and-Shield Puppet, a Spear-and-Shield Puppet and a third Elite Puppet for up to 15 seconds. Costs 2 Remnant Sparks. Summon Health 22,139 at level 30.
  • A Puppet's Wooden Heart +1: summons last 50% longer, so about 22 seconds.
  • Nuo Mask: summon-type Phantom Edges cost 1 less Remnant Spark (the bell now costs 1), and summons gain Berserk.
  • Fine Thread: summons deal 50% more damage and Sha-Chi depletion.
  • Shadowless Edge (level 30): Shadow Form summons a Phantom Double for 20 seconds with your attack and guard break.

How it did

Against Wan Jun, the Coppermaul with no accessories, his health barely moved. With the full build he went down in about 1 minute 40. Against Lei Dang, the Tiger Cannon (two phases), every time he turned on one puppet the rest were on his back, and because the bell only costs one spark we could ring it again the moment they vanished.

The one boss it struggles with is Jade-Face. He's fast enough to dodge most of the puppets and outlast their timer, so it cost us a lot of sparks before he went down.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best summon build in Phantom Blade Zero?

Abyssal Bell with Nuo Mask, A Puppet's Wooden Heart and Fine Thread, plus Shadowless Edge for its Phantom Double. Three puppets and a clone fight at once.

What does the Nuo Mask do?

It reduces the Remnant Spark cost of summon-type Phantom Edges by 1 and gives summons Berserk, slightly increasing their damage.

Which boss counters the summon build?

In our test, Jade-Face: he's fast enough to evade most puppets and outlast their timer.
