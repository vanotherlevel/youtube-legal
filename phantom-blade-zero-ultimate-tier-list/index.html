This is the ultimate Phantom Blade Zero tier list: every main weapon, all 25 Phantom Edges, every accessory and every skill, ranked from C tier up to S, with exactly how to get each one. Every pick was judged on boss fights: damage, safety, and how early you can get it.

It's updated for the November 4th patch, which rewrote item descriptions but not the numbers, so the board holds. Weapons build up from the bottom to a top ten, and the best weapon is revealed last.

How the tiers work

  • S tier: breaks boss fights. Build around it.
  • A tier: strong in any run, held back by a late unlock, setup or strict timing.
  • B tier: good in the right build, outclassed later.
  • C tier: fun or situational. Fine to skip.

Soul Tempered forms count with the weapon they come from (for example, the Sanguine Reach and its Blazing Edge form share a slot). Stats quoted are from the in-game menu at level 30 unless stated.

S tier Phantom Edges

Six edges that genuinely break fights, counting up to the best one.

Bashpole. Damage 862 and a massive Guard Break of 924. Hold R2 and Soul whirls the melon-headed hammer in wide sweeps; let go for a ground-shaking slam. Shields and crowds fold for a single charge, and it smashes wooden planks over sealed floors. Get it: break the chained gate and beat Wan Jun, the Coppermaul, in the Mandate Encampment. Best partner: the Bonesaw, which hits Guard-Broken enemies harder.

Flying Dagger. Damage 979, Guard Break 842, and it never misses once thrown. Tap R2 up to three times for volleys of throwing knives; every hit builds Poison, so you poison and interrupt without stepping into a boss's reach. Get it: bring all three Crimson Forest treasures to Quick Hand Zhang. Best partner: The Fanatic's Drunken Sword.

Iron Gauntlets. Damage 1,018, Guard Break 870, high shield-breaking power. Tap R2 up to three times for heavy punches. The broken part: every punch builds Fist Focus, which raises all damage from your main weapon for eight seconds. Get it: beat Stone Hand Iron Fever in the Crimson Forest. Best partner: a fast weapon like the Fiend Saws, so more hits land inside those eight seconds.

Inanis' Radiant Fan. A summoning edge with HP Recovery 1,334 and Sha-Chi Recovery 807. Press R2 to open an eight-second sanctuary; stand inside and HP and Sha-Chi flow back. On a hard boss that's healing on demand. Get it: finish the Whence the Weeping quest in the Mountain of Imprisonment (talk to Inanis after beating Huangxing). Best partner: Seamless Death, whose Flying Rings hit from range so you never leave the field.

Hunter's Chain Sickle. Damage 963, Guard Break 831, mid to long range. Tap R2 up to three times; land the blade, because the chain only does 30% of its damage. Even unequipped it grapples you across gaps to loot you couldn't reach. Get it: beat The Hunt (Sixfold Scythe) at the Great Waterfall in Weeping Creek Valley, very early. Best partner: the Wild Oriole Sword.

Dread Axe (the best Phantom Edge in the game). Damage 1,205, Guard Break 811, high shield-breaking power. Hold R2 to charge up to two stages, then release for a crushing cleave; one full charge smashes straight through a shielded formation. It also breaks iron chains blocking paths. And it's the earliest S pick. Get it: beat Peng Meng, the Axemaster, at The Axemaster's Fall bell in Weeping Creek Valley (the Mandate Outpost area). Best partner: Jagged Steel: bleed them up close, then crack the guard with one cleave.

Skill tier list: what to buy first

Skills are bought at bells under Train Abilities with Remnant Dust plus Blackstone, Sha-Chi Amber, Complete Memory Flowers or Banyan's Hearts.

  1. Boundless Capacity (all four levels): S. Each level adds one accessory slot. Every accessory above needs a slot; nothing else adds this much power. Lv.1 costs 10,000 Remnant Dust.
  2. Healing Art: S. One extra Medicine use, every fight, for 5,000 Remnant Dust.
  3. Redirect Force: S. Dodging or parrying restores Sha-Chi, so your defence fuels your offence.
  4. Flow Like Water: S. Blocking and dodging don't interrupt your combo. The best first Banyan's Heart unlock.
  5. Fluid Form: S. After a red-flash Perfect Dodge, a blue-flash parry, a Guard Break or a kill, Quick Pursuit dashes you straight back to the enemy. Free.
  6. Flowing Chi: S. Against regular enemies, certain Phantom Edges counter or follow up without spending Remnant Spark.
  7. Endless Renewal: A. Assassinations and Executions restore HP and Sha-Chi.
  8. Still Mind: A. Brief invincibility during a Power Surge, so your biggest hit is also your safest moment.

Worth grabbing next: Ascendance (+1 Sha-Chi Essence, one more Power Surge per fight), Still Depths (+1 Remnant Spark, one more edge cast), Steady Composure (your next attack after a dodge or parry is more likely to interrupt) and Masterful Flow (weapon-swap cooldown -2 s).

Skip early: All or Nothing (only kicks in when your Sha-Chi is already too low), Shadowless Form (just a 15% chance to save a Remnant Spark) and Blood Remedy (only a chance to get Medicine back on kills). Spend that Remnant Dust on slots and heals first.

The top 10 weapons in Phantom Blade Zero

Now the ten that actually broke the game, from high A up to the top of S.

#10: Tianxuan Bronze Sword

High A. One-handed, damage 936, Guard Break 415. The broken part is Natural Order: blocking costs far less Sha-Chi, but you can't parry at all.

How to get it: Beat Master Bi Yuan of the Tianxuan Sect at the Statue Grounds, Village of Lu, in a straight duel in a burning courtyard.

Combo: Block, then swap straight into the White Serpent & Crimson Viper's Twin Serpent Dance. Best Phantom Edge: Kuye Temple Prayer Beads, whose bronze monks keep the boss busy. Surprise partner: the Wooden Sanguine, for fights that demand a parry.

A niche pick, but if you hate parry timing this sword turns blocking into a weapon. At its peak, Greatness Without Form sends a giant green phantom blade sweeping down beside you.

#9: The Fanatic's Broken Blade (Drunken Sword)

High A. Restored into The Fanatic's Drunken Sword: damage 1,014, Guard Break 406. Drinking awakens it, so its attacks fly and hit enemies at range, and once restored the damage no longer falls off over distance.

How to get it: Beat Hua Qianxiao on Pangzhen Bridge in the Crimson Forest. Restore it with Fanatic's Obsession, made from Fanatic's Fragment: Fixation (Gu Wanyue, Pangzhen) and Fanatic's Fragment: Attachment (Yan Qiansui, Meigu Lake).

Combo: Take a swig with Bottomless Drink, then run your Square combo so every swing throws a flying sword. Best Phantom Edge: Flying Dagger, so nothing reaches you. Surprise partner: the Tianxuan Bronze Sword: when something gets close, swap and block it cheap.

Flying swords from a safe distance all fight. It's ninth only because it needs a drink to wake up.

#8: Wooden Sanguine

S. Soul's practice stick: damage 640, Guard Break 320. Low numbers, but Wooden Blade Flurry spends one Sha-Chi orb on a rapid flurry of slashes that's unblockable.

How to get it: Nothing to hunt: it's Soul's practice sword from the Prologue and sits in your inventory from Chapter 1.

Combo: Five Square presses for the Standard Combo, then three Triangles for the Sha-Chi Combo. Best Phantom Edge: Dread Axe, for the guard damage wood is missing. Surprise partner: Jagged Steel, for the Bleed wood can't do.

After testing, it holds its ground against Peng Meng and is enough to fight The Gold-Masked One. A starter stick has no business beating bosses this well.

#7: White Serpent & Crimson Viper

S. Dual-wield, damage 1,014, Guard Break 428. Twin Serpent Dance: alternate Square and Triangle and it loops forever while the damage keeps climbing.

How to get it: Beat Bai Chilian at the end of the Crimson Forest chapter; the blades are waiting when Soul wakes up in the Village of Lu.

Combo: Mid-dance, press L1 + Triangle for Crimson Viper Orbit, which spins the short blade around Soul. Its strongest move, Twin Serpent Frenzy, is an unblockable chain for one Sha-Chi orb. Best Phantom Edge: Hunter's Chain Sickle, so pressure never drops between dances. Surprise partner: the Fiend Saws, for the same fast rhythm.

The longer you stay on a boss, the more it hurts. Learn the rhythm and it's relentless.

#6: Outlaw Warblade

S. Two-handed, damage 936, Guard Break 415. Every hit drains life back into you, and Sha-Chi combos turn your HP into Recoverable HP that your Square hits drink straight back.

How to get it: Beat Jing Ye, the Outlaw, near the Outlaw Trap bell in the Crimson Forest.

Combo: Reaping Retreat: two Triangles, then Square, for a backstep slash that heals. Its peak, Bloodthirst Frenzy, is unblockable and pins the enemy while draining it. Best Phantom Edge: Hunter's Chain Sickle, for targets a two-hander can't reach. Surprise partner: the Wild Oriole Sword.

It heals on every hit and breaks shields, so hard bosses suddenly feel fair.

#5: Freezing Chaos

S. One-handed, damage 1,264, Status Effects 562: the highest damage in the top ten. Threefold Shift: Steel hits hardest, Chaos sets enemies on fire, and Frost builds Chill until they freeze solid. Chaos Erosion cashes the Chill out as an unblockable chain through all three forms.

How to get it: Late: take the end-game quest Triple Threat and chase the Three-Faced Puppet to Mirrorwood, then beat Yucong, No. 34 Twisted Puppet, in the snow (Threadbound Great Tree, Crimson Forest, on a return visit during the Mirage Estate chapter; some guides list Mirage Estate).

Combo: Build Chill in Frost form, then fire Chaos Erosion. Best Phantom Edge: Tiger Cannon: its shell shatters Frozen enemies (except elites), so freeze, then fire. Surprise partner: Whiteshadow, which fills the same Frozen gauge faster; at level 15 its Iceberg makes Frozen enemies take extra damage.

Frost builds melt bosses. Only the late unlock keeps it at five.

#4: Jagged Steel

S. One-handed, damage 936, Status Effects 482. Every hit inflicts Bleeding; fill the gauge and Blood Loss takes a huge chunk at once (no effect on Puppets, Golden Bodies or Phantom Bodies without the Livingwood Talisman).

How to get it: In the very first region: beat Jagged Steel, the Frenzied Brute, near the Rustblade Pavilion bell, Weeping Creek Valley.

Combo: Plant Scarlet Wraithblade's lilies, swap to Jagged Steel and slash through them so the bleed explodes. Best Phantom Edge: Dread Axe: smash the guard, swap back, keep the bleed rolling. Surprise partner: Outlaw Warblade, whose lifesteal turns all that bleeding into health.

Free in the first region, bleeds everything, and still top tier at the end.

#3: Wild Oriole Sword

S. Tempered into Tianmiao Wild Oriole: one-handed, damage 1,095, Guard Break 428. Oriole Song spends Sha-Chi for ten seconds in which every Square attack fires an air-rending blade wave, so a fast sword becomes a ranged weapon.

How to get it: Missable: after the Mu Xiaokui fight in the Village of Lu, pick it up at the end of the lakeside dock before you leave. Temper it with the Supreme Order Token from Mu Xiaokui on the Crimson Forest Sha-Chi Tower rooftop.

Combo: Trigger Oriole Song, run the five-hit Square chain so every swing throws a wave, then finish with three Triangles. Tempering adds Tianmiao's Shadow, Oriole's Cry, an unblockable dual-sword art. Best Phantom Edge: Hunter's Chain Sickle. Surprise partner: the Fiend Saws: the waves mark the boss and the saws pile Erosion on top.

It pressures from every distance.

#2: Fiend Saws

S. Dual-wield (damage 514, Guard Break 204 even at level 8). Every hit stacks Fiend-Aura Erosion until it erupts and takes a chunk of HP, and Fiend Form spends Sha-Chi to make every attack unblockable for ten seconds.

How to get it: Missable: in the Village of Lu, find all five of Mu Xiaokui's ribbons before the boss, then win her extra third phase.

Combo: Five Squares to stack Erosion, four Triangles for the Sha-Chi Combo, then pop Fiend Form for the follow-up. Best Phantom Edge: Fire Meteor, so pressure never stops while Erosion builds. Surprise partner: the Wild Oriole Sword: saws up close, waves from range.

Erosion that stacks on its own plus ten seconds of unblockable hits. Only one weapon breaks bosses harder.

#1: The best weapon in Phantom Blade Zero

At the top of S tier, the most broken weapon in the game is the Armybreaker. It's a two-handed polearm with damage 1,123 and Guard Break 445 at level 30 (1,426 and 528 at +1 on New Game Plus).

Why it's broken: Thrust. Switching to it, or parrying a blue-flash attack, makes Soul rush forward, so every weapon swap becomes a free gap closer and you arrive already swinging. Formation Breaking Art is an unblockable string of slashes that drains the enemy's Sha-Chi, and Mighty Rush charges forward until your Sha-Chi runs dry.

How to get it: beat Cavalrybane, Elite Shadow Warden, in Pangzhen (Pavilion of Knowledge Crossroads; some guides call the area the marketplace). He's the red-eyed rider waiting across a rainy stone plaza, and the weapon drops on the spot.

Combo: swap in to trigger Thrust, run the four-hit Square chain, then cash out with two Triangles. Best Phantom Edge: Flying Guillotine (damage 1,482, Guard Break 1,205) to finish the guard the polearm already cracked. Surprise partner: the Crescent Blade: its green sweeps hold the boss, then you swap back and Thrust rushes you in. That exact build took Mo Yuan's whole health bar in 59 seconds.

The three best builds

  • Bleed and lifesteal: Jagged Steel + Outlaw Warblade, Livingwood Talisman (makes both work on Puppets), Ancient Armor Fragment, Mother's Talisman, Dread Axe as the edge. Trade hits and your health keeps coming back.
  • Armybreaker rush: Mighty Rush with Irresistible Force (makes the rush unblockable), Kuye Dew (40% less damage for 20 s) and Miraculous Scabbard +1 for Super Armor on every swap.
  • Fiend Saws + Wild Oriole: stack Fiend-Aura Erosion, swap to the Oriole for range, then pop Fiend Form on a Guard-Broken boss for ten seconds of unblockable hits.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best weapon in Phantom Blade Zero?

The Armybreaker. Thrust turns every weapon swap or blue-flash parry into a free gap closer, and Formation Breaking Art is an unblockable Sha-Chi drain. Get it from Cavalrybane in Pangzhen. The Fiend Saws and Wild Oriole Sword are close behind.

What is the best Phantom Edge?

The Dread Axe. Its two-stage charged cleave smashes guards and shielded formations, and you get it early from Peng Meng, the Axemaster, in Weeping Creek Valley.

What is the best early weapon in Phantom Blade Zero?

Jagged Steel. It's in the first region (Jagged Steel, the Frenzied Brute, near the Rustblade Pavilion bell), its Bleed stays top tier all game, and it pairs with the Dread Axe from the same region.

Which weapons are missable?

The Wild Oriole Sword (on the dock after Mu Xiaokui) and the Fiend Saws (needs all five of Mu Xiaokui's ribbons before her fight). Get both on your first visit to the Village of Lu.

What is the best accessory in Phantom Blade Zero?

The Reflex Device. It widens your parry and perfect dodge windows by 15% (25% at +2). Find it at the Valley Sha-Chi Tower Base bell by putting the Furnace Inspection Crank in the furnace.

What skill should I buy first?

Boundless Capacity, which adds an accessory slot (four levels in total), then Healing Art for an extra Medicine use.

Did the November 4th patch change the tier list?

No. The patch rewrote item descriptions, not the numbers, so the rankings hold.
