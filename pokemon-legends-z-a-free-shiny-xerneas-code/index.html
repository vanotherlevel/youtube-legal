Free Shiny Xerneas in Pokémon Legends: Z-A: Code and How to Claim It

By vAnother Level · Updated 2026-11-12
Home › Pokémon Legends: Z-A

Free shiny legendary, no hunting. Pokémon Legends: Z-A's newest Mystery Gift is Shiny Xerneas, and it's live now.

Announced by @Pokemon, November 12, 2026
  1. Open Mystery Gift from the Link Play option in the main menu.
  2. Choose to receive with a code.
  3. Enter CELEBRATE30TH.

You can claim it until January 7, 2027. Shiny Yveltal joins on December 10 and runs until February 4, 2027; its code comes closer to the date. The same code also unlocks furniture in Pokémon Pokopia.

Shiny Xerneas and Shiny Yveltal are coming to #PokemonLegendsZA! Receive Shiny Xerneas via Mystery Gift starting today.

— @Pokemon 2026-11-12

Then vs now

Xerneas and Yveltal were the box legendaries of Pokémon X and Y in 2013, the games set in the same Kalos region Z-A returns to. Now they're back as shinies with one code.

Source: The Pokémon Company, reported by VGC. Watch more on our YouTube channel: vAnother Level.

Frequently asked questions

What is the Shiny Xerneas code?

CELEBRATE30TH, entered in Mystery Gift (Link Play menu). Valid until January 7, 2027.

When is Shiny Yveltal available?

From December 10, 2026 until February 4, 2027.
