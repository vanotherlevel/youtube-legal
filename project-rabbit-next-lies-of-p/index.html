Project Rabbit: Is This Dark Alice in Wonderland Soulslike the Next Lies of P?

By vAnother Level · Updated 2026-11-15 · Watch on YouTube

Project Rabbit takes Alice in Wonderland and turns it into a soulslike. It comes from NovaFlare, a 14-person Korean studio founded in early 2025 by members of the NCSoft team whose console game, Project E, was cancelled in October 2024. Some of the team came from Round8 Studio, the makers of Lies of P.

Who is NovaFlare?

The CEO and director, Hwang Seok-yoon, oversaw Master of Knights at Neowiz, was later assigned to Neowiz's Round8 Studio, and joined NCSoft in June 2023 to lead Project E. The studio has startup support from Smilegate and plans to grow to about 30 people by the end of 2026. The developers say the link to Lies of P is mostly the dark, fairy-tale approach: the two games "have almost no connection" otherwise.

The world: a dark Wonderland

You don't play as Alice. You play a third party following her through the Abyss of Time, ruled by the Blood Queen. There are five regions: four areas plus a central castle, and one region uses a Joseon-era Korean look. The trailer boss Karun is the Mad Hatter's son, a "Mad Eater".

Combat and the extraction part

It's about 80 to 90% soulslike and about 10% extraction: dodge, block and parry, solo or with allies, with invasions where you fight or team up. Most dungeons keep your items when you die, but extraction dungeons take everything. The team plans new bosses and content every 2 to 3 months.

Platforms and release

The cinematic trailer's end slate lists Steam, Epic Games Store, PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. In May 2026 the game was about 30 to 40% complete, with an Early Access target at the end of 2027. Treat that date as a hope, not a promise.

No release date, engine or budget has been announced
Sources: MTN and Inven Global (May 2026), Fextralife (Nov 11, 2026), Game Donga, the Steam store page, and NovaFlare's official cinematic gameplay trailer. Watch more on our YouTube channel: vAnother Level.

Is Project Rabbit made by the Lies of P team?

Partly. Some developers came from Round8 Studio, but NovaFlare says the two games have almost no connection beyond a dark fairy-tale style.

When does Project Rabbit come out?
