PS5 Passes 100 Million Consoles Shipped, a Few Months Slower Than PS4

By vAnother Level · Updated 2026-11-11
Home › PlayStation 5

PlayStation 5 is officially past 100 million. Sony says PS5 has shipped more than 100 million consoles as of November 10, 2026, almost six years after its November 2020 launch. That's at least 2.1 million more since September 30, when Sony's count stood at 97.9 million.

Figures from Sony Interactive Entertainment, reported by VGC

PS5 vs PS4

  • PS4: hit 100 million in June 2019, 5 years and 7 months after launch. It ended at about 117 million.
  • PS5: 100 million at almost 6 years, so a few months slower.
  • PS2 is still the king at about 160 million.

And PS5's biggest week might still be ahead: GTA 6 launches November 19 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. See the 30 FPS details for every console and the PS5 preload fix.
