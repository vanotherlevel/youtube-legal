Rockstar Tribunal Ends as GTA 6 Launches: Verdict Pending, Union Vote Nov 26

By vAnother Level · Updated 2026-11-18

The employment tribunal over Rockstar's October 2025 firings heard its closing submissions in Glasgow on November 17-18, 2026, the day before GTA 6 launched. There is no verdict yet: a judgement could take "anywhere from a few weeks up to a few months", according to Chris Bratt of People Make Games, as reported by VGC.

At the same time, Rockstar's UK staff are voting on whether the company should formally recognise a union. That vote runs from November 12 to November 26, 2026.

REPORTED: PA Scotland (via Yahoo News UK), VGC, Dexerto, TechRadar

What the tribunal is about

In October 2025, Rockstar dismissed about 34 staff, 31 in the UK and 3 in Canada, according to TechRadar. Many of them had been working on GTA 6. The union that represents them, the Independent Workers' Union of Great Britain (IWGB), says the firings were union busting. Rockstar denies that and says the staff were dismissed for gross misconduct involving confidential information.

PA reports that 23 claimants are taking part in the case, heard by Employment Judge Lucy Wiseman at the Glasgow Tribunals Centre.

In his closing submission, Cordrey called the claimants "blameworthy" and said the company is "obsessed by confidentiality", according to PA. Rockstar told PA it remains confident of a positive outcome.

We're not taking a side here. Neither has the tribunal yet. Until a judgement is published, both accounts are claims made in court, and we've labelled them that way.

The union vote: Nov 12 to Nov 26

REPORTED: Dexerto (Oct 28) and others
  • Who votes: Rockstar employees in the UK, anonymously and online. Reports put the number eligible at more than 1,600.
  • What it decides: whether Rockstar formally recognises the IWGB Rockstar Game Workers Union.
  • What recognition would cover: workers having a say on pay and bonuses, overtime, layoffs, flexible working and generative-AI policies (per Dexerto).
  • The union's claim: a yes would make it the largest recognised union in the UK games industry. That's the IWGB's own claim.
  • Rockstar's side: head of publishing Jennifer Kolbe told Bloomberg, "So if they want a union, they will have a union" (via Dexerto).

Does this affect GTA 6?

For players: no. The game launched on schedule, at midnight local time on November 19, on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Nothing in the case changes the game, updates or GTA Online plans. Earlier, the fired workers asked supporters not to boycott GTA 6, according to VGC.

VGTimes reports that at least 28 of the 34 fired developers appear in GTA 6's credits. We haven't checked the credits ourselves, so treat that number as reported.

OUR TAKE

Watch two dates. The vote closes on November 26, so a result is likely in late November or early December. The tribunal judgement has no date, and reports say it could take months. We'll update this page when either one lands.
