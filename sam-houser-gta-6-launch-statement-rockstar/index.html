Sam Houser's Rare GTA 6 Launch Statement: What He Said

Rockstar co-founder Sam Houser broke his usual silence for GTA 6's launch. In a statement given to GamesRadar+ on November 18, he thanked players, said the series "returns to Leonida and modern-day Vice City" this week, and closed with a line fans will recognise: "We look forward to many more years of sharing these experiences with you."

REPORTED: statement quoted by GamesRadar+, Nov 18 2026

Houser almost never speaks in public. His last big moment was the November 2023 post that announced the first GTA 6 trailer, which ended with the same "many more years" sign-off. Now he's using it again on the day the game actually starts unlocking, 13 years after GTA 5 came out on PS3 and Xbox 360.

What Sam Houser said

These are the parts of the statement GamesRadar+ published. We're only quoting lines we could verify; we haven't seen the full text on Rockstar's own Newswire yet.

  • Why Rockstar exists: he said Rockstar Games "was founded on the belief that games could achieve the same distinction as all other forms of storytelling".
  • Where GTA 6 goes: "This week, Grand Theft Auto returns to Leonida and modern-day Vice City."
  • Thanks: "We are incredibly grateful."
  • The future: "We look forward to many more years of sharing these experiences with you."
OFFICIAL: the Nov 2023 trailer announcement on Rockstar's X account, signed by Sam Houser, ended with a near-identical 'many more years' line

Why it matters

It lands on a huge day. The review embargo lifted with a 99 Metascore, tying Ocarina of Time as Metacritic's highest-rated game, and the game has already unlocked in New Zealand. Everyone else gets it at local midnight on November 19 (unlock times).

"Many more years" is the part people will read into. Rockstar supported GTA 5 for over a decade through GTA Online, so fans hear it as a promise of long-term support. That's our reading, not something Houser spelled out.

THEORY: 'many more years' hints at long-term updates and Online. Rockstar hasn't said this

What he didn't say

  • No PC date. PC Gamer's launch coverage notes Rockstar has still said nothing about a PC version.
  • No GTA Online date. Online for GTA 6 is still undated (what Rockstar has confirmed).
  • No story DLC news. See what we know about GTA 6 DLC.

Frequently asked questions

Did Sam Houser say anything about GTA 6's launch?

Yes. In a statement to GamesRadar+ on Nov 18 he said Rockstar is 'incredibly grateful', that GTA returns to Leonida and modern-day Vice City this week, and that the team looks forward to 'many more years of sharing these experiences with you'.

Did Sam Houser announce GTA 6 for PC?

No. Nothing in the quoted statement mentions PC, and Rockstar still hasn't announced a PC version.

Does 'many more years' mean GTA 6 Online is coming?

Not officially. It's the same sign-off Houser used in 2023. Fans read it as long-term support, but Rockstar hasn't dated GTA Online for GTA 6.
