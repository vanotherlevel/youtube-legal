Sekiro: How to Beat Every Boss, Best Weapons & Enemies

By vAnother Level · Updated 2026-11-12

This is a complete Sekiro: Shadows Die Twice boss guide: every main boss in story order with the perilous attack answers, deathblow counts and the Prosthetic Tools and Combat Arts that make each fight easier, every mini-boss in tables by area, the best tools and arts tiered, and how to handle the common enemies. Sekiro has one sword, Kusabimaru, so your real loadout is your prosthetic arm, your Combat Art and your consumables, and that is what this page ranks.

Five rules that win most Sekiro fights: 1) Deflect, don't dodge. Tapping block right before a hit fills the enemy's posture bar, and a full bar means a deathblow. 2) Read the red 危 perilous kanji: a thrust gets Mikiri Counter (dodge into it), a sweep gets a jump (then kick their head for extra posture), a grab gets a sidestep or dash back, never a block. 3) Lower their health first; an enemy's posture recovers slower when its vitality is low. 4) Learn Mikiri Counter in the Shinobi Arts tree as early as you can. 5) Stealth deathblows remove a whole health bar on most mini-bosses, so scout before you fight.
Written for the current, fully patched game (after the October 2019 free update, patch 1.05)

Every boss (in order)

Sekiro has four endings, and the late-game boss list changes with them. Shura (side with Owl at Ashina Castle and obey the Iron Code) ends at Emma and Isshin Ashina. The Kuro path (break the Iron Code) gives you Great Shinobi Owl, Fountainhead Palace, Divine Dragon and the final fight against Genichiro, Way of Tomoe and Isshin, the Sword Saint. Purification is the Kuro path plus the Father's Bell Charm and Owl (Father). Bosses tied to a route are labelled below. Tick them off as you go; the list is saved in your browser.

How perilous attacks and deathblows work

Every boss has a number of deathblow markers (the red dots above its health bar). Each marker is one deathblow, earned by breaking posture or emptying vitality. The red 危 kanji flashes before a perilous attack, and there are only three kinds:

  • Thrust (perilous stab): Mikiri Counter. Press dodge toward the enemy as the thrust comes in; you stamp on the weapon and deal big posture damage. Deflecting also works on many thrusts, but Mikiri is far better.
  • Sweep (low swing): Jump. If you jump early and close, press jump again on their head for a kick that does heavy posture damage and puts you in the air for a follow-up hit.
  • Grab (unblockable): Sidestep, dash backward or jump away. Do not block and do not Mikiri. Most grabs leave the enemy open for two or three hits if you dodge to the side.

Gyoubu Masataka Oniwa

Where: end of Ashina Outskirts, the open arena past the Ashina Castle Gate Fortress idol. Required. Deathblows: 2. Reward: Memory: Gyoubu Oniwa and the Mechanical Barrel.

  • Best tool: Shinobi Firecracker. His horse panics at it, which stalls him and leaves him open. Throw it as he rides at you.
  • Grappling Hook: when he gallops away, a green grapple marker appears on him. Pull yourself back in instead of chasing on foot.
  • Perilous charging sweep: jump over it. Detaching thrust: deflect it (this one can't be Mikiri countered).
  • Jumping attack: stand still and deflect both leaps; that is a huge posture hit for him.
  • Never stand directly behind the horse, it kicks.
  • Approach: stay glued to his side, deflect his combos, swing two or three times when he stops. You can hit him during his opening introduction for free damage.

Lady Butterfly (optional)

Where: Hirata Estate, inside the Hidden Temple. You need the Hidden Temple Key found past the Bamboo Thicket Slope idol, and you reach her after Juzou the Drunkard. Deathblows: 2. Reward: Memory: Lady Butterfly and a Sakura Droplet.

  • Best tool: Loaded Shuriken. Whenever she jumps onto her threads above the arena, hit her with a shuriken right as she lifts off. It knocks her down and does solid posture damage.
  • Perilous attack from above: step away or dodge when the kanji shows, then punish her landing.
  • Perilous sweep: jump it, and kick off her head when you're close.
  • Phase 2 illusions: she summons phantom soldiers. Kill them (one hit each, they refund Spirit Emblems) or run around the room's edge until they fade. A Snap Seed used in the middle of the illusions dispels them. When she gathers them into a spell, hide behind a pillar.
  • Combat Art: none needed. If you have the Ascending Carp and Descending Carp skills from the Ashina tree, posture breaks come much faster.
  • Approach: deflect her short combos, attack after her perilous moves, and keep the pressure on, since she is fragile once you stay close.

Genichiro Ashina

Where: the roof of Ashina Castle. Go past the Ashina Dojo idol and Ashina Elite Jinsuke Saze, then out of the window. (You lose a scripted fight to him in the prologue.) Deathblows: 3 (two bars, then he becomes Genichiro, Way of Tomoe for the third). Reward: Memory: Genichiro and Bloodsmoke Ninjutsu.

  • Perilous leaping slam into thrust: Mikiri Counter the thrust. Leaping slam into sweep: jump. He uses this more after losing a bar.
  • Bow: when he runs back and shoots, sidestep the arrow and dash straight in to punish.
  • Phase 3 lightning (Way of Tomoe): when he grips his sword two-handed and crouches, he leaps for a lightning strike. Jump as the bolt comes; if it hits you in the air, press attack before you land to send it back (Lightning Reversal). That stuns him and does huge damage. Never get hit by lightning on the ground.
  • Phase 3 running grab: run away, it can't be blocked.
  • Tools: Shinobi Firecracker and Loaded Axe both work. Ako's Sugar at the start of phase 3 speeds it up.
  • Approach: stay close, deflect his long flurries (they build his posture fast), and attack in pairs. Mikiri every thrust you see.

Folding Screen Monkeys

Where: Senpou Temple, Mt. Kongo, Main Hall. After Genichiro, ask Isshin about the Mortal Blade, then interact with the table in front of the giant Buddha to enter the Hall of Illusion. Deathblows: 4 (one per monkey, no real combat). Reward: Memory: Screen Monkeys and Puppeteer Ninjutsu. Suppress Presence and Suppress Sound skills help a lot.

  1. Invisible monkey (Shizaru): turn around the moment you arrive and hit the spot it's standing on; it doesn't move at the start. Ring the Illusive Hall Bell to reset if you miss.
  2. Orange monkey (Iwazaru, the one that alerts the others): hide behind the orange screen until its alert fades, then grapple to the roof above the monk and drop on it from behind.
  3. Purple monkey (Mizaru, flees when it sees you): let it see you, chase it into the dark back hall, and approach it from the darkness where it can't see. A Shinobi Firecracker as it lands also works.
  4. Green monkey (Kikazaru, flees when it hears you): startle it from the centre tree, follow it to the waterfall room (the water hides your footsteps) and sneak up, or stun it at the tree with a Firecracker.

After the first kill, ghost monkeys start attacking. Ignore them and keep moving.

Guardian Ape

Where: Sunken Valley Passage, the pool at the Guardian Ape's Watering Hole (the Bodhisattva Valley idol is nearby). Required. Deathblows: 2 (phase 1 ends with a fake 'Shinobi Execution'; it gets back up headless). Reward: Memory: Guardian Ape and the Slender Finger (Finger Whistle), plus the Lotus of the Palace in the cave behind it.

  • Phase 1 tools: Shinobi Firecracker (beasts hate it, free hits) and Flame Vent (it can catch fire and stagger). Oil plus Flame Vent sets it burning.
  • Perilous grab swipe: jump it. Body slam grab: dodge sideways as it jumps.
  • Poison: when it leaps high, run underneath and punish the long recovery. Back off from its fart cloud.
  • Phase 2 (headless, sword): Firecracker stops working. Divine Confetti and the Mortal Blade do bonus damage.
  • Phase 2 scream (perilous): it builds Terror and can kill you outright up close. Run far away, or block it with the Loaded Umbrella. Pacifying Agent or the Mottled Purple Gourd cures/limits Terror.
  • Phase 2 sweep: jump and kick. Diving lunge: jump over it and hit it in the air. Deflecting its big overhead slam stuns it; the Loaded Spear can then pull the centipede out of its neck.
  • Approach: phase 1 stay close and hit after its combos, use Mortal Draw when it's staggered; phase 2 keep mid-range and punish dives.

Corrupted Monk (illusion)

Where: the Water Mill at the far end of Mibu Village, Ashina Depths, guarding the Wedding Cave entrance (where the Shelter Stone is). Deathblows: 1. Reward: Memory: Corrupted Monk and the Mibu Breathing Technique (breathe underwater).

  • Perilous thrust: Mikiri Counter. Perilous sweep: jump. Her upward slash can't be blocked but can be deflected with good timing.
  • Tools: Lazulite Axe is excellent, and Fistful of Ash or Snap Seed stun her. The Phantom Kunai upgrade of the shuriken also does heavy damage.
  • Run away from her long naginata spin, then punish the end.
  • She has a big health bar and resists posture, so chip health after her combos (they have long recovery) instead of trying to deflect her to death.

Headless Ape

Where: Ashina Depths, Guardian Ape's Burrow, behind the Poison Pool fog gate that Snake Eyes Shirahagi guards. Only appears after you beat the Guardian Ape. Deathblows: 2 (plus scripted finishers). Reward: Memory: Headless Ape, 2 Prayer Beads, and Bestowal Ninjutsu if you sever it with the Mortal Blade.

  • Phase 1 is the Guardian Ape's phase 2 in a smaller room. After the first deathblow, its brown-furred mate joins.
  • Terror roar (perilous): sprint far away; the Loaded Umbrella negates it.
  • Perilous sweeps: jump, then jump again on its body for posture.
  • Brown ape: a beast, so Shinobi Firecracker, Oil and Flame Vent work on it. Kill it first if you can; it makes the rest easier. You only need to kill the Headless Ape to win.
  • Loaded Spear pull after a deflected overhead slam yanks the centipede out for big damage.
  • Have the Mortal Blade ready for the final blow to sever immortality.

Emma, the Gentle Blade & Isshin Ashina (Shura ending only)

Where: Ashina Castle, only if you side with Owl and forsake Kuro (obey the Iron Code). Emma comes first, then Isshin with no checkpoint between. Deathblows: Emma 1; Isshin 2 (reported). Reward: Memory: Isshin Ashina and the One Mind Combat Art.

  • Emma: deflect her combos (the last hit of her sword-and-hilt flurry is delayed). Her grab: run away or get behind her. Sweep: jump. Thrust: Mikiri. When she sheathes and her blade gleams, back off; she is about to Ashina Cross.
  • Isshin phase 1: sidestep and punish Ichimonji and Ichimonji: Double; run from Ashina Cross; dash away from his grab.
  • Isshin phase 2 (fire): for the vertical firewave pick a side and circle in; for room-wide fire stand away from the glowing ground. Flame Vent, Shinobi Firecracker, Mortal Draw or Ichimonji can interrupt his fire eruption stance.
  • Mist Raven lets you teleport behind him after a big swing.

Great Shinobi Owl (Kuro path)

Where: Ashina Castle after the shinobi invasion, on the route through the Ashina Dojo idol. You fight him if you stay loyal to Kuro (break the Iron Code). Deathblows: 2. Reward: Memory: Great Shinobi.

  • Stomp grab (perilous): he pins you and stabs; huge damage. Dodge or dash back the moment the kanji shows.
  • Shuriken then flip: sidestep the first shuriken, deflect the second, then dodge diagonally away from his downward strike. That opens a long punish window (two Mortal Draws fit).
  • Purple mist charm: it blocks healing and buffs for 30 seconds. Step out of the cloud.
  • Owl Mikiri counters your thrusts. Don't use thrusting arts or charged Loaded Spear unless he's stunned.
  • Tools: Shinobi Firecracker, Loaded Umbrella - Magnet, Loaded Shuriken. He doesn't have super armour on most moves, so plain sword hits interrupt his jumps and throws.
  • When his bar empties and he kneels, he's faking; keep hitting. In phase 2 run from the smoke bombs and dodge back from his poison backflips.

True Corrupted Monk (Kuro path)

Where: the entrance bridge of Fountainhead Palace. Deathblows: 3. Reward: Memory: True Monk and the Dragon's Tally Board.

  • Phase 1: same thrust (Mikiri) and sweep (jump) as the illusion fight, but she takes much more posture damage, so deflecting is the plan.
  • Phase 2: she vanishes into mist and sends three shadow copies. Stand near a wall so they come from one side, deflect them, and hit her when she reappears. Divine Confetti and Ako's Sugar help here.
  • Phase 3: a centipede spits goo that causes Terror. Sidestep or jump-kick it, then punish the long recovery. Run from her whirlwind spin; a Shinobi Firecracker during its wind-up stuns her.
  • Tools: Shinobi Firecracker, Fistful of Ash, Flame Vent with Living Force in phase 3 (burning hurts her posture).

Divine Dragon (Kuro path)

Where: Fountainhead Palace; pray at the altar with flowing water above the Sanctuary idol. Needed for three of the four endings. Deathblows: 1. Reward: Memory: Divine Dragon and the Divine Dragon's Tears.

  • Phase 1 (Old Dragons of the Tree): the white old men cough poison; keep your distance. Grapple onto the sprouting trees and plunge-attack one in the middle of the group. Repeat.
  • Phase 2: you can't hit the dragon directly. Run to a tree that sparkles yellow, grapple up, and you get launched with a lightning spear. Press attack to throw it at the dragon.
  • Perilous sweep: jump. Overhead slam: block or sidestep. Don't grab lightning when it blows you away.
  • When it's nearly dead the trees vanish for about a minute. Survive, then hit it once more when a lightning tree returns and finish it with the deathblow.

Owl (Father) (Purification ending only)

Where: the Hirata Estate memory on your second visit (entered with the Father's Bell Charm you get on the Kuro path by talking to Emma), at the Hidden Temple where you fought Lady Butterfly. Deathblows: 2. Reward: Memory: Foster Father and the Aromatic Flower (needed for Purification).

  • Perilous thrust (Shadowfall): Mikiri Counter. Perilous sweep: jump. Stomp grab: dodge back, it can't be blocked.
  • Double shuriken plus flip: deflect the shuriken, then dodge sideways from the flip.
  • Firecrackers: he blinds you, then attacks. Back away instead of swinging.
  • Phase 2: he goes invisible and drops from above, and throws fire owls. Jump the fire owl and Mikiri the thrust that follows; hide behind a pillar to heal.
  • Tools: Fistful of Ash, Mikiri Counter, Empowered Mortal Draw at range. He resists poison but burn works. Bestowal Ninjutsu on the archer near the arena gives you a free buff to start.
  • He has a nasty Mikiri of his own, so don't throw thrust attacks when you're low.

Demon of Hatred (optional)

Where: Ashina Outskirts, where General Naomori Kawarada was. After returning from Fountainhead Palace (post Divine Dragon), interact with the Sculptor's Idol surrounded by fire (Flames of Hatred). Deathblows: 3. Reward: Memory: Hatred Demon and 2 Lapis Lazuli.

  • Positioning: stay by his leading leg and circle counter-clockwise; most of his attacks miss you there.
  • Phase 1 perilous: jump it, then sprint toward his leading leg to dodge the ground slash and fireballs.
  • Phase 2: the vertical slash spreads fire to both sides; jump right after it lands or grapple back to him.
  • Phase 3: run far away as it starts (a ring of fire). The new perilous slash hits twice; jump both, then jump the follow-up. Stay close and circle to avoid homing fireballs.
  • Best tools: Malcontent (Finger Whistle upgrade) stuns him up to three times for big free damage. Suzaku's Lotus Umbrella blocks most of his fire. Divine Confetti adds damage.
  • His posture is huge, so plan to win by draining health. Blocking his non-fire hits costs no health, so block when unsure.

Genichiro, Way of Tomoe and Isshin, the Sword Saint (Kuro path final boss)

Where: Ashina Reservoir, down the tunnel by the Near Secret Passage idol, in the field of pampas grass. Required on every ending except Shura. Deathblows: 4 in total (1 Genichiro, Way of Tomoe, then 3 Isshin). If either kills you, you restart at Genichiro. Reward: Memory: Saint Isshin and the Dragon Flash Combat Art.

  • Genichiro, Way of Tomoe: one health bar, no lightning. Deflect, Mikiri his leaping thrust, deathblow him.
  • Isshin phase 1: Ashina Cross (flash at his hand): deflect twice fast or run away. Dragon Flash: deflect the first wave, dodge the second. Charged force wave: deflect as his arm moves, or sprint and jump on his head. Stay out of his long-reach slash.
  • Phase 2 (spear and gun): dodge the double projected-force discs. Deflect all four shots of his gun burst (the last is delayed; blocking breaks your posture); a thrust usually follows, so Mikiri it, and if he sweeps instead, jump.
  • Phase 3 (lightning): jump his lightning slashes and send them back (Lightning Reversal). Mist Raven and Sakura Dance also redirect it. Mikiri his thrusts for easy openings.
  • Tools: Loaded Umbrella blocks most of phases 1 and 2 and hurts his posture. Shinobi Firecracker and burning also mark as weaknesses.
  • Approach: spacing wins. Sidestep out of his combos to bait thrusts (Mikiri) and overheads, and drain his health so his posture stops recovering.

Gauntlet-only bosses (free update)

Added in the free October 2019 update (patch 1.05)

The Gauntlets of Strength are boss-rush modes added with Reflections of Strength. They include three harder 'Inner' versions of story bosses: Inner Father (a tougher Owl (Father), found in Gauntlet of Strength: Shura and Mortal Journey), Inner Genichiro and Inner Isshin. Use the same plans as the originals, but expect faster punishes and longer combos.

Mini-bosses: Ashina Outskirts

Most named mini-bosses drop a Prayer Bead; four beads make a Prayer Necklace (more health and posture). They don't respawn. Shichimen Warriors and Headless are apparitions, so bring Divine Confetti (and Pacifying Agent or the Mottled Purple Gourd for Terror).

Best weapons

Sekiro gives you one sword, Kusabimaru, and it never changes or upgrades; your damage grows with Attack Power from Memories. Your second weapon is the Mortal Blade (from the Divine Child in Senpou Temple's inner sanctum after the Folding Screen Monkeys), which unlocks the Mortal Draw Combat Art and is needed to finish immortal bosses. Everything else that changes how you fight is a Prosthetic Tool (fitted by the Sculptor at the Dilapidated Temple) or a Combat Art (bought with skill points or learned from texts). Tool and art advice assumes the current patch; patch 1.03 cut the emblem cost of several arts, including Dragon Flash.

Normal enemies

Regular enemies respawn when you rest. A good rule: if it has fur or feathers, the Firecracker works; if it wears a straw cape, set it on fire; if it's a ghost or Headless, use Divine Confetti; if it has a shield, use the Loaded Axe.

Frequently asked questions

What is the hardest boss in Sekiro?

Most players name Isshin, the Sword Saint (the final boss on the Kuro path) or the optional Demon of Hatred. Isshin has four deathblows including Genichiro, Way of Tomoe at the start, and three phases with guns, a spear and lightning.

Can you skip Lady Butterfly in Sekiro?

Yes. Lady Butterfly in Hirata Estate is optional, though she gives a good chunk of experience and a Sakura Droplet, and Hirata Estate also holds the Loaded Axe, Flame Barrel and Mist Raven's Feathers.

What is the best Prosthetic Tool in Sekiro?

The Shinobi Firecracker is the most useful overall because it stuns most enemies and almost every beast. The Loaded Umbrella and Loaded Axe are close behind for late-game bosses and armoured enemies.

What is the best Combat Art in Sekiro?

Ichimonji: Double from the Ashina Arts tree is the best all-round boss art: it costs no Spirit Emblems, crushes posture and recovers your own. Mortal Draw and Floating Passage are the best damage options.

How do you counter perilous attacks in Sekiro?

When the red kanji appears, check the move: Mikiri Counter (dodge into it) for thrusts, jump for sweeps, and dodge sideways or back for grabs. Jumping on a sweeping enemy's head deals extra posture damage.

Which bosses depend on the ending in Sekiro?

Emma and Isshin Ashina only appear on the Shura ending. Great Shinobi Owl, True Corrupted Monk, Divine Dragon and Isshin, the Sword Saint appear on the Kuro path. Owl (Father) only appears if you go for the Purification ending.

Where do you get the Mortal Blade in Sekiro?

From the Divine Child of Rejuvenation in the inner sanctum of Senpou Temple, Mt. Kongo, after you beat the Folding Screen Monkeys. It also unlocks the Mortal Draw Combat Art.

How many Prayer Beads are there in Sekiro?

There are 40 Prayer Beads, most dropped by mini-bosses. Every four make a Prayer Necklace that raises your vitality and posture. A few are only available on the Purification route.
