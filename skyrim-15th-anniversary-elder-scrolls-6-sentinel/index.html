Skyrim Turns 15 on November 11, and The Elder Scrolls 6 Finally Has a Name: Sentinel

By vAnother Level · Updated 2026-11-10
Home › The Elder Scrolls

Skyrim came out on November 11, 2011. That's 15 years ago tomorrow. And for the first time since then, the next Elder Scrolls game has a real name: Bethesda revealed The Elder Scrolls 6: Sentinel in a trailer on November 3, 2026.

Title and setting revealed by Bethesda (November 3, 2026)

What we know about Sentinel

  • Setting: Sentinel, the capital and largest city of Hammerfell.
  • When: the trailer's quote is dated 4E 206, about five years after Skyrim's story.
  • Release date: not announced yet.

Then vs now

  • 2011: Skyrim launches on Xbox 360, PS3 and PC.
  • 2018: Bethesda shows a short teaser at E3 with no name and no date.
  • 2026: the game finally gets its name, Sentinel, 15 years after Skyrim, a gap longer than the whole time between Morrowind (2002) and Skyrim (2011) and then some.
Title and setting from Bethesda's trailer, reported by Game Rant. Watch more on our YouTube channel: vAnother Level.
