Spectrum Is Giving Free Gig Speeds So You Can Download GTA 6 Faster

By vAnother Level · Updated 2026-11-09
Home › GTA 6 Guides

GTA 6 is a 212 GB download, and one internet provider is speeding it up for free. Spectrum's Gig Speed Lift upgrades eligible US customers to 1 Gig speeds, and customers already on 1 Gig can go up to 2 Gig in select areas. It runs from November 9 to November 30, 2026, which covers the preload, the November 19 launch and Thanksgiving.

Spectrum (Charter) promotion, reported by Dexerto and Tom's Guide

Heads up: some viral posts tonight credited this offer to Xfinity. The GTA 6 speed promotion that's been reported is Spectrum's. We couldn't find a matching Xfinity offer.

How it works

  • Who: existing Spectrum Internet customers in the US with compatible equipment.
  • How: turn it on in the My Spectrum app.
  • How fast: Spectrum says the 212 GB game can download in under 30 minutes at those speeds.
  • After November 30: your speed goes back to your normal plan, with no change to your bill.
  • It doesn't unlock the game early. Everyone still plays at their regional launch time on November 19.

Then vs now

GTA 5 on Xbox 360 shipped on two DVDs in 2013. GTA 6 has no disc at all at launch, and even the boxed version is a download code. At 212 GB, it's the first GTA where your internet speed decides how fast you can start playing.
