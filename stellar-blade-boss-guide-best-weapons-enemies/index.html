Stellar Blade: How to Beat Every Boss, Best Weapons & Enemies

By vAnother Level · Updated 2026-11-12
Home › Guides › Stellar Blade
On this page
  1. Every boss (in order)
  2. Best weapons
  3. Normal enemies

To beat every boss in Stellar Blade you need to read EVE's sword: red means parry, blue means Blink, purple means Repulse and yellow means Perfect Dodge or get out. This guide walks through every boss in story order (plus the optional ones and the post-launch Boss Challenge fights against Mann and Scarlet), then ranks the best Exospines, Gear, drone upgrades and skills, and finishes with the normal Naytiba that keep killing people.

Five rules that win most fights: 1) Watch EVE's blade, not just the boss. The warning glow on her sword is your cue, and the timing is when her weapon lights up. 2) Blue = push the stick toward the enemy + dodge (Blink). Purple = pull the stick away from the enemy + dodge (Repulse). 3) Red combos are parried hit by hit. Do not swing between hits; most bosses will not flinch. 4) Burst Skills give invincibility frames, so they are an emergency exit from a long combo. 5) Bosses have a Retribution counter (the diamonds under the health bar). Perfect Parries fill it; when it is full and the boss is staggered, use the on-screen prompt for a huge hit.
Written for the current PS5 and PC version (update 1.011 and later, which added Mann and the PC release).

How to read the warnings in this guide. Red = combo you parry (L1 at the hit). Blue = Blink: stick toward the boss + Circle as EVE's blade flashes blue; you warp behind it. Purple = Repulse: stick away from the boss + Circle; EVE knocks it back and often opens a weak point. Yellow = unblockable: Perfect Dodge it, jump it, or simply run. Blink, Repulse, Perfect Parry and Perfect Dodge are all Survival-tree skills; buy them as soon as they are offered.

Brute

Where: The prologue, right after the beach landing. It is a tutorial boss, so even if you go down, the story scene with Tachy plays and you move on.

  • What kills people: The explosions, not the arms. When it gets angry its core glows bright yellow and it starts throwing blasts, shockwaves and ground explosions. Back off until the light show ends.
  • Arm swings: It telegraphs every swing by repositioning a hand (right hand tucked to the left = uppercut; left hand on the floor = sweep; right hand hanging in the air = double sweep). Step back on the wind-up and let it whiff.
  • Punish window: It is huge and slow and stays off-balance for a few seconds after each swing. The double sweep has the longest recovery. Walk in after the second pass and do a full combo.
  • Approach: Use this fight to practise blocking and your first parries. Nothing here needs a skill you do not have yet.

Abaddon

Where: Eidos 7, on the roof of the Parking Tower during Scavenger Adam. This is the first real skill check, and the first boss that makes you Blink.

  • Opener: It always starts with a lunging cross slash. Parry it, but stay ready: the follow-up flashes blue and cannot be blocked. Blink through it and you land behind Abaddon with a free combo.
  • Phase 1 (sword combos): The plain cross slash and its one-two combo are both parryable and they are your main source of damage. Perfect-parry them to build Beta Energy and fill the Retribution diamonds.
  • Phase 2 (below 50%): Its blades are electrified, damage goes up and it gains ranged tools: an electric leap that leaves lightning on the floor, a forward shockwave, and small beams from its head. All three are answered with movement, not the guard. Strafe to the side and keep away from where it landed.
  • Punish window: Behind it after a Blink, and whenever its balance gauge breaks from parries. When it breaks, land a few normal hits first, then spend your Beta skill.
  • Approach: Stay close in phase 1 and farm parries. In phase 2, back off during the electric build-up and go back in once it starts swinging again.

Corrupter

Where: Eidos 7, shortly after you visit the Hall of Records. It is a poison-spitting Elite Naytiba that loves to run away from you.

  • Poison ball: It charges a ball on its head and fires it. Far away, just dodge. Close enough, hold Triangle to Rush in and hit it before the shot leaves.
  • Three-hit head combo: Parry all three. This is the attack you want; a clean parry drops its balance and leaves it open for a Triangle counter.
  • Acid rain / poison rain: It sprays poison upward or spits two wide blobs. Get as far away as you can; do not try to guard through it.
  • Charge: Sidestep. If it crashes into a wall or pillar it is briefly stunned, which is the biggest free window in the fight.
  • Spine burst: When its spines are open, do not attack from the front. When it jumps and crashes down, thorns erupt in a ring, so move out.
  • Approach: Keep using Rush to close distance every time it backs off, parry the head combo, and bait the charge near a wall. The fight ends with a short block prompt (L1).

Gigas

Where: The Crater, the last stop on Eidos 7. Gigas is an Alpha Naytiba and the first boss that throws every colour at you. Press X during the opening cutscene to avoid damage (you can skip it on retries).

  • Five-hit red combo (head glows): Parry all five. The timing has to be clean but they do not all need to be perfect. Do not counter between hits; Gigas will not flinch.
  • Blue moves: Drag and Grab (Blink), Double Hook and Slam (parry, parry, Blink), Head Swing (parry, then Blink). Wait until EVE's sword glows blue before you press; early Blinks are the main reason people get grabbed.
  • Back Grab: Also blue, but you are usually too close to Blink. Perfect Dodge it instead.
  • Yellow moves: Jump Slam (Perfect Dodge), Body Slam (it leans back and glows, so run, or Perfect Dodge if it is on top of you), Rock Throw and Earth Shatter (run as far as you can).
  • Below 60%: It mixes attacks less predictably and adds Double Hook and Swing. When it beats its chest, start running.
  • Punish window: Behind it after every Blink, and the Triplet Beta skill is great for interrupting its red attacks.
  • Approach: Half its moves are fist combos you parry, half are big area attacks you run from. Stay mid-range, parry the fists, Blink the blue grabs, and save Beta for after a Blink.

Royal Guard Quiel

Where: The southern Wasteland, guarding the way into Altess Levoire. It is a sparring duel; she does not die and later becomes a companion.

  • Polearm combos: Her strong strings glow red and come in fast bursts. Stay in front of her and parry each hit; aim for Perfect Parries to drain her balance quickly.
  • Her shield regenerates: If you back off, she refills. Keep interrupting her the moment a string ends with your own combo or a Beta skill.
  • Ranged option: She walks rather than charges, so early ranged shots chip her shield. Gear that boosts shield damage (Shield Destruction / Shield Penetration) helps.
  • Approach: Parry, punish immediately, repeat. Treat it as a parry exam before the harder duels later.

Maelstrom

Where: At the bottom of the Heart of the Infection in Altess Levoire. You fight it again, tougher and with more weak points, in Abyss Levoire. This is a gun fight: your sword is not the answer here.

  • Weak points: Three small orange blobs. Shoot all three to incapacitate it, then a large weak point opens in the centre. Unload on that, ideally with Shotgun Shells, then repeat the cycle.
  • Lava attacks: Lava shots and bursts are easy to sidestep. The three tracking lava balls (yellow) can be outrun or shot down. The lava laser and lava throw (yellow) can be outrun or parried.
  • Test Subjects: It flings small Naytiba in orbs that explode on contact. Kill them; they drop the ammo you need.
  • Shock wave: It does no damage but shoves you back, sometimes into exploding Test Subjects. Watch your footing.
  • Before you go in: Buy Enhanced Slugs (and ideally the Shotgun Shell Magazine) from the drone upgrade menu at a camp Repair Console. It turns a long, ammo-starved fight into a short one.

Stalker

Where: The Rail Yard in Matrix 11, during Alpha Signal. Beating it unlocks the Burst ability and skill tree, so collect everything it drops.

  • Four-sweep combos (red): Parry them. If the final swing turns purple, Repulse it; a successful Repulse exposes a weak point on its right shoulder that you can shoot in ranged mode.
  • Rock throw (yellow): Run perpendicular to the rocks. Guarding just shreds your shield.
  • Rush and jumping lunge (yellow): Perfect Dodge. For the lunge, time it to when its body hits the ground.
  • Double Grab (blue): Blink.
  • Below 60%: A seven-sweep string where the first six can be parried and the last is unblockable (dodge it), a roar that pushes you back, and a jumping predator attack (blue, Blink).
  • Punish window: After parries it loses balance; three normal hits into Triplet (and Quadruplet if you have it) is a strong punish. It has a Retribution counter of 12.
  • Approach: Parry the sweeps, Repulse the purple finisher, shoot the shoulder, and keep your distance when the 60% moveset starts.

Juggernaut

Where: Matrix 11, in the Temporary Armoury area of the sewers. Fast and aggressive for its size, and it sets the floor on fire.

  • Opener: Three shock waves from its weapon. Sprint out, or Perfect Dodge all three for free Burst Energy, then Rush in after the third.
  • Blue charged attacks: Its Charged Attack and Charged Slam are Blinks. Swing and Slam: parry the first hit, Repulse the purple second.
  • Below 70%: Its weapon catches fire. The weapon launch becomes unblockable (Perfect Dodge), a five-swing red string appears (strict parry timing on all five), and jump slams turn into fiery slams (yellow: dodge, run or jump).
  • Oil puddles: In phase 2 they ignite when it hits them. Never stand in one when it winds up.
  • Recommended Burst skill: Punishing Edge. Almost everything it does can be Perfect Dodged or Blinked, so Burst Energy fills fast.

Tachy

Where: The end of the Alpha Signal mission in Matrix 11, at the Contaminated Water Purification Plant. She is an Alpha Naytiba with a Retribution counter of 15. Beating her gets you the Tachyon Blade; after you return to Xion, Lily builds the device that unlocks Tachy Mode and the Tachy skill tree.

  • Signature string: A slash plus four swings: parry the red hits, Blink the blue fifth. At 75% it becomes a five-slash string, and at 45% a six-slash string, always ending in a blue Blink.
  • Rushing Slash (yellow): Perfect Dodge or jump.
  • From 75%: Three ranged blades (yellow, easy to avoid) and a purple counterattack she uses if you spam attacks. Repulse it, then switch to ranged mode and fire a Shotgun Shell for heavy damage.
  • From 45%: Ranged blades into a rush (run, then Perfect Dodge), teleport shock wave (run), teleport grab (blue, Blink).
  • Punish window: She flinches easily but hits very hard. Keep your strings short (about three hits) so you do not trigger the counter. Tempest (Burst) is invincible while active and knocks her down.
  • Setup: Reflex-Type Exospine plus Beta Enhancement / Beta Charge Gear.

Shael

Where: The north-eastern Great Desert, guarding the entrance to Abyss Levoire. Like Quiel, it is a sparring duel you must win to get in.

  • Attack pattern: Very similar to Quiel: strings of up to five hits, with the last hit an aerial attack. Strong ones glow red.
  • Bigger than she looks: The bar is short but her health and shield pools are large, and the shield regenerates if you leave her alone.
  • Approach: Parry every string, then hit back straight away with combos, Beta skills, Burst skills and Tachy Mode. Pressure is the whole fight.

Maelstrom (second fight)

Where: Abyss Levoire. Same plan as the first Maelstrom (shoot the small orange weak points, then the big central one), but it has more weak points and more health. Arrive with a full slug and shell stock, upgraded Slug Magazine Expansion, and kill the Test Subjects for ammo when you run low.

Belial

Where: Spire 4, during Eye of the Hurricane. The first time, it retreats at about 70% health. When you meet it again later in the mission, it fully regenerates twice, so you effectively beat it three times in one fight.

  • Phase 1: Double slash, triple slash and a five-slash red string: Perfect Parry all of them. Triple slash into back-step lunge: parry the slashes, Blink the blue lunge. The rushing lunge after each regen glows blue: Blink.
  • Phase 2 (after the first regen): The back-step lunge turns red-then-dodge (Perfect Dodge it now), and a rushing thrust is added (blue, Blink). The double 360 slash and slam is yellow then blue: Perfect Dodge, then Blink.
  • Mixed strings: Upward slash, slam and shockwave: parry, Perfect Dodge the slam, then run from the wave. Kick and slash: parry the kick, Perfect Dodge the yellow slash.
  • Phase 3 (after the second regen): A four-slash yellow string (Perfect Dodge each) and a homing slash that glows purple (Repulse).
  • Approach: Perfect Parries fuel Beta skills that interrupt it; Perfect Dodges fuel Burst skills with invincibility. Save Tachy Mode for phase 3, and learn Judgment Time beforehand so Tachy Mode lasts longer.

Karakuri

Where: Spire 4, in the garden next to the Prestige Lounge. A giant Kokeshi robot that summons waves of little Kokeshi.

  • Opener: It usually starts with a rotating laser beam (yellow). Jump early or back off.
  • Melee: Triple slash, slam-roll-slam (the roll still hurts) and a four-slash red string: Perfect Parry. The charging upward slash is parried when its hands turn red.
  • Lasers and explosions (yellow): Front laser, focused laser (below 60%), small explosion (nearby Kokeshi self-destruct too) and homing slam: back away or Perfect Dodge.
  • Blue moves: Immediate slash and back-dash slash. The blue light on the back-dash can be late, so wait for it before you Blink.
  • Kokeshi waves: Fire the Slash Beta skill (L1 + Triangle) the moment they spawn to clear a row. Below 60% it summons a small wave then a big one; ignore the first and wipe the second. Never leave stragglers.
  • Approach: Clear adds first, then fight the main body at close range where its moves are parryable.

Democrawler and Demogorgon

Where: The end of your Spire 4 visit. Demogorgon starts as soon as Democrawler goes down, so you do not get a break in between.

  • Democrawler, opening (100-80%): Scratches, slams and dashing swings are Perfect Parries. Triple scratch into body slam: parry, then Blink the blue slam. Grab: Blink. Homing light bullets: run, Perfect Dodge or parry to reflect. Homing large bullets: Perfect Parry (you cannot outrun them). Jumping shock wave: jump over it.
  • At 80% it draws a sword: A six-hit string (parry five red hits, Perfect Dodge the yellow sixth), triple slash and slam (parry, Blink), five slashes and tentacle grab (parry, then Repulse the purple grab). Laser sweeps, warp slam and light pillars are yellow: run and Perfect Dodge.
  • At 40%: The jumping shock wave hits twice (double jump), the laser goes to five beams (run as far as possible), and a double sword area attack is Perfect Dodge then Blink.
  • Demogorgon: A scripted gun fight. It does not attack. Shoot the giant eye, then shoot the weak spots that appear to reopen it, then shoot the eye again.
  • Setup: Lots of Perfect Dodges here, so Burst gear (Burst Trance-Type Exospine, Burst Charge, Burst Enhancement) pays off.

Unidentified Naytiba

Where: Xion, after you return from Spire 4. A furious Alpha Naytiba that changes form and mixes melee, fog and bullets.

  • Opening (above 85%): Triple and double diagonal slashes: Perfect Parry. Four slashes into mud crawl (red, then yellow: parry, then run) or into cross slash (parry, then Perfect Dodge). Triple spin into double slash: parry, Blink the blue double slash. Black fog jump: run.
  • At 85% it transforms: Long red strings of five or seven slashes (Perfect Parry), forward mud crawl (run), diving grab (blue, Blink), light bullets into a rushing slam (run, then jump), tracking darkness bullets (run). In the spin combo at this stage, Blink the fifth attack, not the fourth.
  • Around 50-60%: insta-death orbs. It summons three orbs that kill you if left alone. Shoot all three. Perfect Parries make it drop ammo, so keep your gun stocked before this point.
  • Below 40%: Black fog appears behind you as well as in front. Move to a gap between clouds.
  • Approach: If it hangs back, a ranged attack is coming; sprint sideways. Attack only after a move finishes.
  • Drops: Three-star Speed Increase Gear and Combo Attack Enhancement Gear.

Raven

Where: Late in the story, during The Last Fragment, in the Wasteland canyon at the big entrance to the Nest. She is one of the toughest story fights. She baits you with feints and teleports, and guns do not work on her.

  • Core moveset: Rotating slash, round-up and a dash into four red hits: parry or Perfect Parry. If you swing while she raises her sword, she answers with three counter hits; parry those too.
  • Teleports: Her teleport attack is yellow (Perfect Dodge). Teleport into rotating slashes ends in a blue hit (parry, then Blink). Her two-Blink attack needs two Blinks: she dodges the first, the second one lands you behind her.
  • Rotating slashes into purple: Parry the opening hits and Repulse the purple finisher.
  • Blade waves: Three yellow waves at range; dodge or Perfect Dodge.
  • Parry clash: When the prompt appears, mash Square or you take the hit.
  • Below 60%: A five-hit rotating string (Perfect Parry) and Tempest into a diving slash (run out of the Tempest, Perfect Dodge the dive). From here she counters Beta skills, so spend Beta on Counter and Reflection instead.
  • Below 30% (she laughs): An eight-hit string with a yellow hit in the middle. Parry the rest and Perfect Dodge the yellow, or use a Burst skill for its invincibility.
  • Setup: Reflex-Type Exospine and the Focus Boost skill for wider parry timing. Counter right after Perfect Parries, Reflection right after Perfect Dodges.

Adam (Winged)

Where: The Nest. A corrupted, winged Adam. He walks slowly, rarely attacks and goes down to steady pressure. After this fight you make the choice that decides your final boss and ending.

Final boss: Elder Naytiba or Providence

In the Nest, refusing Adam's hand sends you to the Elder Naytiba (the Return to the Colony ending). Taking Adam's hand sends you to Providence (the Cost of Lost Memories ending). You only fight one of them per playthrough, so a second run or a reload of your final save is needed to see both.

  • Elder Naytiba, melee: A double slash, a back-jump with wing projectiles, then a lunge. Parry or dodge the slashes and watch for the lunge at the end. A teleport thrust drops on you from above with an area blast, so move.
  • Elder Naytiba, light attacks: Homing energy balls (keep dodging), a light wall with energy balls (keep moving), and four thrown spears (block or dodge).
  • Elder Naytiba, halos and angels: It summons six halos; shoot all six to bring it down, but each destroyed halo releases an energy ball. When six small angels appear for a light wave, shoot them first with the drone.
  • Elder Naytiba, tip: Its worst attacks glow yellow. Perfect Dodge those for the counter windows.
  • Providence, opening: Single and double strikes are Perfect Parries. Long red strings come in two shapes: if it steps left, expect a seven-hit string; if it steps right, a five-hit string. In both, the late yellow hit is a Perfect Dodge.
  • Providence, mixed hits: Double strike, slam and sweep (parry, then Blink the blue sweep). Sweep and grab (parry, then Repulse the purple grab). Delayed double strike (Perfect Dodge the first, Blink the second). Dash shock waves are jumped.
  • Providence, 70% and below: It adds exploding orbs and a missile launcher (run or Perfect Dodge) and a single, charged double strike and grab (parry, Blink, Repulse).
  • Providence, 50%: insta-death. A shield goes up; break it with your gun right away, then run or Perfect Dodge the blast.
  • Providence, 40%: A cutscene puts you in Tachy Mode. You cannot lose health now, but a timer runs on the right side of the screen. Go all-in and finish it before the timer ends.
  • Setup for either: Reflex-Type Exospine, Burst gear, and a full stock of slugs.

Boss Challenge mode and post-launch bosses

Boss Challenge mode arrived in update 1.003 (May 2024). You pick it from the main menu, and every story boss you have beaten is added to it. It also offers presets, including a Maximum preset with a completed skill tree and top-tier gear, which is the easiest way to learn the post-launch bosses. Beating every boss in the mode on Normal or higher is reported to unlock the Neurolink Suit.

Mann was added in update 1.011 (June 11, 2025, alongside the PC release). Scarlet is part of the paid Goddess of Victory: NIKKE DLC.

Mann (Boss Challenge)

Unlock: Finish the story once (beat whichever final boss your ending gives you), then open Boss Challenge. No New Game+ needed. He is the leader of the Sentinels and is not in the open world or tied to any quest. Beating him on Normal or higher gives EVE a new outfit.

  • Phase 1 (sword and guns): The opening two-hit laser sweep, the three-hit dual leaping strike and the four-hit knife frenzy are Perfect Parries. Two sweeps into a lunging thrust: parry the sweeps, Blink the blue thrust.
  • Phase 1 ranged tricks: Rocket launcher, smoke screen and diving strike are yellow: Perfect Dodge. Incendiary grenades: dodge. The two-bullet leaping strike: parry the bullets, Perfect Dodge the leap.
  • Phase 2 (around 70%): He switches to a big axe, and enough overhead smashes turn it into a glaive with longer strings. Flaming diving strike and uppercut into smash are yellow (Perfect Dodge). The five-hit glaive sweep and overhead smash into leaping thrust are Perfect Parries. Retreating flame wave: parry the first hit, Perfect Dodge the wave. Final laser sweep combo: parry five, Perfect Dodge the yellow last hit.
  • Approach: Stay close. His melee is far easier to parry than his gun and rocket game is to dodge. After parried strings, combo freely; when cornered, burn a Burst skill for the invincibility.
  • Setup: Maximum preset, Reflex-Type Exospine, and Beta Trance-Type to stagger him more often.

Scarlet (NIKKE DLC, Boss Challenge)

Unlock: You need the Goddess of Victory: NIKKE DLC (sold on its own or in the Twin Expansion Pack on PS5; included in the Complete Edition on Steam). With the Wasteland open, talk to the robot dog Volt south of the Wasteland entrance, finish Scarlet and Volt's missions, and buy every NIKKE cosmetic from Volt's shop with Bone-Wrenches. Scarlet then appears in Boss Challenge. Beating her on Normal unlocks her outfit, a hairstyle and a hidden song.

  • Phase 1: Mostly long sword strings, nearly all parryable: five-hit overhead slash, four-hit sweeping slash, five-hit thrust, illusion strike. Her opening four-hit combo ends in a yellow hit, so dodge the fourth.
  • Yellow and blue moves: Backstep dash sweep and Tempest dash are dodges. Roundhouse kick into dive: parry the kick, dodge the dive. Falling Blossom Dive: parry the opening, dodge the yellow dive, Blink the blue follow-up. Whirlwind into iai slash: parry, then dodge.
  • Phase 2 (dark arena, spotlight): She attacks through illusions. Parry at least five illusion hits to drain her shield, then stagger her and unload after her combo ends. Tempest dash into deadly thrust: dodge, then Repulse the purple thrust.
  • Final stretch: Near the end of her health she repeats the illusion attack with a three-hit ranged add-on before her final combo. Parry it out to win.
  • Setup: Reflex-Type Exospine (add Protection-Type in a second slot), all three Beta gears, and the Triplet and Shock Wave Beta skills. Shock Wave staggers her from range, which buys time to heal. She is balanced like an endgame boss, so come in fully upgraded.

Best weapons

EVE only has one sword (it is upgraded through the story and at camp Repair Consoles), so in Stellar Blade your real loadout is your Exospine, your four Gear sockets, your drone (Blaster) upgrades and your skill picks. The tier list below ranks them for boss fights on the current version (1.011+). Defensive picks rank high because the game's damage comes from parries, Blinks and Repulses.

Exospines: where to get them

Exospines come from red cube-shaped crates in the world (none are shop items), and you equip one at a time to start. They level up with materials; upgrading ten to max is the Perfect Exospine trophy.

Skill picks

Spend SP at camp kiosks. The Burst tree opens after Stalker drops the Burst ability in Matrix 11; the Tachy tree and Tachy Mode (L3 + R3) open in Xion after you beat Tachy and Lily builds the Tachyon device.

  1. Survival first: Perfect Parry, Perfect Dodge, Focus Boost (wider parry timing), Threat Detection (more time on Blink/Repulse), Blink, Repulse, then Counter and Reflection for free punishes after perfect parries and dodges.
  2. Attack: Rush (Triangle hold) to close distance, then the Beta Energy Recharge passives.
  3. Beta: Shield Breaker (cheap, cracks shields) and Triplet (interrupts red strings; finish its upgrades before buying another Beta skill). Add Slash for Kokeshi rows and Shock Wave for ranged stagger later.
  4. Burst: Tempest (invincible, knocks bosses down) and Punishing Edge (big single-target hit).
  5. Tachy: Judgment Time to extend Tachy Mode before Belial and the final bosses.

Frequently asked questions

How many bosses are in Stellar Blade?

This guide covers 19 story encounters from Brute to the final boss (counting Democrawler and Demogorgon separately and both Maelstrom fights). You fight only one of the two final bosses per run, and there are two optional side-quest bosses (a second Gigas and Behemoth). Mann and Scarlet were added later and are only in Boss Challenge mode.

What is the hardest boss in Stellar Blade?

Most players point to Raven in the story, because she feints, teleports and is immune to guns, and to Scarlet and Mann in Boss Challenge. Providence is the hardest final boss.

How do you Blink and Repulse in Stellar Blade?

When EVE's blade flashes blue, push the left stick toward the enemy and press Circle to Blink behind it. When it flashes purple, pull the stick away from the enemy and press Circle to Repulse. Both are Survival-tree skills, and Threat Detection widens their timing.

What is the best Exospine in Stellar Blade?

Reflex-Type for most players, because it makes Perfect Parry and Perfect Dodge easier and regenerates your shield faster. Beta Trance-Type is the best damage pick. Both are found in Matrix 11.

How do you unlock Mann in Stellar Blade?

Finish the story once, then open Boss Challenge from the main menu. Mann was added in update 1.011 on June 11, 2025, the same day as the PC release, and is free.

How do you fight Scarlet in Stellar Blade?

You need the Goddess of Victory: NIKKE DLC. Talk to the robot dog Volt in the Wasteland, complete Scarlet and Volt's missions, buy every NIKKE cosmetic from Volt's shop with Bone-Wrenches, and then Scarlet appears in Boss Challenge.

Which final boss do you fight in Stellar Blade?

It depends on your choice in the Nest: refuse Adam's hand and you fight the Elder Naytiba; take his hand and you fight Providence.

When do you unlock Tachy Mode?

After you beat Tachy at the end of Alpha Signal in Matrix 11. Back in Xion, Lily builds the Tachyon device and the Tachy skill tree opens. Press L3 and R3 together when Tachy Energy is full.
