Piece by Piece

Two Completely Different Games Called 'Piece by Piece' Launch on Switch the Same Day, and Nintendo 'Hated' It

By vAnother Level · Updated 2026-11-11
Home › Piece by Piece

Same name. Same publisher. Same release day. Totally different games. On November 25, two unrelated games both called Piece by Piece hit the Switch eShop, and you can buy them together in the Piece by Piece Double Bundle.

Announced by No More Robots; reported by VGC (November 11, 2026)

Which Piece by Piece is which

  • Piece by Piece (Gamkat): a relaxing game about repairing items for woodland animals. On Steam since March 11, 2026. Switch price $11.99 / £9.99.
  • Piece by Piece (Neon Polygons): a 100-level puzzle platformer about connecting and disconnecting jigsaw pieces. On Steam since March 13, 2026, two days later. Switch price $12.99 / £10.99.
  • Double Bundle: $20.99 / £17.85, 10% off until December 2. Each game is also 20% off until December 2.

How did this happen? Neon Polygons couldn't afford a Switch port, so No More Robots, the publisher of the other Piece by Piece, signed it. Founder Mike Rose said on Bluesky that Nintendo's certification team was not happy, because two same-named games from one publisher on the same day broke their database entries: "Nintendo have ABSOLUTELY HATED THIS."

Then vs now: same-name games aren't new (Arkane's Prey in 2017 had nothing to do with 2006's Prey), but two unrelated ones sold as a single bundle might be a first.

Reported by VGC; quote from Mike Rose (No More Robots) on Bluesky. Images: official Steam screenshots from Gamkat and Neon Polygons. Watch more on our YouTube channel: vAnother Level.

Frequently asked questions

When do the Piece by Piece games come out on Switch?

Both launch on the Switch eShop on November 25, 2026, separately or as a Double Bundle.

Are the two Piece by Piece games related?

No. One is Gamkat's cozy repair game; the other is Neon Polygons' jigsaw puzzle platformer. Both are published on Switch by No More Robots.
