Warhammer 40K: Dark Heresy Release Date Is January 28, 2027

By vAnother Level · Updated 2026-11-09
Home › Warhammer 40,000: Dark Heresy
On this page

  2. Platforms, beta and pre-orders
  3. What kind of game is Dark Heresy?

Warhammer 40,000: Dark Heresy finally has a date: January 28, 2027. Owlcat Games, the studio behind Warhammer 40,000: Rogue Trader, announced it today (November 9, 2026) with a new release date trailer. You'll be playing as an Inquisition acolyte in under three months.

Release date January 28, 2027 (Owlcat Games)

The release date trailer

Owlcat posted the trailer on X, and it also went up on YouTube and Steam:

The release date for Warhammer 40,000: Dark Heresy is set. On January 28, 2027, prepare to venture into the Calixis Sector and meet the dire Night Lords.

— Owlcat Games (@OwlcatGames) November 9, 2026

The trailer runs just over a minute. It cuts between the party exploring the Calixis Sector, warp rifts tearing open and a lot of turn-based fire, then ends on the key art and the date. Owlcat's post also names the threat: the Night Lords, the Chaos Space Marine legion, are waiting for you in the Calixis Sector.

Platforms, beta and pre-orders

  • PC: Steam, GOG and the Epic Games Store.
  • Consoles: PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, same day.
  • Nintendo Switch 2: coming, but Owlcat says details will come later. No date yet.
  • Beta: open now to all founders, and it closes January 7, 2027 so the team can prepare for launch.
  • Founder's Packs and Collector's Editions: on sale until the beginning of January. Owlcat asks Collector's Edition buyers to check their shipping address; physical items ship soon after launch.

What kind of game is Dark Heresy?

It's a party-based, story-driven cRPG with turn-based tactical combat. You join the Inquisition as an acolyte, build a war band and hunt heresy across the Calixis Sector. Owlcat says companions bring their own skills to both combat and investigations, and that your choices have consequences, including ones you didn't intend.

Recruitable companions named so far: Cogg, Saro, Discordance, Laarthyr, Ra'akhti, Haymar, Chorda and Touma.

Then vs now: 19 years almost to the day

Here's the detail nobody's pointing out. Dark Heresy started as a pen-and-paper RPG. Black Industries released the original rulebook on January 25, 2008. Fantasy Flight Games put out a second edition in 2014. The video game lands on January 28, 2027, three days short of that book's 19th birthday.

Owlcat did the same thing before: Warhammer 40,000: Rogue Trader (2023) also took its name from a tabletop RPG line. Dark Heresy was first announced in May 2025, so the full release lands about 20 months after the reveal.
