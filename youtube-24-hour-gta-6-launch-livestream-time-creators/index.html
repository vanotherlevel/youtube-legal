YouTube is running a 24-hour GTA 6 launch livestream on its own official channel. A pre-show hosted by RDCWorld starts at 11 PM ET on November 18, and the main marathon starts at midnight ET on November 19, the moment GTA 6 unlocks in the US East.

OFFICIAL: announced by YouTube on its blog and on X ("24 hours. 11/18. 11pm ET.")

YouTube's line for it: "The biggest launch of all time deserves the biggest livestream." It's a joint push with Rockstar Games, and it lands hours after reviews put GTA 6 at the top of Metacritic and New Zealand got the game first.

  • Live gameplay from creators playing GTA 6 on day one
  • Creator podcasts and original shows between play sessions
  • On-the-ground coverage from Miami to close the broadcast, with creators and special guests

Why Miami?

Launch week in Miami is packed. Rockstar's own GTA VI: The Festival runs there on November 21, and the Miami HEAT are holding a Vice City night. The stream's Miami finale ties into that real-world hype.

THEORY: whether the stream's Miami segment visits the Festival site hasn't been announced
Spoiler warning: a 24-hour stream of day-one gameplay will show story missions. If you're avoiding spoilers, watch the pre-show and the Miami segments, and skip the long play sessions.

Frequently asked questions

What time does YouTube's GTA 6 livestream start?

The pre-show with RDCWorld starts at 11 PM ET on November 18 (8 PM PT). The 24-hour marathon starts at midnight ET on November 19 (9 PM PT on November 18).

Where can I watch the YouTube GTA 6 launch stream?

On YouTube's official channel, youtube.com/@YouTube, which has GTA VI branding for launch week.

Which creators are in the GTA 6 YouTube livestream?

31 channels in 10 languages, including Caylus, Jelly, TGG, Kinda Funny Games, Loserfruit, Cinna, Total Gaming, JuanSGuarnizo and Windah Basudara.

Will the stream have GTA 6 spoilers?

Very likely. It includes hours of live day-one gameplay, so story missions will be shown.
