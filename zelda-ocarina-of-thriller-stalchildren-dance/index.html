"Ocarina of Thriller": Hyrule Field's Stalchildren Do Michael Jackson's Thriller

By vAnother Level · Updated 2026-11-09
Home › Zelda: Ocarina of Time Remake Guides

Hyrule Field at night just got a music video. A short clip titled "The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Thriller" shows Link walking through the field while a pack of Stalchildren rise up and dance behind him, Michael Jackson style. It has passed 28,000 views and 1,500 likes on X.

Why it works

Since the original Ocarina of Time in 1998, Hyrule Field has had one rule: when the sun goes down, Stalchildren crawl out of the ground and chase Link until morning. Pair that with Thriller (1983), a video where the dead climb out of their graves to dance, and the joke writes itself.

The remake has given fans plenty of these moments. Earlier this week it was "Link-in Park". Playing the real thing? Track every collectible with our All 100 Gold Skulltula Locations guide.
