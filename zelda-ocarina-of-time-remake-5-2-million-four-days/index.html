Ocarina of Time Remake Sold 5.2 Million in 4 Days, Already Beating Four Zelda Games' Total Sales

By vAnother Level · Updated 2026-11-11
Home › Zelda: Ocarina of Time Remake Guides

Four days. 5.2 million copies. Nintendo says The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time remake sold 5.2 million units by the end of November 9, physical and digital combined. It launched on November 5 as a Switch 2 exclusive, and Nintendo calls it the fastest-selling first-party Switch 2 game globally so far.

Sales figure announced by Nintendo, reported by VGC on November 11, 2026

4 days vs other Zeldas' whole lives

We put Nintendo's numbers side by side. In four days the remake has already passed the total sales of several full Zelda games:

  • Ocarina of Time remake: 5.2M in 4 days.
  • A Link Between Worlds: 4.26M.
  • Phantom Hourglass: 4.13M.
  • Echoes of Wisdom: 4.09M after seven months.
  • Spirit Tracks: 2.61M.
  • Still a long way to go: Breath of the Wild is at 35.76M and Tears of the Kingdom at 22.71M.

Then vs now

The original Ocarina of Time on Nintendo 64 is usually listed at about 7.6 million copies over its entire life. The remake reached roughly two-thirds of that in its first four days. In the UK it also had the series' second-biggest physical launch, behind only Tears of the Kingdom.
