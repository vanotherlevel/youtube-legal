There are 100 Gold Skulltulas in the Zelda Ocarina of Time Remake: 44 inside dungeons and 56 out in the overworld, split evenly between young Link (50) and adult Link (50). Every one drops a token, and the House of Skulltula in Kakariko Village pays you at 10, 20, 30, 40, 50 and 100 tokens.

This guide lists all 100 area by area, with the same numbers as our video. For each one you get the exact spot (floor, room, landmark), whether you need young or adult Link, whether it's a night-only Skulltula, and the item that gets you there. Tick them off as you go: the checklists save in your browser.

Quick rules: 27 of the 100 only appear at night (play the Sun's Song to skip ahead). Ten are soft soil patches that need bugs from a bottle. Trees you can roll into often drop one. If a spot looks empty, check the time of day and your age first.

Gold Skulltula Rewards: What Every Token Milestone Gives You

Bring your tokens to the House of Skulltula in Kakariko Village. A rich man and his sons were cursed into spiders, and every 10 tokens cures one more of the family. Each one hands you a reward when you talk to them. You don't need to turn tokens in one by one: the count is automatic, so visit whenever you pass through Kakariko.

Overworld Gold Skulltula Locations (56)

Areas are in the order you first reach them, which is the same order as the video. Each area lists young Link tokens first; adult tokens are marked, so you can skip them on your first pass and come back with the Hookshot.

Hyrule Castle & Castle Town Gold Skulltulas (4)

Four tokens around the castle: three as young Link and one as adult Link.

#5 is in the Castle Town guard house, the room full of pots with a guard inside. #6 is on the castle grounds: once you sneak past the guards into the garden, roll into the lone tree on the lawn by the stone wall. You need daytime for it, because that's when you can get onto the grounds.

#8 is the one most players miss. Stand by the lone tree next to the castle gate wall and play the Song of Storms to open a hidden grotto. Drop in with Bombs and the Boomerang, walk to the back of the cave and Boomerang the Skulltula off the wall. #7 waits until you're an adult: walk the path up from Castle Town toward Ganon's Castle at night and Hookshot it off the top of the ruined stone archway.

  • GS #5: Guard house full of pots, on top of the crate in the back corner
    Young Link, any time. Needs: Fairy Slingshot. Enter the guard house in Castle Town, and shoot the Gold Skulltula on the crate in the corner with the Slingshot; grab the token.
  • GS #6: Castle grounds garden, lone tree on the lawn by the stone wall
    Young Link, daytime. Needs: No special item (sword or a roll). After sneaking past the guards into the castle gardens, roll into the tree on the lawn next to the wall; the Gold Skulltula drops out, kill it and take the token.
  • GS #7: Top of the ruined stone archway on the path above Castle Town
    Adult Link, night only. Needs: Hookshot. As adult, go up the path from Castle Town toward Ganon's Castle. Look up at the ruined archway and Hookshot the Skulltula on top of it.
  • GS #8: Song of Storms grotto by the tree next to the castle gate wall, Skulltula on the cave wall
    Young Link, any time. Needs: Song of Storms, Boomerang, Bombs. As child, play the Song of Storms next to the lone tree by the castle wall to open a hole. Drop in, walk to the back of the cave and Boomerang the Skulltula on the wall.
#8 needs the Song of Storms, so mark it and come back as young Link later in the game. It sits in the same chapter of the video as the other castle tokens.

Kakariko Village Gold Skulltulas (6)

Kakariko is home to the House of Skulltula where you cash in every token, so start here. It also holds six tokens of its own.

Five of them are young Link at night. Come in at night (or play the Sun's Song) and work through the village in one loop: the blue door, the tree on the village green, the house under construction, the wooden watchtower, then the house with the lit window down the path from the tower. Three of those five need the Fairy Slingshot; the other two you can simply slash or roll into.

The sixth, #14, is an adult token. Come back as adult Link, play the Sun's Song for night, and Hookshot it off the wall next to the house with the red door by the wooden fence.

  • GS #9: Stone wall beside a house's blue front door
    Young Link, night only. Needs: No special item (sword or a roll). At night, climb the steps to the house with the blue door and hit the Gold Skulltula on the wall to the left of the door with the sword.
  • GS #10: Tree in the middle of the village green
    Young Link, night only. Needs: No special item (sword or a roll). At night, roll into the tree in the village; the Gold Skulltula drops to the ground, slash it and take the token.
  • GS #11: On the brick wall of the house under construction, near the village entrance
    Young Link, night only. Needs: Fairy Slingshot. As child at night, go to the half-built house with the wooden scaffolding. Shoot the Skulltula on the brick wall with the Slingshot, then climb the scaffolding planks to grab the token.
  • GS #12: On the wooden lookout tower up the stone steps, climb its ladder for the token
    Young Link, night only. Needs: Fairy Slingshot. As child at night, go up the stone steps to the wooden watchtower. Shoot the Skulltula on the tower with the Slingshot, then climb the ladder to collect the token.
  • GS #13: On the stone side wall of a house below the lit window, down the path from the watchtower
    Young Link, night only. Needs: Fairy Slingshot. As child at night, walk down from the watchtower to the house with the lit window. Shoot the Skulltula on its side wall with the Slingshot and grab the token.
  • GS #14: Between the wooden fence and a house with a red door, on the dark tree/wall beside the house
    Adult Link, night only. Needs: Hookshot, Sun's Song. As adult in Kakariko, play the Sun's Song to make it night. Walk to the house with the red door by the fence and Hookshot the Skulltula off the wall next to it.
By the time you finish Kakariko's child tokens you can have 11 (Deku Tree, Castle Town, the castle garden and these five), which is enough for the first reward. Walk into the House of Skulltula on your way out.

Lon Lon Ranch Gold Skulltulas (4)

Three child tokens and one adult token. Two of the child ones only appear at night, so arrive after dark or wait for the sun to set.

#15 is in the tree beside the path by the ranch house: roll into it and the Skulltula drops at its base. #16 is on the ranch house wall by the lit upper window, right next to the entrance arch; Boomerang it and the token flies back to you. #17 is in the lone tree near the entrance at any time of day.

#94 comes much later. When the ranch belongs to Ingo, go in at night as adult Link, enter the round stone tower at the edge of the field, and Hookshot the Skulltula off the brick wall above the hay pile. The video covers it in its own chapter at 31:10.

  • GS #15: Tree beside the path by the ranch house / fence
    Young Link, night only. Needs: No special item (sword or a roll). At night in Lon Lon Ranch, roll into the tree next to the ranch house path; the Gold Skulltula drops at its base, slash it and grab the token.
  • GS #16: On the ranch house wall by the upper window, next to the entrance arch
    Young Link, night only. Needs: Boomerang. At night, stand near the ranch house by the entrance arch and Boomerang the Skulltula by the lit window; the Boomerang brings the token back.
  • GS #17: In the lone tree near the ranch entrance
    Young Link, any time. Needs: No special item (sword or a roll). Roll or swing into the tree near the entrance; the Skulltula drops out, kill it and grab the token.
  • GS #94: Inside the round stone tower by the ranch, high on the brick wall above the hay pile
    Adult Link, night only. Needs: Hookshot. At night as adult, go into Lon Lon Ranch and enter the round stone tower at the edge of the field. Look up above the hay pile and Hookshot the Skulltula off the brick wall.

Watch it in the video: 6:11 and 31:10.

Kakariko Graveyard Gold Skulltulas (2)

Two child tokens. #18 is a soft soil patch by the graveyard's left wall near the entrance: pour bugs from a bottle on it and kill the Skulltula that pops out. #19 only appears at night, high on the graveyard's wooden wall. Look for the glow and Boomerang it.

  • GS #18: Soft soil patch by the graveyard's left wall
    Young Link, any time. Needs: Bottle with Bugs. Release bugs from a bottle onto the soft soil patch; a Gold Skulltula pops out, kill it with the sword and take the token.
  • GS #19: High on the wooden wall in the graveyard
    Young Link, night only. Needs: Boomerang. At night in the graveyard, look up the wooden wall for the glowing Skulltula and Boomerang it; the Boomerang brings the token back.
Catch bugs under rocks before you leave Kakariko. Soft soil patches give you ten of the 100 tokens, and every one of them needs a bottle of bugs.

Death Mountain Trail Gold Skulltulas (4)

Two child tokens and two adult tokens on the trail.

#20 is a soft soil patch just outside the Dodongo's Cavern entrance: bugs, then sword. #21 is behind the cracked wall of a small red-rock cave on the trail. Blow it open with a Bomb and slash the Skulltula inside.

The adult pair both need the Megaton Hammer and the Hookshot. #22 is on the lower trail above Dodongo's Cavern, by the railing: smash the rock pile in the dark cliff alcove, then Hookshot the Skulltula behind it. #23 is further up, on the falling-rocks path past the rock arch. Smash the boulder against the cliff and Hookshot what's hiding behind it.

  • GS #20: Soft soil patch just outside Dodongo's Cavern entrance
    Young Link, any time. Needs: Bottle with Bugs. Drop bugs on the soft soil patch by the Dodongo's Cavern entrance; kill the Gold Skulltula that bursts out and collect the token.
  • GS #21: Behind the bombable wall in the red-rock cave on the trail
    Young Link, any time. Needs: Bombs. Bomb the cracked wall on Death Mountain Trail to open a small cave; slash the Gold Skulltula inside and take the token.
  • GS #22: Lower trail above Dodongo's Cavern: rock pile in the dark cliff alcove
    Adult Link, any time. Needs: Megaton Hammer, Hookshot. On the lower Death Mountain Trail by the Dodongo's Cavern railing, smash the rock pile in the dark alcove with the Megaton Hammer, then hookshot the Gold Skulltula behind it.
  • GS #23: Upper trail: behind a smashable boulder
    Adult Link, any time. Needs: Megaton Hammer, Hookshot. Head up the trail past the rock arch, smash the boulder against the cliff with the Megaton Hammer and hookshot the Gold Skulltula it was hiding.

Zora's River Gold Skulltulas (5)

Three child tokens and two adult tokens along the river.

#29 is a soft soil patch on the riverbank (bugs). #30 is on the big tree trunk by the path just inside the river; either the Boomerang or the Slingshot works. #31 only shows at night: follow the river up to the stone platforms below the waterfall, just before Zora's Domain, and Boomerang it out of the rock wall alcove on the ledge.

As adult, both tokens are night-only Hookshot targets. #32 is high on the cliff above the raised stone ledge by the waterfall into Zora's Domain. #33 is on the grassy cliff wall below a small tree, beside the river path. Play the Sun's Song if it's daytime.

  • GS #29: Soft soil patch on the riverbank
    Young Link, any time. Needs: Bottle with Bugs. Release bugs on the soft soil patch beside the river; kill the Gold Skulltula that comes out and take the token.
  • GS #30: In the tree trunk near the river entrance
    Young Link, any time. Needs: Boomerang or Slingshot. Just inside Zora's River, look up the big tree by the path; hit the Skulltula on the trunk and grab the token.
  • GS #31: Rock wall alcove on the upper ledge by the stone platforms, just before Zora's Domain
    Young Link, night only. Needs: Boomerang. Follow the river up to the stone platforms below the waterfall; the Skulltula sits in a rock wall alcove on the ledge, knock it down and collect it.
  • GS #32: Upper river by the waterfall to Zora's Domain: high on the cliff above the raised stone ledge
    Adult Link, night only. Needs: Hookshot. At night, go to the top of Zora's River near the waterfall entrance to Zora's Domain, stand on the raised stone ledge and hookshot the Gold Skulltula on the cliff wall above.
  • GS #33: High on the grassy cliff wall below a small tree, beside the river path
    Adult Link, night only. Needs: Hookshot, Sun's Song. As adult, go to Zora's River and play the Sun's Song to make it night. Look up the cliff wall under the small tree and Hookshot the Skulltula.

Lake Hylia Gold Skulltulas (4)

All four tokens here revolve around the Lakeside Laboratory, plus the big dead tree.

As young Link, #34 is the soft soil patch beside the lab (bugs), and #35 appears at night on the lab's back wall by the pipe. Walk around the building and Boomerang it.

As adult Link, #36 is inside the lab at the bottom of the diving pool. Put on the Iron Boots (with the Zora Tunic on), sink to the floor, break the crate and Hookshot the Skulltula hiding in it. #37 is in the hollow of the big dead tree on the lakeside cliff around nightfall. Climb up and slash it with your sword.

  • GS #34: Soft soil patch beside the Lakeside Laboratory
    Young Link, any time. Needs: Bottled Bugs. Release bugs from a bottle over the soft soil next to the lab; the Skulltula pops out of the ground, kill it and grab the token.
  • GS #35: On the outside wall of the Lakeside Laboratory, by the pipe
    Young Link, night only. Needs: Boomerang. At night, walk round the lab to the pipe on its back wall and Boomerang the Skulltula; grab the token.
  • GS #36: Lakeside Laboratory: bottom of the diving pool, inside a crate
    Adult Link, any time. Needs: Iron Boots, Zora Tunic, Hookshot. In the Lakeside Laboratory, put on the Iron Boots and sink to the bottom of the diving pool. Break the crate on the floor and hookshot the Gold Skulltula hiding in it.
  • GS #37: Big dead tree on the lakeside cliff: inside the hollow of the trunk
    Adult Link, night only. Needs: Sword. At Lake Hylia around nightfall, climb up to the big hollow dead tree trunk by the water and slash the Gold Skulltula inside the hollow.

Zora's Fountain Gold Skulltulas (3)

Two child tokens on your first visit, and one adult token in the frozen fountain.

#42 is in the lone tree by the rock wall on the left side: roll into it. #43 is a night token on the mossy rock wall above the pond; Boomerang it.

#98 is the hardest to spot. As adult Link, go to the frozen Zora's Fountain, play the Sun's Song for night, and use the Lens of Truth to reveal a hidden cave. Go in and Hookshot the Skulltula on its back wall. The video covers it at 32:44.

  • GS #42: On the lone tree by the rock wall
    Young Link, any time. Needs: No special item (sword or a roll). In Zora's Fountain go to the tree by the cliff wall and roll or attack into it; the Skulltula drops out, kill it for the token.
  • GS #43: On the mossy rock wall above the water
    Young Link, night only. Needs: Boomerang. At night, face the rock wall near the pond and Boomerang the Skulltula on it; the Boomerang brings the token back.
  • GS #98: Hidden cave in the icy fountain shown by the Lens of Truth, Skulltula high on the cave's back wall
    Adult Link, night only. Needs: Hookshot, Lens of Truth, Sun's Song. As adult at the frozen Zora's Fountain, play the Sun's Song for night and use the Lens of Truth to find the hidden cave. Go in and Hookshot the Skulltula on the back wall.

Watch it in the video: 14:11 and 32:44.

Lost Woods Gold Skulltulas (4)

Three child tokens and one adult token.

All three child tokens are soft soil patches, so bring a full bottle of bugs. #44 is beyond the boulder grotto path, #45 is the second patch deeper in the woods, and #46 is beside a big tree where you can also plant a Magic Bean.

Plant that bean as a child, because #47 needs it. As adult Link at night, ride the bean's leaf platform. When it passes the cliff near the Deku Theater tunnel, Hookshot the Skulltula off the wall.

  • GS #44: Soft soil patch in the Lost Woods
    Young Link, any time. Needs: Bottled Bugs. Release bugs from a bottle over the soft soil; the Skulltula pops out of the ground, kill it for the token.
  • GS #45: Second soft soil patch in the Lost Woods
    Young Link, any time. Needs: Bottled Bugs. Find the other soft soil patch in the Lost Woods and release bugs over it; kill the Skulltula that pops out for the token.
  • GS #46: Soft-soil patch beside a big tree
    Young Link, any time. Needs: Bottle with Bugs. In the Lost Woods, go to the soft-soil patch next to the big tree. Pour bugs on it, kill the Skulltula that comes up and take the token.
  • GS #47: High on the cliff wall, reached by riding the Magic Bean leaf platform
    Adult Link, night only. Needs: Magic Bean (planted as child), Hookshot. As adult, ride the bean leaf platform you planted as a child. When it passes the cliff, Hookshot the Skulltula on the wall and grab the token.
Plant Magic Beans in every soft soil patch you can as a child. Two adult tokens (#47 here and #93 in the Desert Colossus) need a grown bean platform.

Hyrule Field Gold Skulltulas (3)

Hyrule Field's three tokens are all inside grottos.

#49 (adult) is in an open hole near the small pond at the south end of the field, toward the Lake Hylia exit. Kill it with your sword. #50 (adult) is in the grotto in the grove of trees south-west of Lon Lon Ranch. Hookshot it off the cobweb wall.

  • GS #48: Inside the hidden grotto near the Gerudo Valley entrance
    Young Link, any time. Needs: Bombs, Boomerang. Bomb open the hidden grotto by the rocks near Gerudo Valley, drop in and kill the Skulltula on the cave wall, then grab the token.
  • GS #49: South field near the Lake Hylia exit, by the small pond: open grotto hole
    Adult Link, any time. Needs: Sword. Drop into the open hole in the ground near the small pond at the south end of Hyrule Field. Kill the Gold Skulltula inside the grotto with your sword and take the token.
  • GS #50: Tree grove south-west of Lon Lon Ranch: grotto with cobwebs
    Adult Link, any time. Needs: Hookshot. Enter the grotto hole in the grove of trees south-west of Lon Lon Ranch. Inside, hookshot the Gold Skulltula on the cobweb wall and grab the token.

Gerudo Valley Gold Skulltulas (4)

Two child tokens down by the river and two adult tokens on the Fortress side.

As adult Link at night, cross the bridge to the Fortress side. #53 is high on the canyon wall past the natural rock arch (play the Sun's Song if it's daytime). #54 is on the stone wall right behind the carpenters' tent where the Master Craftsman stands. Both need the Hookshot.

  • GS #51: On the wooden wall by the small waterfall under the bridge
    Young Link, any time. Needs: Boomerang. Drop off the bridge into the river below, then Boomerang the Skulltula on the wooden structure by the small waterfall and collect the token.
  • GS #52: Soft soil on the grassy ledge beside the waterfall
    Young Link, any time. Needs: Bottled Bugs. On the grassy ledge by the waterfall, release bugs over the soft soil; kill the Skulltula that pops out for the token.
  • GS #53: High on the canyon rock wall on the far side of the bridge, past the natural rock arch
    Adult Link, night only. Needs: Hookshot, Sun's Song. As adult, cross to the Fortress side of Gerudo Valley and play the Sun's Song to make it night. Walk to the rock wall past the arch and Hookshot the Skulltula high on the wall.
  • GS #54: Stone wall right behind the carpenters' tent where the Master Craftsman stands
    Adult Link, night only. Needs: Hookshot. At night as adult, go to the carpenters' tent past the bridge. Look at the stone wall behind the tent and Hookshot the Skulltula off it.

Death Mountain Crater Gold Skulltulas (2)

Best Route: How to Get All 100 Without Backtracking

  1. First child pass: clear the Deku Tree (#1-4), Castle Town (#5), the castle grounds by day (#6), Kakariko at night (#9-13) and Lon Lon Ranch (#15 at night, #17 any time). That's 13 tokens, enough for the 10-token wallet.
  2. Fill bottles with bugs and hit the soft soil patches in the Graveyard (#18), Death Mountain Trail (#20), Zora's River (#29) and Lake Hylia (#34). Grab Zora's River #30 with the Slingshot on the way. Plant Magic Beans as you go.
  3. Dodongo's Cavern and Jabu-Jabu's Belly: take all four child tokens in each (#24-27, #38-41). The Boomerang from Jabu-Jabu opens up Zora's River #31, Zora's Fountain #43, Lake Hylia #35, Lon Lon #16 and Graveyard #19 at night.
  4. Before you pull the Master Sword: pick up the remaining early child tokens: Death Mountain Trail #21 (Bombs), Zora's Fountain #42, the Lost Woods soil (#44-46), the Hyrule Field grotto (#48), Gerudo Valley (#51-52) and Goron City (#56).
  5. As adult with the Hookshot: Kakariko #14, Goron City #55, Sacred Forest Meadow #57, then the Forest Temple (#58-62), Fire Temple (#63-67), the Death Mountain Trail Hammer pair (#22-23) and Dodongo's Cavern #28 once you know the Scarecrow's Song.
  6. Ice and water: Zora's Domain #68, the Ice Cavern (#69-71), Lake Hylia #36-37 and the Water Temple (#72-76). The Longshot from here opens #91.
  7. Night sweep with the Hookshot: Zora's River #32-33, Hyrule Castle #7, Hyrule Field grottos #49-50, Gerudo Valley #53-54, Kokiri Forest #87, Lost Woods #47 on the bean platform.
  8. Child return trips: the Song of Storms grotto at the castle (#8), the Bottom of the Well (#77-79), Death Mountain Crater (#80-81), and the child side of the Spirit Temple (#95-97) plus Colossus soil (#92).
  9. Endgame: Shadow Temple (#82-86), Gerudo's Fortress (#88-89), Haunted Wasteland (#90), Desert Colossus (#91, #93), Lon Lon Ranch tower (#94), frozen Zora's Fountain cave (#98) and the adult Spirit Temple (#99-100). Then walk into the House of Skulltula for the Gold Rupee.

Frequently asked questions

How many Gold Skulltulas are in the Ocarina of Time Remake?

100. 44 are inside dungeons and 56 are in the overworld. 50 need young Link and 50 need adult Link.

What do you get for collecting all 100 Gold Skulltula tokens?

The curse is lifted from the whole family in the House of Skulltula, and the rich man gives you a Gold Rupee worth 200 rupees.

Where do you turn in Gold Skulltula tokens?

At the House of Skulltula in Kakariko Village. Rewards come at 10, 20, 30, 40, 50 and 100 tokens: a bigger wallet, the Echo Gem, the Giant's Wallet, 20 Bombchus, a Piece of Heart and the Gold Rupee.

What is the Echo Gem in Ocarina of Time Remake?

It's the 20-token reward and it's new in the remake. It reacts more strongly the closer you bring it to a Gold Skulltula, which makes the remaining tokens much easier to find.

Which Gold Skulltulas only appear at night?

27 of them. They're marked 'night only' in every checklist above. Play the Sun's Song to switch to night instead of waiting.

Can you get all Gold Skulltulas as young Link?

No. 50 need adult Link, mostly because they need the Hookshot, Longshot, Megaton Hammer, Iron Boots or a grown Magic Bean platform.

What is the best reward from the House of Skulltula?

The Giant's Wallet at 30 tokens (holds 1,000 rupees) and the Piece of Heart at 50 tokens are the two most useful, so aim for those first.
